## 原文 O1（L117，H1 后替代方案排除段）

> There are two related reasons why we do not hypothesize that institutional investors seek to influence the ratings of their own portfolio firms. First, as warnings about the anticompetitive effects of common owners have been raised among regulators, common owners can decrease their risk of detection by procuring worse ESG ratings for firms not in their portfolios. Second, by handicapping a target firm that competes with their portfolio firms, common owners can realize improved investment prospects among several of their portfolio firms. This relative effect occurs because ESG standards differ widely between industries, forcing stakeholders and investors to make ESG comparisons on a within-industry basis. Taken together, by negatively influencing their portfolio firms' rivals, common owners can realize the rewards of their information-based competitive influence while decreasing the risk of their influence being detected.

**功能特征**：claim=段末 "Taken together" 收束句；moves=2（First 检测风险 / Second 相对收益）；evidence=两处无引文的一般性断言（监管警告、ESG 标准差异——后者带 because 展开为 warrant 性前提）；A&R=整段即对"为何不假设自家组合公司"异议的回应（段级 A&R）；warrant=行业内相对比较原则；段首句是元话语引导句（"There are two related reasons..."）。

## 原文 O2（L125，H2a 推导收束段）

> According to Rhee and Valdez (2009), rating agencies should play a critical role in offsetting the damage to a portfolio firm's reputation by providing "independent" and "expert" assessments of a firm's performance relative to its peers. For a portfolio firm whose reputation is called into question by media coverage of its ESG controversies, subsequent improvements to the firm's ESG ratings could eliminate stakeholders' reservations about the firm. Because stakeholders trust ESG ratings, those rating improvements may make the firm appear to have better ESG performance than its peers. Because ESG ratings effectively counteract media-induced reputational damage, and because stakeholders' assessments are based on relative comparisons between similar firms, common owners may be especially motivated to attempt to enhance the relative reputations of portfolio firms that have recently sustained damage to their reputations due to ESG media controversies. Following our prior logic, we expect common owners to achieve this goal by tarnishing the reputations of (target) firms that compete with their portfolio firms. That is, a target firm is likely to receive worse coverage from a rating agency in which rivals' institutional investors have greater ownership when those rivals face more ESG media controversies. ## 原文 O3（L141，H3a 机制段）

> When a firm has recently won a major award for its ESG initiatives, common owners should find it easier to sway an ESG rating agency in favor of that firm, and against its less-decorated peers. Major awards can send messages to stakeholders about the authenticity and impact of a company's practices and policies (Kovács & Sharkey, 2014). When based on a firm's record of ESG initiatives, these awards can signal that a firm has made laudable efforts to embrace sustainable and responsible practices that promote the interests of different stakeholders. By doing so, ESG raters should become more receptive not only to positive information about firms that have won ESG awards, but also to negative information about their less-decorated peers whose efforts have not attracted such attention. At the same time, since most ESG rating agencies do not directly adjust their ratings based on a firm's ESG awards (an achievement not included in most ratings methodologies), ESG ratings are not directly affected by the awards. Rather, given their publicity, such awards independently shape stakeholders' perceptions about the firm's ESG performance. As a result, ESG awards can affect the receptivity of executives and analysts to the information common owners share about these firms.

**功能特征**：claim=段首 topic sentence（条件性 claim：获奖→更易左右）；moves=2（信号→receptivity 双向；方法不计入→感知通道独立）；evidence=Kovács & Sharkey 2014；warrant=显式边界句 "At the same time, since...not directly affected"（奖项起作用通道在评级方法之外的澄清，概念桥接无锚）；收束="As a result" 点明 receptivity 机制；A&R=无。
