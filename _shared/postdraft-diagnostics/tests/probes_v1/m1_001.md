## Results

Therefore, to be as comprehensive as possible in examining the relationship between MPG and safety defects, we conduct an IV analysis, using fuel prices as an IV for MPG.

This analysis allows us to determine if increasing MPG  causes  a slower or faster time to defect (i.e., mileage at the time of the safety defect) in addition to more defects.

We obtain fuel price data from the Energy Information Administration (EIA) which provides the U.S. gasoline and diesel retail prices used for our IV (EIA, 2026).
