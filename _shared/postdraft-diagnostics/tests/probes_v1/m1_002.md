## Results

We obtain fuel price data from the Energy Information Administration (EIA) which provides the U.S. gasoline and diesel retail prices used for our IV (EIA, 2026).

We use this policy change to examine whether regulatory pressure  causes  the adoption of MPG-enhancing technology bundles.

Specifically, we match vehicles to the fuel price of their own fuel-type: either diesel, regular gasoline, midgrade gasoline, or premium gasoline.
