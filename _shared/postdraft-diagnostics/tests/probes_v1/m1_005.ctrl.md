## Results

A one-standard-deviation increase in *Time-to-Recall* yields a stock market penalty ranging from −1.94% to −4.51% for high-severity recalls.

Our finding that greater CEO stock ownership is associated with a longer time-to-recall may be subject to endogeneity.

Table A3 in the online appendix presents the mean CARs for recalls *below* the median time-to-recall and the mean CARs for recalls *at or above* the median time-to-recall, as well as Patell's *t* -tests.
