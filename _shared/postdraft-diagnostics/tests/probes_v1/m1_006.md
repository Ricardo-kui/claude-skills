## Results

"Time-to-recall: all" is the mean days for all recalls per firm-year.

This means that, whereas changing from no females on the board to one female on the board  causes  an increase in class 3 recalls, the increase in class 3 recall counts continues to climb as a second female is added and a third female or more are added.

"Time-to-recall: C1" is the mean days for class 1 recalls per firm-year.
