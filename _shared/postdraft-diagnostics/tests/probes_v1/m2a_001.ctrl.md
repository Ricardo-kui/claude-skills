## Results

As a second benchmark, we compared MSCI ESG ratings to ASSET4 ESG evaluations captured by the most recent updated ESG combined score (scaled from 0 to 1) provided by Refinitiv.

To address the possibility that common ownership in an ESG rating agency and a target firm's rivals is associated with actual ESG performance, we examined the extent to which MSCI's ESG rating deviates from the reasonable ESG benchmarks.

The inconsistent nature of ESG ratings among different ESG rating agencies provides us with an opportunity to investigate whether such divergence is shaped by ownership in a rating agency.
