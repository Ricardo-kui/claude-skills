## Results

These include three tests that explore potential endogeneity bias, one that examines the level of analysis of our study, and one that uses alternatives to our FBR measure.

Increasing FBR  increases  a faster time-to-recall for the most severe, life-threatening defects needing recall.

Although the exclusion criteria cannot be explicitly tested, we propose that BFC is unlikely to be directly correlated with our dependent variables.
