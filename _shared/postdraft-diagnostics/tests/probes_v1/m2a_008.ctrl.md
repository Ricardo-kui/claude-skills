## Results

Importantly, we only assigned this measure as a one if the CEO was in the role *prior* to a given recall's defect awareness date, not the recall initiation date.

The CEM-weighted recurrent-event AFT model results presented in Table A4 in the online appendix suggest that greater CEO stock ownership is associated with a longer time-to-recall for all recalls and for high-severity recalls, but not for low-severity recalls, although the small sample size precludes us from confidently drawing strong conclusions about low-severity recalls.

The results were robust to using various combinations of our control variables as pretreatment matching variables.
