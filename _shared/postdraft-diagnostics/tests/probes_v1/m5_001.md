## Results

The outcome measures for the two separate PSM models are the count of class 3 recalls and class 1 time-to-recall.

Interpreting similarly, a one standard deviation increase in FBR  may be associated with  a 16-day acceleration in time-to-recall for the most severe class 1 recalls. [^2]

This PSM analysis performs a one-to-one match of all firm-year observations on equal propensity of being above the median of FBR as predicted by our control variables.
