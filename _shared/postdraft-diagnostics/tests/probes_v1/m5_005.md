5.1.3 Empirical evidence: Engine technology bundles, MPG, and safety defects.

The negative binomial coefficient interpretation indicates that each one-unit increase in MPG  may be associated with  a 17.5% (i.e., $\exp(0.161)-1=0.175$) increase in the expected number of safety defects in that model-engine-year.
