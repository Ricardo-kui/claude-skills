# Closing Elevation

## 功能定义
在 Discussion 最后一段将论文发现升华到更高理论或实践层面——不是"我们研究了 X"，而是"我们的发现对理解 Y 有什么更广泛的启示"，给读者留下持久印象。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（理论升华型）**：
```
In sum, our study challenges the prevailing assumption that
[prevailing assumption] and points to the need for a more nuanced
understanding of [phenomenon]. By [specific contribution], we show
that [core insight]. These findings have broad implications for
[broad theoretical domain], suggesting that [elevated insight].
```

**模板 B（实践呼吁型）**：
```
Our findings carry important implications for [practitioners / policy
makers]. In an era of [current trend], organizations must recognize
that [core insight]. Rather than [common practice], our findings
suggest that [alternative practice]. This shift in perspective is
not merely academic—it has tangible consequences for [outcome].
```

**模板 C（领域拓展型）**：
```
Beyond the immediate context of [specific domain], our findings speak
to broader questions about [general phenomenon]. Just as [analogy],
[core mechanism] may operate across [other domains]. We hope our
study inspires future research to explore these connections.
```

**模板 D（谦逊而坚定型）**：
```
Despite the limitations noted above, we believe our findings offer
important insights into [phenomenon]. By [method / finding], we
have [specific contribution]. We hope this study serves as a
foundation for [future direction] and encourages scholars to
[specific call to action].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：State Ownership and Firm Innovation — Zhou et al., 2017 (ASQ)

> "In sum, our study challenges the prevailing assumption that state
> ownership is uniformly detrimental to innovation. By integrating
> institutional and efficiency logics, we show that the effect of state
> ownership on innovation follows an inverted U-shaped pattern. These
> findings have broad implications for emerging economy research,
> suggesting that the role of government ownership is more nuanced
> than previously recognized."

**来源**：Waiting to Inhale — Lashley & Pollock, 2020 (ASQ)

> "Our findings carry important implications for organizations operating
> in stigmatized industries. In an era of increasing social scrutiny,
> organizations must recognize that legitimacy is not given but constructed
> through strategic action."

**来源**：Why Elites Love Authentic Lowbrow Culture — Hahl et al., 2017 (ASQ)

> "Beyond the immediate context of cultural consumption, our findings
> speak to broader questions about how high-status actors manage
> social evaluations. Just as CEOs use strategic nonconformity to
> attain celebrity, elites may use cultural choices to navigate
> status hierarchies."

**改写模板**：
> "In sum, our study challenges the prevailing assumption that
> [prevailing assumption]. By [specific contribution], we show that
> [core insight]. These findings have broad implications for [theoretical
> domain], suggesting that [elevated insight]. We hope this study
> serves as a foundation for [future direction]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型——所有期刊都需要有力的结尾 |
| **理论类型** | 通用型——任何实证论文 |
| **前提条件** | 升华必须基于论文的具体发现，不能脱离数据空谈 |
| **风险** | 升华过高会显得空洞；升华过低会显得平淡 |

## 关键技巧

结尾升华的核心是**连接具体发现与普遍意义**：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "Our findings are important" | "Our findings challenge the prevailing assumption that X is uniformly Y, pointing to a more nuanced understanding that has broad implications for Z" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 空洞升华 | "Our findings have important implications for theory and practice" | 必须具体说明对什么理论、什么实践 |
| 重复摘要 | 结尾与摘要内容几乎相同 | 结尾应升华到比摘要更高的层面 |
| 引入新理论 | 结尾突然引入论文未讨论的理论 | 升华必须基于论文已有的理论框架 |
