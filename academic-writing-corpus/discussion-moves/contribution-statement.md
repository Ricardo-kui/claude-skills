# Contribution Statement

## 功能定义
清晰、具体地陈述论文的理论贡献——不是"我们丰富了文献"，而是"我们解决了文献中的哪个具体问题，如何解决的"，让每个贡献都可被审稿人独立评估。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（问题-解决型）**：
```
Our study contributes to [literature] by [specific contribution],
offering insights on [phenomenon]. Prior research has [established
X / focused on Y], but it has largely overlooked [specific gap].
We address this gap by [method / finding], which reveals that
[specific insight].
```

**模板 B（新视角型）**：
```
We offer a new and opposing view of [phenomenon]. Whereas prior
research has emphasized [dominant perspective], our findings suggest
that [alternative perspective]. This reconceptualization is important
because it [theoretical implication].
```

**模板 C（拼图型）**：
```
We provide a missing piece to extant research by showing that
[finding]. Prior work has established that [A leads to B] and that
[C moderates B], but the mechanism linking [A and C] has remained
unexplored. Our study fills this gap by demonstrating that [specific
mechanism].
```

**模板 D（三贡献型）**：
```
Our study makes three contributions. First, we contribute to [Literature
A] by [specific contribution]. Second, we contribute to [Literature B]
by [specific contribution]. Third, we contribute to [Literature C] by
[specific contribution].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early? — Eilert et al., 2017 (JM)

> "Our study contributes to research on [product recalls] by [identifying
> the antecedents of recall timing], offering insights on [how brand
> characteristics shape firm responsiveness to safety investigations]."

**来源**：A Rising Tide Lifts All Boats — DesJardine et al.

> "We offer a new and opposing view of [common ownership]. Whereas prior
> research has emphasized [the negative effects of common ownership on
> competition], our findings suggest that [common ownership can enhance
> CSR performance]."

**来源**：CEO Regulatory Focus and Myopic Marketing Management

> "We provide a missing piece to extant research by showing that [CEO
> regulatory focus shapes myopic marketing management]. Prior work has
> established that [CEO characteristics affect firm strategy], but the
> role of regulatory focus in marketing decisions has remained unexplored."

**来源**：CEO Stock Ownership, Recall Timing, and Stock Market Penalties — Darby et al.

> "Our study makes three contributions. First, we contribute to the
> CEO-recall nomological network by [linking CEO ownership to recall
> timing]. Second, we contribute to research on governance by [showing
> how ownership structure shapes crisis response]. Third, we contribute
> to practice by [highlighting the market penalties of delayed recalls]."

**改写模板**：
> "Our study contributes to [literature] by [specific contribution].
> Prior research has [established X], but it has largely overlooked
> [specific gap]. We address this gap by [method / finding], which
> reveals that [specific insight]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型——所有期刊都需要贡献声明 |
| **理论类型** | 通用型——任何实证论文 |
| **前提条件** | 每个贡献必须具体、可验证；不能只是"我们丰富了文献" |
| **风险** | 贡献过大（"我们彻底改变了领域"）会被质疑；贡献过小（"我们用了新数据"）会被拒 |

## 关键技巧

贡献声明的核心是**具体化**：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "We contribute to the literature" | "We contribute to research on X by showing that Y, which prior work has overlooked" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 贡献空洞 | "We enrich the literature" | 必须说明丰富了什么、如何丰富 |
| 贡献过多 | 声称 5-6 个贡献 | 2-3 个核心贡献足够；过多分散焦点 |
| 贡献与假设脱节 | 贡献声明中的发现不在假设中 | 贡献必须直接来自假设检验结果 |
