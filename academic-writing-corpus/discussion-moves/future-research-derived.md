# Future Research Derived from Limitations

## 功能定义
从实际局限性出发推导未来研究方向——不是"未来可以研究更多"，而是"因为我们无法做到 X，所以未来研究可以做 X"，让每个建议都有具体的方法论或理论依据。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（局限推导型）**：
```
Future research could address these limitations by [specific method].
For example, because our study relies on [data type], future research
could use [alternative data type] to [specific capability]. This would
allow researchers to [specific insight].
```

**模板 B（机制深化型）**：
```
Our findings point to several promising avenues for future research.
First, because our study examines [macro/meso-level phenomenon], future
research could investigate the underlying micro-level mechanisms. For
example, [specific micro mechanism] could be examined using [method].
Second, [theoretical extension].
```

**模板 C（情境扩展型）**：
```
Future research could extend our findings by examining [phenomenon]
in [new context]. Our study focuses on [context], but [phenomenon]
might operate differently in [alternative context] because [theoretical
reason].
```

**模板 D（方法创新型）**：
```
Because our study is limited to [methodological approach], future
research could employ [alternative method] to [specific advantage].
For instance, [method] would enable researchers to [capability that
current study lacks].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Do Political Ties Facilitate Operational Efficiency

> "Future studies can corroborate our framework in other emerging or
> developed economies. Second, because of data limitations, we are
> unable to disentangle different types of political ties... Future
> studies could examine and distinguish how these different types of
> political ties function. Relatedly, the archival data prevent us from
> examining the underlying mechanisms through which political ties affect
> operational efficiency. Field studies or interviews are needed to
> examine such mechanisms."

**来源**：CEO Regulatory Focus and Myopic Marketing Management

> "Future research could investigate whether regulatory focus shapes
> other types of short-term decisions, such as R&D cuts or employee
> layoffs. Additionally, because our study relies on archival data,
> future research could use experiments to establish causal mechanisms."

**来源**：State Ownership and Firm Innovation — Zhou et al., 2017 (ASQ)

> "Future research could examine whether our findings generalize to
> other emerging economies. In addition, longitudinal case studies
> could provide richer insights into how state ownership affects
> innovation processes at the firm level."

**改写模板**：> "Future research could address these limitations by [specific method].
> For example, because our study relies on [data type], future research
> could use [alternative data type] to [specific capability]. Second,
> our findings point to [theoretical extension]. Future research could
> investigate [specific question] using [method]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型——所有期刊都接受未来研究方向 |
| **理论类型** | 通用型——任何实证论文 |
| **前提条件** | 每个建议必须具体、可操作；不能只是"more research is needed" |
| **风险** | 若建议过于宽泛，会被视为敷衍；若建议过于具体，会限制读者想象 |

## 关键技巧

未来研究建议的核心是**可操作化**：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "Future research could examine this in other contexts" | "Future research could examine whether our findings hold in developed economies, where institutional voids are less prevalent and state ownership operates through different mechanisms" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 泛泛而谈 | "More research is needed" | 必须说明需要什么研究、为什么需要、如何开展 |
| 自我贬低 | "Our study is so limited that future research must redo everything" | 建议应是对论文的自然延伸而非否定 |
| 与论文无关 | 建议完全脱离论文主题 | 建议必须基于论文的具体发现或局限性 |
