# Limitation Boundary Control

## 功能定义
诚实地承认研究局限性，同时控制局限性的理论边界——不是"我们有局限性"，而是"这些局限性不影响核心结论的有效性"，展示研究者对方法论威胁的清醒评估。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（标准局限型）**：
```
Our study is not without limitations. First, [limitation 1].
This may [affect generalizability / introduce bias / constrain
inference]. However, [why this limitation does not invalidate core
findings]. Second, [limitation 2]. [Implication and mitigation].
Third, [limitation 3]. [Implication and mitigation].
```

**模板 B（数据局限型）**：
```
Our research context is [specific context], which limits the
generalizability of our results. [Context-specific features].
Hence, future studies can corroborate our framework in [other contexts].
Second, because of data limitations, we are unable to [specific
measurement / analysis]. [Why this does not undermine main findings].
```

**模板 C（方法局限型）**：
```
Our study relies on [method / data type], which [specific limitation].
While this approach allows us to [strength], it does not enable us to
[capability]. We mitigate this concern by [robustness check / alternative
analysis]. Nevertheless, we acknowledge that [remaining limitation].
```

**模板 D（边界控制型）**：
```
Despite these limitations, our findings offer important insights into
[phenomenon]. The [specific limitation] does not threaten our core
conclusion that [core finding] because [justification]. Future research
could address these limitations by [specific suggestion].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Do Political Ties Facilitate Operational Efficiency

> "This study has several limitations that may be addressed in future
> research. First, our research context is China, a nation with a unique
> institutional environment where political ties are prevalently used,
> which limits the generalizability of our results. Hence, future studies
> can corroborate our framework in other emerging or developed economies."

> "Second, because of data limitations, we are unable to disentangle
> different types of political ties (e.g., cultivated vs. obligatory).
> Future studies could examine and distinguish how these different types
> of political ties function."

**来源**：The Push and Pull of Attaining CEO Celebrity — Lovelace et al., 2022 (AMJ)

> "It is important to note that, by selecting CEOs who had at least modest
> WSJ coverage, we were not preordaining their scores on our eventual
> multi-item celebrity index, but it did help ensure that we had individuals
> with at least some foundational signs of celebrity... That said, there
> are limitations with this approach, which we discuss below."

**改写模板**：
> "Our study is not without limitations. First, [specific limitation].
> This may [affect generalizability]. However, [why this limitation
> does not invalidate core findings: e.g., 'our main results hold across
> multiple subsamples and alternative measures']. Second, [specific
> limitation]. Future research could address this by [specific suggestion].
> Despite these limitations, our findings offer important insights into
> [phenomenon]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型——所有期刊都接受诚实的局限性讨论 |
| **理论类型** | 通用型——任何实证论文 |
| **前提条件** | 每个局限性必须具体；不能只是"样本有限"；必须说明为什么不影响核心结论 |
| **风险** | 若局限性过大会被 desk reject；若局限性过小会被认为缺乏自省 |

## 关键技巧

局限性控制的核心是**展示方法论意识**：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "Our study has limitations" | "While our context limits generalizability, our main results hold across [alternative specifications], suggesting that the core mechanism is robust" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 过度道歉 | "Our study is extremely limited" | 承认局限性但强调核心结论的稳健性 |
| 无边界控制 | 只讲局限性不讲为什么不影响核心发现 | 每个局限性后必须接缓解论证 |
| 与结果矛盾 | 局限性暗示核心发现可能不成立 | 若局限性真的威胁核心发现，需重新设计研究 |
