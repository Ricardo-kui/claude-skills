# Reversal Silver Lining Narrative

## 功能定义
将负面发现（如正向调节不显著、主效应方向相反）重新框架为理论贡献——不是"假设没支持"，而是"我们发现了一个被忽视的正向机制"，从失望的灰烬中提取理论价值。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（反直觉发现型）**：
```
Although we predicted that [X] would [increase/decrease] [Y], our
results show that [X] actually [decreases/increases] [Y]. At first
glance, this finding appears to contradict [theory]. However, we
argue that this apparent contradiction reveals a more nuanced
understanding of [phenomenon]. Specifically, [alternative theoretical
mechanism]. This suggests that [new theoretical insight].
```

**模板 B（从负面中提取正面型）**：
```
While [negative outcome] is typically viewed as [undesirable],
our findings suggest that it may have an underappreciated positive
consequence: [positive outcome]. This reversal is theoretically
important because it challenges the prevailing assumption that
[prevailing assumption] and points to the need for a more balanced
understanding of [phenomenon].
```

**模板 C（矛盾发现型）**：
```
Our finding that [X] [negatively/positively] affects [Y] appears
inconsistent with [prior theory]. However, this inconsistency may
be more apparent than real. [Theoretical reconciliation]. Thus,
rather than viewing this finding as a failure to replicate [prior
research], we see it as an opportunity to refine [theory].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：On the Tip of the Brain — Paruchuri et al.

> "While negative reputational events are typically viewed as harmful,
> our findings suggest that they can have positive reputation spillovers
> under certain conditions. This reversal is theoretically important
> because it challenges the prevailing assumption that all negative
> events uniformly damage reputation."

**来源**：Two Sides of the Same Coin — Pontikes, 2012 (ASQ)

> "While market categories confer legitimacy that enables organizations
> to access resources, they also impose constraints that can stifle
> innovation. This dual nature suggests that the benefits of category
> membership are not unalloyed."

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early? — Eilert et al., 2017 (JM)

> "Although problem severity delays recalls (a seemingly negative outcome),
> this delay may reflect the firm's thorough investigation process rather
> than negligence. This nuance is important for stakeholders assessing
> firm responsiveness."

**改写模板**：
> "Although we predicted that [X] would [increase] [Y], our results show
> that [X] actually [decreases] [Y]. At first glance, this finding appears
> to contradict [theory]. However, we argue that this apparent contradiction
> reveals a more nuanced understanding of [phenomenon]. Specifically,
> [alternative mechanism]. This suggests that [new theoretical insight]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | ASQ, AMJ — 适合挑战现有理论的论文 |
| **理论类型** | 反直觉发现、矛盾结果、边界条件 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有可信的替代理论解释反转发现；不能只是"数据可能有问题" |
| **风险** | 若反转解释牵强，会被批评为事后合理化；必须有前置理论或机制支撑 |

## 关键技巧

反转叙事的核心是**展示理论进步**：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "Our results are opposite to predictions" | "Our results reveal a countervailing mechanism that prior theory has overlooked" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 数据怪罪 | "The opposite finding may be due to measurement error" | 必须提供理论解释而非方法解释 |
| 过度道歉 | "We are surprised by this finding" | 反转发现应是论文亮点而非尴尬 |
| 无机制解释 | "X has opposite effect"但不解释为什么 | 必须提出替代机制 |
