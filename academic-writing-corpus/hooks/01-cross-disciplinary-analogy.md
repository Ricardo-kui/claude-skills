# Cross-Disciplinary Analogy Hook

## 功能定义
从一个 distant discipline 的成熟概念/现象出发，通过类比建立认知桥梁，再转移到本学科的 focal phenomenon，制造"这个概念在我们领域也混乱不堪"的悬念。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（三段式递进）**：
```
In [Discipline A], scholars have long recognized that [core concept] is not a single entity but rather a set of related yet distinct constructs (e.g., [Example A1], [Example A2]).
More recently, researchers in [Discipline B] have begun to draw similar distinctions (e.g., [Example B1], [Example B2]).
Yet in [Social Science/Our Field], [core concept] has remained largely [undifferentiated / tangled / treated as unitary], despite its growing prominence in research on [topic domain].
```

**模板 B（两段式压缩）**：
```
[Discipline A] has long understood that [phenomenon X] comprises multiple distinct processes.
[Our field], by contrast, has tended to treat [phenomenon X] as a [single construct / black box], even as it has become central to [research area].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：(Un)Tangled — Pollock et al., ASQ

> "Biologists have long recognized that symbiosis is not a single, unified phenomenon but rather a set of related yet distinct processes... Astronomers, too, have drawn similar distinctions... Yet in the social sciences, the concept of [X] has remained largely tangled, despite its growing prominence in research on [Y]."

**改写模板**：
> "In [distant field A], scholars have long recognized that [concept] is not a single entity but rather a set of related yet distinct constructs (e.g., [subtype 1], [subtype 2]). More recently, researchers in [adjacent field B] have begun to draw similar distinctions (e.g., [subtype 3]). Yet in [our field], [concept] has remained largely [undifferentiated / conflated], despite its growing prominence in research on [topic]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | ASQ, Organization Science 极度适合；SMJ 也可用，但需更快切入商业现象 |
| **理论类型** | 最适合 **概念澄清/解构型** 论文（typology, conceptualization, dimensionalization） |
| **前提条件** | 你的核心概念确实在文献中被混用；你能找到至少两个 distant disciplines 的先例 |
| **风险** | 如果类比牵强，会被 reviewer 批评为"forced analogy"；确保 distant discipline 的区分确实能映射到本领域 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 类比过近 | 从"战略管理类比到组织行为"，距离不够，无法制造认知刷新感 | 至少跨越一级学科（生物学→社会学；物理学→管理学） |
| 类比后无转移 | 花了两段讲生物学，第三段突然出现"所以我们研究企业联盟"，中间缺乏概念映射 | 必须在转移句中显性说明 "just as biologists distinguish between... we argue that social scientists must distinguish between..." |
| 类比装饰化 |  distant discipline 的知识只是点缀，不影响核心论证 | 类比必须构成论文的核心逻辑支点（如后续假设直接从类比中的区分导出） |
