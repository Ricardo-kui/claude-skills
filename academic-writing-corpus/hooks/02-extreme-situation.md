# Extreme Situation / "Imagine..." Hook

## 功能定义
用一个极端、反常规的情境（通常是非法、危险、被污名化的情境）作为叙事入口，迫使读者进入一个他们从未想象过的组织场景，从而悬浮常规判断，为后续的 concept-building 创造认知空间。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（直接式 "Imagine..."）**：
```
Imagine [doing X] when [extreme institutional condition]: [specific consequence].
[Second sentence: compound the extremity].
[Third sentence: convert the scenario into a research puzzle].
```

**模板 B（间接式场景描写）**：
```
[Actor] must routinely [extremely difficult action] in an environment where [institutional constraint].
Not only must they [challenge 1], but they also face [challenge 2].
[Convert to puzzle: How do they manage / survive / legitimate their existence?]
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Waiting to Inhale — Lashley & Pollock, ASQ

> "Imagine starting a business when the federal government has declared your product illegal, when traditional sources of financing are unavailable because bankers fear being accused of money laundering, and when you cannot advertise through conventional media because your product violates federal advertising standards."

**改写模板**：
> "Imagine [founding/managing/operating] [an organization / a venture] when [institutional actor] has declared your [core product/activity] [illegal/illegitimate/taboo], when [key resource] is unavailable because [stigma mechanism], and when you cannot [standard business practice] because [institutional barrier]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | ASQ 极度适合（定性/民族志论文）；Organization Science 也可用；AMJ/SMJ 需更快显示理论通用性 |
| **理论类型** | 最适合 **过程理论/合法性理论/制度理论** 的质性论文；也适用于任何需要"去自然化"常规组织现象的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 你的情境确实足够极端；读者第一眼就能感受到"这不可能"或"这怎么做到的" |
| **风险** | 过于戏剧化会稀释学术严谨性；必须在前两段内完成从"场景"到"理论问题"的转换 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| "Imagine" 后无理论回收 | 场景很震撼，但后文没有回到这个场景来解释发现 | 必须在 Discussion 中重新引用 hook 中的意象（如"just as entrepreneurs in our opening scenario..."） |
| 极端情境只是案例 | 情境虽然极端，但论文只是描述它，没有从中提炼一般化机制 | Pollock 的教训：极端情境是理论透镜，不是研究对象本身 |
| 过度煽情 | 使用太多情感性形容词（horrifying, heartbreaking） | 保持分析的冷静，让情境本身的结构产生冲击力 |

## 关键技巧：Goffman 框架的隐性铺垫

Waiting to Inhale 的精妙之处在于，hook 中的三个约束恰好对应 Goffman 的三个舞台：
- "federal government declared illegal" → **front stage** 的法律污名
- "bankers fear money laundering" → **back stage** 的金融排斥
- "cannot advertise through conventional media" → **side stage** 的传播困境

**操作化建议**：设计 hook 时，让极端情境的多重约束自然映射到你将要使用的理论维度。
