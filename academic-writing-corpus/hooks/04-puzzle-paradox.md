# Puzzle / Paradox Hook

## 功能定义
用一个直接的对比性提问（"为什么A而B不...？"）或一个表面上违反直觉的现象作为开场，迫使读者意识到现有理解存在缺口。这种hook不依赖数据冲击，而是依赖逻辑张力。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（对比性提问）**：
```
Why do some [actors] achieve [positive outcome], while others with seemingly equal [characteristics] do not?
[Second sentence: what makes this puzzle consequential].
[Third sentence: current explanations are insufficient because...]
```

**模板 B（反直觉现象）**：
```
[Actor] — not [expected actor] — decide whether and when to [critical action].
[Second sentence: this is surprising because conventional wisdom holds that...]
[Third sentence: yet we know little about what drives their decisions].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：CEO Celebrity — 待拆解（来自 memory mapping）

> "Why do some CEOs become celebrities, while others with seemingly equal accomplishments do not?"

**来源**：CEO Stock Ownership, Recall Timing, and Stock Market Penalties

> "Firm executives — not regulators — decide whether and when to recall defective products."

**改写模板（对比提问型）**：
> "Why do some [actors] [experience outcome A], while others with seemingly equal [attributes] do not? This puzzle is consequential because [stakes]. Existing explanations focusing on [dominant factor] are insufficient because they overlook [hidden mechanism]."

**改写模板（反直觉型）**：
> "[Actual decision maker] — not [expected decision maker] — decide whether and when to [critical organizational action]. This is surprising because conventional wisdom holds that [expected process]. Yet we know little about what drives [actual decision maker]'s [decision behavior]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型，所有管理学期刊均适用；特别适合 AMJ/SMJ 的微观-中观论文 |
| **理论类型** | 适合 ** upper echelons, governance, status/reputation, individual differences** 等关注"同样条件下为何结果不同"的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 对比的两方必须确实"看似平等"；读者第一眼就能感受到"这确实说不通" |
| **风险** | 如果对比的两方实际上并不平等（有显而易见的混淆变量），reviewer 会质疑 puzzle 的真实性 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 伪 puzzle | "为什么有的企业成功有的失败？" — 过于宽泛，答案显而易见（资源、能力、运气） | 必须限定"在 seemingly equal [specific condition] 下" |
| 提问后无解答承诺 | 抛出问题后没有暗示你将用什么理论来解答 | 第三句必须预告理论视角："Drawing on [theory], we argue that [mechanism] explains this variation" |
| 只有疑问无张力 | "这是一个有趣的问题" | 必须嵌入 stakes："This puzzle matters because [consequence]" |
