# Paradigm Challenge Hook

## 功能定义
直接挑战一个被广泛接受的理论预测或管理常识，展示该预测在特定情境下不仅不成立，而且其反向模式持续存在。这种hook适合"反转"或"边界条件"型论文。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（共识→反常识证据）**：
```
[Theoretical consensus / Conventional wisdom / Received theory] holds that [prediction] ([foundational citations]).
[Second sentence: why this prediction seems intuitively correct].
However, [empirical phenomenon / institutional fact / market behavior] [directly contradicts / systematically deviates from / persists in defiance of] this prediction.
[Third sentence: specific evidence showing the deviation is systematic rather than anecdotal].
[Fourth sentence: convert to research question].
```

**模板 B（管理常识→学术反转）**：
```
Practitioners and scholars alike have [assumed / advocated / recommended] that [best practice].
[Second sentence: the theoretical rationale seems compelling].
Yet [counter-evidence] suggests that [best practice] may [backfire / have unintended consequences / produce opposite effects] under [specific conditions].
[Third sentence: why this reversal demands theoretical attention].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Two Sides of the Same Coin（来自 memory mapping）

> "Consensus says ambiguous classification hurts market participants, but reality says it persists across numerous markets."

**改写模板**：
> "[Theoretical consensus / Conventional wisdom] holds that [prediction] ([foundational citation]). This prediction seems [intuitively correct / theoretically compelling] because [reason]. However, [empirical phenomenon] [directly contradicts / systematically deviates from] this prediction: [specific evidence]. This [persistence / deviation / reversal] suggests that [mechanism] may be [more complex / fundamentally different / contingent on unstated conditions] than [theory] assumes."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | ASQ, Organization Science, 任何欢迎理论挑战的期刊 |
| **理论类型** | **制度理论 / 种群生态学 / 信号理论** — 任何存在"清晰度/模糊性"、"合法性/边缘性"张力的话题 |
| **前提条件** | 被挑战的理论必须有坚实文献基础；反例必须系统、不可轻易解释掉 |
| **风险** | 如果 reviewer 认为反例只是特殊情况，挑战会失败 |

## 关键技巧：让挑战看起来是"建设性的"

最有效的 paradigm challenge 不是"你们全错了"，而是"你们的理论在[特定边界]内有效，但越界后需要修正"。

**建设性挑战句法**：
- "We do not argue that [theory] is wrong, but that its predictions hold only under [condition]."
- "Rather than rejecting [theory], we identify a boundary condition that limits its generalizability."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 稻草人攻击 | 把理论描述得过于简单，然后轻易推翻 | 必须承认理论的解释力，然后精确指出其边界 |
| 反例是边缘案例 | 用极端例外来推翻一般规律 | 反例必须在"核心地带"出现（如"不仅小公司模糊，大公司也模糊"） |
| 挑战后无替代 | 只说"现有理论不行"，不提供替代框架 | 必须在后文提供修正理论或整合框架 |
