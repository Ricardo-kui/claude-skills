# Authority Quotation + Everyday Analogy Hook

## 功能定义
先引用一句来自权威人物或匿名格言的引语建立主题基调，再通过一个与读者自身经验直接相关的日常类比场景（如校园欺凌事故、朋友圈误会扩散），将抽象理论概念转化为可体验的认知直觉，最后自然过渡到研究问题。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（格言 + 反问场景 + 类比）**：
```
"[Epigraph quotation evoking core tension]." — [Author or "Unknown"].
When you read about a [negative event], how widely do you spread the blame?
For example, if you read that [specific everyday scenario — e.g., a fraternity hazing incident], would that affect your view of just [level 1: immediate actor], [level 2: broader group], [level 3: institution], or [level 4: entire system]?
And would your perceptions of the entities beyond [level 1] be more negative, or more positive?
These questions lie at the heart of research on [topic].
```

**模板 B（权威引语 + 日常场景桥梁）**：
```
"[Quotation from recognized authority about the dilemma]." — [Name], [Title] of [Institution] ([source], [date]).
Consider a [familiar experience]: when [everyday situation], most people [intuitive reaction]. But what happens when [scaling up to organizational context]?
This tension between [force A] and [force B] defines the puzzle we investigate.
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Paruchuri, Pollock & Kumar 2020 (SMJ)

> "'Drama does not just walk into your life. Either you create it, invite it or associate with it.' — Unknown. When you read about a negative event occurring, how widely do you spread the blame? For example, if you read that a pledge suffered a serious injury during a fraternity hazing incident, would that affect your view of just that particular fraternity chapter, the whole fraternity system at the school, the University in its entirety, or the fraternity system writ large?"

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | SMJ, ASQ 可用；AMJ/OS 需确保格言与理论高度相关 |
| **理论类型** | 认知心理学/归因理论/声誉与分类理论——任何涉及"人类如何将事件归类并扩散判断"的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 日常类比必须精准映射到核心理论机制（分类距离、认知可得性、归因范围） |
| **风险** | 格言如果来源为 "Unknown" 会削弱权威感；日常类比如果过于牵强会被视为 forced analogy |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 格言装饰化 | 引语与后文理论机制无关，仅为装饰 | 格言的主题词必须在 P2 被理论化回收 |
| 类比场景与目标现象不匹配 | 校园欺凌类比用于研究供应链中断 | 类比的内在逻辑结构必须与目标现象同构 |
| 反问后无回收 | 反问句建立的 reader engagement 在后续段落彻底消失 | 在 Discussion 中回扣开篇的问题 |
