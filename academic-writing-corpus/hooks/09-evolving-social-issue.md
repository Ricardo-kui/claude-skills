# Evolving Social Issue Hook

## 功能定义
以一个正在演变中的社会热点事件（如社交媒体审查、平台言论政策）作为 Hook，展示某种社会规范或组织实践正处于剧烈变动中——过去的共识正在瓦解，新的张力正在形成——从而建立研究的历史紧迫性。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（事件 + 政策趋势型）**：
```
In [year], [organization] [took action against] [actor] for [behavior], despite [countervailing context]. Although [organization] is located in [place], the incident went viral and [social consequence]. [Broader trend: Organizations increasingly implement policy X] that [effect]. Yet [theoretical puzzle: how does the same practice affect systematically different groups?]
```

**模板 B（规范演变 + 立场反转型）**：
```
Traditionally, [Group A] supported [position X] and [Group B] supported [position Y]. However, in recent years, [Group A] have grown wary of [negative aspect of X], while [Group B] now embrace [positive aspect of Y]. This inversion of traditional positions creates an organizational dilemma: [tension between competing values].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Employee Free Speech vs. Organizational Censorship 2024 (OS)

> "In [year], [organization] fired [employee] for [social media post]. Although [organization] is located in [location], the post went viral and [reputational consequence]. [Organizations] increasingly implement [policy type] that tend to [effect]. But how does the same organizational practice affect [outcome] when employees systematically differ in [ideological dimension]?"

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | Organization Science 极度适合；AMJ 可用；ASQ 定性论文也可用 |
| **理论类型** | 价值观冲突/制度逻辑/心理契约——任何涉及多元价值观碰撞和组织政策差异化效应的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 社会议题确实在演变中（有历史对比数据或明确的两极分化迹象） |
| **风险** | 社会议题如果过于党派化（纯美国政治话题），国际 reviewer 可能认为缺乏普遍性 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 事件孤证 | 只举一个事件就声称这是"趋势" | 必须搭配至少一家其他公司或数据趋势证明现象是系统性的 |
| 只讲事不讲理论 | Hook 停留在社会新闻层面 | 必须在 P2-P3 完成从新闻事件到组织理论概念的转译 |
| 单边论证 | 只分析一个群体的逻辑而忽略对立群体 | 如果现象的本质是差异化效应，Hook 必须对称呈现两方立场 |
