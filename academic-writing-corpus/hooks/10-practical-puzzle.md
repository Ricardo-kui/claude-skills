# Practical Puzzle / Practitioner Dilemma Hook

## 功能定义
从一个真实的管理两难问题出发，让从业者感到"这正是我的困惑"，同时让学者感到"这是一个未被理论化的有趣问题"。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（直接提问式）**：
```
[Practitioner group] face a difficult choice: [Option A] or [Option B]?
[Second sentence: why both options have compelling rationales].
[Third sentence: conventional wisdom recommends [one option], but [counter-evidence / changing conditions] suggest this advice may be [outdated / incomplete / context-dependent].
[Fourth sentence: convert to theoretical question].
```

**模板 B（行业困惑式）**：
```
In [industry / practice domain], [widespread behavior] has [produced mixed results / created unintended consequences].
[Second sentence: practitioners are increasingly unsure whether [conventional practice] still applies].
[Third sentence: yet academic guidance is [scant / contradictory / based on outdated assumptions]].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Political ties（来自 memory mapping）

> "Should practitioners follow conventional wisdom by pursuing political ties?"

**改写模板**：> "[Practitioners / Managers / Policymakers] face a difficult choice: [pursue Strategy A] or [avoid Strategy A]? On one hand, [rationale for pursuing]. On the other hand, [rationale for avoiding]. Conventional wisdom recommends [one option], but [counter-evidence] suggests this advice may be [outdated / incomplete]. We argue that the answer depends on [theoretical contingency]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | AMJ, SMJ, JOM — 强调 managerial relevance 的期刊 |
| **理论类型** | **战略选择 / 制度创业 / 组织变革** — 任何存在"做还是不做"两难的话题 |
| **前提条件** | 两难必须是真实的行业困惑；不能是人为制造的问题 |
| **风险** | 如果问题太具体，会被质疑理论贡献 |

## 关键技巧：从业者的语言回响

最有效的 practical puzzle hook 会在论文后文（尤其是 Discussion）中**回应**开头提出的 practitioner question。

**闭环结构**：
- Hook: "Should managers do X?"
- Discussion: "Our findings suggest that managers should do X when [condition], but avoid X when [condition]"

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 问题过于简单 | "企业应该创新吗？" — 答案显而易见 | 问题必须是有争议的，双方都有强论据 |
| 只有问题无理论 | "这是一个重要实践问题" | 必须立即转化为理论缺口："This dilemma reflects a broader theoretical tension between [Theory A] and [Theory B]" |
