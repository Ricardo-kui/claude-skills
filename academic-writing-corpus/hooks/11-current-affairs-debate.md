# Current Affairs / Social Debate Hook

## 功能定义
以一个同时存在于学术文献和公共舆论中的争议性共识作为开场——"大家都认为 X 成立"——然后用不可辩驳的反例（具体人物+公司+数据）挑战该共识，迫使读者重新审视一个"已被接受的答案"并思考情境条件。

## 句法模板

```
Popular press and scholarly articles are replete with the "[near-universal assumption]" that [Group A] are more [trait] than [Group B] ([citations]). Although the theoretical mechanisms proposed differ, the collective findings suggest [consensus]. However, we argue that conceptualizing [Group A] as universally [trait] may paint too simplistic a picture. As [Example 1], [specific case with data: name, company, numbers]. Others have made larger [examples 2 and 3]. These examples raise the question of [research question about contingency].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Gamache et al. 2023 (SMJ)

> "Popular press and scholarly articles are replete with the 'near universal assumption that [women] are more [risk averse] than [men].' Although the theoretical mechanisms proposed differ, the collective findings suggest [consensus]. However, we argue that conceptualizing [women] as universally [conservative] may paint too simplistic a picture. As CEO of [Company 1], [Name 1] acquired [number] companies. Others have made larger acquisitions, such as [Name 2]'s purchase of [Target 2] for $[amount] and [Name 3]'s $[amount] acquisition of [Target 3]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | SMJ 极度适合；AMJ 也可用；ASQ 需理论化更深 |
| **理论类型** | Upper Echelons/性别与多样性/调节焦点理论——任何挑战"主效应"并提出情境调节的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 被挑战的共识必须是真实的（有元分析或多个独立研究支持）；反例必须有具体数据和名字 |
| **风险** | 如果反例只是特例（n=3），reviewer 会质疑其代表性；共识不能被漫画化为愚蠢观点 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 共识漫画化 | "大家愚蠢地认为女性都保守"——实际上文献 nuanced | 承认三种不同的理论机制均指向同一结论后再挑战 |
| 反例无数据 | "有些女性 CEO 也做并购"缺乏说服力 | 必须给出具体金额（$25 billion）和具体公司名 |
| 挑战共识后无新理论 | 只说"这太简化了"但不提供替代解释 | 必须立即引入能够解释差异的理论 |
| 只讲反例不讲边界条件 | 提出"有时候女性也激进"但不说明何时 | 核心贡献必须在 Introduction 中预告边界条件 |
