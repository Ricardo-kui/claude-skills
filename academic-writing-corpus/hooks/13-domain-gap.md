# Domain Gap / "Surprising Lack of Research" Hook

## 功能定义
在一个重要现象已经被广泛认可的前提下，指出某个特定学科/领域对该现象的研究严重不足，从而建立跨领域转移的合法性。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（现象重要性 + 研究分布不均）**：
```
[Phenomenon X] is a [central/critical/pervasive] feature of [domain/industry/economic activity].
[Second sentence: quantify its importance with a striking proportion].
Yet little [research/theory/evidence] investigates [its effect on / its antecedents in / its implications for] [target domain].
[Third sentence: this gap is consequential because...]
```

**模板 B（学科间不对称）**：
```
Scholars in [Field A] have devoted considerable attention to [phenomenon X], establishing that [core findings].
[Field B], by contrast, has largely ignored [phenomenon X], even though it [plays a central role / has significant consequences] in [B's domain].
[Third sentence: bridging these conversations promises to...]
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：From Finance to Marketing: The Impact of Financial Leverage on Customer Satisfaction

> "Debt financing constitutes approximately 80% of firms' external financing, yet little research investigates the effects of debt on marketing strategy and customer outcomes."

**改写模板**：
> "[Phenomenon X] constitutes approximately [percentage]% of [relevant aggregate], yet little research investigates [its effects on / its relationship with] [target outcome/domain]. This gap is consequential because [theoretical reason] and [practical reason]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 适合跨学科期刊或希望引入新视角的旗舰期刊（如 SMJ 引入 finance 概念到 strategy） |
| **理论类型** | **跨领域理论迁移** — 将成熟领域的概念引入新领域 |
| **前提条件** | 源领域必须有成熟研究；目标领域确实忽视了这个概念；迁移必须有理论逻辑，不能是"因为没人做" |
| **风险** | 容易被 reviewer 质疑"为什么这个领域需要关心那个领域的概念" |

## 关键技巧：数字的杠杆

"80% of external financing" 这个数字的功能：
1. 证明 phenomenon 的**规模重要性**（不是边缘现象）
2. 与研究忽视形成**强烈反差**（这么重要却没人研究）
3. 为后文的**理论引入**创造紧迫感

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| "没人研究"作为唯一理由 | 这是 weakest 的 gap 声明 | 必须解释为什么这个缺口导致了**理论扭曲**或**实践误判** |
| 领域间迁移生硬 | "Finance 研究很多杠杆，所以我们研究营销" | 必须建立**概念亲和性**：杠杆如何影响营销资源的配置？ |
