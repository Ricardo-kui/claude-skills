# Cost-Benefit Tension Hook

## 功能定义
通过精确呈现一个决策情境中双方对立的成本结构，制造"两边都是深渊"的决策困境，使"时机/选择/策略"成为不可避免的研究问题。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（双面对称 + 案例锚定）**：
```
[Action A] is costly; [specific direct costs] and [specific indirect costs] make it a [devastating/threatening] prospect for firms.
[Second sentence: compelling reason to delay or avoid].
However, delaying [Action A] may lead to even higher costs through [fine mechanism], [liability mechanism], and most importantly, [reputational mechanism] ([citation]).
[Specific case with dollar figure].
[Second case with even larger dollar figure].
Therefore, although [Action A] are adverse events in general, a quick response may attenuate the damage ([citation]).
```

**模板 B（组织行为约束 + 制度惩罚）**：
```
Firms face a dilemma when [external event occurs].
On one hand, [reason to delay].
On the other hand, [reason to act quickly].
[Institutional evidence of variation].
This tension raises important questions for [audience].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early? — Eilert et al., JM

> "Recalls are costly; announcing and implementing one is associated with both direct costs in repair, restitution, or liability and indirect costs such as losses in reputation and market value. Consequently, recalls could have a devastating impact on a firm's performance, sometimes even threatening its survival. Thus, a firm has reasons to avoid a quick recall and instead wait for the investigation to conclude. However, delaying a product recall may lead to higher direct and indirect costs through fines, liability damages, and most importantly, diminished reputation. In 2012, Toyota was fined $17.35 million dollars for delaying a floor mat recall. The U.S. Department of Justice fined General Motors $900 million for willfully delaying the recall for a faulty ignition switch..."

**改写模板**：
> "[Actions] are costly; [announcing/implementing/undertaking] one is associated with both direct costs in [specific cost categories] and indirect costs such as losses in [reputational/asset categories]. Consequently, [actions] could have a devastating impact on a [firm's/organization's] performance, sometimes even threatening its survival. Thus, [actor] has reasons to avoid a quick [response] and instead wait for [process] to conclude. However, delaying [action] may lead to higher direct and indirect costs through [penalty 1], [penalty 2], and most importantly, [most salient penalty]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | JM, JOM, Marketing Science, 任何涉及组织决策的期刊 |
| **理论类型** | **行为理论/有限理性/制度理论** — 适合解释为什么"理性"决策者在明确成本结构下仍然做出"次优"选择 |
| **前提条件** | 双方成本都必须有文献或案例支撑；至少有一个具体案例带有精确美元数字 |
| **风险** | 如果成本结构不对称（一方明显更大），则 tension 感会流失 |

## 关键技巧：精确数字的递进

Notice how the Recall paper escalates:
1. 先抽象陈述"recalls are costly"（无数字）
2. 再具体化"direct costs in repair, restitution, or liability"（类别）
3. 案例 1：Toyota $17.35m（具体但相对小）
4. 案例 2：GM $900m（数量级跳跃，制造震撼）

**数字递进公式**：抽象 → 类别 → 小案例 → 大案例（10x-100x 跳跃）

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 只有一面 | 只讲召回成本，不讲延迟成本 | 必须对称呈现，让读者感受到"无论选哪边都疼" |
| 数字无来源 | "罚款可能高达数百万" | 给出精确数字和权威来源（SEC, DOJ, 公司财报） |
| 停留在案例 | 讲完 Toyota 和 GM 就结束了 | 案例必须转化为普遍问题："The considerable variation in time taken by firms to recall... raises important questions" |
