# Forward-Looking Perspective Shift Hook

## 功能定义
从传统的研究视角（supply-side / production-side / firm-centric）出发，承认其贡献后指出，新兴的市场/技术条件要求研究进行一次"前瞻性视角转移"——从旧透镜转向一个能解释新动态的替代视角——从而建立理论创新的合法性。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（视角转移型：supply-side → demand-side）**：
```
Traditional approaches to [phenomenon] have focused on [conventional lens — e.g., what firms produce / how firms organize internally]. While this lens has yielded important insights, the [new condition — e.g., proliferation of heterogeneous offerings in digital markets] creates a fundamentally different challenge: [specific new puzzle]. We therefore conceptualize [phenomenon] not as [old definition], but as [new definition — e.g., positioning in a functional space defined by the set of functions a product performs for customers].
```

**模板 B（机制转移型：内部因素 → 外部反馈）**：
```
In principle, [actors] would choose [ideal strategy] based on [internal factors]. In practice, they often face substantial uncertainty about [external factor]. Existing approaches may be insufficient in this context. Hence, we propose that [actors] learn about [environment] by observing [external cue] and use this [cue type] to inform their [decision].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Zhao & Ding 2022 (OS)

> "In principle, entrants would choose functional combinations that closely align with the target customers' needs and preferences. In practice, they often face substantial demand uncertainty about the functional attributes customers are likely to value... Hence, we propose that entrants in digital markets learn about the demand environment by observing customers' evaluations of other products and use this external market feedback to inform their initial market positions."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | OS, SMJ 极度适合；AMJ 理论型也可用 |
| **理论类型** | 市场进入/定位理论/组织学习——任何涉及从传统视角转向一个新的解释透镜的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 旧视角确实有价值（不能 strawman）；新视角必须能解释旧视角无法解释的新现象 |
| **风险** | 如果新视角只是"加了另一个变量"而非真正的视角转移，缺乏理论冲击力 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 旧视角 strawman | "过去的学者愚蠢地只看 supply side" | 用 "In principle... In practice..." 认可旧视角的合理性，再展示其边界 |
| 视角转移无实际后果 | 换了视角但预测的东西完全相同 | 新视角必须导致至少一个不同于旧视角的实证预测 |
| 只谈视角不谈机制 | "我们采取需求侧视角"但没有具体的学习/反馈机制 | 新视角必须有可操作的机制 |
| 视角过于宏观 | "我们需要从 ecosystem 视角看" | 必须将宏观视角操作化为具体的理论 construct 和可观测的变量 |
