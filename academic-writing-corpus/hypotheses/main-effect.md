# Main Effect Hypothesis

## 功能定义
陈述自变量对因变量的直接主效应——不引入调节或中介，直接建立核心预测关系。这是理论论证的基石假设。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（直接因果型）**：
```
[Theoretical argument building on citations].
Thus we predict:
Hypothesis 1: [X] has a [positive/negative] effect on [Y].
```

**模板 B（机制推导型）**：
```
[Theoretical mechanism]. Thus, [X] should [increase/decrease] [Y].
Formally:
Hypothesis 1 ([Abbreviation]): [X] [increases/decreases] [Y].
```

**模板 C（对比预测型）**：
```
[Theoretical argument for positive direction].
[Counterargument for negative direction].
Because [theoretical reason why positive dominates], we predict:
Hypothesis 1: [X] is [positively/negatively] associated with [Y].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Activism Risk and Corporate Self-Regulation (ASQ)

> "Following the enactment of anti-SLAPP laws, institutional CSP increases."
> **Hypothesis 1.** Following the enactment of anti-SLAPP laws, institutional CSP increases.

**来源**：State Ownership and Firm Innovation (ASQ)

> "We propose that state ownership should enable firms to gain more resources to invest in R&D activities... Thus we predict:"
> **Hypothesis 1a (H1a):** State ownership of a firm has a positive effect on its R&D input.

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early? (JM)

> "Overall, when the firm is investigated for severe defects, the process may take longer and delay the recall decision. We offer the following baseline hypothesis:"
> **H₁:** Problem severity increases time to recall.

**来源**：The Push and Pull of Attaining CEO Celebrity (AMJ)

> "Thus, we argue:"
> **Hypothesis 1.** A firm's strategic nonconformity (relative to its industry) will be positively associated with its CEO's degree of celebrity attainment, specifically B-list attainment and, beyond that, A-list attainment.

**改写模板**：
> "[Theoretical mechanism]. Thus, [X] should [increase/decrease] [Y].
> **Hypothesis 1:** [X] has a [positive/negative] effect on [Y]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型——所有期刊都需要主效应假设 |
| **理论类型** | 直接效应、基础关系、行为预测 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有明确的理论机制支撑，不能只是描述性预测 |
| **风险** | 若主效应方向存在理论争议，必须用模板 C 展示为什么选择某一方向 |

## 关键技巧

主效应假设的清晰度决定论文的理论根基：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "We predict X affects Y" | "Because [mechanism], X should increase Y" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 无机制的主效应 | "H1: X positively affects Y"（没有为什么） | 必须在假设之前给出至少一段理论论证 |
| 方向模糊 | "H1: X is related to Y" | 必须明确正负方向，除非有竞争假设 |
| 假设过宽 | "H1: X affects Y and Z" | 一个假设只预测一个关系；多个 DV 应拆分为 H1a, H1b |
