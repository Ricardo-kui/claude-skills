# Synthesized Coherence Literature Strategy

## 功能定义

承认文献中存在多个相关但视角不同的研究传统，展示每个传统的贡献，然后揭示这些传统在交汇处的集体盲区——即没有任何单一传统能够单独解释的理论空间。与 Progressive Coherence（从共识逐步聚焦到缺口）不同，Synthesized Coherence 的核心动作是"并置 + 合成"；与 Non-Coherence（建立对立然后颠覆）不同，它承认多方观点的合理性而非否定它们。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（多传统并置型）**：
```
Research in [Tradition A] has established that [finding A] (e.g., [citations]).
Complementarily, [Tradition B] suggests that [finding B] (e.g., [citations]).
Together, these perspectives imply that [synthetic insight].
Surprisingly, however, neither tradition has examined [specific gap at the intersection].
```

**模板 B（理论互补 + 盲区型）**：
```
[Theory A] explains [mechanism A] but leaves [phenomenon B] underspecified.
Conversely, [Theory B] accounts for [phenomenon B] but assumes [condition that Theory A challenges].
By integrating these lenses, we can ask a question that neither alone can address: [new question].
```

**模板 C（共识承认 + 维度遗漏型）**：
```
Previous research acknowledges that [broad consensus] (e.g., [citations]).
[Specific sub-stream] has examined [aspect 1], while [another sub-stream] has focused on [aspect 2].
Yet no studies have examined [the parallel effects / the intersection / the trade-off] for [outcome dimension].
Building on [Theory C], we argue that [mechanism connecting the streams].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源 1**：Mannor et al. 2016 (SMJ)

> "Behavioral theory suggests that job-induced anxiety can harm firm performance by distracting executives from their responsibilities and reducing their willingness to take the risks necessary to improve firm outcomes. Prospect theory, on the other hand, implies that anxiety may not always be detrimental, as it can motivate executives to work harder to avoid poor performance. These perspectives suggest that anxiety may have fundamentally different effects depending on the context in which it is experienced."

> "Upper echelons theory further suggests that executive psychological states influence strategic choices. Yet prior research has not integrated these perspectives to examine how job anxiety affects strategic decision making under different performance conditions."

**来源 2**：Vadakkepatt et al. 2022 (JM)

> "Previous research acknowledges that lobbying positively influences firm value, but no studies have examined the parallel effects for customers. Building on the attention-based view (ABV) of the firm, the authors examine these customer effects."

> "Regulatory capture theory alludes that lobbying may adversely affect customer outcomes, but no research in marketing directly examines this relationship. Moreover, extant theory does not explain why a focus on the regulatory environment reduces the firm's customer focus."

**改写模板**：
> "[Tradition A] has established that [finding A] (e.g., [citations]). [Tradition B], in contrast, suggests that [finding B] (e.g., [citations]). Together, these perspectives imply that [synthetic insight], yet neither tradition has examined [gap at the intersection]. By integrating [Theory C], we seek to address this gap."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | SMJ, AMJ, JM, JMR — 欢迎跨理论整合的期刊 |
| **Gap 类型** | **Inadequacy**（中等能量）— 最适合存在竞争性解释或互补性理论的文献 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有两个以上真正不同的研究传统，且每个都有实质性贡献；不能为了"合成"而强行制造虚假二元对立 |
| **最佳位置** | P2-P3 的文献综述核心段；在 Hook 之后，Gap 之前 |

## 关键技巧：合成的"升级"动作

Synthesized Coherence 的修辞力量不在于"文献很多"，而在于展示"文献各自正确但 collectively blind"。最有效的升级动作是：

| 步骤 | 动作 | 例句（来自 Vadakkepatt 2022） |
|------|------|------------------------------|
| 1. 承认 A | 确认 Tradition A 的贡献 | "Previous research acknowledges that lobbying positively influences firm value" |
| 2. 承认 B | 确认 Tradition B 的洞察 | "Regulatory capture theory alludes that lobbying may adversely affect customer outcomes" |
| 3. 展示盲区 | 指出没有 tradition 看交汇点 | "but no studies have examined the parallel effects for customers" |
| 4. 引入新理论 | 用新理论解释盲区为何存在 | "Building on the attention-based view... firms have limited attention available to devote to distinct strategic priorities" |
| 5. 重新定义问题 | 把 gap 从"遗漏"升级为"理论必然" | "We extend the ABV to reveal that otherwise-beneficial firm actions (lobbying) can simultaneously harm customer outcomes" |

**与 Progressive Coherence 的区分**：

| Progressive Coherence | Synthesized Coherence |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| "已有共识 → 逐步聚焦 → 发现缺口" | "多个传统 → 各自合理 → 交汇处盲区" |
| 适合单一理论传统丰富的领域 | 适合存在多个相关但分离的理论传统的领域 |
| 能量：低到中 | 能量：中 |
| 典型句："Despite progress... remains unaddressed" | 典型句："Together, these perspectives imply... yet neither has examined" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 虚假合成 | 两个"传统"实际上是同一流派的细微变体 | 确保两个传统在理论假设、分析层次或核心机制上有实质性差异 |
| 承认无度 | 花了 80% 篇幅综述文献，gap 只有一句 | 每个传统的综述控制在 2-3 句，留出空间给"合成盲区" |
| 合成无理论 | "A 研究了 X，B 研究了 Y，所以我研究 X+Y" | 必须有理论解释为什么 X 和 Y 的交集会产生新的理论问题 |
| 否定式合成 | "A 错了，B 也错了，我对了" | 这是 Non-Coherence，不是 Synthesized Coherence；合成策略承认各方的合理性 |

## 验证状态

- **跨论文复现**: VERIFIED（2+ distinct papers: Mannor et al. 2016 SMJ, Vadakkepatt et al. 2022 JM; 类似结构见于 From Finance to Marketing JM, On the tip of the brain SMJ）
- **来源论文**: Mannor et al. 2016 (SMJ) × 1; Vadakkepatt et al. 2022 (JM) × 1
- **生成力**: GENERATIVE — 可迁移到任何存在多个相关理论传统但缺乏整合的研究领域
- **排他性**: MEDIUM — 最常见于 Inadequacy 型 Gap；在 Incompleteness 中也可用但能量稍弱
- **期刊限制**: 无限制；特别适合鼓励理论整合的期刊（AMJ, SMJ, OS, JM）
- **收录状态**: ✓ STANDARD
