# Quantified Economic Loss Stakes

## 功能定义
将抽象的研究问题转化为精确的、可感知的经济损失（或收益），使读者（尤其是审稿人和编辑）立即感受到研究的政策 relevant 性和实践紧迫性。数字必须具体到能引发"这太多了"或"这值得研究"的反应。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（直接损失型）**：
```
[Shocking dollar amount] — this is the [estimated / reported / documented] [cost / loss / waste] associated with [phenomenon] in [scope / industry / time period].
[Second sentence: translate the abstract number into a concrete, imaginable scale].
[Third sentence: why this loss persists despite awareness].
```

**模板 B（递进损失型）**：
```
At the [micro / firm] level, [phenomenon] costs an average of $[X] per [unit].
At the [macro / industry] level, this translates to $[Y billion] annually.
[Third sentence: what this scale implies for the urgency of research].
```

**模板 C（比较损失型）**：
```
The [penalty / loss / cost] for [Action A] was $[X million].
In comparison, the [penalty / loss / cost] for [Action B] — the same offense under different conditions — was $[Y million], [orders of magnitude difference].
[Third sentence: this disparity reveals [theoretical mechanism] at work].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：CEO Regulatory Focus

> "...it is estimated to have cost U.S. companies $79 billion in foregone earnings."

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early?

> "In 2012, Toyota was fined $17.35 million dollars for delaying a floor mat recall. The U.S. Department of Justice fined General Motors $900 million for willfully delaying the recall..."

**来源**：Recall 论文（Discussion / Table 11）

> "...the corresponding losses in shareholder wealth... are $112 million..."
> "The difference in shareholder losses for lower- and higher-reliability brands because of recall timing is $84 million."

**改写模板**：> "[Phenomenon] is estimated to have cost [relevant population] $[X billion] in [foregone earnings / lost productivity / preventable harm] ([authoritative source]). To put this figure in perspective, [concrete comparison: 'this exceeds the annual GDP of [country]' or 'this represents [percentage]% of [industry] revenue']. Despite the magnitude of these losses, [theoretical gap / lack of understanding persists]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | AMJ, SMJ, JOM, JM, IJRM — 任何强调 managerial relevance 的期刊 |
| **理论类型** | 通用型，尤其适合 governance, marketing strategy, operations |
| **数字来源** | 咨询公司（McKinsey, BCG）、政府机构（GAO, DOJ）、权威媒体（WSJ）、公司财报 |
| **风险** | 数字过时或来源不可靠会立即损害 credibility |

## 关键技巧：数字的"具象化翻译"

最有效的经济损失不是单纯给出数字，而是**翻译**成读者能想象的尺度：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "costs $79 billion" | "costs U.S. companies $79 billion in foregone earnings — equivalent to [X]% of annual R&D spending" |
| "fined $17.35 million" | "fined $17.35 million — the maximum civil penalty allowed under [law] at the time" |
| "shareholder loss of $168 million" | "shareholder loss of $168 million for an average recall, roughly the cost of [concrete reference]" |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 数字无来源 | "Studies show this costs billions" | 必须给出来源或说明估算方法 |
| 数字无理论功能 | 数字 shock 后没有连接到研究缺口 | "These losses persist because [theoretical mechanism is poorly understood]" |
| 数字通货膨胀 | 为了震撼而使用最大估计值 | 使用保守估计更有 credibility；如果要震撼，可以说"even conservative estimates suggest..." |
