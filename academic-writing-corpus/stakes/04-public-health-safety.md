# Public Health and Consumer Safety Stakes

## 功能定义

将研究问题连接到对公众健康、消费者安全或关键利益相关者的可感知伤害，使研究获得伦理 relevant 性和政策紧迫性。这种 stakes 特别适合涉及产品安全、质量失败、公共卫生、环境风险和监管响应的研究领域。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（具体伤害型）**：
```
[Shocking human cost] — [number] [injuries / deaths / illnesses] are believed to have occurred [prior to / as a result of] [phenomenon].
[Second sentence: describe the vulnerable population and their dependence on the product/service].
[Third sentence: why this harm persists despite awareness or regulation].
```

**模板 B（风险递进型）**：
```
[At the individual level], [phenomenon] subjects [vulnerable population] to [extended / prolonged] [safety / health / reliability] risks.
[At the societal level], delays in [action] increase the likelihood that [escalating harm] will occur.
[Third sentence: the scale of exposure — how many people or communities are affected].
```

**模板 C（监管-现实差距型）**：
```
Although [regulatory body] establishes guidelines for [action], they rely upon [actors] to actually initiate [response].
[Second sentence: the gap between regulation and reality — delays of weeks, months, or even years].
[Third sentence: the human cost of this implementation gap].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Darby et al. 2023 (MSOM)

> "Fifteen injuries and one death are believed to have occurred prior to recall initiation."

> "Unfortunately, despite the potential human costs, such examples of recall delays are all too common in the medical device industry. Patients, physicians, and regulators are left wondering why this is and what can be done to shift this paradigm."

> "Understanding the effects of CEO stock ownership on recall timing is thus crucial because delays put public safety at prolonged risk, especially for medical devices with dangerous defects."

> "Class I and Class II recalls involve severe defects; they can cause temporary illness, serious injury, or death. An example of a Class I recall, which can lead to death, is a defective heart rate monitor used in a surgical ward that may inaccurately signal cardiac arrest."

**改写模板**：
> "[Phenomenon] is associated with significant [human / public health / consumer safety] risks. [Specific figure: number of injuries/deaths/exposure incidents] are believed to have occurred [as a result of / prior to] [event]. These risks are particularly acute for [vulnerable population], who depend upon [product/service] for [critical need]. Despite [regulatory frameworks / industry awareness], such [delays / failures / exposures] remain all too common, suggesting that [theoretical gap / organizational mechanism] requires deeper understanding."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | MSOM, JOM, POM, JM, JBR, OS — 涉及产品质量、安全、供应链、运营、制度的期刊 |
| **理论类型** | **组织失败 / 质量治理 / 监管响应 / 制度理论 / 利益相关者理论** — 任何涉及组织行为与安全后果的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有真实的或可信的伤害证据；不能夸大或渲染；伤害与研究问题之间需有清晰逻辑链 |
| **风险** | 如果伤害数字来源不可靠或只是推测，会被质疑危言耸听或操纵情感 |

## 关键技巧：脆弱性的具象化

最有效的公共安全 stakes 不是笼统地说"影响消费者"，而是**让脆弱人群变得具体可感**：

| 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|--------|--------|
| "delays harm patients" | "subjecting heart patients to extended product safety and reliability risks" |
| "recalls affect consumers" | "individuals who depend upon the ICD for life-sustaining therapy" |
| "defective products are dangerous" | "a defective heart rate monitor used in a surgical ward that may inaccurately signal cardiac arrest" |

**双重 Stakes 策略**：
公共安全 stakes 可以与组织/财务 stakes 同时出现，形成双重紧迫性：
> "Recalling too slowly can put patients at prolonged risk and threaten the firm's reputation, whereas recalling too quickly can result in excessive costs or errors."

**严重等级分层**：
当研究领域存在梯度差异时，用分类框架（如 FDA Class I/II/III）来展示伤害的层次性，既增加专业性又避免过度概括：
> "Class I and Class II recalls involve severe defects; they can cause temporary illness, serious injury, or death. In contrast, Class III recalls involve low-severity defects... They do not pose a health risk, but nonetheless violate FDA regulations."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 伤害无来源 | "Many people are affected by this" | 给出具体数字或引用权威来源（FDA, WHO, CDC） |
| 伤害与研究脱节 | 描述了伤亡但没说研究如何减少伤害 | 必须连接到理论缺口："These losses persist because [mechanism] is poorly understood" |
| 过度情感渲染 | "Innocent victims suffer terribly" | 保持分析性距离，用"subjected to prolonged risks"或"exposed to safety hazards" |
| 单一维度 stakes | 只谈公共安全，忽略组织/财务维度 | 产品召回类论文适合展示"公共安全 vs 组织利益"的张力 |

## 变体：品牌声誉与合法性危机 Stakes

当研究主题涉及公众信任、媒体关注或制度压力时，可将模板 A 中的"人身伤害"替换为"声誉损害"：

> "The announcement of a recall can directly impact the firm's reputation, so the temptation to delay bad news may grow as the firm faces greater media scrutiny."

> "Reputationally, delays in recall initiation will likely garner additional media attention, as delays signal to investors that the firm may not prioritize public health and safety."

## 验证状态

- **跨论文复现**: SINGLE-INSTANCE（目前仅 Darby et al. 2023 一个明确完整案例；Hora et al. 2011, Desai 2011 有片段性出现）
- **来源论文**: Darby et al. 2023 (MSOM) × 1; Hora, Bapuji, & Roth 2011 (JOM) 提及公共安全 stakes × 1; Desai 2011 (AMJ) 涉及合法性危机 stakes × 1
- **生成力**: ADAPTABLE — 可迁移到食品安全、药品安全、环境泄漏、建筑安全等领域，但需替换具体领域术语和监管机构名称
- **排他性**: MEDIUM — 最常见于产品安全、公共卫生、CSR、治理领域；也可用于一般性制度研究
- **期刊限制**: MSOM, JOM, POM, JM 最适合；需要 reviewer 认可"公共安全"作为合法的学术 stakes（而非单纯的情感诉求）
- **收录状态**: 🔬 EXPERIMENTAL
