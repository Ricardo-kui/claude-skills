# Reputation, Legitimacy, and Field-Level Crisis Stakes

## 功能定义

将研究问题连接到组织声誉、制度合法性或场域层面的危机传导后果，使研究获得制度理论和利益相关者理论层面的理论紧迫性。这种 stakes 特别适合涉及合法性威胁、声誉溢出、场域防御、媒体危机和制度变革的研究领域。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（场域合法性传染型）**：
```
[Disruptions] can impair legitimacy throughout an organizational field, as scrutiny diffuses broadly following crises, potentially affecting organizations far from the initial events.
[Second sentence: concrete spillover mechanisms — resource access, operational uncertainty, survival threats].
[Third sentence: the gap in knowledge about field-level responses].
```

**模板 B（声誉-资本市场反应型）**：
```
[Events] can have adverse effects on how a firm is perceived, including [specific capital market consequences: reduced analyst coverage, sharp increases in cost of capital, vigorous trading indicating reevaluation].
[Second sentence: how intangible assets amplify or buffer these effects].
[Third sentence: the theoretical cost of conflating distinct constructs].
```

**模板 C（制度防御悖论型）**：
```
Although [theory] recognizes that [disruptions] can lead to significant change, examples exist of [domains] that fail to reorient, transform, or substantially improve following crises.
[Second sentence: the lack of a formal theoretical framework for understanding [defensive actions / status quo preservation]].
[Third sentence: the risks of both action and inaction].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源 1**：Desai 2011 (AMJ)

> "Contagion of judgment can impair other organizations' access to resources, increase operational uncertainty, and ultimately threaten the survival of organizations that were not directly involved in the disruptions."

> "Organizations in these situations have strong incentives to protect their fields to maintain stability and preserve access to resources. However, defensive efforts by organizations not directly involved in crises are risky, and it is important to determine whether firms engage in these efforts."

> "Although institutional theory recognizes that fieldwide disruptions can lead to significant change, examples exist of industries that fail to reorient, transform, or substantially improve following crises. These anecdotal examples highlight the lack of a formal theoretical framework for understanding what actions organizations may pursue to preserve the status quo following disruptions in their field."

**来源 2**：Pfarrer et al. 2010 (AMJ)

> "Material earnings surprises, both positive and negative, can have adverse effects on how a firm is perceived. For example, material earnings surprises have been associated with reduced analyst coverage and stock ownership for a firm, sharp increases in its cost of capital, and more vigorous trading in its stock, indicating that investors are reevaluating their perceptions of the firm."

> "Both high-reputation and celebrity firms experience greater market rewards for positive surprises and smaller market penalties for negative surprises than other firms."

**改写模板**：
> "[Phenomenon] threatens not only the [focal organization] but also [spills over to] other organizations in the same [field / industry / category]. [Specific mechanism: contagion of judgment / blame attribution / similarity-based evaluation] can impair [unaffected actors]' access to [resources], increase [operational uncertainty], and ultimately threaten [survival / performance]. Yet the existing literature in [theory domain] provides little guidance regarding how these [other actors] respond when [an entire field's legitimacy / collective reputation] is threatened."

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | AMJ, ASQ, OS, SMJ — 制度理论、组织理论、战略期刊欢迎场域层面 stakes |
| **理论类型** | **制度理论 / 组织声誉 / 利益相关者理论 / 印象管理** — 任何涉及集体评价和制度压力的研究 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有理论依据证明危机或评价会跨越组织边界；不能假设溢出自动发生 |
| **最佳位置** | Gap 段末尾或独立 Stakes 段；在 Hook（案例/危机叙事）之后提供理论升级 |

## 关键技巧：多层次 stakes 叠加

最有效的场域合法性 stakes 不是只谈一个层次，而是展示从个体组织到场域的制度链条：

| 层次 | 弱表达 | 强表达 |
|------|--------|--------|
| 组织 | "firms suffer from crises" | "organizations that were not directly involved in the disruptions" |
| 场域 | "the industry is affected" | "legitimacy throughout an organizational field... as scrutiny diffuses broadly" |
| 机制 | "bad reputation spreads" | "contagion of judgment can impair other organizations' access to resources, increase operational uncertainty, and ultimately threaten survival" |
| 悖论 | "firms should defend themselves" | "defensive efforts may backfire, drawing more negative attention" |

**双重 Stakes 策略**（来自 Desai 2011）：
场域 stakes 可与组织行动悖论同时出现——既说明不行动的风险（资源丧失、合法性崩溃），也说明行动的风险（印象管理失败、引火上身）：
> "Defensive efforts may help organizations to repair a field's legitimacy and avoid the contagion of judgment following disruptions, but these efforts may instead backfire, drawing more negative attention to the defensive organizations."

**构念区分型 Stakes**（来自 Pfarrer 2010）：
当研究涉及构念辨析时，stakes 应强调混淆的理论成本：
> "Labels and definitions have proliferated, making it difficult to determine if different studies consider the same or different phenomena, leading to a fragmented body of work and limiting the development of theory that can explain and predict the effects of different intangible assets."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 溢出无机制 | "This affects everyone" | 指定溢出机制：相似性、媒体传染、制度同形 |
| 只有行动 stakes | 只谈不防御的损失 | 也要谈防御的风险（悖论型 stakes 更有理论张力） |
| 混淆构念无成本 | "These constructs are different" | 必须说明混淆后"什么重要的东西被隐藏了" |
| 场域stakes滥用 | 个体层面的研究强行拔高到场域 | 只有分析单位或理论机制确实跨越边界时才使用 |
