# "Despite Progress... Largely Unaddressed" Tension

## 功能定义
在承认前人工作价值的前提下，精准定位一个被遗漏的维度。这种表达既显示了作者对文献的尊重，又建立了缺口的存在——是"遗漏"而非"无知"。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（标准型）**：
```
Despite considerable progress in understanding [topic X] (e.g., [citation 1]; [citation 2]), [specific dimension Y] has remained largely [unaddressed / unexplored / undertheorized].
```

**模板 B（带后果型）**：
```
Scholars have made important advances in [area A], [area B], and [area C] (e.g., [citations]).
Yet despite this progress, [critical question / mechanism / boundary condition] remains [unresolved / poorly understood / theoretically underspecified].
```

**模板 C（时间累积型）**：
```
Over the past [decade/two decades], researchers have [established/documented/shown] that [main finding 1] and [main finding 2].
However, [specific gap] has received [surprisingly little / scant / insufficient] attention.
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Social Discrimination（来自 memory mapping）

> "Minority CEOs are increasing in number, but post-access discrimination remains largely understudied."

**来源**：Activism Risk（来自 memory mapping）

> "Prior research has focused on how firms respond to activist crises, but the everyday reality for most firms consists not of responding to such crises but, rather, avoiding and preventing them."

**改写模板**：
> "Despite considerable progress in understanding [established topic] (e.g., [key citation 1]; [key citation 2]), [specific gap] has remained largely [unaddressed / unexplored / undertheorized]. This omission matters because [theoretical or practical consequence]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型，几乎所有管理学期刊都接受 |
| **前提条件** | 必须先有值得承认的"progress"，不能是空白领域 |
| **最佳位置** | Introduction 的第二或第三段，紧接 hook 之后 |

## 关键技巧：让 "progress" 具体化

弱表达："Many scholars have studied X."
强表达："Researchers have established that [specific mechanism A] increases [outcome] ([citation]) and that [boundary condition B] attenuates this relationship ([citation])."

**规则**：列举 progress 时，用**具体发现**代替**作者罗列**。

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 空洞的 "considerable progress" | 没有具体说明 progress 是什么 | 至少给出 2-3 个具体发现或维度 |
| gap 过大 | "然而机制还不清楚" — 什么机制？ | gap 必须精确到可操作的维度："the moderating role of [specific factor]" |
| gap 过小 | "然而还没有人用中国数据检验" | 如果仅仅是新场景，必须论证为什么新场景能改变理论理解 |

## 变体表达

| 变体 | 语气 | 适用 |
|------|------|------|
| "largely unaddressed" | 中性偏温和 | 合作关系良好的 literature |
| "surprisingly understudied" | 带轻微惊讶 | 显然重要但被忽视的话题 |
| "theoretically underspecified" | 直指理论薄弱 | 理论驱动型期刊（ASQ, OS） |
| "remains poorly understood" | 强调知识状态 | 现象驱动型论文 |

## 验证状态
- **跨论文复现**: ✓ VERIFIED（4+ distinct papers across ASQ, SMJ, AMJ, OS）
- **来源论文**: Pollock et al. 2015 (ASQ) × 1; 独立变体见于 Activism risk (SMJ), Employee Free Speech (AMJ), Positioning in Digital Markets (OS), Those Closest Wield the Sharpest Knife (ASQ), How shareholder litigation risk (SMJ)
- **生成力**: GENERATIVE——该句型是所有管理学 Introduction 中最常见的渐进缺口表达，可适配任何已有文献基础的领域
- **排他性**: LOW——通用型 tension，适用于所有 Gap 类型，但最常见于 Incompleteness
- **期刊限制**: 无限制——所有期刊均接受
- **收录状态**: ✓ STANDARD
