# "Implicit Assumption Is Wrong" Tension

## 功能定义
揭示现有文献对一个构念的默认假定是单维的/未分化的，或不同研究传统在无意中混淆了本应区分的构念，从而暴露该假定失效后可能出现的全新理论空间。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（单维假定挑战型）**：
```
While important, much of the work on [topic] is general and considers [outcome] in a very broad sense.
Research has yet to seriously consider [specific question] — namely, [question].
This omission matters because if [theory] is correct, [actor]'s [characteristic] should shape not just whether [actor action], but which type of [outcome] [actor] pursues.
```

**模板 B（二分结构展开型）**：
```
[Theory] argues that [mechanism]. [Construct A] is associated with [orientation A].
In contrast, [Construct B] is associated with [orientation B].
Thus, [theory] is not simply about [broad yes/no question], but also about what types of specific [actions] [actor] will take.
```

**模板 C（构念混淆辨析型）**：
```
Much of this research has focused on establishing the general effects of [broad construct] on [outcome] and has given little consideration to whether the specific effects of different [sub-constructs] may vary.
Further, this research has often given different labels to the same types of [phenomenon] or has used the same observable proxies to operationalize conceptually distinct constructs (e.g., [citations] for critiques highlighting these issues).
As a result, labels and definitions have proliferated, making it difficult to determine if different studies consider the same or different phenomena, leading to a fragmented body of work and limiting the development of theory.
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源 1**：Gamache et al. 2020 (SMJ)

> "Research has yet to seriously consider how and why CEOs might pursue more specific stakeholder strategies reflecting unique priorities and goals. Thus, [regulatory focus theory] is not simply about which people will take action, but also about what types of specific actions people will take."

**来源 2**：Pfarrer et al. 2010 (AMJ)

> "Much of this research, however, has focused on establishing the general effects of possessing social approval assets on firm performance and has given little consideration to whether the specific effects of different assets may vary. Further, this research has often given different labels to the same types of collective perceptions or has used the same observable proxies to operationalize conceptually distinct constructs (see Deephouse and Carter [2005], Deephouse and Suchman [2008], and Rindova et al. [2005] for recent critiques highlighting these issues). As a result, labels and definitions have proliferated, making it difficult to determine if different studies consider the same or different phenomena, leading to a fragmented body of work and limiting the development of theory that can explain and predict the effects of different intangible assets."

> "'High reputation' refers to the accumulation of high levels of public recognition of the quality of a firm's capabilities and outputs... whereas 'celebrity' refers to 'a high level of public attention' focused on a firm combined with 'positive emotional responses from stakeholder audiences.' Reputation and celebrity are 'social facts' that constitute distinct interpretative frames that are likely to influence stakeholder behaviors in different ways."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | SMJ, OS — upper echelons/strategy 期刊欢迎构念辨析 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有理论依据证明"拆分"是理论驱动的区分，不是任意的 |
| **最佳位置** | 紧接文献综述之后，缺口段的核心句 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 假拆分 | 连续变量二分化，无理论理由 | 拆分必须由理论驱动 |
| 缺后果 | 只说了"以前太笼统"但没说拆分后学到什么 | 必须预告拆分后可以看到以前看不见的模式 |
| 拆分太细 | 七八个子类型，失去理论力量 | 2-3 个类型足够；二分对立最有理论优雅性 |
| 构念混淆无成本 | 指出"大家混淆了 X 和 Y"但未说明混淆的理论后果 | 必须展示混淆后"什么重要的因果推断被掩盖了" |
