# "Implicit Assumption Is Wrong" Tension

## 功能定义
揭示现有文献对一个构念的默认假定是单维的/未分化的，从而暴露该假定失效后可能出现的全新理论空间。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（单维假定挑战型）**：
```
While important, much of the work on [topic] is general and considers [outcome] in a very broad sense.
Research has yet to seriously consider [specific question] — namely, [question].
This omission matters because if [theory] is correct, [actor]'s [characteristic] should shape not just whether [actor action], but which type of [outcome] [actor] pursues.
```

**模板 B（二分结构展开型）**：
```
[Theory] argues that [mechanism]. [Construct A] is associated with [orientation A].
In contrast, [Construct B] is associated with [orientation B].
Thus, [theory] is not simply about [broad yes/no question], but also about what types of specific [actions] [actor] will take.
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Gamache et al. 2020 (SMJ)

> "Research has yet to seriously consider how and why CEOs might pursue more specific stakeholder strategies reflecting unique priorities and goals. Thus, [regulatory focus theory] is not simply about which people will take action, but also about what types of specific actions people will take."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |

| **Outlet 偏好** | SMJ, OS — upper echelons/strategy 期刊欢迎构念辨析 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有理论依据证明"拆分"是理论驱动的区分，不是任意的 |
| **最佳位置** | 紧接文献综述之后，缺口段的核心句 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 假拆分 | 连续变量二分化，无理论理由 | 拆分必须由理论驱动 |
| 缺后果 | 只说了"以前太笼统"但没说拆分后学到什么 | 必须预告拆分后可以看到以前看不见的模式 |
| 拆分太细 | 七八个子类型，失去理论力量 | 2-3 个类型足够；二分对立最有理论优雅性 |
