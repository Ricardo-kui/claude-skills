# "Structural Blind Spot" Tension

## 功能定义
揭示整个文献流因为共享一个理论起点（如"商业效率视角"）而系统性地忽略了一个重要的解释维度，该维度只有通过切换理论镜头才能看见。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（理论视角切换型）**：
```
Existing [literature] predominantly adopts a [dominant orientation] — typically [core assumption].
Under this lens, [standard prediction].
However, a [alternative theoretical perspective] suggests that [different prediction].
Thus, the [dominant orientation] cannot accommodate [overlooked dimension].
```

**模板 B（基准预期-挑战预期型）**：
```
[Strategy/condition] does not alter [underlying mechanism], so a [standard perspective] would suggest [null prediction].
Yet, a [alternative perspective] suggests that [non-null prediction].
This tension reveals a blind spot: [standard literature] has neglected [overlooked dimension], focusing almost exclusively on [limited dimension].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Singh & Grewal 2023 (JMR)

> "Lobbying does not alter product quality, so an efficiency perspective would suggest no relationship. However, a legitimacy-based institutional theory perspective suggests that lobbying reduces recalls. More broadly, we extend beyond existing recall literature, which mainly adopts a business orientation."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | JMR, JM, ASQ — 制度理论/社会学视角引入"硬"商业话题 |
| **前提条件** | 盲点必须是文献流的结构性产物（共享范式），而非个别论文疏忽 |
| **最佳位置** | Introduction 中段，Hook 和文献综述之后，conversation 收束处 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| "没人做过"包装成盲点 | "Few studies have examined X" 不是结构性盲点 | 必须证明整个文献的默认视角导致了系统性遗漏 |
| 盲点过窄 | 只有一两篇论文疏忽 | 必须对照至少一个完整文献流的共享假定 |
| 新视角无推论差异 | 换了视角但预测一样 | 新视角必须产生不同的（最好是反直觉的）预测 |
