# "Overlooked Alternative Strategy / Explanation" Tension

## 功能定义
指出文献中一个系统性的解释偏倚：学界默认从某一侧（如供给侧）解释现象，却始终忽略另一侧（如需求侧）的解释。这种偏倚不是偶然的，而是源于学科范式的"路径依赖"。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（供给侧vs需求侧转向型）**：
```
The dominant approach to explaining [phenomenon] has focused on [supply-side/actor-internal factor] — namely, [specific factors].
This emphasis is understandable given [context].
However, this [supply/internal]-side focus has overlooked a more fundamental driver: [demand/external factor].
We argue that [actor]'s [decision] is better understood not by [internal orientation] but by [external cue].
```

**模板 B（战略替代型）**：
```
[Literature X] suggests that [actor] responds to [standard driver].
Yet, [actor] faces an alternative route: rather than [strategy A], they could [strategy B].
Under what conditions does this overlooked alternative become viable?
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Zhao & Ding 2022 (OS)

> "We propose that entrants in digital markets learn about the demand environment by observing customers' evaluations of other products and use this external market feedback to inform their initial market positions."（需求侧解释替代了传统的供给侧定位能力解释）

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | OS — 理论创新型论文欢迎视角转换 |
| **前提条件** | 被忽视的替代方案必须是先前文献可观察到但从未系统检验的 |
| **最佳位置** | 在建立现有主流解释之后，核心理论主张之前 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 假替代 | 两个解释其实是同一机制的两个名称 | 必须证明替代解释基于不同的理论逻辑 |
| 替代但无后果 | 替代解释没有改变预测 | 替代解释必须产生不同于主流解释的推论 |
| 过度强调新颖性 | 忽略了已有少量文献也在用这个替代视角 | 必须承认先行者，但指出它们未系统化检验 |
