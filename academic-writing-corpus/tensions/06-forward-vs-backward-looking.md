# "Forward-Looking vs Backward-Looking" Tension

## 功能定义
指出既有研究是"回顾式"的（关注现象发生后的后果），而忽略了对现象"前瞻式"理解（关注什么预先驱动了现象的发生及其时机）。不是否定回顾式研究，而是主张两者结合才能形成完整因果链。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（时序缺口型）**：
```
Research on [phenomenon] has primarily examined its [post-hoc consequences] — that is, what happens [after] [outcome Y] occurs.
However, this backward-looking emphasis has left a critical question unexamined: what predicts [when / how fast] [outcome] occurs in the first place?
Our review uncovered only [small number N] studies that connect [antecedent] to [phenomenon], and none examine [temporal dimension].
```

**模板 B（因果链延展型）**：
```
We then turn to the [forward-looking consequences / second-stage outcomes] of [behavioral outcome].
Although related studies have explored [related topic], findings have been mixed.
Moreover, no studies examine how [temporal/behavioral dimension] influences [downstream consequence].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Darby et al. 2024 (MSOM)

> "Our review of the literature uncovered three studies that spotlight a connection between a firm's CEO and recalls, though none of them examine recall timing. We build upon this nascent stream of research by examining whether a CEO's stock ownership influences the speed with which the firm initiates medical device recalls."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | MSOM, JOM — 运营/供应链期刊欣赏从"后果"到"过程/时机"的扩展 |
| **前提条件** | 必须有真实的时序区分，且"when"的差异能产生显著的理论/实践后果 |
| **最佳位置** | 建立既有文献共识后，研究问题提出处 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 伪时序 | 纯粹添加一个时间变量但无实质理论创新 | 必须论证 timing/proactiveness 本身是理论上有意义的行为 |
| 否定回顾式研究 | 贬低既有文献为"不完整" | 应把回顾式研究定位为"必要但不充分"，而非"做错了" |
| 无第二层因果 | 只预测了 antecedents，但不说为什么重要 | 展示完整的 causal chain：antecedent → timing → downstream consequence |
