# "Same Policy, Opposite Effects" Tension

## 功能定义
展示同一个组织实践对两个不同子群体产生相反方向的影响，从而建立不可化约的张力——管理者必须面对的不是"要不要"的问题，而是"对谁有利/对谁有害"的权衡困境。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（对称对立型）**：
```
How does the same [organizational practice] affect [outcome] when [stakeholders] systematically differ in [ideological/identity dimension]?
[Group A], who [characteristic A], tend to [value A over B]. Thus, the practice should increase their [outcome].
In contrast, [Group B], who [characteristic B], tend to [value B over A]. Thus, the practice should decrease their [outcome].
This cross-cutting tension makes [outcome] simultaneously facilitated and undermined by the same [practice].
```

**模板 B（双轨机制型）**：
```
We hypothesize that [practice] (a) increases [outcome] for [Group A] and (b) decreases [outcome] for [Group B].
The divergence arises because [Group A] interprets [practice] as [positive signal], whereas [Group B] interprets it as [negative signal], each rooted in divergent [value/ideology frameworks].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Employee Free Speech vs. Censorship 2024 (OS)

> "Organizational censorship (a) increases psychological safety for liberals and (b) decreases psychological safety for conservatives. Liberals... have grown wary of damage that certain rhetoric can inflict... In contrast, conservatives... now tend to embrace free speech..."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 对立不对称 | 一方机制论证充分，另一方浅薄 | 两个群体的逻辑必须完全对称展开，各占相近篇幅 |
| 缺乏解决路径 | 只说"左右为难"但没有提出缓解策略 | 最高级的处理是找到调节因素，使管理者能"两边同时提升" |
| 伪对立 | 表面相反，实则只是同向的大小差异 | 必须是真正的符号反转（H1a正, H1b负） |
