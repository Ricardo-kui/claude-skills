# "Cost vs Benefit Trade-off" Tension

## 功能定义
呈现组织决策中两种相互竞争的压力，使决策困境成为核心研究问题。这种张力不仅是描述性的，更是理论性的——它解释了为什么"理性"行为者会做出看似非理性的选择。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（对称成本结构）**：
```
[Decision A] is costly; [direct costs] and [indirect costs] make it a [devastating/threatening] prospect.
[Reason to delay or avoid].
However, delaying [Decision A] may lead to even higher costs through [penalty 1], [penalty 2], and most importantly, [most salient penalty] ([citation]).
[Specific case evidence].
[Second case evidence with escalated magnitude].
Therefore, although [Decision A] are adverse events in general, a quick response may attenuate the damage.
```

**模板 B（竞争目标型）**：
```
Firms must balance [Goal A] against [Goal B].
[Why Goal A pushes toward action].
[Why Goal B pushes toward inaction or delay].
[Evidence that this tension produces systematic variation in outcomes].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early? — Eilert et al., JM

> "Recalls are costly; announcing and implementing one is associated with both direct costs in repair, restitution, or liability and indirect costs such as losses in reputation and market value. Consequently, recalls could have a devastating impact on a firm's performance, sometimes even threatening its survival. Thus, a firm has reasons to avoid a quick recall and instead wait for the investigation to conclude. However, delaying a product recall may lead to higher direct and indirect costs through fines, liability damages, and most importantly, diminished reputation."

**改写模板**：
> "[Actions] are costly; [implementing/undertaking] one is associated with both direct costs in [categories] and indirect costs such as losses in [categories]. Consequently, [actions] could have a devastating impact on [actor]'s performance, sometimes even threatening its survival. Thus, [actor] has reasons to avoid a quick [response] and instead wait for [process]. However, delaying [action] may lead to higher costs through [mechanisms]."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | 通用型，特别适合 marketing, operations, governance 论文 |
| **理论类型** | **行为理论 / 有限理性 / 注意力基础观** — 解释在双重约束下的"满意解" |
| **前提条件** | 双方成本都必须有证据支撑；决策确实艰难（不能是一边倒） |

## 关键技巧：数字的递进结构

有效的成本-收益张力需要**数量级递进**：
1. 抽象成本（无数字）
2. 类别成本（direct vs indirect）
3. 小案例 + 精确数字
4. 大案例 + 更大数字（10x-100x 跳跃）

Example progression:
- "Recalls are costly" (abstract)
- "direct costs in repair, restitution, or liability" (categories)
- "Toyota was fined $17.35 million" (small case)
- "General Motors $900 million" (large case)

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 成本不对称 | 一方成本巨大，另一方微不足道 | 必须让读者感到"两边都疼" |
| 无决策主体 | "有成本也有收益"但没有说明是谁在决策 | 必须明确决策主体（firms, managers, regulators） |
| 停留在描述 | 描述了困境但没有转化为研究问题 | 必须以问题结束："This tension raises important questions about [theoretical mechanism]" |

## 理论升级路径

成本-收益张力可以从三个层面理论化：

| 层面 | 理论化方式 | 示例 |
|------|-----------|------|
| **制度层** | 不同制度环境下的成本结构差异 | 反垄断法改变并购的成本-收益计算 |
| **组织层** | 组织结构如何影响对竞争压力的响应 | 多元化vs聚焦型组织的不同响应 |
| **个体层** | 决策者认知如何扭曲成本-收益感知 | 短视主义导致未来成本被低估 |
