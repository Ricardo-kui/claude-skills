# "Resource Acquisition vs Resource Utilization" Tension

## 功能定义
将一种组织策略的"硬收益"（获取更多资源）与其"软代价"（损害资源使用效率）构成张力，从而重设评价标准：问题不是"能不能拿到资源"，而是"拿到了之后用得好不好"。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（核心转向型）**：
```
Based on [theoretical perspective], we suggest that although [focal strategy] may benefit firms' resource acquisition,
it imposes constraints on resource utilization, leading to [negative outcome].
We suggest [N] reasons for this:
(1) [motivation/agency-slack mechanism];
(2) [constraint/lock-in mechanism];
(3) [learning/path-dependence mechanism].
```

**模板 B（成本条件型）**：
```
[Strategy] confers clear benefits in [resource type A], [resource type B], and [resource type C].
Yet, these acquisition benefits may conceal a utilization cost: [mechanism].
The extent of this cost depends on how [resource-availability condition] shifts the trade-off.
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Shen et al. 2022 (JOM)

> "Based on political embeddedness perspective, we suggest that although political ties may benefit firms' resource acquisition, they impose constraints on resource utilization, leading to reduced operational efficiency. We suggest three reasons: complacency, government expectations, and path dependence."

## 使用场景

| 维度 | 建议 |
|------|------|
| **Outlet 偏好** | JOM — OM 期刊关注"效率"多于"资源量" |
| **前提条件** | "获取"和"利用"在理论上必须可分离；应展示不止一个可行的机制 |
| **最佳位置** | 核心理论主张句，紧接问题化之后 |

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| 获取和利用混淆 | 获取本身被定义为"有效获取"，消解了张力 | 必须区分"拿到"(volume) 与"用好"(efficiency) |
| 只给一个机制 | 感觉是孤证 | 至少给 2-3 个独立运作但又指向同一方向的机制 |
| 否定获取价值 | 暗示资源获取没有价值 | 必须承认资源获取是真实收益，只是伴随隐性代价 |
