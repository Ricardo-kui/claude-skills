# Literature-to-Gap Transition

## 功能定义
在建立了文献共识或对话之后，用一句精准的话刺破这个共识的完整性，将读者从"我们已知很多"转向"但我们还不知道这个关键问题"。这是 Introduction 中最需要"手术刀般精确"的句子。

## 句法模板

**模板 A（直接缺口型）**：
```
Despite [this progress / these advances / considerable attention], [specific dimension / mechanism / boundary condition] has remained [largely unaddressed / poorly understood / theoretically underspecified].
```

**模板 B（后果型缺口）**：
```
[This body of work / These studies] has [treated / assumed / implied] that [condition].
However, [what happens when condition does not hold / what has been overlooked] has [received scant attention / remained unexplored].
[Third sentence: why this omission matters].
```

**模板 C（方法/视角型缺口）**：
```
[Existing research] has [predominantly / almost exclusively] [adopted / relied on / examined] [approach A].
[Second sentence: what approach B would reveal].
[Third sentence: the costs of this methodological/theoretical monoculture].
```

**模板 D（反直觉型缺口）**：
```
[Theoretical prediction] follows naturally from [theory].
[Second sentence: yet empirical pattern consistently violates this prediction].
[Third sentence: this anomaly suggests that [mechanism] is [incomplete / misspecified]].
```

## 例句（来自 MVP30）

**来源**：Does it Pay to Recall your Product Early?

> "To date, no study to our knowledge has examined the factors that influence the timing of product recalls. The product recall literature in marketing... has predominantly focused on the consequences of recalls... However, little attention has been paid to the actual recall behavior of the firm."

**来源**：State Ownership and Firm Innovation

> "These conflicting images are not merely academic. In 2014, 106 Chinese firms appeared on the Fortune Global 500 list; two-thirds were SOEs. This striking empirical pattern suggests that neither caricature fully captures the reality of state ownership."

**改写模板**：
> "[Existing literature / Prior research] has [predominantly / largely] focused on [established topic] (e.g., [citation 1]; [citation 2]). However, [specific gap] has [remained largely unaddressed / received surprisingly little attention]. This omission is consequential because [theoretical reason] and [practical reason]."

## 关键技巧：Gap 的精确性公式

最有效的 gap 声明不是"没人研究过这个"，而是"已有研究在[特定维度]上的不足导致了[特定后果]"。

**Gap 精确性检查表**：
- [ ] 我是否说明了文献**已经做了什么**？（建立 goodwill）
- [ ] 我是否精确指出了**遗漏了什么**？（不能是"机制不清楚"这种泛泛之谈）
- [ ] 我是否解释了**为什么这个遗漏重要**？（stakes）

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 问题 | 修正 |
|--------|------|------|
| "文献很少" | 最弱的 gap 声明 | 改为"文献在[维度X]上丰富，但在[维度Y]上沉默" |
| Gap 过大 | "然而理论还不完善" | 精确到"the moderating role of [specific factor]" |
| Gap 过小 | "然而还没有人用中国数据" | 必须论证为什么新场景改变理论理解 |
| 无 stakes | 指出 gap 后没有说明重要性 | 必须紧跟"This omission matters because..." |

## 变体表达库

| 强度 | 表达 | 适用情境 |
|------|------|----------|
| 温和 | "has received relatively little attention" | 合作关系良好的 literature |
| 中等 | "remains poorly understood" | 一般性缺口 |
| 强烈 | "is theoretically underspecified" | 理论驱动型期刊 |
| 强烈 | "rests on an untested assumption" | 直接挑战基础假设 |
| 惊讶 | "surprisingly understudied given its importance" | 显然重要但被忽视 |
