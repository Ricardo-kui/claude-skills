# Corpus Patterns — MVP30 Introduction Exemplars

## A. Incompleteness (low tension) + Progressive Coherence

| Exemplar | Journal | Hook type | Core feature | Use when |
|----------|---------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| **Wu 2025** | SMJ | Background + definition + consequence list | Phenomenon definition with authoritative quote pivot to gap | Natural experiment / institutional shock; cold-start from academic definition |
| **Eilert 2017** | JM | Data hook: trend + scale | Statistical data opening establishing severity | Marketing / consumer domain with striking industry data |
| **Darby 2024** | MSOM | Anecdote hook | Concrete industry event establishing context | Operations / supply chain with specific practice cases |
| **Toh 2023** | SMJ | Ecosystem background | Industry ecosystem structure opening | Platform / ecosystem research |
| **Pollock 2015** | ASQ | Interdisciplinary analogy | Other-discipline analogy introducing core construct | Construct differentiation needing cross-disciplinary borrowing |
| **Shi 2021** | JMR | Industry news anecdote | News event establishing phenomenon relevance | Organizational behavior / market reaction |
| **Park 2013** | ASQ | Literature consensus → gap | Literature consensus start, pointing to missing dimension | Social discrimination / institutional theory |
| **Malshe 2015** | JM | Classic literature review hook | Three-reason gap argument: finance → marketing cross | Interdisciplinary (finance → marketing) / capital structure |
