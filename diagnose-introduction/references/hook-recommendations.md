# Hook Recommendations by Gap Strength and Journal Style

Use this reference to recommend hook strategies after gap diagnosis.

## Hook by Gap Strength

| Gap strength | Recommended hook | Rationale | Exemplars |
|-------------|------------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Incompleteness** (low) | Cold-start definition / trend data / practitioner quote | Gently establish the domain | Wu 2025, Eilert 2017, Malshe 2015 |
| **Inadequacy** (medium) | Contrast case / classic debate / quote pivot | Establish cognitive tension | Han 2024, Keeves 2017, Employee Free Speech |
| **Incommensurability** (high) | Consensus challenge / interdisciplinary analogy / immersive narrative | Challenge existing framework | Pontikes 2012, Zhou 2017, Hahl 2017 |

## Journal Style Guide

| Journal | Preferred hook style | Cautions |
|---------|---------------------|----------|
| **SMJ** | Calm, professional opening; minimal anecdote / emotion | Consensus challenge needs concrete counterexamples (Mayer/Fiorina/Rometty) |
| **ASQ** | Theoretical hooks allowed: interdisciplinary analogy (Pollock 2015), classic theory establishment (Zhou 2017), extreme scenario (Lashley & Pollock 2020) | — |
| **AMJ** | Managerial relevance; consequence → antecedent (Lovelace 2021); multi-level bridging potential | — |
| **ASR** | Classic theory disruption (Weber/Bourdieu → shattered image); theoretical runway must be sufficient; experiment preview comes after theory derivation | Do not treat experiment as main contribution |
| **OS** | Social hotspot / policy tension opening; symmetric dual-track must balance opposing logics | Do not treat policies as uniformly beneficial or harmful |
| **JM/JMR/MSOM/IJRM** | Data/statistical scale opening or concrete industry event; economic significance must be previewed | Do not use "Interestingly" to introduce findings |
