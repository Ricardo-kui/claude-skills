# Makadok Contribution Dimensions

Use this reference to diagnose which theoretical lever the user's research changes.

## Dimension Table

| Core question | What does your research do? | Dimension | Exemplars |
|--------------|----------------------------|-----------|-----------|
| What construct/variable? | Introduce new construct, redefine, change role | **Constructs** | Han 2024, Pollock 2015, Gamache 2020 |
| What causal mechanism? | Introduce new mechanism, replace mechanism, decompose mechanism | **Mechanism** | Wu 2025, Keeves 2017, Hahl 2017 |
| What boundary condition? | Tighten, loosen, add, or challenge scope | **Boundary** | Eilert 2017, Han 2020, Gamache 2023 |
| What phenomenon domain? | Apply existing theory to new phenomenon | **Phenomenon** | DesJardine 2023, Employee Free Speech |
| What level of analysis? | Move, add, critique, or integrate across levels | **Level** | Keeves 2017 |
| How to theorize? | Inductive↔deductive, process↔variance, static↔dynamic | **Mode** | Lashley & Pollock 2020 |
| What research question? | Propose new question, reframe old question | **Question** | Park 2025 |
| What theory output? | New explanation, prediction, prescription | **Output** | Pontikes 2012 |

## Quick Diagnostic: Natural What-We-Learn Phrases

| Phrase pattern | Dimension |
|----------------|-----------|
| "We differentiate X from Y..." | Constructs |
| "We explain why X affects Y by identifying Z..." | Mechanism |
| "We identify [context] as a key boundary condition..." | Boundary |
| "We examine [phenomenon]..." | Phenomenon |
| "We bridge [micro] and [macro]..." | Level |
| "We adopt a [process/variance] lens..." | Mode |
| "We redirect attention from... to..." | Question |
| "Our theory generates a counter-intuitive prediction..." | Output |
