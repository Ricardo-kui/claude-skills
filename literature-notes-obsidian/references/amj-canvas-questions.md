# AMJ Management Research Canvas

Source: Dorobantu, S., Gruber, M., Ravasi, D., and Wellman, N. (2024). "From the Editors: The AMJ Management Research Canvas." *Academy of Management Journal*, 67(5), 1163-1174.

These questions are **already folded into the evidence card (天堂模版)**. Do not emit a separate AMJ seven-module note as the `literature/` formal note. Use this file as the question bank when filling §1–§6, or when upgrading an old AMJ-canvas note onto the card.

## Module 1: The Puzzle

- What is the real phenomenon, puzzle, or unresolved empirical pattern?
- Why should a reader care from a practical or theoretical perspective?
- Does the paper start from a genuine puzzle or from a manufactured "gap"?

## Module 2: Audience and Prior Research

- Which scholarly conversation is the paper entering?
- Which scholars, assumptions, and theories define that conversation?
- What does prior work already claim to know?
- What exactly has prior work not resolved: mechanism, boundary condition, comparison, measurement, or causal inference?

## Module 3: The Research Question

- Can the research question be stated in one sentence?
- What constructs or variables are implicit in that question?
- What is the intuitive answer, and why is that intuition not good enough?
- How does answering the question move the literature rather than merely add another setting?

## Module 4: Constructs and Relationships

### WHAT

- What are the core constructs?
- Are the constructs inherited from prior work, sharpened, or newly introduced?

### HOW

- What relationships among constructs does the paper propose?
- Are the relationships linear, moderated, mediated, sequential, recursive, or comparative?

### WHY

- What mechanism explains those relationships?
- Which broader theory or theoretical lens makes the mechanism believable?
- Does the explanation do real causal or behavioral work, or does it rely on labels and citations?

## Module 5: Research Setting and Design

- Why is this setting appropriate for the question?
- What data, sample, and comparison structure does the paper use?
- How are the focal constructs operationalized?
- What identifying variation or inferential leverage does the design rely on?
- Was endogeneity addressed? How? If IV or control function, which instrument / excluded variable was chosen, and why?
- What assumptions must hold for the authors' strongest claims to be credible?
- Where is the slippage between the ideal test and the actual design?

## Module 6: Findings

- What are the main empirical or analytical patterns?
- Are the findings only statistically significant, or also substantively meaningful?
- How much interpretive weight can the design support?

## Module 7: Contributions and Boundary Conditions

- What theoretical, empirical, or methodological contribution is actually earned?
- What is genuinely new, and what is expected?
- Where are the boundaries, threats, and limitations?
- If the paper were absent from the literature, what would be missing?
