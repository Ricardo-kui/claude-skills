## Fast Decision Rules

| If the phenomenon centers on… | Start with | Ask first |
|---|---|---|
| External knowledge becoming useful | Ch01 | Which absorption stage fails? |
| Innovation spreading | Ch09 | Which adoption stage and social channel? |
| Reconfiguring capabilities | Ch10 | Is change systematic and repeatable? |
| Delegated authority | Ch03 | Hidden action, hidden information, or risk sharing? |
| Make–buy or contracting | Ch40 | How specific, uncertain, and frequent is the transaction? |
| Conformity across organizations | Ch17 | Coercive, mimetic, or normative pressure? |
| Population founding or survival | Ch21 | Legitimacy, competition, niche, or inertia? |
| Structure–context alignment | Ch38 | What exact form of fit is predicted? |
| Fairness reactions | Ch22 | Outcome, procedure, treatment, or explanation? |
| Motivation | Ch08/15/28/31 | Discrepancy, goal, need support, or efficacy? |
| Decisions under risk | Ch24 | What is the reference point? |
| Ambiguous events | Ch29 | Which cues and frames create the story? |
| Group-based behavior | Ch35 | Which identity and comparison group are salient? |
| Relational resources | Ch30/36 | Resource mechanism or network structure? |

## Closest-Theory Tests

- **Ch01 vs Ch18**: Receiving and using external knowledge vs coordinating specialized knowledge within the firm.
- **Ch03 vs Ch40**: Delegation and incentive conflict vs comparative governance of a transaction.
- **Ch04 vs Ch29**: Collective issue salience vs construction of plausible meaning from cues and frames.
- **Ch05 vs Ch25**: Security regulation in relationships vs perceived reciprocal promises.
- **Ch08 vs Ch15**: Feedback discrepancy regulation vs goal effects on attention, effort, persistence, and strategy.
- **Ch09 vs Ch17**: Communication and staged adoption vs legitimacy and conformity pressure.
- **Ch10 vs Ch26**: Reconfiguring the resource base vs explaining protected resource advantage.
- **Ch12 vs Ch22**: Normative criteria for right conduct vs perceptions of fairness.
- **Ch14 vs Ch24**: Strategic interdependence under specified payoffs vs reference-dependent valuation under risk.
- **Ch16 vs Ch20**: Compatibility screening against images vs internal causal representations of a system.
- **Ch17 vs Ch38**: Legitimate sameness vs efficient structure–context fit.
- **Ch20 vs Ch29**: Stored causal representation vs ongoing social construction after disruption.
- **Ch23 vs Ch31**: Intention from attitude, norm, and control vs efficacy, modeling, and reciprocal determinism.
- **Ch25 vs Ch33**: Perceived promises vs broader reciprocal exchange.
- **Ch27 vs Ch35**: Expectations attached to a position vs self-definition through group membership.
- **Ch28 vs Ch31**: Need-supported motivation quality vs belief in task capability.
- **Ch30 vs Ch36**: Resources available through relations vs formal tie structure and position.
- **Ch37 vs Ch40**: Multi-party claims and value distribution vs economizing on exchange hazards.
