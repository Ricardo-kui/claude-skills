# Pollock Stage Gates

## Preparing

Purpose: discover the paper's question, knot, characters, and feasible evidence.

- Allowed: diagnosis, story contract, evidence inventory, competing formulations.
- Not allowed: polished full-section prose.
- Exit condition: a defensible provisional contract and an explicit evidence plan.

## Blocking

Purpose: establish the paper's rough architecture.

- Allowed: section maps, placeholder skeletons, provisional causal or theoretical chains.
- Every inferred claim must be labelled.
- Exit condition: the section sequence can resolve the promised storylines without contradiction.

## Refining

Purpose: improve logic, pacing, alignment, and evidentiary credibility.

- Requires `story.status: confirmed`.
- Allowed: full-section drafting, restructuring, cross-section repair.
- New main characters or storylines require an explicit contract update before drafting continues.

## Finishing

Purpose: polish and quality-control a stable paper.

- Requires confirmed story and stable or explicitly mixed evidence.
- Allowed: clarity edits, compression, transitions, alignment and overclaim audits.
- Not allowed: new unsupported storylines, hypotheses, findings, or contribution claims.

## Gate Outcomes

- `PASS`: all fields and stage conditions are met.
- `PROVISIONAL`: blocking-stage scaffold is allowed, with assumptions displayed.
- `BLOCKED`: return Story Intake and repair instructions only.

## Section Gate Matrix

| Section | Additional gate |
|---|---|
| Introduction/front end | theoretical stakes and reader shift are explicit |
| Theory | character roles are explicit; each hypothesis has a `storyline_id` |
| Methods | each storyline maps to constructs, operational variables, model, and validity burden |
| Results | evidence exists; each storyline can be classified supported, mixed, unsupported, or unresolved |

Local-only requests use the bypass notice from the core skill and never mutate the contract.
