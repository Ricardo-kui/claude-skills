# Learning Affordance Protocol

Extract a learning affordance only when it answers: “What structural choice in this section could help a writer facing a genuinely comparable problem?”

For each affordance, specify:

1. **Move** — a structural action, not wording to copy.
2. **Why it works here** — the role it plays in this paper's whole story.
3. **Transfer conditions** — what must be true in a new paper before the move is sensible.
4. **Do not copy** — the setting, theory, evidence, or rhetorical device that belongs only to the exemplar.

Do not force an affordance for every section. A strong Results story does not make a paper a useful Introduction exemplar.

When the affordance concerns a mechanism, extract its theoretical narrative work first—how it turns the opening problem into a prediction or gives later evidence meaning. State empirical verification only as a single calibration judgment; do not make repeated mechanism caveats the card's main learning content.
