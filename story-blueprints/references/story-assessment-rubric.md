# Story Assessment Rubric

Apply this rubric only after completing the descriptive story reading.

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Theme coherence | Does the research question consistently organize the paper? |
| Character discipline | Are main and supporting constructs distinguishable, with no distracting storyline? |
| Knot integrity | Is there a genuine challenge that the study can plausibly address? |
| Plot emergence | Do theory, design, and results arise from the constructs and situation rather than a forced plot? |
| Tie–unravel alignment | Does the evidence actually answer the question that the front end made salient? |
| Ending quality | Does the ending return to the opening with a changed understanding rather than merely repeat findings? |

Record only a local judgment—`works`, `partly_works`, `does_not_work`, or `not_assessable`—and a concise rationale. Do not total scores.

Treat a theoretical mechanism as the middle of the story, not as a criterion that must be empirically observed for theme coherence, plot emergence, or tie–unravel alignment. After the assessment, record at most one separate **Mechanism evidence check**: `directly_tested`, `partly_probed`, `not_directly_tested`, or `not_assessable`, with a brief basis. This check calibrates the evidence claim only.

Use a second reading only for cards intended to be repeatedly recommended, for contested judgments, or for a deliberately assembled contrast set. A second reading should state its alternative interpretation, not seek artificial coder agreement.
