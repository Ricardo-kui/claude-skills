## Story Reading

### Theme question

What does the structure of a manufacturer's dependence on its supplier do to the manufacturer's performance in that relationship — specifically, does joint dependence (the summed mutual dependence the field forgot) help through an embeddedness logic of value creation, while dependence asymmetry (the half the field kept) works through a power logic of value appropriation?

### Whole-story synopsis

The paper opens inside the discipline's own memory. Emerson (1962) split interdependence into two dimensions — dependence asymmetry, which he labeled "power advantage," and joint dependence, which he labeled "cohesion" — a non-zero-sum conception in which both can vary simultaneously. The knot is tied with the canon's own thread: "it is puzzling that, with few exceptions, most subsequent research has been drawn only to the implications of dependence asymmetry and its related power dynamics." By grounding itself in a logic of power, the field omitted the logic of embeddedness that arises from joint dependence — whose implications for the total value created and for exchange performance "have yet to be considered." The gap is not stated once but three times, each narrowing bound to a different literature stream's close: the organizational power tradition (logic-level omission), the closest recent study — Casciaro and Piskorski (2005), honored for restoring the dichotomy but differentiated for studying constraint absorption rather than alternative logics of action and aggregate performance (outcome-level omission), and the sociology/social-psychology/marketing-channels line that kept seeing joint dependence through the power prism (concept-separation plus outcome omission, the crystallizer). A fourth stream, the automotive industry literature, is functionally reversed: the same histories once read as power plays (Dore, Perrow's profit-auditing manufacturers) are re-read as suggestive evidence of jointly dependent ties benefiting manufacturers (Honda's collaboration), and the contribution lands as a restrained triplet — "we take a step further by making systematic connections between joint dependence, the underlying logic of action, and actors' performance" — followed by a setting-warranted preview (sequential interdependence makes the performance effect of joint dependence visible).

The middle builds two engines, one per logic. The power block runs lineage (Pfeffer and Salancik, Blau) to mechanism (dependence advantage -> adversarial tactics -> value capture at the partner's expense) to industry history (manufacturers auditing weaker suppliers to strip excess profits) to a mirror-pole pair of hypotheses: the manufacturer's advantage helps (H1a), the supplier's advantage hurts (H1b). The embeddedness block revives Emerson's own hint — the Loeb-and-Leopold footnote about parties "controlled by the relation itself" — and assembles value-creation reasons (attitudinal and structural convergence, preference for non-coercive tactics, conflict decay) behind a positive main effect (H2). Then the section's most consequential paragraph locks the test's shape before any data appear: embeddedness is "a multifaceted construct" with three taxonomy-sourced elements — joint action, trust, fine-grained information transfer — each expected to partially mediate (H3/H4/H5), and the Baron-Kenny protocol is mapped onto the hypothesis system so that H2 doubles as mediation's step 1. Methods build the arena to the constructs' exact shape: 37 fieldwork interviews shape the instrument; a component-level survey of Ford and Chrysler buyers yields 151 dyads; scales are built with cross-construct discrimination and validated against a 20 percent archival subsample; and dependence asymmetry is decomposed by splines into two directional advantages — the measurement literally mirroring the theory's two poles.

The climax is a single verdict block with the story's whole reversal in it. Because satisfaction could plausibly drive more business and hence more joint dependence, the relationship might run backwards — the paper concedes this in its own voice, pretests it (Hausman p = .078), and answers with a just-identified 3SLS system instrumenting both joint dependence and performance. In Model 2 the canon's favored half fails twice — the manufacturer's dependence advantage significantly hurts (H1a reversed), the supplier's advantage does nothing (H1b null) — while the neglected half wins: joint dependence significantly enhances performance (H2). Mediation then splits the embeddedness cast: joint action and information quality are confirmed as partial mediators; trust and information scope fail at step 2, rejecting H4 and half of H5; jointly the two survivors come close to full mediation. Falling action absorbs the remainder of the tension: the suspected reciprocity is real (performance also raises joint dependence) but designed for and controlled; diagnostics and robustness are woven in by threat (VIF and trace, Breusch-Pagan, cluster bootstrap, 2SLS cross-check, reverse-mediation checks, a curvilinear null). The denouement transforms the opening rather than recapping it: the two reversed power verdicts become an audit of power logic's buried assumptions — coercion shrinks the value-creating pie faster than it enlarges the manufacturer's slice, and small suppliers facing giant assemblers often neither dare nor can afford to convert structural power into use — argued with the paper's only human voices (fieldwork quotes). The unconfirmed mediators are explained, the over-embeddedness downside is located outside the sample's range, and the contributions restate the story's spine: value appropriation and value creation must be considered simultaneously, and joint dependence belongs to the second.

### Characters and storylines

- **Main character — joint dependence:** the reclaimed half of the canon and the paper's title construct; every hypothesis funnels toward its performance consequence, and it survives the story's two greatest hazards (reciprocity and mediation protocol) with its effect intact.
- **Foil — dependence asymmetry (the two dependence advantages):** the canon's favored half, given a fair and falsifiable stage through mirror-pole hypotheses; its predicted benefits are tested and reversed, which is what makes the embeddedness revival a discovery rather than a straw-man win.
- **Supporting — the embeddedness mechanisms (joint action, trust, information-exchange scope/quality):** the logic's hands; two are confirmed, two are refuted, and the refusals are kept on stage and explained rather than dropped.
- **Supporting — the two logics (power vs embeddedness):** the story's rival principles, not personified opponents; they attach to different constructs, predict through different channels (appropriation vs creation), and coexist in the ending's synthesis.
- **Supporting — reciprocity (performance <-> joint dependence):** an offstage design risk conceded in the authors' own voice, promoted to an on-stage finding, and absorbed by the 3SLS system — the antagonist that never gets to be one.
- **Ensemble — Emerson (offstage canonical authority whose half-forgotten distinction licenses the entire plot); manufacturer's performance in the procurement relationship (the single stage on which all six hypotheses land); the two automakers and their buyers (the arena); the fieldwork voices (who appear only in the last act, as the paper's human face).**
- **Storyline 1 (power):** H1a/H1b predicted from value-appropriation logic, reversed and nullified in Model 2, paid off in the Discussion's assumption audit — the storyline whose failure is the story's second discovery.
- **Storyline 2 (embeddedness):** H2 through H3/H4/H5 — main effect plus a pre-declared mediation chain that half-confirms; the headline promise, delivered.
- **Storyline 3 (reciprocity):** reverse causation conceded in Analysis, pretested, designed against, then confirmed as a finding and controlled — converts the design's biggest vulnerability into corroborating texture.

### Five acts

- **Exposition:** Genealogy cold open restoring Emerson's two-facet distinction with original in-world labels; the puzzle flag plus single-logic attribution declare the field's selective inheritance; the gap triplet varies across three literature streams (power tradition, Casciaro & Piskorski, channels research); the industry stream is functionally reversed and the contribution triplet lands ("systematic connections between construct, logic of action, performance"); the preview warrants the setting (sequential interdependence) and promises mechanisms, not directions.
- **Rising action:** Two logic blocks tighten the knot — the power block stages the adversary's expected victory with industry history (supplier profit audits), the embeddedness block revives the canon's own hint and assembles three value-creation reasons behind H2; the mediator preamble triple-locks the test (taxonomy sourcing, a priori partial mediation, Baron-Kenny mapping with H2 as step 1). Methods build the arena to spec: fieldwork-shaped instrument, component-level dyads, cross-validated scales, spline decomposition mirroring the two poles of asymmetry.
- **Climax:** The Model 2 verdict block — H1a reverses sign, H1b is null ("Hence, hypotheses 1a and 1b are not supported"), and H2 is supported, the canon's two halves judged in one breath; the mediation protocol then splits the cast (joint action and information quality confirmed; trust and scope refuted), with the joint model indicating nearly full mediation.
- **Falling action:** Reciprocity confirmed and controlled ("the suspected recursive relationship indeed exists"); multicollinearity, heteroskedasticity, and clustering handled with VIF/trace, Breusch-Pagan, and cluster bootstrap; 2SLS cross-check and reverse-mediation checks; the curvilinear over-embeddedness test returns null; just-identification's untestable exclusion honestly declared.
- **Denouement:** The Discussion audits power logic's two buried assumptions in light of the reversals, voiced by fieldwork quotes; the null supplier-power effect is explained (intimidation, resource limits); the unconfirmed mediators are attributed (narrow component focus; trust belonging to interaction history); over-embeddedness is located outside the sample's range; contributions culminate in the appropriation-plus-creation synthesis and the RDT point that social governance can work as well as formal control.

### Tension

- **Source:** The challenge is internal to the discipline's memory — a canonical two-facet distinction half-forgotten — so the paper must prove the forgotten half is real, distinct, and consequential, not a relabeling. A second, sharper tension is structural: the headline claim (joint dependence -> performance) is exposed to reverse causation by its own psychology (satisfied partners shift business), so the central effect is on trial from the moment it is named.
- **Construction:** The puzzle flag plus the single-logic attribution make the blindspot systemic rather than personal; the closest prior study is honored and differentiated rather than dismissed; the Analysis section voices the reciprocity objection in the authors' own words before naming the bias and answering it with a system estimator; and the reversed H1a verdict is reported without softening and explicitly handed forward ("we explore them further in the discussion section") — the tension the front end built is paid, not buried. (Knot reading in the v0.3 type vocabulary — overlooked-alternative primary with half-domain-gap compound, resolved by revelation — is retained in the classification tags as a retrieval note only.)

### Alternative readings

- **author_signaled_alternative:** The null supplier-power effect invites a rival explanation the paper itself stages: classical power-dependence reasoning says asymmetry becomes power use, but Molm (1981) and the fieldwork suggest small suppliers neither want nor can afford to convert structural advantage into coercion — the Discussion argues for the bounded version rather than leaving the null as a failure.
- **author_signaled_alternative:** Over-embeddedness theory (Uzzi) predicts a curvilinear downside of joint dependence; the authors test it, find none, and attribute the null to sampling at the low end of the dependence distribution (American vs Japanese/Korean ties) — a boundary reading they raise themselves.
- **analyst_counterfactual:** The paper could be read as paradigms-at-war adjudication (power logic vs embeddedness logic) rather than overlooked-alternative revelation; the text resists this — the logics attach to different constructs, the power logic is refined rather than defeated, and the contributions frame the result as "not just value-appropriation... but also value creation." Recorded as analyst-generated.
- **analyst_counterfactual:** The trust mediator's failure could be read as the theory's own perception mechanism cutting the other way (dependence generates positive bias in trustworthiness assessments, so trust could be an outcome rather than a conduit of joint dependence); the L2 theory distillation flags the structural/perceptual mechanism mix as causally unaligned. Recorded as analyst-generated; the paper does not pursue it.

## Learning Affordances

### Introduction

- **Suitable:** `yes`
- **Learn:** (1) Canonical-dichotomy selective-inheritance gap — restore the canon's own two-facet distinction with verbatim in-world labels, then flag the field's one-sided inheritance with a puzzle flag plus a single-logic attribution, so one sentence both declares the gap and explains why the blindspot is systemic while the criticism lands on the inheritance trajectory rather than on any scholar. (2) Gap-triplet variation with functional reversal — restate the same gap at three levels, each bound to a different literature stream's close, then re-read the industry stream's power narratives as joint-dependence narratives and cash the contribution as a restrained "systematic connections" triplet ahead of the preview.
- **Do not copy:** Requires a real canonical precedent whose labels are quotable and a documented one-sided inheritance; named exceptions must be honored and differentiated (L2 risk ledger: fabricated genealogy and rhetorically inflated puzzle flags are the mimicry failure modes). The eight-paragraph, no-human-face, no-data cold open is 2007-ASQ-native and was the L2 profile's weakest_aspect; modern submissions likely need an empirical shock or stakes scene earlier, and the preview deliberately promises no direction — unsuitable if your venue expects findings previewed.

### Theory

- **Suitable:** `yes`
- **Learn:** (1) Dual-logic block organization — give each logic a complete lineage-mechanism-evidence-hypothesis cycle with a logic-contrast pivot between blocks, using mirror-pole paired hypotheses (same DV, opposite signs) to give the rival logic a fair, falsifiable stage; requires that the two logics attach to different constructs and make non-competing predictions (L2: not a rival/dialectical structure — do not route to adjudication machinery). (2) Mediator preamble triple-lock — source the mediator set from an existing construct taxonomy, declare partial mediation a priori at the set level, and map the testing protocol onto the hypothesis system so the main effect doubles as mediation's step 1, making the hypothesis block one testable machine.
- **Do not copy:** The a priori partial-mediation claim here lacks an independent second-channel warrant (L2 contradiction) — a modern version must argue why each mediator is only partial; the trust block's structural and perceptual mechanisms are causally unaligned (L2 flag) and the data later refuse this mediator; the section has no boundary conditions at all (T5 absent), leaving the over-embeddedness downside to post hoc Discussion handling — legitimate in 2007, a reviewer risk now.

### Methods

- **Suitable:** `yes`
- **Learn:** (1) The construct-validity chain as story infrastructure — theoretical-dimension item sourcing, cross-construct discrimination with non-discriminating items named and dropped, rotation cross-verification, the low-alpha conservative-test reversal, and a random-subsample archival benchmark (> .90) that converts perception measures into audited ones inside the design chapter. (2) Specification defense as anticipated counterargument — canon-anchored choice, alternative's failure cases analyzed, alternative re-run, and the stronger-but-infeasible technique ruled out by stating its infeasibility condition; the spline decomposition mirrors the theory's two directions of advantage, the card's cleanest example of measurement embodying plot.
- **Do not copy:** Single-wave same-source CMV defense is Harman-only while IV and DV are concurrently measured — the L2 honesty boundary says Harman passing does not dissolve the timing threat (modern stack needs temporal separation or marker variables); the estimation narration is absent from this section (it lives in the results slice's Analysis subsection, L2 C2); one-sided dyad data and the undisclosed Firm-dummy baseline are 2007-tolerated confessions, not patterns to copy.

### Results

- **Suitable:** `yes`
