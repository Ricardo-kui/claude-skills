## Story Reading

### Theme question

Is the fifty-year rigor-relevance debate in management research a real trade-off between two legitimate goods, or a socially constructed artifact of business schools' identity politics — and if constructed, what normative program lets the field pursue both at once?

### Whole-story synopsis

The paper opens inside a fight the field has been having with itself: rigor versus relevance has hardened into an either/or argument that sorts studies, researchers, journals, and whole programs into silos. Gulati's first move is to refuse the fight's premise. A definitional check shows the two terms are not antithetical — "most management researchers prefer research to be both applicable and exact, relevant and rigorous" — so the camps' very existence becomes the anomaly, and he resolves it in one stroke: "I argue that this debate is largely socially constructed by forces both internal and external to business schools." The construction is perpetuated by tribes that sequester themselves into closed loops of scholarship and dismiss outsiders; consequences include the exclusion of boundary spanners. The promise is a normative model of managerial research bridging the artificial divide through problem-oriented research grounded in theory. A testimony chorus then raises the stakes: an AOM president's "incestuous, closed loop of scholarship" (Hambrick), another president's dream of dichotomies reframed as tensions (Bartunek), and finally a business journalist's mockery — "the rest is just inane" — so the doubt is no longer internal grumbling but an external verdict on the field's meaning. The theory section does the argument's plot work in three movements. First, a pendulum history: 1950s-60s relevance criticism (Gordon & Howell, Pierson) produced the rigor movement; Simon's dissent — knowledge and action "an intrinsic relation" — was heard and set aside; by the 1980s the pendulum had swung too far (Porter & McKibben, Beyer, Miner); today the debate is "hotter than ever," now entangled with an existential crisis of business schools ("Who are we, really, and what are we here to do?" — Leavitt's "lopsided brains, icy hearts, and shrunken souls," Ghoshal's Enron charge, deans demanding real-world experience), and the zero-sum frame is named in the participants' own words: "To gain more of one, we must lose some of the other, in an ongoing zero-sum game." Then the pivot: "Yet reality is much more complex, as either/or in this case is a false dichotomy that in most instances is socially constructed" — and the construction mechanism is revealed: projections about who writes for whom harden into identities ("serious scholars" vs "management types"), identities into journal labels, and the social construction of polar identities and of the polar debate "reinforce one another." Second, the tribal anatomy: causes (simple heuristics equating rigor with one's own paradigm; subtribe paradigm wars; the inherent duality of business school researchers as social scientists employed by professional schools; arbiters of "good taste" and an "invisible politburo" dispensing badges of honor or shame) and effects (relevant work dismissed as non-rigorous for being merely outside a paradigm; practitioners ignoring academic work; boundary spanners — the very people through whom innovation happens at intersections — shut out). Third, refutation by genealogy and the constructive turn: "the greatest irony of these multidimensional tribal wars" is that the founders of the social sciences — Weber, Durkheim, Marx, Lewin, Freud, Smith, Keynes, Schumpeter — were theorist-practitioners who "seemed to have no trouble crossing this divide to see themselves as both"; the either/or premise is contradicted by the field's own ancestry, and scholars who take a rigor-or-relevance approach "have lost touch with their roots." The dissolved binary is cashed into a big-tent view (replacing "either-or" with "and," a positive-sum world) and an enumerated five-step integrative process: managerial sensibility shapes research questions ("talking pigs," problems with "sizzle"), the classroom as petri dish, theory building, the theory-phenomenon dialectic ("rigor abets relevance, and vice versa"), and bilingual interpretation shading into co-creation with practitioners. The discussion anticipates the strongest objection: Simon's structural verdict that "left to themselves, the oil and water will separate again." Gulati concedes partial ground — separate tracks are "a fine idea" — but subordinates structure to identity: reconfigure identities as fluid and inclusive, nurture the few ambidextrous boundary spanners, and "raise the tent poles of collaboration, rather than chopping down even their possibility," closing on Churchill's "period of consequences" — the era of talking about the debate is over.

### Characters and storylines

- **Main character — the bifurcated researcher identity (the field as "we"):** business school scholars split between scientist and practitioner selves; the whole paper is the drama of this collective protagonist discovering that its split is self-made and can be reconfigured as "more fluid and inclusive." The editorial "we" puts the author inside the character he diagnoses.
- **Antagonist construct — the either/or zero-sum frame:** not a rival scholar but a framing the field reproduces ("To gain more of one, we must lose some of the other"); dissolved by the definitional check, explained by the construction mechanism, and refuted by genealogy.
- **Supporting characters — the tribes and subtribes:** the rigor tribe, the relevance tribe, and their internal factions; collective characters whose boundary-keeping behavior (fence posts, closed loops, silos) is the mechanism of the divide.
- **Villain-most figure — the arbiters / "invisible politburo":** covert dispensers of the "rigorous"/"non-rigorous" badge, "masked as impartiality"; they build and destroy reputations and make tribalism look like quality judgment.
- **Witness characters — the founders (Weber, Durkheim, Marx, Lewin, Freud, Smith, Keynes, Schumpeter, Roethlisberger):** the testimony troupe whose biographies are the existence proof that rigor and relevance co-occur; they refute the premise by genealogy rather than argument.
- **Supporting character — the boundary spanner / ambidextrous scholar:** the excluded hero of the diagnosis who becomes the ending's program — nurture them "so that even the notion of boundaries will eventually become a thing of the past."
- **Destination construct — the big tent:** the image that carries the positive-sum resolution; voiced by Bartunek in the intro, inherited as the paper's own metaphor, raised again in the final line.
- **Storyline 1 (identity):** the field's bifurcated self ("Who are we, really?") is diagnosed as socially constructed and reconfigured as fluid and inclusive.
- **Storyline 2 (the frame):** the zero-sum either/or premise is definitionally dissolved, genealogically refuted, and replaced by "and."
- **Storyline 3 (program):** the five-step integrative process converts the diagnosis into research practice.
- **Storyline 4 (metaphor):** the imagery of enclosure and gathering (silos -> tribes -> fence posts -> small umbrellas -> big tent -> tent poles) carries the emotional arc from exclusion to collectivity.
- **Intersection:** all four storylines meet at the boundary spanner — the character the divide excludes, the identity the program cultivates, and the ending's practical lever.

### Five acts

- **Exposition:** the either/or debate and its silos; the definitional check that shows the poles are compatible ("most management researchers prefer research to be both applicable and exact"); the one-stroke resolution ("largely socially constructed") with its supporting cast (tribes, closed loops, excluded boundary spanners); the promise of a normative model bridging the artificial divide through problem-oriented, theory-grounded research.
- **Rising action:** the testimony chorus lifts doubt from internal grumbling to external verdict; the pendulum history shows the debate chronic "with how little consequence" and now entangled with an existential crisis of business schools; the zero-sum frame is named in the participants' own words; the pivot to social construction and the mechanism reveal (projections -> identities -> journal labels -> mutual reinforcement); the "cusp of a social movement from words to action" claim converts diagnosis into timeliness.
- **Climax:** the tribal anatomy — causes (heuristic rigor definitions, subtribe paradigm wars, the scientist/practitioner duality, arbiters and the invisible politburo) and effects (mutual dismissal, oil-and-water fatalism, boundary spanners shut out where innovation lives) — culminating in the great irony: the founders were theorist-practitioners, so the field's own ancestry refutes the either/or premise; the big-tent reframe ("replaces either-or with and") and the five-step program cash the dissolution into practice.
- **Falling action:** the discussion's confrontation with structuralism — Simon's verdict that oil and water will separate again, echoed by Garten's multiple-tracks proposal; the concession ("I do believe that pursuing separate tracks is a fine idea") narrows the claim to what identity reconfiguration can reach and lowers the program's temperature before the close.
- **Denouement:** the subordination of structure to identity ("reconfigure our identities as more fluid and inclusive"); nurturing ambidextrous boundary spanners until boundaries become "a thing of the past"; the tent-pole bookend ("raise the tent poles of collaboration, rather than chopping down even their possibility"); Churchill's "period of consequences" — the call to operate "as a collective rather than as warring tribes."

### Tension

- **Source:** the challenge is a framing, not a finding — a fifty-year debate with no fact of the matter to settle and no dataset that could settle it; and a commentary's structural challenge: earn consequential claims in eight pages with no new data, where the only available evidence classes are history, testimony, and consistency.
- **Construction:** the paper makes the social construction visible three ways — definitional check (the poles are compatible in principle), mechanism reveal (projections harden into identities and labels that reinforce the debate), and genealogy (the founders as existence proof). The zero-sum frame is always quoted in the participants' own words, so the refutation never fights a straw man, and external scorn (journalist, deans) supplies urgency the author cannot manufacture alone.

### Alternative readings

- **author_signaled_alternative:** the author pre-signals the big tent's feasibility risk — "If such a tent is not feasible, especially in the short-term, can we at least learn to sit simultaneously under multiple umbrellas" — and concedes again in the discussion that separate tracks are "a fine idea"; the normative model is offered with its fallback built in.
- **analyst_counterfactual:** the essay can be read as a boundary spanner's self-portrait: published in AMJ (the rigor tribe's flagship) by a scholar known for rigorous empirical work, the big-tent advocacy implicitly enlists the author as the ambidextrous scholar it calls for. The pattern (rigorous methods plus manager-facing sensibility) is visible, but reading the essay as self-positioning is the analyst's inference, not the paper's claim.

## Story Assessment

- **Theme coherence:** `works` — one question (is the divide real or constructed, and how to bridge it) organizes the definitional check, the testimony chorus, the pendulum history, the tribal anatomy, the founders' irony, the five steps, and the ending's concession; the title's three images (tent poles, tribalism, boundary spanning) are the theme in miniature, and each gets its act.
- **Character discipline:** `works` — the bifurcated "we" holds the protagonist slot, the zero-sum frame is the disciplined antagonist, and tribes, arbiters, boundary spanners, and founders hold subordinate functional roles; the dozens of quoted authorities are sorted into camps (presidents, critics, deans, founders) rather than competing as characters — quote density is the genre's texture, not a distracting storyline.
- **Tie–unravel alignment:** `works` — the intro's two-part promise (case that the gap is socially constructed via historical trajectory and underlying dynamics; discuss ways to foster boundary spanning grounded in the earliest architects and recent observers) is delivered movement by movement: history + construction mechanism + tribal anatomy; founders + five-step program. The unravel is persuasion, not test — genre-appropriate, since the promised deliverable was "recommendations," and the promise is kept at exactly that strength. What the argument cannot do is fail: the normative model has no falsifier, which the author partially answers with the built-in fallback (multiple umbrellas; separate tracks).
- **Ending quality:** `works` — the ending returns to the opening's imagery and antagonists with a changed position: it concedes the strongest objection (Simon's structuralism) and repositions the fix from programs to identity; the tent-pole metaphor bookends the paper and the Churchill close converts diagnosis into urgency. Mild sag: the structural-solution discussion opens a question the body never staged, so the last act argues a slightly new topic.
- **Boundary:** This evaluates storytelling only — no inference about the validity of the social-construction thesis, the accuracy of the founder biographies, or journal value. The PDM L2 identity (intro Incommensurability ↔ theory dialectical-integration arc; Mode-primary contribution; design/estimator n/a by genre) is carried as genre learning signals in the caveats and the tie-unravel rationale, not as story failures.

## Learning Affordances

### Introduction

- **Suitable:** `yes`
- **Learn:** (1) The premise-dissolution hook — open inside a long-standing debate, show by definitional check that the two poles are compatible so the camps' existence becomes the anomaly, and resolve the anomaly in one stroke ("socially constructed"), converting a fight into a phenomenon. (2) The escalating testimony chorus with roadmapped promise — order quotations so stakes climb from internal grumbling to external verdict, then promise the proof in delivery order and name the deliverable in a first-person roadmap.
- **Do not copy:** first-person "I argue," the editorial "we," and the quote-dense chorus are genre-licensed for commentaries; in an empirical introduction they read as assertion without evidence. The definitional move requires terms that genuinely are compatible in principle; the chorus requires names that carry weight with the addressed audience.

### Theory

- **Suitable:** `yes`
- **Learn:** (1) The pendulum-history pivot with construction mechanism — narrate the debate as chronic swings with little consequence, quote the zero-sum frame in participants' own words, pivot to "false dichotomy... socially constructed," and immediately supply the mechanism (projections -> identities -> labels -> mutual reinforcement) so the reframe lands as explanation, not opinion. (2) Cause-effect tribal anatomy resolving into genealogy irony — run explicit causes and effects of the tribalism, then refute the premise with an existence proof from the field's own ancestry and cash the dissolved binary into an enumerated positive-sum program whose closure is the contribution.
- **Do not copy:** the genealogy move requires a real ancestry that genuinely contradicts the premise — biographies are an existence proof, not a prevalence estimate; the five steps are asserted, warrant-cited prescriptions, not derived necessities, and the author's own hedges ("one doesn't have to follow them in this sequence") mark the limit; the authority-chorus texture depends on writing inside a debate whose voices are already famous to the readers.

### Discussion

- **Suitable:** `yes`
- **Learn:** (1) The objection-first concession close — stage the strongest counterposition in its author's own words, concede its partial validity, then subordinate it to the thesis (identity reconfiguration over program structure), so the opposition is absorbed rather than defeated. (2) The metaphor bookend with urgency close — return the title's image as the final image and amplify with an external historical voice to convert diagnosis into a call for action.
- **Do not copy:** opening the ending with a question the body never staged is latitude the commentary genre tolerated; the Churchill amplification is oratorical rather than evidentiary and only works because the essay has already earned its stakes.
