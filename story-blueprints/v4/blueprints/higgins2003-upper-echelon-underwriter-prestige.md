## Story Reading

### Theme question

Where do a young firm's endorsements come from — what makes a prestigious lead underwriter attach itself to one IPO-bound firm and not another — and does the same career-based signal that wins the endorser also deliver IPO success?

### Whole-story synopsis

The paper opens inside an established program: status and interorganizational ties shape young-firm outcomes, and Stuart, Hoang, and Hybels (1999) has shown that prominent affiliations pay off. The program documents only the payoff side; where powerful relationships come from — the origins of endorsement — has drawn no attention, and network theory's own premise (a pre-existing social structure as a necessary precondition) steers researchers away from asking how such ties form. The paper re-anchors the question where endorsement matters most: a mediated-market IPO, where valuation ambiguity is extreme, young firms lack any reputation to stand on, and a highly visible critic — the lead investment bank — decides who reaches investors credibly. The proposed answer imports Spence's signaling logic: because direct quality evidence is unavailable in biotech (seven-to-ten-year development cycles, contested patent indicators), the five-year career histories that SEC rules force firms to disclose become the quality evidence. The theory decomposes the upper echelon's affiliations into upstream (technological quality), horizontal (organizational quality), and downstream (market-based quality) signal types, predicts each raises underwriter prestige, adds a range hypothesis in which coverage across all three complements the separate signals, and then extends the same affiliations to IPO success with underwriter prestige as a declared partial — not strong-form — mediator. The design hand-collects those five-year careers for over 3,200 officers and directors across 296 IPO firms drawn from an 838-firm risk set, repairing the source listing's survivor bias and anchoring prominence judgments to third-party lists. The results answer the antecedent question differentially: horizontal, downstream, and above all the range of affiliations raise underwriter prestige, while upstream affiliations do not; the same affiliations predict all three success measures directly, and underwriter prestige partially mediates the downstream and range paths, with additional analyses locating the signaling value in early-product-stage uncertainty and in the whole team rather than a few key members. The discussion returns to the opening by naming what the field had overlooked — the origins of endorsements and the broker role of intermediaries — reframes the contribution as a micro-to-macro link within the prominence tradition, explains the upstream null with two candidate explanations, and ends in a practitioner's own words about assembling a signal-carrying team.

### Characters and storylines

- **Main character:** upper echelon affiliations — the young firm's career-based ties to prominent organizations — because they are the signal carrier that enters as the antecedent being explained and stays to drive the consequences.
- **Supporting characters:** lead underwriter prestige, in a deliberate double role — the prize firms compete for (the dependent variable of the first hypothesis family) and the broker that carries affiliation effects to investors (the mediator of the second); institutional investors as second-order endorsers, extending the endorsement chain beyond the bank; IPO success (net proceeds, investor count, dedicated investors) as the stake that makes endorsement worth explaining.
- **Storyline 1 (antecedent):** career affiliations are read as quality signals during the acquaintanceship period and road show, and the underwriter's endorsement decision responds to them.
- **Storyline 2 (consequence):** the same affiliations reach investors both directly and through the bank's brokerage, producing IPO success.
- **Intersection:** storyline 2 runs partially through storyline 1's outcome (the declared partial mediation), so the two storylines form one closed loop around the endorsement relation rather than two parallel tests.

### Five acts

- **Exposition:** the status and embeddedness consensus, with SHH 1999's consequences finding; the blind spot (origins unattended, because network theory presupposes a pre-existing structure); and the arena (a mediated-market IPO, young firms without reputation, biotech's long cycles and vast cash needs).
- **Rising action:** the signaling lens is imported; S-1 disclosure and the road show institutionalize careers as observable signals; the typology decomposes the protagonist into three signal types; six hypotheses accumulate to the range capstone and the a-priori partial-mediation pair; the methods build the empirical arena (838-firm risk set with survivorship repair, hand-coded careers, list-based prominence judgments).
- **Climax:** the Table 3 verdict — affiliations do buy endorsement, but differentially: horizontal, downstream, and range are supported while upstream is not. The differential pattern, not a uniform yes, is the story's first answer.
- **Falling action:** the loop closes across the three success tables — affiliations predict proceeds, investor counts, and dedicated investors; prestige partially mediates (Baron-Kenny and Sobel), with range persisting for dedicated investors; additional analyses add the uncertainty boundary (early product stage) and the whole-team-versus-core adjudication, strengthening rather than discarding the climax answer.
- **Denouement:** the discussion names the overlooked origins, generalizes to intermediaries as brokers and to the micro-macro linkage, offers two explanations for the upstream null, projects the typology onto future alliance research, and hands the story to a finance VP's quote before a two-sentence close that refuses a design formula while restating the endorsement-resource link.

### Tension

- **Source:** a genuine circularity in endorsement research — endorsements matter because audiences trust prominent endorsers, but a theory that treats the social structure as given cannot say how an unendorsed, no-reputation firm ever secures one; in biotech the direct evidence that would settle quality questions does not exist inside an IPO window.
- **Construction:** the paper makes the challenge legible by institutional staging (SEC-mandated career disclosure, the road show) that turns an unobservable quality judgment into an observable career record, and by a two-sided design that forces the origins question and the payoff question into one loop.

### Alternative readings

- **author_signaled_alternative:** the upstream null may be a measurement artifact — the authors themselves suggest that science-based affiliations may be underrepresented in final prospectuses, or that bankers assess technological quality through other information; under that reading the typology's technological-quality signal exists but is mismeasured rather than absent.
- **analyst_counterfactual:** sorting rather than signaling could generate the same association — the theory's own sorting warrant ("prominent individuals join only firms that show promise") predicts that affiliations track unobserved quality, and while selection into going public is corrected by the Heckman procedure, the selection of people into firms is not; no design element separates transfer from sorting.

## Story Assessment

- **Theme coherence:** `works` — the origins-of-endorsement question organizes the introduction's relay, the typology, the measurement chain, and the two result families; every section serves either the antecedent question or the loop that closes it.
- **Character discipline:** `works` — the affiliations construct stays grammatically and conceptually central; the underwriter's double role (prize, then broker) is declared in advance (H5's a-priori partial mediation) rather than sprung on the reader; controls and the fieldwork component stay in their lanes.
- **Knot integrity:** `works` — the origins question is a real, non-trivial challenge: the structural reason (network theory presupposes a pre-existing structure) explains why the arena was neglected, and the mediated-market setting makes the missing antecedent consequential. The knot is quiet and consensus-respecting rather than dramatic.
- **Plot emergence:** `works` — the typology arises from the industry's value-chain situation, the measures from the typology, the hypotheses from the signaling mechanism, and the coverage hypothesis from the three quality dimensions' complementarity; no section forces a plot the constructs do not generate.
- **Tie–unravel alignment:** `partly_works` — the evidence answers the antecedent question at the association level and closes the loop, but the perceptual signaling mechanism is never observed; H1's null leaves one promised signal type (technological quality) unraveled with only deferred, candidate explanations; the selection correction carries no exclusion restriction; and in this preprint the payoff cannot be verified at the coefficient level because tables are placeholders.
- **Ending quality:** `works` — the ending returns to the opening blind spot, transforms it into intermediary-broker and micro-macro agendas, honestly processes the null, and re-enacts the claim through a practitioner's voice; its causal phrasing inherits the results' overreach without disturbing the narrative closure.
- **Boundary:** This evaluates storytelling, not research quality, identification, or journal value.

## Learning Affordances

### Introduction and Theory

Use this card when a writer must convert an established consequence-side literature into an antecedent study without accusing that literature of error. The transferable moves are the two-stage gap escalation with the mirror-direction relay (consequences studied, origins not), and the single-construct typology that turns one protagonist into parallel, isomorphic signal hypotheses capped by a coverage claim and a declared partial-mediation loop. Do not copy the absolute gap claim without literature verification, the monotonic counting form, or the unadjudicated dual warrant — and the typology's biotech content itself is not transferable.

### Methods, Results, and Discussion

The paper is useful for learning structural staging: survivorship-repaired sampling frames, list-externalized prominence judgments, an estimator preflight written for non-method readers, a verdict chain that keeps the null inside it, and a hypothesized mediation family replayed across DVs. Its mechanism calibration is `partly_probed`: the pattern fits signaling, but perceptions are unmeasured and transfer versus sorting remain confounded — a writer may borrow the staging, not the evidentiary confidence.
