# DesJardine et al. 2022 × DesJardine et al. 2025 — Externality Management versus Intermediary Competition

## Scope

This record compares `desjardine2022` and `desjardine2025`. Both concern common ownership, ESG-related outcomes, and portfolio-level incentives. They make different claims about the relevant ownership network and must not be reduced to a conclusion that common ownership either promotes cooperation or worsens competition.

| Card | Ownership topology | Portfolio objective | Focal organizational outcome | Middle mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DesJardine, Grewal, and Viswanathan (2022) | An investor owns several peer firms in one industry | Manage systematic risk from CSR spillovers across the peer portfolio | Higher material CSR at portfolio firms | Positive stakeholder and regulatory spillovers make one firm's CSR valuable across owned peers |
| DesJardine, Li, and Shi (2025) | An investor owns a rating agency and firms that rival the target | Improve relative portfolio positions amid reputational threats or opportunities | Lower ESG rating for the target firm | Ownership influence over the information intermediary enables an indirect information-based competitive attack |

## Central comparative finding

The 2022 paper is an **externality-internalization** story: ownership makes peer firms' industry-wide CSR spillovers financially relevant, so an investor may encourage a portfolio-wide positive action.

The 2025 paper is an **intermediary-leverage** story: ownership gives the investor a channel outside the direct rivalry through which it can change the target's information environment for the benefit of rival portfolio firms.

The ownership topology determines the narrative. The first asks what a focal portfolio firm's conduct does to other firms the investor owns. The second asks what an investor's control over a rater can do to a target the investor does not need to own.

## Learning boundary for future retrieval

Retrieve `desjardine2022` softly for `common-ownership-positive-externality`, `portfolio-systematic-risk`, or `material-csr-spillover`. Retrieve `desjardine2025` for `information-intermediary-bias`, `indirect-competitive-attack`, or `ownership-network-topology`. Before applying either, map the actual investor, portfolio firms, target, and intermediary; similar ESG outcomes do not establish a shared mechanism.
