# Mao × Eilert — Information Production and Response Timing

## Scope

This second-reading record compares `mao2022` and `eilert2017`, with `darby2024` and `wowak2025` as necessary contrasts. It is a corpus-learning asset, not a project-story recommendation and not a claim that all recall-delay measures capture the same theoretical process.

## Central distinction

| Card | Observed or theorized clock | What produces delay? | What closes the story? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mao 2022 | defect notice → identification → recall | a firm can choose weak investigation effort, manipulate inquiry, or delay after identification | policy instruments targeted at different timestamps |
| Eilert 2017 | external investigation opening → recall | severity can deepen search and accountability pressures; brand characteristics change ability and motivation | market penalty shows that duration is value-relevant |
| Darby 2024 | awareness → recall initiation | CEO ownership can make known bad news costly to disclose | delay creates a later stock-market penalty that reverses the apparent protection motive |
| Wowak 2025 | pre-market quality production and post-awareness recall response | different governing forces affect distinct product-safety processes | a dimension split, not one timing mechanism |

## Comparative finding

Mao is the missing upstream contrast to the Darby group. It does not simply add another antecedent of recall timing: it disputes the assumption that the firm has already acquired the information required to decide. Eilert occupies an intermediate position. Its clock begins with an externally opened investigation and explains variation in the subsequent response interval, but it theorizes search and accountability pressures without observing whether firms deliberately shape the production of information.

Together, the cards prohibit collapsing four clocks into one generic `time-to-recall` construct. A study must identify whether it observes awareness, external investigation, internal identification, or recall initiation before borrowing a narrative move or making a theoretical comparison.

## Learning boundaries for retrieval

- `mao2022` may be softly retrieved for `information-production-stage`, `investigation-as-decision`, and `delay-through-inquiry`.
- `eilert2017` may be softly retrieved for `investigation-to-action-window`, `severity-complicates-response`, and `timing-as-signal`.
- Neither card establishes that slow action is deliberate concealment; Mao models strategic incentives, while Eilert expressly cannot separate inability from unwillingness.
- Their shared automobile setting is not a transfer condition. The comparable feature is a documented sequence in which information acquisition changes the meaning or feasibility of later action.

## Next comparison question

Find a paper in which an outside regulator, consumer complaints, or an internal investigation changes not only the information available to the firm but also the actor's incentive to disclose or act on it. That comparison should determine whether the corpus needs a distinct card for contested awareness, rather than relabeling every uncertain investigation as strategic information production.
