# Pupovac 2025 × Li 2026 — Vertical Spillover Lenses Record

## Scope

This is a comparison of `pupovac2025` and `li2026`, two papers on supplier shareholder losses after an automaker's recall. It is a corpus distinction, not a finding that all supplier losses arise from one mechanism.

| Card | Focal question | Organizing lens | Main evidence beyond supplier CAR | Mechanism status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pupovac 2025 | How do shareholders assess a supplier's customer exposure when a manufacturer recalls? | Screening under information asymmetry. | Prior customer disclosure and, in a selected subsample, revenue dependence. | Demand uncertainty and sequential screening are inferred; neither beliefs nor demand is observed. |
| Li 2026 | Why do buyer–supplier ties transmit recall loss, and when do extended ties alter it? | Networks as pipes and prisms. | Common business ties, common owners, common analysts, forecast downgrades, and supplier media tone. | Cash-flow and impression paths receive auxiliary traces, but are not directly separated in the event process. |

## Central comparative finding

The papers share the **outcome**—supplier value loss after a buyer recall—but not the same explanatory object.

- Pupovac treats the supplier's customer portfolio as an information problem for shareholders. Its disclosure paradox is about what outsiders can infer from a cue.
- Li treats buyer–supplier and extended-network relationships as channels of operational exposure and evaluative attribution. Its dual pathway is about what can flow through, and be read from, a tie.

Thus, `revenue dependence` is not a generic substitute for Li's pipes, and customer disclosure is not a generic substitute for prisms. The first measures an observable exposure cue; the latter theorize transmission and attribution through network structure.

## Learning boundary

For future writing, first ask whether the theoretical mechanism is:

```text
event → hidden exposure → outsider screens observable cue → valuation
```

or:

```text
event → direct tie transmits disruption / enables attribution → valuation
```

Some projects may have both, but they must establish separate evidence for the cue, the tie, the exposure, and the audience inference. A shared CAR outcome does not establish a shared mechanism.
