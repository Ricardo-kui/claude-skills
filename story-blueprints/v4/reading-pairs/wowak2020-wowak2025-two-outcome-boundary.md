# Wowak 2020 × Wowak 2025 — Two-Outcome Boundary Record

## Scope

This is a second-reading comparison of the v0.4 cards `wowak2020` and `wowak2025`. It is a corpus learning record, not a claim that either board gender composition or political ideology can be substituted for a writer's focal construct.

Both papers make product safety legible through two outcomes rather than one generic recall measure. They demonstrate, however, that a two-outcome architecture can carry very different stories.

| Card | Focal condition | Why two outcomes are needed | What each outcome represents | Shape of answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wowak 2020 | Female board representation | Severity changes the available managerial decision margin. | Class-3 count: whether to initiate a potentially concealable, rule-required recall; class-1 time-to-recall: how quickly to act after a known life-threatening defect. | Aligned: more low-severity initiation and faster severe-defect response. |
| Wowak 2025 | TMT political ideology | Product safety has distinct pre-market quality and post-awareness response stages. | Serious-recall count: earlier design/manufacturing quality process; time-to-recall: later response process. | Divergent: fewer serious recalls but slower response. |

## Central comparative finding

A two-outcome story does **not** necessarily require a paradox or mixed result. In `wowak2020`, severity is a decision-architecture device: it tells the reader why the relevant discretion shifts from **whether** to initiate to **when** to initiate. The outcomes therefore show two aligned consequences of an inferred board-level decision tone.

In `wowak2025`, the two outcomes do not arise because severity changes the margin of one decision. They represent different stages of product safety, and ideology plausibly pushes those stages in opposing directions. The differing signs are the substantive answer.

The shared learning rule is therefore narrow: split outcomes only after identifying the distinct theoretical process or decision margin each measures. Do not add outcomes merely to make a governance story look more nuanced.

## Evidence and transfer boundaries

- `wowak2020` does not observe deliberation, rule-following, or stakeholder concern at the board level. Its mechanism interpretation is consistent with the severity-specific associations and contextual interviews, not direct process observation.
- `wowak2025` does not make every recall count a quality measure; its count outcome has a specific earlier-stage theoretical role. That role should not be retroactively assigned to the class-3 count in `wowak2020`.
- Neither card licenses a generic claim that demographic or ideological categories possess fixed decision traits. A future project must establish its own actor, decision authority, and observable margins of discretion.

## Next comparison question

For a prospective card with multiple outcomes, ask first: does a contextual condition change the actor's **choice set** (`wowak2020`), or do the outcomes observe **different causal stages** of the focal process (`wowak2025`)? If neither is true, a single outcome or a different story architecture is likely more coherent.
