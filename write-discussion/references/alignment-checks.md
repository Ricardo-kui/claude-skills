# Introduction ↔ Discussion Alignment Checks

Use this reference to verify that Discussion delivers on Introduction promises.

## Promise-Delivery Mapping

| Introduction promise | Discussion corresponding paragraph | Check question |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------|----------------|
| "We differentiate X from Y..." | Theoretical contribution P2 | Has it clarified the boundary between X and Y? |
| "We explain why X affects Y by identifying Z..." | Theoretical contribution P2-P3 | Has it confirmed the Z mechanism? |
| "We identify [context] as a key boundary condition..." | Theoretical contribution P3-P4 | Has it explained the theoretical implication of the boundary condition? |
| "We examine [phenomenon]..." | Research question answer P1 | Has it returned to that phenomenon? |
| "Our theory generates a counter-intuitive prediction..." | Counter-intuitive finding explanation P2-P3 | Has it explained why it is counter-intuitive? |

**Risk reminder**: If Introduction promised 3 contributions, Discussion cannot discuss only 2; if construct differentiation was promised, Discussion must return to clarifying construct boundaries.

## Narrative Rhythm Guide

**Discussion's narrative role**: Denouement — fully unravel the knot.

1. **Return to start**: Briefly answer the research question (P1)
2. **Explain meaning**: Theoretical contributions (not restating results, but explaining what results mean) (P2-P4)
3. **Ground application**: Practical implications (translate abstract findings into concrete advice) (P5-P6)
4. **Face honestly**: Limitations (demonstrate academic honesty, enhance credibility) (P7)
5. **Look ahead**: Future research directions (convert limitations into opportunities) (P8)
6. **Elevated close**: Conclusion (show conversation has changed, stop at elevated plane) (P9)

**Paragraph length distribution**:
| Function | Recommended words | Length label |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Research question answer | 60-100 | Medium |
| Theoretical contribution (each) | 70-110 | Medium-long |
| Practical implication (each) | 50-90 | Medium |
| Limitations | 60-100 | Medium |
| Future research | 40-80 | Medium |
| Conclusion elevation | 40-70 | Short (most forceful) |

## Journal Style Preferences

| Journal | Discussion style | Signature feature |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **ASQ** | Theory-expansion | Discussion longer than Introduction; detailed theoretical contribution; prefers process-model theoretical implications |
| **SMJ** | Concise contribution | 2-3 contribution paragraphs sufficient; emphasizes precise literature conversation positioning; brief practical implications |
| **AMJ** | Multi-level implications | Micro-macro bridging theoretical implications; deep boundary-condition discussion |
| **OS** | Policy-sensitive | Detailed policy implications; emphasizes institutional implications of counter-intuitive findings |
| **JM** | Consumer-oriented | Practical implications focus on consumer welfare, brand management, marketing strategy |

## Universal Pitfalls (Pollock Ch08 Four Flaws)

1. **Rehashing results**: Discussion is not an extended Results section
2. **Superficial interpretations**: Every finding must rise to theoretical level
3. **Meandering**: Every paragraph must have a clear contribution focus
4. **Overreaching**: Contribution must match Introduction promise

**Other pitfalls**:
- Do not use "Interestingly" to introduce findings (reserved for JM)
- Do not push all limitations to future research (explain constraints on current interpretation)

## Paragraph Craft QC（说服体裁）

Discussion 是全文中说服体裁规则适用最充分的 section（Dunleavy 模型真正 fit 的位置）。完整规则见 `../../write-introduction/academic-writing-corpus/storytelling/prose-craft-checklist.md` §0.0/§0.1/§0.3/§0.6；本节列出 Discussion 语境的高优先级检查：

- [ ] **Claim-first**：每段段首句为本段理论主张（"These findings suggest that..." / "Our theory explains why..."），非证据或他人观点
- [ ] **Wrap 句存在**：每段段末有收束句把证据/解释上升为 claim，回扣研究问题或贡献声明；禁止段末停在引用或数据上（abrupt stop）
- [ ] **无作者名开头**：段首主语非 "Smith (2020)"——文献服务于本段 claim，不主导段首
- [ ] **无 caveat-first**：局限性内容集中在 P7；理论贡献段不以 "Although..." 让步开头
- [ ] **长度分布**：遵循上方 Paragraph length distribution 表；超出 §0.2 期刊区间（200–300 词）需检查是否多论点
- Do not only state contributions at Discussion end (contributions should run through Discussion)
- Do not ignore theoretical implications of non-significant / unexpected findings
