# Contribution Type Variants

Use this reference when the user specifies a contribution type.

## Standard Structure (all types share)

| Paragraph | Function | Recommended words | Required |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| P1 | Answer the research question (do not repeat Results) | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P2-P4 | Theoretical contributions (one per paragraph) | 70-110 each | ✅ |
| P5-P6 | Practical / policy implications | 50-90 each | ✅ |
| P7 | Limitations | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P8 | Future research | 40-80 | ✅ |
| P9 | Conclusion elevation (elevated plane) | 40-70 | ✅ |

## Variant A: Theory Integration

| Paragraph | Function | Recommended words | Required |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| P1 | Answer the research question | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P2 | Theory A contribution | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P3 | Theory B contribution | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P4 | Integrated framework contribution | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P5 | Practical implications | 50-90 | ✅ |
| P6 | Limitations | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P7 | Future research | 40-80 | ✅ |
| P8 | Conclusion elevation | 40-70 | ✅ |

**Key sentence frames**:
- Challenge dominant perspective: "Our study offers a new and opposing view of [construct/role]. Alongside the growth in..., research on this topic has ballooned in recent years but thus far has focused almost exclusively on... From this angle, some have concluded that..."
- Integration frame: "Thus while existing theory shows how... can [positive outcome] by [mechanism], our theory suggests that... can also [negative outcome] toward..., which may in turn trigger [consequence]."

**Source**: Zhou 2017, Keeves 2017

| P6 | Limitations | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P7 | Future research | 40-80 | ✅ |

**Key sentence frames**:
- Boundary condition claim: "We contribute to the literature on [topic] by highlighting the importance of..., identifying... as a key boundary condition for..."
- Theory refinement: "These findings are theoretically important because they offer a basis for assessing which [construct] has more value, particularly in [context]."

**Source**: Han 2020, Zhou 2017

## Variant C: Counter-Intuitive Finding

| Paragraph | Function | Recommended words | Required |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| P1 | Answer the research question | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P2 | Counter-intuitive finding theoretical explanation | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P3 | Mechanism deepening | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P4 | Theory revision | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P5 | Practical implications | 50-90 | ✅ |
| P6 | Limitations | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P7 | Future research | 40-80 | ✅ |
| P8 | Conclusion elevation | 40-70 | ✅ |

**Key sentence frames**:
- Counter-intuitive implication: "Our finding that [counter-intuitive result] is surprising because existing theory predicts [opposite]. We suggest that this unexpected pattern arises because [mechanism]. This implies that [theoretical implication]."
- Revise existing theory: "These findings suggest that the prevailing view of [phenomenon]—that [existing assumption]—may be incomplete. Instead, our results point to the importance of [new factor] in shaping [outcome]."

**Source**: Paruchuri 2020, Han 2024

## Variant D: Policy Implication

| Paragraph | Function | Recommended words | Required |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| P1 | Answer the research question | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P2 | Theoretical contribution | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P3 | Policy implication (specific) | 70-110 | ✅ |
| P4 | Manager / practitioner implication | 50-90 | ✅ |
| P5 | Limitations | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P6 | Future research | 40-80 | ✅ |
| P7 | Conclusion elevation | 40-70 | ✅ |

**Key sentence frames**:
- Policy recommendation: "For practice and public policy, our findings point to a need for [intervention/mechanism]. Specifically, policymakers should consider... because our results show that..."
- Manager implication: "Our findings also provide insights into how [Actor] should allocate their scarce resources and attention to build... Overall success could suffer if they focus on [wrong action] at [wrong time]."

**Source**: Wu 2025, Eilert 2017, DesJardine 2023
