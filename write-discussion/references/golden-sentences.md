# Golden Sentences and Practical Implications

Use this reference for reusable contribution sentence frames and practical-implication templates.

## Golden Contribution Sentences

**"Thus while... our..." frame (classic contrast)**
> "Thus while existing theory shows how [X] can [positive outcome] by [mechanism], our theory suggests that [X] can also [negative outcome] toward [target], which may in turn trigger [consequence]."
> — Source: Keeves 2017

**"Shifting the focus from... to..." frame**
> "The findings contribute to [literature] by shifting the focus from [dominant perspective] to [alternative perspective]. Unlike most research that [limitation], our study explores [new focus]."
> — Source: Wu 2025

**"We offer a different view by developing a '[new perspective name]'" frame**
> "We offer a different view by developing a '[new perspective name],' which reasons that [core logic]."
> — Source: DesJardine 2023

## Practical Implications: Concretization Principle

**Theory-to-practice conversion template**:
> "Our findings provide insights into how [specific actor] should [specific action] to [specific outcome]. For example, [concrete scenario]."

**Key characteristics**:
- Specific actor (not "managers," but "CEOs of young VC-backed firms")
- Specific decision (not "be careful," but "allocate resources to reputation building before status")
- Consequences of wrong action (not "it is important," but "overall success could suffer if...")

**Case grounding template**:
> "[Company]'s early days provide a useful illustration of the relationships we identified."
> — Source: Pollock 2015
