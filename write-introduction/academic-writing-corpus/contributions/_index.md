# Contribution Statements — Introduction 贡献声明句法

## 功能描述

P7-P8 的功能：明确回答 "so what"，告诉读者这项研究对文献、实践或政策有什么价值。贡献声明不是谦虚地罗列，而是**自信地定位**——说明研究填补了哪个缺口、连接了哪些文献、改变了什么理解。

---

## 变体 G：三层递进型（haunschild2015 型）

**模板**:
> "As a result, if we find that [actors] do [predicted behavior], our model will provide the foundation for a phenomenon that is not well explained by existing theory. Predicting [specific shifts] as a function of [antecedent] extends our knowledge of how [actors] [process A] and then [process B]. Showing that these shifts in [foci] subsequently affect [outcome 1] and [outcome 2] indicates the consequential nature of this phenomenon."

**原文锚定**:
> "As a result, if we find that firms do oscillate between learning (toward safety) and forgetting (away from safety), our model will provide the foundation for a phenomenon that is not well explained by existing theory. Predicting shifts between competing foci as a function of serious errors extends our knowledge of how organizations learn and then forget. Showing that these shifts in foci subsequently affect innovation and future organizational errors indicates the consequential nature of this phenomenon."

**来源**: haunschild2015 (OS), P8

**关键特征**:
- **条件性开头**: "if we find that..." —— 谦逊但自信，避免过度承诺
- **第一层（现象）**: "provide the foundation for a phenomenon not well explained by existing theory" → Makadok Phenomenon
- **第二层（知识）**: "extends our knowledge of how..." → Makadok Mechanism
- **第三层（后果）**: "indicates the consequential nature" → Makadok Output
- 三层之间形成"发现→扩展→后果"的逻辑递进

**适用**: 多维贡献（Mechanism + Phenomenon + Output）的实证研究

**反模式提醒**:
- 空洞的"important" → 用三层具体化替代
- 过度承诺 → 使用谦逊措辞（"foundation", "extends"）而非绝对化措辞
- 缺少后果层 → 审稿人会问"so what?"

## 变体 A：单句维度定位型（Makadok 型）

**最精简形式**，一句话锚定贡献维度。适用于贡献聚焦、不需要展开的研究。

| 维度 | 声明句式 |
|------|---------|
| **Constructs** | "We clarify [construct] by distinguishing [A] from [B], resolving confusion in the literature." |
| **Mechanism** | "We explain why [X] affects [Y] by identifying [mechanism] that translates [cause] into [outcome]." |
| **Boundary** | "We show that [relationship] depends on [moderator], reconciling conflicting findings." |
| **Phenomenon** | "We document a novel phenomenon: [phenomenon], and demonstrate its importance for [outcome]." |
| **Level** | "We bridge [level A] and [level B] by showing how [micro] aggregates to [macro]." |
| **Mode** | "We reveal how [process] unfolds over time, showing that [pattern] rather than [alternative]." |
| **Question** | "We ask a new question: [question]? And answer it by [approach]." |
| **Output** | "We provide a [tool/typology/framework] that enables [actors] to [action]." |

**来源**: Makadok et al. (2011, SMJ) + 跨论文验证

**适用**: 贡献单一明确、篇幅受限的 Introduction

## 变体 B：文献对话型（han2020 型）

**模板**:
> "We contribute to [literature 1] by [contribution 1]. [Authority] has argued that [insight]... We address these limitations by [specific contribution]. Further, our theory and findings offer new insights into [literature 2]."

**原文锚定**:
> "We contribute to the literatures on status and organizational misconduct by highlighting the importance of the context surrounding a focal firm's transgression... [citation] has argued that most theories give contextual factors too little consideration... We address these limitations by suggesting that past transgressors' status and categorical proximity... are important yet overlooked contextual factors... Further, our theory and findings offer new insights into how audiences combine status and categories in their evaluations."

**来源**: han2020 (AMJ), P5

**关键特征**:
- 明确命名贡献所对话的文献流（通常 1-2 个）
- 引用权威观点建立对话合法性
- "We address these limitations by" 将贡献与缺口精确对应
- "Further, our theory and findings offer new insights into" 展示溢出贡献

**适用**: 研究横跨两个文献流、需要展示对话能力的情境

## 变体 C：过程模型型（lashley_pollock2020 型）

**模板**:
> "We contribute to [literature] by developing a process model of [phenomenon] that considers [nuance]. We explore [aspect 1], [aspect 2], and [aspect 3]. We also examine how [related process] may differ when [condition]."

**原文锚定**:
> "We contribute to the stigma literature by developing a process model of category-level core stigma reduction that considers how to remove as opposed to manage a deeply engrained stigma. We explore the coordinated but conflicted nature of this process, how resource considerations can lead firms to continue privately engaging in stigmatized behaviors that are publicly denounced, and the separation required for this to occur. We also examine how category emergence may be used to integrate the actions of industry actors... and how the category emergence process may differ when the core product is stigmatized."

**来源**: lashley_pollock2020 (ASQ), P6

**关键特征**:
- "developing a process model of" → 明确理论形式
- 用 "We explore..." 列举模型涵盖的多个方面
- "We also examine how... may differ when" → 增加边界条件意识

**适用**: 质性研究、过程理论、多案例比较研究

## 变体 D：列表展开型（malshe2015 型）

**模板**:
> "We contribute to the [field] literature in several ways. First, we [contribution 1]. Second, we [contribution 2]. Third, we [contribution 3]."

**来源**: malshe2015 (JM), P4

**关键特征**:
- 明确预告"several ways"，让读者有预期
- 用 First / Second / Third 结构清晰
- 每个贡献不宜超过一句话

**适用**: 贡献维度较多（3+）、需要逐项展示的研究

**反模式提醒**: 不要列举 5+ 个贡献，每个只有一行——贡献散弹是审稿人最常批评的问题。聚焦 2-3 个核心贡献，每个充分展开。

## 变体 E：三段闭环型（darby2026 型）

**模板**:
> "Research on [field] and [phenomenon] has largely been limited to *[internal/domestic/established]* [factor type], such as [example 1] ([citation]) and [example 2] ([citation]). This is a notable shortcoming because [one study in field] ([citation]) as well as a larger body of work in neighboring fields (e.g., [citations]) suggest that *[external/foreign/emerging]* [factor type] might influence [field] phenomena such as [phenomenon]. Conducting research along this pathway allows us to build understanding about [research question echo]. This is important because, as [citation] note, '[quote about missing critical agents]' ([citation]). For practice, [practical actor] are wary of [phenomenon], but our findings highlight an important benefit: [benefit]. For public policy, our findings may help [policy body] better allocate its limited [resources] by identifying factors that influence [outcome]."

**原文锚定**:
> "Research on corporate governance and product recalls has largely been limited to *internal* governance factors, such as female board representation (Wowak et al. 2021) and chief executive officer (CEO) stock ownership (Darby et al. 2023). This is a notable shortcoming because one operations and supply chain management (OSCM) study (Cheung et al. 2020) as well as a larger body of work in neighboring fields (e.g., Connelly et al. 2019; Lewellen and Lewellen 2022; Helmuth et al. 2023) suggest that *external* governance factors might influence OSCM phenomena such as product recalls. Conducting research along this pathway allows us to build understanding about what—or who—might encourage more timely recalls. This is important because, as Bendoly and Oliva (2024, 520) note, 'If we fail to consider at least some of the critical operational agents and pathways in a given process, then we are missing out on some critical opportunities for informing our discipline.' For practice, many executives are wary of the increased monitoring that large institutional investors bring, but our findings highlight an important benefit of this pressure: faster recalls. For public policy, our findings may help the FDA better allocate its limited monitoring resources by identifying factors that influence how quickly—or slowly—companies recall medical devices with dangerous defects."

**来源**: darby2026 (JOM), P10

**关键特征**:
- 首句用 "largely been limited to" 明确缺口，斜体强调对比
- "This is a notable shortcoming because" 建立缺口的严重性
- 用 "one... as well as a larger body of work" 平衡领域内外证据
- "allows us to build understanding about..." 回应急迫感段落的问题（**回声效应**）
- 学科引语为 "why this matters to the discipline" 提供权威背书
- **三段式贡献**：理论（填补缺口）→ 实践（wary but benefit）→ 政策（资源配置）

**适用**: 跨学科引入外部因素的研究；有明确政策/实践含义的研究

## 变体 F：发现预览+贡献混合型（paruchuri2020 型）

**模板**:
> "In this study we test these assumptions and explore how [construct 1] intersects with [construct 2] to influence [mechanism] and create [outcome], and how long [outcome] persist. We use a natural experiment to study [specific setting]. We show that [main finding 1], and that [main finding 2]. We also develop theory to explain when [nuanced outcome]. This study makes several contributions."

**来源**: paruchuri2020 (SMJ), P5-P6

**关键特征**:
- 发现预览与贡献声明放在同一段，先展示发现再声明贡献
- "test these assumptions" 回收前文的假设解构
- 以 "This study makes several contributions" 作为段落收束和贡献预告

**适用**: 发现预览与贡献紧密相连、不需要独立展开的研究

## 附加技巧

### 技巧 1：为新构念创造标签（mannor2016 型）

当研究的核心机制需要一个简洁、易记的标签时，在提出假设的同时创造并命名它。

**句式**:
> "We build on these ideas to hypothesize that [construct] will [effect] by [mechanism], a dynamic we refer to as '[new label]'. In this sense, our theory addresses recent calls for research examining [open question] ([citation])."

**来源**: mannor2016 (SMJ), P4

**关键特征**:
- 标签简洁（如 "social buffering" = 两个词）
- 立即将该标签与已有文献呼吁联系起来
- 标签最好借用或改编自已有心理学/社会学概念（如 Cohen & McKay 1984 的 "buffering hypothesis"）

---

### 技巧 2：方法论合法性建立（mannor2016 型）

当使用非传统/多方法数据收集时，将方法选择框定为回应领域批评，并引用权威引语背书。

**句式**:
> "Our [method description] afforded us a unique opportunity to test our ideas. Data sources included [source 1]; [source 2]; [source 3]; and [source 4]. This comprehensive approach reflects our attempt at overcoming a common criticism of [field] studies: [criticism] (e.g., [citation]). Addressing this shortcoming, [authority] noted that '[quote]' ([year]: [page]). We believe our study represents a significant step in this direction."

**来源**: mannor2016 (SMJ), P7

**关键特征**:
- 多个数据来源的并列展示
- "overcoming a common criticism" → 将方法选择框定为回应批评
- 引用权威引语为方法论定位背书
- "significant step in this direction" → 谦逊但自信的贡献声明

---

### 技巧 3："暗面"叙事框架（vadakkepatt2022 型）

当研究揭示某行为的负面后果（而该行为通常被认为是有益的），使用"dark side"叙事框架组织贡献声明。

**句式结构**:
> "First, we extend [theory] to reveal that otherwise-beneficial firm actions ([phenomenon]) can simultaneously harm [outcome]. This view helps augment shortcomings in extant theoretical frameworks (i.e., [existing theory]) for explaining how [mechanism] reduce[s] the necessary focus on [stakeholder], with detrimental effects for [outcome 1] and [outcome 2]. Second, we offer solutions in the form of a set of theoretically informed, managerially relevant moderators that can [action] and thereby attenuate the negative effects of [phenomenon] on [outcome]. Third, we detail how the negative effects emerge, by showcasing a key pathway leading to [mechanism]."

**来源**: vadakkepatt2022 (JM), P6

**关键特征**:
- **三段式暗面叙事**: 理论扩展（揭示负面）→ 管理解决方案（调节变量）→ 机制路径（如何发生）
- "otherwise-beneficial firm actions... can simultaneously harm" → 核心张力：同一行为的双重效应
- "augment shortcomings in extant theoretical frameworks" → 将负面发现框定为理论完善而非否定
- "offer solutions" → 暗面研究必须提供管理出路，否则审稿人会问"那怎么办"

**适用**: CSR、创新、全球化、数字化转型、政治关联等"看起来好但可能有害"的研究主题

**反模式提醒**:
- 不要只展示暗面而不提供解决方案——审稿人会质疑研究的管理价值
- 不要过度否定已有文献——"augment shortcomings" 比 "fundamentally flawed" 更安全

---

### 技巧 4：调节变量系统分类（vadakkepatt2022 型）

当研究需要引入多个调节变量时，与其简单罗列，不如用理论驱动的二元分类框架组织它们。

**句式**:
> "Accordingly, we predict that [N] moderators might effectively channel [actor] attention toward [target]: [moderator 1], [moderator 2], [moderator 3], and [moderator 4]. These [N] moderators work as [category A] and/or [category B] mechanisms. [Category A] mechanisms ensure consistency between [priority 1] and [priority 2]... [Category B] mechanisms work to offset [actor] attention on one strategic priority by redirecting focus to another..."

**来源**: vadakkepatt2022 (JM), P4

**关键特征**:
- 先列出所有调节变量，再用理论框架分类
- 两个分类必须有明确的理论边界（如 Aligning vs Counterbalancing）
- 每个分类必须有定义 + 机制解释 + 具体例子
- 分类框架必须来自核心理论的前提（如 ABV 的"资源与规则配置"）

**适用**: 需要系统引入 3+ 个调节变量的研究；调节变量可以从理论上分为不同机制类型的研究

**反模式提醒**:
- 不要为了分类而分类——分类必须反映真实的理论差异
- 不要让某个分类只有1个调节变量——至少2个才能构成"类"

---

### 技巧 5：双代码/双逻辑对比定义（lehman2014 型）

当研究涉及两个竞争性标准或逻辑时（如科学 vs 传统、效率 vs 合法性、全球 vs 本地），用**代码类型学**重新框定问题，而非简单罗列两个因素。

**句式结构**:
> "On the one hand, [actor A] were invoking an [code type A] that relied on [basis A] to [purpose A]. Per [theory], we regard such [codes of type A] as default codes in [context], codes that are taken for granted and invoked automatically for [audience]. On the other hand, [actor B] were acting on an [code type B] that was embedded in [basis B]..."

**来源**: lehman2014 (MS), P7-P8

**关键特征**:
1. **给每个逻辑一个名称**（imperative vs interpretative / default vs activated）
2. **定义每个代码的来源**（科学理性 vs 文化传统 / 法律环境 vs 文化环境）
3. **指出哪个是默认/自动的，哪个是情境激活的**
4. **说明冲突如何产生**（adhering to one code guarantees the violation of the other）

**适用**: 制度冲突、规范碰撞、双重标准、跨文化张力、多重制度逻辑的研究

**反模式提醒**:
- 不要为了创造对立而强行二分——两个代码必须有真实的理论或经验差异
- 不要只命名不定义——每个代码必须有明确的来源、功能和激活条件
- 不要让默认代码和激活代码的区分模糊不清——这是整个框架的分析基础

### 变体 H：命名机制 + 力量分类法（kim2022 型）

**模板**:
> "The analysis reveals [N] primary forces that drive [actors'] [core decision]. The first, termed *[Force 1]*, plays a role when [condition A]. This force drives the firm to [strategy 1]. However, when [condition B], a *[Force 2]* effect kicks in, prompting the firm to [strategy 2]. Interacting with these two countervailing forces is [contextual factor]. If [condition C], an *[Force 3]* stimulates [behavior C]. However, when [condition D], a *[Force 4]* is also present and reflects [motivation D]."

**来源**: kim2022 (MS), P8

**原文锚定**:
> "The analysis reveals four primary forces that drive firms' testing and launch-timing decisions. The first, termed *recall avoidance*, plays a role when the probability of new product failure is high, or the costs associated with a recall are large. This force drives the firm to 'play it safe' and delay the launch to ensure its innovation is fail-proof. However, when the potential consequences of a recall are limited and consumer benefits from the innovation are large, a *market pre-emption* effect kicks in, prompting the firm to 'take on the risk' of skipping rigorous testing..."

**关键特征**:
- **命名仪式**: "termed *X*" 宣告作者正在创造新的理论语言——每个力量名称成为论文的知识产权
- **先触发条件再驱动方向**: 'plays a role when [condition]' → 'This force drives the firm to [action]'——每个力量是可检验的命题奠基石
- **力与力的互动**: 用 "Interacting with these... / However, when..." 连接——不是静态列表，而是动态系统
- **功能描述性命名**: 名称遵循功能描述逻辑（recall avoidance = 回避召回）而非花哨隐喻——非专业读者也能从名称推断功能

**适用**: 适用于分析模型论文、理论论文——有多个可清晰分离的理论机制；特别适合 MS、ASQ、AMR、SMJ 等接受理论贡献呈现为 "机制命名" 的期刊

**禁忌**: "力量数量应为 3-5 个（过少显得 trivial，过多让读者记忆负担过重）；名称必须是机制功能描述而非隐喻（'recall avoidance' 优于 'the ostrich effect'）；每个力量必须在后文的分析中有对应的 formal treatment——如果某力只在 Introduction 中出现一次，审稿人会质疑其必要性"

## 变体 J：Challenge Implicit Assumption 型（zhao-ding_gaba 型）

**模板**:
> "Our paper makes [N] contributions. First, we contribute to the emerging research on [field] ([citations]) by theorizing and testing antecedents of [phenomenon]. In doing so, our study challenges an implicit premise that [commonly held assumption]. Although prior work often assumes that [assumption] ([citations]), we show that [key finding that contradicts assumption]."

**原文锚定**:
> "Our paper makes two contributions. First, we contribute to the emerging research on positioning strategies in digital markets (Brynjolfsson et al. 2003, Miller and Wang 2024, Benner 2025) by theorizing and testing antecedents of heterogeneous positions. In doing so, our study challenges an implicit premise that lower entry barriers mechanically translate into greater product variety. Although prior work often assumes that entrants in digital markets expand product variety by targeting heterogeneous customer preferences (Boudreau 2012, Waldfogel 2017), we show that novelty in positioning varies systematically with external market feedback."

**来源**: zhao-ding_gaba (ORSC), P7

**关键特征**:
- **"challenges an implicit premise that..."** → 将贡献定位为纠正领域内未被言明的假设（而非填补空白）
- **"Although prior work often assumes that... we show that..."** → 假设-反证结构，比 "few studies have examined" 更有力
- 贡献声明的攻击性适中——挑战的是 "implicit premise" 而非 "prior research is wrong"
- 适合 Constructs + Phenomenon 组合——当论文引入新概念框架后，旧框架的隐含假设自然暴露

**适用**: Incompleteness (主导) + mild Inadequacy 混合型 Gap；Constructs 或 Phenomenon 贡献维度

**禁忌**:
- 不要用于纯 Incompleteness Gap——"challenges an implicit premise" 暗示了 Inadequacy 元素
- "implicit premise" 必须确实存在于文献中且可被引用支撑——不能 strawman

## 变体 K：贡献前置型（Contribution-Fronted，kalaignanam2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Our study makes [N] contributions to [field] theory and practice. First, our study is one of the first to test [specific relationship]. [Brief literature context + specific gap]. Second, our study tests [specific distinction] and contributes to prior research in important ways. [Prior research limitation]. Our study responds to this call. We distinguish between [dimension A] and [dimension B] in an effort to provide a more complete picture of [consequences]. Third, our study contributes to conversations in prior research about [trade-offs inherent in choices]. [Prior research on trade-off]. Our study contributes to this stream by showing [specific finding direction]."

**原文锚定**:
> "Our study makes three contributions to marketing theory and practice. First, our study is one of the first to test the impact of NPD make/buy decisions on product quality. A small but emerging body of literature in marketing has investigated issues related to firm–supplier relationships... However, to the best of our knowledge, no study has examined the product quality effects of both NPD modes... Second, our study tests the impact of NPD make/buy choices on immediate and future product quality... Moorman and Day (2016) note that the emphasis in prior research has been on understanding factors that enhance the effectiveness of NPD buy decisions, and they call for more research on this topic. Our study responds to this call... Third, our study contributes to conversations in prior research about the trade-offs inherent in the NPD make/buy choices..."

**来源**: kalaignanam2017 (JM), P3-P5

**关键特征**:
- **贡献前置**：在 Preview/Methods 之前详细展开三段贡献声明
- **每个贡献引用具体文献**：不是泛泛声明，而是回应对话
- **第二/三贡献中包含"结果预告"**：提前暗示发现方向（differential quality effects）
- **"responds to this call"**：将贡献与文献呼吁精确对应

**适用场景**:
- 实证发现复杂、需要提前预告的研究
- 多维度贡献（≥3 个）需要逐项展开的研究
- 营销/运营类期刊（JM, JOM, MSOM）偏好管理相关性前置

**风险与禁忌**:
- **风险**：Contribution 前置要求每个贡献都有充分文献支撑，否则审稿人会质疑"为什么现在就说贡献"
- **禁忌**：不要用于纯理论论文——理论论文应在 Theory 部分展示贡献，而非 Introduction 中提前承诺
- **禁忌**：不要预告无法兑现的发现——结果预告必须与 Results 完全一致

**与标准结构的对比**:

| 维度 | 标准结构 | 贡献前置型 |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 顺序 | Hook → Lit Turn → Tension → Stakes → Theory Lens → Preview → Contribution | Hook → Tension → Contribution → Preview |
| Contribution 位置 | 倒数第 1-2 段 | 第 3-5 段 |
| Stakes 处理 | 独立段落 | 嵌入 Contribution |
| Theory Lens 处理 | 独立段落 | 嵌入 Tension/Contribution |
| 适用期刊 | AMJ, ASQ, OS | JM, JOM, SMJ |
| 风险等级 | 低 | 中（需要充分文献支撑）|

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 贡献声明必须与前文的 Gap 精确对应：Gap 说"漏了什么"，贡献说"我们补了什么"
- 贡献声明必须与 SKILL.md 中的 Makadok 贡献维度声明句式一致
- 贡献声明必须被 Discussion 兑现——`write-discussion` 会检查此对齐

### 反模式提醒
- **不要过度承诺**: 用 "extend""refine""reconcile""clarify" 替代 "revolutionize""first to"
- **不要贡献散弹**: 列举 5+ 个贡献，每个只有一行 → 聚焦 2-3 个，每个充分展开
- **不要让贡献与 Gap 脱节**: Gap 说 Incompleteness，贡献不能说 "we overturn"
- **不要忽略实践/政策含义**: 除非目标期刊是纯理论期刊（如 ASR），否则至少提及一个实践含义

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[field extension]` — 文献流拓展

❌ "This study contributes to the literature on political connections, innovation, and resource allocation." → 三个文献流各只提了名字，没有说拓展了什么——贡献散弹

✅ "We extend the political connections literature by showing that resource acquisition — the very mechanism presumed to drive political ties' benefits — can undermine innovation when it distorts allocation efficiency. For the resource allocation literature, we introduce political connections as a novel antecedent of misallocation, extending a framework previously applied to internal governance." → 每个文献流 + 具体拓展点，2-3 句充分展开

### `[Makadok dimension]` — 贡献维度声明

❌ "We contribute to theory by examining the role of political ties in innovation." → 没有 Makadok 维度——"examining the role" 可以是 Mechanism/Boundary/Phenomenon 任意一个

✅ "We explain why political connections affect firm innovation by identifying resource allocation efficiency — rather than resource acquisition volume — as the mediating mechanism." → 明确 Mechanism 贡献，"explain why... by identifying [mechanism]"
