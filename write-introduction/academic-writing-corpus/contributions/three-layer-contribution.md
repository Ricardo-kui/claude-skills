# Contribution: Three-Layer Contribution / 三层递进贡献声明

## 功能描述

将贡献声明组织为**三层递进结构**：
1. **现象层**: 为未被解释的现象提供理论基础
2. **知识层**: 扩展已有理论（预测/解释特定机制）
3. **后果层**: 展示发现的具体后果/影响

这是比单句 Makadok 定位更丰富的贡献声明形式，适用于**Mechanism + Phenomenon + Output** 多维贡献。三层之间形成"发现→扩展→后果"的逻辑递进。

## 适用场景

- Contribution 维度涉及**多个 Makadok 维度**（如 Mechanism + Phenomenon + Output）
- 研究揭示了**新的循环/动态现象**且该现象有**明确后果**
- 目标期刊: OS, ASQ, AMJ, SMJ

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (1 paper): haunschild2015 (OS)
- **可迁移性验证**: 三层结构（现象→知识→后果）通用性强

### 生成力
- **ADAPTABLE**: 可填入任何具有多维贡献的研究

### 排他性
- **LOW**: 适用于绝大多数实证研究

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：条件发现 + 三层递进（haunschild2015 型）

> "As a result, if we find that [actors] do [predicted behavior], our model will provide the foundation for a phenomenon that is not well explained by existing theory. Predicting [specific shifts] as a function of [antecedent] extends our knowledge of how [actors] [process A] and then [process B]. Showing that these shifts in [foci] subsequently affect [outcome 1] and [outcome 2] indicates the consequential nature of this phenomenon."

**来源**: haunschild2015 (OS), P8

**原文锚定**:
> "As a result, if we find that firms do oscillate between learning (toward safety) and forgetting (away from safety), our model will provide the foundation for a phenomenon that is not well explained by existing theory. Predicting shifts between competing foci as a function of serious errors extends our knowledge of how organizations learn and then forget. Showing that these shifts in foci subsequently affect innovation and future organizational errors indicates the consequential nature of this phenomenon."

**关键特征**:
1. **条件性开头**: "if we find that..." —— 谦逊但自信，避免过度承诺
2. **第一层（现象）**: "provide the foundation for a phenomenon not well explained by existing theory" → Makadok **Phenomenon**
3. **第二层（知识）**: "extends our knowledge of how..." → Makadok **Mechanism**
4. **第三层（后果）**: "indicates the consequential nature" → Makadok **Output**
5. **无 "we contribute by..."**: 不使用显性贡献标签，而是通过三层递进让读者自行推断贡献维度

## 与 Makadok 贡献维度的映射

| 层次 | 核心动词 | 对应 Makadok 维度 | 替代表达 |
|------|---------|------------------|---------|
| 现象层 | "provide the foundation for" | Phenomenon | "document a novel pattern", "identify a recurring cycle" |
| 知识层 | "extends our knowledge of" | Mechanism | "explain why", "illuminate the process by which" |
| 后果层 | "indicates the consequential nature" | Output | "demonstrates the impact on", "reveals implications for" |

---

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 Preview 中的发现预告配对**: 三层贡献必须与 Preview 中的假设/发现方向一致
- **与 Gap 精确对应**: 第一层对应 Gap（"未被解释的现象"），第二层对应 Mechanism（"如何发生"），第三层对应 Stakes/后果

### 互斥
- **不能与过度承诺同用**: "provide the foundation" 是谦逊表达，不能与 "first to" / "revolutionize" 等过度承诺并存
- **不能与单一贡献同用**: 若研究只有一个 Makadok 维度，三层结构会显得空洞

---

## 反模式提醒

- **空洞的"important"**: "This study is important" 是 generic 表达。Haunschild 用三层具体化（phenomenon→knowledge→consequence），避免了"important"的空洞感。
- **过度承诺**: "provide the foundation for" ≠ "fully explain"。使用谦逊措辞（"foundation", "extends"）而非绝对化措辞（"resolves", "definitively proves"）。
- **缺少后果层**: 如果贡献声明只到"extends our knowledge"，审稿人会问"so what?"第三层后果是必需的。

## 变体与扩展

### 变体 B：四段式贡献（加入实践/政策层）

在三层基础上增加第四层（实践/政策）：
> "For practice, these findings suggest that [managerial implication]. For public policy, [policy implication]."

来源: darby2026 (JOM)

### 变体 D：Makadok 多维度 + 政策 stakes 型（darby2023 型）

**模板**:
> "Our study provides theoretical contributions for scholars and practical implications for [audience 1] and [audience 2]. For researchers, we contribute to [stream 1] by demonstrating how [X] influences [Y]. In doing so, we demonstrate how [theoretical implication] and thereby [theoretical network enrichment]. We also contribute to [stream 2] by highlighting [key role]. For practice and public policy, our findings point to a need for [practical recommendation]."

**来源**: darby2023 (MSOM), P8

**原文锚定**:
> "Our study provides theoretical contributions for scholars and practical implications for firms and regulators. For researchers, we contribute to the corporate governance literature by demonstrating how CEO stock ownership influences recall timing. In doing so, we demonstrate how governance mechanisms can shape operational outcomes and thereby extend the governance literature beyond traditional financial metrics. We also contribute to the operations management literature by highlighting the role of executive incentives in product safety. For practice and public policy, our findings point to a need for regulators to consider the incentive structures of firm executives when designing recall policies."

**关键特征**:
- **双文献流贡献**: 明确命名两个对话的文献流（stream 1 + stream 2）
- **"In doing so, we demonstrate how... and thereby..."** → 第一层贡献的理论溢出效应
- **"We also contribute to..."** → 第二层贡献，用 "also" 自然过渡
- **"For practice and public policy"** → 将实践和政策合并为一段，简洁有力
- **"point to a need for"** → 政策建议的谦逊但明确表达

**适用**: 跨学科研究（如治理+运营管理）、有明确政策含义的研究

**与变体 B 的区别**: 变体 B 是"理论→实践→政策"三段式，变体 D 是"理论（双文献流）→实践/政策"两段式，更紧凑

### 变体 C：列表式贡献（malshe2015 型）

若贡献维度较多（3+），可用列表：
> "We contribute to the [field] literature in several ways. First... Second... Third..."

来源: malshe2015 (JM)

**注意**: 三层递进型 vs 列表式选择的判断标准：
- 若贡献维度之间有**逻辑递进关系**（现象→机制→后果），用三层递进
- 若贡献维度之间是**并列关系**（理论贡献 + 方法贡献 + 实践贡献），用列表式

### 变体 F：多文献流并列贡献 + 反直觉修正（kundro_rothbard 型）

**模板**:
> "By exploring [core research question]—and understanding [specific insight]—we provide important theoretical contributions. First, we contribute to research on [stream 1] by [specific finding], showing that [nuanced conclusion]. Instead, we suggest and show that [intervention/remedy] is a more effective antidote because [mechanism]. Second, we contribute to research on [stream 2] by demonstrating that [mechanism] plays a key role in explaining the relationship between [X] and [Y], extending [prior theory] ([citations]). Third, we contribute to theories of [stream 3] by demonstrating that, counter to past assumptions, [counter-intuitive finding]."

**来源**: kundro_rothbard (AMJ), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "By exploring whether female moral objectors, relative to males, are protected by power—and understanding specific antidotes that can mitigate this potential problem—we provide important theoretical contributions. First, we contribute to research on gender and power by systematically investigating the interaction between them, showing that power does not automatically protect female moral objectors from retaliation. Instead, we suggest and show that moral objection framing is a more effective antidote because organizational frames do not violate gender role expectations and thus can reduce retaliation. Second, we contribute to research on moral objection by demonstrating that self-control perceptions play a key role in explaining the relationship between moral objection and retaliation, extending threat-based accounts of moral objection. Third, we contribute to theories of organizational ethics by demonstrating that, counter to past assumptions, power does not shield all moral objectors, particularly women, from retaliation."

**关键特征**:
1. **导语**: "By exploring... and understanding... we provide important theoretical contributions" — 以研究问题+洞察作为贡献导语
2. **文献流明确命名**: 每个贡献点都明确指向一个具体文献流（gender and power / moral objection / organizational ethics）
3. **反直觉修正**: 第一点用 "Instead, we suggest and show that..." 给出与直觉/文献假设相反的修正
4. **机制扩展**: 第二点用 "extending [prior theory]" 明确标注理论扩展对象
5. **反直觉声明**: 第三点用 "counter to past assumptions" 明确标注反直觉贡献
6. **无实践层**: 纯理论贡献声明，无 managerial implications（留到 Discussion）

**适用**: AMJ/ASQ 等偏好紧凑理论贡献的期刊；有多文献流对话需求的研究

**与变体 C（malshe2015 型）的区别**: 变体 C 是通用列表，变体 F 要求每个贡献点都明确命名文献流并包含具体理论动作（showing/extending/demonstrating）

### 变体 E — Policy-Closure Contribution (Mayo et al. POMS)

**模板**:
> "This study contributes to [stream 1] in the context of [stream 2], by showing that [main finding]. Furthermore, to the best of our knowledge, this study is the first to show that [boundary contribution]. Understanding [boundary variable] leads to specific [policy type] suggestions. Specifically, we provide [policy output] for [regulatory body]."

**来源**: Mayo et al. POMS

**关键特征**:
- **双文献流贡献**: 明确命名两个对话的文献流（stream 1 + stream 2），用 "in the context of" 连接
- **边界贡献**: "to the best of our knowledge, this study is the first to show that..." 谦逊但明确的首创声明
- **政策闭合**: 从理论发现直接推导到具体政策建议，形成 "发现→边界→政策" 的三段闭合
- **政策受众明确**: 具体指明政策输出面向的监管主体

**适用**: 有明确政策含义的跨学科研究；需要向监管/政策制定者传递具体建议的研究

**与变体 D（darby2023 型）的区别**: 变体 D 是"理论（双文献流）→实践/政策"两段式，更紧凑；变体 E 是"理论→边界→政策"三段式，更强调边界条件对政策的约束作用
