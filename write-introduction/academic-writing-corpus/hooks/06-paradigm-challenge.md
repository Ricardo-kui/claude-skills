# 06-paradigm-challenge — 范式挑战 Hook

## 功能描述

直接挑战读者默认接受的理论范式或广泛共识。不是"文献有缺口"，而是"文献赖以成立的基础假设可能是错的"。这是 Introduction 中最强能量的 Hook 类型，暗示读者需要重新思考他们以为已知的东西。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incommensurability**（不同理论/证据推出不兼容解释）
- 已有广泛接受的理论共识（如代理理论、homology 理论、distinction 理论）
- 实证证据与理论预测系统性地矛盾
- 目标期刊偏好理论驱动型开场（ASQ、ASR、AMJ、SMJ）

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **ROBUST** (≥6 papers): 在 ASQ (zhou2017, keeves2017, pontikes2012), ASR (hahl2017), AMJ (kundro2023), SMJ (gamache2023) 中独立出现
- 跨越不同研究领域：公司治理、组织行为、文化社会学、性别研究

### 生成力
- **GENERATIVE**: 可在战略管理、组织理论、经济社会学等不同领域生成有效变体

### 排他性
- **HIGH**: 几乎只在 Incommensurability 中出现，选择此 Hook 向读者发送"范式挑战"信号

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：理论共识 → 反例颠覆（gamache2023 型）

**模板**:
> "[Domain] research echoes this view, with several studies finding that [group A] are [behavior] than [group B]. Although the theoretical mechanisms proposed differ, the collective findings suggest [consensus]. However, we argue that [criticism of oversimplification]."

**来源**: gamache2023 (SMJ), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "Upper echelons research echoes this view, with several studies finding that female top executives are less likely to engage in risky endeavors than male top executives. Although the theoretical mechanisms proposed to motivate such divergent behavior differ, the collective findings suggest that female top executives tend to favor more conservative strategic actions than their male counterparts. However, we argue that conceptualizing female CEOs as universally conservative decision-makers may paint too simplistic a picture."

**关键特征**:
- "research echoes this view" → 承认共识存在
- "Although... the collective findings suggest" → 概括共识
- "However, we argue that... may paint too simplistic a picture" → 精准挑战，不攻击学者

---

### 变体 B：经典理论建立 → 当代颠覆（hahl2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Classic [discipline] treatments depict [phenomenon] as [classic view]. The most famous statements may be found in [Classic A]'s observations on [topic A] and in [Classic B]'s argument that [topic B]. But a large body of [discipline] research since [decade] has shattered this image."

**来源**: hahl2017 (ASR), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "Classic sociological treatments depict cultural consumption as homologous with socioeconomic status. The most famous statements of this idea may be found in Weber's observations on how different status groups 'express' their positions by enacting a particular 'style of life,' and in Bourdieu's argument that elite socialization produces sophisticated consumers whose discourse and practices mark them off from lower classes. But a large body of sociological research since the 1990s has shattered this image of homology between status and expressed taste."

**关键特征**:
- 点名经典理论家（Weber, Bourdieu）建立智识权威
- "has shattered this image" → 强烈的颠覆性动词
- 适用于需要与学科经典对话的理论论文

---

### 变体 C：效率逻辑建立 → 现实反驳（zhou2017 型）

**模板**:
> "According to the conventional, [theory]-based view, mostly rooted in [theory], [phenomenon] plays a minor role in [outcome]. Because [actor] are governed by [logic] rather than [alternative logic], [intervention] is unavoidable, and [consequence] hinders [outcome]. In reality, however, many [actor] in [context] have evolved into [positive label], rather than the predicted [negative label]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P1-P3

**原文锚定**:
> "According to the conventional, efficiency-based economic view, mostly rooted in agency theory, state ownership plays a minor role in spurring firms' innovation and performance. Because state-owned enterprises (SOEs) are governed by administrative rather than economic imperatives, government intervention is unavoidable, and political tasks hinder firms' development. In reality, however, many SOEs in emerging economies have evolved into dynamic dynamos, rather than the predicted dying dinosaurs."

**关键特征**:
- "According to the conventional... view" → 先完整陈述主流立场
- "In reality, however..." → 用经验事实直接挑战
- "dynamic dynamos, rather than the predicted dying dinosaurs" → 经典反讽对仗（ASQ 标志性修辞）
- 必须引用具体数据或事实支撑反例

---

### 变体 D：悖论核心 → "不同甚至相反的后果"（keeves2017 型）

**模板**:
> "In this study, we explain why [behavior] can have different and even opposing [consequences] for [actor A] than for [actor B]. We suggest that by violating [ideal 1], [ideal 2], and [ideal 3], [behavior] can pose a threat to [actor A]'s [psychological need], and [attribution mechanism] causes [actor A] to externalize blame... which elicits [negative outcome] toward [actor B]."

**来源**: keeves2017 (ASQ), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "In this study, we explain why ingratiation can have different and even opposing social and psychological consequences for the target of the ingratiation than for the ingratiator. We suggest that by violating prevailing ideals of authenticity, autonomy, and meritocracy, the act of ingratiation can pose a threat to the ingratiator's positive self-regard, and attribution biases can cause the ingratiator to externalize blame for the esteem-threatening behavior to the influence target, which elicits negative affect toward him or her in the form of resentment."

**关键特征**:
- "different and even opposing consequences" → 直接声明悖论
- "by violating [ideal A], [ideal B], and [ideal C]" → 三点列举增强说服力
- 完整的微观机制链：行为→自尊威胁→外化归因→负面情绪

---

### 变体 E：共识建立 → 反常揭示 双段式（pontikes2012 型）

**模板**:
> P1 (Consensus): "A consensus is building that [dominant finding] ([citations spanning multiple contexts]). It seems that [summary of consensus logic]."
> P2 (Anomaly): "Despite this, in many contexts, [actors] continue to [behavior contradicting consensus]. This is especially evident in [empirical setting], in which [specific evidence]. If [consensus logic], then how does [anomalous outcome] come to be? The answer may lie in [resolution hint—often audience heterogeneity or facet decomposition]."

**来源**: pontikes2012 (ASQ), P1-P2

**原文锚定**:
> "A consensus is building that organizations with multiple market identities are hard to understand and therefore less likely to be successful on a range of outcomes than more focused competitors. This has been demonstrated in a number of contexts, from discounted valuations on the stock market (Zuckerman, 1999) to fewer sales on eBay (Hsu, Hannan, and Koçak, 2009), to lower ratings for feature films (Hsu, 2006). It seems that when organizations muddy their identities, they reduce the appeal of their offerings."
> 
> "Despite this, in many contexts, organizations continue to expand their offerings across market labels and identify with labels that have a relatively unclear social meaning. This is especially evident in the software industry, in which organizations frequently claim multiple labels... If part of an organization's identity is derived from its label affiliation, and organizations suffer when they do not have clear identities, then how does an ambiguous classification structure like the software industry's come to be? The answer may lie in different audiences' evaluations of the labels with which organizations affiliate."

**关键特征**:
- **P1 建立不可辩驳的共识**: 跨越 3+ 个不同 empirical context（stock market, eBay, film）证明共识的广度和稳健性
- **P2 用 "Despite this" 转折**: 不攻击共识的正确性，而是指出共识无法解释的 persistent anomaly
- **"especially evident in [setting]"**: 用具体行业/情境的可观察事实锚定反常——不是理论推测，而是 empirical reality
- **以问题收束双段**: "If [X], then how does [Y] come to be?"——将矛盾转化为研究问题
- **"The answer may lie in..."**: 在 Hook 末尾即暗示解决方案方向，但不展开——制造"继续读下去"的驱动力
- 与变体 A（gamache2023）的区别: gamache2023 在同一段内完成共识→反例；pontikes2012 用两个完整段落分别建立共识和反常，适合需要充分展示反常证据的场景

**适用**: Incommensurability × Constructs 组合；ASQ 标志性双段 Hook 结构；需要充分建立共识后再揭示反常的研究

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **`04-reality-contradicts-consensus` (Tension)**: 范式挑战 Hook 建立颠覆性开场后，Gap 段必须用"现实与共识矛盾"来支撑挑战，否则 Hook 沦为稻草人

### 互斥
- **不能与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` (Tension) 同用**: 范式挑战是高能量颠覆，"尽管已有进展"是低能量渐进，能量不匹配
- **不能与 `03-data-shock` (Hook) 同用**: 数据冲击是低能量 Hook，与范式挑战的能量等级冲突

### 反模式提醒
- **不要树立稻草人**: 必须承认现有理论的解释力（"we do not argue that [theory] is wrong"），而不是把对手描绘成愚蠢
- **不要挑战无人持有的观点**: 必须引用具体的、被广泛引用的文献来证明共识确实存在
- **不要在缺乏有力反例时使用**: 反例必须有具体数据、具体案例或系统性证据支撑

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[consensus]` — 建立共识

❌ "Many studies have examined the relationship between X and Y." → 无引文、无具体发现、无法判断共识是否真实

✅ "A consensus is building that organizations with multiple market identities are hard to understand and therefore less likely to be successful on a range of outcomes than more focused competitors. This has been demonstrated in contexts from discounted stock market valuations (Zuckerman, 1999) to fewer eBay sales (Hsu, Hannan, and Koçak, 2009) to lower film ratings (Hsu, 2006)." → 具体发现 + 3 个跨 context 引文（不同期刊/不同行业/不同方法），证明共识不是稻草人

**填充检查**: 共识引用是否跨了 ≥3 个不同 empirical context？是否引用了 ≥2 本不同期刊？如果两个答案都是"是"→ 共识建立合格。

### `[anomaly]` — 揭示反常

❌ "However, some studies have found contradictory results." → "some studies" 是模糊的——哪些研究？在什么条件下？反常证据无法评估

✅ "Despite this, organizations continue to expand across market labels. In the software industry, organizations frequently claim multiple labels, and many labels have ambiguous boundaries such as 'enterprise software' and 'CRM.'" → 具体行业事实 + 可验证的例证（label 名称可查）

**填充检查**: 反常是否基于可观察的行业/情境事实（非"some scholars argue"）？如果是具体事实→反常合格。
