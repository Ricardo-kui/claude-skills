# 08-consequence-cascade — 后果清单型 Hook

## 功能描述

以负面事件为核心，通过递进式后果清单建立问题的严重性和研究的紧迫性。从最直接的影响开始，逐步扩展到更间接、更长期的后果，最后以量化冲击收尾。适用于涉及负面事件（召回、丑闻、事故、失败）的 OM/管理研究。

---

## 变体 A：负面事件后果递进型（mayo2021 型）

**模板**:
> "From the perspective of [stakeholders], '[phenomenon]' is a dreaded word. Rarely do good things follow when [phenomenon] are under consideration. In addition to the [direct problem] that may necessitate [action], there are numerous negative implications that may ensue from [action initiation]. These include damage to [consequence 1] ([citations]), [consequence 2] ([citations]), and even [consequence 3] ([citations]). Direct [cost category] include [specific cost], which can reach [quantification]. No less significant are indirect [cost category], which include [indirect consequence 1] ([citations]), [indirect consequence 2] ([citations]), and [indirect consequence 3] ([citations]). While there are potential upsides of [phenomenon], such as [positive 1] ([citations]) and [positive 2] ([citations]), [actors] may nonetheless seek to avoid [phenomenon] when possible, or at least avoid the blame for them. Exploring this possibility is the focus of our study."

**来源**: mayo2021 (POM), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "From the perspective of the C-suite, 'recall' is a dreaded word. Rarely do good things follow when product recalls are under consideration. In addition to the faulty products that may necessitate a recall, there are numerous negative implications that may ensue from a recall initiation. These include damage to the firm's financial performance (Chen et al. 2009, Pruitt and Peterson 1986, Thirumalai and Sinha 2011), market reputation (Isidore 2014, Tabuchi 2010), and even the CEO's career (Liu et al. 2016). Direct recall costs include repairing or replacing defective inventory, which can reach millions, if not hundreds of millions of dollars (Jarrell and Peltzman 1985, Shah et al. 2017). No less significant are indirect recall costs, which include negative stock market reactions (Davidson and Worrell 1992, Thirumalai and Sinha 2011), legal actions (Daughety and Reinganum 1995), and the cost consequences of recall-related negative media coverage (Liu and Shankar 2015, Rhee and Haunschild 2006). While there are potential upsides of a recall, such as firm learning (Haunschild and Rhee 2004) and goodwill with customers and regulators (Ahluwalia et al. 2000, Dawar and Pillutla 2000), firm leaders, and particularly CEOs, may nonetheless seek to avoid recalls when possible, or at least avoid the blame for them. Exploring this possibility is the focus of our study."

**关键特征**:
- 以 " dreaded word" 建立情感冲击
- **递进结构**: 直接问题 → 负面后果（3项）→ 直接成本（量化）→ 间接成本（3项）→ 转折到潜在正面 → 最终聚焦研究动机
- "No less significant are..." 保持并列节奏，避免平铺直叙
- 每个后果都有 2-3 个引用支撑，展示文献基础
- 结尾 "Exploring this possibility is the focus of our study" 干净收束

**适用**: 产品召回、企业丑闻、安全事故、供应链中断等负面事件研究

## 变体 B：精简版（适用于篇幅受限期刊）

**模板**:
> "[Phenomenon] poses significant risks to [primary stakeholder]. Beyond [direct consequence], [phenomenon] can damage [consequence 1] ([citations]), [consequence 2] ([citations]), and [consequence 3] ([citations]), with costs reaching [quantification]. Despite potential benefits such as [positive aspect] ([citations]), [actors] may seek to avoid [phenomenon] or shift blame for it. We examine [specific focus]."

**适用**: 当期刊 Introduction 字数限制较紧时（如 JM/JMR 偏好更紧凑的 Hook）

### 变体 C：定义优先+后果清单型（wu2025 型）

**模板**:
> "[Phenomenon] imposes [type of pressure] on [actor] to [action]. Examples of such [phenomenon] include [example 1], [example 2], and other [tactics/forms]. Scholarship on [topic] indicates that [actors] perceive [phenomenon] as [framing] that can [consequence 1], [consequence 2], [consequence 3], and ultimately [ultimate consequence]. Accordingly, a substantial body of research has examined [dominant research focus]."

**来源**: wu2025 (SMJ), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "Stakeholder activism imposes external governance pressures on firms to consider stakeholder interests. Examples of such activism include protests, boycotts, and other social-movement tactics. Scholarship on stakeholder activism indicates that firms perceive stakeholder activism as a nonmarket risk that can prevent firms from obtaining critical resources, provoke elite employee turnover, disrupt corporate political activities, and ultimately damage firm performance. Accordingly, a substantial body of research has examined whether such pressures influence firms' corporate social performance."

**关键特征**:
- **定义先行**: 先定义现象及其核心机制（"[X] imposes [Y] on [Z] to [action]"），建立分析框架
- **例证列表**: 用简短并列（"protests, boycotts, and other..."）快速建立范围
- **四重后果递进**: 从资源获取→人才流失→政治活动受阻→最终绩效损害（由轻到重）
- **"Accordingly" 自然过渡**: 后果清单自然导出文献回顾的方向，不加"however/yet/but"张力词
- 与 mayo2021 变体 A 的区别：mayo2021 从情感冲击（"dreaded word"）起步，wu2025 从分析框架（定义+机制）起步

**适用**: 社会运动、制度压力、监管行动等外部治理现象；Incompleteness 开场（后果清单建立重要性但不用反例挑战文献）

**禁忌**: 不要在需要高能量张力时使用——这是低能量 Incompleteness Hook，不适合 Incommensurability

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 后果清单型 Hook → 渐进式文献对话（Progressive Coherence），因为 Hook 展示问题严重性，文献对话展示已有研究如何处理/未处理此问题
- 后果清单型 Hook → Incompleteness 或 Inadequacy Gap，因为 Hook 建立了"问题存在"，Gap 解释"已有理解不足"

### 互斥
- **不适合与 Incommensurability 配对**: 后果清单建立的是"问题严重性"，不是"理论对立"

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只列后果不聚焦**: 清单后必须有明确的收束句指向研究焦点
- **不要忽略正面效应**: 简单罗列负面后果会削弱可信度——承认潜在正面（如学习效应、善意）后再转折，更符合学术审慎
- **不要没有量化**: 如果可能，至少有一个后果带具体数字（"millions, if not hundreds of millions"）
