# 23-popular-debate — 流行观点对立 Hook

## 功能描述

用一个广为人知的流行争论或对立观点开场——不依赖学术文献建立共识，而是利用 TED talk、畅销书、商业媒体等**大众文化素材**中的对立叙事。读者（尤其是 practitioner-oriented 期刊的审稿人）在进入学术对话前，已经通过流行文化意识到"这里有个 unresolved debate"。

与 `02-epigraph-quote-pivot` 的区别：epigraph 是单句权威引语作为 pivot——本模板是**两个对立流行观点**的并置，每个都有具体出处（TED talk、畅销书、商业案例）。与 `06-paradigm-challenge` 的区别：本品不挑战学术共识，而是锚定**大众话语中的 unresolved tension**。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incompleteness / Inadequacy**
- 研究问题在流行商业话语中有明确的对立观点（如 Sinek vs Grant, Christensen vs Blank, Porter vs Prahalad）
- 目标期刊接受 practitioner-relevant Hook（SMJ, AMJ, JM, JOM 首选；ASQ 谨慎）
- 研究的核心贡献 = 解决这个流行争论（"neither side is fully right——the answer depends on X"）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：TED talk vs Bestseller 对立型（falchetti2022 型）

**模板**:
> "In an exceptionally popular [TED talk / bestseller], [Author A] argues that if we want to [achieve outcome], we should [prescription A] ([citation]). [Author B], on the other hand, in [their best-selling book / influential article], points out that this approach may not be the preferred one because [counter-argument]. Accordingly, [Author B] suggests, [actors] should [prescription B] rather than [prescription A]. This debate illustrates one of the central challenges that [actors] face as they ponder [the core question]: Not only do they have to establish [choice dimension A], but understanding [contingency factor] should also inform their choice ([citations])."

**来源**: falchetti2022 (SMJ), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "In an exceptionally popular TED talk, Simon Sinek argues that if we want to mobilize people and resources around novel ideas, we should start our pitches with *why* (Sinek, 2009). Grant (2016), on the other hand, in his best-selling book, *Originals*, points out that this approach may not be the preferred one because innovators championing novel ideas risk running into deep-seated convictions about what is possible. Accordingly, he suggests, innovators should spell out not why they do what they do but *how* they would implement their ideas... This debate illustrates one of the central challenges that innovators face as they ponder the proper framing for pitching their ideas to the world. Not only do they have to establish whether an abstract or concrete framing is the most suitable way to bolster their ideas' appeal... but understanding which audiences they are targeting should also inform their choice."

**关键特征**:
- **双流行来源开场**: TED talk + 畅销书——不是学术引用而是大众文化锚点，立即建立 practitioner relevance
- **"'Author A argues... Author B, on the other hand, points out...'"**: 对称对立结构——两个观点都有姓名和出处，不是 strawman
- **"'This debate illustrates one of the central challenges...'"**: 从流行争论 pivot 到学术问题——不宣称自己能解决 debate，而是展示 debate 揭示了更深层的学术问题
- **"'Not only do they have to establish X, but understanding Y should also inform their choice'"**: 争论的 resolution 不在 A vs B 本身，而在被双方忽略的 contingency factor——这是论文的理论贡献预告

**适用**: 当研究问题在流行商业话语中有明确的对立观点时。Incompleteness × Boundary 组合特别适配（"the answer depends on audience type / context"）。SMJ, AMJ, JM, JOM 首选——practitioner relevance 是这些期刊的核心评价标准

**禁忌**: 两个流行观点必须都是真实且有具体出处的——不能编造或夸大一个"debate"。如果只有一个流行观点而无对立面→降级为 `02-epigraph-quote-pivot`。如果流行 debate 与学术 debate 不完全对应→必须在 Hook 末尾解释二者的映射关系。"This debate illustrates..." pivot 不能跳过——没有这句，Hook 只是流行文化展示而不是学术问题的引子。ASQ 投稿避免使用——ASQ 偏好理论深度而非 practitioner relevance

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 Boundary 贡献配对**: 流行争论的 resolution 通常是 "it depends on X"（contingency）——因此最适合 Boundary 或 Constructs（受众区分）贡献
- 与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` (Tension) 或 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` (Tension) 配对

### 互斥
- **不能与 `06-paradigm-challenge` (Hook) 同用**: 流行争论是 practitioner-level 的 debate；范式挑战是 academic-level 的颠覆——叙事层级不匹配
- **不能与纯理论驱动型研究同用**: 流行 debate Hook 暗示 practitioner relevance——纯理论论文（ASQ/ASR 风格）不应使用

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只有一个观点而无对立面**: 单观点 = epigraph，不是 debate
- **不要选择 obscure 的流行观点**: 必须是 TED talk、NYT bestseller、HBR article 等级别的大众素材
- **"This debate illustrates..." pivot 不可省略**: 这是从流行文化到学术问题的唯一桥梁
