# Cold-Start Cost Cascade Hook

## Skeleton
From the perspective of [stakeholder], "[phenomenon]" is a dreaded word.
Rarely do good things follow when [phenomenon] are under consideration.
In addition to [primary problem], there are numerous negative implications that may ensue from [action].
These include damage to [dimension 1], [dimension 2], and even [dimension 3].
Direct [costs] include [specific cost], which can reach [magnitude].
No less significant are indirect [costs], which include [cost type 1], [cost type 2], and [cost type 3].
While there are potential upsides of [action], such as [benefit 1] and [benefit 2], [stakeholder] may nonetheless seek to [behavior].

## Source
Mayo et al. POMS

## Gap Type
General — applicable to any phenomenon with significant costs
