# Literature Turn — 从 Hook 到学术对话的过渡句法

## 功能描述

P2 的功能：将 Hook 建立的注意力转化为学术对话。这不是文献综述，而是"将 Hook 中的张力重新框定为学术问题"。这个 Transition 的质量决定读者是否感觉 Hook 是"营销噱头"还是"真正的学术贡献起点"。

三种 Conversation 策略对应三种 Gap 类型，各有不同的过渡句式。

---

## 策略 1：Progressive Coherence（递进式）

**适用**: Incompleteness

**核心逻辑**: "已有文献做了 A 和 B，但遗漏了 C——不是因为他们错了，而是因为该领域自然发展到此时才出现 C 的问题。"

### 句式模板

**变体 A：自然推进型**
> "Research in [field] has long recognized the importance of [phenomenon] and has often attempted to understand [outcome]. As part of this effort, recent research has drawn on [theory] to focus on [specific aspect] ([citations]). For example, research in this tradition has considered [specific examples of what IS known]."

来源: gamache2020 (SMJ), adapted

**变体 B：快速文献回顾型**
> "A substantial body of research has examined [reactive/post-hoc response to phenomenon]. [Author A] found that [finding]. [Author B] showed that [finding]. Most recently, [Author C] demonstrated [finding]. These studies collectively establish that [core consensus]. However, what remains unclear is [our focus]."

来源: eilert2017 (JM), adapted

**变体 C：权威引语过渡型**
> "Accordingly, a substantial body of research has examined [dominant focus]. [Authority], however, note that '[insightful observation that reframes focus]' (emphasis original). Yet, despite some noteworthy exceptions, [our focus] remains poorly understood."

来源: wu2025 (SMJ), P1-P2

**关键特征**:
- 先承认文献的累积进展（"a substantial body of research"）
- 用引语或转折词建立缺口
- 不暗示已有文献有错误

## 策略 2：Synthesized Coherence（综合式）

**适用**: Inadequacy

**核心逻辑**: "多个文献流各自抓到了现象的一部分，但都没有意识到它们共享一个盲点——构念的混淆、层次的误置、或边界的模糊。"

### 句式模板

**变体 A：多文献流连接型**
> "Two streams of research inform our understanding of [phenomenon]. The [stream A] literature has shown that [finding A]. Meanwhile, the [stream B] literature has demonstrated that [finding B]. Although both streams offer valuable insights, they have developed largely in parallel, leaving [gap at the intersection] underexplored."

来源: gamache2020 (SMJ), adapted

**变体 B：文献混淆识别型**
> "The interdependence of [construct A] and [construct B] is reflected in the literature. For example, [Scholar 1] used [terms] interchangeably; [Scholar 2] combined them; and some scholars have conceptualized [construct] as a component of [other construct]. Further, the two constructs have many similarities: [list]. Despite this recognition of interdependence, the question of how [construct A] and [construct B] [coevolve/interact/diverge] has gone largely unaddressed."

来源: pollock2015 (ASQ), P2

**关键特征**:
- 展示多个文献流之间的未连接状态
- 用具体引用证明混淆确实存在
- 暗示需要一个新的整合框架

## 策略 3：Non-Coherence（冲突式）

**适用**: Incommensurability

**核心逻辑**: "这两个理论分别预测了相反的结果，各自都有支持证据。它们不能同时正确——除非我们重新理解这个现象。"

### 句式模板

**变体 A：理论对立型**
> "A long-standing debate in [field] centers on two perspectives: [view A], which emphasizes [focus A], and [view B], which focuses on [focus B]. These perspectives offer incompatible predictions about [outcome]. [View A] predicts [prediction A], while [view B] suggests [prediction B]. Resolving this tension requires [our approach]."

来源: zhou2017 (ASQ), adapted

**变体 B：非对称关系型**
> "Although extant theory and research has yielded considerable insight and convincing evidence on [topic], it has focused almost entirely on [one direction/one level/one actor]. The [related literature] has provided compelling evidence, however, that [asymmetry insight]. Scholars have devoted little theoretical or empirical attention to understanding how [phenomenon] may operate in [the opposite direction/at a different level/through a different mechanism]."

来源: keeves2017 (ASQ), P2

**关键特征**:
- 明确命名两个对立的理论或观点
- 每个都有文献支持（不是稻草人）
- "Resolving this tension requires" → 自然引出研究方案

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- Progressive Coherence → Incompleteness Gap + `03-data-shock` 或 `10-practical-puzzle` Hook
- Synthesized Coherence → Inadequacy Gap + `05-literature-consensus-blindspot` 或 `04-puzzle-paradox` Hook
- Non-Coherence → Incommensurability Gap + `06-paradigm-challenge` Hook

### 反模式提醒
- **不要把 Literature Turn 写成完整文献综述**: P2 是过渡，不是文献回顾。2-3 句即可
- **不要让 Hook 和 Literature Turn 脱节**: 必须在语义上连接（如 Hook 中的"反直觉现象"→ Lit Turn 中的"已有理论预测相反方向"）
- **不要只引用自己导师的论文**: 展示文献广度
