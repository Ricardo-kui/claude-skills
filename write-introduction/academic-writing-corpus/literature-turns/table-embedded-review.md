# Table-Embedded Literature Review

## Skeleton
In fact, research outside of [target field] indicates that [actors] may [behavior] via at least two mechanisms: [mechanism 1] or [mechanism 2], and that the chosen [mechanism] may depend on [key variable].
The contexts of these empirical studies are predominantly in [field 1] and [field 2]; see [Table X].
These studies indicate that [early phase behavior], leading to [consequence 1].
[Late phase behavior], [consequence 2].

## Note
Use a systematic table (4-12 rows) summarizing prior studies when migrating literature from an external field. Keep narrative summary to 1-2 paragraphs before/after the table.

## 变体 B：本领域结构性回顾表格（Eilert 2017 型）

**适用**：文献分支明确且存在清晰结构性遗漏的领域

**骨架**：
```
The [field] literature in [discipline], as summarized in [Table X], has predominantly focused on [theme 1] (e.g., [citations]) and [theme 2] (e.g., [citations]). However, little attention has been paid to [neglected topic]. By examining [our focus], our study offers insights on [value proposition].
```

**关键特征**：
- 表格呈现清晰的文献分类维度（如 Outcomes / Timing / Industry / Findings）
- "predominantly focused on X and Y" 后接表格中已总结的文献主题
- "However, little attention has been paid to Z" 精确指向表格中空白列/行
- 表格同时承担 Literature Turn 和 Tension 的功能

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — Table 1: Literature Overview on Product Recalls（按 Study / Performance Outcomes / Recall Timing / Prerecall/Postrecall / Industry / Relevant Findings 组织）

**反模式**：
- 表格变成附录式罗列（无分类维度）→ 沦为 citation list
- "However" 转折后未明确指向表格中的空白 → 读者无法验证 gap

**适用组合**：Incompleteness x Mechanism / Boundary / Output
