# Phrasebank: 批判性表达（Morley 08 章收割）

> **层级定位**：auxiliary 语言实现层。在 Theory 段处理竞争机制、Methods/Discussion 评估他人识别策略时，提供**单篇研究/单一理论的方法学批判**短语。
>
> **填补的空白**：现有 `literature-turns/`（3 种 coherence）+ `tensions/`（17 种 gap）覆盖了**对话定位层**的批判（problematize 整个文献对话）。本文件补**单研究/单理论的方法学批判**——当你在 Theory 段说"prior study 受 X 局限"、在 Discussion 说"该替代解释因 Y 不可信"时的措辞。两者层次不同：前者是宏观对话批判，后者是微观研究批判。
>
> **使用规则**（每次调用必读）：
> 1. **调用顺序**：确定要批判的具体研究/理论及批判点 → 本文件取短语 → 语境化改写（必须指明具体研究 + 具体局限）。
> 2. **Specificity gate 极严**：Morley 批判短语极通用（"fails to account for..."），**必须配具体研究 + 具体局限**。替换后若仍可放进任何论文 → 不合格。批判必须**公平**（承认被批研究的贡献）且**精确**（指出具体方法/数据/理论缺陷）。
> 3. 批判后应**提出建设性方向**（本文件末段），而非只破不立——FT50 期望批判后紧跟"本研究如何回应"。
> 4. **与 tensions/ 的分工**：tensions 管"整个对话缺什么"（gap framing）；本文件管"某个具体研究/理论弱在哪"（study critique）。不要用本文件做 gap framing（那是 tensions 的职责）。
>
> **退役规则**：某批判模式一旦被顶刊蒸馏语料覆盖（经 distill-* 验证），对应条目删除。本文件不计入 MVP30 paper_count。

## 单研究/单理论的方法学局限批判

### 指出方法/数据局限

- [Prior study / This stream of research] is limited by its reliance on [specific data source / method].
- [A shortcoming of X (Year)] is that [specific measurement / sample / identification] does not fully capture [construct/threat].
- [X (Year)] [focuses on / examines] [context], which [limits / constrains] the [generalizability / external validity] of its findings to [other contexts].
- Although [X (Year)] [contribution], the study [does not address / cannot rule out] [specific alternative explanation / threat].
- The [findings / conclusions] of [X (Year)] should be interpreted in light of [specific limitation].

### 指出理论/机制局限

- [Prior theory / This perspective] [fails to account for / overlooks / neglects] [specific factor / mechanism].
- [Existing research] has [tended to / predominantly] [assumed / focused on] [X], leaving [Y] [unexplored / undertheorized].
- While [theory A] explains [phenomenon], it does not address [specific boundary / mechanism] that [our study examines].
- A [limitation / gap] of this [approach / framework] is that it [assumes / treats] [X] as [uniform/static], whereas [evidence/theory] suggests [X is heterogeneous/dynamic].

### 指出适用范围局限（boundary of prior work）

- [X's (Year)] findings are [specific to / bound by] [context/condition], and may not [extend to / hold in] [other context].
- The [generalizability / applicability] of [X (Year)] is [constrained / limited] by [scope condition].
- [Prior work] has examined [X] primarily in [context A]; whether [the relationship] holds in [context B] remains an [open / empirical] question.

## 建设性批判（critique + constructive suggestion）

FT50 期望批判后紧跟建设性方向——只破不立的批判会被审稿人视为"搭便车贬低前人"。

### 从局限引出本研究

- [This limitation / gap] motivates our [examination / approach], which [addresses / overcomes] it by [specific design choice].
- We [build on / extend] [X (Year)] by [specific contribution] that [addresses the limitation / relaxes the assumption].
- To [address / overcome] this [limitation / concern], we [employ a different design / incorporate additional data / theorize the mechanism].

### 承认贡献后指出不足（公平批判）

- Although [X (Year)] [makes an important contribution / advances our understanding of], its [approach / findings] are [limited / constrained] by [specific issue].
- [Building on / Extending] [X's (Year)] [contribution], we [address / examine] [the limitation / the unanswered question] by [approach].
- [X (Year)] [advanced / pioneered] [the study of], yet [left open / did not resolve] [specific question]—which we [take up / address] here.

## 与现有批判资产的关系

- **`tensions/`（17 种 gap）**：宏观对话批判——"整个文献对话缺什么"。本文件是微观研究批判——"某个具体研究弱在哪"。先用 tensions 定位 gap，再用本文件在 Theory/Literature Review 段具体批判支撑该 gap 的研究。
- **`literature-turns/`（3 种 coherence）**：决定你如何组织文献关系（progressive/synthesized/non-coherence）。本文件提供组织时的批判措辞。
- **`write-theory/corpus/sentences/acknowledgment_response.md`**：已覆盖**让步反论**（concession + rebuttal）——本文件的"承认贡献后指出不足"与之互补，acknowledgment_response 管假设论证层的让步，本文件管文献综述层的批判。

## 反模式

- **泛泛而批**（"Prior research has limitations"）——无具体研究 + 无具体局限，违反 specificity gate。
- **只破不立**——批判后不提本研究如何回应，FT50 期望建设性。
- **不公平批判**（抹杀被批研究贡献）——必须先承认贡献再指出不足。
- **用研究批判做 gap framing**——gap framing 是 tensions 的职责；本文件只管具体研究的方法学批判。
- **过度批判**（把次要局限说成致命缺陷）——审稿人会质疑你的公平性；局限的严重性应与措辞强度匹配（参考 `hedging-strength.md` 强度阶梯）。
