# DV Methodology Defense — DV 方法论辩护型预览

## 功能描述

当研究使用的因变量（DV）天然具有正负/好坏/成功失败双情境时，将此固有特征重新框定为**方法论优势**——不是"碰巧用了这个 DV"，而是这个 DV 的特性为检验边界条件提供了天然实验场。

---

## 变体 I：正负不对称性辩护型（pfarrer2010 型）

**模板**:
> We empirically test the effects of [IVs] on [DV1] and [DV2]. [DV name] occur when [definition] ([citation]). [DV name] are [positive condition] when [criterion for positive] and [negative condition] when [criterion for negative]. Both types of [DV] violate [stakeholder expectations] and [consequence 1] ([citations]). Further, [negative condition] not only [consequence 1], but also [consequence 2] and can elicit [stronger reaction] ([citations]). [Positive condition], on the other hand, are viewed more ambivalently. Although they [negative aspect], they are, nevertheless, [positive aspect]; as a result, they usually generate [moderate reaction] ([citations]). These variations in the effects of [positive] and [negative] provided an opportunity to empirically examine the effects of different [constructs] under different circumstances.

**来源**: pfarrer2010 (AMJ), P5

**原文锚定**:
> We empirically test the effects of possessing high levels of reputation and celebrity on firms' propensities to announce earnings surprises and on investors' reactions to these surprises. Earnings surprises occur when a firm's actual earnings deviate from market analysts' consensus estimates (Degeorge, Patel, & Zeckhauser, 1999). Earnings surprises are positive when the announced earnings exceed analysts' estimates and negative when the announced earnings fail to meet their estimates. Both types of surprises violate investors' expectations of predictable earnings and negatively affect the accuracy of financial evaluations (Degeorge et al., 1999; Kasznik & Lev, 1995; Tan, Libby, & Hunton, 2002; Williams, 1996). Further, negative surprises not only violate expectations of predictability, but also reflect disappointing firm performance and can elicit strong, negative responses from investors (Brown, 2001; Kasznik & Lev, 1995; Skinner, 1994; Skinner & Sloan, 2002). Positive surprises, on the other hand, are viewed more ambivalently. Although they affect the predictability of a firm's performance and hence the accuracy of market forecasts, they are, nevertheless, signals of good performance; as a result, they usually generate relatively smaller, but positive, responses from investors (Brown, 2001; Kasznik & Lev, 1995; Skinner & Sloan, 2002; Westphal & Clement, 2008). These variations in the effects of positive and negative surprises provided an opportunity to empirically examine the effects of different social approval assets under different circumstances.

**关键特征**:
- 先陈述 DV 的**操作化定义**，再解释其理论意义
- 用 "Both types of..." → "Further, negative..." → "Positive..., on the other hand..." 的**三段对比结构**建立正负不对称性
- "provided an opportunity to empirically examine... under different circumstances" → **核心技巧：将 DV 的固有特征转化为方法论优势**
- 大量引用（8+）建立 DV 的文献合法性

## 组装规则

### 适用场景
- 任何使用**正负/好坏/成功失败双情境**作为边界条件的研究
- 构念精细化论文（区分两个相似构念在不同 DV 条件下的差异化效应）

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只定义 DV 不解释其理论意义**: 定义后必须立即解释为什么这个 DV 对研究问题重要
- **不要忽略不对称性的理论含义**: 正负情境的不对称性本身必须有理论依据（如损失厌恶、期望违背理论等）
- **不要用少量引用敷衍**: DV 的文献合法性需要 5+ 个引用支撑
