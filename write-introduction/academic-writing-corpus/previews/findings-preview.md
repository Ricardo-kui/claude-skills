# Findings Preview — 发现预览句法

## 功能描述

P5-P6 的下半段功能：向读者预告实证结果。这是 Introduction 中的"承诺"段落——读者由此判断是否值得继续读下去。发现预览必须有实证数据支撑，不能空承诺。

---

## 变体 D：经济显著性型

**模板**:
> "Using [data description] over [time period], we find that [main finding]. The impact is economically significant: [quantification—effect size in practical terms]. We also find [additional finding]. However, [nuance/condition/boundary]."

来源: eilert2017 (JM) + malshe2015 (JM)

**关键特征**:
- 数据+方法简要预览
- 经济显著性必须在 Intro 中给出具体数字
- "However" 展示 nuance，暗示不止一个简单发现

**反模式提醒**: 经济显著性必须有基线对比——不能说 "effect is large" 而不说 "large relative to what"

---

## 变体 E：反例驱动型

**模板**:
> "We find that, although [group] may, on average, [dominant behavior], [contingency] matters. As [example 1 with specific data], [counter-example that contradicts average]. These examples and our systematic evidence raise the question of whether [contingency] influences [relationship], which we answer by [approach]."

来源: gamache2023 (SMJ), P2

**关键特征**:
- 用具体反例（人名、公司名、数字）建立不可辩驳的直观证据
- "on average" vs "contingency matters" → 从平均效应到异质性的经典转
- 反例递进排列以增强冲击力

---

## 变体 F：两层因果链型

**模板**:
> "Using [data] and [method], we first examine how [IV] affects [DV1]. We find [finding about IV→DV1]. We then turn to the consequences of [DV1] by examining whether [DV2] and whether this [varies by moderator]. We find that [finding about DV1→DV2]. This two-stage analysis reveals [overall insight]."

来源: darby2024 (MSOM), P5-P6

**关键特征**:
- 先预览第一层因果，再预览第二层
- "We then turn to" → 自然过渡到第二层
- 适合 IV→DV1→DV2 的研究设计

---

## 变体 G：调节效应预览型

**模板**:
> "Our results support the prediction that [main effect]. In particular, [specific effect size with baseline comparison]. We further find that this relationship is moderated by [moderator 1] and [moderator 2], such that [direction of moderation]. These findings suggest that [broader implication]."

来源: ceo_regulatory_focus_ijrm (IJRM), P4-P5

**关键特征**:
- 主效应→效应量→调节效应→含义，四层递进
- 调节效应方向明确（不说"moderates"而不说方向）
- 以 broader implication 收束

## 变体 J：数字-理论循环型（mayo2021 型）

**模板**:
> "Using data on [sample description], [number] [actor], and [number] [events] during the [time period], we find that [main finding with exact number]. We theorize that this may occur because [theoretical explanation]. This is supported empirically, as we find that [supporting finding 1]. We also find that [supporting finding 2]. In fact, we find that [nuanced finding]. [Robustness statement]."

**来源**: mayo2021 (POM), P7

**原文锚定**:
> "Using data on 125 publicly traded consumer product firms, 307 CEO appointments, and 584 recalls during the 25-year period from 1992 to 2016, we find that the hazard of a recall is 77% higher early in a CEO's tenure, or approximately their first three years on the job, compared to later in tenure. We theorize that this may occur because CEOs can more easily blame predecessors in this early phase. This is supported empirically, as we find that the early tenure recall hazard is higher when the previous CEO was forced out of their job. We also find that the low hazard of a recall later in a CEO's tenure is more prominent for recalls that involve significant discretion, symptomatic of recalls that can be more easily avoided. In fact, we find that the relatively low hazard of a recall later in a CEO's tenure is only present for the more discretionary recalls, while the relationship between CEO tenure and recall hazard is absent when examining recalls that have less discretion. Robustness checks indicate consistent support for our results after examining model selection, measurement sensitivity, sample selection, reverse causality, and alternative explanations."

**关键特征**:
- 样本数字精确（firms, appointments, recalls, years）
- 效应量具体且带百分比（77% higher）
- "We theorize that this may occur because" → 每个发现都附带理论解释
- "This is supported empirically, as we find that" → 发现→理论→更多发现的循环
- "In fact" → 递进到最反直觉的发现
- 结尾一句话概括稳健性检验的多个维度

**适用**: 效应量显著、有调节效应、需要展示数据可信度的实证论文

### 变体 K：紧凑四段式（vadakkepatt2022 型）

**模板**:
> "The tests of our hypotheses rely on [data description]. We find a significant, [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV1], providing novel evidence of [phenomenon characterization]. We also replicate previous findings of a [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV2] but identify a [direction] counteracting effect when we account for [mediator]. Consistent with our expectations, we also show that [moderator 1], [moderator 2], [moderator 3], and [moderator 4] each [direction] moderate the [IV-DV1] relationship. Finally, [mechanism] helps explain the [IV-DV1] link."

**来源**: vadakkepatt2022 (JM), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "The tests of our hypotheses rely on an unbalanced panel of 758 observations involving 87 publicly traded firms during the period 2000–2014. We find a significant, negative effect of lobbying on customer satisfaction, providing novel evidence of the dark side of firm lobbying. We also replicate previous findings of a positive effect of lobbying on firm value but identify a negative counteracting effect when we account for customer satisfaction. Consistent with our expectations, we also show that the CEO's background, advertising spend, R&D spend, and product market lobbying each positively moderate the lobbying–customer satisfaction relationship. Finally, a decrease in customer focus helps explain the negative lobbying–customer satisfaction link."

**关键特征**:
- **一句话数据说明** → **主效应** → **复制+修正已有发现** → **调节效应** → **机制**
- 四句话覆盖主效应、中介、调节、机制，极度紧凑
- "replicate previous findings... but identify" → 先承认已有发现，再用新视角修正
- "providing novel evidence of" → 将统计发现上升为概念性证据（如"dark side"）
- 适合复杂模型（1主效应+1中介+4调节+1机制）但期刊要求简洁的 Introduction

**适用**: 模型复杂但 Introduction 篇幅受限；需要同时展示复制已有发现和新增发现的论文

### 变体 L：双 DV 竞争方向 + Post Hoc 型（wowak2025 型）

**模板**:
> "We collect data on [data_description] across [N] [units] that had [N] [events] from [year_start] through [year_end]. We find that [finding_DV1]. Specifically, [magnitude_DV1]. In contrast, we find that [finding_DV2_opposite_direction]. Specifically, [magnitude_DV2]. Given that [stakes_restatement], [implications]. Two important post hoc analyses examine why we find what we do, as well as what may be done about it. First, [post_hoc_1_summary_with_result]. Second, [post_hoc_2_summary_with_result]."

**来源**: wowak2025 (MS), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "We collect data on political donations from thousands of TMT members across 88 firms that had 4,072 serious medical product recalls from 2002 through 2015. We find that firms with more liberal TMTs experience fewer serious product recalls than those with more conservative TMTs. Specifically, firms with TMTs one standard deviation more liberal than the mean experience approximately 1.10 fewer serious recalls per year. In contrast, we find that firms with more liberal TMTs are slower to recall products with serious quality issues than firms with more conservative TMTs. Specifically, firms with TMTs one standard deviation more liberal than the mean take approximately 22 days longer to initiate serious recalls. ... Two important post hoc analyses examine why we find what we do, as well as what may be done about it. First, we examine our proposed mechanism... We find that firms with more liberal TMTs experience fewer product quality-related adverse events and that this lower level... partially mediates the relationship... Second, ... political diversity on the TMT leads to fewer and faster recalls, capturing the best of both political ideologies."

**关键特征**:
- **"In contrast"**: 双 DV 的相反方向发现增加了叙事的反直觉性
- **Post Hoc 在 Introduction 中预告**: 极其少见——建立了"不仅发现，还解释了为什么+该怎么办"的完整研究印象
- **具体数字贯穿**: 1.10 fewer recalls / 22 days longer — 每个发现都有具体 magnitude
- **禁忌**: 仅在 Post Hoc 分析是研究的核心增值时才在 Introduction 中预告；不要预告所有稳健性检验

**适用**: 竞争假设设计、双 DV 设计、Post Hoc 分析是核心贡献的研究

### 变体 M：发现+调节完整预览型（darby2025 型）

**模板**:
> "The hypotheses are tested in the [industry] sector, wherein [phenomenon_stakes]. Analyses of data on [N] [events] initiated by [N] [units] from [year_start] to [year_end] reveal that [main_finding]. Turning to the moderators, [moderation_finding_1] and [moderation_finding_2]."

**来源**: darby2025 (JSCM), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "The hypotheses are tested in the medical products sector, wherein defective products have significant ramifications for society. Analyses of data on 5,427 medical product recalls initiated by 138 firms from 2002 to 2020 reveal that greater stock ownership by activist investors who have not targeted the focal firm—but have targeted other firms—is associated with faster recalls by the focal firm, providing evidence of a spillover effect. Turning to the moderators, the spillover effect is stronger for design-related defects relative to manufacturing-related defects and for high-severity defects that can harm consumers as opposed to low-severity defects."

**关键特征**:
- **极简数据描述**: 一句话完成数据、样本、时间跨度
- **主效应+概念标签**: 不仅报告关联方向，还标注为 "providing evidence of a spillover effect"
- **"Turning to the moderators"**: 自然过渡到调节效应，不突兀
- **禁忌**: 不要在 Preview 中列举所有假设的统计结果——用自然语言叙事，不用 "β = X, p < Y"

**适用**: 主效应+调节设计、需要在 Introduction 中快速建立贡献感的研究

### 变体 N：分体式双预览型（darby2023 型）

**模板**:
> "Using [data description], we first examine how [IV] affects [mediator/DV1]. We find that [finding 1]. [Theoretical implication of finding 1]. We then turn to the consequences of [mediator/DV1] by examining whether [DV2]. We find that [finding 2]. [Theoretical implication of finding 2]."

**来源**: darby2023 (MSOM), P5-P6

**原文锚定**:
> "Using data on 5,427 medical product recalls initiated by 138 firms from 2002 to 2015, we first examine how CEO stock ownership affects recall timing. We find that CEO stock ownership is associated with faster recalls. This finding is consistent with the notion that CEOs with stock ownership are more likely to initiate recalls in a timely manner. We then turn to the consequences of recall timing by examining whether faster recalls are associated with lower stock market penalties. We find that faster recalls are associated with lower stock market penalties. This finding suggests that timely recalls can mitigate negative investor reactions."

**关键特征**:
- **P1 预览第一层因果（IV→M/DV1）**: 先建立自变量对中介/第一结果的影响
- **P2 预览第二层因果（M/DV1→DV2）**: 再建立中介/第一结果对第二结果的影响
- 两段之间用 "We then turn to" 自然过渡，形成"因果链"叙事
- 每段末尾附带一句理论含义，将统计发现上升为概念性结论
- 与变体 F（两层因果链型）的区别：变体 F 是单段内完成两层预览，变体 N 是分成两段，每段更充分展开

**适用**: 双缺口论文（第一个缺口在 IV→M，第二个缺口在 M→DV）需要分别预览两层发现的研究

**禁忌**: 如果两层发现都很简单，不要强行分成两段——单段更简洁

### 变体 O：Preview-first 反直觉发现型（shipilov_greve_rowley2019 型）

**模板**:
> "We look at the relationship between [broad IV domain] and [broad DV domain]. First, existing research focuses on [narrow scope] ([citation]). In general, research predicts that [conventional prediction]. While we follow the same logic in our hypotheses and replicate [expected finding], we find the opposite effects of [unexpected finding]. [One-sentence summary of surprising result].
>
> Second, we propose to supplement the focus on [existing scope] with [new scope/angle]. Indeed, prior research has already shown that [related mechanism supports this extension] ([citations]). We propose that [new theoretical claim].
>
> Empirically, we examine [specific empirical setup]. We build on prior research finding [established effects] ([citation]), and add data on [new variable]. We also exploit an exogenous shock of [event], which [creates treatment variation]. We predict and find that [summary of findings]. [Standard prediction confirmed]. However, contrary to the conventional prediction that [standard expectation], we find that [surprising result].
>
> We believe that this surprising effect occurs because [theoretical explanation for unexpected finding], consistent with [alternative theoretical lens] ([citations])."

**来源**: shipilov_greve_rowley2019 (SMJ), P4-P7 (无标题序言)

**原文锚定**:
> "We look at the relationship between firm media coverage and board reform adoption. First, existing research focuses on the impact of a firm's own media coverage on its actions (Bednar, 2012). In general, research predicts that firms dislike governance changes and that they change only due to the exposure to negative coverage while resisting changes when coverage is positive. While we follow the same logic in our hypotheses and replicate the negative coverage results, we find the opposite effects of positive coverage... Second, we propose to supplement the focus on the firm's own media coverage with the network view... We also exploit an exogenous shock of 2007–08 financial crisis... We predict and find that firms respond to both direct and indirect media coverage... However, contrary to the conventional prediction that positive media coverage should shield the firm from the need to change... we find that positive direct and indirect coverage is also responsible for adoptions. We believe that this surprising effect occurs because positive coverage makes decision makers believe more in transparency as a solution to the agency problem..."

**关键特征**:
- **预览在 Theory/Tension 之前** → 这个 Introduction 的 Preview/Contribution 段落出现在 Tension 之前。读者先知道"做了什么、发现了什么"，再进入 Theory 的 gap 论证
- **"First... Second..." 双贡献标记** → 显式拆分为两个维度（现象扩展 + 机制扩展）
- **"While we follow the same logic... we find the opposite effects"** → 在 Introduction 中就预告了反直觉发现，预判读者/审稿人的质疑
- **"We believe that this surprising effect occurs because..."** → 在 Introduction 中对反直觉发现提供初步理论解释（而不是等到 Discussion）
- **"We also exploit an exogenous shock"** → 方法可信度信号：用 DiD 处理内生性问题
- **功能等价于 Discussion 摘要前置** → 将贡献、发现、解释打包成 Introduction 的"执行摘要"

**适用条件**:
- 论文至少有一个假设在实证中被反转（方向与理论预测相反）
- 方法复杂，需要提前建立可信度（如 DiD + CEM matching）
- 目标期刊接受发现驱动、方法透明的 Introduction 风格（SMJ/JM 首选）
- 读者预期需要被提前管理——如果到最后才揭示反直觉发现，审稿人可能在 Theory 阶段就已质疑假设方向

**禁忌**:
- 不要在没有反直觉发现时使用——标准排列（Tension→Preview）更适合
- 不要在 Theory 部分重复 Preview 中的理论解释——Preview 是预告，Theory 是完整论证
- 不要过度依赖 "exogenous shock" 解决所有内生性问题——仍需讨论 identification assumptions
- Preview-first 排列削弱叙事悬念——如果论文的核心张力是 "我们发现了文献没有发现的东西"，标准排列更有效

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 发现预览 → 必须有实证数据支撑
- 不要在发现预览中列举所有假设: Preview 是选择性预告，不是假设列表
- 不要只说 "we find support for our hypotheses": 必须说方向、大小、或意外发现
- 两段必须语义连接：机制预览解释"为什么"，发现预览展示"我们找到了什么证据"
