# Findings Preview — 发现预览句法

## 功能描述

P5-P6 的下半段功能：向读者预告实证结果。这是 Introduction 中的"承诺"段落——读者由此判断是否值得继续读下去。发现预览必须有实证数据支撑，不能空承诺。

---

## 变体 D：经济显著性型

**模板**:
> "Using [data description] over [time period], we find that [main finding]. The impact is economically significant: [quantification—effect size in practical terms]. We also find [additional finding]. However, [nuance/condition/boundary]."

来源: eilert2017 (JM) + malshe2015 (JM)

**关键特征**:
- 数据+方法简要预览
- 经济显著性必须在 Intro 中给出具体数字
- "However" 展示 nuance，暗示不止一个简单发现

**反模式提醒**: 经济显著性必须有基线对比——不能说 "effect is large" 而不说 "large relative to what"

---

## 变体 E：反例驱动型

**模板**:
> "We find that, although [group] may, on average, [dominant behavior], [contingency] matters. As [example 1 with specific data], [counter-example that contradicts average]. These examples and our systematic evidence raise the question of whether [contingency] influences [relationship], which we answer by [approach]."

来源: gamache2023 (SMJ), P2

**关键特征**:
- 用具体反例（人名、公司名、数字）建立不可辩驳的直观证据
- "on average" vs "contingency matters" → 从平均效应到异质性的经典转
- 反例递进排列以增强冲击力

---

## 变体 F：两层因果链型

**模板**:
> "Using [data] and [method], we first examine how [IV] affects [DV1]. We find [finding about IV→DV1]. We then turn to the consequences of [DV1] by examining whether [DV2] and whether this [varies by moderator]. We find that [finding about DV1→DV2]. This two-stage analysis reveals [overall insight]."

来源: darby2024 (MSOM), P5-P6

**关键特征**:
- 先预览第一层因果，再预览第二层
- "We then turn to" → 自然过渡到第二层
- 适合 IV→DV1→DV2 的研究设计

---

## 变体 G：调节效应预览型

**模板**:
> "Our results support the prediction that [main effect]. In particular, [specific effect size with baseline comparison]. We further find that this relationship is moderated by [moderator 1] and [moderator 2], such that [direction of moderation]. These findings suggest that [broader implication]."

来源: ceo_regulatory_focus_ijrm (IJRM), P4-P5

**关键特征**:
- 主效应→效应量→调节效应→含义，四层递进
- 调节效应方向明确（不说"moderates"而不说方向）
- 以 broader implication 收束

## 变体 J：数字-理论循环型（mayo2021 型）

**模板**:
> "Using data on [sample description], [number] [actor], and [number] [events] during the [time period], we find that [main finding with exact number]. We theorize that this may occur because [theoretical explanation]. This is supported empirically, as we find that [supporting finding 1]. We also find that [supporting finding 2]. In fact, we find that [nuanced finding]. [Robustness statement]."

**来源**: mayo2021 (POM), P7

**原文锚定**:
> "Using data on 125 publicly traded consumer product firms, 307 CEO appointments, and 584 recalls during the 25-year period from 1992 to 2016, we find that the hazard of a recall is 77% higher early in a CEO's tenure, or approximately their first three years on the job, compared to later in tenure. We theorize that this may occur because CEOs can more easily blame predecessors in this early phase. This is supported empirically, as we find that the early tenure recall hazard is higher when the previous CEO was forced out of their job. We also find that the low hazard of a recall later in a CEO's tenure is more prominent for recalls that involve significant discretion, symptomatic of recalls that can be more easily avoided. In fact, we find that the relatively low hazard of a recall later in a CEO's tenure is only present for the more discretionary recalls, while the relationship between CEO tenure and recall hazard is absent when examining recalls that have less discretion. Robustness checks indicate consistent support for our results after examining model selection, measurement sensitivity, sample selection, reverse causality, and alternative explanations."

**关键特征**:
- 样本数字精确（firms, appointments, recalls, years）
- 效应量具体且带百分比（77% higher）
- "We theorize that this may occur because" → 每个发现都附带理论解释
- "This is supported empirically, as we find that" → 发现→理论→更多发现的循环
- "In fact" → 递进到最反直觉的发现
- 结尾一句话概括稳健性检验的多个维度

**适用**: 效应量显著、有调节效应、需要展示数据可信度的实证论文

### 变体 K：紧凑四段式（vadakkepatt2022 型）

**模板**:
> "The tests of our hypotheses rely on [data description]. We find a significant, [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV1], providing novel evidence of [phenomenon characterization]. We also replicate previous findings of a [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV2] but identify a [direction] counteracting effect when we account for [mediator]. Consistent with our expectations, we also show that [moderator 1], [moderator 2], [moderator 3], and [moderator 4] each [direction] moderate the [IV-DV1] relationship. Finally, [mechanism] helps explain the [IV-DV1] link."

**来源**: vadakkepatt2022 (JM), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "The tests of our hypotheses rely on an unbalanced panel of 758 observations involving 87 publicly traded firms during the period 2000–2014. We find a significant, negative effect of lobbying on customer satisfaction, providing novel evidence of the dark side of firm lobbying. We also replicate previous findings of a positive effect of lobbying on firm value but identify a negative counteracting effect when we account for customer satisfaction. Consistent with our expectations, we also show that the CEO's background, advertising spend, R&D spend, and product market lobbying each positively moderate the lobbying–customer satisfaction relationship. Finally, a decrease in customer focus helps explain the negative lobbying–customer satisfaction link."

**关键特征**:
- **一句话数据说明** → **主效应** → **复制+修正已有发现** → **调节效应** → **机制**
- 四句话覆盖主效应、中介、调节、机制，极度紧凑
- "replicate previous findings... but identify" → 先承认已有发现，再用新视角修正
- "providing novel evidence of" → 将统计发现上升为概念性证据（如"dark side"）
- 适合复杂模型（1主效应+1中介+4调节+1机制）但期刊要求简洁的 Introduction

**适用**: 模型复杂但 Introduction 篇幅受限；需要同时展示复制已有发现和新增发现的论文

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 发现预览 → 必须有实证数据支撑
- 不要在发现预览中列举所有假设: Preview 是选择性预告，不是假设列表
- 不要只说 "we find support for our hypotheses": 必须说方向、大小、或意外发现
- 两段必须语义连接：机制预览解释"为什么"，发现预览展示"我们找到了什么证据"
