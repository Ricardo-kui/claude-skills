# Preview Templates — 机制预览与发现预览句法

## 功能描述

P5-P6 的功能：在理论框架建立之后，向读者预告"我们做了什么、发现了什么"。这是 Introduction 中的"承诺"段落——读者由此判断是否值得继续读下去。Preview 通常分为两类：(1) 机制预览——理论逻辑的简要陈述；(2) 发现预览——实证结果的预告。

---

## 类�� 1：机制预览（Mechanism Preview）

### 变体 A：理论整合型

**模板**:
> "Drawing on [theory] ([citation]), we argue that [core mechanism]. Specifically, we theorize that [construct A] and [construct B] pertain to different facets of [phenomenon]: [A] focuses on [facet 1], and [B] refers to [facet 2], so they are intertwined in explaining [outcome]. To test this theory, we examine [empirical setting]."

来源: zhou2017 (ASQ), P4-P5

**关键特征**:
- "Drawing on [theory]" → 明确理论来源
- "pertain to different facets of" → 展示理论整合而非二元选择
- "To test this theory, we examine..." → 从理论自然过渡到实证

---

### 变体 B：分层机制型

**模板**:
> "We theorize that [mechanism 1] mediates the relationship between [X] and [Y]. When [condition], [mechanism 1] operates through [sub-mechanism A], leading to [outcome A]; conversely, when [opposite condition], [mechanism 2] dominates, producing [outcome B]. We further argue that this effect [varies by moderator / cascades to second-level outcome]."

来源: keeves2017 (ASQ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- 先陈述核心中介机制
- 再分层展开不同条件下的不同路径
- "We further argue" → 增加理论丰富度

---

### 变体 C：挑战性预测型

**模板**:
> "Drawing on [theory], we argue that [phenomenon] creates [novel construct]—a [definition]. We theorize that [construct] [mediates/moderates] the relationship between [X] and [Y], leading to a counter-intuitive prediction: [prediction]. This prediction challenges the conventional assumption that [conventional view]."

来源: gamache2023 (SMJ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- 以反直觉预测结尾，制造悬念
- "challenges the conventional assumption" → 明确理论张力
- 适合 Inadequacy/Incommensurability

---

## 类型 2：发现预览（Findings Preview）

### 变体 D：经济显著性型

**模板**:
> "Using [data description] over [time period], we find that [main finding]. The impact is economically significant: [quantification—effect size in practical terms]. We also find [additional finding]. However, [nuance/condition/boundary]."

来源: eilert2017 (JM) + malshe2015 (JM)

**关键特征**:
- 数据+方法简要预览
- 经济显著性必须在 Intro 中给出具体数字
- "However" 展示 nuance，暗示不止一个简单发现

---

### 变体 E：反例驱动型

**模板**:
> "We find that, although [group] may, on average, [dominant behavior], [contingency] matters. As [example 1 with specific data], [counter-example that contradicts average]. These examples and our systematic evidence raise the question of whether [contingency] influences [relationship], which we answer by [approach]."

来源: gamache2023 (SMJ), P2

**关键特征**:
- 用具体反例（人名、公司名、数字）建立不可辩驳的直观证据
- "on average" vs "contingency matters" → 从平均效应到异质性的经典转
- 反例递进排列以增强冲击力

---

### 变体 F：两层因果链型

**模板**:
> "Using [data] and [method], we first examine how [IV] affects [DV1]. We find [finding about IV→DV1]. We then turn to the consequences of [DV1] by examining whether [DV2] and whether this [varies by moderator]. We find that [finding about DV1→DV2]. This two-stage analysis reveals [overall insight]."

来源: darby2024 (MSOM), P5-P6

**关键特征**:
- 先预览第一层因果，再预览第二层
- "We then turn to" → 自然过渡到第二层
- 适合 IV→DV1→DV2 的研究设计

---

### 变体 G：调节效应预览型

**模板**:
> "Our results support the prediction that [main effect]. In particular, [specific effect size with baseline comparison]. We further find that this relationship is moderated by [moderator 1] and [moderator 2], such that [direction of moderation]. These findings suggest that [broader implication]."

来源: ceo_regulatory_focus_ijrm (IJRM), P4-P5

**关键特征**:
- 主效应→效应量→调节效应→含义，四层递进
- 调节效应方向明确（不说"moderates"而不说方向）
- 以 broader implication 收束

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 机制预览 → Theory 段之后，发现预览之前
- 发现预览 → 必须有实证数据支撑，不能空承诺
- 两段必须语义连接：机制预览解释"为什么"，发现预览展示"我们找到了什么证据"

### 反模式提醒
- **不要在发现预览中列举所有假设**: Preview 是选择性预告，不是假设列表
- **不要把机制预览写成理论综述**: 2-3 句核心逻辑即可
- **不要只说 "we find support for our hypotheses"**: 必须说方向、大小、或意外发现
- **经济显著性必须有基线对比**: 不能说 "effect is large" 而不说 "large relative to what"
