# 02-explicit-dual-question-narrowing — 显式双问题收窄法

## 功能描述

将 broad inadequacy gap 收窄为两个可回答的具体研究问题，以显式问句（带问号）作为 Introduction 中段的焦点锚定。与变体 01（编号列表式三问）不同，本变体将 RQ 嵌入一个连贯段落中，以 "To fill this void, we ask:" 作为 preamble，然后给出两个有理论层次（主效应→边界条件）的问句。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Inadequacy** 或 **Incompleteness**（Incommensurability 通常以 debate 框架组织）
- 研究有 2 个清晰可分离的研究问题（主效应 + 调节/边界）
- 期刊接受显式问句（JMS, JM, SMJ, AMJ；ASQ/ASR 较少使用）
- Introduction 结构偏紧凑（5-6 段），需要 RQ 段同时承担 gap 收窄和预览功能

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **EMERGING** (1 paper): weng_yang (JMS)

### 生成力
- **GENERATIVE**: "To fill this void, we ask: [RQ1]? [RQ2]?" 框架可迁移到任何有主效应+调节的研究

### 排他性
- **LOW**: 所有 Gap 类型均可使用，但 Incommensurability 通常以 debate 框架组织 RQ

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：显式双问题收窄型（weng_yang 型）

**模板**:
> "Specifically, we contend that [IV construct] — as reflected in [operational definition] — affects [actor]'s [outcome action] to address the issue of [DV phenomenon]. Researchers have found that [IV] is crucial in influencing [related DV 1] ([citations]), [related DV 2] ([citations]), and [related DV 3] ([citations]). Nevertheless, it remains unclear how [IV] affects [specific DV]. To fill this void, we ask: [RQ1: main effect question]? [RQ2: moderator question]?"

**来源**: weng_yang (JMS), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "Nevertheless, it remains unclear how CEO political ideology affects vertical pay disparity within firms. To fill this void, we ask: how does CEO political ideology affect within-firm pay disparities between a CEO and his or her employees? Which contextual factors moderate this proposed effect?"

**关键特征**:
- 以 "Specifically, we contend that..." 开启——直接从 gap 跳到核心主张，不绕弯子
- "Nevertheless, it remains unclear" 作为二级 tension marker——在 P2 的 inadequacy 之后再收窄到具体 RQ
- 两个显式问句（带问号）作为段落结尾——非常直白的风格，视觉上清晰标记了论文要回答的问题
- "To fill this void, we ask:" 作为 RQ 前的明确信号句——为读者做好预期管理，区别于隐式 RQ（陈述句伪装）
- 两个 RQ 有理论层次：RQ1 是主效应（does X affect Y?），RQ2 是边界条件（what moderates?）——而非两个并列的不相关问题

**适用**: 适用于研究问题清晰可陈述为 1-2 个问句的场景；期刊接受显式 RQ（JMS, JM, SMJ, AMJ）；RQ 最好有一个主效应问 + 一个调节问——展示研究的递进逻辑；特别适合 Gap 类型为 Inadequacy（因为 Inadequacy 的 gap 本身就暗示了一种 direction，RQ 是让这个 direction 可检验）

**禁忌**: "ASQ/ASR 审稿人可能觉得显式问句过于直白——考虑将问句转为陈述句（'We examine whether...'）；两个 RQ 必须理论上有层次（主效应→边界条件），不应是并列的不相关问题；不要用超过 2 个问句——3+ 个 RQ 应使用变体 01（编号列表式）；'To fill this void' 的前提是前文确实建立了 void——如果 P2 的 inadequacy 论证不充分，这句会放大弱点"

### 变体 B：直接双问句整合理论透镜型（cui_vertinsky 型）

**模板**:
> "Our study addresses these limitations by integrating [theory A] and [theory B] perspectives in order to study the mechanisms through which [IV] influences [DV]. We ask, how does [IV construct] affect [actor]'s [DV action] in [context], and how does [boundary construct] influence this relationship?"

**来源**: cui_yang_vertinsky (SMJ), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "Our study addresses these limitations by integrating the alliance learning and network perspectives in order to study the mechanisms through which strategic alliances influence allies' competition in the product market. We ask, how does the composition of the collaborative portfolio between a firm and its partner affect the firm's competitive aggressiveness against its partner in the product market, and how does network embeddedness influence this relationship?"

**关键特征**:
- 无前导 "To fill this void, we ask:" ——直接以 "We ask," 给出双问题，节奏更紧凑
- 问句前先用一句话说明理论整合视角（"integrating [theory A] and [theory B] perspectives"），使 RQ 不是凭空出现
- 两个问句有清晰理论层次：RQ1 主效应（IV→DV），RQ2 边界条件（moderator）
- 问句后立即进入构念定义和机制预览，形成 "RQ → Construct → Mechanism → Boundary" 的紧凑链

**适用**: 当 RQ 前已充分建立 gap，不需要 "To fill this void" 这类显式信号句时；理论整合型研究（双视角）；SMJ/JM/JMS 紧凑风格

**禁忌**: 不要在前文 gap 论证不充分时使用直接 "We ask,"——缺少 preamble 会暴露 gap 薄弱；两个问句必须有理论层次，不能是并列的无关问题

### 变体 C：序贯双问 + 机制预期铺垫型（hoffmann2024 型）

**模板**:
> "In particular, we design an empirical strategy to address two main research questions. We first ask whether [treatment/shock] affects [outcome]. In this regard, we expect that if [reduced discipline condition], [private incentive to avoid immediate costs] could come to dominate [decision making]. This expectation is grounded in [primary theory documenting agency conflict] ([citation]) and [adjacent literature on short-termism] ([citation]). We then ask what factors could mitigate the [underlying conflict] underlying [opportunistic response] and thus weaken the main effect of [treatment] on [outcome]."

**来源**: hoffmann_cheong_phan_zurbruegg2024 (JM), P6

**原文锚定**:
> "In particular, we design an empirical strategy to address two main research questions. We first ask whether the adoption of UD laws affects the likelihood of firms to recall products. In this regard, we expect that if an external shock leads to less discipline being imposed on managers, the private incentives to try to avoid a recall to avert its immediate costs could come to dominate their decision making. This expectation is grounded in finance literature documenting agency conflicts in publicly listed firms (Jensen and Meckling 1976) and marketing literature on how such conflicts can induce managerial short-termism (Mizik 2010). We then ask what factors could mitigate the agency conflicts underlying a potential opportunistic response from managers to a reduction in shareholder litigation risk and thus weaken the main effect of UD law adoption on a firm's product recall likelihood."

**关键特征**:
- **"We first ask... We then ask..."** 序贯编号替代 "To fill this void, we ask"——适合 quasi-natural experiment 论文，RQ1 锁定处理效应、RQ2 锁定边界条件
- **RQ1 前嵌入机制预期**: "In this regard, we expect that if..." 把主效应 RQ 与 agency/short-termism 机制绑定，RQ 不是裸问句而是带理论方向的检验
- **RQ2 用 "mitigate the [conflict] underlying [response]"** 将调节问题锚定在已建立的代理冲突逻辑上，而非 generic "what moderates?"
- **与 Preview 融合**: 同一节继续展开 moderator 理论（business ethics 双机制）→ 数据 → 样本限制 → 发现预告，RQ 段是长 Preview 的起手

**适用**: Incompleteness × (Mechanism + Boundary)；quasi-natural experiment / 法律冲击设计；JM/SMJ 接受 "We first/then ask" 序贯 RQ 的期刊

**禁忌**: RQ1 前的 "we expect" 必须与 Theory 假设方向一致——不能 Intro 预期 A 方向、Theory 假设 B 方向；不要用超过两个 "We ask"——第三层问题应转入 Hypothesis 编号而非继续 RQ 问句

### 变体 D：双阶段 ex post / ex ante 问题组型（thirumalai2011 型）

**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）

**功能节拍**: ex post 后果问题组（主问题 bullet + 两个破折号子追问，含威慑充分性追问）→ 显式时间转折（"While the questions above are focused on... (ex post), ... points to... (ex ante). In other words,"）→ ex ante 前因问题组（主问题 + 一个子追问）→ "This study is an attempt to address these questions" 收束

**模板**:
> "[Puzzle 陈述: The prevalence of X in [industry], despite [stakes], raises many questions:]
>
> • What are the [consequences] of [phenomenon] in [industry]?
>
> —Are the [consequences] severe enough to [expected deterrent/incentive effect]?
>
> —How do the [consequences] vary across [heterogeneous actors]?
>
> While the questions above are focused on the [consequence domain] of [phenomenon] and its subsequent impact on [actor] behavior (ex post), the prevalence of [phenomenon] also points to the inability of [actors] to overcome [underlying problem]. For [actors] to overcome such [failures], it is important to identify factors that systematically increase or decrease the likelihood of [phenomenon] (ex ante). In other words,
>
> • What are the [sources/antecedents] of [phenomenon] in [actors]?
>
> —Are some [actors] more likely than others to [experience phenomenon]?
>
> This study is an attempt to address these questions."

**来源**: thirumalai_sinha2011 (MS), P5-P7

**原文锚定**:
> "The prevalence of quality failures in the medical device industry, despite the health risks to consumers, raises many questions:
> • What are the financial consequences of recalls in the medical device industry?
> —Are the performance implications severe enough to deter firms from introducing potentially hazardous products into the market?
> —How do the financial consequences vary across firms in the medical device industry?
> While the questions above are focused on the performance implications of quality failures and its subsequent impact on firm behavior (ex post), the prevalence of quality failures also points to the inability of firms to overcome defects in the medical device industry. For firms to overcome such failures, it is important to identify factors that systematically increase or decrease the likelihood of having device failures (ex ante). In other words,
> • What are the sources of product recalls in medical device firms?
> —Are some firms more likely than others to have device failures?
> This study is an attempt to address these questions."

**关键特征**:
- **RQ 分组结构本身即研究设计预告**：ex post 组（后果 + 威慑充分性 + 异质性）映射 event study + 横截面回归；ex ante 组（前因 + 异质倾向）映射 panel count 模型——读者从 RQ 结构就能预测论文有两套独立实证
- **显式时间标记**："(ex post)" / "(ex ante)" 直接写进转折段——把现象的时间结构（失败发生→后果显现 vs 失败之前→什么导致失败）提升为组织问题的框架
- **子追问用破折号缩进**：主问题用 bullet "•"，子问题用 "—" 缩进——视觉层级区分"研究问题"与"对研究问题的细化追问"，比全部平列更清晰
- **威慑充分性追问**（"severe enough to deter firms from..."）是政策含义追问——把后果实证连接到市场纪律/监管哲学（Bromiley & Marcus 1989 市场威慑框架），使 null 发现（不够 severe）自动获得政策重要性
- **同一 prevalence 事实被使用两次**：ex post 组和 ex ante 组都从"质量失败频发"派生——"In other words," 把同一现象重新框定为前因问题，一鱼两吃而不重复论证

**与变体 A-C 的区别**: 变体 A（weng_yang）/B（cui_vertinsky）/C（hoffmann2024）都是**单组**双问（主效应 + 调节）；本变体是**两组问题**（后果组 + 前因组），组间用显式时间框架转折。当研究同时回答"X 的后果是什么"和"什么导致 X"两个问题时使用——两组 RQ 对应两套独立实证模型与两个文献流

**适用**: 现象驱动研究同时检验事件的 ex post 后果与 ex ante 前因（召回、事故、违约、诉讼等）；两套实证模型各自独立（不同 DV、不同方法、不同样本）；Inadequacy × (Phenomenon + Mechanism) 组合；MS/MSOM/POM/JOM 等接受编号 RQ 列表的期刊

**禁忌**:
- 两组 RQ 必须对应两套**真实独立**的实证分析——若 ex ante 组只是稳健性检验，不要用双组结构
- "(ex post)" / "(ex ante)" 标记必须真实反映时间方向——后果测量窗口在事件之后、前因测量在之前
- 不要为对称而对称：本文 ex post 组 3 问、ex ante 组 2 问——问题数量由文献缺口决定，不要求两组等长
- 威慑充分性追问仅在后果有明确政策/监管含义时使用；纯学术问题不要强加政策追问
- 双组 RQ 的 Theory 章节也必须双区组织（本文 §2.2-2.3 后果假设 + §2.4 前因假设）——RQ 分组与 Theory 结构必须同构

### 变体 E：First/Second 条件收窄型（li_narayanan_2026_jscm 型）

**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）

**模板**:
> "This [article/study] focuses on two research questions. First, is there a [phenomenon] such that [buyer event] causes [outcome] for [third-party actors]? Second, if so, what factors affect the strength of the [phenomenon]?"

**来源**: li_bapuji_talluri_singh_narayanan_2026_jscm (JSCM), §1 P4

**原文锚定**:
> "This article focuses on two research questions. First, is there a vertical spillover effect such that a buyer's recall causes firm value losses for its suppliers? Second, if so, what factors affect the strength of the vertical spillover effect?"

**关键特征**:
- **"Second, if so," 条件逻辑**：RQ2 仅在 RQ1 成立时才有意义——比 weng_yang 的并列双问更贴近**存在性→边界**的实证结构（先 event study H1，再 OLS H2–H4）
- **无 "To fill this void" preamble**：前文 Tension 已建立 horizontal-only 缺口，RQ 段直接收窄，节奏紧凑
- **RQ 后立即接 Theory lens**：social network + pipes/prisms 预览，形成 RQ→Lens→Mechanism→Extended network 链

**与变体 A–D 的区别**: A/B/C 用 "we ask" 或 bullet 结构；本变体用 **First/Second 编号 + 条件从句**，不嵌入机制预期（机制在 RQ 后一段展开，非 hoffmann 式 RQ 内嵌 "we expect"）

**适用**: 主效应（存在性/幅度）+ 调节/边界条件的两阶段实证；溢出/传播类 event study + 横截面回归；JSCM/JOM/MSOM

**禁忌**:
- RQ1 必须是可 event-study 或等价检验的**存在性问题**——若主效应已是已知共识，不应再问 "is there"
- "if so" 要求 Results 先报告主效应再报告调节——顺序不可颠倒
- 不要用超过两个编号 RQ——第三层问题应转入 Hypothesis 编号

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 Tension 配对**: RQ 必须在 Tension（Gap）之后，收窄 gap 为可操作问题
- **与 Preview 配对**: RQ 提出后必须有 Preview 段回应（"Building upon this central argument, we investigate..."）
- **与 Contribution 配对**: 两个 RQ 对应两个贡献维度（如 RQ1→Phenomenon 贡献，RQ2→Boundary 贡献）

### 互斥
- **不适合与 Incommensurability 直接配对**: Incommensurability 的 RQ 通常以 "debate" 或 "paradox" 框架组织，而非 "we ask: [question]?"

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只问不答**: 每个 RQ 必须在分析中有对应的假设检验
- **不要让 RQ 和 Hypothesis 完全重复**: RQ 是 broad question，Hypothesis 是 specific prediction
- **不要在 RQ 中做理论承诺**: RQ 是问题——不要在 question mark 前夹带 "Drawing on..." 等理论框架引入（理论引入应在 P2/P4）

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[RQ1: main effect question]` — 主效应研究问题

❌ "How does CEO political ideology affect firm outcomes?" → 太宽泛——"firm outcomes" 可以是任何东西

✅ "How does CEO political ideology affect within-firm pay disparities between a CEO and his or her employees?" → 具体到 DV、actors、context

**填充检查**: RQ1 必须包含 (a) IV (b) DV (c) 分析单元。三者缺一即太宽泛。

### `[RQ2: moderator question]` — 调节效应研究问题

❌ "What are the boundary conditions?" → 太 generic——审稿人会问 "which ones?"

✅ "Which contextual factors moderate this proposed effect?" → 暗示有具体的 factors（后文会列举），但不在此处展开

**填充检查**: RQ2 应暗示 boundary condition 的类型（contextual/organizational/individual level），但不需在此处命名具体的 moderator——给 Preview 留空间。
