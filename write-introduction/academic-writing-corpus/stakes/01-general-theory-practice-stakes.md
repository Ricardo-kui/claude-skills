# 01-general-theory-practice — 通用 Stakes（理论重要性 + 实践后果）

## 功能描述

当研究**不含量化经济损失**（`02-quantified-economic-loss`）、**不含股价/资本市场结果**（`05-firm-value-stock-market`）、也**不涉及声誉/丑闻/合法性**（`07-reputation-legitimacy-crisis`）时，使用本模板回答 "so what"。这是管理学期刊中**使用频率最高**的 Stakes 类型——它用理论重要性论证学术价值，用实践后果论证管理相关性。

与 `03-disciplinary-gap-stakes` 的区别：本模块不聚焦于学科结构性缺口，而是聚焦于**具体研究问题的理论深化和实践启示**。

## 适用场景

- 研究结果是理论深化、机制澄清、构念辨析（无直接财务/市场后果）
- 研究关注组织过程、决策机制、人际动态、制度演化
- 需要同时说服学术读者（理论贡献）和实践读者（管理启示）
- **所有期刊的通用基底**：ASQ 偏好理论端，JM 偏好实践端，AMJ/SMJ/OS 要求两端平衡

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (≥5 papers): zhou2017 (ASQ), keeves2017 (AMJ), han2020 (AMJ), pollock2015 (ASQ), pontikes2012 (ASQ)
- 跨越理论整合、机制发现、情境效应、市场分类等多元领域

### 生成力
- **GENERATIVE**: "Understanding [X] is important because..." 是学术写作中最基础、最可迁移的 Stakes 句式

### 排他性
- **LOW**: 几乎所有论文都需要 Stakes；本模块是当其他专门化 Stakes 不适用时的默认选择

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：理论整合型（zhou2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Understanding how [phenomenon] affects [outcome] is critical because [theoretical tradition 1] and [theoretical tradition 2] offer competing predictions. [Tradition 1] predicts [prediction A], whereas [tradition 2] suggests [prediction B]. Resolving this tension is essential not only for [theoretical advancement] but also for [practical guidance], because [actor] must navigate both [logic 1] and [logic 2] in [context]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P1-P2

**原文锚定**:
> "Understanding how state ownership affects firm innovation is critical because institutional theory and efficiency logic offer competing predictions. Institutional theory predicts that state ownership constrains innovation by imposing political goals, whereas efficiency logic suggests state ownership facilitates innovation by providing resources and reducing uncertainty. Resolving this tension is essential not only for advancing our theoretical understanding of state ownership but also for guiding managers and policy makers in emerging economies."

**关键特征**:
- "Understanding how [X] affects [Y] is critical because..." → 最标准的通用 Stakes 开场
- 两个理论传统的对垒作为 Stakes 的理论深度来源
- "not only for... but also for..." 平衡理论+实践

**适用**: 理论整合/竞争逻辑型研究（制度vs效率、代理vs stewardship、交易成本vs关系治理等）

---

### 变体 B：机制盲区型（keeves2017 型）

**模板**:
> "This is important because [mechanism] has [theoretical consequence] that prior research has overlooked. [Authority / seminal work] has argued that [established insight], yet [gap in mechanism understanding]. Without understanding [mechanism], [actor] cannot [desired action], leading to [negative outcome]."

**来源**: keeves2017 (AMJ), P2-P3

**原文锚定**:
> "This is important because ingratiation has unintended consequences that prior research has overlooked. Westphal and colleagues have argued that ingratiation helps CEOs build social capital, yet the psychological mechanisms through which ingratiation backfires remain poorly understood. Without understanding why ingratiation leads to resentment, CEOs cannot effectively manage their relationships with board members."

**关键特征**:
- "This is important because [mechanism] has [consequence] that prior research has overlooked" → 直接锚定机制缺口
- 引用权威建立对话合法性
- 从理论盲区自然过渡到实践盲区（"cannot effectively manage"）

**适用**: 机制发现型研究（揭示某行为背后的未被认识到的机制）

---

### 变体 C：情境误判型（han2020 型）

**模板**:
> "This is problematic because [stakeholder] may [misjudge / misallocate / misreact] for reasons that have little to do with [focal actor] and may impose [penalties / rewards] that are both [quality 1] and [quality 2]. Without understanding [contextual factor], [actor] cannot [desired action], and [broader system] suffers."

**来源**: han2020 (AMJ), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "This is problematic because audiences may be more or less drawn to transgressions for reasons that have little to do with the focal actor and may impose penalties that are both inconsistent and unfair. Without understanding how context shapes scandalization, managers cannot effectively manage their firms' reputational risks."

**关键特征**:
- "This is problematic because..." → 直接陈述不研究的负面后果
- "for reasons that have little to do with [focal actor]" → 强调系统性误判的不公平性
- 从个体误判上升到系统后果

**适用**: 情境/边界条件研究（如果不理解情境，评估就会出错）

---

### 变体 D：构念混淆代价型（pollock2015 型）

**模板**:
> "Clarifying the relationship between [construct A] and [construct B] is essential because [stakeholders] rely on [assessment mechanism] to make [decisions], and confusion between [A] and [B] leads to [systematic error]. For [actor], this confusion means [practical consequence]. For scholars, it means [theoretical consequence]."

**来源**: pollock2015 (ASQ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "Clarifying... is essential because..." → 构念辨析型研究的标准 Stakes
- 同时展示对利益相关者（投资者、消费者、决策者）和学者的双重后果
- 将抽象构念混淆转化为具体决策错误

**适用**: 构念辨析、测量改进、分类澄清型研究

---

### 变体 E：过程不可见代价型（pontikes2012 型）

**模板**:
> "Although [phenomenon] is increasingly prevalent, we know little about [process/dynamic]. This is consequential because [actor] must make [decisions] under [condition], yet existing frameworks do not account for [nuance]. As a result, [systematic bias/error] persists."

**来源**: pontikes2012 (ASQ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "increasingly prevalent" → 建立现象的时效性和普遍性
- "existing frameworks do not account for [nuance]" → 将缺口框定为理论框架局限
- "systematic bias/error persists" → 暗示如果不解决，错误会持续

**适用**: 过程理论、动态演化、新兴现象型研究

## 插入位置指南

| 布局类型 | 推荐插入位置 | 形式 | 长度 |
|---------|------------|------|------|
| 紧凑型 | Gap 段末尾（1-2句） | Inline | "Understanding [X] is important because [theory]. In addition, [practice]." |
| 标准型 | 独立 P4（2-3句） | 独立段 | 理论重要性 → 实践后果 |
| 扩展型 | 独立 P4 + 引用背书（3-4句） | 独立段 | 权威引语 + 理论后果 + 实践后果 + 系统后果 |

**期刊差异**:
- **ASQ**: 偏好扩展型的理论端（"not only for advancing theory..."），实践端可压缩到1句
- **AMJ**: 要求标准型的两端平衡，各占1-2句
- **SMJ**: 偏好"战略行动者 cannot [action]"的实践后果句式
- **JM/JMR**: 偏好紧凑型，直接嵌入 Gap 段末尾
- **OS**: 偏好"broader implications"和"systematic"语言

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **必须在 Gap 之后出现**: Stakes 是缺口重要性的论证
- **必须与目标期刊的 stakes 偏好配对**: ASQ 理论优先，JM 实践优先
- **必须具体化**: 不能只说"this is theoretically important"，必须说"为什么重要"（对哪个理论、哪个辩论）

### 互斥
- **不能与 `02-quantified-economic-loss` 同用**: 若研究有明确可量化的经济后果，优先用量化型
- **不能与 `05-firm-value-stock-market` 同用**: 若研究涉及股价/估值，优先用资本市场型
- **不能与 `07-reputation-legitimacy-crisis` 同用**: 若研究涉及声誉/丑闻，优先用声誉型
- **不能与 `03-disciplinary-gap-stakes` 同用**: 若研究的核心贡献是"引入外部/跨学科因素"，优先用学科缺口型

**选择原则**: 本模块是**默认选项**——当以上专门化 Stakes 都不适用时，使用本模块。

### 反模式提醒
- **不要说"this is important"而不解释 why**: "Understanding X is important" 后面必须跟 "because..."
- **不要把 stakes 写成贡献**: Stakes 是"不解决会怎样"，贡献是"解决了能学到什么"
- **不要两端都空泛**: 理论端不能只说"advances theory"，实践端不能只说"has managerial implications"
- **不要忽略受众**: 必须明确"对谁说重要"——学者、管理者、政策制定者、投资者

| 如果你的研究... | 使用 Stakes |
|----------------|------------|
| 有明确的财务/市场损失数字 | `02-quantified-economic-loss` |

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[quantified cost / scale]` — 量化后果

❌ "This issue has significant economic implications for firms worldwide." → 无数字、无来源、无具体对象

✅ "An estimated $[X] billion in government subsidies is allocated annually to politically connected firms — resources explicitly justified as promoting innovation. If political ties distort allocation rather than enhancing it, a substantial share of this public investment may be misdirected." → 有数字框架（$X billion）+ 具体 stakeholder + 如果 Gap 不解决的后果链

**不能量化时**: 改用 narrative Stakes（haunschild2015 的 14 条生命）或 theoretical Stakes（"without this mechanism, X theory makes systematically wrong predictions in Y condition"）。不要用模糊的 "billions of dollars" 糊弄。

### `[who suffers]` — 谁承担后果

❌ "Managers need to understand this relationship." → 哪些 managers？在什么情境下？

✅ "Supply chain managers in high-velocity industries, where recall timing can mean the difference between a $17 million fine and a $900 million penalty, need to understand when rapid response attenuates damage versus when it triggers additional liability." → 具体群体（supply chain managers in high-velocity industries）+ 具体情境 + 数字对比
