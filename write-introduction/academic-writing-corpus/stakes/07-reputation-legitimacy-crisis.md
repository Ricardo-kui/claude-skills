# 07-reputation-legitimacy-crisis — 场域合法性/声誉危机 Stakes

## 功能描述

在 Gap 建立之后，论证"so what"——如果不解决这个研究问题，会对组织声誉、场域合法性或社会评价产生系统性后果。这个 Stakes 模块的核心逻辑是：研究缺口不仅仅是一个学术空白，它会导致管理者、监管者或公众对组织行为的误判，进而引发声誉危机或合法性丧失。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及组织声誉、丑闻、合法性、社会评价
- 需要论证"为什么这个缺口有实际后果"而非仅仅是"文献遗漏"
- 场域层面的后果（行业/制度）而非仅仅是单个企业的绩效
- 目标期刊偏好社会/制度层面的 stakes（ASQ、AMJ、OS、SMJ）

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (≥2 papers): Desai 2011 (AMJ), Pfarrer 2010 (AMJ) — 原始验证来源
- MVP30 中最接近的论证模式在 han2020 (AMJ), paruchuri2020 (SMJ), singh2023 (JMR) 中出现

### 生成力
- **ADAPTABLE**: 声誉/合法性危机逻辑在 misconduct、recall、spillover、CSR 等子领域高度可迁移

### 排他性
- **HIGH**: 与组织声誉、丑闻、危机管理研究强绑定

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：去情境化后果型（han2020 型）

**模板**:
> "This is problematic because [consequence 1]. Further, most research on [topic] has treated [construct] as decontextualized. This is also problematic because the context in which an event or action occurs can differentially shape assessments and responses, sometimes even inverting the relationships ([citations]). Thus, '[quote about context importance]' ([citation]). We have little insight, however, about how context shapes [effect]. Without understanding [contextual factor], [stakeholders] may [negative consequence]."

**来源**: han2020 (AMJ), P1-P2

**原文锚定**:
> "Further, most research on the relationship between status and scandalization has treated the misbehaving actor's status as decontextualized. This is also problematic because the context in which an event or action occurs can differentially shape assessments and responses, sometimes even inverting the relationships."

**关键特征**:
- "This is problematic because" → 直接陈述不研究的后果
- "may impose penalties that are both inconsistent and unfair" → 声誉危机的具体表现
- "sometimes even inverting the relationships" → 暗示忽略情境会导致方向性误判
- 引述权威声音强化语境重要性的论点

---

### 变体 B：声誉溢出误判型（paruchuri2020 型）

**模板**:
> "Further, most research on [topic] has focused on situations where [dominant valence]. We are aware of only one study where [opposite valence]... While they explored this effect, [limitation 1]... They also [limitation 2]... Without a clearer understanding of [mechanism], [stakeholders] risk [misjudging/misallocating resources/misattributing cause]."

**来源**: paruchuri2020 (SMJ), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "Further, most research on reputation spillovers has focused on situations where a focal firm's negative action has negative spillover effects. We are aware of only one study where the valence of the spillover is different than the valence of the action... Piazza and Jourdan (2018) found that... While they explored this effect over a long time period, the 'pedophile priest' sex scandals continued to be in the news... keeping the Catholic Church's wrongdoing cognitively available... They also inferred reputational effects by observing parishioners' behaviors, rather than directly assessing Catholics' perceptions of the reputations of other denominations after the scandal broke."

**关键特征**:
- 用"only one study"强调知识的稀缺性
- 同时指出例外研究的方法局限
- 暗示如果不解决，利益相关者将持续误判溢出效应

---

### 变体 C：系统性风险不可分散型（desjardine2023 型）

**模板**:
> "Although [phenomenon] may seem [positive attribute], it has created a new [type of challenge]: [specific dilemma]. Such risk is critical to manage because [reason]. At the same time, [existing solutions] cannot [solve problem] because [limitation]. Therefore, as [actor] contend with [escalating challenge], the question arises: [research question]."

**来源**: desjardine2023 (OS), P2-P3

**原文锚定**:
> "Although common ownership—and the power it bestows—may seem advantageous for investors, it has created a new investment challenge: When a single investor owns multiple firms in an industry, it becomes even more necessary to manage systematic risk... Such risk is critical to manage because one firm's errant behavior can inflict harm on an entire industry of bystander firms (Paruchuri and Misangyi 2015), amplifying losses for the industry and its common owners... At the same time, systematic risk cannot be reduced by diversification... or divestment... Therefore, as common owners contend with an elevated exposure to systematic risk, the question arises: How can common owners use their unique boundary-spanning ownership positions..."

**关键特征**:
- "Although... it has created a new challenge" → 从表面好处转向深层困境
- 指出已有工具（diversification, divestment）无法解决新问题
- 系统性风险的语言适合制度/场域层面的 stakes

---

### 变体 D：不对称惩罚型（singh2023 型）

**模板**:
> "The consequences of [phenomenon] extend beyond [immediate outcome] to [broader consequence]. [Stakeholders] face [specific risk/dilemma]. Without understanding [mechanism], [actor] cannot [desired action], leading to [systematic negative outcome]."

**来源**: singh2023 (JMR), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "extend beyond X to Y" → 将 stakes 从企业层面扩展到制度/社会层面
- 不对称性（企业承受后果 vs 监管者缺乏工具）是关键的 stakes 信号

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **必须在 Gap 之后出现**: Stake 的功能是回答"so what"，因此必须在明确指出研究缺口之后
- **与具体的研究领域后果配对**: 不能只说"important"，必须说具体对谁重要、为什么重要

### 互斥
- **不能与 `02-quantified-economic-loss` (Stakes) 同用**: 经济损失是量化+效率逻辑，声誉危机是定性+制度逻辑，重复使用会造成 stakes 冗余
- **不能出现在 Gap 之前**: Stakes 是缺口重要性的论证，逻辑上不能前置

### 反模式提醒
- **不要说"this is important"而不解释"to whom, for what"**: 声誉危机 Stakes 必须明确受影响的利益相关者（管理者、监管者、投资者、公众）
- **不要用模糊的"implications for practice"替代具体的声誉机制**: 必须说明声誉如何受损、合法性如何丧失
- **不要把 stakes 写成贡献**: stakes 是"不解决会怎样"，贡献是"解决了能学到什么"
