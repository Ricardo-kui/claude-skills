# 08-goal-conflict — 目标冲突型 Stakes

## 功能描述

当研究涉及**两个有价值的目标可能互斥**时，用 "goal conflict" 结构回答 "so what"。核心逻辑：如果 [A] 和 [B] 都是企业/决策者想要的，但追求 [A] 会损害 [B]，那么决策者将面临两难——这个两难就是研究的 stakes。

与 `01-general-theory-practice` 的区别：通用 Stakes 分别论证理论和实践重要性；本模块强调两个目标之间的**张力本身**即是重要性——不是"分别重要"，而是"冲突让决策者无法两全"。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及两个有价值但可能互斥的目标（如 CSR vs 价格竞争力、创新 vs 稳定性、合规 vs 效率）
- 可以具体描述一个条件句式的决策困境
- 目标期刊接受实践导向的 stakes 论证（JM、JMR、SMJ、MSOM 尤其适配）
- Incompleteness × Mechanism / Incompleteness × Phenomenon 组合中最常见

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：条件式目标冲突型（habel2016 型）

**模板**:
> "This prevailing lack of clarity is not only troublesome for [theoretical domain] but also problematic for [practical domain], because [stakeholder] lacks guidance on how to [action]. If [competing outcome A], [stakeholder] will face a goal conflict, requiring [trade-off management]. Thus, examining [linkage] is highly relevant to [domain-specific outcome]."

**来源**: habel2016 (JM), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "This prevailing lack of clarity is not only troublesome for marketing research but also problematic for managerial practice, because firms lack guidance on how to effectively manage the price-related consequences of their CSR. If being perceived as socially caring comes at the expense of a firm's price image, the firm will face a goal conflict, requiring careful management of the trade-off between being perceived as fair in terms of social engagement and fair in terms of price. Thus, examining the CSR–price fairness linkage is highly relevant to the effectiveness of firms' strategies to manage price-related consequences of their social activities."

**关键特征**:
- **"goal conflict"** — 核心概念。将 stakes 具体化为可观察的决策困境，而非 generic "theoretically important"
- **"not only...but also"** — 同时覆盖理论和实践 stakes
- **条件句 "If [A], [stakeholder] will face [conflict]"** — 使后果可想象。读者可以立即在脑中构建场景
- **"requiring [trade-off management]"** — 指出困境的具体后果（需要权衡管理）

**适用**: 两个有价值目标可能互斥的研究（CSR vs profitability, innovation vs stability, compliance vs efficiency, exploration vs exploitation）

**禁忌**:
- 两个竞争目标必须都是有价值的（不能一个有价值一个无价值，否则不是 "conflict"）
- 条件句必须可想象，不能太抽象（"If innovation comes at the expense of stability" ✓ / "If A comes at the expense of B" ✗ 太泛化）
- 不要只有 "not only...but also" 而缺少 "goal conflict" 核心句——双重要性≠目标冲突

---

### 变体 B：目标冲突 + 量化支撑型（建议扩展）

**模板**:
> "If [competing outcome A], [stakeholder] will face a goal conflict, requiring [trade-off management]. [Quantified evidence] underscores this dilemma: [N]% of [stakeholders] report [manifestation of conflict] ([source]). Thus, examining [linkage] is highly relevant to [domain-specific outcome]."

**与变体 A 的差异**: 在 goal conflict 后追加量化数据支撑，增强说服力。适用于有行业数据可引用的场景。

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` (Tension) 配对**: Incompleteness Tension 指出遗漏，goal conflict Stakes 将遗漏升级为决策困境
- **与 `12-contrary-to-belief` (Hook) 配对**: 反直觉发现建立问题，goal conflict 展示问题的实践后果

### 互斥
- **不能与 `02-quantified-economic-loss` (Stakes) 同用**: 量化经济损失是单向 stakes（损失多少），goal conflict 是双向张力（A vs B）——两者逻辑不同

## 期刊适配

| 期刊 | 适配度 | 注意事项 |
|------|--------|---------|
| JM/JMR | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 极高 | 实践导向的 stakes 最适配 JM 风格 |
| SMJ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 高 | 需要更强的理论 side——"not only" 理论部分需充实 |
| MSOM | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 高 | 运营决策的两难情境天然适配 |
| AMJ | ⭐⭐⭐ 中 | 需要同时论证理论重要性（不能只靠实践困境） |
| ASQ | ⭐⭐ 中低 | ASQ 偏好纯理论 stakes，goal conflict 可能被认为"太实践" |

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[goal conflict]` — 两个有价值目标互斥

❌ "This is theoretically important and has practical implications." → generic，审稿人无法判断重要性在哪

✅ "If being perceived as socially caring comes at the expense of a firm's price image, the firm will face a goal conflict, requiring careful management of the trade-off between being perceived as fair in terms of social engagement and fair in terms of price." → 具体化：两个目标是什么、为什么冲突、后果是什么
