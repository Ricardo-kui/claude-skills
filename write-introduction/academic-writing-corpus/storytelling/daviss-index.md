# Davis's Index of the Interesting

## 来源

Davis, M. S. (1971). That's interesting! *Philosophy of the Social Sciences*, 1(4), 309-344.

Pollock 在 Ch05 中将其作为判断贡献"有趣性"的核心工具。

> "When trying to assess what makes your study interesting, peruse this list and assess which types of interestingness your study invokes. It also provides you with the language necessary to explain why your contribution is interesting." — Pollock 2025, Ch05

## 26 种有趣性类型

| 类型 | 英文表述 | 中文解释 | 学术写作中的典型表达 |
|------|---------|---------|-------------------|
| **Order from Chaos** | What seems disorganized is really organized | 看似无序实则有结构 | "What appears chaotic is actually governed by..." |
| **Chaos from Order** | What seems organized is really unstructured | 看似有序实则无序 | "What appears systematic is actually..." |
| **Simplicity in the Complex** | What seem like heterogeneous phenomena are really a single phenomenon | 异质现象实为单一 | "Despite their surface differences, X and Y share a common underlying..." |
| **Complexity in the Simple** | What seems like a single phenomenon is really heterogeneous | 单一现象实为异质 | "What appears to be a uniform process is actually composed of..." |
| **The Psychological is Social** | What seems like an individual phenomenon is really holistic | 个体现象实为整体 | "What appears to be an individual decision is actually shaped by..." |
| **The Social is Psychological** | What seems like a holistic phenomenon is really individual | 整体现象实为个体 | "What appears to be a collective outcome is actually driven by individual..." |
| **The Social-Psychological** | What seems holistic or individual is really a property of the relation | 看似整体或个体，实则是关系属性 | "Neither the individual nor the group alone explains...; rather, it is the relationship between..." |
| **Local is General** | What seems like a local phenomenon is really generalizable | 局部现象具有普遍性 | "What was previously considered unique to X actually applies to..." |
| **General is Local** | What seems like a general phenomenon is really local | 普遍现象实为局部 | "What was assumed to be universal is actually contingent on..." |
| **Unobserved Dynamism** | What seems stable is really changing | 看似稳定实则变化 | "What appears stable is actually in continuous flux..." |
| **Unobserved Regularity** | What seems unstable is really regular | 看似变化实则有规律 | "What appears erratic actually follows a systematic pattern..." |
| **Unobserved Functionality** | What seems ineffective is really functional | 看似无效实则有用 | "What appears to be a dysfunction is actually serving a functional purpose..." |
| **Unobserved Dysfunction** | What seems functional is really ineffective | 看似有用实则无效 | "What appears to be an effective practice is actually counterproductive..." |
| **Unobserved Good** | What seems bad is really good | 看似坏实则是好 | "What appears to be a liability is actually an asset..." |
| **Unobserved Bad** | What seems good is really bad | 看似好实则是坏 | "What appears to be beneficial is actually harmful..." |
| **Unobserved Correlation** | What seem independent are really interrelated | 看似独立实则相关 | "What were assumed to be unrelated are actually connected..." |
| **False Correlation** | What seem interrelated are really independent | 看似相关实则独立 | "What were assumed to be related are actually independent..." |
| **Surprise Coexistence** | What seem unable to coexist can really coexist | 看似不能共存实则可以 | "What were thought to be mutually exclusive can actually coexist..." |
| **False Coexistence** | What seem able to coexist really cannot | 看似可以共存实则不能 | "What were assumed to be compatible are actually mutually exclusive..." |
| **False Similarity** | What seem similar are really opposite | 看似相似实则相反 | "What appear to be similar processes are actually fundamentally different..." |
| **False Difference** | What seem different are really the same | 看似不同实则相同 | "What appear to be distinct phenomena are actually manifestations of..." |
| **False Positive** | What seems like positive covariation is really negative | 看似正相关实则负相关 | "What was expected to increase actually decreases..." |
| **False Negative** | What seems like negative covariation is really positive | 看似负相关实则正相关 | "What was expected to decrease actually increases..." |
| **Other False Variants** | Incremental is continuous, etc. | 其他形状误判 | "What was assumed to be linear is actually curvilinear..." |
| **Dependent Variable is IV** | What seems like the predictor is really the outcome | 看似自变量实则因变量 | "What was treated as a cause is actually an effect..." |
| **Independent Variable is DV** | What seems like the outcome is really the predictor | 看似因变量实则自变量 | "What was treated as an outcome is actually a cause..." |
| **One-Way Relationship is Complex** | What seems like a direct relationship is really mutual | 看似单向实则双向 | "What was assumed to be a one-way effect is actually a reciprocal..." |

## 与 Makadok 贡献维度的交叉映射

| Makadok 维度 | 最匹配的 Davis 类型 | 为什么匹配 |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Constructs** | False Similarity / False Difference | 构念辨析 = 重新定义"看似相同/不同" |
| **Mechanism** | Order from Chaos / Chaos from Order | 机制揭示 = 发现"看似无序/有序"背后的规律 |
| **Boundary** | Local is General / General is Local | 边界条件 = 挑战"普遍/局部"假设 |
| **Level** | The Psychological is Social / The Social is Psychological | 跨层 = 挑战"个体/整体"归因 |
| **Mode** | Unobserved Functionality / Unobserved Dysfunction | 模式转换 = 揭示"有效/无效"的反转 |
| **Question** | Dependent Variable is IV / Independent Variable is DV | 问题重构 = 反转因果方向 |
| **Output** | Unobserved Good / Unobserved Bad | 结果重估 = 重新判断"好/坏" |
| **Phenomenon** | Surprise Coexistence / False Correlation | 现象发现 = 揭示"共存/独立"的新模式 |

## 使用方式

### 诊断问题

向用户提问：

1. "你的研究挑战了哪个被广泛持有的假设？"
   - 如果能回答 → 对应 False Similarity / False Difference / Local is General 等
2. "你的发现与主流预期相反的地方在哪里？"
   - 如果能回答 → 对应 False Positive / False Negative / Unobserved Bad 等
3. "你的研究揭示了哪个被忽视的机制或规律？"
   - 如果能回答 → 对应 Order from Chaos / Unobserved Dynamism 等
4. "你的研究是否反转了因果方向？"
   - 如果能回答 → 对应 Dependent Variable is IV 等

### 最少匹配规则

- **至少 1 种**：Contribution 的有趣性得到支撑（推荐在 Contribution 中突出这 1-2 种）
- **0 种**：不自动标记为问题——Davis's Index 是充分条件，不是必要条件。但建议用户确认："如果不匹配任何 Davis 类型，你的贡献是否通过其他方式（如方法创新、重要复制、精确测量）建立了价值？"
- **2 种以上**：选择最核心的 1-2 种在 Contribution 中突出，避免稀释焦点

### 在 Contribution 骨架中的嵌入

**标准句式**：

> "Our study challenges the prevailing assumption that [被挑战的假设] by showing that [反直觉发现]. This suggests that [Davis 类型对应的重构]."

**示例**（Order from Chaos）：

> "Our study challenges the prevailing assumption that digital transformation initiatives fail randomly by showing that their failure follows a systematic pattern tied to organizational routine rigidity. This suggests that what appears chaotic is actually governed by predictable institutional mechanisms."

## 注意事项

1. **Davis's Index 是充分条件，不是必要条件**。有些贡献（如精细的方法改进、重要的复制研究）可能不触发任何 Davis 类型，但仍然有价值。
2. **不要强行匹配**。如果研究确实不挑战任何假设，应诚实说明，而不是强行套用。
3. **与 HARKing 的界限**。Davis's Index 用于**诊断已有研究的有趣性**，而不是**事后编造有趣性**。如果用户在数据分析后才"发现"有趣性 → 标记 ⚠️ "可能存在 HARKing 风险"。

## 范文标注

### Greenwood & Suddaby (2006) — Institutional entrepreneurship

- **Davis 类型**: Unobserved Bad（看似好的制度稳定性实际上限制了变革）+ Dependent Variable is IV（看似因变量的制度实际上可以被主动改变）
- **表述**: "Contrary to extant theory, according to which the network centrality of elites embeds them within prevailing logics of action and dulls them to the possibilities of change, we show how such a network location can sharpen awareness of alternatives."

### Hirsch & Lounsbury (1997) — Old vs new institutionalism

- **Davis 类型**: False Difference（看似不同的新旧制度主义实际上可以共存）+ Surprise Coexistence（看似对立的两种制度主义实际上可以同时成立）
- **表述**: "We believe that if taken literally, however, the dichotomous contrast they set up between the old and the new is a false and misleading one."

### Soublière & Gehman (2020) — Legitimacy threshold

- **Davis 类型**: False Correlation（看似独立的合法性评估实际上是相互关联的）+ Local is General（Kickstarter 的发现可以推广到更广泛的创业情境）
- **表述**: "Extant research has depicted resource providers as relatively independent audience members, which understates the fact that their legitimacy assessments are sensitive to social cues."
