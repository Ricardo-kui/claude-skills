# 04-reality-contradicts-consensus — 现实与共识矛盾 Tension

## 功能描述

在 Gap 段揭示文献共识或理论预测与经验现实之间的系统性矛盾。这是 Incommensurability 问题化的核心 Tension：不是"文献有缺口"，而是"文献说的和现实发生的不一致"，暗示需要新的理论框架来调和矛盾。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incommensurability**（不同理论/证据推出不兼容解释）
- 已有明确的理论预测（来自已有理论）
- 实证证据与理论预测方向相反或结果混杂
- 两个对立理论各自有支持证据，但各自只解释了一部分

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (≥3 papers): 在 ASQ (zhou2017, keeves2017), ASR (hahl2017), SMJ (gamache2023) 中独立出现
- 跨越从组织理论到文化社会学的多个领域

### 生成力
- **GENERATIVE**: "Whereas prior studies show that X, empirical evidence is mixed" 模板可高度适配

### 排他性
- **HIGH**: 几乎只在 Incommensurability 中出现，是区分 Incommensurability 与 Inadequacy 的标志性 Tension

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：文献不一致 + 实证混杂型（zhou2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Whereas prior studies generally show that [dominant finding] (see [review], for a review), empirical evidence is mixed on [specific topic]. Some studies report that [finding A]... In contrast, other studies have shown that [finding B]... Meanwhile, [finding C]... To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies, we theorize that [theory 1] and [theory 2] pertain to different facets of [phenomenon]: [theory 1] focuses on [facet 1], and [theory 2] refers to [facet 2], so they are intertwined in explaining [outcome]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "Whereas prior studies generally show that SOEs perform worse than their private counterparts... empirical evidence is mixed on the effects of state ownership on innovation. Some studies report that state ownership has negative effects... In contrast, other studies have shown that SOEs produce more patent registrations... Meanwhile, Choi, Park and Hong (2012) found that state ownership has no significant bearing... To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies... we theorize that the institutional and efficiency logics pertain to different facets of state ownership: the institutional logic focuses on resource allocation, and the efficiency logic refers to resource utilization..."

**关键特征**:
- "Whereas prior studies generally show... empirical evidence is mixed on..." → 经典文献不一致开场
- 系统呈现三种发现（负、正、无），而非只挑有利的
- "To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies... we theorize that..." → 整合句式，从"问题"自然过渡到"方案"
- 将对立理论分配到一个构念的不同 facet

---

### 变体 B：反例+数据爆发型（gamache2023 型）

**模板**:
> "Nevertheless, although [group] may, on average, [dominant behavior], many do [opposite behavior], and some make substantial [extreme behavior]. As [example 1], [specific case with data]. Others have made larger [examples 2 and 3 with data]. These examples and the research noted above raise the question of [research question]."

**来源**: gamache2023 (SMJ), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "Nevertheless, although female CEOs may, on average, make fewer acquisitions than male CEOs, many do acquire, and some make substantial acquisitions. As CEO of Yahoo!, Marissa Mayer acquired 53 companies, spending well over $2 billion in 4 years. Others have made larger acquisitions, such as Hewlett Packard CEO Carly Fiorina's purchase of Compaq for $25 billion and IBM's Ginni Rometty's $34 billion acquisition of Redhat. These examples and the research noted above raise the question of whether important contingencies influence the relationship between CEO gender and acquisitions."

**关键特征**:
- "Nevertheless, although... on average... many do..." → 经典让步-转折
- 用具体人名、公司名、金额建立不可辩驳的反例
- 三个反例递进（53家→$25B→$34B）增强冲击力
- "raise the question of whether important contingencies influence..." → 自然过渡到研究问题

### 变体 C：经典理论 vs 当代现象型（hahl2017 型）

**模板**:
> "The phenomenon of [phenomenon] has been widely documented, but the social mechanisms behind it are still unclear. [Existing explanation] seems to be part of the story... Yet [limitation 1]. Moreover, [observation that existing theory cannot explain]. As we will discuss, [alternative characteristic] appears to derive from [source]."

**来源**: hahl2017 (ASR), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "The phenomenon of elite consumption of lowbrow culture has been widely documented, but the social mechanisms behind it are still unclear. The pursuit of 'distinction' through manifesting superior taste seems to be part of the story... Yet insofar as cultural omnivorousness can be achieved by consuming a wide range of genres, it is unclear why this cannot be accomplished by restricting consumption to a wide range of highbrow genres. Moreover, the lowbrow genres elites consume are often touted less for conveying superior taste than for their 'authenticity.'"

**关键特征**:
- "has been widely documented, but... still unclear" → 承认现象存在但机制未知
- "seems to be part of the story... Yet..." → 部分承认现有解释但指出其不充分
- "Moreover" → 追加第二个更致命的局限
- 将"authenticity"作为现有理论无法解释的 residual 引入

### 变体 D：悖论核心陈述型（keeves2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Although extant theory and research has yielded considerable insight and convincing evidence on the consequences of [behavior], it has focused almost entirely on the beneficial outcomes of [behavior] for [actor A]. The [related literature] has provided compelling evidence, however, that [asymmetry insight]. Scholars have devoted little theoretical or empirical attention to understanding how [behavior] may influence [actor B] in the opposite direction."

**来源**: keeves2017 (ASQ), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "Although extant theory and research has yielded considerable insight and convincing evidence on the consequences of ingratiatory behavior, it has focused almost entirely on the beneficial outcomes of ingratiation for the person who engages in flattery and opinion conformity. The social network literature has provided compelling evidence, however, that social relations are not necessarily symmetrical... Scholars have devoted little theoretical or empirical attention to understanding how engaging in ingratiation may influence the valence of the ingratiator's feelings toward the target..."

**关键特征**:
- "focused almost entirely on [one direction]" → 精确指出文献的不对称关注
- "The [related] literature has provided compelling evidence, however, that..." → 从相邻文献借理论资源来挑战主文献
- "asymmetry" 是 Incommensurability 的核心信号词

### 变体 E：双受众对立评价型（pontikes2012 型）

**模板**:
> "There are at least two roles for [audiences] in a [domain]: [Role A], who [function A], and [Role B], who [function B]. Previous studies that document [dominant finding] take the perspective of [Role A]—for them, [characteristic] makes [target] [negative valence] because [mechanism A]. Previous literature has not investigated how [Role B] react to [same characteristic]. [Role B] should *prefer* [same characteristic] because [opposite mechanism]. Thus the way [characteristic] is regarded depends on the perspective of the person evaluating. For [Role A], [characteristic] makes [target] [negative]. But for [Role B], this same [characteristic] represents [opposite positive]."

**来源**: pontikes2012 (ASQ), P3-P4

**原文锚定**:
> "There are at least two roles for audiences in a market: 'market-takers,' who consume or evaluate goods, and 'market-makers,' who construct markets by developing new niches and enforcing boundaries. Previous studies that document the hazards of unclear classification take the perspective of market-takers... Previous literature has not investigated how audiences in the role of market-makers react to ambiguity... Market-makers should *prefer* ambiguous classification because it allows organizations the flexibility to develop novel offerings... Thus the way an ambiguous identity is regarded depends on the perspective of the person evaluating the organization. For market-takers, ambiguous classification makes organizations unclear. But for market-makers, this same ambiguity represents flexibility."

**关键特征**:
- **"There are at least two roles..."**: 以角色区分开场，不攻击任何一方——两个视角各自在其领域内是正确的
- **以斜体强调反直觉预测**: "should *prefer*"——视觉标记让读者不会错过核心反转
- **最后一句话是整篇 Introduction 的黄金收束**: 对称结构，同一主语（this same ambiguity），相反谓语（makes unclear vs represents flexibility）
- **不同于其他 Incommensurability 变体**: 不是"实证发现与理论预测矛盾"，而是"同一个现象的相反评价同时成立"——矛盾不在现象与理论之间，而在两个受众之间
- **天然为 Constructs 贡献奠基**: Role A vs Role B 的区分本身就是构念贡献

**适用**: Incommensurability × Constructs 组合；任何"X 对不同受众有相反意义"的研究；ASQ 标志性 facet 分解策略（将矛盾归因于不同视角而非声称一方错误）

**禁忌**: 不要制造虚假对称——必须证明两个受众都重要（都有真实的经济/战略后果），而不是一个明显比另一个重要

### 变体 F：理论预测 vs 宏观量化事实型——替代透镜的入场券（zhou2017 P3 型）

**模板**:
> "According to [Lens A / theory], [prediction of decline / failure / marginalization] ([citations]). According to this view, [actors] should [predicted outcome]. **In reality, however**, [actors] have [opposite outcome — vivid contrast phrase] ([citations]). [Quantified macro / aggregate fact: [N] [actors] now [achievement metric]—[multiple]× more than in [base year]—about [proportion] of which are [focal type].] [An alternative lens / Lens B] helps to explain this [growth / puzzle]. According to [Lens B] ([citations]), [alternative mechanism that accounts for the reality]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P2–P3

**原文锚定**:
> "According to the conventional, efficiency-based economic view, mostly rooted in agency theory, state ownership plays a minor role in spurring firms' innovation and performance... According to this view, SOEs should gradually lose their innovativeness and competitiveness over time. In reality, however, many SOEs in emerging economies have evolved into dynamic dynamos, rather than the predicted dying dinosaurs (Ralston et al., 2006; Musacchio and Lazzarini, 2014; Stan, Peng, and Bruton, 2014). China now has 106 companies in the 2015 Fortune Global 500—four times more than in 2006—about two-thirds of which are SOEs. An institutional perspective helps to explain this growth. According to the institutional view... governments... have critical influences on regulatory policies and control over scarce resources..."

**关键特征**:
- **"In reality, however"** — 现实反转理论预测的标志性 pivot
- **Vivid contrast phrase**（"dynamic dynamos, rather than the predicted dying dinosaurs"）——把抽象的"理论与现实矛盾"压缩成一对画面感对偶，让读者过目不忘
- **Quantified macro / aggregate fact**（106 家 Fortune Global 500、4× 增长、2/3 为 SOE）——用**经济体/行业层级的统计事实**而非个案或单篇研究来制造不可辩驳的反证
- **宏观事实是"替代透镜的入场券"**——矛盾本身被用作引入第二个理论透镜（institutional）的 warrant：现实无法用 Lens A 解释 → 需要 Lens B 来 account for the reality
- **区别于其他 Incommensurability 变体**:
  - ≠ 变体 A（多篇**个别研究**证据 mixed）
  - ≠ 变体 B（**具名个案**反例驳斥均值——Mayer/Fiorina）
  - ≠ 变体 C（经典理论 vs 当代现象，**机制不明**）
  - 本变体 = **理论的方向性预测** vs **宏观聚合统计事实** → 用以合法化**第二个理论透镜**的引入

**适用**: 当一个理论预测 decline / failure / marginalization，但宏观 aggregate 数据显示 growth / success / prevalence——用作引入替代透镜（institutional / resource-based / evolutionary）的 warrant。新兴市场（国企、家族企业、国家资本主义）、行业变迁、组织形态演变、技术复兴。

**禁忌**: 宏观事实必须**真实且可核查**（Fortune/World Bank/统计局等权威源），不可估算；"In reality, however" 前须先**公正陈述** Lens A 的预测（不可稻草人化）；替代透镜必须**真的能解释**该宏观事实（否则只是引入了一个同样无力的第二视角）；vivid contrast phrase 不可沦为噱头——必须与后续理论论证衔接。

### 变体 G：共识惩罚 vs 行为持续 + 修辞问 pivot（pontikes2012 P1–P2 型）

**模板**:
> "**A consensus is building** that [X is penalized / detrimental] ([citations across N contexts]). It seems that when [actors do X], they [suffer the penalty]. **Despite this**, in many contexts, [actors] continue to [do X], [especially in setting where X is widespread]. If [the consensus is right that X is penalized], **then how does [this widespread X-pattern] come to be?** The answer may lie in [a heterogeneity the consensus ignores—e.g., different audiences' evaluations of X]."

**来源**: Pontikes (2012, ASQ), P1–P2

**原文锚定**:
> "A consensus is building that organizations with multiple market identities are hard to understand and therefore less likely to be successful on a range of outcomes than more focused competitors. This has been demonstrated in a number of contexts, from discounted valuations on the stock market (Zuckerman, 1999) to fewer sales on eBay (Hsu, Hannan, and Koçak, 2009), to lower ratings for feature films (Hsu, 2006)... Despite this, in many contexts, organizations continue to expand their offerings across market labels and identify with labels that have a relatively unclear social meaning. This is especially evident in the software industry... If part of an organization's identity is derived from its label affiliation, and organizations suffer when they do not have clear identities, then how does an ambiguous classification structure like the software industry's come to be? The answer may lie in different audiences' evaluations of the labels with which organizations affiliate."

**关键特征**:
- **"A consensus is building"** — 先建立一个**强、单一方向**的共识（X 受惩罚），用跨 N 个情境的引用加固（股市折价 / eBay 销量低 / 电影评分低）。注意：这不是 mixed evidence（≠ 变体 A），而是**单方向强共识**——矛盾因此更尖锐。
- **"Despite this"** — reality pivot：尽管共识说 X 受惩罚，actor 仍**持续做 X**（行为持续 / 扩散）。矛盾不在"证据 mixed"，而在"**共识预测的惩罚不足以阻止行为**"。
- **Rhetorical question pivot**（"how does [this widespread X-pattern] come to be?"）——用修辞问把"行为持续"从 anomaly 升级为理论问题，制造悬念。
- **"The answer may lie in"** — 预告 resolution：共识忽略了一个**异质性**（不同受众），正是它解释了为何 X 不被普遍惩罚（某些受众反而 reward X）。
- **区别于其他 Incommensurability 变体**:
  - ≠ 变体 A（个别研究证据 mixed）
  - ≠ 变体 B（具名个案反均值）
  - ≠ 变体 F（宏观事实反理论预测）
  - 本变体 = **强共识预测的惩罚不 deter 行为** → 共识必不完整 → 某个被忽略的异质性（受众类型 / 情境条件 / 时间阶段）在 reward X。这是 "persistence despite penalty" 型 puzzle。

**适用**: 当文献有**强共识**说某行为/特征受惩罚，但现实中该行为/特征**持续存在或扩散**——用作引入"被忽略的异质性"（受众类型、情境条件、时间阶段）的 warrant。分类与身份（ambiguous identity 持续）、公司治理（被诟病的实践持续）、战略（看似次优的选择普及）、制度（低效惯例顽固）。

**禁忌**: 共识必须**真实且强**（不可为了制造矛盾而夸大共识强度；若证据其实 mixed，应用变体 A 而非本变体）；"行为持续"必须有可观察证据（行业现象、扩散数据），不可仅凭印象；修辞问必须**本文能回答**（resolution 须在后文兑现，pontikes2012 即用受众二分回答）；"被忽略的异质性"须是共识**真的没考虑**的维度，不可是稻草人。

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `06-paradigm-challenge` (Hook) 配对**: 范式挑战建立颠覆性开场，现实矛盾 Tension 提供具体证据支撑
- 或与 `04-puzzle-paradox` (Hook) 配对：谜题 Hook 设置悬念，现实矛盾 Tension 将其系统化为理论问题

### 互斥
- **不能与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` (Tension) 同用**: 前者是"已有进展但遗漏"，这是 Incompleteness 逻辑；现实矛盾是 Incommensurability 逻辑，两者问题化策略互斥

### 反模式提醒
- **不要选择性呈现证据**: 必须系统呈现正、负、无三种实证发现，不能只挑支持自己立场的
- **不要在缺乏真实反例时声称"现实矛盾"**: 反例必须有具体来源（案例、数据、文献）
- **不要说"results are mixed"而不解释为什么 mixed**: 你的理论框架正是用来解释为什么结果会 mixed
