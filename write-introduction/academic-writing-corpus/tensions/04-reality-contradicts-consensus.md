# 04-reality-contradicts-consensus — 现实与共识矛盾 Tension

## 功能描述

在 Gap 段揭示文献共识或理论预测与经验现实之间的系统性矛盾。这是 Incommensurability 问题化的核心 Tension：不是"文献有缺口"，而是"文献说的和现实发生的不一致"，暗示需要新的理论框架来调和矛盾。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incommensurability**（不同理论/证据推出不兼容解释）
- 已有明确的理论预测（来自已有理论）
- 实证证据与理论预测方向相反或结果混杂
- 两个对立理论各自有支持证据，但各自只解释了一部分

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (≥3 papers): 在 ASQ (zhou2017, keeves2017), ASR (hahl2017), SMJ (gamache2023) 中独立出现
- 跨越从组织理论到文化社会学的多个领域

### 生成力
- **GENERATIVE**: "Whereas prior studies show that X, empirical evidence is mixed" 模板可高度适配

### 排他性
- **HIGH**: 几乎只在 Incommensurability 中出现，是区分 Incommensurability 与 Inadequacy 的标志性 Tension

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：文献不一致 + 实证混杂型（zhou2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Whereas prior studies generally show that [dominant finding] (see [review], for a review), empirical evidence is mixed on [specific topic]. Some studies report that [finding A]... In contrast, other studies have shown that [finding B]... Meanwhile, [finding C]... To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies, we theorize that [theory 1] and [theory 2] pertain to different facets of [phenomenon]: [theory 1] focuses on [facet 1], and [theory 2] refers to [facet 2], so they are intertwined in explaining [outcome]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "Whereas prior studies generally show that SOEs perform worse than their private counterparts... empirical evidence is mixed on the effects of state ownership on innovation. Some studies report that state ownership has negative effects... In contrast, other studies have shown that SOEs produce more patent registrations... Meanwhile, Choi, Park and Hong (2012) found that state ownership has no significant bearing... To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies... we theorize that the institutional and efficiency logics pertain to different facets of state ownership: the institutional logic focuses on resource allocation, and the efficiency logic refers to resource utilization..."

**关键特征**:
- "Whereas prior studies generally show... empirical evidence is mixed on..." → 经典文献不一致开场
- 系统呈现三种发现（负、正、无），而非只挑有利的
- "To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies... we theorize that..." → 整合句式，从"问题"自然过渡到"方案"
- 将对立理论分配到一个构念的不同 facet

---

### 变体 B：反例+数据爆发型（gamache2023 型）

**模板**:
> "Nevertheless, although [group] may, on average, [dominant behavior], many do [opposite behavior], and some make substantial [extreme behavior]. As [example 1], [specific case with data]. Others have made larger [examples 2 and 3 with data]. These examples and the research noted above raise the question of [research question]."

**来源**: gamache2023 (SMJ), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "Nevertheless, although female CEOs may, on average, make fewer acquisitions than male CEOs, many do acquire, and some make substantial acquisitions. As CEO of Yahoo!, Marissa Mayer acquired 53 companies, spending well over $2 billion in 4 years. Others have made larger acquisitions, such as Hewlett Packard CEO Carly Fiorina's purchase of Compaq for $25 billion and IBM's Ginni Rometty's $34 billion acquisition of Redhat. These examples and the research noted above raise the question of whether important contingencies influence the relationship between CEO gender and acquisitions."

**关键特征**:
- "Nevertheless, although... on average... many do..." → 经典让步-转折
- 用具体人名、公司名、金额建立不可辩驳的反例
- 三个反例递进（53家→$25B→$34B）增强冲击力
- "raise the question of whether important contingencies influence..." → 自然过渡到研究问题

### 变体 C：经典理论 vs 当代现象型（hahl2017 型）

**模板**:
> "The phenomenon of [phenomenon] has been widely documented, but the social mechanisms behind it are still unclear. [Existing explanation] seems to be part of the story... Yet [limitation 1]. Moreover, [observation that existing theory cannot explain]. As we will discuss, [alternative characteristic] appears to derive from [source]."

**来源**: hahl2017 (ASR), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "The phenomenon of elite consumption of lowbrow culture has been widely documented, but the social mechanisms behind it are still unclear. The pursuit of 'distinction' through manifesting superior taste seems to be part of the story... Yet insofar as cultural omnivorousness can be achieved by consuming a wide range of genres, it is unclear why this cannot be accomplished by restricting consumption to a wide range of highbrow genres. Moreover, the lowbrow genres elites consume are often touted less for conveying superior taste than for their 'authenticity.'"

**关键特征**:
- "has been widely documented, but... still unclear" → 承认现象存在但机制未知
- "seems to be part of the story... Yet..." → 部分承认现有解释但指出其不充分
- "Moreover" → 追加第二个更致命的局限
- 将"authenticity"作为现有理论无法解释的 residual 引入

---

### 变体 D：悖论核心陈述型（keeves2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Although extant theory and research has yielded considerable insight and convincing evidence on the consequences of [behavior], it has focused almost entirely on the beneficial outcomes of [behavior] for [actor A]. The [related literature] has provided compelling evidence, however, that [asymmetry insight]. Scholars have devoted little theoretical or empirical attention to understanding how [behavior] may influence [actor B] in the opposite direction."

**来源**: keeves2017 (ASQ), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "Although extant theory and research has yielded considerable insight and convincing evidence on the consequences of ingratiatory behavior, it has focused almost entirely on the beneficial outcomes of ingratiation for the person who engages in flattery and opinion conformity. The social network literature has provided compelling evidence, however, that social relations are not necessarily symmetrical... Scholars have devoted little theoretical or empirical attention to understanding how engaging in ingratiation may influence the valence of the ingratiator's feelings toward the target..."

**关键特征**:
- "focused almost entirely on [one direction]" → 精确指出文献的不对称关注
- "The [related] literature has provided compelling evidence, however, that..." → 从相邻文献借理论资源来挑战主文献
- "asymmetry" 是 Incommensurability 的核心信号词

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `06-paradigm-challenge` (Hook) 配对**: 范式挑战建立颠覆性开场，现实矛盾 Tension 提供具体证据支撑
- 或与 `04-puzzle-paradox` (Hook) 配对：谜题 Hook 设置悬念，现实矛盾 Tension 将其系统化为理论问题

### 互斥
- **不能与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` (Tension) 同用**: 前者是"已有进展但遗漏"，这是 Incompleteness 逻辑；现实矛盾是 Incommensurability 逻辑，两者问题化策略互斥

### 反模式提醒
- **不要选择性呈现证据**: 必须系统呈现正、负、无三种实证发现，不能只挑支持自己立场的
- **不要在缺乏真实反例时声称"现实矛盾"**: 反例必须有具体来源（案例、数据、文献）
- **不要说"results are mixed"而不解释为什么 mixed**: 你的理论框架正是用来解释为什么结果会 mixed
