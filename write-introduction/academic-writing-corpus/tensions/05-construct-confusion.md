# 05-construct-confusion — 构念混淆型 Inadequacy Tension

## 功能描述

Inadequacy 问题化的一种高价值变体：已有文献不仅"理解偏了"，而是**在构念层面陷入了混乱**——不同研究用不同标签指同一现象、用同一可操作化指标测不同构念、或在不同分析层次上使用同一概念。这种混淆导致文献碎片化，阻碍了能够解释和预测不同构念效果的连贯理论发展。

与 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong`（隐性假设错误）的区别：隐性假设错误关注的是"文献依赖了一个未经验证的假设"，而构念混淆关注的是"文献在构念定义和操作化上缺乏一致性"。
与 `03-structural-blindspot`（结构性盲点的区别）：结构性盲点关注的是"文献由于结构性原因遗漏了某类因素"，而构念混淆关注的是"文献把不同构念混为一谈"。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Inadequacy**（构念层面的理解偏差）
- 一个文献流中存在**多个相似构念**（如 reputation / celebrity / visibility / status / prominence）
- 不同研究**操作化方式不一致**（同一指标被声称测量不同构念，或不同指标被声称测量同一构念）
- 需要为**构念精细化**（construct refinement）或**构念辨析**（construct differentiation）建立缺口合法性
- 目标期刊接受构念贡献（AMJ, ASQ, SMJ, OS）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：双层构念混淆型（pfarrer2010 型）

**模板**:
> Much of this research, however, has focused on establishing the _general_ effects of possessing [broad construct] on [outcome] and has given little consideration to whether the _specific_ effects of different [subtypes] may vary. Further, this research has often given different labels to the same types of [phenomenon] or has used the same observable proxies to operationalize conceptually distinct constructs (see [citations] for recent critiques highlighting these issues). As a result, labels and definitions have proliferated, making it difficult to determine if different studies consider the same or different phenomena, leading to a fragmented body of work and limiting the development of theory that can explain and predict the effects of different [subtypes]. Thus, despite the large number of studies examining [topic], a coherent body of knowledge about the differences in these [constructs] and their effects has not been developed.

**来源**: pfarrer2010 (AMJ), P2

**原文锚定**:
> Much of this research, however, has focused on establishing the general effects of possessing social approval assets on firm performance and has given little consideration to whether the specific effects of different assets may vary. Further, this research has often given different labels to the same types of collective perceptions or has used the same observable proxies to operationalize conceptually distinct constructs (see Deephouse and Carter [2005], Deephouse and Suchman [2008], and Rindova et al. [2005] for recent critiques highlighting these issues). As a result, labels and definitions have proliferated, making it difficult to determine if different studies consider the same or different phenomena, leading to a fragmented body of work and limiting the development of theory that can explain and predict the effects of different intangible assets. Thus, despite the large number of studies examining the performance consequences of social approval assets, a coherent body of knowledge about the differences in these assets and their effects has not been developed.

**关键特征**:
- **双层结构**:
  - 第一层（分析层次混淆）：过度关注 general effects，忽视 specific effects
  - 第二层（操作化混淆）：不同标签指同一现象 + 同一指标测不同构念
- "(see [citations] for recent critiques highlighting these issues)" → **引用已有批评**，证明混淆是文献共同体已承认的问题，避免稻草人
- "As a result, labels and definitions have proliferated" → 展示混淆的系统性后果
- "fragmented body of work" + "limiting the development of theory" → 将操作化问题上升到理论发展受阻
- "Thus, despite the large number of studies... a coherent body of knowledge... has not been developed" → 经典"多而不精"收尾

**适用**: 构念精细化论文（区分两个相似构念）；需要同时挑战分析层次和操作化两个维度的研究

### 变体 B：构念纠缠型（pollock2015 型）

**模板**:
> A growing body of research has examined the [phenomenon] of [actors] by focusing on [construct A] (e.g., [citations]), [construct B] (e.g., [citations]), and [construct C] (e.g., [citations]). Although this research has generated considerable insight, the relationships among these [constructs] and their independent effects on [outcome] are not well understood. In particular, researchers have used the same measures to assess different [constructs] and have often discussed [construct A], [construct B], and [construct C] interchangeably. Because these [constructs] are likely to have both independent and interactive effects on [outcome], it is important to develop an empirical model that simultaneously examines their [distinct effects].

**来源**: pollock2015 (ASQ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- 先承认每个构念各自的文献积累
- 指出关系不明 + 操作化混用 + 讨论互换
- 用"independent and interactive effects"为后续同时检验铺垫

**适用**: 多个构念（3+）同时被混淆的研究情境

---

### 变体 C：操作化错配型（精简版）

**模板**:
> Although considerable research has examined [topic], scholars have frequently used the same [measures/proxies] to operationalize conceptually distinct constructs, or have applied different [labels] to the same underlying [phenomenon]. This has made it difficult to compare findings across studies and has limited the development of [theoretical goal]. We address this issue by [approach].

**来源**: pfarrer2010 (AMJ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- 更简洁的单层操作化混淆论证
- 适用于篇幅受限或混淆维度较单一的情境

### 变体 D：兄弟维度互换测量型（li_bapuji_talluri_singh_venkataraman_2026_pom 型）

**模板**:
> Prior research has produced valuable insights on the relationship between [umbrella construct] and [outcome] ([citations]). However, this stream of research [does not adequately theorize [umbrella] and] measures it using [dimension A] and [dimension B] interchangeably ([citations of studies that treat them as substitutes]). We argue that these are conceptually distinct dimensions with potentially different effects on [outcome]. [Dimension A] reflects [definition A]; it increases [challenge type A — e.g., monitoring / access / transportation]. In contrast, [dimension B] captures [definition B]; it exacerbates [challenge type B — e.g., coordination / institutional heterogeneity]. Recognizing this distinction is necessary to [improve design / theorize distinct effects] for better [outcomes].

**来源**: li_bapuji_talluri_singh_venkataraman_2026_pom (POM), §1 P2

**原文锚定**:
> Prior research has produced valuable insights on the relationship between supply chain geographical complexity and firm outcomes (Attari et al., 2025; Lu and Shang, 2017; Sharma et al., 2020). However, this stream of research has two limitations. First, prior work does not adequately theorize geographical complexity and measures it using geographical distance and geographical dispersion interchangeably (Dong et al., 2020; Lu and Shang, 2017; Sharma et al., 2020). We argue that these are conceptually distinct dimensions with potentially different effects on product quality. Geographical distance reflects the spatial separation between a focal firm and its suppliers; it increases the challenges of managing suppliers due to factors related to access, communication, and transportation. In contrast, geographical dispersion captures the diversity of supplier locations across multiple countries/regions; it exacerbates challenges in coordinating with suppliers due to heterogeneity in institutional environments and quality standards. Recognizing this distinction is necessary to improve supply chain design for better firm outcomes.

**关键特征**:
- **兄弟维度互换，不是标签增殖**: 文献把 umbrella 的两个维度（distance / dispersion）当可互换测量，而非给同一现象贴不同标签（变体 A/B）
- **定义→机制分工一步到位**: 每一定义句紧跟其主导挑战类型（distance→monitoring；dispersion→coordination），为 Theory 双轨埋伏笔
- **用混淆实践本身作 warrant**: 引用的是**做了互换测量的研究**（Dong / Lu & Shang / Sharma），不是独立 meta-critique——见组装规则例外
- **常作双缺口的 First**: li_bapuji_talluri_singh_venkataraman_2026_pom 把本变体作 First limitation，WEIRD 情境泛化作 Second（`tensions/18`）

**适用**: 伞形构念下两个（或多个）兄弟维度被文献互换测量/互换理论化；贡献落在 Constructs + 机制分工（而非单纯换样本）

**禁忌**:
- 两维度必须能给出**不同主导机制**；若只是测量冗余而无机制差异，不要用本变体抬升为 Inadequacy
- 指出互换后必须立刻给定义对；不可只骂混淆
- 不要与变体 A 的"labels proliferated"混用——本变体问题是 interchangeability，不是 label proliferation

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `05-literature-consensus-blindspot` (Hook) 配对**: 文献共识盲点建立"领域有积累"，构念混淆 Tension 揭示"积累中的混乱"
- **与 Constructs 贡献维度配对**: 构念混淆 Tension 的存在是为了justify构念精细化（区分A与B）或构念辨析（澄清定义边界）
- **混淆证据必须有出处**: 优先引用文献流内部的自我批评（"see [Author A] [year] for recent critiques"）；**例外（变体 D）**：若尚无独立 meta-critique，可引用**实际互换测量/互换讨论的研究**作为混淆实践证据，但须点名具体做法（interchangeably / same measure for different labels），禁止空断"prior research confuses"
- **双缺口时与 `18-context-generalizability` 配对**: 构念混淆（First）+ 情境泛化（Second）可同篇并存；两者都是 Inadequacy，互不替代

### 互斥
- **不能与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` (Tension) 同用**: 前者是 Incompleteness（遗漏），本品是 Inadequacy（混淆）
- **不能与 `04-reality-contradicts-consensus` (Tension) 同用**: 后者是 Incommensurability（理论对立），本品是构念层面的操作化混乱

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只批评别人不定义构念，自己不定义**: 指出混淆后，必须在后续段落立即给出清晰的新定义
- **不要把所有文献都说成混淆**: 必须区分"哪些文献确实做了清晰区分"和"哪些文献混淆了"
- **不要制造稻草人**: 引用具体文献证明混淆确实存在（如"Deephouse and Carter [2005]... Rindova et al. [2005]"；或变体 D 的互换测量实践）
- **不要把数据局限说成构念混淆**: 如果只是因为某构念难以测量而用代理变量，这是方法局限，不是构念混淆
- **Registry 与语料必须同步**: 不得只在 `_evidence_registry.yaml` 登记 source、却不写本文件变体（li_bapuji_talluri_singh_venkataraman_2026_pom 曾发生；2026-08-06 已回填）
