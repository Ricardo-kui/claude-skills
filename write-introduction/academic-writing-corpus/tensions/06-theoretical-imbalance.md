# 06-theoretical-imbalance — 理论不平衡型 Inadequacy Tension

## 功能描述

已有文献在理论上偏向某个方向（如制度变革、技术进步、积极效应），积累了大量研究，但对相反方向（如维护现状、技术倒退、消极效应）关注不足。这种不平衡导致理论发展片面，无法解释为什么许多现象未能按照主流理论预测的方向发展。

与 `04-reality-contradicts-consensus` 的区别：后者是"现实证据与理论共识矛盾"，而理论不平衡是"文献本身在理论方向上发展不均衡"——不是现实出了问题，而是学术共同体的问题化视角出了问题。

---

## 变体 A：理论偏向型（desai2012 型）

**模板**:
> "Although [field] recognizes that [general truth] ([citations]), examples exist of [phenomena] that fail to [expected outcome] following [events] ([citations]). These anecdotal examples highlight the lack of a formal theoretical framework for understanding [alternative question]. Indeed, although [field] originally aimed to [original goal] ([citations]), much attention has turned to [current trend] ([citations]). Perhaps presciently, [scholars] implied the need for greater research into '[new direction]' ([citation])."

**来源**: desai2012 (AMJ), P5-P6

**原文锚定**:
> "Although institutional theory recognizes that fieldwide disruptions can lead to significant change (Greenwood, Suddaby, & Hinings, 2002; Hoffman, 1999; Meyer, 1982; Suchman, 1995), examples exist of industries that fail to reorient, transform, or substantially improve following crises (Baumard & Starbuck, 2005; Perrow, 1984; Shrivastava, 1988). These anecdotal examples highlight the lack of a formal theoretical framework for understanding what actions organizations may pursue to preserve the status quo following disruptions in their field. Indeed, although new institutional theory originally aimed to explain forces that preserve the status quo (DiMaggio & Powell, 1983; Meyer & Rowan, 1977), much attention has turned to developing theories regarding institutional change (Arndt & Bigelow, 2000; Fiss & Zajac, 2006; George, Chattopadhyay, Sitkin, & Barden, 2006; Greenwood et al., 2002; Hoffman, 1999; Suddaby & Greenwood, 2005). Perhaps presciently, Arndt and Bigelow implied the need for greater research into 'organizational agency in the preventive use of the very institutional forces that create isomorphism' (2000: 513)."

**关键特征**:
- "Although [field] recognizes that..." → 先承认主流理论的正确性
- "examples exist of... that fail to..." → 用反例展示理论盲区
- "These anecdotal examples highlight the lack of a formal theoretical framework" → 从反例上升到理论缺口
- "Indeed, although... originally aimed to... much attention has turned to..." → **核心技巧：展示领域发展的不平衡**
- "Perhaps presciently" → 优雅地引用前人呼吁

**适用**: 制度理论、创新研究、组织变革等存在"变革偏向"的领域

## 变体 B：经典理论颠覆型（hahl2017 型）

**模板**:
> "For decades, [classic theory] has served as a foundational framework for understanding [phenomenon] ([citations]). [Theory] predicts that [prediction]. However, a growing body of evidence suggests that [counter-evidence] ([citations]). These observations raise a puzzle: why do [actors] [counter-intuitive behavior]? We argue that [existing theory] cannot adequately explain this phenomenon because [limitation]. Drawing on [alternative theory], we propose that [new explanation]."

**来源**: hahl2017 (ASR), P1-P2

**关键特征**:
- "For decades, [classic theory] has served as a foundational framework" → 尊重经典
- "However, a growing body of evidence suggests" → 用证据而非情绪挑战
- "raise a puzzle" → 将矛盾转化为研究机会
- "cannot adequately explain" → 明确 inadequacy
- "Drawing on [alternative theory]" → 提供替代框架

**适用**: 挑战经典理论（Weber, Bourdieu, Williamson 等）的研究

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 理论不平衡 Tension → 承认已有主流理论的文献积累（不能制造稻草人）
- 理论不平衡 Tension → Inadequacy Gap（因为不是文献遗漏了，而是文献"偏了"）
- 理论不平衡 Tension → 维护现状/反向行动的 Hook 或 Stakes

### 互斥
- **不能与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` 同用**: 前者是 Incompleteness（遗漏），本品是 Inadequacy（偏向）
- **不能与 `04-reality-contradicts-consensus` 同用**: 后者是 Incommensurability（现实与理论矛盾），本品是文献内部的方向性不平衡

### 反模式提醒
- **不要全盘否定主流理论**: "Although [field] recognizes..." 承认其正确性，只是指出盲区
- **不要用个别反例否定整个文献流**: 必须展示"系统性"的反例积累
- **不要忘记引用前人内部批评**: "Perhaps presciently, [Author] implied..."
- **不要把数据局限说成理论不平衡**: 如果只是因为某方向难以测量而研究少，这是方法局限，不是理论偏向
