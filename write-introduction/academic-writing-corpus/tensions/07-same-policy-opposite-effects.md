# 07-same-policy-opposite-effects — 同一政策相反效应张力

## 功能定义

展示同一个组织实践对两个不同子群体产生相反方向的影响，从而建立不可化约的张力——管理者必须面对的不是"要不要"的问题，而是"对谁有利/对谁有害"的权衡困境。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及组织政策对不同利益相关者/子群体的差异化效应
- 两个子群体在价值观、身份或意识形态维度上系统性地不同
- 同一实践对 Group A 有利，对 Group B 有害
- 目标期刊接受价值观/伦理/身份张力（OS 首选）

---

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：对称对立型（employee_free_speech2024 型）

**模板**:
> "How does the same [organizational practice] affect [outcome] when [stakeholders] systematically differ in [ideological/identity dimension]? [Group A], who [characteristic A], tend to [value A over B]. Thus, the practice should increase their [outcome]. In contrast, [Group B], who [characteristic B], tend to [value B over A]. Thus, the practice should decrease their [outcome]. This cross-cutting tension makes [outcome] simultaneously facilitated and undermined by the same [practice]."

**来源**: employee_free_speech2024 (OS), adapted

**原文锚定**:
> "We highlight the tension created when the same censorship policy fosters psychological safety for some individuals (i.e., liberals) while undermining psychological safety for others (i.e., conservatives). Organizations that censor employees for prejudiced social media posts should signal to liberals—who prioritize safeguards—that their concerns and opinions related to inclusivity and social equity are aligned with the organization's values and can be expressed freely without fear of being dismissed or disparaged (e.g., labeled 'woke'). But conservatives—who prioritize free speech—should feel less psychologically safe, as policies that exert such control in nonwork contexts marginalize their values."

**关键特征**:
- 以研究问句开场，直接声明差异化效应
- 两组论证完全对称，各占相近篇幅
- "cross-cutting tension" → 建立不可化约的悖论

---

### 变体 B：双轨机制型

**模板**:
> "We hypothesize that [practice] (a) increases [outcome] for [Group A] and (b) decreases [outcome] for [Group B]. The divergence arises because [Group A] interprets [practice] as [positive signal], whereas [Group B] interprets it as [negative signal], each rooted in divergent [value/ideology frameworks]."

### 反模式提醒

- **缺乏解决路径**: 只说"左右为难"但没有提出缓解策略 → 最高级的处理是找到调节因素
- **伪对立**: 表面相反，实则只是同向的大小差异 → 必须是真正的符号反转（H1a正, H1b负）
