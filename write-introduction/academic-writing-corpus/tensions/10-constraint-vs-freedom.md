# 10-constraint-vs-freedom — 约束 vs 自由张力

## 功能定义

展示组织行为者在"自由/选择空间"和"约束/系统逻辑"两种对抗性力量之间的紧张关系。揭示两种不同的秩序逻辑在同一个决策空间中不可调和地并存。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及技术/制度变化扩大了行为者的选择空间
- 但市场结构、制度环境或认知限制又回拉了这种自由
- 自由和约束来自不同的逻辑源（如技术降成本=自由，市场结构=约束）
- 目标期刊接受结构性张力论证（OS、SMJ）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：自由扩展-约束回拉型（zhao_ding2022 型）

**模板**:
> "The advent of [technology/condition] has sharply reduced the costs of [activity], greatly expanding [actor]'s [freedom / choice space]. In principle, [actor] would choose [ideal/unconstrained choice]. In practice, however, [actor] faces substantial [uncertainty/liquidity/legitimacy gap] about [specific constraint]. Hence, [actor] must reconcile [expanded freedom] with [binding constraint]. How [actor] navigates this tension shapes [outcome]."

**来源**: zhao_ding2022 (OS), adapted

**原文锚定**:
> "In principle, entrants would choose functional combinations that closely align with the target customers' needs and preferences. In practice, they often face substantial demand uncertainty about the functional attributes customers are likely to value in products."

**关键特征**:
- "The advent of... has sharply reduced" → 建立自由扩展
- "In principle... In practice, however..." → 经典双句法，引入约束
- "must reconcile... with..." → 明确张力不可逃避

---

### 变体 B：两种 logic 不可调和型

**模板**:
> "[Logic A] pushes [actor] toward [action A], promising [benefit]. Meanwhile, [Logic B] pushes [actor] toward [action B], threatening [cost] if ignored. The two logics cannot be simultaneously satisfied. Understanding when [actor] prioritizes [Logic A] over [Logic B] — and the consequences — is the puzzle."

### 反模式提醒
- **假张力**: 自由和约束指向同一方向，只是同一现象的两种说法 → 两种力量必须有独立的因果来源和相反的行为建议
- **无解决**: 张力提出后没有预告如何调和 → 必须给出锚定在理论中的解决框架
- **自由过大**: "无限选择"只在理论中存在 → 自由必须是有经验对应物的真实扩展
