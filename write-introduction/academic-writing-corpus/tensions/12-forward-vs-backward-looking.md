# 12-forward-vs-backward-looking — 回顾式 vs 前瞻式张力

## 功能定义

指出既有研究是"回顾式"的（关注现象发生后的后果），而忽略了对现象"前瞻式"理解（关注什么预先驱动了现象的发生及其时机）。不是否定回顾式研究，而是主张两者结合才能形成完整因果链。

## 适用场景

- 研究领域积累了大量"后果研究"（Y 的影响因素、Y 的结果）
- 但缺乏对"时机/过程/前因"的系统研究（when / how fast / what predicts）
- 时机差异本身具有理论和实践重要性
- 目标期刊欣赏时序扩展（MSOM、JOM、POM）

---

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：时序缺口型（darby2024 型）

**模板**:
> "Research on [phenomenon] has primarily examined its [post-hoc consequences] — that is, what happens [after] [outcome Y] occurs. However, this backward-looking emphasis has left a critical question unexamined: what predicts [when / how fast] [outcome] occurs in the first place? Our review uncovered only [small number N] studies that connect [antecedent] to [phenomenon], and none examine [temporal dimension]."

**来源**: darby2024 (MSOM), adapted

**原文锚定**:
> "Our review of the literature uncovered three studies that spotlight a connection between a firm's CEO and recalls, though none of them examine recall timing. We build upon this nascent stream of research by examining whether a CEO's stock ownership influences the speed with which the firm initiates medical device recalls."

**关键特征**:
- "primarily examined its [post-hoc consequences]" → 承认回顾式研究的价值
- "backward-looking emphasis" → 精准定位缺口类型
- "what predicts [when]" → 将时机本身理论化
- 用具体数字（"only three studies"）证明缺口真实性

---

### 变体 B：因果链延展型

**模板**:
> "We then turn to the [forward-looking consequences / second-stage outcomes] of [behavioral outcome]. Although related studies have explored [related topic], findings have been mixed. Moreover, no studies examine how [temporal/behavioral dimension] influences [downstream consequence]."

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `03-data-shock` (Hook) 配对**: 数据冲击讨论的"后果损失"是回顾式视角的自然起点

### 反模式提醒
- **伪时序**: 纯粹添加一个时间变量但无实质理论创新 → 必须论证 timing/proactiveness 本身是理论上有意义的行为
- **否定回顾式研究**: 贬低既有文献为"不完整" → 应把回顾式研究定位为"必要但不充分"
- **无第二层因果**: 只预测了 antecedents，但不说为什么重要 → 展示完整的 causal chain：antecedent → timing → downstream consequence
