# 21-institutionalized-extreme-structural-warrant — 拥挤文献中制度化极端结构正当性 Tension

## 功能描述

在**已被广泛研究的治理特征→结果**文献中，正当化研究一个「新结构特征」——不是声称 few studies examine X，而是证明：制度化实践已外推到理论未要求的极端；该极端是 **kind（定性结构转变）而非 degree（边际增减）**；移除某一历史群体隐含「该群体无价值」的错误假设；文献已回答采纳/扩散，近端后果仍不清；本意强化监督的解法可能削弱监督。

这是 **Inadequacy（主）+ 近端后果 Incompleteness（辅）** 的组合问题化：挑战制度化极端所依赖的视角，同时用文献分工解释为何仍值得做后果研究。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Inadequacy**（首选）；可带近端后果上的 Incompleteness 辅层
- 目标对话已「拥挤」：大量研究已检视各类董事/高管特征对同一类 DV（pay、misconduct、performance）的影响
- 新特征是制度化实践的**极端外推**，而非全新异质现象
- 可用 kind-vs-degree 区分「移除一整类行动者」与「independence % / 特征强度的边际变化」
- 存在少数派理论流可反驳「被移除群体无价值」的隐含假设
- 目标期刊接受修正性贡献（SMJ, AMJ, OS, ASQ）

## 验证状态

- **EMERGING** (1 paper): Zorn et al. (SMJ, 2017)

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：七拍正当性链（Zorn et al. 2017 型）

**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）

**功能节拍**（按序，可跨 Intro 正文章节 + Conversation 扩展背景节）:
1. **制度化压力锚定** — 监管/交易所要求多数独立；主流理论支持多数外部人
2. **实践超越理论** — 极端结构非立法强制，却已高度普及
3. **kind vs degree** — 不是 [连续特征]% 的边际增加，而是移除整个历史群体
4. **隐含零价值假设** — 移除 = 假设该群体无贡献；少数派理论流给出信息/继任等价值
5. **文献分工** — 采纳/为何扩散已有答案；近端后果仍不清；相邻领域仅作 power composite 或间接指标
6. **反直觉 problematization** — 本意强化监督的制度解，可能削弱监督
7. **实践 critically-evaluate stakes** — 若不理论化危害，实践者无理由 critically evaluate 已制度化实践

**模板**:
> "[Field] practice continues to evolve under pressure to increase [institutionalized virtue — e.g., independence / transparency / outsider control] ([citations]). One evolution that has [largely escaped scholarly attention / been treated only as a continuous intensifier] is [extreme structural configuration X], in which [definition: remove entire historical group G, leaving only actor A]. Although [theory / regulation] recommends [majority / moderate threshold], adopting [X] is [not mandated]—yet [X] now accounts for [prevalence statistic].
>
> Moving to [X] is a change in **kind** rather than **degree**: it is not simply increasing [continuous proxy], because it completely removes [group G] from [decision body]. Removing [G] implicitly assumes that such [actors] bring little value. Not only is there [weak / absent] evidence for that assumption ([citation]), a [small but established / minority] stream explains how [G] strengthens [monitoring / advice] by providing [benefit 1] and [benefit 2] ([citations]).
>
> [Prior paper / stream] answers why firms adopt [X] ([citation]). The consequences of [X] for [proximal outcomes: pay / misconduct / performance], however, remain unclear. Adjacent work treats [X] only as [composite power item / performance variability], leaving [proximal governance outcomes] unknown. If researchers fail to theorize why [X] might be harmful and demonstrate its consequences, practitioners may see little reason to critically evaluate this increasingly institutionalized practice."

**来源**: Zorn, Shropshire, Martin, Combs, & Ketchen (2017, SMJ), Introduction P1–P5 + background "Lone-Insider Boards: Changing Dynamics" (Intro 扩展模块)

**原文锚定**（仅溯源，勿作生成句）:
> "practical extension of agency theory that goes well beyond the theory's recommendations" → "change in kind rather than degree" → "completely removes a historically represented group" → "implicitly assumes that such directors bring little value" → "Given that lone-insider boards are a relatively new but increasingly prevalent governance structure, that this practice extends beyond the recommendations of established theory, and that their efficacy remains unknown, investigating the consequences of lone-insider boards is both timely and warranted" → "practitioners might see little reason to stop and critically evaluate this increasingly institutionalized practice."

**关键特征**:
- **正当性来自结构质变，而非文献空白声明** — 禁止退化为 "few studies examine X"
- **kind vs degree 是核心说服动作** — 把新特征从「又一个 independence 相关变量」升级为「移除历史群体的定性转变」
- **practice beyond theory** — 用「非强制却已普及」制造 puzzle，而非仅用趋势数字
- **文献分工取代弱缺口** — 承认采纳研究已完成，把缺口精确钉在 proximal outcomes
- **反直觉闭环** — 制度化「解法」本身成为监督风险源

**与邻近模板区分**:
| 模板 | 说服动作 | 本变体差异 |
|------|---------|-----------|
| `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` | 揭示文献共享假设错误 | 本变体把隐含假设嵌在七拍链中，且以 kind-vs-degree + 文献分工为主轴 |
| `15-practical-puzzle` | 实践信念 vs 学术 downside | 本变体针对**结构特征正当性**，非传统智慧清单 |
| `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` | 进展后遗漏 | 能量过低；单独使用会被读成弱增量 |
| `05-literature-consensus-blindspot` (Hook) | 共识盲点开场 | Hook 层；本变体是 Tension 层完整 warrant |

**适用**: 公司治理、董事会结构、委员会构成、所有权结构等「特征→CEO pay / misconduct / performance」拥挤对话；用户需要回答「为什么还要研究这个新特征」；SMJ/AMJ/OS。

**禁忌**:
- **禁止**把缺口写成 "few studies examine [X]" 或仅 "remains understudied"
- 若新特征只是连续变量的另一个切点（无群体移除），不要声称 kind vs degree
- 文献分工必须真实：采纳研究或相邻 composite 用法须可引用；不可伪造「已有人回答采纳」
- 少数派反驳流必须先于贡献段出现，否则「隐含假设错误」像作者独白
- 不要与 `01-despite-progress-unaddressed` 同用（能量与 Gap 逻辑冲突）；可与 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` 概念呼应，但同一 Intro 只选一条 Tension 主链

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **Hook**: `03-data-shock` 变体 H（逃逸注意力的极端普及）或 `05-literature-consensus-blindspot`
- **Literature Turn**: `01-progressive-coherence` 变体 N（文献分工：采纳已答 / 近端后果未清）— Progressive × Inadequacy 非对角合法
- **Stakes**: `01-general-theory-practice` 变体 G（若不理论化危害，实践者无 critically evaluate 动机）
- **Theory Lens**: 少数派/被忽略的 pro-[removed-group] 理论流（信息、继任/contestation 等）

### 互斥
- 不能与弱 Incompleteness 主链（仅 "little attention"）同用作为唯一 Gap
- 不能与 `04-reality-contradicts-consensus` 同用，除非另有决定性反例要求 Incommensurability 能量

### 反模式提醒
- **稻草人独立**：不要把「多数独立有益」写成「完全无 insider 最优」——范文保留多数独立的主流合理性，只攻击极端外推
- **变量堆砌**：不要列举所有已研究的董事会特征来证明「拥挤」——用 kind-vs-degree 一刀切开即可
- **Background 喧宾夺主**：背景节可深化 Conversation（文献分工、kind vs degree），但不得把 Theory 机制推演提前写完
