# 01-agency-theory-standard — Agency Theory 标准引入

## 功能描述

在 Introduction 中引入 Agency Theory 作为理论透镜，解释 principal-agent 关系及其信息不对称/利益冲突。这是战略管理、公司治理、供应链管理中**最常用的理论引入模板之一**。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及 shareholders→executives / buyers→suppliers / regulators→firms 的委托-代理关系
- 需要解释为什么代理人(agent)的行为偏离委托人(principal)的利益
- 需要引入信息不对称、监督成本、或印象管理作为机制

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：Impression Management 型（darby2025 型）

**模板**:
> "Examination of the research question is guided by agency theory, which explains relationships wherein principals (e.g., [stockholders]) delegate authority to agents (e.g., [executives]) to act on the principals' behalf ([citations]). In this context, [specific_actors] are a subset of the principals who [role_description]. Principals and agents often have different goals, so principals must try to ensure that agents act on their behalf ([citation]). However, because principals lack full knowledge of agents' actions and motives ([citation]), agents may find it useful to engage in impression management tactics to influence how principals perceive them ([citation]). Building on this premise, this study examines whether [prediction]."

**来源**: darby2025 (JSCM), P4

**关键特征**:
- "guided by agency theory" → 直接声明理论透镜
- "principals delegate authority to agents" → 先建立一般框架
- "In this context, [actors] are a subset of the principals" → 再具体化到研究情境
- "principals lack full knowledge" → 信息不对称是印象管理的逻辑前提
- "impression management tactics" → 从理论到具体行为预测的桥梁

### 变体 B：Information Asymmetry 型（darby2026 型）

**模板**:
> "Agency theory suggests that principals (e.g., [stockholders]) face information asymmetry when monitoring agents (e.g., [executives]). This information asymmetry is exacerbated when [condition], making it difficult for principals to [monitoring_action]. As a result, [negative_outcome] may occur."

**来源**: darby2026 (JOM), adapted

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 与 **Incompleteness × Mechanism** 组合配对：Agency Theory 的标准角色是提供 "why X affects Y" 的理论机制
- 在 Tension 之后引入：先建立文献缺口，再引入 Agency Theory 作为解释框架

### 反模式提醒
- **不要在 Introduction 中展开完整的 Agency Theory 文献综述**: 2-3 句建立框架即可，完整讨论留给 Theory section
- **不要只声明理论而不连接具体机制**: 从 "agency theory" 到 "impression management" 到 "faster recalls" 必须有一条清晰的因果链
- **不要用 Agency Theory 解释一切**: 如果研究不涉及 principal-agent 关系，另选理论
