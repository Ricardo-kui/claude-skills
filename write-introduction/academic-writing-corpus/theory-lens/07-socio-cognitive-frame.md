# 07-socio-cognitive-frame — 社会认知解释框架型 Theory Lens

## 功能描述

P4–P5 的功能：从成熟心理学/社会学概念跳跃到"解释框架/透镜"视角，向读者展示同一组织信号如何被不同 interpretive frames 选择性解读，从而产生差异化的受众反应。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incompleteness**（已有理论/构念，但需要解释受众如何解读其信号）
- 研究借用心理学/社会学概念解释外部受众（投资者、媒体、利益相关者）对组织信号的解读
- 需要为后续机制假设提供"意义建构"层面的理论承诺

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：社会认知解释框架型（paruchuri2026型）

**模板**:
> "In this paper, we adopt a [theoretical perspective] and propose that [IV] influence [DV] because [core construct] act as [number] distinct [interpretive frames/lenses] that shape how [audience] construe [information] ([citations]). [Frame definition]. By honing attention onto [information fragments], while concurrently downplaying others, these [frames] foster [selective interpretations], leading to variance in [outcome]—even when [actors] achieve similar outcomes ([citation]). [Main contention metaphor]."

**来源**: paruchuri_andrus_sullivan2026 (AMJ), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "In this paper, we adopt a socio-cognitive perspective and propose that the CEO regulatory-focused language influence investor reactions to earnings deviations because CEO regulatory foci act as two distinct interpretive frames or lenses that shape how investors construe firm performance information (Cornelissen & Werner, 2014; Hubbard, Pollock, Pfarrer & Rindova, 2018). Interpretive lenses represent a paradigm used by investors to structure and attribute significance to information in their sensemaking process (Pfarrer, Pollock & Rindova, 2010). By honing attention onto specific information fragments, while concurrently downplaying others, these lenses foster selective interpretations of the presented information (Fiss & Hirsch, 2005), leading to variance in investor reactions to earnings deviations from different firms—even when those firms achieve similar outcomes (Plummer, Allison & Connelly, 2016). Because investors are eager to incorporate available information into their evaluations of firms, they are also likely to attend to communications of the CEO, who is the most visible and powerful symbol of the firm's leadership (Hambrick & Mason, 1984; Higgins & Pinelli, 2020). Specifically, we posit that investors not only discern the direction and intensity of the CEO's underlying motivations via the extent of promotion- and prevention-focused language used by CEOs in their communications, but that investors' reactions to new information about firm performance is also shaped by perceived CEO regulatory foci. Our main contention is that 'just as two lenses that vary in shape can receive identical beams of light and yet refract that light in markedly dissimilar ways' (Smith, 2011: 62), perceived CEO promotion and prevention focus each foster selective interpretations of firm performance information; thus, investor reactions to the same firm outcome are shaped differently by perceived CEO promotion and prevention focus."

**关键特征**:
1. 从成熟心理学理论（regulatory focus）跳跃到社会认知视角（interpretive frames）
2. 用光学隐喻（lenses refract light）具象化抽象机制
3. 强调选择性注意（honing attention / downplaying）作为机制核心
4. 在 Theory Lens 段落内完成核心构念的操作化定义

**适用情境**: 研究借用心理学/社会学概念解释受众如何解读组织信号；投资者反应、媒体评估、利益相关者感知等。

**禁忌**: 必须确保 interpretive frame 在后续 Theory 章节有充分机制展开；光学隐喻不要过度延伸；不要与已有构念混淆而不加定义。

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- Theory Lens 必须直接回应 Tension 提出的 gap（关键词重叠，如 external audience → investors, mechanism → interpretive frames）
- Theory Lens 必须与 Preview 中的研究设计一致（如 archival investor reactions + experiments on attributions）
- Theory Lens 必须与 Theory Development 章节的实际理论来源一致

### 互斥
- 不要与 `01-agency-theory-standard` 混用：agency theory 解释的是激励与信息不对称，而 socio-cognitive frame 解释的是意义建构与选择性解读

### 反模式提醒
- **不要把 Theory Lens 写成理论综述**: 2-3 句核心逻辑即可，不是文献回顾
- **不要引入与 Gap 无关的理论**: 如果 Tension 说的是受众如何解读信号，Theory Lens 必须引入解释意义建构的理论
- **不要过度承诺**: "adopt a socio-cognitive perspective" ≠ "fully develop a new theory of sensemaking"

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[interpretive frame]` — 解释框架/透镜

❌ "Drawing on framing theory, we examine how CEO language affects investor reactions." → 未说明 CEO language 作为何种框架、如何选择性解读信息

✅ "Drawing on a socio-cognitive perspective, we propose that CEO regulatory-focused language acts as two distinct interpretive frames—promotion focus makes gain-related information salient, whereas prevention focus makes loss-related information salient—thereby altering investor reactions to the same earnings deviation." → 理论视角 + 框架定义 + 选择性注意机制 + 结果方向

### `[metaphor]` — 隐喻使用

❌ "CEO regulatory focus is like a lens." → 隐喻孤立，未与机制连接

✅ "Just as two lenses that vary in shape can receive identical beams of light and yet refract that light in markedly dissimilar ways, perceived CEO promotion and prevention focus each foster selective interpretations of firm performance information." → 隐喻紧接机制说明（selective interpretations），服务理论主张而非装饰

### 语气光谱
- **主语气**: assertive — 证据: "we adopt... and propose that..." / "Our main contention is that..."
- **次语气**: cautious — 证据: 引用多个理论来源支撑新视角应用

### 段落节奏
- **典型节奏**: 理论视角声明 → 核心构念定义 → 选择性注意机制 → 光学隐喻收束

### 标志性叙事标记
- [paruchuri2026]: 光学隐喻 + "honing attention onto... while concurrently downplaying others" 选择性注意句式

### 刻意回避
- [paruchuri2026]: avoids treating regulatory focus as direct causal force — instead frames it as interpretive lens that shapes attributions

### 质量标记
- **最值得模仿**: 用具体隐喻（lenses refract light）将抽象的"意义建构"机制可视化
- **已知风险**: 隐喻若过度延伸，可能让读者误以为 CEO regulatory focus 改变客观信息本身，而非改变解读方式
