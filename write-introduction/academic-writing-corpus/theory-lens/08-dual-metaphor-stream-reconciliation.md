# 08-dual-metaphor-stream-reconciliation — 单理论双隐喻调和双文献流 Theory Lens

## 功能描述

当两个**独立的文献流各自只解释了同一现象的一半**，而**单个理论内部恰好包含两个互补的隐喻/子机制**时，用该理论的两个隐喻分别映射到两个文献流，从而把两条割裂的研究线索整合为一个完整解释。

核心逻辑：`[One theory] offers two complementary metaphors — [metaphor 1] and [metaphor 2]. [Metaphor 1] maps onto [literature stream A] (which captures [sub-effect 1]); [metaphor 2] maps onto [literature stream B] (which captures [sub-effect 2]). Together they reconcile the two streams, each of which explains only part of the phenomenon.`

与 `02-dual-theory-layered` 的关键区别：后者是**两个不同的理论**分工（主理论解释主效应 + 次要框架系统化边界条件）。本模板是**一个理论内部的两个隐喻/子机制**分工，每个隐喻拯救一个被割裂的文献流。`02` 的互斥规则明确指出"主效应和边界条件若可用同一理论解释则不该用双理论结构"——本模板正是"同一理论内部的双机制"情形，是对 `02` 那条排除规则的正面承接。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incompleteness**（某维度/方向未被研究），且贡献层带 **Incommensurability 调和味**（两条文献流各执一词、各解释一半）
- Conversation 策略 = **Synthesized Coherence**（整合两个成熟但割裂的文献流）
- 存在一个公认理论，其内部有两套并行的、可被分别命名的子机制/隐喻（如 Podolny 2001 的 pipes vs prisms；强弱连带的信息传输 vs 信号推断；制度理论的规制性 vs 规范性 vs 认知性支柱）
- 两个文献流已分别捕获其中一个子机制，但从未被整合
- 目标期刊接受 multi-mechanism integration（JSCM/JOM/AMJ/SMJ 适配度高）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：双隐喻流映射型（li2025 型）

**模板**:
> "[Two-sentence gap recap: stream A captures sub-effect 1; stream B captures sub-effect 2; each explains only part of the phenomenon.] Drawing on [single theory] ([citation]), we [argue/suggest] that [relationship tie / connective construct] serve as '[metaphor 1]' and '[metaphor 2]' that transmit [sub-effect 1] and [sub-effect 2], respectively, of [phenomenon] from [source actor] to [recipient actor]. Specifically, as [metaphor 1], [connective construct] [mechanism logic mapping onto stream A], resulting in [sub-effect 1]. As [metaphor 2], [connective construct] [mechanism logic mapping onto stream B], leading to [sub-effect 2] through '[label for stream-B process]'."

**来源**: li_bapuji_talluri_singh_narayanan2025 (JSCM), P4 (lens) + P6 (contribution 1 reconciliation)

**原文锚定**:
> "This research draws on social network theory (Podolny 2001) to suggest that relationship ties between a recalling firm and its suppliers serve as 'pipes' and 'prisms' that transmit the cash flow effect and impression effect, respectively, of product recalls from the recalling firm to its suppliers. Specifically, as pipes, buyer–supplier relationships transmit operational disruptions caused by product recalls, resulting in a reduction in the future cash flow of the supplier firm (i.e., cash flow effect). As prisms, buyer–supplier relationships shape categorization such that the buyer and suppliers are grouped together, leading to negative impressions spreading from the recalling firm to suppliers through 'guilt by association' (i.e., impression effect)."
>
> （contribution 段的调和声明）: "Previous studies on the spillover effects of crises have focused primarily on the impression effect... whereas research on supply chain propagation of crises has primarily focused on the cash flow effect... However, each research stream only explains a part of the phenomenon. Drawing on social network theory (Podolny 2001), this study integrates these two views... by theorizing relationship ties as pipes and prisms."

**关键特征**:
- **先报缺口再给透镜（gap→lens 桥接）**: 先点明两条流各捕获一个子效应、"each explains only part of the phenomenon"，再用单理论的两个隐喻分别填补——理论承诺与 gap 精确咬合
- **"serve as '[metaphor 1]' and '[metaphor 2]' that transmit [sub-effect 1] and [sub-effect 2], **respectively**"**: "respectively" 是本模板的标志性铰链词——把两个隐喻与两个子效应一一锁定，读者无歧义
- **"As [metaphor 1]... As [metaphor 2]..." 平行展开**: 每个隐喻独立给一句机制逻辑，结构对称，便于读者把每个隐喻映射回对应文献流
- **给每个子效应命名（cash flow effect / impression effect）**: 在 lens 中就把两个子效应固化为可引用的标签，后续假设可直接引用这些标签——lens 同时是构念命名仪式
- **调和动作显性化**: contribution 段用 "integrates these two views... by theorizing [ties] as [pipes and prisms]" 把"整合"这个理论动作本身写出来——不是含糊地说"we integrate"，而是说清"靠什么整合"

**适用**: 任何"两条文献流各解释一半"的研究——尤其 supply chain（运营传导 vs 印象/声誉）、网络（资源流 vs 信号推断）、stakeholder（物质影响 vs 合法性判断）。Incompleteness × Mechanism 组合首选；带 synthesis 贡献时尤为契合。

**禁忌**:
- 两个隐喻必须来自**同一理论内部**（pipes/prisms 都在 Podolny 的网络理论里），不能临时把两个不同理论的隐喻拼在一起伪装成"单理论双隐喻"——那是 `02-dual-theory-layered`
- 两个隐喻必须**各自对应一条真实存在的、可引用的文献流**；若某条流是作者臆造的稻草人，"调和"就失去意义
- "respectively" 锁定后，后文假设/Results 必须分别检验两个子效应（本文 Methods 的 cash-flow test + impression test 正是如此）——若只检验其一，lens 承诺无法兑现
- 不要让两个子效应方向矛盾却不解释——本模板的价值在于"互补"，两个隐喻应共同指向同一总体现象（如都放大/都传导 negative event），只是经由不同通道

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 Synthesized Coherence literature turn 配对**: 本 lens 假定前文已呈现两条割裂文献流，literature-turn/02-synthesized-coherence 是天然搭档
- **与"分别检验两个子效应"的 Preview/Methods 配对**: lens 承诺了双机制，后续必须有 cash-flow-style 与 impression-style 的分离检验（mechanism test × 2）
- **与 integration-flavored contribution 配对**: contribution 段需显式写出 "integrates these two views" 的调和声明，兑现 lens 承诺

### 互斥
- **不能与 `02-dual-theory-layered` 同用**: 两理论分工（02）vs 一理论双隐喻（08）是互斥结构——先判断子机制是否来自同一理论
- **不能与单一机制 Theory Lens 同用**: 本模板要求 ≥2 个子机制

### 反模式提醒
- **伪双隐喻**: 把同一个机制换个词说两遍（如 "pipes = 信息流" 和 "channels = 资源流" 实为同一物）→ 不是真双隐喻。两个隐喻必须在概念上可分离、可分别证伪
- **调和声明空泛**: 只说 "we integrate the literature" 而不说 "by theorizing [X] as [metaphor 1] and [metaphor 2]" → 审稿人看不到整合的**机制抓手**。整合必须经由一个具体的 connective construct（本文是 relationship ties）
- **隐喻与文献流错配**: 把本属 stream A 的子效应硬挂到隐喻 2 上 → "respectively" 锁定一旦写错，全文论证链断裂

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[single theory]` + `[two internal metaphors]` — 单理论双隐喻

❌ "Drawing on social network theory and institutional theory, we argue ties serve as pipes and prisms." → 两个隐喻分别来自两个理论（pipes 属网络、prisms 属网络——但若误挂到制度理论就成了理论堆砌）；且未说明哪个隐喻救哪条文献流。

✅ "Drawing on social network theory (Podolny 2001), we argue that buyer–supplier ties serve as *pipes* and *prisms* that transmit the *cash flow effect* (mapped onto the supply chain propagation stream) and the *impression effect* (mapped onto the categorization stream), respectively." → 单一理论、双隐喻、与双文献流一一锁定。

### `[connective construct]` — 整合抓手

❌ "We integrate the two streams." → 没有抓手——靠什么整合？

✅ "We integrate these two views by theorizing *relationship ties* as pipes and prisms." → 整合经由一个具体可操作化的 connective construct（relationship ties），后续可直接进入假设推导。

## 诚实边界

- **EMERGING 状态**: 本模板当前仅 1 篇范文（li2025 JSCM）。pipes/prisms 这一具体隐喻对来自 Podolny (2001)，迁移到其他理论时必须确认目标理论内部确有可分离的双隐喻——不可把单机制硬拆成两半。
- **不可把单理论双隐喻降格为修辞**: 两个隐喻必须各自承担可检验的子效应。若后文只检验合并后的总效应，本 lens 的"双隐喻"承诺即落空——应改用标准单机制 Theory Lens。
- **"each explains only part of the phenomenon" 必须为真**: 若两条文献流其实并不割裂（只是作者没读到整合文献），强行套用本模板会被审稿人指出"伪割裂"。务必先做 literature-turn 确认割裂真实存在。
