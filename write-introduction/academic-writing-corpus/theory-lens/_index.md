# Theory Lens 索引

Theory Lens（P4-P5）的功能：在 Gap/Tension 建立之后，向读者提供**新的解释框架**——告诉他们"我们可以用什么透镜来看这个问题"。这不是理论综述，而是**理论承诺**："Drawing on X, we argue that..."

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- Theory Lens 必须直接回应 Tension 提出的 gap（关键词重叠）
- Theory Lens 必须与 Preview 中的研究设计一致
- Theory Lens 必须与 Theory Development 章节的实际理论来源一致

### 反模式提醒
- **不要把 Theory Lens 写成理论综述**: 2-3 句核心逻辑即可，不是文献回顾
- **不要引入与 Gap 无关的理论**: 如果 Tension 说的是机制缺口，Theory Lens 必须引入解释该机制的理论
- **不要过度承诺**: "Drawing on X" ≠ "We fully develop X"——承诺要与 Theory 章节的实际深度匹配

## 按 Gap 类型选择 Theory Lens

| Gap 类型 | 推荐 Theory Lens 策略 |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Incompleteness** | 引入连接两个成熟领域的桥梁理论；或使用 maxim contrast 具象化 trade-off |
| **Inadequacy** | 引入新的理论透镜来重新框定问题；或展示现有透镜的局限性 |
| **Incommensurability** | 直接提出替代框架，用新构念重新组织文献对话 |

## 槽位填充正误对比

### `[core claim / mechanism]` — 核心主张

❌ "Drawing on resource dependence theory, we examine the role of political ties in innovation." → "examine the role of X" 不给出方向性预测——是正向还是负向？是 mediation 还是 moderation？审稿人看完不知道你的理论主张是什么

✅ "Drawing on the behavioral theory of the firm (Cyert & March, 1963), we argue that political connections affect innovation not through the volume of resources acquired, but through the efficiency with which those resources are allocated. When firms rely on political channels for resources, they develop allocation routines oriented toward rent maintenance — directing resources toward preserving political relationships — rather than toward innovation." → 理论来源 + 方向性主张 + 机制逻辑链，三要素齐备

### `[theory name]` — 多理论场景

❌ "We draw on resource dependence theory, institutional theory, and the attention-based view." → 三个理论各只提了名字——读者不知道各自负责解释什么。理论堆砌

✅ "We integrate resource dependence theory, which explains why firms seek political resources, with the behavioral theory of the firm, which explains how those resources are subsequently deployed — or misdeployed — within the organization." → 两个理论各有明确的功能分工：一个解释 acquisition，一个解释 allocation
