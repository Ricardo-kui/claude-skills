# 04-field-migration — 领域迁移型 Gap/Transition

## 功能描述

当已有研究主要集中于学科 A（如 finance/accounting/psychology），而本研究希望将这些发现迁移到学科 B（如 OM/strategy/marketing）时，需要一种既能承认源学科积累、又能正当化迁移的过渡句法。这不是"别人做错了"，而是"别人在另一个领域做了，我们来填补这个领域的空白"。

---

## 变体 A：标准领域迁移型（mayo2021 型）

**模板**:
> "We apply this [source field] research stream to [target field] by examining whether [IV] impacts [DV]. Specifically, we posit that [condition 1], [actor] may [behavior 1], either to [reason 1] or because [reason 2], particularly when [moderator 1]. Conversely, [condition 2], [actors] may [behavior 2], particularly when [moderator 2]."

**来源**: mayo2021 (POM), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "We apply this CEO tenure and risk management decision-making research stream to an OM setting by examining whether CEO tenure impacts recall decisions. Specifically, we posit that early in tenure, a CEO may initiate recalls more frequently, either to 'clean up shop' or because blame can be placed on the prior CEO, particularly when the predecessor was forced out of the role. Conversely, later in tenure, CEOs may avoid initiating recalls altogether, particularly when the defect underlying the contemplated recall contains more discretion."

**关键特征**:
- "We apply this... to..." → 经典的领域迁移句式
- "by examining whether..." → 明确迁移后的研究问题
- 两个"Specifically, we posit that... Conversely..."对称结构
- 每个条件都附带一个 moderating contingency

**适用**: 跨学科迁移研究（finance→OM, psychology→strategy, marketing→finance 等）

## 变体 B：文献缺口+情境差异型

**模板**:
> "Although a substantial body of research in [source field] has examined [topic] ([citations]), relatively little is known about how [phenomenon] unfolds in [target field]. This is surprising because [reason why target field matters]. We address this gap by [approach]."

**来源**: 通用模板，基于多个跨学科论文归纳

**关键特征**:
- 先承认源学科的文献积累
- "relatively little is known about... in [target field]" → 明确指出迁移缺口
- "This is surprising because" → 建立迁移的正当性

**适用**: 当目标领域与源领域有显著情境差异时（如发达国家→新兴市场，制造业→服务业）

---

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 领域迁移型 Transition → Progressive Coherence 的跨学科变体（literature-turns/ 变体 D）
- 领域迁移型 Transition → Incompleteness Gap（因为不是已有文献错了，而是它们不在目标领域）

### 反模式提醒
- **不要说源学科做错了**: 领域迁移是"补充"不是"修正"
- **不要忽略情境差异**: 必须说明为什么源学科的发现不能直接套用到目标领域
- **不要没有具体机制**: "apply to X" 后必须立即说明 "by examining whether..." 和具体假设
