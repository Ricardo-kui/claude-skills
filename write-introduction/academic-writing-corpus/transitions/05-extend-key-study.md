# 05-extend-key-study — 与单一关键文献的 Extend 定位

## 功能描述

当研究需要站在领域内一篇关键文献的肩膀上时，使用"build upon + extend"结构，而非简陋的"they studied X, we study Y"。这种定位既展示了对前人工作的尊重，又清晰地划定了本研究的增量贡献边界。

---

## 变体 A：双方向 Extend 型（mayo2021 型）

**模板**:
> "In this work, we build upon a notable related study, [Key Study] ([citation]). They explore [their setting and finding]. We extend the insight gained from [Key Study] in [number] key ways. First, we examine [our focus], as opposed to [their focus]. In doing so, we contribute to the burgeoning research stream examining [stream] ([citations]). Second, by studying [our setting], as opposed to [their setting], we explore [relationship] in a significantly [different characteristic] environment. This juxtaposition allows us to develop meaningful and practical recommendations that arise from cross-industry comparisons."

**来源**: mayo2021 (POM), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "In this work, we build upon a notable related study, Wowak et al. (2015). They explore the FDA-regulated food, pharmaceutical, and medical device industries, and find that stock options offered early in a CEO's tenure may incent the CEO to make riskier product design, manufacturing, or distribution decisions, necessitating hard-to-avoid future severe recalls. We extend the insight gained from Wowak et al. (2015) in two key ways. First, we examine how CEO tenure may influence the recall decision itself, as opposed to the underlying reason for the recall. In doing so, we contribute to the burgeoning research stream examining recall decision-making (Ball et al. 2018, Wowak et al. 2021). Second, by studying the consumer product industry, as opposed to tightly controlled FDA-regulated industries, we explore the CEO tenure and recall relationship in a significantly less regulated environment. This juxtaposition allows us to develop meaningful and practical recommendations that arise from cross-industry comparisons."

**关键特征**:
- "build upon a notable related study" → 尊重而非贬低前人
- "We extend... in two key ways" → 明确数字化的 extend
- 每个 extend 都说明 "as opposed to"（对比其研究）
- 第一个 extend 通常是**机制/焦点层次**的转移（从原因到决策本身）
- 第二个 extend 通常是**情境/边界**的拓展（从严格监管到宽松监管）
- "This juxtaposition allows us to..." → 将跨行业/跨情境比较转化为方法论优势

**适用**: 需要与领域内一篇关键文献对话的推进式研究

## 变体 B：单一 Extend 型（精简版）

**模板**:
> "We build upon [Key Study] ([citation]), who find that [their finding]. Whereas they focus on [their focus], we examine [our focus], thereby extending their insight to [new boundary]."

**适用**: 当只有一个核心 extend 方向、篇幅受限时使用

---

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- Extend 定位 → Incompleteness Gap（因为本研究是推进而非颠覆）
- Extend 定位 → 后续发现预览中必须兑现所宣称的 extend（如果说了"we examine the decision itself"，结果部分必须展示决策层面的发现）

### 反模式提醒
- **不要贬低关键文献**: 用 "build upon" 而非 "unlike" 或 "in contrast to"
- **不要只列差异不说明增量价值**: 每个 "as opposed to" 后必须有 "In doing so, we contribute to..."
- **不要 extend 方向模糊**: "two key ways" 必须具体、可数、可验证
- **不要忘记引用后续工作**: 如果该文献流已有后续发展，需简要提及以展示对话的时效性
