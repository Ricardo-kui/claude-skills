# 07-construct-migration — 新概念定义 + 层次迁移

## 功能描述

用于将一个已在低层/其他领域验证的构念引入到高层/新领域时的标准过渡。核心动作：精确定义→归类到已有框架→承认低层研究→指出高层空白→用已有证据支撑迁移的合理性。

---

## 变体 A：标准层次迁移型（mannor2016 型）

**模板**:
> "[New construct], which we define as [precise definition], is one such [construct type] that has been shown to influence a variety of outcomes among [lower-level actors] (c.f., [citations]). Yet no studies to date have explored its implications for [top-level actors], even though abundant research suggests that it will meaningfully affect their decisions and actions."

**来源**: mannor2016 (SMJ), P2 后半

**原文锚定**:
> "Job anxiety, which we define as the general level of worry experienced in connection with one's job over time, is one such psychological construct that has been shown to influence a variety of outcomes among lower-level employees (c.f., Abdel-Halim, 1978; Griffith and Hebl, 2002; Muschalla, Linden, and Olbrich, 2010). Yet no studies to date have explored its implications for top executives, even though abundant research suggests that it will meaningfully affect their decisions and actions."

**关键特征**:
- "which we define as" → **在首次出现时立即给出操作化定义**
- "one such [construct type]" → 将新概念归类到已有文献框架中
- "among [lower-level actors]" → 承认在低层已有研究（不制造稻草人）
- "Yet no studies to date have explored its implications for [top-level actors]" → 经典的层次迁移缺口
- "even though abundant research suggests" → 用已有证据支撑迁移的合理性

---

## 变体 B：跨领域迁移型

**模板**:
> "[New construct], which we define as [precise definition], has been extensively studied in [source field] (e.g., [citations]), where scholars have shown that it [effect in source field]. However, its role in [target field] remains unclear. Because [theoretical rationale for migration], we expect that [construct] will [predicted effect in target field]."

**适用**: 当构念需要从学科 A 迁移到学科 B（如 psychology→strategy, finance→marketing）时

---

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 构念迁移 → 数据障碍型缺口论证（先解释为什么长期被忽视，再定义并迁移）
- 构念迁移 → Progressive Coherence 的文献对话

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只批评别人不定义构念，自己不定义**: 在首次出现时立即给出清晰的操作化定义
- **不要把层次迁移说成从零开始**: 必须承认低层/源领域已有研究的存在
- **不要没有理论理由就迁移**: "even though abundant research suggests" 后面必须有为什么迁移合理的理论依据
