# 08-case-to-field-generalization — 从个案到领域一般化

## 功能描述

当 Hook 是一个具体案例/故事时，不能直接从个案跳到文献回顾。需要一个过渡段，将个案重新框定为更广泛的领域层面现象。这是叙事型 Hook 与学术对话之间的关键桥梁。

---

## 变体 A：媒体调查扩展型（desai2012 型）

**模板**:
> "Following this and several other incidents at [organization], reporters at [media outlet] published a series of articles questioning [domain] [practices]. However, their investigations did not focus exclusively on [organization]. Rather, the reporters attempted to expose what some observers called a '[systemic term]' in [domain] across the [industry/field] ([citation]). Their research revealed [finding 1] at other [organizations] ([citation]) as well as [finding 2] ([citation])."

**来源**: desai2012 (AMJ), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "Following this and several other incidents at Union Pacific, reporters at the New York Times published a series of articles questioning railroad safety procedures. However, their investigations did not focus exclusively on Union Pacific. Rather, the reporters attempted to expose what some observers called a 'systemic failure' in safety across the industry (Bogdanich, 2004a). Their research revealed flawed accident investigations at other major railroad companies (Bogdanich, 2004b) as well as conflicts of interest among railroads and federal investigators (Bogdanich, 2004c)."

**关键特征**:
- "However... did not focus exclusively on..." → 从个案到系统的关键转折
- "Rather, the reporters attempted to expose..." → 展示调查的广度
- "what some observers called a 'systemic failure'" → 用引语建立概念标签
- 两个并列发现（flawed investigations + conflicts of interest）展示问题的系统性

---

## 变体 B：直接一般化型

**模板**:
> "Although the [case above] involved [specific organization], the [phenomenon] it illustrates is far from unique. Similar [events] have occurred at [organization 1] ([citation]), [organization 2] ([citation]), and [organization 3] ([citation]). These cases collectively suggest that [broader pattern]."

**适用**: 当有多个案例可以并列展示系统性问题时

---

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- 个案到领域一般化 → 沉浸式叙事 Hook（因为故事型 Hook 必须有过渡才能连接到学术对话）
- 个案到领域一般化 → Progressive Coherence 或 Synthesized Coherence

### 反模式提醒
- **不要跳过一般化直接进文献**: 读者需要知道"这个故事不是孤立的"
- **不要只有一个案例就说"systemic"**: 必须有媒体调查、统计数据或多个案例支撑
- **不要一般化后丢失案例的具体性**: 后续 Gap 段需要用 "as the example above suggests" 重新激活案例

### 适用场景
- 故事型 Hook（事故、丑闻、决策瞬间）后必须使用的过渡
- 案例研究论文从个案到理论的一般化
- 任何使用具体事件作为 Hook 的 Introduction
