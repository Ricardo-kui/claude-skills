# 09-micro-macro-complement — 微观视角补充宏观理论

## 功能描述

当研究从组织/宏观层面下降到个体/微观层面，或从已被充分研究的宏观过程转向未被关注的微观机制时，使用本过渡句式。核心功能是将"微观研究"定位为对"宏观理论"的**补充**（complement）而非**挑战**（challenge）——这确保了宏观理论研究者不会感到被冒犯，同时确立了微观研究的独立价值。

## 适用场景

- 研究将分析层次从组织降到个体、从制度场域降到具体行动者
- 宏观理论已被广泛接受，但微观机制未被探索
- 需要避免"层次错误"的批评
- 目标期刊接受跨层次研究（AMJ, ASQ, MS, SMJ）

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (≥2 papers): lehman2014 (MS), zhou2017 (ASQ)

### 生成力
- **ADAPTABLE**: "complement the widely studied macro process" 句式可适配几乎所有宏观→微观的过渡

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：微观补充型（lehman2014 型）

**模板**:
> "In our view, [field] would be enhanced when coupled with a fuller view of [process], one focused on the micro aspects of [phenomenon]. This microleaning view would complement the widely studied macro process of [established process] (e.g., [citation 1], [citation 2], [citation 3]). In an effort to better understand this transition process, we study [context] in greater depth."

**来源**: lehman2014 (MS), P5-P6

**原文锚定**:
> "In our view, organization theory would be enhanced when coupled with a fuller view of the transition process to rationalized norms, one focused on the micro aspects of acceptance and choice by individuals. This microleaning view would complement the widely studied macro process of organizational adoption and diffusion of rationalized structural features (e.g., Dobbin et al. 1993, Sutton et al. 1994, Hedström 1994, Dobbin and Kelly 2007). In an effort to better understand this transition process, we study the restaurant context in greater depth."

**关键特征**:
- "would be enhanced when coupled with" → 谦逊但自信的贡献声明：不是推翻已有理论，而是丰富它
- "one focused on the micro aspects of..." → 用同位语精确说明补充的内容
- "This [adjective] view would complement" → "complement" 是关键词，不能用 "replace" 或 "challenge"
- 引用 3-4 篇宏观文献展示对已有研究的尊重和掌握
- "In an effort to better understand this transition process" → 将研究目标与宏观理论缺口直接连接

**适用**: 宏观理论已成熟，微观机制空缺的领域

---

### 变体 B：双理论整合型（zhou2017 型）

**模板**:
> "Understanding how [phenomenon] affects [outcome] is critical because [theoretical tradition 1] and [theoretical tradition 2] offer competing predictions. [Tradition 1] predicts [prediction A], whereas [tradition 2] suggests [prediction B]. Resolving this tension is essential not only for [theoretical advancement] but also for [practical guidance], because [actor] must navigate both [logic 1] and [logic 2] in [context]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P1-P2

**关键特征**:
- "Understanding how [X] affects [Y] is critical because..." → 最标准的通用 Stakes 开场（也是过渡）
- 两个理论传统的对垒作为研究重要性的来源
- "not only for... but also for..." 平衡理论+实践

**适用**: 理论整合型研究，需要同时对话两个文献流

---

### 变体 C：多层次桥接型

**模板**:
> "Although macro-level research has established that [macro finding], the micro-level mechanisms through which [process] operate remain poorly understood. We bridge this gap by examining how [micro phenomenon] shapes [outcome], thereby connecting [macro construct] to [micro construct]."

**关键特征**:
- 先承认宏观发现的正确性
- 指出微观机制的空缺
- "bridge this gap" 明确跨层次贡献

**适用**: 明确的跨层次研究（如制度压力→个体认知→组织行为）

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 Incompleteness Gap 配对**: 微观补充宏观的核心逻辑是"宏观已有进展，微观遗漏了"
- **与 3-4 篇宏观文献引用配对**: 必须展示对宏观文献的掌握，否则会被质疑"是否了解已有研究"

### 反模式提醒
- **不要用 "challenge" 或 "overturn"**: 微观研究的价值是"补充"而非"推翻"宏观理论
- **不要跳过宏观文献引用**: 至少引用 3 篇权威宏观文献
- **不要模糊微观和宏观的边界**: 必须明确说明你的分析层次是什么，以及为什么这个层次被忽视了
- **不要微观研究宏观现象**: 如果研究的是组织层面的结果，不要声称自己在做"微观研究"
