# 11-roadmap — Introduction 结尾路线图

## 功能描述

Introduction 最后一段（或最后一句）的功能：向读者预告论文的剩余结构。这不是简单的"接下来我们讲什么"，而是**对 Introduction 承诺的收束和展开**——告诉读者你将如何兑现前面做出的理论承诺和发现预告。

## 适用场景

- 所有类型的论文都需要某种形式的 roadmap
- 理论复杂的论文尤其需要（帮助读者导航）
- 方法论创新明显的论文需要（预告识别策略或数据来源）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：标准三段式（vadakkepatt2022 / JM 型）

**模板**:
> "We begin by developing the conceptual framework, which blends [theory A] with [theory B]. After we explain our empirical approach, we report the focal study findings. We also present a series of additional analyses. Finally, we conclude with theoretical implications and insights for [managerial actor] and [policy actor]."

**来源**: vadakkepatt2022 (JM), P7

**原文锚定**:
> "We begin by developing the conceptual framework, which blends regulatory capture theory with the ABV of the firm. After we explain our empirical approach, we report the focal study findings. We also present a series of additional analyses. Finally, we conclude with theoretical implications and insights for marketing managers and policy makers."

**关键特征**:
- "We begin by developing..." → 先讲理论
- "After we explain..." → 再讲方法
- "we report..." → 再讲结果
- "Finally, we conclude..." → 最后讨论
- 简洁明了，每部分一句话

**适用**: 标准实证论文（理论→方法→结果→讨论）

---

### 变体 B：理论承诺展开型（zhou2017 / ASQ 型）

**模板**:
> "The rest of the paper proceeds as follows. We first develop [theoretical framework] and derive hypotheses concerning [predictions]. We then describe the [research setting/data/methods]. Next, we present the results. We conclude with a discussion of the findings and their implications for [theory] and [practice]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "The rest of the paper proceeds as follows" → 经典 ASQ 开场
- 每部分明确预告核心内容（不是只说"我们讲方法"，而是"describe the research setting"）

**适用**: 理论驱动型期刊（ASQ, ASR, OS）

---

### 变体 C：方法论强调型（darby2026 / JOM 型）

**模板**:
> "The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. We first develop hypotheses. We then describe the [data source], [sample], and [methodology]. We subsequently present our [analysis approach] and the results. We conclude by discussing the implications of our findings for [theory], [practice], and [policy]."

**来源**: darby2026 (JOM), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "is organized as follows" → 比 "proceeds" 更正式
- 明确命名数据来源和方法论
- "implications for theory, practice, and policy" → 三段式收尾

**适用**: 方法复杂或有政策含义的研究

---

### 变体 D：假设驱动型（mannor2016 / SMJ 型）

**模板**:
> "We first develop hypotheses about [main effect] and [boundary condition]. We then describe [data/method]. Next, we present results, followed by [robustness/additional analyses]. We conclude with a discussion of our findings and their implications."

**来源**: mannor2016 (SMJ), adapted

**关键特征**:
- 明确预告假设内容（"about X and Y"）
- 暗示稳健性检验的存在
- 简洁收尾

**适用**: 假设清晰的实证论文

---

### 变体 E：极简型（MS / 某些 ASQ 型）

**模板**:
> "We turn now to [next section]."

**关键特征**:
- 一句话结束
- 不预告全文结构
- 适用于结构简单、读者可以预期的论文

**适用**: MS, ASQ 中结构极其简单的论文；或当 Introduction 已经很紧凑时

---

## 关键技巧：Roadmap 的三要素

一个有效的 roadmap 必须包含：
1. **理论部分预告**: 让读者知道理论框架在哪里展开
2. **方法/数据预告**: 建立方法论可信度
3. **结果/讨论预告**: 收束 Introduction 的承诺

**可选但推荐**:
- **稳健性/额外分析预告**: 暗示研究的严谨性（"we also present a series of additional analyses"）
- **理论+实践+政策三段式**: 适用于有明确政策含义的研究

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **必须与 Introduction 的承诺对齐**: Roadmap 中出现的内容必须与前面 Hook/Gap/Preview/Contribution 中承诺的内容一致
- **必须与实际论文结构一致**: 如果 roadmap 说"we first develop hypotheses"，论文必须真的有独立的 hypotheses 段

### 反模式提醒
- **不要把 roadmap 写成目录**: "Section 2 reviews literature..." → 改为 "We first develop the conceptual framework..."
- **不要预告不存在的内容**: 如果论文没有稳健性检验，不要说 "we also present robustness checks"
- **不要过长**: 超过 4-5 句话的 roadmap 会显得 Introduction 结构松散
- **不要与 abstract 重复**: Roadmap 是结构预告，不是内容摘要

### 长度控制

| 期刊 | 推荐长度 | 风格 |
|------|---------|------|
| JM/JMR | 1-2 句话 | 极简，快速收束 |
| AMJ | 2-3 句话 | 标准 |
| SMJ | 2-3 句话 | 标准，可包含稳健性预告 |
| ASQ | 1-3 句话 | 可极简（"We turn now to..."）或标准 |
| MS | 1-2 句话 | 极简 |
| OS | 2-3 句话 | 标准，可包含 broader implications 预告 |
