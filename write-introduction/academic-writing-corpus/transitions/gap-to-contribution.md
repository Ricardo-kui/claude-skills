# gap-to-contribution — Gap → Contribution 过渡

## 功能描述

这是 Introduction 中最重要的 Transition 之一。它将此前的批评能量（"文献有缺口/矛盾/盲点"）转化为建设性的贡献声明（"我们提供了什么新知识"）。这个过渡的质量直接决定了读者从"问题"到"答案"的心理转换是否自然。

## 适用场景

- 所有 Gap 类型和贡献维度
- 在 Gap 段（P3-P4）和 Contribution 段（P6-P7）之间
- 通常出现在 P4 末尾或 P5 开头

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (universal): 所有 MVP30 论文都包含 gap→contribution 过渡，但句式因 Gap 类型和期刊而异

### 生成力
- **GENERATIVE**: 这是一个功能性过渡而非内容模板，高度可迁移

### 排他性
- **LOW**: 所有 Introduction 都需要 gap→contribution 过渡，不具有 Gap 类型诊断价值

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：整合方案型（Incommensurability → Mechanism）

**模板**:
> "To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies, we theorize that [theory 1] and [theory 2] pertain to different facets of [phenomenon]: [theory 1] focuses on [facet 1], and [theory 2] refers to [facet 2], so they are intertwined in explaining [outcome]."

**来源**: zhou2017 (ASQ), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "To resolve the theoretical and empirical inconsistencies, we theorize that the institutional and efficiency logics pertain to different facets of state ownership: the institutional logic focuses on resource allocation, and the efficiency logic refers to resource utilization, so they are intertwined in explaining innovation."

**关键特征**:
- "To resolve... we theorize that" → 从问题诊断直接跳到解决方案
- 将对立理论分配到一个构念的两个 facet → 超越"二元对立"
- "so they are intertwined" → 暗示整合框架而非选择一边

**适用 Gap 类型**: Incommensurability

---

### 变体 B：对立视角型（Incommensurability → Phenomenon/Mechanism）

**模板**:
> "Thus while existing theory shows how [behavior] can [positive effect], our theory suggests that [behavior] can also [opposite effect]. Though [literature] has examined [known mechanism], few studies have examined [new mechanism]. We suggest that [contribution]."

**来源**: keeves2017 (ASQ), P6 (contribution section, functioning as transition)

**原文锚定**:
> "Thus while existing theory shows how ingratiation can build social capital by inducing positive affect, our theory suggests that ingratiation can also elicit negative affect toward the influence target... Though a growing body of research has examined the behavioral mechanisms by which social capital is acquired, few studies have examined the micro-level, interpersonal mechanisms by which social capital is lost or damaged."

**关键特征**:
- "Thus while existing theory shows X, our theory suggests Y" → 对比句式，立即确立贡献的张力
- 从缺口（被忽视的方向）过渡到贡献（我们揭示了新方向）
- "few studies have examined" → 在过渡中再次确认缺口，然后立即提供解决方案

**适用 Gap 类型**: Incommensurability / Inadequacy

---

### 变体 C：文献推进型（Incompleteness → Boundary/Mechanism）

**模板**:
> "The article makes [N] contributions. First, to date, no study to our knowledge has examined [gap]. The [topic] literature has predominantly focused on [dominant focus 1] and [dominant focus 2]. However, little attention has been paid to [neglected focus]. By examining [our focus], our study offers insights on [value proposition]."

**来源**: eilert2017 (JM), P5

**原文锚定**:
> "The article makes two contributions... First, to date, no study to our knowledge has examined the factors that influence the timing of product recalls. The product recall literature has predominantly focused on the consequences of recalls and the firm's ability to learn and prevent future recalls. However, little attention has been paid to the actual recall behavior of the firm. By examining time to recall as its key dependent variable, our study offers insights on the behavioral aspects of product recalls."

**关键特征**:
- "no study to our knowledge has examined" → 谨慎但明确的贡献定位
- "predominantly focused on X and Y. However, little attention has been paid to Z" → 三段式：已有+然而+我们
- "By examining [our focus], our study offers insights on..." → 从"我们做了什么"到"读者获得了什么"

**适用 Gap 类型**: Incompleteness

---

### 变体 D：选择性忽视型（Inadequacy → Constructs/Boundary）

**模板**:
> "Most research underscores [dominant perspective] and overlooks [neglected aspect]. A number of studies conclude that [dominant finding]. We propose an alternative but overlooked strategy: [our contribution]."

**来源**: desjardine2023 (OS), P4

**原文锚定**:
> "Most research underscores the devious influence common owners have on corporate strategy and overlooks their systematic risk dilemma. A number of studies conclude that common owners engage firms to foster cooperation and anticompetitive behaviors... We propose an alternative but overlooked strategy: Common owners impel firms in their portfolios to adopt corporate strategies that minimize negative externalities and create spillover benefits..."

**关键特征**:
- "Most research underscores X and overlooks Y" → 简洁的两极对比
- "We propose an alternative but overlooked strategy" → 从"被忽视的"直接过渡到"我们提出的"
- "alternative but overlooked" → 暗示贡献的创新性和必要性

**适用 Gap 类型**: Inadequacy

---

### 变体 E：层层递进贡献型（通用）

**模板**:
> "We make several key contributions. First, we provide evidence that [contribution 1], an important refinement to [literature]. Second, our study provides evidence that [contribution 2]. Third, we contribute to [literature 3] by [contribution 3]."

**来源**: gamache2020 (SMJ), P5-P7

**原文锚定**:
> "We make several key contributions. First, we provide evidence that attributes of CEOs... influence the specific orientation of their firms' stakeholder strategies... Second, our study provides evidence that there are important nuances to consider in firms' stakeholder strategies. Most prior research... considers all stakeholder strategies collectively... Third, we contribute to research on CEO regulatory focus by expanding our understanding of its influence on firm outcomes."

**关键特征**:
- "We make several key contributions" → 最直接的过渡
- "First... Second... Third..." → 清晰的可追踪结构
- 每个贡献定位到具体文献流

**适用**: 当贡献维度清晰且可区分时（SMJ 风格）

---

### 变体 F：理论提供型（ASQ/ASR 理论型）

**模板**:
> "In this article, we develop and test the argument that [core mechanism] helps address [puzzle]. To develop this argument, we build on [prior theory] that highlights [insight]. We build, in particular, on [specific theory] for why [phenomenon]."

**来源**: hahl2017 (ASR), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "In this article, we develop and test the argument that authentic lowbrow culture helps address a sense of insecurity in authenticity that is common among elites, depending on how they are understood to have attained status. To develop this argument, we build on recent research that highlights a potential 'soft underbelly' of status hierarchies... We build, in particular, on Hahl and Zuckerman's (2014) theory for why high-status actors are often perceived—by themselves and others—as less authentic."

**关键特征**:
- "In this article, we develop and test the argument that" → 从 gap 直接过渡到核心主张
- "To develop this argument, we build on..." → 承认理论债务而非宣称原创
- 适合 ASQ/ASR 理论论文（不一定要用列表式贡献声明）

## 过渡链位置

```
P2 Literature ──[literature-to-gap]──→ P3 Gap ──[gap-to-contribution]──→ P6 Contribution
```

**位置变体**:
- 标准型（6-7段）: gap-to-contribution 出现在 P4 末尾或 P5（Theory/Preview 段）开头
- 扩展型（8-9段）: 出现在 P5（Preview）末尾或独立过渡句，连接 Gap+Stakes 与 Contribution 段
- 紧凑型（5段）: 出现在 P3 末尾，Gap 段最后一句直接过渡到 Contribution

## 组装规则

### 能量守恒
- **高能量 Gap (Incommensurability)** → 需要"整合方案型"或"对立视角型"过渡来承接颠覆性能量
- **中能量 Gap (Inadequacy)** → "选择性忽视型"或"层层递进型"
- **低能量 Gap (Incompleteness)** → "文献推进型"最合适，不要过度承诺

### 反模式提醒
- **不要从 Gap 直接跳到 "We find that..."**: 必须在 Gap 和 Findings 之间插入理论逻辑（Theory/Preview 段）
- **不要用 "This paper is organized as follows"**: roadmap 是过渡的后半段，不是 gap-to-contribution 本身
- **不要在过渡中过度承诺**: gap-to-contribution 的贡献预告必须与 Discussion 中实际展开的贡献一致
- **不要让过渡句成为孤岛**: 必须与前段（Gap）和后段（Contribution）都有语义连接，不能是突兀的 "We contribute to..."
