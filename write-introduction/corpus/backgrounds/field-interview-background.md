## 句法模板
## 变体 A：高管访谈流程前提型（mukherjee2022 型）

> 论证角色：[D] 定义前提（用田野访谈建立决策流程的两个制度事实——问题池与内外部扫描——作为理论模型的前提设定，并显式桥接至理论节）

**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）

**功能节拍**: 访谈来源与人脸（职级+汇报线+任期/事件量三连）→ 流程描述（监测→会议→建议→决策权）→ 提炼两个与研究直接相关的制度事实 → 二手来源交叉印证 → 显式桥接句宣告进入理论节

**模板**:
> "To obtain a comprehensive understanding of [the decision process] and [its timing] in [the industry], we interviewed [N] senior executives at [one of the dominant firms]. The first interviewee was [title] who [reported directly to the CEO]; the second was [title] who was personally responsible for [dozens of events] for more than a decade; and the third was [title] who [handled numerous events] over a [20-year] period. These interviews revealed that [firms] establish [monitoring teams] responsible for continuously monitoring [quality signals] using multiple internal and external data sources. [Process narrative: team → meeting → recommendation → decision authority]. Two important aspects of this process relevant to our study are (1) [institutional fact 1: pool of pending issues under consideration] and (2) [institutional fact 2: environmental scanning for new signals]. In other words, [actors] are constantly trying to determine if [issues] are \"[action-worthy]\" or if they should remain in the \"[wait-and-see]\" category. This process described to us is consistent with [secondary accounts] ([citation]). We build upon this anecdotal [process] understanding in the next section as we develop a theoretical model that motivates our [outcome] hypotheses."

**来源**: Mukherjee, Ball, Wowak, Natarajan, and Miller (2022), *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management*, §2 The Recall Process

**原文锚定**:
> "Two important aspects of this process relevant to our study are (1) firms maintain a pool of product quality issues that are under recall consideration and (2) firms scan their internal and external environment for new quality issues ... We build upon this anecdotal recall decision-making understanding in the next section as we develop a theoretical model."

**关键特征**:
- 访谈人脸三连：职级（vice chairman / VP / assistant general counsel）+ 直接汇报关系 + 任期与事件量（decade / 20-year / dozens）——来源合法性由制度位置而非学术身份建立
- 流程叙述只为提炼两个"与研究相关的制度事实"服务：问题池=延迟的可行集，扫描=竞争者信号进入决策的信息通道——每个事实都被理论节逐字消费（"wait-and-see"→"recall-worthy" 转移成为假设推演的核心装置）
- 二手来源交叉印证（popular press 流程报道）防止访谈孤证
- 显式桥接句宣告模块功能边界："We build upon this ... in the next section"——背景节不发展理论，只供给前提词汇

**适用情境**: 现象先开型 OM/MSOM 引言之后、理论节之前；决策过程细节构成模型前提（而非仅提供动机）时；有可具名的高管访谈来源；理论节将实际消费本节制度词汇。

**使用禁忌**: 访谈人数少（≤3）时必须靠职级与任期补偿合法性，不可虚报饱和度；制度事实必须被后文理论/假设实际消费——若理论节不用这些词汇，本模块沦为贴皮实践意义；单公司访谈的泛化边界需在 Data/Methods 节补强。
