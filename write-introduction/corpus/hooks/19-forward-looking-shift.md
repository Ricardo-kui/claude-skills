# 19-forward-looking-shift — 前瞻性视角转移 Hook

## 功能描述

从传统的研究视角（如 supply-side / production-side / firm-centric）出发，承认其贡献后指出，新兴的市场/技术条件要求研究进行一次"前瞻性视角转移"——从旧透镜转向一个能解释新动态的替代视角。核心机制是**新现实使旧透镜失效**——不是旧透镜错了，而是它设计的条件不再成立。

与 `05-literature-consensus-blindspot`（共识遗漏）不同，本 Hook 不声称文献有盲点，而是说**条件变化使已有的优秀研究不再充分**。与 `01-cross-disciplinary-analogy`（跨学科类比）不同，本 Hook 的视角转移发生在同一领域内（同一现象的另一种看法），而非跨领域借鉴。

## 适用场景

- 新兴市场/技术条件（数字平台、AI、共享经济）改变了传统组织现象的基本逻辑
- 论文提出一个新理论透镜，与现有透镜形成 complementary 而非 competitive 关系
- 研究涉及市场进入/定位/组织学习/战略更新
- 目标期刊偏好理论创新 + 新现象（OS, SMJ 首选）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：视角转移型（supply-side → demand-side）

**模板**:
```
Traditional approaches to [phenomenon] have focused on [conventional lens — e.g., what firms produce / how firms organize internally]. While this lens has yielded important insights, the [new condition — e.g., proliferation of heterogeneous offerings in digital markets] creates a fundamentally different challenge: [specific new puzzle]. We therefore conceptualize [phenomenon] not as [old definition], but as [new definition — e.g., positioning in a functional space defined by the set of functions a product performs for customers].
```

**来源**: zhao_ding2022 (OS), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "The advent of digital technologies has sharply reduced the costs of producing, distributing, and promoting products... These lower costs have contributed not only to exponential growth in the number of product offerings but also to unprecedented variety and a proliferation of heterogeneous market positions."

**关键特征**:
- "While this lens has yielded important insights" — 对旧视角的尊重，不 strawman
- "creates a fundamentally different challenge" — 新条件是驱动视角转移的因果力量
- "not as X, but as Y" — 清晰的概念重定义，确立新视角的操作化

**适用**: 数字化、平台经济、AI 驱动的新市场环境

---

### 变体 B：机制转移型（内部因素 → 外部反馈）

**模板**:
```
In principle, [actors] would choose [ideal strategy] based on [internal factors — e.g., capabilities, preferences]. In practice, they often face substantial uncertainty about [external factor — e.g., customer demand, competitor response]. Existing approaches may be insufficient in this context. Hence, we propose that [actors] learn about [environment] by observing [external cue] and use this [cue type] to inform their [decision].
```

**来源**: zhao_ding2022 (OS), adapted

**原文锚定**:
> "In principle, entrants would choose functional combinations that closely align with the target customers' needs and preferences. In practice, they often face substantial demand uncertainty about the functional attributes customers are likely to value in products... Hence, we propose that entrants in digital markets learn about the demand environment by observing customers' evaluations of other products and use this external market feedback to inform their initial market positions."

**关键特征**:
- "In principle... In practice..." — 认可旧逻辑在理想条件下的合理性
- "substantial uncertainty" — 新条件制造的新挑战
- 以具体行为机制收尾（learn by observing → use to inform）

**适用**: 组织学习、创业决策、战略更新中的反馈机制研究

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `11-overlooked-alternative` (Tension) 配对**: 视角转移的本质就是提出被忽视的替代方案（不是替代变量，而是替代透镜）
- **或与 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` (Tension) 配对**: 当旧视角的隐性假设（如"企业知道顾客要什么"）在新条件下不再成立时
- **与 Progressive Coherence (Literature Turn) 配对**: 承认现有研究的贡献，然后展示其在新条件下的边界

### 互斥
- **不能与 `06-paradigm-challenge` (Hook) 同用**: 本 Hook 说"旧透镜不够用"，范式挑战说"共识是错的"——前者是补充关系，后者是颠覆关系，逻辑矛盾
- **不能与 `04-puzzle-paradox` (Hook) 同用**: 视角转移的"新条件驱动"和谜题的"反直觉驱动"是不同的叙事机制

### 反模式提醒
- **旧视角 strawman**: "过去的学者愚蠢地只看 supply side"。必须用 "In principle... In practice..." 或 "While this lens has yielded insights..." 认可旧视角的合理性
- **视角转移无实际后果**: 换了视角但预测的东西完全相同。新视角必须导致至少一个不同于旧视角的实证预测
- **只谈视角不谈机制**: "我们采取需求侧视角"但没有具体的学习/反馈机制。新视角必须有可操作的行为机制
- **视角过于宏观**: "我们需要从 ecosystem 视角看"。必须将宏观视角操作化为具体的理论 construct 和可观测的变量
