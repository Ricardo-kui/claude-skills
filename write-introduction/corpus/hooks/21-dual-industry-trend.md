# 21-dual-industry-trend — 宏观趋势 + 双行业平行案例 Hook

## 功能描述

用一个宏观技术/制度变革（典型如数字化）开场，说明它如何打开新的定位/策略空间，再用**两个行业的平行案例**展示同一现象的普遍性——每个案例内部又以 incumbents vs entrants（或策略 A vs 策略 B）的分化结构呈现。这是 **phenomenon-driven（现象驱动）** 的中能量 Hook：不靠冲击数字、不靠灾难叙事，而是靠"同一驱动力在多个行业催生相似的分化"建立现象的可信度与复杂度，再段末升级到研究框架。

与 `03-data-shock` 的关键区别：本型以**宏观趋势陈述 + 案例配对**开场，没有"具体冲击数字 → yet little is known"的结构；与 `14-paired-disasters` 的区别：本型的双案例是**平行异质性展示**，不需要"修正-复发"的因果重演链。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Inadequacy**（首选：现有视角把现象理解偏了）或 **Incompleteness**（适配：现象尚缺系统刻画）
- Contribution 维度 = **Constructs / Mechanism / Phenomenon**（数字化、技术冲击、制度变革催生定位或策略异质性的研究）
- 存在**同一宏观变革下的两个行业案例**，且每个案例内部可呈现对比性分化
- 研究问题指向"actors 在多维空间中如何选择定位/策略"
- 目标期刊：OS（最适配）、SMJ、AMJ（分析型双案例可接受）

## 能量级

**中** — 宏观趋势 + 平行案例，建立现象复杂度但不靠情感冲击或数字震撼。通常需要独立的或嵌入式的 Stakes 段补足"So what"。

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：宏观趋势 + 双行业平行分化型（zhao_ding2022 型）

**模板**:
> "The advent of [macro technological / institutional shift] has sharply lowered the costs of [producing, distributing, and promoting] [offerings]. As a result, [positions / strategies] with [moderate or niche appeal] have become economically viable and attractive in [market type]. These [lower barriers] have contributed not only to [growth in the number of offerings] but also to [unprecedented variety and a proliferation of heterogeneous positions]. For example, in [industry A], [the shift] coincided with a clearer separation in positioning: [incumbent type] increasingly concentrated on [strategy A], whereas [entrant type] targeted [strategy B]. In [industry B], [the shift] similarly enabled divergent positioning strategies: [incumbent type] leaned into [strategy A'], whereas [entrant type] pursued [strategy B']. Across [market type], [actors] not only introduce new [offerings] but also choose among a wide range of feasible [positions], deciding where to differentiate and where to imitate across multiple dimensions."

**来源**: zhao_ding2022 (OS, 2023), P23

**原文锚定**:
> "The advent of digital technologies has sharply reduced the costs of producing, distributing, and promoting products. As a result, positions with moderate or niche appeal have become economically viable and attractive in digital markets. These lower costs have contributed not only to exponential growth in the number of product offerings but also to unprecedented variety and a proliferation of heterogeneous market positions. For example, in the film industry, digitization coincided with a clearer separation in positioning: Major movie studios increasingly concentrated on costly blockbusters aimed at theatrical release, whereas younger, newcomer producers targeted both 'long tail' low-budget, low-commercial-intent films and 'middle tail' films with moderate budgets and commercial intent released directly through digital streaming. In music, digitization similarly enabled divergent positioning strategies: Major labels leaned into reproducing previously successful artists and genres, whereas independent labels discovered new artists and released music aimed at smaller lower-revenue audiences. Across digital markets, firms not only introduce new products but also choose among a wide range of feasible market positions, deciding where to differentiate and where to imitate across multiple dimensions."

**关键特征**:
- 以**宏观驱动力句**开场（技术/制度变革降低了某类成本），而非冲击数字——这是与 `03-data-shock` 的分界
- 两个行业案例**平行**展示同一现象（digitization → divergent positioning），各自内部又以 incumbent vs entrant 的对比结构展开
- 每个案例是**平行论证**（证明现象的跨行业普遍性），不是单一沉浸式案例研究
- 段末从案例**升级到研究框架**："deciding where to differentiate and where to imitate across multiple dimensions"——这是 hook→puzzle 的桥，把现象转化为选择问题（下一段随即抛出 RQ）

## 关键功能短语

| 短语 | 功能 | 替换 |
|------|------|------|
| "The advent of [shift] has sharply [lowered / reshaped] the costs of..." | 建立宏观驱动力 | "A [shift] has transformed the economics of..." |
| "As a result, [positions] with [moderate or niche appeal] have become economically viable..." | 说明新定位空间为何打开 | "[strategies] once uneconomical are now viable" |
| "For example, in [industry A]... whereas in [industry B]..." | 双行业案例平行论证现象普遍性 | "Consider [industry A] and [industry B]..." |
| "Across [market type], [actors] not only... but also choose among..., deciding where to differentiate and where to imitate across multiple dimensions" | 从案例升级到研究框架（hook→puzzle 桥） | "More broadly, [actors] face choices along multiple dimensions" |

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` 变体 D（Tension）配对**: 双行业案例建立的"定位异质性"现象，需要挑战"低进入壁垒机械地转化为更多产品多样性"的隐含前提，才能转化为学术问题。zhao_ding2022 即此配对。
- **或与 `11-overlooked-alternative`（Tension）配对**: 若贡献是引入一个被忽视的解释变量（如 external market feedback 作为 pre-entry learning 来源）。

### 互斥
- **不能与 `03-data-shock`（Hook）同用**: 两者同属 Trend，但本型是"宏观趋势 + 案例配对"（无冲击数字），data-shock 是"具体数字 → scale → yet little is known"；机制不同，选其一。
- **不能与 `14-paired-disasters`（Hook）同用**: 双案例叙事但灾难型依赖"修正-复发"因果重演链；本型是平行异质性展示，无因果重演，混用会焦点分裂。

## 反模式提醒

- **必须有两个平行案例**: 单一案例不足以建立"跨行业普遍现象"的可信度。若只有一个案例，改用 `11-institutional-anecdote` 或 `10-practical-puzzle`。
- **每个案例内部必须有 contrast（incumbent vs entrant，或策略 A vs B）**: 纯描述性单案例不构成"divergent positioning"，只是举例，无法支撑"异质性"主张。
- **宏观驱动力句必须具体**（何种技术/制度变革降低了何种成本）: 泛泛的"环境变化"无法解释定位空间为何打开，读者不知道机制。
- **收束句必须升级到研究框架**: 停在案例描述、不抛出 puzzle，读者不知道为何要研究——必须用"deciding where to... across multiple dimensions"类句式把现象转化为选择问题。

## 诚实边界

- **本 Hook 能量为"中"（趋势 + 案例），不自带 Stakes**。与 `14-paired-disasters`（灾难严重性隐性承担 Stakes）不同，使用本 Hook 时**通常需要独立 Stakes 段或嵌入式 Stakes**说明为何定位选择重要。zhao_ding2022 用嵌入式（P27 "firm-specific knowledge may be insufficient to map the demand landscape"）。
- **双案例必须真实处于同一宏观变革之下**: 若两个案例分属不同驱动力，"平行论证现象普遍性"的逻辑链断裂。
- **仅 1 篇范文（zhao_ding2022 OS），status EMERGING**: 使用时标注"来自单篇范文"，待第二篇同类 Hook（宏观变革 + 双行业平行分化）交叉验证后升 VERIFIED。
