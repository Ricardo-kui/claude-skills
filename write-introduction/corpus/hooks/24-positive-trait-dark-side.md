# 24-positive-trait-dark-side — 正向特质的阴暗面 Hook

## 功能描述

从一个被文献和实践普遍视为**正向/理想型的高管特质**（如 confidence, charisma, optimism, founder identity）出发，揭示其在特定组织决策情境中可能产生的**反直觉负面后果**。核心逻辑是："我们长期认为 [positive trait] 对组织有利，但当 [boundary_condition] 出现时，它反而可能导致 [dark-side outcome]。"

## 适用场景

- 研究对象的 IV 在文献中主要与积极结果关联，但研究揭示其阴暗面
- 存在实践层面的"正向偏好"（如董事会偏爱自信 CEO、创业者崇拜）
- 需要建立反直觉张力但又不全盘否定该特质的已有价值
- 适合 upper echelons、领导力、创业、公司治理研究

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：文献共识 → 边界反转型

**模板**:
> "[Positive trait] is widely regarded as a desirable attribute in [actors]. Prior research has linked [positive trait] to [positive outcome 1], [positive outcome 2], and [positive outcome 3] ([citations]). Yet this literature has largely overlooked the possibility that [positive trait] may also have a darker side when [actors] confront [specific situational pressure]. We argue that, under such conditions, [positive trait] can lead [actors] to [dark-side behavior], thereby eroding [long-term value]."

**来源**: chung_low_rust_2022_jams (JAMS), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "Conviction in one's ability and judgment is often considered a valuable trait in CEOs... Being confident generally entails having relative certainty about one's ability to be successful... However, it is unclear which direction the relationship may take in the specific context of myopic marketing management."

**关键特征**:
- 先承认正向特质的已有价值（建立共识）
- 用 "Yet" / "However" 引入边界条件
- 不否定特质本身，而是否定"无条件积极"的隐含假设
- 将阴暗面锚定到具体组织情境（如 short-term earnings pressure）

---

### 变体 B：实践偏好 → 风险揭示型

**模板**:
> "[Practitioner community] increasingly prize [positive trait] when selecting [actors]. [Business press / consulting report] routinely lists [positive trait] among the essential qualities of effective [actors] ([citation]). This preference is not without reason: confident [actors] are more likely to [positive behavior A] and [positive behavior B] ([citations]). However, we contend that this emphasis on [positive trait] obscures a consequential risk: when [actors] face [pressure], [positive trait] may incline them toward [dark-side behavior]."

**来源**: chung_low_rust_2022_jams (JAMS), P1-P2

**关键特征**:
- 从实践选择标准切入，增强现实感
- 用 "However, we contend" 反转实践共识
- 强调"偏好本身制造了盲区"

---

### 变体 C：简洁悖论型

**模板**:
> "The very [quality] that makes [actors] effective at [positive domain] may also make them prone to [negative domain]. We examine when and why [positive trait] — typically celebrated as a source of [long-term orientation / innovation / stakeholder commitment] — can instead motivate [short-termist / risky / self-serving behavior]."

**关键特征**:
- 用 "the very quality that..." 强化悖论感
- 适合短引言或高能量开场

---


### 变体 D：构念条件效价对偶开篇型（dewan2020 型）

> 论证角色：Framing（用定义+双效价对偶在开篇即立起条件化研究问句，直接锚定全文 central knot）

**模板**:
> "The [construct], defined by [definition] ([citations]), can be an asset or a liability in the context of [consequential context] by [decreasing/increasing] the [consequences] ([citations]). To better understand whether [construct] is an asset or a liability in the context of [context], we examine how [construct] and [moderator] jointly determine when [construct] is an asset and when it is a liability. We focus specifically on the role of [construct] in determining whether [authority agent] [decision about target]."

**来源**: dewan_jensen_2020 (AMJ), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "The status of an organization, defined by the hierarchical position it occupies in a social system (Gould, 2002; Jensen, Kim, & Kim 2012), can be an asset or a liability in the context of organizational misconduct (Jensen, 2006; McDonnell & King, 2018; Sharkey, 2014). To better understand whether status is an asset or a liability in the context of organizational misconduct, we examine how status and scandal jointly determine when status is an asset and when it is a liability."

**关键特征**:
- 定义句即 Hook：首句给出构念的正式定义+引用，第二拍立即落在同一情境中的双效价（asset or liability），不用轶事或数据冲击起手，register 是理论先行的 definition-first
- 双效价保持开放而非阴暗面反转：与变体 A-C 的"正向共识→阴暗面揭示"不同，本变体两效价并存不预设立场，研究问句是 when（条件问句），谦逊语气适配 Incompleteness（区别于本文件 frontmatter 的 Inadequacy 主标注）
- when 问句在第 2 句即出现：Hook-to-puzzle 距离极短（2 句）；第三拍收窄到具体决策者（[authority agent] 的 [decision]），完成 Puzzle→RQ 的第一次收缩
- 可附带层次转移句（"We shift attention from how the [construct] of [unit A] affects [outcome] to how the [construct] of [unit B] affects [outcome]"）在同一开篇段内预告 Makadok Level 贡献

**适用**: 构念在同一情境内已有两侧文献支持的双向效应、且本文用调节变量回答"何时为资产何时为负债"的研究；Incompleteness × Mechanism/Boundary 组合；AMJ/SMJ 理论先行（definition-first）风格开篇

**禁忌**: 双效价必须有两侧文献支撑（每侧至少 2 篇引用），否则变成自造悖论；定义句压缩在一句内，避免开篇变成教科书条目；不要在 Hook 段就给出答案——when 问句必须保持开放直到 Tension 的调节者登场

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `14-debate-unresolved` (Tension) 配对**: 若文献对该特质的预测存在分歧（如自信→长期主义 vs 自信→短视）
- **与 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` (Tension) 配对**: 若 Hook 揭示的是"正向特质总是好"的隐性假设错误
- **与 `E 调节效应型` (Theory Variant) 配对**: 阴暗面通常由 boundary condition 触发，Theory 部分用调节效应解释

### 互斥
- **避免与 `06-paradigm-challenge` 同用**: 正向特质阴暗面是边界反转，不是范式颠覆；若反差极大可升级为 paradigm-challenge
- **避免与 `03-data-shock` 同用**: 数字冲击会稀释特质悖论的叙事焦点

### 反模式提醒
- **不要妖魔化正向特质**: 必须承认其已有价值，否则显得为反转而反转
- **边界条件必须具体**: 不能说 "under certain conditions"，必须指明是哪种压力/情境
- **阴暗面必须有机制**: 不能只说 "confidence causes myopia"，要在 Theory 部分解释心理机制（如 cost-benefit calculus）
