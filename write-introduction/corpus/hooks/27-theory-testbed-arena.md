**模板**:
> "[Focal network/site] has been viewed as an ideal arena in which to develop and test [theory perspective] on [phenomenon domain]. [The site] is a unique [formal mechanism] linking [actors]; it provides an opportunity for [actors] to [exchange information], [observe peers' practices], and [witness firsthand the consequences]. Thus, from this perspective, [site ties] should have a strong influence over [outcome domain]."

**来源**: gulati_westphal_1999_cooperative_or_controlling (ASQ), P1

**原文锚定**:
> "The board interlock network has been viewed as an ideal arena in which to develop and test the embeddedness perspective on interorganizational relations." / "Thus, from this perspective, board ties to other firms should have a strong influence over corporate policy and strategy decisions."

**关键特征**:
- 零能量冷启动：无数字、无轶事、无反例——能量全部来自"场域×理论适配"的排他性判断（ideal arena）
- 适配论证三件套：独有连结机制（unique formal mechanism linking actors）+ 观察机会列举 + firsthand 因果见证——证明为什么在这个场域检验该理论最有信息量
- 期望推导句 "Thus, from this perspective..." 显式安装理论基准期望，Hook 与 Tension 构成"安装-击破"对——Tension 的全部火力打在这句推导上
