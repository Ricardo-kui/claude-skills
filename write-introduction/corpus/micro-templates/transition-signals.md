# Transition Signals — 句子/短语级过渡信号词

> 本文件提供句内和句间的逻辑连接信号词。与 `transitions/` 目录下的模块级 transition 模板不同：模块级模板处理段落功能转换（如 Hook→Literature Turn），本文件处理微观逻辑关系（如 因果、对比、递进）。

---

## 1. 按逻辑关系分类

### 1.1 递进 / 补充（Addition）

**常用词**: again, and, also, besides, equally important, first (second, etc.), further, furthermore, in addition, in the first place, moreover, next, too

**学术写作偏好**: furthermore, in addition, moreover, equally important

**慎用**: "besides" 偏口语；"too" 通常放句末，学术段落中少用

**示例**:
> "Prior research has focused on CEO overconfidence as a driver of risky decisions. **Furthermore**, recent work suggests that overconfidence also shapes how leaders interpret stakeholder feedback."

---

### 1.2 举例（Example）

**常用词**: for example, for instance, in fact, specifically, that is, to illustrate

**学术写作偏好**: for example, for instance, specifically, that is, to illustrate

**注意**: "in fact" 语气较强，用于强调反直觉或具体化抽象主张

**示例**:
> "CEO communications can signal psychological traits. **For instance**, promotion-focused language tends to emphasize gains and aspirations, whereas prevention-focused language emphasizes safety and responsibility."

---

### 1.3 比较（Comparison）

**常用词**: also, in the same manner, likewise, similarly

**学术写作偏好**: similarly, likewise, in the same manner

**示例**:
> "Investors attribute positive outcomes more to promotion-focused CEOs. **Similarly**, they may discount negative outcomes when prevention-focused CEOs are perceived as vigilant."

---

### 1.4 对比 / 转折（Contrast）

**常用词**: although, and yet, at the same time, but, despite, even though, however, in contrast, in spite of, nevertheless, on the contrary, on the other hand, still, though, yet

**学术写作偏好**: however, in contrast, nevertheless, although, despite, yet, conversely

**注意**: 
- "but" 在学术写作中完全可用，不要刻意替换为 "however"
- "on the other hand" 暗示两个对立面都有合理性；不要滥用
- "on the contrary" 用于反驳前文，语气强，少用

**示例**:
> "Prior research has emphasized internal organizational processes. **However**, little is known about how external audiences interpret CEO motivational signals."

---

### 1.5 因果（Cause and Effect / Logical Relationship）

**常用词**: accordingly, as a result, because, consequently, for this reason, hence, if, otherwise, since, so, then, therefore, thus

**学术写作偏好**: therefore, thus, consequently, as a result, because, since, hence

**注意**:
- "so" 偏口语，学术段落中可用但不宜连续使用
- "then" 表时间顺序时与因果关系易混淆，谨慎使用
- "hence" 较正式，适合 Theory 段

**示例**:
> "Promotion-focused CEOs are perceived as pursuing maximal gains. **Therefore**, investors attribute positive earnings deviations more strongly to these CEOs."

---

### 1.6 总结 / 结论（Summary / Conclusion）

**常用词**: all in all, in conclusion, in other words, in short, in summary, on the whole, that is, therefore, to sum up

**学术写作偏好**: in sum, together, taken together, in other words, thus, therefore

**注意**:
- "in conclusion" 仅用于文章结尾，不用于段落结尾
- "in other words" 是优秀的 clarification transition，适合解释复杂机制

**示例**:
> "Promotion and prevention foci shape attributions through distinct mechanisms. **In other words**, the same earnings deviation can yield different investor reactions depending on the perceived CEO regulatory focus."

---

### 1.7 时间 / 顺序（Time / Sequence）

**常用词**: after, afterward, as, as long as, as soon as, at last, before, during, earlier, finally, formerly, immediately, later, meanwhile, next, since, shortly, subsequently, then, thereafter, until, when, while

**学术写作偏好**: subsequently, then, next, finally, earlier, previously, meanwhile

**示例**:
> "First, investors perceive the CEO's regulatory focus from annual report language. **Subsequently**, they use this perception as an interpretive frame when reacting to earnings deviations."

## 2. 学术写作中的 transition 使用原则

### 2.1 不要堆砌

- 每个段落通常只需要 1–2 个 explicit transition
- 连续使用 "Furthermore... Moreover... In addition..." 会显得机械

### 2.2 优先使用 subtle transition

以下技术有时比连接词更自然：
- **关键词重复**: 前句结尾的词在下句开头重现
- **代词/指示词回指**: "This finding...", "Such effects...", "These arguments..."
- **平行结构**: 连续句子使用相同语法结构

### 2.3 区分逻辑关系

| 想表达的关系 | 避免 | 推荐 |
|------------|------|------|
| 因果 | "So..."（偏口语） | "Therefore", "Thus", "Consequently" |
| 对比 | "But..." 连续使用 | "However", "In contrast", "Conversely" |
| 递进 | "And also..." | "Furthermore", "In addition" |
| 解释 | "Which means..."（非限制性定语从句堆砌） | "In other words", "That is" |

### 2.4 与模块级 transitions 配合

| 模块转换 | 推荐 module-level transition | 可选 sentence-level signal |
|---------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Hook → Literature Turn | `01-hook-to-literature` | "While this research..." / "Despite these advances..." |
| Literature Turn → Tension | `10-literature-to-gap` | "However..." / "Nevertheless..." / "Yet..." |
| Tension → Stakes | — | "This omission matters because..." / "Consequently..." |
| Stakes → Theory Lens | — | "To address this gap, we draw on..." / "Building on this..." |
| Theory Lens → Preview | — | "Specifically, we hypothesize that..." / "We test this thesis by..." |

## 3. 反模式

### 3.1 False transition

> "CEO overconfidence affects risk taking. **Moreover**, the sky is blue."

**问题**: "Moreover" 暗示递进关系，但两句之间无逻辑联系。

### 3.2 Over-reliance on adverbial transitions

> "However, prior research has focused on X. However, little is known about Y. However, our study addresses Z."

**问题**: 连续使用同一 transition 会削弱其功能，显得单调。

### 3.3 Transition without direction

> "CEO regulatory focus matters. **Therefore**, we study earnings deviations."

**问题**: "Therefore" 暗示直接因果，但前句与后句之间缺少中间推理步骤（为什么 focus 导致研究 earnings deviations？）。

## 4. 快速选择表

| 你的目的 | 选择 |
|---------|------|
| 在已有观点上增加一个观点 | furthermore, in addition, moreover |
| 用具体例子说明抽象观点 | for example, for instance, specifically |
| 指出与前文不同的观点 | however, in contrast, nevertheless, conversely |
| 解释前文（换种说法） | in other words, that is |
| 推导因果结论 | therefore, thus, consequently, as a result |
| 总结多个观点 | in sum, taken together, together |
| 按步骤推进 | first, second, then, subsequently, finally |
| 承认限制后立即转折 | although, while, despite |
