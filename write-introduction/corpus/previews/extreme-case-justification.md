# Preview: Extreme Case + Mixed Methods Justification / 极端案例+混合方法辩护

## 功能描述

为使用**极端案例**（extreme cases）和**混合方法**（qualitative + quantitative）的研究设计提供方法论合法性辩护。主动引用 Eisenhardt (1989) 和 Pettigrew (1990) 等经典方法论权威，将"使用极端案例"框定为**理论优势**而非**局限性**。同时论证混合方法（案例研究+大样本检验）比单一方法提供更"comprehensive, grounded, and generalized test"。

这是多方法/极端案例研究设计的**标准辩护模板**。

## 适用场景

- 研究设计涉及**极端案例/关键案例**（如灾难、危机、突破性创新）
- 研究设计涉及**混合方法**（质性理论构建 + 量化检验，或反之）
- 需要在 Introduction 中**主动防御**审稿人对案例选择和方法论的潜在质疑
- 目标期刊: OS, ASQ, AMJ, SMJ, JOM

- **可迁移性验证**: 极端案例辩护逻辑（Eisenhardt 1989, Pettigrew 1990）和混合方法互补性论证通用性强

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：极端案例 + 混合方法互补型（haunschild2015 型）

> "Our study therefore draws on both [qualitative method] to build theory and [quantitative method] to test that theory. Following prior research, we rely on extreme examples (e.g., [extreme case]) to inform our theoretical model because it is in such cases that the underlying dynamics and processes of our theory become more apparent ([citations]). In particular, the process of [theoretical process] is stronger in the presence of [extreme condition] than in more moderate ones ([citation]). The resulting model then serves as the basis for hypothesis generation and empirical testing in a different context, [Context 2]. The advantage of this study design is that it provides a more comprehensive, grounded, and generalized test of new theory than either [method 1] or [method 2] alone can provide ([citations])."

**来源**: haunschild2015 (OS), P7

**原文锚定**:
> "Our study therefore draws on both qualitative data to build theory and quantitative data to test that theory. Following prior research, we rely on extreme examples (e.g., shuttle disasters) to inform our theoretical model because it is in such cases that the underlying dynamics and processes of our theory become more apparent (Eisenhardt 1989, Pettigrew 1990). In particular, the process of organizational learning is stronger in the presence of serious errors than in more moderate ones (Madsen and Desai 2010). The resulting model then serves as the basis for hypothesis generation and empirical testing in a different context, the pharmaceutical industry. The advantage of this study design is that it provides a more comprehensive, grounded, and generalized test of new theory than either case study or deductive empirical work alone can provide (cf. Eisenhardt and Graebner 2007)."

**关键特征**:
1. **方法并列**: "both [qualitative] to build theory and [quantitative] to test" —— 清晰区分两种方法的功能
2. **极端案例权威引用**: Eisenhardt (1989) + Pettigrew (1990) 是**标准引用组合**，为案例研究提供经典合法性
3. **极端条件强度论证**: "stronger in the presence of [extreme condition] than in more moderate ones" —— 用理论过程本身的特性证明极端案例的合理性
4. **双情境连接**: "The resulting model then serves as basis for... testing in a different context" —— 质性→量化的桥梁
5. **三词总结**: "comprehensive, grounded, and generalized" —— 混合方法的三个优势
6. **cf. 引用**: "(cf. Eisenhardt and Graebner 2007)" —— 用 cf. 表示引用的是对比性/补充性观点，而非直接引用

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `14-paired-disasters` (Hook) 配对**: Hook 中的极端案例（NASA 灾难）需要在 Preview 中被方法论辩护
- **与双情境 Theory Lens / Preview 配对**: 混合方法通常对应多情境设计

### 互斥
- **不能与纯量化辩护同用**: 若研究是纯量化大样本，使用此骨架会造成方法论错配
- **不能与 Convenience sampling 同用**: 极端案例 ≠ convenience sample。必须论证极端案例的**理论必要性**

---

## 反模式提醒

- **不能只说"我们用了案例研究"**: 必须解释**为什么**极端案例适合本研究（"underlying dynamics become more apparent"）
- **不能混淆极端案例和典型案例**: 极端案例（extreme/critical/deviant）有特定的理论目的，不同于典型案例（typical/paradigmatic）
- **混合方法必须真正互补**: "qualitative to build + quantitative to test" 是经典组合。若两种方法回答同一个问题而不是互补，不要使用此辩护
