# 03-disciplinary-gap-stakes — 学科缺口 + 学科引语 + 实践政策 Stakes

## 功能描述

在 Gap 建立之后，用**学科层面的结构性空白**论证研究重要性，而非单纯量化经济损失。核心结构是：文献被局限在某类因素（通常是内部/传统因素）→ 这是严重缺陷因为外部/新兴因素已被证明重要 → 学科权威引语背书 → 实践收益 → 政策意义。这是将"外部治理引入内部领域"或"跨学科因素引入传统领域"的**标准 stakes 结构**。

与 `02-quantified-economic-loss` 的区别：本模块聚焦于**学科认识论后果**（我们遗漏了哪类行动者/机制/层次），而非财务数字。
与 `07-reputation-legitimacy-crisis` 的区别：本模块聚焦于**理论盲区**和**制度/政策后果**，而非声誉损害。

## 适用场景

- 研究将**外部因素**（外部治理、外部监督、制度压力）引入传统上关注**内部因素**（CEO、TMT、董事会、组织文化）的文献流
- 研究将**跨学科因素**（政治学、社会学、法学）引入管理学/运营/营销传统领域
- 需要同时说服**学术读者**（学科引语）和**实践/政策读者**（实践+政策意义）
- 目标期刊偏好"理论补白 + 管理相关性"组合（JOM、OS、SMJ、AMJ）

- **HIGH**: 与 Incompleteness / Inadequacy 的"因素遗漏"型缺口强绑定。不适用于 Incommensurability（后者需要呈现理论矛盾而非因素遗漏）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：外部治理引入型（darby2026 型）

**模板**:
> Research on [field] and [phenomenon] has largely been limited to *[internal/domestic/established]* [factor type], such as [example 1] ([citation]) and [example 2] ([citation]). This is a notable shortcoming because [one study in field] ([citation]) as well as a larger body of work in neighboring fields (e.g., [citations]) suggest that *[external/foreign/emerging]* [factor type] might influence [field] phenomena such as [phenomenon]. Conducting research along this pathway allows us to build understanding about [research question echo]. This is important because, as [citation] note, "[disciplinary quote about missing critical agents]" ([citation]). For practice, [practical actor] are wary of [phenomenon], but our findings highlight an important benefit of this pressure: [benefit]. For public policy, our findings may help [policy body] better allocate its limited [resources] by identifying factors that influence [outcome].

**来源**: darby2026 (JOM), P10 (Contribution/Stakes hybrid paragraph)

**原文锚定**:
> Research on corporate governance and product recalls has largely been limited to *internal* governance factors, such as female board representation (Wowak et al. 2021) and chief executive officer (CEO) stock ownership (Darby et al. 2023). This is a notable shortcoming because one operations and supply chain management (OSCM) study (Cheung et al. 2020) as well as a larger body of work in neighboring fields (e.g., Connelly et al. 2019; Lewellen and Lewellen 2022; Helmuth et al. 2023) suggest that *external* governance factors might influence OSCM phenomena such as product recalls. Conducting research along this pathway allows us to build understanding about what—or who—might encourage more timely recalls. This is important because, as Bendoly and Oliva (2024, 520) note, "If we fail to consider at least some of the critical operational agents and pathways in a given process, then we are missing out on some critical opportunities for informing our discipline." For practice, many executives are wary of the increased monitoring that large institutional investors bring, but our findings highlight an important benefit of this pressure: faster recalls. For public policy, our findings may help the FDA better allocate its limited monitoring resources by identifying factors that influence how quickly—or slowly—companies recall medical devices with dangerous defects.

**关键特征**:
- 首句用 "largely been limited to" 明确缺口，**斜体**强调 "internal"（与后文 "external" 形成视觉对比）
- "This is a notable shortcoming because" 建立缺口的严重性
- "one... as well as a larger body of work" 平衡领域内外证据，避免"只引用一篇文献"
- "Conducting research along this pathway allows us to build understanding about..." **回声首段问题**（darby2026 中回应急迫感段落的 "what—or who—might encourage more timely recalls"）
- 学科引语（Bendoly & Oliva 2024）不是装饰，而是为 "why this matters to the discipline" 提供权威背书
- 三段式收尾：理论（填补缺口）→ 实践（高管 wary but benefit）→ 政策（FDA 资源配置）

**适用 Gap 类型**: Incompleteness（文献遗漏了某类因素）/ Inadequacy（文献视角过度集中于某类因素）

### 变体 B：跨学科机制引入型（singh2023 型）

**模板**:
> The [phenomenon] literature has predominantly adopted an [efficiency/economic/operational] perspective, focusing on [dominant focus 1] and [dominant focus 2]. However, this perspective overlooks [neglected dimension], which is surprising given that [evidence of neglected dimension's importance]. By integrating [external field] insights into [phenomenon] research, we can better understand [new outcome]. This is particularly important because [practical/policy consequence].

**来源**: singh2023 (JMR), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "predominantly adopted an [X] perspective" → 指出文献的主导范式
- "surprising given that" → 用已有证据说明被忽视的维度其实很重要
- "By integrating [field A] insights into [field B]" → 明确跨学科贡献

**适用**: 跨学科研究（如政治学到运营、法学到战略、社会学到营销）

---

### 变体 C：对立视角补白型（desjardine2023 型）

**模板**:
> Most research underscores [dominant perspective] and overlooks [alternative/neglected aspect]. A number of studies conclude that [dominant finding]. [权威文献引用/summary quote]. We propose an alternative but overlooked strategy: [our alternative]. This matters because [theoretical consequence] and [practical consequence].

**来源**: desjardine2023 (OS), adapted

**关键特征**:
- "Most research underscores... overlooks..." → 简洁的两极对比
- "We propose an alternative but overlooked strategy" → 从"被忽视的"直接过渡到"我们提出的"
- 适合在 Contribution 段内同时完成 stakes 功能

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 Incompleteness / Inadequacy Gap 配对**: 本 stakes 的核心逻辑是"文献遗漏了某类因素"，必须前置相应的 Gap 语言
- **与 `05-literature-consensus-blindspot` (Hook) 或 `02-epigraph-quote-pivot` (Hook) 配对**: 若 Hook 已建立"现实紧迫"，本 stakes 提供"学科紧迫"的补充

### 互斥
- **不能与 `02-quantified-economic-loss` (Stakes) 同用**: 两者都是 Stakes，重复使用会造成 stakes 冗余。选择原则：若研究有明确可量化的经济后果，用量化型；若研究的核心贡献是"引入新因素/新视角"，用学科缺口型
- **不能与 Incommensurability Gap 的 `04-reality-contradicts-consensus` (Tension) 直接同用**: Incommensurability 需要呈现"两个理论互相矛盾"，而本 stakes 呈现的是"文献遗漏了一个因素"。如果研究确实是 Incommensurability，应该使用强调理论矛盾的 stakes，而非因素遗漏型

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只引用一篇文献支撑"外部因素重要"**: 必须用 "one study... as well as a larger body of work" 或 "emerging evidence from neighboring fields" 建立证据的广度
- **学科引语不能是装饰**: 引语必须**直接**关于"遗漏关键行动者/因素"，而不是一般性地谈"研究很重要"
- **不要跳过实践和政策意义**: 本 stakes 的五段式结构中，最后两段（实践+政策）是 JOM/OS 风格的核心特征，缺失会削弱管理相关性
- **不要只说"填补了空白"**: 必须解释"如果不填补，学科会失去什么机会"（引语的功能）
