# 09-resource-acquisition-vs-utilization — 资源获取 vs 资源利用张力

## 功能定义

将一种组织策略的"硬收益"（获取更多资源）与其"软代价"（损害资源使用效率）构成张力，从而重设评价标准：问题不是"能不能拿到资源"，而是"拿到了之后用得好不好"。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及某种被广泛认为"有益"的组织策略（如政治关联、联盟、多元化、融资）
- 但该策略可能在资源利用层面产生隐性成本
- "获取"和"利用"在理论上必须可分离
- 目标期刊关注效率/运营后果（JOM、POM、SMJ）

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：核心转向型（shen2022 型）

**模板**:
> "Based on [theoretical perspective], we suggest that although [focal strategy] may benefit firms' resource acquisition, it imposes constraints on resource utilization, leading to [negative outcome]. We suggest [N] reasons for this: (1) [motivation/agency-slack mechanism]; (2) [constraint/lock-in mechanism]; (3) [learning/path-dependence mechanism]."

**来源**: shen2022 (JOM), adapted

**原文锚定**:
> "Based on the political embeddedness perspective (Okhmatovskiy, 2010), we suggest that although political ties may benefit firms' resource acquisition, they impose constraints on resource utilization, leading to decreased operational efficiency. We suggest three reasons for this: (1) the resource benefits of political ties may induce complacency, inhibiting an efficiency-oriented culture; (2) political ties may bind firms to government expectations, limiting their ability to remove inefficiencies from their operations systems; and (3) the resource benefits of political ties may lead to path dependence, decreasing firms' ability to learn and apply operational knowledge."

**关键特征**:
- "although... may benefit... it imposes constraints" → 经典的让步-转折结构
- 立即给出 2-3 个机制解释，增强说服力
- 将评价标准从"有没有"转向"用得好不好"

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `05-literature-consensus-blindspot` (Hook) 配对**: 文献只看到获取的好处，忽略了利用的代价
- **与 `02-implicit-assumption-wrong` (Tension) 配对**: 隐性假设"资源越多越好"可能是错误的

### 反模式提醒
- **获取和利用混淆**: 获取本身被定义为"有效获取"，消解了张力 → 必须区分"拿到"(volume) 与"用好"(efficiency)
- **只给一个机制**: 感觉是孤证 → 至少给 2-3 个独立运作但又指向同一方向的机制
- **否定获取价值**: 暗示资源获取没有价值 → 必须承认资源获取是真实收益，只是伴随隐性代价
