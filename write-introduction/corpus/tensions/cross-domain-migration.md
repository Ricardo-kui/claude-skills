# Cross-Domain Migration Tension

## Skeleton
We apply this [source literature stream] to [target field] by examining whether [IV] impacts [DV].
Specifically, we posit that [early phase prediction], either to "[metaphor 1]" or because [mechanism 1], particularly when [condition 1].
Conversely, [late phase prediction], particularly when [condition 2].

## Source
Mayo et al. POMS

**原文锚定**:
> "We apply this CEO tenure and risk management decision-making research stream to an OM setting by examining whether CEO tenure impacts recall decisions. Specifically, we posit that early in tenure, a CEO may initiate recalls more frequently, either to "clean up shop" or because blame can be placed on the prior CEO, particularly when the predecessor was forced out of the role."

## Gap Type
Incompleteness


### 变体 A：邻近领域滞后型（gulati_higgins_2003 型）

> 论证角色：Claim（缺口主张——邻近领域已开始考察某可能性而焦点领域滞后的 Incompleteness 立论，为 RQ 授权）

**模板**:
> "While [adjacent-domain] scholars have begun to examine [the same phenomenon] for [adjacent unit] ([citations]), there is less understanding of this possibility for [focal unit] ([citation]). However, prior research has not examined if and when the effects of [X] vary for [focal unit], and so yield different consequences for [outcome]."

**来源**: Gulati & Higgins (SMJ, 2003), P1 + P3

**原文锚定**:
> "While organizational scholars have begun to examine the contingent value of ties for interpersonal networks (e.g., Burt, 1992; Podolny and Baron, 1997), there is less understanding of this possibility for interorganizational networks (Gulati and Westphal, 1999)." / "However, prior research has not examined if and when the effects of interorganizational embeddedness vary for young firms, and so yield different consequences for new venture performance."

**关键特征**:
- 邻近领域→焦点领域的滞后不对称：不否认邻近领域进展，用 "begun to examine"（承认起步）反衬焦点领域的沉默
- "this possibility" 回指上一句现象，缺口句无需复述现象即可自立
- "if and when" 一个短语同时声明存在性（if）与条件性（when）双重遗漏——比 "has not examined the effect" 信息量大
- 缺口句以后果收束（"different consequences for [outcome]"），把缺口直接焊在因变量上

**适用**: Incompleteness × Boundary/Mechanism；论文把成熟邻近领域（不同层次/单位/邻域）的现象迁移到焦点领域，且核心贡献是条件化（when）的研究

**禁忌**: 邻近领域必须真的"已开始"（有可引用文献），否则变成稻草人；"if and when" 后必须能给出真实调节维度，不能空转
