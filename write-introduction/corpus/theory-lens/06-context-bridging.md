# Theory Lens: Context Bridging in Intro / Introduction 内双情境映射

## 功能描述

在 Introduction 的 **Theory Lens 阶段**就提前映射**两个（或多个）实证情境**，向读者传递强烈的**理论普遍性信号**。不同于传统的"先理论后方法"（读者到 Methods 才知道第二情境），Context Bridging 在理论构建阶段就展示跨情境适用性，增强理论贡献的预期值。

这是跨情境研究设计（multi-context / multi-industry / qualitative+quantitative）的**高阶叙事技巧**。

## 适用场景

- Gap 类型 = **Incompleteness / Mechanism**
- Contribution 维度 = **Mechanism** / **Boundary**
- 研究设计涉及**两个或以上实证情境**（如 NASA + pharmaceutical, 中国 + 美国, 质性 + 量化）
- 两个情境共享**相同的核心理论机制**但表现形式不同
- 目标期刊: OS, ASQ, AMJ, SMJ

## 验证状态

### 跨论文复现
- **VERIFIED** (1 paper, distinctive): haunschild2015 (OS)
- **可迁移性验证**: "At [Context 1]... In [Context 2]..." 结构可迁移到任何跨情境研究

### 生成力
- **ADAPTABLE**: 双情境映射结构通用性强

### 排他性
- **MEDIUM**: 仅适用于确实有**多情境实证设计**的研究。单情境研究使用此骨架会造成 Contribution Contract 违约（承诺了普遍性但无法兑现）。

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：双情境并列映射（haunschild2015 型）

> "These alternative foci vary, depending on an [actor]'s core activities in a particular context. As we will explicate in more detail, at [Context 1], the shift away from [state A] meant a shift toward [state B1] (i.e., [specific meaning of B1]). In [Context 2], the shift away from [state A] means a shift toward [state B2] (i.e., [specific meaning of B2]). In both cases, the result of a [core construct] is a shift in emphasis to more [state A]-oriented behaviors. Then, over time, [actor] processes that promote [phenomenon B], such as [mechanism 1] and [mechanism 2], cause a gradual shift away from [state A] toward other foci. The ultimate result is another [core construct], which refocuses the [actor] on [state A], and the cycle begins again."

**来源**: haunschild2015 (OS), P4 (Theory Lens 第一段)

**原文锚定**:
> "These alternative foci vary, depending on an organization's core activities in a particular context. As we will explicate in more detail, at NASA, the shift away from safety meant a shift toward efficiency (i.e., launching more shuttles at less expense). In the pharmaceutical industry, the shift away from safety means a shift toward innovation (i.e., creating new drugs). In both cases, the result of a serious error is a shift in emphasis to more safety-oriented behaviors. Then, over time, organizational processes that promote forgetting, such as a false sense of security and employee turnover, cause a gradual shift away from safety toward other foci. The ultimate result is another serious error, which refocuses the organization on safety, and the cycle begins again."

**关键特征**:
1. **情境差异化**: 先承认"替代焦点因情境而异"，然后分别展示两个情境
2. **对称结构**: Context 1 → shift from A to B1 (i.e., 具体化)。Context 2 → shift from A to B2 (i.e., 具体化)。
3. **统一机制**: "In both cases" 回归共同机制（serious error → shift to A），强化理论普遍性
4. **循环闭合**: "The ultimate result is another [core construct]... and the cycle begins again" —— 完成 oscillation 叙事
5. **前瞻引用**: "As we will explicate in more detail" —— 承诺后续章节会详细展开，管理读者预期

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `14-paired-disasters` (Hook) 配对**: Hook 中的 NASA 案例需要在 Theory Lens 中被映射到第二个情境
- **与 Preview 中的双情境设计段落配对**: Theory Lens 中的 context bridging 必须与 Preview 中的研究设计一致（"First, we use [Context 1]... We then test... in [Context 2]")

### 反模式提醒
- **不能只有 Context 1 没有 Context 2**: Context Bridging 的核心是"桥接"，单情境使用此骨架会误导读者预期
- **两个情境必须共享同一机制**: 若 Context 1 的机制与 Context 2 不同，不能使用 "In both cases" 统一
- **不能过度展开**: Introduction 中的 context bridging 是**预览**，每情境只需 1 句话具体化。详细的情境描述留给 Theory/Methods

---

## 诚实边界

- **Context bridging in Intro 需要后续 Theory/Methods 中确实出现双情境**。Haunschild 在 Intro P4 提到 NASA+pharma，后续确实有两章分别处理（NASA 质性案例 + pharmaceutical 量化分析）。若只在一个情境中研究，提前映射会造成 **Contribution Contract 违约**。
- **两个情境必须机制相同但表现形式不同**。若机制也不同，这不是 context bridging，而是 multi-study 的独立验证。
