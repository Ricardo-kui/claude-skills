# 09-construct-contrast-introduction — 构念对照引入型 Theory Lens

## 功能描述

在 Theory Lens 段落引入一个**新构念**，方法是将它与读者已熟悉的**旧构念**（同属一个构念家族）进行对照。新构念通过与旧构念的差异获得合法性，其价值被锚定在**旧构念失效的 scope condition** 中。这是 Constructs 贡献维度的核心 Theory Lens 策略：不是综述理论，而是用最小认知成本让读者接受一个新概念。

## 适用场景

- 贡献维度 = **Constructs**（引入新构念/细分构念）
- 新构念是某个已有构念家族的"被忽视成员"
- 旧构念在特定情境下失效或冗余——新构念恰好在该情境中凸显价值
- 特别适合 knowledge / capability / attention 类构念的细分研究

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：构念对照引入型（du_tsolmon2024 型）

**模板**:
> "We theorize that [new construct], a key component of [established construct family], shapes [outcome]. [New construct] refers to the '[plain-language gloss]': how [actors] [function] within [context] ([citations]). It contrasts with [established construct], which concerns '[gloss]', such as [examples] ([citations]). In [scope condition], where [established construct] overlaps/[fails], we propose that [new construct] becomes especially relevant for [outcome]."

**来源**: du_tsolmon2024 (ORSC), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "Structural knowledge refers to the 'how-to' of organizational operations: how managers navigate and coordinate effectively within specific organizational structures. It contrasts with technical knowledge, which concerns 'what to do,' such as market and industry-specific expertise. In related acquisitions, where technical knowledge overlaps between firms, we propose that structural knowledge becomes especially relevant for determining which target managers are retained."

**关键特征**:
- 用 plain-language gloss 定义抽象构念：'how-to' vs 'what to do'——一句口语化对照让读者秒懂构念差异，无需学术术语
- 'It contrasts with [established construct]'——新构念通过与读者已熟悉的旧构念对照获得合法性（不需要从零论证）
- 'where [established construct] overlaps, [new construct] becomes especially relevant'——把新构念的价值锚定在旧构念失效的 scope condition 中
- 构念引入即回应 Tension：redundancy 争论只在 technical knowledge 层面成立，在 structural knowledge 层面不成立——Theory Lens 直接溶解 P2 的悖论

**适用**: Constructs 贡献维度（新构念引入）；当新构念是某个已有构念家族的'被忽视成员'，且旧构念在特定情境下失效时

**禁忌**:
- 新旧构念必须同属一个家族且有真实差异（不能是同一构念换标签）
- plain-language gloss 必须准确——'how-to'/'what to do' 式的口语化不能牺牲构念精度
- scope condition 必须在 Theory/Methods 中被操作化（否则'where X overlaps'只是修辞）

### 变体 B：镜像 whereas 配对定义 + 解释框架承接（pfarrer2010 型）

**模板**:
> "'[Construct A]' refers to [gloss A: accumulation of public recognition of the quality of a firm's capabilities and outputs] ([citations]), whereas '[Construct B]' refers to '[quoted gloss B1]' focused on a firm combined with '[quoted gloss B2]' ([citation]). We focus on [outcomes] that deviate from prior expectations because they require [stakeholder audiences] to make sense of these deviations, drawing on the interpretative frames that [the asset class] provides. [Construct A] and [Construct B] are '[shared ontological label]' that constitute distinct interpretative frames that are likely to influence [stakeholder behaviors] in different ways ([citations]). Further, we consider how their effects may vary under different conditions by considering both [valence 1] and [valence 2] deviations from expectations."

**来源**: pfarrer2010 (AMJ), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "'High reputation' refers to the accumulation of high levels of public recognition of the quality of a firm's capabilities and outputs..., whereas 'celebrity' refers to 'a high level of public attention' focused on a firm combined with 'positive emotional responses from stakeholder audiences'.... Reputation and celebrity are 'social facts' that constitute distinct interpretative frames that are likely to influence stakeholder behaviors in different ways."

**关键特征**:
- 与变体 A（du_tsolmon2024 型）的差异：本变体在引言内**同时定义两个同家族对等构念**（非"新构念 vs 旧构念"单侧对照），whereas 镜像句让两个定义互为参照系，差异即刻可见
- 定义后立即接**承接句回答"差异为什么重要"**：两构念共享一个存在论类别标签（'social facts' 式），但构成不同的 interpretative frames → 影响受众行为的方式不同——构念差异直接供货给后文方向相反的预测
- 构念 B 的定义用**引语合成**（quoted gloss + 出处页码），不另造定义——适用于构念已有权威定义、本文贡献在辨析而非重定义的情境
- 末句**条件拆分预告**（both [valence 1] and [valence 2] deviations）把 Boundary 维度提前写进定义段，为正负双情境假设组铺路

**适用**: Inadequacy × Constructs（构念辨析）的 Introduction Theory Lens 拍；两构念地位对等、需在引言内完成"定义即辨析"的研究；与 `05-construct-confusion`（构念混淆 Tension）直接配对——混淆 Tension 的答案就是镜像配对定义

**禁忌**:
- 两构念必须真有理论差异且差异能映射到后文预测（否则 whereas 只是修辞对仗）
- 引语合成定义需给出可核查出处；若本文贡献正在于提出新定义，改用变体 A 的单侧对照
- 共享类别标签必须真实成立（两构念同属一个存在论/机制类别），不能为了对称硬造

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `14-debate-unresolved` (Tension) 配对**: 构念对照天然用于调和"两阵营各执一词"的辩论——新构念揭示辩论只在旧构念层面成立
- **与 Constructs 贡献声明配对**: 贡献声明必须明确"We clarify/distinguish [new construct] from [established construct]"

### 反模式提醒
- **不要只做对照不做 scope condition**: "A contrasts with B" 之后必须接"在 [某情境] 中 A 失效、B 凸显"——否则对照只是定义练习
- **不要用 jargon 定义 jargon**: gloss 必须是 plain-language（'how-to'/'what to do'），不能是 "structural knowledge refers to structurally-embedded coordination capabilities"
- **不要让新构念脱离 Tension**: 新构念必须溶解或重构 P2 的悖论，不能是无关的新概念
