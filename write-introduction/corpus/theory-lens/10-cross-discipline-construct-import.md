# 10-cross-discipline-construct-import — 跨学科构念导入型 Theory Lens

## 功能描述

在 Theory Lens 段落向读者导入一个**来自相邻学科、目标读者完全不认识的新构念**，四步教学式完成：开场句把新构念直接插进 Tension 的缺口 → 日常轶事让读者先"体验"构念 → 引语式正式定义（quoted definition）给合法性 → 点名构念的已命名后果。最后用触发桥接句把外来构念焊回本领域谜题。

## 适用场景

- 贡献维度 = **Mechanism**（从相邻学科移植解释机制）
- Tension 段已用 rival-rebuttal 证明既有解释失效，需要一个读者不认识的新构念补位
- Theory 章将系统发展该构念——引言只做预告级导入
- 特别适合社会心理学 / 行为经济学微观构念进入组织与战略情境

### 变体 A：跨学科构念导入——轶事演绎+引语定义+触发桥接型（westphal_bednar2005 型）

**模板**:

> "Although [focal actors] may [possess expected quality] than [other actors], they may yet fail to [remedial action] due to a different kind of [bias/force] characteristic of [source domain]: '[construct].' [Construct] was popularized in [popularizer]'s writings on '[anecdote label],' in which [one-sentence everyday anecdote]. [Anecdote] is an instance of the more general phenomenon of [construct], defined by [source discipline] scholars as '[formal definition quote]' ([citation]). Research in [source discipline] has shown how [construct] can lead to '[labeled consequence]' ([citation]). [Construct] is a general phenomenon that can occur in [range of settings]. But there is evidence that [specific trigger] in particular can [activate] [construct] in [target groups], as [micro process] ([citation])."

**来源**: westphal_bednar2005 (ASQ), P4

**原文锚定**:

> "Pluralistic ignorance is a general phenomenon that can occur in a variety of circumstances in which events raise concerns among individuals about the viability of group policies or practices."

> "But there is evidence that negative performance feedback in particular can trigger pluralistic ignorance in groups, as individual members become concerned about the viability of the group's strategy and practices but tend to underestimate the extent to which others share their concerns."

**关键特征**:

- 教学式构念导入四步：开场句直接把新构念插进 Tension 的缺口（"a different kind of ... bias"）→ 日常轶事让读者先"体验"构念 → 引语式正式定义（quoted definition）给合法性 → 点名构念的已命名后果（如 attitude-behavior disjunction）
- 触发桥接句（"general phenomenon → But [trigger] in particular"）把外来构念焊回本领域谜题，防止构念悬空——这是本变体与一般构念介绍的根本区别
- 开场句用 "may yet fail ... due to a different kind of" 直接承接 rival rebuttal，使 Theory Lens 与 Tension 关键词重叠
- 与 09-construct-contrast-introduction 的区别：那里靠同家族旧构念对照获得合法性，这里靠源学科定义+轶事教学，适用于读者完全不认识的新构念

**适用**: 从相邻学科（社会心理学、行为经济学等）导入目标读者不熟悉的微观构念并移植到组织情境；Inadequacy × Mechanism；Theory 章将系统发展该构念时的引言级预告

**禁忌**: 轶事不能替代理论——必须给出可引用的正式定义（引语+出处）；桥接句必须点名本领域 trigger 与 micro process，否则构念悬在源学科；不要一次导入多个新构念，本变体只服务单一主角构念


### 变体 B：田野观察奠基型（gulati1998 型）

**模板**：
The notion that [透镜核心主张] is closely rooted in [底层过程]. I first observed this when I was conducting field interviews at [多现象场景] and found that [标准解释并不成立]. Rather, [观察到的替代模式]——[透镜变量] was likely to influence [议程中的关键决策1..k]. My fieldwork suggested that [透镜机制] not only influenced [结果1] but also affected [结果2..k].

**关键特征**：
- 用作者第一人称田野观察（而非文献或数据）为综述透镜提供经验担保——“I first observed this when...” 把 lens 锚进真实观察
- 先立“标准解释”再以 “Rather,” 推翻，制造小规模反常（anomaly）证明旧层级解释不足
- 观察发现逐一映射回议程中的编号决策（频率/伙伴/合同/演化），使 lens 与议程闭环
- 收束句 “not only... but also...” 把单点观察升级为贯穿全文的机制主张

**原文锚定**：I first observed this when I was conducting field interviews at a number of firms with multiple alliances and found that firms don't necessarily follow the sequence of events that is usually offered for alliances... My fieldwork suggested that the social networks of prior ties not only influenced the creation of new ties but also affected their design, their evolutionary path, and their ultimate success.
**来源**：gulati_1998_alliances_and_networks (SMJ), Introduction P5
**适用**：综述/perspective 文有真实田野或一手观察时；为“引入透镜”型贡献补经验可信度；也可用于实证文 theory 段前的动机叙事
**禁忌**：无一手观察勿虚构田野故事；观察叙述必须最终落到可研究的变量与决策上，不能停留在趣闻

### 变体 C：学科透镜导入+定谳桥接型（gulati1999 型）

**验证状态**: VERIFIED (expert_audit_override 2026-09-05: 用户点名 Gulati 为最喜爱学者之一，其论文蒸馏单源即 VERIFIED)；单篇来源，仅作 section_variant

**功能节拍**: 源学科实证演示链（结构→信息流→机会与约束→行为与绩效）→ 层级桥接（个体层透镜合法搬到组织层）→ 定谳句：用源透镜给本领域文献整体贴诊断性标签

**模板**:
> "[Source discipline] scholars have convincingly demonstrated how [lens construct: the structure of ties within which actors are embedded] can influence their subsequent actions ([founding citations]). They have shown that [structural pattern] shapes [flow/consequence] ([citations]). This in turn provides both opportunities and constraints for actors and can have implications for [behavior and performance]. Such a [lens] perspective is applicable to both [original level] and [target level] ([citations]). Viewed from this standpoint, much of the research on [target phenomenon] represents a [verdict label] account of [actors]' behavior."

**来源**: gulati_1999_network_location_and_learning_the_influence_of_n (SMJ), P3

**原文锚定**:
> "Viewed from this standpoint, much of the research on strategic alliances represents an undersocialized account of firm behavior."

**关键特征**:
- 导入的是宏观学科透镜（经济社会学的嵌入性），不是微观构念——用"源学科已证明的实证演示链"替代变体 A 的轶事+引语定义教学
- 桥接句是"定谳"（verdict label）：给本领域文献整体贴一个诊断性标签（"undersocialized account"），一锤定音把 Tension 升格为透镜裁决
- 中间补层级桥接句（"applicable to both individual and interorganizational networks"）——把源学科的个体层透镜合法搬到组织层
- 与变体 A 的区别：A 教读者一个不认识的微观构念；C 借成熟宏观透镜裁决本领域的解释缺陷

**适用**: 从相邻学科（经济社会学、社会学）导入宏观透镜并为既有文献整体"定性"时；Inadequacy × Constructs；透镜在源学科已有成熟实证传统、目标领域仅零星应用

**禁忌**: 定谳标签必须能被透镜的实证演示链支撑（引 2–3 篇源学科奠基作），不能只造词；标签一词须可被后续构念反转为补救（本文用 network resources 补 undersocialized），否则只剩攻击无建设；目标读者已熟悉透镜时教学链应压缩

## 组装规则

- 必须配对 rival-rebuttal 类 tension（如 tensions/11-overlooked-alternative.md 既存解释实证反驳型）——Tension 已证旧解释失效，新构念才有落点
- 反模式：构念悬空（无 trigger 桥接）、轶事代理论（无正式定义）、多构念并导

## 期刊适配

- ASQ / AMJ：高——两刊历来接纳社会心理学构念移植
- SMJ：中——需更强调构念的战略后果而非心理过程本身

## 槽位填充正误对比

- 正例（westphal_bednar2005）：构念 = pluralistic ignorance；轶事 = Abilene Paradox（Harvey 2001）；定义 = Miller, Monin & Prentice (2000) 引语；已命名后果 = attitude-behavior disjunction；trigger = negative performance feedback；target groups = groups facing strategic uncertainty
- 误例：只给轶事不给引语定义（读者无从引用）；或只给定义不给 trigger（构念与本领域谜题无接口）
