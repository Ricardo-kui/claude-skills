# 13-multi-actor-upper-echelons-funnel — 多行为者 Upper-Echelons 漏斗过渡

## 功能描述

在 Introduction 中从**单一高管层级**（CEO）逐步扩展到**多个组织层级**（CEO → CMO/高管团队 → Board）的互动框架。这不是简单的"我们也要研究 CMO 和 board"，而是论证：要理解 CEO 的决策，必须考虑与其互动的其他层级行为者及其相对权力/影响力。

## 适用场景

- 研究涉及 upper echelons 中多个层级的互动（CEO + TMT + board）
- 理论框架需要跨越个人、团队、治理三个分析层级
- 需要解释为什么单一 CEO 视角不足以回答研究问题
- 常见于 upper echelons、公司治理、营销战略、TMT 研究

## 句法模板

### 变体 A：三层互动框架引入

**模板**:
> "To understand how organizational factors may [exacerbate/mitigate] the impact of [CEO trait] on [DV], we draw upon the upper echelons [theoretical framework] ([citation]) to model how the players at other hierarchical levels of the organization interact with the [CEO]. [Lower-level actor] has incentives to [oppose/support] [DV] but only a [persuasive/formal] role because [resource/decision] is largely controlled by the CEO. We further model how the [board-level actor], being the top decision-making body in a firm, affects the [DV] tendencies of the [CEO] through its [monitoring/influence]. Figure [N] shows our conceptual model."

**来源**: chung_low_rust_2022_jams (JAMS), P2

**原文锚定**:
> "To understand how organizational factors may help exacerbate or mitigate the impact of CEO confidence, we draw upon the upper echelons marketing strategy theoretical framework in Whitler et al. (2021) to further model how the players at the other hierarchal levels of the organization (CMO and board of directors) interact with the confident CEO to affect myopic marketing management."

**关键特征**:
- 明确说明为什么要引入多层级（理解组织因素如何调节 CEO 效应）
- 每一层都说明其角色（劝说 vs 正式决策）
- 引用 upper echelons 理论框架增强合法性
- 以概念模型图收束

---

### 变体 B：文献缺口驱动型

**模板**:
> "Although firm decisions are made by the CEO with help from other C-suite members, and the board of directors also plays an important role in the process, there is a dearth of research examining more than one level of the upper echelons simultaneously ([citation]). To our knowledge, we are among the first to examine [lower-level actor]'s psychological traits and how they interact with the CEO's [trait] to influence [DV]. We also extend beyond the top management team to incorporate a [board-level perspective]. By considering the interactions between the different strata of the organization, we extend the understanding of how firms make strategic decisions."

**来源**: chung_low_rust_2022_jams (JAMS), P3

**关键特征**:
- 以文献缺口为由引入多层级
- 强调研究的创新点（首次同时考虑某 lower-level actor 和 board）
- 适合贡献陈述段落

---

### 变体 C：简洁多层预览型

**模板**:
> "We therefore examine [DV] through a multi-actor lens. Specifically, we ask: (1) how does [CEO trait] affect [DV]? (2) how does [lower-level actor] moderate this relationship? and (3) how does [board-level actor] further shape these dynamics? This layered approach allows us to capture the interplay between [individual trait], [intra-TMT persuasion], and [governance pressure]."

**关键特征**:
- 用研究问题串起三个层级
- 简洁，适合短引言

## 组装规则

### 必须配对
- **与 `24-positive-trait-dark-side` (Hook) 配对**: 当 Hook 建立了 CEO 特质的阴暗面时
- **与 `E 调节效应型` (Theory Variant) 配对**: 多层级通常以调节效应形式进入理论
- **与 `write-theory/corpus/subprotocols/intra_tmt_persuasion.md` 配对**: CMO 层级的劝说逻辑
- **与 `write-theory/corpus/subprotocols/board_governance_boundary_condition.md` 配对**: Board 层级的治理压力逻辑

### 互斥
- **不要在没有理论理由的情况下使用**: 如果研究只涉及 CEO，不需要此过渡
- **不要堆砌层级**: 每一层必须有明确的理论功能（劝说、监督、决策）

### 反模式提醒
- **不要把层级关系写成并列**: CEO → CMO → Board 有明确的权力/决策层级
- **不要忽略权力不对称**: 必须说明 lower-level actor 是劝说而非决策
- **不要在没有引用 upper echelons 理论的情况下使用**: 这是该过渡的理论基础
