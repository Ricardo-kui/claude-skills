# 多研究 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 填空段落骨架 → `多研究`。

## 设计特征摘要

多研究实验设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**研究梯度的可读性**：读者必须一眼看出每个研究回答什么问题、与前一个研究的关系、以及为何不能合并为一个研究。典型结构包括：(1) 项目总览段落；(2) 每个研究的独立 Methods（参与者→材料/程序→操纵→操纵检验→测量）；(3) 跨研究一致性与变异性的明确说明。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: 多研究实验项目总览（研究梯度图）
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M1/M15
**骨架**:
> We conducted [number] experimental studies to examine [core relationship]. Study [1] tests [hypothesis] with [audience 1] using [stimulus type] and measures [DV]. Study [2] replicates the test with [audience 2] to examine [boundary]. Study [3] extends the findings to [new stimulus/DV] and explores [mechanism]. Study [4] confirms the pattern with [audience/stimulus/DV]. Across studies, [manipulation] is held constant while [audience/stimulus/DV] varies, allowing us to assess the robustness and boundary conditions of [theoretical claim].
**与原骨架差异**: 多研究实验必须在 Methods 开头给出**研究梯度图**，不是简单罗列"Study 1... Study 2..."，而要说明每个研究的 unique contribution 和变异维度。关键：明确什么保持不变（manipulation）、什么变化（audience/stimulus/DV）、以及变化的目的。

### 变体 2: 操纵检验 Pilot Study 段落（嵌入主 Methods）
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M4/M8
**骨架**:
> To verify that our manipulation of [IV] produced the intended difference in [perceived construct], we conducted a pilot study with [N] participants recruited from [source]. Participants were randomly assigned to [condition A] or [condition B] and asked to rate the [stimulus] on [measure]. We excluded participants who [attention-check rule]. A one-way ANOVA confirmed that participants in [condition A] perceived the [stimulus] as significantly more [construct] (M = [value], SD = [value]) than those in [condition B] (M = [value], SD = [value]), F([df1], [df2]) = [value], p [relation] [threshold]. We therefore concluded that the manipulation was effective and used it in the main studies.
**与原骨架差异**: 操纵检验可以作为独立 pilot study 嵌入 Methods，而不是放在附录。本骨架强制包含：(1) pilot 目的；(2) 样本与排除规则；(3) 测量；(4) ANOVA 结果（M/SD/F/p）；(5) 结论。关键：让读者相信主实验的 IV 确实操作化了 intended construal。

**骨架**:
> We conducted [N] complementary studies using different data sources and methods. Study 1 uses [data_type_A] from [N_A] [units/respondents] in [setting_A] to test [Hypotheses_set_A]. This multi-study design allows us to [triangulate / test robustness / extend generalizability] while leveraging the complementary strengths of [method_A: internal/measurement precision] and [method_B: external validity/longitudinal scope].

### 变体 4: M9 三研究递进设计论证 — 内部效度→概念复制→生态效度 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M9
**骨架**:
> We conducted three complementary studies to examine [research question]. Specifically, we conducted [design_type_1] (Study 1) with [sample_description]. [Design_type_1_advantages: e.g., Experiments are quite common in... research because... Random assignment equates the different experimental conditions...]. In these studies, we manipulated [IV] and [moderator]. The measured dependent variables were [DV_list]. Study 2 was similar to Study 1, except that we operationalized [key_variable] differently ([replication_type: conceptual replication]). In Study 3, we used [design_type_3] to directly test [theoretical_relationship], demonstrating the [validity_type: ecological validity] of our model at the [level: market level].
**与原骨架差异**: 本骨架与变体1（Falchetti 四研究梯度图）和变体3（Bamberger 混合方法衔接）互补——Li et al. 提供了**内部效度→概念复制→生态效度**的三研究递进叙事：(1) 实验1 建立内部效度（随机分配排除混淆）；(2) 实验2 概念复制——"same design except operationalization"（将字面死亡词替换为伪死亡词），测试跨操作化稳健性；(3) 现场面板研究3 建立生态效度——将实验发现的机制推广到真实市场反应。关键策略：每个研究的设计选择都有显式的方法论理由（"experiments avoid endogeneity" / "conceptual replication" / "ecological validity at the market level"），而非仅罗列三个研究。名称标签（internal validity / conceptual replication / ecological validity）让审稿人一眼看懂研究梯度的逻辑。
**诚实边界**: 研究梯度的命名标签（如"conceptual replication"、"ecological validity"）必须与设计实质匹配——若 Study 2 仅改变样本而非操作化，应称为"direct replication"而非"conceptual replication"。
