# 文本构念测量 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 填空段落骨架 → `文本构念测量`。

## 设计特征摘要

文本构念测量指用文本挖掘（典型为 LDA topic modeling、word embeddings）从非结构化文本痕迹（产品描述、专利、新闻、10-K、招聘贴）中**构建潜在构念**，再把每个单元在该构念空间上的概率/权重向量转化为可分析的 DV/IV。本设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**效度论证链**：为何选该文本方法、为何选 K 个主题/维度、概率向量如何映射为分析变量、与替代操作化是否一致。Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (OS) 是该设计类型的黄金范文：LDA → 18 functions → 概率向量 → focus on core (DV1) / peripheral overlap (DV2) / dissatisfaction (IV1) / heterogeneity (IV2)，全程配 coherence + interpretability 双标准与 Herfindahl(0.955)/Blau(0.99) 交叉验证。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M2 样本漏斗（含语料构建与语言筛选）、M3 构念效度链（统计标准+理论标准+交叉验证）、M4 测量选择 because。常见薄弱点：M8 identification（文本构念仍为观测数据，需讨论测量误差与内生性）、M7 未报诊断（VIF 等）。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M2 文本构念样本漏斗（语料构建 → 分析样本 三段式）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M2
**骨架**:
> To identify [construct], we restricted the sample to [initial population] with [language/filtering criteria], ensuring comparability. Our final sample includes [N_corpus] [units] launched between [year_start] and [year_end], whose [text traces] we used to build the corpus for textual analysis and to identify [latent structure]. For analysis, we tracked [target events] from [date_start] to [date_end] as [feedback/contextual data] became systematically available since [date]. The procedure yielded [N_mid] total [units] launched by [N_actors], of which [N_final_actors] initially launched [N_final_units] upon entering the market.
**与原骨架差异**: 文本构念设计的样本漏斗需区分**语料构建样本**（全历史，用于训练 LDA/embedding）与**分析样本**（有反馈/上下文数据的子集）。关键：语言筛选前置（机器语言识别）+ 时间分层（语料用长窗口、分析用反馈可得窗口）+ 单位明确（units vs actors）。原文 12,459（语料）→ 6,864（有反馈）→ 4,957 by 3,780 developers（主分析）。

### 变体 2: M3 LDA 参数选择效度论证链（双重标准）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> One of the most important researcher-imposed parameters in the [LDA topic] model is the number of [topics/dimensions]. We selected the number of [topics] to ensure that the [topics] are [clear], [well bounded], and [interpretable] ([citation]). Therefore, we first selected [topics] based on [local optimal coherence scores] (high coherence suggests clear and well-bounded topics) and then based on the [interpretability of the constructs] to the [intended audience], resulting in [K] [constructs]. Using [top keywords] that characterize each [topic], we gave [labels] to each of the [constructs] identified and present them in [Table/Figure] with [top keywords] characterizing them.
**与原骨架差异**: 任何文本构念测量都必须回答"为何选 K 个主题"——本骨架给出**双重效度标准**：统计标准（coherence score）+ 理论标准（interpretability to everyday user）。关键策略：(1) 显式承认"researcher-imposed parameters"并透明化；(2) 统计与理论标准并列（非仅靠其一）；(3) 用 top keywords + 标签表让读者可审计。这是 LDA 论文 M3 的黄金效度论证链。

### 变体 3: M3 文本构念 DV 操作化（概率向量 → 分析变量 → 交叉验证）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> The model assigned each [unit] a [probability vector] of [K] dimensions, and the weights in these [K] dimensions add up to one. This [probability vector] defines the [unit]'s position in the [multidimensional construct space]. The [highest-weight] topic represented its [core construct], defining its main [demand/competitive environment].
>
> Our [first] dependent variable is [DV_name], which captures the extent to which a [unit] focuses on its [core construct] relative to [peripheral constructs]. We calculated the measure by the [highest weight] in the [probability vector] ([citation]). Because [weights add up to one], the more weight allocated to the [core construct], the less is allocated to [peripheral constructs]. This measure is highly correlated ([0.9X]) with alternative operationalizations, such as the [Herfindahl index / concentration measure].
**与原骨架差异**: 从文本概率向量到 DV 的完整操作化三层：(1) 利用数学约束（weights add up to one → 核心与外周零和）；(2) DV = 最高权重（或阈值化权重）；(3) 与替代操作化（Herfindahl/concentration）交叉验证（高相关 → 构念效度）。关键：**效度交叉验证是文本构念 DV 的必要步骤**——不可只报一种操作化。

### 变体 4: M4 测量选择 because 链（多选一操作化的辩护）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> We calculated [IV_name] using an [entropy measure]. First, we [pooled all individual ratings] of existing [units] and calculated the [proportion of observations] at [category_1, category_2, ..., category_K] (*P_i*); second, we used these [K] proportions to calculate the [entropy measure] *E* at the [aggregate level], defined as [E = Σ P_i · ln(1/P_i)]. A high value of [IV_name] indicates [theoretical meaning].
>
> The correlation between our [entropy measure] and the [alternative index, e.g., Blau index = 1 − Σ P_i²] is very high ([0.9X]), but we use the [entropy measure] because it has better features in [aggregation and decomposition] ([citation]).
**与原骨架差异**: 任何在多个等价操作化中"多选一"的测量决策（entropy vs Blau、Jaccard vs cosine、Herfindahl vs concentration），都应回答"为何选 A 而非 B"。本骨架的黄金范式：(1) 给出所选指标的完整公式 + 理论含义；(2) 报告与替代指标的相关（高相关 → 量效度）；(3) 给出选择的 because（如 entropy 的 aggregation/decomposition 性质）。关键：**because 必须是统计性质（aggregation / decomposition / additivity），不能只是"习惯"**。

### 变体 5: M3 复合文本指标构建（多子维度 → 分别测量 → 平均合成）
**来源论文**: Cutolo & Ferriani 2024 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> [Construct] is a multidimensional property that reflects [theoretical facet 1], [theoretical facet 2], and [theoretical facet 3]. We capture each facet with a distinct textual indicator. [Facet 1] is measured by [tool/dictionary] as [score description]. [Facet 2] is measured by [tool/dictionary] as [score description]. [Facet 3] is measured by [tool/dictionary] as [score description]. We then compute [construct] as the [mean/sum/weighted average] of the three component scores. Higher values indicate [interpretation]. In Additional Analyses we also examine each component separately to assess whether the effect is driven by a particular facet.
**与原骨架差异**: 当理论构念本身是多维的（如 cohesion = lexical + causal + semantic），文本测量也应分层：(1) 每个子维度对应一个理论facet；(2) 每个facet用最适合的工具测量（可能跨 LIWC/TAACO/LSA）；(3) 用平均/加权合成复合指标；(4) 预告将做component disaggregation。关键：**不能把所有文本信号塞进一个黑箱字典**——要让审稿人看到构念-测量映射。

### 变体 6: M3 类别相对文本常规性操作化（LDA topic → category-average regression slope）
**来源论文**: Cutolo & Ferriani 2024 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> We conceptualize [construct] as the extent to which a [unit]'s [text] emphasizes topics that are also central in other [units]' [texts] within the same [category]. We first estimate an LDA topic model on the full corpus and identify [K] topics. For each [category], we compute the average weight of each topic across all other [units] in that [category]. We then regress each [unit]'s topic distribution on the corresponding [category]-average topic distribution. The slope of this regression serves as our measure of [construct]: a slope greater than [1/threshold] indicates that the [unit] overemphasizes conventional topics, while a slope below [1/threshold] indicates underemphasis.
**与原骨架差异**: "常规性/典型性"在平台/市场场景中必须是**相对概念**（相对同类别其他卖家）。本骨架给出从 LDA topic weights 到 category-relative 分数的完整步骤：(1) 全语料训练 LDA；(2) 计算每个 category 的 topic 平均分布；(3) 个体分布对 category 平均分布回归；(4) slope 作为常规性指标。关键：此方法把"与谁比较"（category peers）和操作化规则都透明化。

**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M10
**骨架**:
> To ensure that our automated measure captures the intended construct, we manually examined a random sample of [N] [texts]. Two coders independently rated each [text] on [dimension(s)] using a [scale]. Inter-rater reliability was [reliability statistic]. The automated scores correlated [correlation] with the manual ratings, suggesting that the measure adequately reflects [construct]. We also inspected cases at the extremes of the distribution to confirm that high [construct] texts indeed [description] and low [construct] texts [description].
**与原骨架差异**: 任何文本自动化测量都需要**人工校验环**来闭合效度链。本骨架给出最小可行版本：(1) 随机样本；(2) 独立编码 + 信度；(3) 自动分与人工评分相关；(4) 极端案例质性检查。关键：人工验证不必大规模，但必须在 Methods 中明确报告，否则审稿人会质疑 dictionary/tool 是否真的捕捉了理论构念。
