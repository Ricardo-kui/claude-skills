# 生存分析 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 填空段落骨架 → `生存分析`。

## 设计特征摘要

- **核心估计器**: AFT (Accelerated Failure Time) with Weibull distribution
- **标准误**: robust standard errors (clustered by firm/unit)
- **复发事件**: recurrent-event AFT models for multiple events per unit
- **分布选择**: 通过 BIC/AIC/LR test 比较备选分布
- **右删失**: explicit censoring justification required
- **跨论文复现率**: AFT+Weibull 骨架在 4/4 产品召回顶刊论文中完全复现

### 变体 6: Piecewise Exponential — 因 Cox 比例风险失败 + 理论时段分割 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Qiao, Hiatt & Sine 2026 (SMJ)
**验证状态**: 通过 (单篇高价值，参数生存估计器选择论证的稀缺范式)
**写入日期**: 2026-06-16
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> We transformed [DV: e.g., time-to-event / hazard of market entry] into a "hazard rate" and estimated it under the single-event survival analysis framework ([citation]). Although the Cox proportional hazard model might be more robust to different assumptions of distributions, our data analysis with the partial residual approach showed that the proportionality assumption did not hold ([Schoenfeld-type test]; [citation]). Following established recommendations for modeling social event data ([citations]), we employed [exponential piecewise / piecewise-constant] survival models that provide semi-parametric flexibility without imposing restrictive parametric assumptions about [monotonic hazard patterns]. This approach allows hazard rates to vary across temporal segments, accommodating [non-monotonic patterns characteristic of organizational events]. We divided our observation period into meaningful segments based on [theoretically motivated industry / institutional transitions]: the [period 1, e.g., 1919–1950] characterized by [period-1 conditions], and the [period 2, e.g., 1950–early 1980s] marked by [period-2 conditions]. This theoretically driven temporal division captures the [institutional shifts central to our hypotheses], providing interpretable baseline hazard estimates. We estimated separate baseline hazards for each period by including a [period dummy variable], and used [decadal dummies] as a robustness check.
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体 1–5（AFT+Weibull，分布由 BIC 或理论选定的"正向选择"）的根本区别——本变体是**由诊断失败驱动的负向选择**：(1) 先报告比例风险检验失败（Schoenfeld residual / partial residual），（2）再据此排除 Cox 并改用 piecewise exponential，让 baseline hazard 随**理论上有意义的时间段**分段变化（pre/post 一个产业制度转折点），而非假设单一单调风险。两层论证：**估计器选择由诊断驱动** + **分段选择由理论驱动**。诚实边界：分段断点必须有理论依据（产业制度变迁），不可事后搜最优断点；需用 decadal dummies 等替代分段做稳健性。适用于风险率随时间呈非单调、且存在明确制度断点的研究。

### 变体 7: 复发事件指数风险模型 + 连续递增时间 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Mayo, Ball & Mills 2022 POM
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> Because our objective was to examine how [predictor] is associated with the hazard of a future [event], we use a hazard model. Hazard models use two measures: a time measure and a failure measure. We measure time as elapsed [time_unit] from the first observed data point for the [unit] to each subsequent [event] for the [unit] ([citations]). We measure total time from this date to one of two types of events in our data: [failure_event] (failure = 1) and [competing_event] (failure = 0). Because many [units] in our sample experience more than one [event], we use a recurrent-event hazard model with clustered standard errors at the [unit] level ([citation]). We assume a [distribution] distribution, which assumes [distribution_property], making it one of the more parsimonious distributions in parametric hazard modeling ([citation]). To ensure that this modeling choice is not the underlying reason for our results, we demonstrate that results are robust to two other commonly used distributions, [alt1] and [alt2] (see [Appendix]).
>
> There are two main ways to handle the time measure in a recurrent-event hazard model. One way is to allow time to continue to grow after each event for a given [unit] ([citations]); that is, time to an event is always measured since the beginning of the data. The other approach is to reset time to zero after each failure ([citations]). We chose [choice] because longer panels like ours tend to have a large number of failures within a [unit] (in our data, [N] average [events] per [unit]) and may therefore be better suited toward a continuously incremented time measure due to shared variance that develops within a [unit] with multiple failures ([citations]). We evaluate the robustness of this methodological choice in [section].
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体1（AFT+Weibull）的关键区别：(1) 这是 Cox-type 风险模型（指数/Weibull/Gompertz 参数风险），不是加速失效时间模型；(2) 增加了 **competing event**（非失败事件）作为 failure=0 的处理，而非简单右删失；(3) 连续递增时间 vs gap-time 的二元选择加上了正式论证（长面板+多次失败→共享方差→连续时间更优）；(4) 分布选择在正文声明但在附录稳健性中验证（Weibull + Gompertz），而非像 Eilert2017 那样 BIC 比较前置。诚实边界：适用于企业层面多次事件的复发数据；单次事件研究不需要。

### 变体 8: 三分位离散化 IV（等样本量理由 + 同模型双向检验）
**来源论文**: Mayo, Ball & Mills 2022 POM
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [Variable] is the number of [time_unit] from [start_event] until the date of the [end_event]. This measure resets each time a new [reset_trigger] is appointed. Because we hypothesize two different mechanisms that dominate in [early] and [late] phases respectively, we divide this time into tertiles. Using tertiles provides a means of measuring diverging effects of [early] and [late] phases as separate indicator variables in the same regression model, with an equal number of observations in each category. We treat the middle tertile as the reference category. The first tertile is [Phase1_Label], spanning [range1], and the last tertile is [Phase3_Label], spanning [range3].
**与原骨架差异**: 适用于理论预测**阶段性机制**（早期一种、晚期另一种）而非连续线性的情况。三分位离散化的核心理由：(1) 等样本量——每个类别观测数相等；(2) 同模型双向检验——早期和晚期可同时作为哑变量放入同一回归，不需求助于分样本。备选方案：ln 变换假定单调递减；二次项假定 U 型——两者均无法捕捉"早期高位平台→晚期低位平台"的阶段性模式。诚实边界：需报告替代测量稳健性（ln 线性 + 四分位切割）以排除切割点选择 artifact。

### 变体 12: CEM 匹配程序（作为稳健性，非主识别）
**来源论文**: Mayo, Ball & Mills 2022 POM
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> We used a coarsened exact matching (CEM) analysis. A CEM analysis creates matches between observations based on observed variables and uses matched observations to compare an outcome of interest ([citation]). The CEM model coarsens the data by binning continuous or categorical variables into coarser ranges and then assigns binary indicator variables to each bin. These coarsened variables are used to match pairs between treated and untreated groups, where each pair shares the same set of coarsened binary indicators. In contrast to propensity score matching (PSM), where covariates generate a propensity score for receiving the treatment, CEM groups along specific covariates rather than overall propensity ([citation]).
>
> We follow the procedure for CEM as laid out by [Author] ([Year]). First, we coarsen the data with respect to [N] theoretically motivated continuous measures: [measure_1], [measure_2], [measure_3], and [measure_4]. We chose these measures as a relatively parsimonious set that are commonly used in literature predicting [DV] ([citations]) that also incorporate multiple levels of analysis including [level_1], [level_2], and [level_3]. We coarsen into [N] strata using these [M] covariates. We then conduct [K] separate CEM analyses: one with a treatment variable for [treatment_A] and one for [treatment_B]. [Treatment definitions with thresholds]. Finally, we use [software] to identify one-to-one pairs of "treated" and "control" observations that are in the same strata. This procedure generated [N_A] matched pairs in the [analysis_A], and [N_B] matched pairs in the [analysis_B].
**与原骨架差异**: 与 PSM 变体的关键区别——(1) 明确解释 CEM vs PSM 的差异（"groups along specific covariates rather than overall propensity"）；(2) 匹配协变量的选择有理论维度覆盖理由（firm-level + CEO-level + industry-level）；(3) 双向处理变量——同时匹配 early tenure 和 late tenure 两个"相反方向"的处理组，对应理论预测的双向效应。诚实边界：CEM 是 observed variable matching——不控制未观测选择。应仅用于稳健性而非主识别。

### 变体 13: CEM 作为主模型前置预处理 + L₁ 平衡报告 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Darby, Wowak, Ketchen, Connelly & Skowronski 2026 (Journal of Operations Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M8/M7
**骨架**:
> While [estimator] models account for the fact that the likelihood of an event changes with the passage of time, they may not account for characteristics that influence [treatment] and [DV]. To address concerns related to selection on observable characteristics, we first processed our data using CEM ([citation]). The underlying goal of CEM is to achieve better balance between treatment and control observations by generating strata based on a set of matching covariates ([citation]). We used a [median split] of the focal variable for the treatment. The treatment group consists of observations with [focal_var] at or above the median of [value], whereas the control group consists of observations with below-median [focal_var].
>
> We selected [covariate_name] as the primary matching covariate to address [characteristics] that may influence [treatment] as well as [outcome]. The underlying rationale is that [theoretical_reason]. For the primary analysis, the aim was to sufficiently account for heterogeneity while also ensuring sufficient sample size. As described in robustness checks, we iteratively examined [N] additional vectors of matching covariates and obtained consistent results. The primary matching covariate was coarsened using the [automated/sturges/scott] algorithm. This process yielded [N] matched strata containing [N_treated] treated and [N_control] control observations for a total of [N_total]. Weights capturing the level of similarity within each stratum were used to weight the [estimator] models. An imbalance measure, L₁, decreased from [before] to [after], indicating improved covariate balance.
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体12（Mayo CEM as robustness）的关键区别——(1) CEM 在这里是**主识别策略的一部分**（在估计前预处理数据），而非事后稳健性检验；(2) 使用中位数分割而非三分位为处理阈值；(3) 单协变量精简匹配（"parsimonious set to avoid large sample loss"）并迭代增加协变量的方式；(4) 正式报告 L₁ 不平衡统计量（before/after）+ 单变量均值差。适用于处理组/控制组可比性是核心识别挑战的研究。
