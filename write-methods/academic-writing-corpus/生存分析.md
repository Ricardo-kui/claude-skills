# 生存分析 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 填空段落骨架 → `生存分析`。

## 设计特征摘要

- **核心估计器**: AFT (Accelerated Failure Time) with Weibull distribution
- **标准误**: robust standard errors (clustered by firm/unit)
- **复发事件**: recurrent-event AFT models for multiple events per unit
- **分布选择**: 通过 BIC/AIC/LR test 比较备选分布
- **右删失**: explicit censoring justification required
- **跨论文复现率**: AFT+Weibull 骨架在 4/4 产品召回顶刊论文中完全复现

### 变体 5: CPH稳健性对比
**来源论文**: Darby2023 MSOM
**验证状态**: 可选变体 (1/4，预防审稿人质疑)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-20
**槽位**: M7 (robustness)
**骨架**:
> Cox proportional hazard (CPH) models are also commonly used to examine the hazard of a failure and benefit from requiring no specification of the underlying distribution ([citation]). In robustness checks, we present consistent support for [Hypotheses] using CPH models as well as generalized linear regression models. Although survival analysis models such as AFT and CPH may be preferred to linear regression when there are time-varying covariates ([citation]), as is the case in our study, generalized linear models can also be used to examine the time to failure for duration data. Thus, to enhance confidence that our modeling approach does not substantively influence our conclusions, we replicated our survival analysis models using [alternative_estimator] and [alternative_DV_form].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是**防御性写作**——在主模型 M7 段落中即预告稳健性检验使用了 CPH，降低审稿人对分布选择的质疑。关键策略：(1) 承认CPH存在但说明为什么不选为主模型（"address somewhat different questions"）；(2) 预告CPH结果与主模型一致；(3) 增加GLM作为第二层稳健性。适用于任何使用参数生存模型的研究。

### 变体 6: Piecewise Exponential — 因 Cox 比例风险失败 + 理论时段分割 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Qiao, Hiatt & Sine 2026 (SMJ)
**验证状态**: 通过 (单篇高价值，参数生存估计器选择论证的稀缺范式)
**写入日期**: 2026-06-16
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> We transformed [DV: e.g., time-to-event / hazard of market entry] into a "hazard rate" and estimated it under the single-event survival analysis framework ([citation]). Although the Cox proportional hazard model might be more robust to different assumptions of distributions, our data analysis with the partial residual approach showed that the proportionality assumption did not hold ([Schoenfeld-type test]; [citation]). Following established recommendations for modeling social event data ([citations]), we employed [exponential piecewise / piecewise-constant] survival models that provide semi-parametric flexibility without imposing restrictive parametric assumptions about [monotonic hazard patterns]. This approach allows hazard rates to vary across temporal segments, accommodating [non-monotonic patterns characteristic of organizational events]. We divided our observation period into meaningful segments based on [theoretically motivated industry / institutional transitions]: the [period 1, e.g., 1919–1950] characterized by [period-1 conditions], and the [period 2, e.g., 1950–early 1980s] marked by [period-2 conditions]. This theoretically driven temporal division captures the [institutional shifts central to our hypotheses], providing interpretable baseline hazard estimates. We estimated separate baseline hazards for each period by including a [period dummy variable], and used [decadal dummies] as a robustness check.
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体 1–5（AFT+Weibull，分布由 BIC 或理论选定的"正向选择"）的根本区别——本变体是**由诊断失败驱动的负向选择**：(1) 先报告比例风险检验失败（Schoenfeld residual / partial residual），（2）再据此排除 Cox 并改用 piecewise exponential，让 baseline hazard 随**理论上有意义的时间段**分段变化（pre/post 一个产业制度转折点），而非假设单一单调风险。两层论证：**估计器选择由诊断驱动** + **分段选择由理论驱动**。诚实边界：分段断点必须有理论依据（产业制度变迁），不可事后搜最优断点；需用 decadal dummies 等替代分段做稳健性。适用于风险率随时间呈非单调、且存在明确制度断点的研究。
