# 生存分析 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 填空段落骨架 → `生存分析`。

## 设计特征摘要

- **核心估计器**: AFT (Accelerated Failure Time) with Weibull distribution
- **标准误**: robust standard errors (clustered by firm/unit)
- **复发事件**: recurrent-event AFT models for multiple events per unit
- **分布选择**: 通过 BIC/AIC/LR test 比较备选分布
- **右删失**: explicit censoring justification required
- **跨论文复现率**: AFT+Weibull 骨架在 4/4 产品召回顶刊论文中完全复现

### 变体 1: AFT + Weibull 规格 (4/4 复现)

**验证状态**: 通过

**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> Our theorizing centers on [DV], which varies because [justification_for_variation] ([citation]). We thus followed recent studies and conducted our analysis at the [level] ([citations]). Specifically, we relied on [estimator], which are used to examine [estimator_purpose]—that is, [specific_application] ([citation]). Some [units] experienced [special_structure], so we used [specialized_estimator] with [SE_type] clustered by [cluster_level] and a [distribution] ([citations]).
**与原骨架差异**: 这是生存分析设计的标准引入结构。关键要素：(1) DV变异的理由；(2) 分析层级声明；(3) 估计器选择及文献背书；(4) 特殊结构处理(复发事件/右删失)；(5) 分布与标准误声明。
**4/4复现细节**: 四篇产品召回论文使用AFT+Weibull的措辞几乎逐句相同——"frequently used to examine how predictor variables that change across time influence the time to an event"，"one of the more commonly used and parsimonious distributional forms"。诚实边界：仅 Eilert2017 报告了 BIC 比较过程；Darby2023/Darby2025 未比较备选分布。

### 变体 2: 分布选择论证 (BIC 比较)
**来源论文**: Eilert2017 JM
**验证状态**: 通过 (1/4 但高价值)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-19
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> Not much theory or empirical evidence is available to predict the shape of [process]. Therefore, to overcome misspecification bias, we compare several distributions. These distributions are [type] because [reason]. In particular, we estimate [model] incorporating [distribution_1], [distribution_2], [distribution_3], and [distribution_4]. [Table_reference] reports the model fit for these distributions. As [table_reference] shows, the [winning_distribution] offers the best fit based on the [fit_statistic] statistic. Accordingly, we use a [winning_distribution] distribution for [process].
**与原骨架差异**: 大多数生存分析论文(包括 Darby2026/Darby2025/Darby2023)**缺失**分布选择论证，直接声明使用Weibull。Eilert2017提供了完整的BIC比较模板。诚实边界：若理论明确支持某分布(如指数分布对应恒定风险率)，可直接引用理论，无需比较。

### 变体 5: CPH稳健性对比
**来源论文**: Darby2023 MSOM
**验证状态**: 可选变体 (1/4，预防审稿人质疑)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-20
**槽位**: M7 (robustness)
**骨架**:
> Cox proportional hazard (CPH) models are also commonly used to examine the hazard of a failure and benefit from requiring no specification of the underlying distribution ([citation]). In robustness checks, we present consistent support for [Hypotheses] using CPH models as well as generalized linear regression models. Although survival analysis models such as AFT and CPH may be preferred to linear regression when there are time-varying covariates ([citation]), as is the case in our study, generalized linear models can also be used to examine the time to failure for duration data. Thus, to enhance confidence that our modeling approach does not substantively influence our conclusions, we replicated our survival analysis models using [alternative_estimator] and [alternative_DV_form].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是**防御性写作**——在主模型 M7 段落中即预告稳健性检验使用了 CPH，降低审稿人对分布选择的质疑。关键策略：(1) 承认CPH存在但说明为什么不选为主模型（"address somewhat different questions"）；(2) 预告CPH结果与主模型一致；(3) 增加GLM作为第二层稳健性。适用于任何使用参数生存模型的研究。
