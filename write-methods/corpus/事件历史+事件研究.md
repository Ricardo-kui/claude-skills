## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `事件历史+事件研究` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

- **事件研究法**: Fama-French 三因子/四因子模型估计异常收益(AR/CAR)
- **估计窗口**: 通常 [-2, 2] 或 [-1, 1] around announcement
- **预期收益模型**: 市场模型 / Fama-French / CAPM
- **适用场景**: 测量股票市场对公司事件(召回、公告、丑闻等)的反应
- **跨论文复现率**: 事件研究法引入在 2/4 产品召回论文中复现 (仅 Eilert2017 和 Darby2023 包含事件研究)
- **概念边界**: 本文件同时容纳资本市场 event study 与围绕宏观事件的纵向 discontinuous growth model；二者共享“事件定时”叙事，但估计量、识别目标与诊断不可互换。

### 变体 3: 事件研究信息泄露检验
**来源论文**: Darby2023 MSOM
**原始句锚点**: "One advantage of an event study is that it is premised on event exogeneity. In our case, a recall announcement is an exogenous shock to the stock market, so our event study comparing expected market returns against actual market returns can generally presume upon exogeneity."
**验证状态**: VERIFIED (1/4，事件研究内生性处理的推荐做法)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-20
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> One advantage of an event study is that it is premised on event exogeneity. In our case, [event] is an exogenous shock to [outcome], so our event study comparing [expected] against [actual] can generally presume upon exogeneity. However, it is possible that [event] might not be exogenous if [threat_scenario]. To examine this concern, we leveraged the richness of our [data_type] to conduct an additional event study that uses [alternative_date] in place of [primary_date] as the date of the event. [Table_reference] provides the [test_statistics] to assess the significance of the CARs. The results indicate that the CARs are not statistically significantly different from zero in any time window for any [subgroup]. This suggests that there is no significant response from [outcome] surrounding [alternative_date], which helps to build additional confidence in the validity of our event study findings and the exogeneity of the [primary_date].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是**事件研究内生性处理的经典模式**：(1) 先声明事件外生性优势；(2) 再诚实承认潜在威胁（信息泄露）；(3) 用替代日期构造安慰剂型检验；(4) null result=exogeneity supported. Darby2023用defect awareness date替换recall initiation date，发现CAR不显著，证明市场反应确实是针对"召回公告"而非"缺陷知晓"。此骨架可迁移至任何使用事件研究法的研究。

### 变体 6: M3 CAR 完整公式 + 窄窗口保守性辩护 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Gulati, Lavie & Singh 2009 (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: "We calculated abnormal returns using residual analysis of the market model (Fama et al., 1969). The choice of a narrow two-day event window demonstrates a conservative approach that excludes unrelated events occurring prior to or following the relevant announcement."
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> We calculated abnormal returns using residual analysis of the market model ([citation]). Based on the [event] announcement, the event date was set at t = 0. We estimated the market model for the period t = [[estimation_start], [estimation_end]]: r_{it} = α_i + β_i r_{mt} + ε_{it}, where r_{it} is the common stock return of firm i on day t, r_{mt} is the corresponding daily market return on the [index], α_i and β_i are firm-specific parameters, and ε_{it} is the error term. The resulting estimates were used for predicting the daily returns for firm i over the [N]-day event window [[start], [end]]: r̂_{it} = α̂_i + β̂_i r_{mt}. We calculated the daily firm-level abnormal returns: ε̂_{it} = r_{it} − r̂_{it}, and then computed the cumulative abnormal return of firm i during the event window: CAR_i = Σ_t ε̂_{it}, where t ranges from [start] to [end].
>
> The choice of a narrow [N]-day event window demonstrates a conservative approach that excludes unrelated events occurring prior to or following the relevant announcement. According to [citation], researchers should capture the change in stock price immediately following the [event] announcement. Since most announcements are actually made prior to the close of the previous trading day, the optimal window is set at [[-1,0]]. Subsequent research has demonstrated that the [N]-day window is much more effective in capturing market responses than longer windows ([citations]).
**与原骨架差异**: 与 Pfarrer（AMJ 版）的关键区别——(1) Gulati 将**完整公式**嵌入 Methods（市场模型 + 预测 + 异常 + CAR 累加——四个方程），而非像 Pfarrer 那样仅声明；(2) 窗口选择有两层辩护——先引用方法论文献（Woolridge & Snow 1990→窄窗口优越性），再引用经验文献（Anand & Khanna 2000→实证支持）；(3) 不需要软件声明——2009 年的 SMJ 标准。适用于任何将 CAR 作为 DV 的回归设计。

**诚实边界**: 若主要假设预测的是价格反应（如"announcement leads to positive abnormal returns"），CAR 应是主 DV，ATV 和 sentiment 应定位为补充证据。不能因 CAR 不显著而将 ATV/sentiment 上升为主证据（除非事前如此假设）。

### 变体 11: M7 事件中心分段时间的 discontinuous growth model（DGCM）
**来源论文**: Reinwald et al. (2026, *Organization Science*)
**原始句锚点**: "We modeled the negative interpersonal interaction trajectory using a set of three growth variables that allowed us to disentangle the trajectory before the election (time variable), the transition directly following the election (election variable), and the trajectory following the election (postelection trend variable) (Bliese and Lang 2016). First, the time variable captures the baseline rate of change in interpersonal interactions. Second, the election variable captures the vertical transition effect between the election day (November 3) and the next workday (November 4). We coded this variable as zero for all pre-election days and the election day and one for all postelection days. Thus, the election variable measures how the level of negative interpersonal interactions changed with the election. Third, the postelection variable captures the relative trend after the election and takes values of zero up to the first day after the election and then increases by increments of one with each postelection day."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-02
**槽位**: M7/M8
**骨架**:
> We aligned repeated observations to [event date] and represented time with three conceptually distinct variables: (1) a preevent linear trend, coded [scheme], to assess whether the focal relationship was already changing before the event; (2) an event-transition indicator, coded [0 before / 1 after], to capture the discontinuous shift at the event; and (3) a postevent trend, coded [0 before and at transition, then elapsed units after], to assess subsequent change. We interacted each time component with [focal difference] and estimated random coefficients for the temporal terms. Because [Study N] includes only one preevent observation, the preevent trend is perfectly collinear with the transition term and is omitted; that design can estimate the transition and postevent trajectory but cannot diagnose a pretrend.

**报告配套**:
1. 给出每个时间变量在实际观测日的编码表，避免只写“piecewise model”。
2. 先报告随机时间斜率相对固定斜率的拟合比较，再解释固定效应。
3. 持续性需要同时报告 `difference × posttrend` 与观察窗末端的条件差异；前者不显著不能单独证明持续。

**与资本市场事件研究/DiD 的区别**:
- 不是用估计窗口计算 AR/CAR，不调用有效市场假说。
- 若所有人共同经历同一宏观事件，event transition 的主效应与日历时间重合；识别重点是事件时点前后 focal difference 的斜率/水平差异，而非平均处理效应。
- 只有一个 preevent 观测时不能检验平行趋势或预趋势；增加随机斜率也不能补回缺失的前期时间信息。

**诚实边界**: 宏观事件并未随机发生，同期新闻与社会冲击仍可能解释时间变化；使用 “event-centered association/pattern” 而非自动使用 causal effect。将 posttrend 的不显著系数解释为“未检测到衰减”，除非事先定义等效界限并完成等效性检验。

**适用**: 事件时点信息扩散速度不确定（公告前泄漏可能+渐进消化可能并存）的 CAR 研究；审稿环境对窗口选择敏感、需要展示结论窗口无关性的场景

**禁忌**: 多窗是稳健性策略不是辩护替代品——主窗仍需一句选择理由（本篇由下一 variant 的日期选择论证承担）；长窗混杂事件更多，只作辅助证据不可作主检验

**验证状态**: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override (user 2026-08-28: 单源足矣; paper_count=1)



### 变体 20: M3 证据驱动短主窗选择 + 长窗 thoroughness 交棒（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: m3_event_window_tradeoff
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
status: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）
-->

**模板**:
> "Relatively long [windows] allow for the possibility of [gradual diffusion of information]. However, research has generally shown that [the outcome] adjusts very quickly to [the focal event] ([citations]). Moreover, longer [windows] increase the likelihood of contamination from extraneous [events] ([citations]). Thus, we estimated [outcomes] over [short window] and [medium window]. These [windows] are commonly used in the [field] literature. In the interest of thoroughness, we also ran analyses using a [long window], and these results are presented separately below."

**原文锚定** (Zajac & Westphal 2004, ASR, Method §Dependent Variables；同段删节已标注):
> "However, research has generally shown that stock prices adjust very quickly to the announcement of significant corporate events ... Moreover, longer event periods increase the likelihood of contamination from extraneous organizational events during the time period."

**关键特征**:
- 先给长窗应得的理由（gradual diffusion），再用两记证据反打：调整速度文献（"within 15 minutes"）证明无需长窗 + 长窗污染风险证明长窗有害——然后 "Thus" 收束到短窗，两难被证据裁决而非搁置
- 与变体 16 的分工：变体 16 用于扩散速度真不确定的场景（多窗三角化+窗口无关性声明）；本变体用于文献已有调整速度证据的场景——主窗有正向选择论证，多窗仍是稳健性配置
- 长窗以 "In the interest of thoroughness" 引入并 "presented separately below"——把长窗结果交棒给 Results 的独立呈现，Methods 不预支其结论
- "commonly used in the event study literature" 惯例锚定降低非常规操作疑虑

**适用**: 事件后市场/受众反应速度有文献证据的 CAR/事件研究；审稿人对窗口选择敏感但证据明确支持短窗的场景

**禁忌**: 短窗论证必须有速度证据或污染证据支撑，不能只引惯例（惯例句是配菜不是主菜）；"presented separately below" 是硬承诺——长窗结果必须在 Results 兑现；文献调整速度证据不成立时改走变体 16 的三角化路线

**验证状态**: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）

### 变体 17：污染效应规避型事件日选择（westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management 型）

**模板**:
> "Following [prior study], we observed [returns] from [the chosen event date], or the date on which [the announcement] was sent to [the audience]. While some researchers have observed returns from [the alternative date] ([citations]), [author] ([year]) found evidence for [a documented contaminating effect] that could contaminate results of an event study: for [a random sample of units], he found [a spurious reaction] over the period following [the alternative date], but no such effect for [the chosen date]. Moreover, [the chosen date] is the most commonly chosen date for this kind of study ([citation]: [page]). Nevertheless, we also conducted separate analyses using [the alternative date], or the date on which [the document] was registered as received by [the authority] ([citation]). The results were substantively unchanged from the findings presented below."

**来源**: westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management (ASQ), Method §Dependent Variables / Stock Market Reaction（P3）

**原文锚定**:
> "Brickley (1986) found evidence for an annual meeting effect that could contaminate results of an event study: for a random sample of firms, he found a positive stock market reaction over the two-day period following the annual meeting, but no such effect for the proxy mailing date."

**关键特征**:
- 事件日选择不是惯例声明而是证据论证：用文献中已文档化的污染效应（年会效应）+ 其在备择日期出现、在本日期不出现的证据结构，反向证明所选日期的洁净性
- "the most commonly chosen date for this kind of study ([citation]: 346)" 的惯例声明带页码——惯例与证据双轨辩护，惯例降低审稿人的非常规操作疑虑
- "Nevertheless, we also conducted separate analyses using..." 的让步式稳健性：即便如此仍用备择日期重跑——三段式（选择理由→惯例→备择日期稳健）完整闭环

**适用**: 事件日在多个候选日期（公告日/登记日/生效日）之间需要选择、且备择日期存在已文档化污染效应的市场反应研究

**禁忌**: 污染效应必须有文献证据（随机样本证据最有力），不能凭空宣称备择日期"可能有污染"；备择日期稳健性若不显著一致，需报告并解释而非隐藏

**验证状态**: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override (user 2026-08-28: 单源足矣; paper_count=1)



### 变体 19: M7 实现窗→样本时钟链 + 短窗双轨辩护（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: m7_implementation_window_clock_chain
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
status: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）
-->

**模板**:
> "[Focal predictors] were coded as [state] if [condition held] within [the implementation window]. Given this [measurement window], the first [window length] of the sample are excluded from the regression analysis. Thus, the sample for this analysis includes all [focal events] adopted from [year1] through [year2] (N = [N]). To ensure that our results are not sensitive to this research design, we ran analyses with different [window lengths], and the results were consistent with those we report below. A relatively short [window] seems justified, given that [actors] typically have no obvious economic reason for [delaying the outcome], and from an empirical standpoint, [quantified anchor]."

**原文锚定** (Zajac & Westphal 2004, ASR, Method §Analysis；跨段拼接已标注):
> "Given this implementation window, the first three years of the sample are excluded from the regression analysis. ... A relatively short implementation window seems justified, given that firms typically have no obvious economic reason for delaying implementation."

**关键特征**:
- 测量定义不是无代价的：实现窗（3 年）决定了回归样本起点（1983 起，N=778）——把"测量窗→数据损失→最终 N"显式连成时钟链，而非在变量节与样本节各说各话
- 窗口稳健性先行承诺："In separate models we measured plan implementation over different time periods... results substantively unchanged"——在操作化处预告，在 Analysis 处兑现
- 短窗辩护双轨制：理论轨（"firms typically have no obvious economic reason for delaying implementation"——延迟本身无预期逻辑）+ 量化轨（"the portion of plans that are not implemented increases by less than 10 percent when implementation is measured over a one-year window"）——比单纯"惯例如此"强两级

**适用**: 预测变量需按"事件后 [t] 年内是否发生 [行为]"分类的扩散/实施研究——凡测量窗会吃掉样本早期年份的设计

**禁忌**: 时钟链必须显式（"Given this implementation window, the first [x] years are excluded"）——隐式换样本期会让读者对不上 N；量化轨数字必须是真实计算，不可用模糊量词替代

**验证状态**: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）

### 变体 21: M6 事件窗混杂控制清单 + 虚拟变量收口（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: m6_event_confound_control_block
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
status: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）
-->

**模板**:
> "We controlled for [domain A] and [domain B] characteristics that might influence [the focal outcome]. The [domain A] variables include [variable list]. The [domain B] variables included [variable list]. We controlled for [major announcements and publicized incidents] that occurred during the [observation window], including the possible confounding [events] listed by [published checklist source]. Finally, we included dummy variables for [time period] and [industry/category]."

**原文锚定** (Zajac & Westphal 2004, ASR, Method §Independent Variables):
> "We controlled for major policy announcements and other publicized incidents that occurred during the event period (other events), including the possible confounding events listed by McWilliams and Siegel (1997:640)."

**关键特征**:
- 控制变量按域分组（financial / governance / confounds / 时间-行业）而非逐个罗列——一段完成全套控制布局
- 事件窗污染有专门控制：以方法论文献的混杂事件清单（带页码引用）为控制变量池的权威锚——回应的是事件研究综述中标准化的审稿质疑（"你控制混杂了吗"）
- 虚拟变量收口句 "coefficients for these dummy variables are not reported and are available from the authors"——高维控制不占表格的惯例声明

**诚实边界**: 本变体的域内控制变量多为平铺（"might influence the market's reaction"一层 because）——顶刊后续标准已要求逐变量 because；模仿时应至少为核心控制变量补 because 从句，平铺仅可接受于标准惯例控制（规模、行业、年份）

**适用**: 事件研究/市场反应回归的控制变量段布局；与变体 13 的进入前筛选互补（筛观测 vs 控制回归内变异）

**验证状态**: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）

### 变体 22: M7 多期 CAR 汇归估计栈（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: m7_pooled_car_regression_stack
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
status: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）
-->

**模板**:
> "[Event studies] often analyze the effects of [predictors] on [outcomes] using subgroup analyses. Several authors have advocated the use of multiple regression analysis instead, in order to control for possible third variables. Given that our data set includes [outcomes] from different time periods, autocorrelation could occur in the data. We use the [AR(1) transformation] to correct for first-order autocorrelation. In separate analyses we use [alternative estimator], and the results are very similar. Aside from [the correction], we address the potential for spuriousness resulting from [time trends] in the data in two ways: (1) in separate models we control for [higher-order dependence] ... and (2) we control for [time effects] with [robust standard errors clustered by period] given evidence that [the institutional context shifted in period X]."

**原文锚定** (Zajac & Westphal 2004, ASR, Method §Analysis；同段删节已标注):
> "We use the Cochrane-Orcutt transformation to correct for first-order autocorrelation. ... we control for time effects with robust standard errors clustered by year or by longer time periods ... given evidence that the agency logic emerged in the mid-1980s."

**关键特征**:
- 估计器升级有文献锚：先陈述领域惯例（subgroup analyses）再引方法论文献倡导回归（McWilliams & Siegel）——升级是被倡导的，不是自选的
- 汇归的特有风险点对点处理："excess returns from different time periods → autocorrelation could occur"——多期混合数据的诊断风险由数据结构本身推出，非通用清单
- 时间趋势虚假性双路防御编号化（"(1)... (2)..."）：高阶自相关控制 + 时期聚类稳健误，且聚类的粗细由理论时点锚定（"the agency logic emerged in the mid-1980s"）——聚类层级选择本身有 because
- 每步校正配一句替代估计器稳健性（Prais-Winsten "results are very similar"）

**适用**: 把多期事件窗 CAR 汇入单一回归（横截面为事件、时间为混合维度）的设计；任何"混合期数据+时间趋势 rival"的汇归场景

**禁忌**: AR(1) 校正与聚类是两道独立防线（残差自相关 vs 时期共同冲击），不可互相替代声明；(1)(2) 防御必须各自报结果方向（"substantively unchanged"），只列不报等于没做

**验证状态**: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）

### 变体 23: M8 Selection 最小预告 + 结果等价收口（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: m8_selection_model_preview
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
status: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）
-->

**模板**:
> "Finally, to ensure that [sample selection biases] would not affect our results, we estimate separate [selection models], in which one equation estimates the likelihood of [entering the sample / experiencing the selection event], and parameters from that model are included in a second equation that estimates [the focal outcome] ([citation]). The results are similar, and the hypothesized effects are unchanged, suggesting that selection biases are not affecting our results."

**原文锚定** (Zajac & Westphal 2004, ASR, Method §Analysis):
> "to ensure that sample selection biases would not affect our results, we estimate separate Heckman selection models, in which one equation estimates the likelihood of repurchase plan adoption, and parameters from that model are included in a second equation that estimates excess returns from adoption."

**关键特征**:
- Methods 内一句话预告完整识别栈（对应 registry 反模式「多阶段识别栈仅出现在 Results」的正向示范）：两方程结构（选择方程→结果方程）+ 参数流向（"parameters from that model are included in a second equation"）一句讲清
- "to ensure that [bias] would not affect our results" 开头即声明该分析的防御角色——selection 校正定位为排除 rival 而非主识别手段
- 结果等价收口三连（"The results are similar, and the hypothesized effects are unchanged, suggesting that..."）——防御性稳健性报告的最短完整形态

**诚实边界**: 本变体未报告排除性约束（exclusion restrictions）——当 selection 校正承担识别职能（λ 有实质解释、用于主表）时，须升级为 Chen et al. 2009 型（无先验+主表排除约束+λ=0 检验）；"results are similar" 若不成立，须报告差异而非静默

**适用**: selection 威胁存在但非设计核心（样本进入由可建模的采用/披露行为决定）的档案研究；识别栈导航性收尾段

**验证状态**: EMERGING — 单源（正常评级）

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|---|---|---|
| **威胁筛选有 because、无逐步 N（M2）** | 混杂/泄漏/分类模糊剔除逻辑齐全，但只报最终 N，无逐步剔除量 | 每步报告进入/剔除/剩余 N；审稿人须能审计数据损失（Chen et al. 2009: 描述三类剔除后直接 "final sample of 153"） |
| **多阶段识别栈仅出现在 Results（M7）** | Heckman/中介规格只在 Results 展开，Methods 未预告 AR→截面→中介→自选择校正 | Methods 导航至少一句预告完整估计栈，避免读者以为设计止于 AR 均值/组间检验 |

## 诚实边界（设计级）

- 零事故=proactive 不适用于慢性/潜伏危害；分类边界必须与样本预筛选一致。
- 泄漏剔除兼 AE ruling 只排除「媒体事先报道」路径，不排除私下信息或知情交易。
- 监管四理由 M1 与威胁三联 M2 提高内部效度，可能降低外部代表——须在 limitation 声明。
