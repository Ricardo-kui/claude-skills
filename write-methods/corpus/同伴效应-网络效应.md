## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `同伴效应-网络效应` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

同伴效应/网络效应设计指核心解释变量或边界条件来自 actor 之间的关系结构（dyadic ties、network position、structural embeddedness）。这类设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**网络变量的操作化透明化**：如何定义关系、如何构建网络矩阵、如何计算 centrality/embeddedness/density，以及如何处理网络内生性（reflection problem / common shock）。Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (SMJ) 是本设计类型的典型范文：从 alliance 矩阵到 degree centrality 差值、common ties 计数、ego network density 的完整计算链。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M4 网络变量操作化（关系定义、矩阵构建、指标计算）、M6 网络相关控制变量、M8 网络内生性讨论。常见薄弱点：未讨论反射性问题（Manski reflection problem）、未报告网络变量描述性统计、将网络位置视为外生而未做 falsification。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M4 相对中心性 — degree centrality 差值 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: Relative centrality captures the relative positional embeddedness of two firms. Relative centrality is calculated as the firm’s degree centrality minus its partner’s degree centrality.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [Variable] captures the relative positional embeddedness of two [actors]. We first constructed yearly [relationship] matrices within [industry] from [year_start] to [year_end], using a [N]-year moving window. Altogether, we identified [N] pairs of [relationships], including those started between [earlier_start] and [earlier_end]. We constructed a symmetric (non-directional) matrix of these [actors] for each year using [software] ([citation]), then calculated the [centrality type] of each [actor] within the above [network] matrices. [Variable] is calculated as [actor]'s [centrality] minus [partner]'s [centrality].
**与原骨架差异**: 网络研究中"相对"权力/地位的常用操作化。关键要素：(1) 时间窗口明确（5 年 moving window）；(2) 对称矩阵构建说明；(3) 软件声明（Ucinet 6）；(4) 差值计算方向（focal actor − partner）。可替换为 betweenness / closeness centrality，但 dyad-level 差值逻辑不变。
**诚实边界**: 中心性差异可能内生于共同冲击或双方历史选择，应在 M8 讨论反射性问题并补充至少一项 falsification test。

### 变体 2: M4 结构嵌入性 — common ties 计数 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: Common ties capture the structural embeddedness between the firm and its partner. Following prior studies, we measured this variable by counting the number of common third-party partners that the firm and its partner shared during the previous 5 years (Gulati & Gargiulo, 1999; Polidoro et al., 2011).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [Variable] captures the structural embeddedness between [actor] and [partner]. Following prior studies, we measured this variable by counting the number of common third-party [actors] that [actor] and [partner] shared during the previous [N] years ([citation_1]; [citation_2]).
**与原骨架差异**: 结构嵌入性的标准测量。关键：时间窗口（previous N years）和共同第三方计数。与闭合三元组（closed triad）概念一致。
**诚实边界**: common ties 可能反映行业共同伙伴或地域聚类，需控制行业/地域固定效应或做 placebo test。

### 变体 3: M6 ego network density — two-mode incidence transformation (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: This ego network includes the two firms within the alliance as well as all other firms that have at least one alliance with at least one of the two firms. We first transformed the firm-by-firm alliance network into an incidence network (i.e., the output is a two-mode firm-by-alliance network) using the command “transform | graph theoretic | incidence” in Ucinet 6.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M6
**骨架**:
> We controlled for the density of the ego network for each [dyad]. This ego network includes the two [actors] within the [relationship] as well as all other [actors] that have at least one [relationship] with at least one of the two [actors]. We first transformed the [actor]-by-[actor] [relationship] network into an incidence network (i.e., a two-mode [actor]-by-[relationship] network) using the command "[command]" in [software]. We then transformed the two-mode incidence network into a one-mode [relationship]-by-[relationship] square network by using the command "[command]" and selecting [unit] as the unit of interest. Finally, we calculated ego network density within this new network for each [relationship] in our sample.
**与原骨架差异**: ego network density 的高级操作化，涉及 two-mode incidence transformation。关键：明确命令、软件、转换逻辑。Cui et al. 用 alliance-by-alliance 网络密度控制 dyad 层面的网络闭合效应。
**诚实边界**: two-mode transformation 可能因 alliance 数量多而稀疏，需报告 density 分布并讨论其作为控制变量（非核心解释变量）的角色。

### 变体 4: M3/M4 status as global network centrality — Bonacich beta centrality (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pollock, Lee, Jin & Lashley (2015, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: We used available data on all the VCs in the TBO database, not just our sample, to calculate this measure. We operationalized status using Bonacich’s (1987) beta centrality, a measure of global centrality that considers the focal actor’s centrality, its connected actors’ centralities, their connected actors’ centralities, and so on (Bonacich, 1987).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-30
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> "Research has established that a good proxy for [actor] status is centrality in [relationship] networks ([citations]). We used available data on all the [actors] in the [database], not just our sample, to calculate this measure. For each [actor] we constructed one-year adjacency matrices. To smooth patterns of affiliation ([citation]), each annual adjacency matrix included [relationship] networks based on [N]-year moving periods beginning with the [actor]'s [founding/year-zero] year. For [actors] less than [N] years [old] we used all available data. We operationalized status using [Bonacich's (1987) beta centrality], a measure of global centrality that considers the focal actor's centrality, its connected actors' centralities, their connected actors' centralities, and so on. When beta is set to zero, network centrality is akin to degree centrality, focusing only on the local structure. The larger the value of beta, the more the centrality measure reflects the global structure. In our analysis, we set beta to [X] percent of the reciprocal of the largest eigenvalue. We also standardized our status measure so that its effect size could be compared with the effects of [other construct]. We used [software] to calculate our status measure."
**与原骨架差异**: 区别于变体1 的 degree centrality（仅局部结构），Bonacich beta centrality 是**全局/特征向量型中心性**——递归地把"联系人的中心性"纳入权重，β 控制局部→全局的光谱（β=0 退化为 degree；β 越大越反映全局结构），设为最大特征值倒数的 X%。三个配套手法：(1) 用**全数据库**（非仅样本）计算以保证网络完整性；(2) **平滑**——年度邻接矩阵用 N 年移动窗口；(3) **标准化**以与另一构念（reputation）的效应大小可比（为跨方程系数比较铺路）。适用于 status / prestige / rank 等由 affiliation 模式推导的层级构念。
**诚实边界**: 单一指标 centrality 可能含测量误差且无法分解多维度（pollock2015 自承 limitation）；β 的选择（最大特征值倒数的百分比）需声明具体值并做敏感性检验。


### 变体 5: M4 加权平均操作化的反例辩护（carpenterwestphal2001 型）

```
[定义式操作化] [Composite IV] was measured as [count of appointments meeting criterion c] divided by [the total number of appointments], where [criterion c] is defined as [threshold/measure] ([citations for c]).
[数值反例] The reason for calculating [the measure] as an average can be illustrated with the following example: say [unit A] has [many ties with relatively dissimilar counterparts (score .1 each)], and [unit B] has [few ties with very similar counterparts (score .6 each)].
[反事实警告] Failing to normalize these measures would give each [unit] a comparable [score], when in fact [unit B's members] have more related experience.
[备选加权稳健] In separate analyses, we weighted [ties] according to [alternative weighting]; the results presented below were substantively unchanged, demonstrating that they were robust to [alternative operationalizations].
```
**要点**:
- "The reason for calculating X as an average can be illustrated with the following example: say..." 是操作化辩护的现成句式——用一个两单元 toy example 让读者"看见"不归一化的失真，比公式更直观
- "Failing to [do X] would [mislead], when in fact [correct reading]" 反事实警告句可直接迁移到任何复合加权指标
- 收口用备选操作化的独立稳健性声明（in separate analyses ... substantively unchanged）双重加固
**范文锚点**: "Failing to normalize these measures would give each board a comparable relatedness score, when in fact B's directors have more related experience."

### 变体 6: M4 构念区分对照对 — 排除"频率近似构念"混淆（gulati1999 型）

**来源论文**: Gulati 1999 (Strategic Management Journal, 20(5), 397-420)
**原始句锚点**: "This is distinct from its frequency of alliances. ... In contrast, a firm may have fewer alliances, but those ties are with partner firms that are themselves very well connected to other firms"（同段，删节已标注）
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（单源；expert_audit_override 2026-09-05）
**写入日期**: 2026-09-05
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [构念区分声明] [Network measure] is distinct from [confusable behavioral count: e.g., its frequency of ties]. [正面刻画] A [actor] that [scores high on the measure] is one that [structural description of position]. [构念动机] [Why the theory needs this facet rather than the count]: given [theoretical mechanism], access to [what the structure provides] can be [an enabling condition for the behavior]. [对照反例] For instance, a [actor] may have [numerous ties], but all of them with [a few partners] that are not very well connected to other [actors], or it may have [widely dispersed ties], but with [isolated partners] who have no other [ties]. [对照正面] In contrast, a [actor] may have [fewer ties], but those [ties] are with [partners] that are themselves very well connected to other [actors], who in turn may be well connected themselves ... [构念收口] Thus, [the measured property] results not only from [the behavioral count] but also from [the structural reach of the partners] and, in turn, [their partners' reach], and [tie back to the construct the hypothesis names].
**与原骨架差异**: 现有变体 1–5 都是"怎么算"的操作化配方；本变体是"为什么这个结构测量不是行为频次"的**构念区分论证**——预判审稿人最自然的质疑（"这个中心性不就是联盟次数吗"），先一句显式声明 distinct from，再用一个两极假想对照（多而弱连 vs 少而强连）让读者看见行为计数与结构位置的分离，最后收口到构念涵义（测量同时捕获计数与伙伴可达性）。Gulati 对 Cliques 与 Closeness 两个测量各做一次（"This is distinct from its frequency of alliances" 原文重复出现），构成"同一构念、多个互补侧面"的组织方式：Cliques=稠密冗余信息可达，Closeness=关系广度可达。
**诚实边界**: 对照对是 thought experiment，不提供经验证据；若结构测量与行为频次在样本中高度相关，区分论证失效，应报告两者相关并论证增量效度。区分声明只对"结构 vs 频次"这一混淆有效，不处理网络内生性（见本文件反模式表）。

### 变体 7: M4 构造选择×备选项枚举契约 — 逐条配对 + 外部基准定参（gulati1999 型）

**来源论文**: Gulati 1999 (Strategic Management Journal, 20(5), 397-420)
**原始句锚点**: "I tested all these choices against alternatives to ensure the robustness of my findings. ... The length of the window was chosen on the basis of research suggesting that the average life span for alliances is about 5 years"（同段，删节已标注）
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（单源；expert_audit_override 2026-09-05）
**写入日期**: 2026-09-05
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [总开关] We made a number of choices in constructing these [network] matrices that related to [choice domain 1: treatment of different types of ties], [choice domain 2: accumulation of multiple ties by the same partners], and [choice domain 3: which past ties to include]. We tested all these choices against alternatives to ensure the robustness of our findings. [选择一] First, we weighted [ties] by [strength indicator], using a [scale] ([citation]) and checked the results against a [simpler alternative: e.g., dichotomous measure]. [选择二] Second, to take into account [cumulative history], we used [aggregation rule capturing the strongest tie] ... and checked the results against [alternative aggregations: simple additive and normalized scores]. [选择三] Third, we included all [ties] since [origin year] in the construction of the [networks] but checked the results against a [moving window] with only [N] years of prior [ties]. The length of the window was chosen on the basis of research suggesting that [substantive benchmark: e.g., the average life span of the relation] is about [N] ([citation]).
**与原骨架差异**: 变体 5（carpenterwestphal2001）是**单一**加权选择的数值反例辩护 + 一个备选加权稳健收口；本变体是**枚举契约**——在操作化正文开头一次性预告构造中的全部自由裁量维度（tie 类型处理/重复 tie 累积/历史窗），以 "tested all these choices against alternatives" 立契约，随后 First/Second/Third 逐条配对"选择 → 备选项"，且每个自由参数（窗长 5 年）由外部文献基准证成而非任意设定。与变体 1–4（各讲一个指标的算法配方）互补：本变体管的是跨指标的**构造层稳健性组织**。适用场景：网络操作化含多处研究者裁量、审稿人可能逐一质疑时。
**诚实边界**: 枚举契约只保护作者显式声明的选择维度；未声明维度（关系方向性、多层级网络、自我连接处理）不在保护内。备选项结果只在正文声明"checked ... consistent"，细节通常移交结果节或附录——Methods 侧不预告具体数字。

### 变体 8: M4 间接联结路径长度分离 — 嵌套总量控制 + 效应强度排序（gulati_westphal1999 型）

**来源论文**: Gulati & Westphal 1999 (Administrative Science Quarterly, 44(3), 473-506)
**原始句锚点**: "our measure of third-party ties effectively includes indirect ties with a distance of two links ... When this measure is held constant, the control variable for total appointments captures the effect of indirect ties of greater length (i.e., three links or more)." ... "We controlled for such ties because they could amplify trust or distrust in the focal CEO-board relationship, although we would expect them to have a weaker effect than ties to third parties that are directly connected to both members"（同段，删节已标注）
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单源；Gulati 系单源即 VERIFIED，expert_audit_override 2026-09-06）
**写入日期**: 2026-09-06
**槽位**: M4（嵌套总量控制部分兼具 M6 色彩）
**骨架**:
> [焦点间接联结定义] [Focal indirect ties] were measured for each [dyad] as the number of [shared affiliations] by [actor A] and [the members of actor B's affiliation circle], excluding [the focal relation] ([operation detail]). [嵌套总量声明] [Our measure of focal indirect ties] effectively includes indirect ties with a distance of [two] links: if [actor A] shares [an affiliation] with [C], who is connected to [actor B], [A] and [B] are separated by [two] links. [层距分离控制] We also controlled for the total number of [affiliations] held by [the actors] ([total-connectivity control]). When [the focal measure] is held constant, [the total] control variable captures the effect of indirect ties of greater length (i.e., [three] links or more). [控制理由 + 强度排序预登记] We controlled for such ties because they could [amplify the focal mechanism: e.g., trust or distrust], although we would expect them to have a weaker effect than [ties] that are directly connected to both members of [the focal relationship].
**与原骨架差异**: 变体 2（common ties 计数）与变体 1（degree centrality）是单一指标的算法配方；变体 6 做"构念≠频次"的构念区分；本变体处理**间接联结的路径长度分离**——当焦点构念（距离 2 的第三方联结）嵌套于更宽的联结总量（总任命数）时，把总量作控制变量，使焦点测量的残差效应识别特定路径长度的机制，并预登记效应强度排序（直接共同联结 > 更长间接路径）。这是网络构念的"嵌套测量→分层控制→机制归因"论证，属测量-控制接口，与反射性问题讨论（反模式层）互补而非重复。
**诚实边界**: 嵌套假设须成立——总量控制须真包含焦点测量的更长路径成分，否则"残差=更长路径效应"不成立（构造两测量时须显式核对包含关系）。强度排序是理论预期而非检验，Results 须按排序方向解读系数，不得事后翻转。

### 变体 12：重叠消解式构念区分（Overlap-Eliminating Construct Discrimination，Gulati_1999_AJS 型）

**适用场景**: 两个构念在经验上同源（同一批底层事件可同时生成为两个测量——直接纽带与共同伙伴都来自既往联盟），若各测各的，共线与构念混淆会同时污染系数解释；需要把区分"做进"数据构造规则而非停留于概念论述。

**骨架**:
```
[构念定义] [Construct B] indicates the extent to which [a pair] shared [common partners] from past ties. For each [dyad-period] record, we computed [the count] as a result of [their events] in the previous [k] years.
[重叠消解规则] To differentiate [Construct B] from [Construct A], we set [the shared measure] to zero if [the members] had entered at least one previous direct [A-event] with one another (cf. [prior operationalization source]).
```

**为什么有效**: 一条 if-then 构造规则完成三件事——构念边界（B 的信息价值在于"未直接相连者间的第三方中介"）、系数可解释性（B 的系数不再混入 A 的直接效应）、与近亲构念的区分效度（审稿人无法再用"这不就是 A 吗"攻击）；"(cf. Mizruchi 1992: 126)" 把规则挂靠到先例，显示这是沿袭而非 ad hoc——两句话的区分效力胜过一段概念辨析。

**注意事项**: 消解方向要有理论依据（为何 A 吸收重叠而不是 B）；置零规则改变测量分布，须在稳健性中报告不置零的替代（本论文以矩阵构造备选链统一处理）；若两构念理论上本应重叠（中介机制），此法不适用——它只服务于"判据必须互斥"的平行机制设计。

**反模式**: 两个构念的测量共享底层事件却不作互斥处理，区分靠正文断言；置零规则无先例挂靠也无理由；用 PCA/因子分析事后"证明"区分而测量本身重叠。

**与近亲变体的区分**: Gulati_1999 SMJ 的 2×M4 条目（构念区分/操作化备选）是概念层辨析与测量备选谱系；本变体是数据构造层的互斥规则——区分由 if-then 置零规则在测量定义内执行，属同族（构念区分）的 EXTEND 新面：从"论证区分"推进到"构造区分"。

**原文锚点** (Gulati & Gargiulo 1999, AJS):
> "To differentiate structural embeddedness from relational embeddedness, we set common ties to zero if the members of a dyad sharing common ties had entered at least one previous direct alliance with one another (cf. Mizruchi 1992: 126)."

### 变体 10：描述性结构预分析×理论回扣（Structural Preanalysis with Theory Loopback，Gulati_1999_AJS 型）

**适用场景**: 网络类设计中，理论预言某种涌现结构（位置分化、核心-边缘、小圈子等），估计模型之前先用描述性网络分析把该结构"拍下来"——既为后续操作化（尤其系统水平 moderator）提供实证底座，又让读者在看到回归前已亲眼见过机制在起作用。

**骨架**:
```
[预分析定位] We conducted a detailed analysis of [the network(s)] in [each unit] to explore [the theorized structural pattern] and visually examine [the emergence of theorized differentiation].
[目标声明] Our goal was to assess the structural patterns that would clarify and illustrate [the differentiation process depicted in the theoretical model].
[快照设计] We examined [the structure] resulting from [cumulative activity] by conducting separate analyses of each [unit] in [the penultimate period], including all [events] in the previous [k] years.
[技术选择+概念辩护] We used [equivalence concept] to identify [classes of actors]—[one-sentence concept contrast with nearest rival concept, with concrete example].

[理论回扣] The emergence of [observed structure], such as the one revealed by [our analysis], is consistent with the mechanisms in our [model name]: [one-sentence mechanism restatement chaining each observed feature to the theory's amplification logic].
```

**为什么有效**: 描述性分析被升格为理论参照物而非表格前置装饰——每项结构发现（位置数、梯度、缺席证据）都即时回读理论模型，读者在回归之前已"看见"机制；缺席证据（"no evidence of X"）与在场证据同等对待， rival 模式被主动排除而非沉默放过；快照时点（penultimate period + k-year window）给出明确的结构定格协议，结果可复现；理论回扣句用一个"consistent with"把描述证据定位为机制一致性检验而非证明——因果语言与描述性设计的强度精确匹配。

**注意事项**: 只在理论确实预言结构模式时使用（否则退化为摆图）；技术排除必须给具体失败理由（计算困难/解释困难）而非引用堆叠；效度自检要有阈值数字（如第一主成分解释方差 ≥90%）；回扣句是"一致性"语言，不得写成"证明"；系统水平构念若由此段引出（如结构分化随时间上升 .12→.34），趋势证据须在此段或 M5 段给出。

**反模式**: 描述性分析与理论各说各话（图归图、假设归假设）；只报告在场模式不排查 rival 结构（派系/分层）；用描述性结果提前宣称因果机制；备择技术仅一句"we used X"无排除理由。

**与近亲变体的区分**: 家族既有 M4 条目（Gulati_1999 SMJ 构念区分/操作化备选）管的是"构念→测量"的辨析；本变体管的是"理论→结构快照→回扣"的 M1 级模式确立段——描述性分析作为独立说服动作，产出的是对理论化过程的视觉确认与 moderator 的实证底座。

**原文锚点** (Gulati & Gargiulo 1999, AJS):
> "Our goal was to assess the structural patterns in the networks that would clarify and illustrate the differentiation process depicted in the theoretical model."

### 变体 11：风险集宽度辩护×递减稳健性阶梯（Risk-Set Breadth Defense，Gulati_1999_AJS 型）

<!--
pattern_id: risk_set_breadth_defense
build_type: 网络效应/dyadic 事件设计（M3 DV 操作化段）
source_papers: ["gulati_1999_where_do_interorganizational_networks (AJS)"]
verification_status: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override (Gulati 系单源裁定 2026-09-06, paper_count=1)
story_fidelity: section_variant
-->

**适用场景**: DV 是离散配对事件（tie formation、缔约、并购对）且"哪些配对处于风险中"本身是自由裁量决策时——风险集定义直接改变估计对象，必须在 DV 段把它作为一等方法决策辩护，而非默认所有可能配对。

**骨架**:
```
[单位与事件史] Since the unit of analysis was [the dyad], for each [panel] we listed all possible [dyads] within each [stratum], discarding [reverse-ordered duplicates] to avoid double counting. For each [dyad-period] record, we coded a dichotomous dependent variable that indicated whether [the pair] [entered the event] in the given year.
[结构命名] The resulting data structure is best characterized as [a cross-sectional time-series panel] in which the units are [unique dyads]. Each record included [the state of the dependent variable], along with [time-varying and time-constant covariates] characterizing [the dyad].
[宽度辩护] Such a broad definition of the risk set, which included all possible [dyads], was considered essential to uncovering unbiased results.
[反向偏差承认+不可筛选论证] Including many [dyads] that never [experience the event] can, of course, lead to its own set of biases, but we had no observable criteria to determine a priori which [dyads] were likely to [experience the event].
[稳健性阶梯] To address this issue and test the robustness of the results, we conducted the analysis with [N] additional risk sets that were more restrictive. [阶梯定义：at least one member experienced → both members experienced]. The results obtained with different sets were comparable. The results reported here are based on [the complete risk set].
```

**为什么有效**: 把"风险集"从数据准备细节升格为影响无偏性的识别决策——先给正向理由（宽集=无偏性要件），再主动承认反向偏差（"can, of course, lead to its own set of biases"），用"无先验可观测标准"论证无法人工筛选，最后把残余疑虑转为可执行的递减阶梯（3 档）并报告收敛——审稿人的两条攻击线（太宽引入噪音/太窄引入选择）在同一拍内被预答。

**注意事项**: "essential to uncovering unbiased results" 是强主张，须真有遗漏风险集导致选择偏差的机制（被筛掉的配对非随机）；递减阶梯各档定义须可复现；"results were comparable" 需与 Results/附录呼应；若用 restrictive set 报告主结果则辩护方向反转（窄集防误删——此时用附录的"removing men from pregnancy studies"类比）。

**反模式**: 风险集只给数字不辩护；承认反向偏差后不处理（让步无收口）；稳健性阶梯只跑一档；把风险集辩护写在附录让 DV 段裸奔。

**与近亲变体的区分**: Gulati & Westphal 1999（非线性模型.md M8）的风险集条目是"风险集-假设映射+Heckman 泛化"——把风险集结构与假设层次对齐再泛化到 Heckman；本变体是"宽度-偏差权衡+不可筛选论证+递减稳健性阶梯"——管的是风险集宽度本身的辩护语法，两者互补不重叠。

**原文锚点** (Gulati & Gargiulo 1999, AJS):
> "Such a broad definition of the risk set, which included all possible dyads for the sample of firms in each industry, was considered essential to uncovering unbiased results."

**适用场景**: 网络类设计的"反射问题"（reflection problem）软着陆：核心预测变量是网络构念（嵌入性、同伴行为、位置），若与 DV 同期测量则与内生反馈模型不可区分——用滞后结构把"网络塑造行为"与"行为即网络"在时间轴上切开。

**骨架**:
```
[模型句] We modeled [the DV event] using the following dynamic panel model, in which a variable's [positive] coefficients indicate that it promotes [the event]: [equation: p = F(a + b·x_time-constant + c·y(t-1)_time-varying + u_unobserved)].

```

**为什么有效**: 反射问题最经济的处理不是引入工具变量大动干戈，而是把时间结构写进模型定义并用一句脚注声明家族区分——"distinct from the class of models known as X" 先承认 X 的存在与合法性（不误伤同族文献），再以 "rather than being linked to ... in the same period" 精确指出自己的参数落在哪个时间位；估计器细节全部让渡给附录指针，模型段只留估认对象与时间结构——读者的注意力被导向"估计的是什么"而非"软件怎么跑"。

**注意事项**: 滞后必须真实（协变量确实测于 t-1 及以前，且窗口选择有依据——本论文的 5 年窗挂靠联盟寿命文献）；声明放在脚注位即可，正文模型句保持单一功能；若审稿人可能追问"滞后不充分"，需在附录补充近亲模型（p* 类）的诚实边界讨论（见家族 M8 条目）。

**反模式**: 网络构念与 DV 同期测量却宣称动态模型；脚注写成方法学综述（失去"一句切开"的锋利）；滞后声明与数据段实际测量窗矛盾；用滞后回避而非声明内生性残余（后者应转入 M8）。

**与近亲变体的区分**: Gulati_1999 SMJ 的非线性模型 M7 条目是异质性威胁分解（unit 内异质性来源）；本变体是模型规格层的时间分离声明——处理的是构念层面同时性（网络构念的定义域问题），非误差结构异质性；两者是 M7 的两个不同威胁面。

**原文锚点** (Gulati & Gargiulo 1999, AJS):
> "The postulated network effects here result from a lagged network of cumulative prior ties until the previous year, rather than being linked to network elements in the same period."

### 变体 14：dyadic 非独立性×随机化检验（Dyadic Nonindependence Randomization Test，Gulati_1999_AJS 型）

<!--
pattern_id: dyadic_nonindependence_randomization
build_type: 网络效应/dyadic 设计（M8 识别/效度拍——观测相依）
source_papers: ["gulati_1999_where_do_interorganizational_networks (AJS)"]
verification_status: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override (Gulati 系单源裁定 2026-09-06, paper_count=1)
story_fidelity: section_variant
-->

**适用场景**: 分析单元是配对（dyad-year），同一行为者必然复现于大量配对——经典 SE 聚类难以直接套用（相依结构是整个矩阵而非分组），需要矩阵置换类随机化检验兜底推断。

**骨架**:
```
[威胁命名] Another consideration with analyzing [dyadic] data relates to concerns about interdependence across observations, [which led to the presence of the same actor across multiple dyads].
[检验设计+差异声明] To ensure the robustness of our results, we employed a procedure similar to [MRQAP], routinely used by researchers studying [dyads]. Our approach differs from [MRQAP] in that we used [the actual estimator] for each iteration of the simulation. As a result, we randomized [the key variables] for each [stratum-period].
[迭代协议] We ran [K] iterations of a completely specified [model] with a new randomized [variable] obtained by random permutations of [the rows and columns] in each [matrix] for each [stratum-period]. The coefficients obtained were compared with those obtained in the original formulation.
[读出规则] The percentage of frequency with which [the simulated results] exceeded the original estimates divided by [the number of permutations plus one] indicates the statistical reliability (pseudo t-test) of the original results. A result of less than [5] percent provides evidence that the original estimates are indeed accurate.
[卖点句] The benefit of a randomization procedure is that obtaining satisfactory results does not require an assumption of independent observations, a random sample, or a specified distribution function.
[近亲模型诚实边界] Our model is akin to [p*-type models that build in dyadic interdependence parameters], but since [our lagged parameters] cannot be considered an adequate safeguard against the potential effects of non-independent observations, we used [the randomization procedure] to limit concerns of interdependence.
[细节指针] Details of these tests are reported in the Appendix.
```

**为什么有效**: 威胁被命名后立刻给出"近亲工具+改造点"（similar to MRQAP, differs in that we used the actual estimator）——既借力成熟惯例的可信度，又把改造理由（估计器不同故须在估计器内置换）说透；读出规则给出完整计算式（超频次数/置换数+1）让 pseudo-t 可复算；卖点句以三连否定（no independence / no random sample / no distribution assumption）把随机化检验的适用优势一次性说完；最难得的是诚实边界拍——主动承认自己的滞后参数"cannot be considered an adequate safeguard"，把随机化检验定位为必要而非充分防线，先于审稿人说出这句话。

**注意事项**: 置换须保持矩阵结构（行列表同时置换）且分层进行（每 stratum-period 一轮）；迭代数与"未穷举全部置换"的局限要如实报告（本论文脚注承认穷举不可行）；该检验救的是推断效率（efficiency），不解决系数一致性——正文声明不得越界；配对设计若可标准聚类（如组织聚类稳健 SE）应先考虑常规方案。

**反模式**: 只说"we used MRQAP"不说明与原版的差异与改造理由；置换单位错误（置换个案而非矩阵行/列）；报告 p 值格式如同参数检验而不给置换协议；用随机化检验掩盖模型设定问题。

**与近亲变体的区分**: Gulati & Westphal 1999（非线性模型.md M8）条目是"风险集-假设映射+Heckman 泛化"（样本选择威胁）；本变体处理的是 dyadic 观测相依（矩阵结构相依）——家族 M8 的 falsification/诊断缺口由此填补，registry dyad/事件史关键词零命中的空白区。

**原文锚点** (Gulati & Gargiulo 1999, AJS):
> "The benefit of a randomization procedure is that obtaining satisfactory results does not require an assumption of independent observations, a random sample, or a specified distribution function."

**适用场景**: 论文的核心主张是"某经验模式是系统性的、由结构驱动的"（而非随机涨落或跟随噪音）——需要一个能让整套解释彻底翻车的证伪基线，而不只是逐个系数的显著性。

**骨架**:
```
[理论赌注重申] The primary theoretical contention underlying [our use of these measures] is that [the observed events] are not random but are driven by [the structure of prior relationships].
[基线设计] To verify our claims of systematic [events], we compared the results for [our sample] against results obtained with a sample in which [the event] was assigned randomly [on the basis of its original distribution].
[零假设显式化] The implicit null hypothesis here is that an observed pattern in the data is due purely to chance. Such a comparative analysis serves as a valuable baseline.
[败局预声明] Finding no differences in the predictive power of [the independent variables] for [the actual and random dependent variables], or greater predictive power for [the random dependent variable], would suggest that [the postulated effects] could have predicted [the random occurrence] just as well or better. As a result, our claims for [systematic patterning] would be moot.
[结果读出] The results indicated that none of [the hypothesized effects] are better predictors of [randomly assigned events] than [in the actual models]. This finding allows us to reject the implicit null hypothesis and suggests that [the postulated effects] are not at all good predictors of [the random occurrence] of [events].
```

**为什么有效**: 证伪检验的设计语法是把赌注推到极致——作者先亲手写下自己的败局条件（"would be moot"），检验因此有了无可指摘的预登记性质；"implicit null hypothesis ... due purely to chance" 把通常隐匿的零假设显式化，随机化基线（保持原始分布的随机分配）排除了"随机化改变了边际分布"的辩护口；结果读出句（"not at all good predictors"）用最强否定修辞收尾，与败局预声明首尾呼应——整套动作给读者的是"这套解释本可以死，但它没死"的确证感。

**注意事项**: 随机分配必须保持 DV 原始分布（等比例重排而非均匀伯努利）；该检验证伪的是"模式系统性"整体主张，不能替代逐假设检验；"valuable baseline" 挂靠先例（cf. 引用）以示非自创仪式；若随机基线下部分变量仍显著，须如实报告并降格主张。

**反模式**: 只跑随机基线不预声明败局条件（事后解读）；随机化破坏原始分布；把通过基线检验写成"证明了理论"（它只排除了纯机会解释）；基线检验从论文中消失只在脚注留痕。

**与近亲变体的区分**: 本变体（M8 falsification 拍）与 c6（dyadic 非独立性随机化检验）分工明确——c6 救推断（观测相依下的显著性可信度），本变体证伪主张（模式是否纯机会）；与 Gulati & Westphal 1999 的风险集/Heckman 条目亦不重叠（那是样本选择威胁）。家族 M8 此前为空，两条互补填充。

**原文锚点** (Gulati & Gargiulo 1999, AJS):
> "The implicit null hypothesis here is that an observed pattern in the data is due purely to chance. Such a comparative analysis serves as a valuable baseline (cf. Zajac 1988)."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **网络变量无反射性讨论** | 将 centrality / common ties 视为外生，未讨论 Manski reflection problem 或共同冲击 | 在 M8 增加网络内生性讨论，并报告至少一项 falsification test（如基于时间错位的安慰剂检验） |
| **网络矩阵构建不透明** | 仅报告 "we calculated degree centrality"，未说明关系定义、时间窗口、有向/无向 | 明确关系方向、时间窗口、矩阵对称性、软件/命令 |

## 诚实边界（全局）

- **网络变量的内生性**：common ties / centrality / density 可能受共同冲击或反射性问题驱动，应在 M8 专门讨论并补充 falsification test（如基于时间错位的安慰剂检验）。
- **网络效应的识别**：dyad FE 只能消除时不变遗漏变量；若存在时变混淆（如共同市场冲击同时影响 alliance 结构与竞争行为），FE 无法识别因果。
- **相对中心性的方向**：差值方向（focal − partner）需与理论预测一致；若理论关注绝对地位，应使用 raw centrality 而非差值。
