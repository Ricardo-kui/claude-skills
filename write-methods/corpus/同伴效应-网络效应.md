## 设计特征摘要

同伴效应/网络效应设计指核心解释变量或边界条件来自 actor 之间的关系结构（dyadic ties、network position、structural embeddedness）。这类设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**网络变量的操作化透明化**：如何定义关系、如何构建网络矩阵、如何计算 centrality/embeddedness/density，以及如何处理网络内生性（reflection problem / common shock）。Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (SMJ) 是本设计类型的典型范文：从 alliance 矩阵到 degree centrality 差值、common ties 计数、ego network density 的完整计算链。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M4 网络变量操作化（关系定义、矩阵构建、指标计算）、M6 网络相关控制变量、M8 网络内生性讨论。常见薄弱点：未讨论反射性问题（Manski reflection problem）、未报告网络变量描述性统计、将网络位置视为外生而未做 falsification。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M4 相对中心性 — degree centrality 差值 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: Relative centrality captures the relative positional embeddedness of two firms. Relative centrality is calculated as the firm’s degree centrality minus its partner’s degree centrality.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [Variable] captures the relative positional embeddedness of two [actors]. We first constructed yearly [relationship] matrices within [industry] from [year_start] to [year_end], using a [N]-year moving window. Altogether, we identified [N] pairs of [relationships], including those started between [earlier_start] and [earlier_end]. We constructed a symmetric (non-directional) matrix of these [actors] for each year using [software] ([citation]), then calculated the [centrality type] of each [actor] within the above [network] matrices. [Variable] is calculated as [actor]'s [centrality] minus [partner]'s [centrality].
**与原骨架差异**: 网络研究中"相对"权力/地位的常用操作化。关键要素：(1) 时间窗口明确（5 年 moving window）；(2) 对称矩阵构建说明；(3) 软件声明（Ucinet 6）；(4) 差值计算方向（focal actor − partner）。可替换为 betweenness / closeness centrality，但 dyad-level 差值逻辑不变。
**诚实边界**: 中心性差异可能内生于共同冲击或双方历史选择，应在 M8 讨论反射性问题并补充至少一项 falsification test。

### 变体 2: M4 结构嵌入性 — common ties 计数 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: Common ties capture the structural embeddedness between the firm and its partner. Following prior studies, we measured this variable by counting the number of common third-party partners that the firm and its partner shared during the previous 5 years (Gulati & Gargiulo, 1999; Polidoro et al., 2011).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [Variable] captures the structural embeddedness between [actor] and [partner]. Following prior studies, we measured this variable by counting the number of common third-party [actors] that [actor] and [partner] shared during the previous [N] years ([citation_1]; [citation_2]).
**与原骨架差异**: 结构嵌入性的标准测量。关键：时间窗口（previous N years）和共同第三方计数。与闭合三元组（closed triad）概念一致。
**诚实边界**: common ties 可能反映行业共同伙伴或地域聚类，需控制行业/地域固定效应或做 placebo test。

### 变体 3: M6 ego network density — two-mode incidence transformation (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: This ego network includes the two firms within the alliance as well as all other firms that have at least one alliance with at least one of the two firms. We first transformed the firm-by-firm alliance network into an incidence network (i.e., the output is a two-mode firm-by-alliance network) using the command “transform | graph theoretic | incidence” in Ucinet 6.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M6
**骨架**:
> We controlled for the density of the ego network for each [dyad]. This ego network includes the two [actors] within the [relationship] as well as all other [actors] that have at least one [relationship] with at least one of the two [actors]. We first transformed the [actor]-by-[actor] [relationship] network into an incidence network (i.e., a two-mode [actor]-by-[relationship] network) using the command "[command]" in [software]. We then transformed the two-mode incidence network into a one-mode [relationship]-by-[relationship] square network by using the command "[command]" and selecting [unit] as the unit of interest. Finally, we calculated ego network density within this new network for each [relationship] in our sample.
**与原骨架差异**: ego network density 的高级操作化，涉及 two-mode incidence transformation。关键：明确命令、软件、转换逻辑。Cui et al. 用 alliance-by-alliance 网络密度控制 dyad 层面的网络闭合效应。
**诚实边界**: two-mode transformation 可能因 alliance 数量多而稀疏，需报告 density 分布并讨论其作为控制变量（非核心解释变量）的角色。

### 变体 4: M3/M4 status as global network centrality — Bonacich beta centrality (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pollock, Lee, Jin & Lashley (2015, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: We used available data on all the VCs in the TBO database, not just our sample, to calculate this measure. We operationalized status using Bonacich’s (1987) beta centrality, a measure of global centrality that considers the focal actor’s centrality, its connected actors’ centralities, their connected actors’ centralities, and so on (Bonacich, 1987).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-30
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> "Research has established that a good proxy for [actor] status is centrality in [relationship] networks ([citations]). We used available data on all the [actors] in the [database], not just our sample, to calculate this measure. For each [actor] we constructed one-year adjacency matrices. To smooth patterns of affiliation ([citation]), each annual adjacency matrix included [relationship] networks based on [N]-year moving periods beginning with the [actor]'s [founding/year-zero] year. For [actors] less than [N] years [old] we used all available data. We operationalized status using [Bonacich's (1987) beta centrality], a measure of global centrality that considers the focal actor's centrality, its connected actors' centralities, their connected actors' centralities, and so on. When beta is set to zero, network centrality is akin to degree centrality, focusing only on the local structure. The larger the value of beta, the more the centrality measure reflects the global structure. In our analysis, we set beta to [X] percent of the reciprocal of the largest eigenvalue. We also standardized our status measure so that its effect size could be compared with the effects of [other construct]. We used [software] to calculate our status measure."
**与原骨架差异**: 区别于变体1 的 degree centrality（仅局部结构），Bonacich beta centrality 是**全局/特征向量型中心性**——递归地把"联系人的中心性"纳入权重，β 控制局部→全局的光谱（β=0 退化为 degree；β 越大越反映全局结构），设为最大特征值倒数的 X%。三个配套手法：(1) 用**全数据库**（非仅样本）计算以保证网络完整性；(2) **平滑**——年度邻接矩阵用 N 年移动窗口；(3) **标准化**以与另一构念（reputation）的效应大小可比（为跨方程系数比较铺路）。适用于 status / prestige / rank 等由 affiliation 模式推导的层级构念。
**诚实边界**: 单一指标 centrality 可能含测量误差且无法分解多维度（pollock2015 自承 limitation）；β 的选择（最大特征值倒数的百分比）需声明具体值并做敏感性检验。


### 变体 5: M4 加权平均操作化的反例辩护（carpenterwestphal2001 型）

```
[定义式操作化] [Composite IV] was measured as [count of appointments meeting criterion c] divided by [the total number of appointments], where [criterion c] is defined as [threshold/measure] ([citations for c]).
[数值反例] The reason for calculating [the measure] as an average can be illustrated with the following example: say [unit A] has [many ties with relatively dissimilar counterparts (score .1 each)], and [unit B] has [few ties with very similar counterparts (score .6 each)].
[反事实警告] Failing to normalize these measures would give each [unit] a comparable [score], when in fact [unit B's members] have more related experience.
[备选加权稳健] In separate analyses, we weighted [ties] according to [alternative weighting]; the results presented below were substantively unchanged, demonstrating that they were robust to [alternative operationalizations].
```
**要点**:
- "The reason for calculating X as an average can be illustrated with the following example: say..." 是操作化辩护的现成句式——用一个两单元 toy example 让读者"看见"不归一化的失真，比公式更直观
- "Failing to [do X] would [mislead], when in fact [correct reading]" 反事实警告句可直接迁移到任何复合加权指标
- 收口用备选操作化的独立稳健性声明（in separate analyses ... substantively unchanged）双重加固
**范文锚点**: "Failing to normalize these measures would give each board a comparable relatedness score, when in fact B's directors have more related experience."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **网络变量无反射性讨论** | 将 centrality / common ties 视为外生，未讨论 Manski reflection problem 或共同冲击 | 在 M8 增加网络内生性讨论，并报告至少一项 falsification test（如基于时间错位的安慰剂检验） |
| **网络矩阵构建不透明** | 仅报告 "we calculated degree centrality"，未说明关系定义、时间窗口、有向/无向 | 明确关系方向、时间窗口、矩阵对称性、软件/命令 |

## 诚实边界（全局）

- **网络变量的内生性**：common ties / centrality / density 可能受共同冲击或反射性问题驱动，应在 M8 专门讨论并补充 falsification test（如基于时间错位的安慰剂检验）。
- **网络效应的识别**：dyad FE 只能消除时不变遗漏变量；若存在时变混淆（如共同市场冲击同时影响 alliance 结构与竞争行为），FE 无法识别因果。
- **相对中心性的方向**：差值方向（focal − partner）需与理论预测一致；若理论关注绝对地位，应使用 raw centrality 而非差值。
