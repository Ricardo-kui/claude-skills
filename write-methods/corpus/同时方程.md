## 设计特征摘要

同时方程设计用于**两个（或多个）相互因果的构念**——每个构念作为因变量的方程中，把对方当期值作为自变量（reciprocal / simultaneous causation）。核心挑战：(1) 同时性引入内生性（双方当期值与误差项相关）；(2) 若再加滞后因变量则同时面临 path dependence；(3) 跨方程系数比较（"A 对 B 的影响是否大于 B 对 A"）需要两个**非嵌套**模型的系数协方差，而分方程估计不自动给出该协方差。典型场景：status↔reputation、supply↔demand、legitimacy↔performance 等互构关系。

首篇蒸馏：Pollock, Lee, Jin & Lashley (2015, *ASQ*) — status↔reputation 共演，并用 Weesie (1999) stack 法做跨方程系数比较。

### 变体 1: Pollock et al. 2015 (ASQ) — 动态同时双方程规格（path dependence + simultaneity + FE）(2026-07-30)
**原始句锚点**: "Second, this model incorporates the simultaneously determined nature of status and reputation by including the current measures of each variable as an independent variable in the other variable's estimation equation. Thus this model implicitly presumes that two reciprocal causal forces are at work simultaneously (Wooldridge, 2010)."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇）
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> "We modeled the [coevolutionary] processes of [construct A] and [construct B] as a system of two interdependent structural equations: [A_it = ρ1·A_{i,t−1} + β1·B_it + γ1·X_it + μ1i + ε1it]; [B_it = ρ2·B_{i,t−1} + β2·A_it + γ2·X_it + μ2i + ε2it]. This model incorporates three essential features. First, it conceptualizes the evolutionary processes of [A] and [B] accumulation as dynamic and path dependent ([citations]), causing serial correlation of the error terms unless controlled for. Using the lagged dependent variable as an explanatory variable takes path dependence into consideration and addresses serial correlation stemming from persistence. The degrees of path dependence or persistence are reflected in ρ, the coefficients of the lagged values. Second, this model incorporates the simultaneously determined nature of [A] and [B] by including the current measures of each variable as an independent variable in the other variable's estimation equation; thus this model implicitly presumes that two reciprocal causal forces are at work simultaneously ([citation]). Third, this model accounts for unobserved heterogeneity using fixed effects, which is important because we used panel data."
**与原骨架差异**: 首个同时方程变体。把方程规格呈现为**三个理论特征的显式映射**——path dependence（→滞后因变量 + ρ）、simultaneity（→互为自变量）、unobserved heterogeneity（→FE）。每个计量特征都绑定一个理论承诺，使方程不是统计黑箱而是理论的直接形式化。ρ 被明确诠释为"path dependence 的度量"，为 Results 中报告持久性百分比铺路。

### 变体 2: Pollock et al. 2015 (ASQ) — 堆叠非嵌套 Wald χ² 检验（跨方程系数比较，Weesie 1999）(2026-07-30)
**原始句锚点**: "Given that these two models are non-nested and thus estimated separately, this covariance is not automatically generated from the estimation of individual models. To obtain this covariance, we followed the procedure recommended by Weesie (1999) and used the stack command in STATA to combine the two non-nested datasets into a single "stacked" dataset in which each set of the two non-nested but jointly determined observations (i.e., one from the status model and the other from the reputation model) was given the same identifier."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇）
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> "To test [whether the effect of A on B differs from the effect of B on A] we compared the [A] and [B] coefficients across two models—one in which [A] predicted [B] and one in which [B] predicted [A]—and assessed whether the effects of [A on B] were different than the effects of [B on A] across [conditions]. We employed the Wald χ²-test; the null hypothesis assumed the non-nested models' parameter estimates are the same. [Test statistic: (β1 − β2)² / var(β1 − β2), distributed χ² with one degree of freedom.] This statistic requires not only each parameter estimate's variance but also the covariance between them. Given that these two models are non-nested and thus estimated separately, this covariance is not automatically generated from the estimation of individual models. To obtain this covariance, we followed the procedure recommended by [Weesie (1999)] and used the *stack* command in STATA to combine the two non-nested datasets into a single 'stacked' dataset in which each set of the two non-nested but jointly determined observations (i.e., one from the [A] model and the other from the [B] model) was given the same identifier. This allowed us to cluster the two non-nested models and generate covariances using the [estimator] command with the *vce(cluster)* option specified. The parameter estimates generated by the stacked model were exactly the same as those generated by the individual models."
**与原骨架差异**: 跨方程系数比较的**核心难题**——两个非嵌套方程分估不给出系数间协方差——及其解法：用 *stack* 把两个非嵌套数据集合并为同一标识符的"堆叠"数据集，再用 *vce(cluster)* 聚类估计恢复跨方程协方差，从而可计算 Wald χ² 的分母 var(β1−β2)。这是同时方程/reciprocal-causation 论文检验"不对称方向"假设（如 H1a/H1b）的关键方法学创新，可迁移到任何"两个非嵌套模型系数比较"场景。诚实点："堆叠模型系数与分估完全相同"——证明 stack 仅用于获取协方差，不改变估计。

### 变体 3: Malshe & Agarwal 2015 (JM) — 辅助反向因果方程（system 内吸收 reverse-causal channel）(2026-07-30)
**原始句锚点**: "Thus, anticipated fund needs can affect decisions about leverage. To account for these potential effects, we include lagged advertising, R&D, and customer satisfaction as predictor variables."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇）
**槽位**: M7/M8
**骨架**:
> "We added Equation [N] to account for the notion that the anticipated need for funds can influence firms' [policy/endogenous variable] decisions. [Actors] can fund their increased [downstream activity] (e.g., [examples]) from their internal sources, debt, or equity. For example, [Author] ([year]) show that [firms use internal cash flows to finance the downstream activity]. A stream of [source] literature has argued that [actors] with more [downstream assets] will tend to [choose different policy levels] because [theoretical reason] ([citations]). Along the same lines, [related] literature has argued that [actors] in need of [ongoing funding] will want [a different policy level], which provides greater [slack/flexibility] ([citation]). Thus, anticipated [downstream] needs can affect decisions about [the policy variable]. To account for these potential effects, we include lagged [downstream variable 1], [downstream variable 2], and [downstream variable 3] as predictor variables [in the policy-variable equation]."
**与原骨架差异**: 在同时方程系统中**增设一个辅助方程**（policy/endogenous variable 作 DV、下游变量滞后项作预测变量），专门吸收"下游需求 → 政策变量"的**反向因果**通道（如 anticipated fund needs → leverage）。区别于变体1（互为当期自变量的同时性）：本变体处理反向因果（下游 Y 的预期需求反过来决定上游 X），用滞后项 + 独立方程将其参数化纳入系统，使主方程系数免受反向因果污染。适用于"政策/财务变量与下游战略变量互为因果"的系统设计（capital structure↔marketing、R&D↔financing、compensation↔performance）。诚实点：明示这是 to account for reverse-causal concerns，且用滞后项创造时序距离。

### 变体 4: Malshe & Agarwal 2015 (JM) — DWH 检验裁决"是否需要 IV"：SUR（有效）vs 3SLS（一致）+ Hansen-Sargan 工具有效性 (2026-07-30)
**原始句锚点**: "The econometrics literature has advocated for the use of seemingly unrelated regression (SUR) estimation for estimating a system of equations because it provides consistent and efficient estimates by modeling the correlated error terms across equations (Wooldridge 2010). Following the well-established literature stream in marketing and econometrics (Hennig-Thurau, Henning, and Sattler 2007; Stahl et al. 2012; Ter Braak, Dekimpe, and Geyskens 2013; Wooldridge 2010), we used the Durbin–Wu–Hausman test to check for endogeneity."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇）
**槽位**: M7/M8
**骨架**:
> "The econometrics literature has advocated for the use of [seemingly unrelated regression (SUR)] estimation for estimating a system of equations because it provides consistent and efficient estimates by modeling the correlated error terms across equations ([citation]). However, in the presence of endogeneity, it may be necessary to use instrumental variables. Prior literature has suggested using [lags of dependent variables] as instruments (e.g., [citation]). We used a combination of [third and fourth lags] of endogenous variables as instruments in a [three-stage least squares] estimation and performed the [Hansen–Sargan test] of overidentifying restrictions to determine the validity of the instruments. The test statistic was statistically nonsignificant (χ²([df]) = [value], p > .10), suggesting that the instruments are valid. Next, we examined whether the [SUR] estimates differed significantly from the [3SLS] estimates. Following the well-established literature stream ([citations]), we used the [Durbin–Wu–Hausman] test to check for endogeneity. The nonsignificant test statistic (χ²([df]) = [value], p > .10) suggests that the [SUR] estimates are preferable to the estimates of instrumental regressions and that endogeneity is not a concern."
**与原骨架差异**: 与"用 IV 处理内生性"的常规叙事**反向**——先估计 SUR（有效但需外生性）与 3SLS（一致但需工具），再用 **DWH 检验裁决是否真有必要上 IV**：DWH 不显著 → 内生性不是问题 → 选用更有效的 SUR。配套 **Hansen-Sargan 过度识别检验**确认工具有效（不显著 = 工具外生）。这是 Hausman 式"效率 vs 一致性"权衡的 simultaneous-system 版本（区别于 `面板数据-OLS` 变体10/13 的 FE-vs-RE / RE 选择）。适用于系统估计中审稿人必然追问"为何不用 IV / 是否有内生性"的场景——用检验统计量而非声明回应。诚实点：报告两个检验统计量与 p 值，让读者可独立判断；与 `write-results/OLS-FE` 变体39（用替代估计器失败反向佐证主估计器）互补——那是"比较以佐证"，本变体是"检验以豁免"。
