## 设计特征摘要

多行为者设计指分析单元高于个体（如 dyad、team、network、alliance），且原始关系数据常包含多于两个参与方，需要研究者将多边关系拆分为可分析的 dyad 或聚合到更高层级。这类设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**数据预处理透明化**：如何处理 multiparty relationships、如何定义分析单元、如何控制由拆分带来的混淆。Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (SMJ) 是本设计类型的入门范文：将多企业 alliance 拆分为 dyad，并用控制变量处理与 structural embeddedness 的混淆。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M2 数据预处理（多边→dyad）、M6 拆分相关控制变量、M7 分析单元与标准误层级声明。常见薄弱点：未说明拆分规则、未控制 multiparty 带来的混淆、标准误聚类层级错误。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M2/M6 多边 alliance 拆 dyad + 混淆控制 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: In cases where an alliance includes more than two firms, we split the alliance into a set of dyads. Empirically, this may result in a confounding effect with structural embeddedness (i.e., number of common ties).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M2/M6
**骨架**:
> In cases where a [relationship] includes more than two [actors], we split the [relationship] into a set of [dyads]. Empirically, this may result in a confounding effect with [other_variable]. We parceled out the possible confounding effect caused by [multiparty_relationships] by including a control variable ([Variable]) that measures [definition]. We also ran the models using a sample excluding [multiparty_relationships]; the results are robust.
**与原骨架差异**: 多行为者设计的基础预处理步骤。关键要素：(1) 拆分规则说明（多边→dyad）；(2) 预判混淆变量（如 multiparty 与 structural embeddedness 相关）；(3) 用控制变量 parcel out 混淆；(4) 排除多边样本的稳健性检验预告。适用于 alliance、team、consortium 等多方关系研究。
**诚实边界**: 拆分 dyad 会创建非独立观测，需在 M7 说明聚类标准误层级或使用多层模型；若多边 alliance 占比较高，排除样本可能导致选择偏误。


### 变体 2: M8 个体→组级聚合辩护链（westphal_bednar2005 型）
**来源论文**: Westphal & Bednar (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: Thus we calculated the $r_{wg}$ index, a measure of within-group agreement, as well as the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC), which estimates the proportion of total variance that can be explained by group membership.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-29
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> Although [construct] is typically defined at the [group] level, [our theoretical perspective suggests that the biases/perceptions of individual members are interdependent: if others are less biased than a focal person about [X], then they should [behavior], thus lessening the focal person's bias]. Thus we would expect limited variance within [groups] in [individual-level variable of interest]. In this respect, the relationship between [construct] and individual-level perceptions is a kind of consensus composition model ([Chan, 1998]). Thus we calculated the [r_wg] index, a measure of within-group agreement, as well as the [intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC)], which estimates the proportion of total variance that can be explained by [group] membership. The [r_wg] index was [value] for [variable], well above the [.70] threshold commonly used to justify aggregation ([George, 1990]). [When using the ICC procedure,] a significant F-test is commonly used to justify the aggregation of data to the [group] level ([Klein et al., 2001]). These results support our theoretical expectations and suggest that within-[group] variance in [perceptions] is sufficiently small to warrant aggregation to the [group] level.
**与原骨架差异**: 三段功能：(1) 构念层次理论化先行——把"为何聚合"写成个体感知相互依赖的机制论证并挂 consensus composition model（Chan 1998），而非直接报统计量；(2) 双统计判据——r_wg（>.70 阈值，George 1990）+ ICC（显著 F-test，Klein et al. 2001）；(3) 聚合决策收口句回扣理论预期（"support our theoretical expectations ... warrant aggregation"）。适用于个体报告须聚合到 dyad/team/董事会/组织层级的组级构念研究。
**诚实边界**: r_wg>.70 与 ICC 显著 F-test 是最低判据；若构念理论预期允许组内个体差异，不得用本链强行聚合；当代审稿常在此链之外要求组内-组间方差分解或一致性敏感度检查。


### 变体 3: M4 个体/组层同构测量 + 等价性检查（carpenterwestphal2001 型）

```
[同构计算] To test these hypotheses, we developed a set of aggregate variables for [the construct] across all [members] on a [group]; for analyses conducted at the level of the [individual], the variables were calculated for the particular [individual].
[等价声明] In our measures of [the construct], [subgroups within units] were combined, as separate analyses revealed no significant differences in results when we distinguished between [them].
[口径注] (note that this [group]-level measure excludes duplicate [ties to the same counterpart])
```
**要点**:
- 同一构念在个体层与组层用同一计算程序各算一遍（同构），使跨层结果可比
- "separate analyses revealed no significant differences in results when we distinguished between [subgroups]" 一句话完成子群合并的事后等价辩护——比 r_wg/ICC 事前聚合正当性更轻量，适合合并是测量选择而非理论主张的情形
- 括号口径注（去重/排除规则）防止读者误算组层指标
**范文锚点**: "In our measures of relatedness, inside and outside directors were combined, as separate analyses revealed no significant differences in results when we distinguished between the two. ... (note that this board-level measure excludes duplicate ties to the same firm)"

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **多边关系拆分不透明** | 仅报告 "we focus on dyads"，未说明如何将 multiparty alliance 拆分为 dyad | 明确拆分规则、是否产生重复 dyad、如何处理同一 alliance 内的多个 dyad |
| **忽略拆分带来的混淆** | 拆分后未控制 multiparty 相关变量 | 增加控制变量或排除多边样本的稳健性检验 |

## 诚实边界（全局）

- **分析单元非独立**：多边关系拆分为 dyad 后，来自同一 alliance 的 dyads 不独立，必须在 M7 声明聚类层级或使用多层模型。
- **选择偏误**：若排除多边样本后结果变化较大，需讨论多边 alliance 是否代表不同的关系类型。
- **拆分规则的稳健性**：同一 multiparty alliance 的不同拆分方式（如仅保留 focal-partner 对 vs 所有两两组合）可能影响结果，应在稳健性中比较。
