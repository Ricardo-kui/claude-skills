## 主骨架

目前 `write-methods/SKILL.md` 中暂无专门的定性过程研究主骨架。本文件 Lashley & Pollock 2020 系变体（1–5）经 expert_audit_override（user 2026-09-06）升为 VERIFIED，变体 6（Haunschild 2015）仍为 EMERGING；待第二篇**纯定性过程研究**论文交叉验证后，建议提升为独立主骨架。

## 设计特征摘要

定性过程研究的 Methods 叙事核心是**从现象到理论的可信路径**：读者需要相信研究者为何使用归纳/质性方法、为何选择该情境、多源数据如何三角互证、编码如何逐步抽象为理论维度，以及研究发现为何可信。典型结构包括：(1) 现象与方法正当化；(2) 情境选择理由；(3) 数据来源与角色；(4) 抽样与进入；(5) 时间边界；(6) 分析过程（编年史→阶段划分→编码→理论维度）；(7) 可信性机制。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: 现象与方法正当化
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: Given the limited knowledge about category-level core stigma removal, we used a qualitative, inductive research approach to both generate theory and supplement existing theory (Edmondson and McManus, 2007).
**验证状态**: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override（user 2026-09-06：Pollock/Westphal/Gulati 系单源即 VERIFIED）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: Q1
**骨架**:
> Given limited understanding of [process], we used a qualitative, inductive approach to both generate theory and supplement existing theory. This approach is appropriate because [phenomenon] is [dynamic/contested/emergent], and prior research has not identified the [phases/mechanisms/conditions] through which [outcome] unfolds.
**与原骨架差异**: 定性过程研究必须在 Methods 开头明确说明为何采用归纳/质性/过程方法，而不仅是“我们做了访谈/观察”。关键：把方法选择与研究缺口（limited understanding of process）直接挂钩。

### 变体 2: 极端情境选择理由
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: The medical cannabis industry is an “extreme situation” (Eisenhardt, 1989: 537) of a core-stigmatized industry category seeking to reduce its stigma, making it an ideal setting to study the stigma reduction process.
**验证状态**: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override（user 2026-09-06：Pollock/Westphal/Gulati 系单源即 VERIFIED）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: Q2
**骨架**:
> [Setting] is an extreme situation of [phenomenon], making it an ideal setting for studying [process]. In this setting, [actors] face [conditions] that make [theoretical tensions] especially visible, while [background factors] provide relative stability for observing variation in [process element].
**与原骨架差异**: 情境选择段落需要同时说明 (1) 为何是“极端/启示性情境”；(2) 核心理论张力在该情境下为何可见；(3) 哪些背景因素保持相对稳定以便观察过程变异。

### 变体 3: 多源数据角色分配
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly) — 主来源；Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 — 部分槽位交叉
**原始句锚点**: The data for this study came from three main sources: direct observations, interview data, and archival data. Our data sources, summarized in table A1 in the Online Appendix, allowed us to triangulate the insights that we were generating about the industry.
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（Lashley 为纯定性三源三角；Park/Keeves 为混合方法中访谈用于问卷校准/预试；expert_audit_override 2026-09-06）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: Q3
**骨架**:
> Our data came from three main sources: [source A], [source B], and [source C]. [Source A] allowed us to capture [X]. [Source B] provided [Y]. [Source C] supplied [Z]. Together these sources allowed us to triangulate on [issues/claims] and distinguish between [public claims] and [private actions].
**与原骨架差异**: 不是简单罗列数据来源，而是为每个来源分配独特功能。关键：说明各来源如何互补，尤其是哪些来源能捕捉公开话语、哪些能捕捉私下行为、哪些能提供历史背景。

### 变体 4: 过程阶段划分与编码进阶
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly) — 主来源；Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 — 编码信度部分槽位交叉
**原始句锚点**: We began by using the archival data to develop a chronology of key events in the stigmatization of cannabis, as well as key events in the subsequent development of the medical cannabis industry. We paid close attention to critical junctures or “ruptures” that “durably transform previous structures and practices” (Sewell, 1996: 843) in the evolution of the industry.
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（完整 grounded-theory 进阶链仅 Lashley；Park/Keeves 提供内容编码与信度报告的参照；expert_audit_override 2026-09-06）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: Q6/Q7
**骨架**:
> We began by developing a chronology of key events in [phenomenon]. We paid close attention to critical junctures or "ruptures" that durably transformed previous structures and practices. These chronologies allowed us to tentatively bracket the process into [number] overlapping phases that served as a framework for our analysis. We then used open coding of [data source] to develop first-order codes and provisional categories from the raw data, iterating among the raw data, emerging insights, and theoretical literature. We used axial coding to understand relationships across codes and aggregated them into second-order themes, which we further aggregated into higher-level theoretical dimensions.
**与原骨架差异**: 把“编年史→阶段划分→开放式编码→主轴编码→聚合维度”串联为一个连贯段落。关键：显示分析不是一次性跳跃，而是逐步抽象，并且阶段划分来自数据中的关键事件（ruptures），而非外部强加。

### 变体 5: 可信性机制组合
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly) — 主来源；Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 — 测量效度机制部分槽位交叉
**原始句锚点**: We established trustworthiness through several means. First, we triangulated on issues and claims across diverse data sets.
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（Lashley 提供 Lincoln & Guba 式可信性组合；Park/Keeves 提供 CFA/信度/编码一致率等测量效度组合；expert_audit_override 2026-09-06）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: Q8
**骨架**:
> We established trustworthiness through several means. First, we triangulated on issues and claims across [data sources]. Second, we established credibility through prolonged engagement at the site, which allowed us to build rapport and access sensitive information. Third, we used peer debriefing, discussing our findings with [a researcher skilled in qualitative methods]. Finally, after developing emerging categories, we used [a secondary coder / external expert] to confirm the categories ([agreement metric]).
**与原骨架差异**: 定性研究的可信性段落需要明确对应 Lincoln & Guba 的 credibility/transfersability/dependability/confirmability 等传统，但本骨架采用更实用的组合：三角验证 + 长期参与 + 同行汇报 + 二级编码。关键：每个机制都要说明它解决了什么具体威胁。

## 与 Findings 的衔接

定性过程研究的 Methods 必须向 Findings 做出以下承诺：
1. **过程模型承诺**: Methods 中提出的阶段划分将在 Findings 中展开为完整的 process model。
2. **关系空间承诺**: 如果在 Methods 中引入了 front-stage / backstage / side-stage 等分析透镜，Findings 必须 consistently 使用。
3. **数据来源承诺**: Methods 中声称某数据源能捕捉私下行为或公开话语，Findings 必须实际展示来自该源的证据。
4. **可信性承诺**: Methods 中报告的可信性机制限制了 Findings 的 claim strength（例如二级编码后的类别才能作为核心维度）。

### 变体 6: M1 多方法情境合法性 — 极端案例构建理论 × 大样本量化检验的双情境堆叠 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Haunschild, Polidoro & Chandler 2015 (Organization Science)
**原始句锚点**: We do so by drawing on two very different contexts. First, we use the example of NASA’s Challenger and Columbia accidents to develop theory about how mechanisms of learning and forgetting cause an organization to oscillate between safety and efficiency.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M1
**骨架**:
> We do so by drawing on two very different contexts. First, we use the example of [extreme_case] to develop theory about how [mechanism_description]. We then test this model empirically in the context of [large_N_setting]. [Large_N_setting] represents a good test of [theory] because, by examining [phenomenon], we can go beyond the [qualitative evidence from extreme case] to conduct [large-scale quantitative analysis] across a whole [population/industry]. An important consideration here is that [phenomenon in large-N setting], although potentially very serious, occur more frequently than [phenomenon in extreme case]. This enables us to collect fine-grained, longitudinal data to examine [specific mechanism] and the fading of this effect over time.
**与原骨架差异**: Haunschild 的情境辩护不是简单的“X is an appropriate context because...”，而是**双情境堆叠**：先声明两个情境的互补角色（一个构建理论，一个检验理论），再为量化情境提供增量正当性（“we can go beyond qualitative evidence from a single organization” + “[event] occurs more frequently than [extreme event]”）。适用于质性→量化跨方法设计。

### 变体 7: 研究者旅程与 Reflexivity 段落 (Pollock Ch09)
**来源论文**: Pollock (2025) Ch09 + Bansal & Corley (2012, AMJ FTE) + Elsbach & Kramer (2003, AMJ) 实例
**原始句锚点**: "Qualitative methods sections also reveal more of the researcher's personal journey. It's common that as the research unfolds the research question evolves or changes, the researchers seek out other types of actors for interviews, and they collect different data than they originally anticipated. The researcher features prominently throughout the narrative—using lots of first-person pronouns, describing all the twists and turns they took, and the reasons they took them."
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（Pollock Ch09 + Bansal & Corley 双源确立）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-01
**槽位**: Q8（与变体 5 trustworthiness 互补——变体 5 管"可信性机制组合"，本变体管"研究者作为工具的角色披露"）
**骨架**:
> Our understanding of [phenomenon] evolved over the course of the research. We began by focusing on [initial focus], but as we collected data, it became clear that [emergent focus] was more central. This shift prompted us to [new data collection/analytical move]. For example, after [N] interviews we realized that [informants were emphasizing a dimension we had not anticipated], so we revised our interview protocol to probe [new dimension] and sought out [additional informants who could speak to it].
>
> As the research unfolded, [researcher role/position] shaped what we could see. [Author]’s background in [domain] facilitated [access/understanding], while [limitation of researcher position] meant we had to [compensating move, e.g., member checking / peer debriefing / additional data source]. We report these choices transparently so readers can assess how the inquiry process—not just the context—may have influenced the findings.
**与原骨架差异**: 变体 5（trustworthiness 四判据）覆盖了 prolonged engagement / triangulation / peer debriefing / 二级编码等**机制性可信性**，但把研究者当作中立的工具。本变体补 Pollock Ch09 明确的 **reflexivity / 研究者旅程**——Pollock 指出定性 Methods 的一个 distinctive 特征是"the researcher features prominently throughout the narrative, using lots of first-person pronouns, describing all the twists and turns they took, and the reasons they took them"。研究问题在过程中**演进或改变**、研究者寻求新类型的访谈对象、收集与最初预期不同的数据——这些都是定性研究的常态而非缺陷，应被透明披露。**关键纪律**：(1) 用第一人称（we began / we realized / we revised）——不要用被动语态隐藏研究者的主动选择；(2) 把研究过程的曲折当作可信度来源（透明 = 读者可评估 inquiry 的影响）而非弱点；(3) 说明研究者位置如何 shape 了能看到什么，以及如何补偿局限。适用于任何研究者角色/位置会影响数据可及性或解读的定性研究（尤其 ethnography / 参与式观察 / 研究者与田野有身份关联的研究）。
