## 设计特征摘要

实验 Methods 的叙事核心是**操纵的透明性与可审计性**：读者必须清楚知道被试经历了什么、条件间差异在哪里、以及如何排除混淆。典型结构包括：被试招募与排除→材料与程序→操纵描述→操纵检验→因变量测量→分析计划。

### 变体 1: 单实验 Methods 标准段落（被试→材料→操纵→测量）
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: In both experiments, participants were randomly assigned to one of the two conditions (construal framing: concrete vs. abstract) in a between-subjects experiment.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M2–M7
**骨架**:
> We recruited [N] [participants] from [source]. Participants received [incentive] for completing the study. We excluded [cases] using [rule]. Participants were randomly assigned to one of [number] between-subjects conditions ([condition A] vs. [condition B]). They viewed [stimulus type], which was based on [real case/control] to enhance [psychological/mundane] realism. The [IV] was manipulated by varying [stimulus feature]. In [condition A], the [stimulus] emphasized [description]. In [condition B], the [stimulus] emphasized [description]. After viewing the [stimulus], participants completed measures of [DV] and [controls/covariates].
**与原骨架差异**: 本骨架给出单实验 Methods 的最小完整版本：(1) 被试来源与排除；(2) 被试间随机分配；(3) 刺激类型与现实主义理由；(4) 操纵描述（两个条件并列）；(5) 因变量测量。关键：操纵描述必须让读者能想象出两个条件的实际差异，不能停留在抽象标签。

### 变体 2: M8 开放式文本 RA 评分操纵检验 — 替代 traditional self-report (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**原始句锚点**: To assess our manipulation of CEO achievement expression, we asked all participants to share their thoughts and feelings as investors after reading the CEO's speech in at least 30 words.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> To assess our manipulation of [IV], we asked all participants to [open_ended_task] in at least [N] words. We then asked [N] research assistants to independently rate each [participant_type]'s [written/spoken] answer using a [N]-item measure: "[item_1]" and "[item_2]." The ratings were on a [N]-point scale, ranging from 1 ([anchor_low]) to [N] ([anchor_high]). Participants were told to skip [task] that did not reference [target] (N = [excluded]). The reliability scores of the [N] raters were high (α = [value_1] and α = [value_2]). The ratings from the [N] raters were highly correlated (r = [value], p < [threshold]), indicating high inter-rater reliability. Accordingly, we combined the ratings from the [N] raters. The [output] of the participants in the high [IV] condition (n = [N], M = [value], SD = [value]) was rated to indicate a higher level of [construct] than the [output] of the participants in the low [IV] condition (n = [N], M = [value], SD = [value]) (t([df]) = [value], p < [threshold]).
**与原骨架差异**: 传统操纵检验使用 self-report 量表（如"To what extent did the CEO appear confident?"），但 self-report 存在 demand characteristics 风险——参与者可能猜到研究目的。Li et al. 的 RA 评分法通过以下方式规避此风险：(1) 参与者完成开放式任务（如"写下你作为投资者的想法"），而非直接回答操纵问题；(2) 2名独立 RA 对开放式文本进行评分，RA 不知道实验条件；(3) 评分者间信度（r > .80）作为操纵检验的信度基础；(4) 独立样本 t-test 确认条件间差异。适用于 subtle manipulations（如语言操纵、框架操纵）——参与者可能无法自觉意识到操纵效果的场景。
**诚实边界**: 开放式文本+RA评分法更耗时且对 RA 培训要求更高。若操纵效果非常明显（如 stark visual differences），传统 self-report 量表可能足够。RA 评分仅适用于参与者难以直接自我报告的心理构念（如 implicit stereotypes、subtle frames）。

### 变体 3: 视频操纵 + 两阶段数据收集 — 替代纯文本vignette (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ahmadi, Khanagha, Berchicci & Jansen 2017 (Journal of Management Studies)
**原始句锚点**: The data collection was planned in two stages. In the first stage, two weeks before the experiment, participants were asked to complete a personality test, which included items relating to regulatory focus trait.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M1/M2/M8
**骨架**:
> The data collection was planned in two stages. In the first stage, [time_interval: e.g., two weeks] before the experiment, participants were asked to complete a [personality/individual-difference] test, which included items relating to [trait_construct]. In the second stage, each participant received a [brief manual / instruction] and [an electronic link / invitation] to the experiment. Each participant was given a [vignette/scenario] and asked to [watch a video / review the case], on a random basis. Then, they were asked to review the case and think [time] before making any decision. Subsequently, the dependent variable was measured and manipulation checks were administered, and participants were thanked and told that they would be informed of the results.
>
> For the manipulation of [manipulated_construct_2: e.g., organizational context], we created two different situations for the decision-making of participants via [manipulation_modality: e.g., the framing of the context]. Each participant was exposed to either [condition_A] or [condition_B] by being asked to watch one of two videos after reading the vignette. In these videos, [speaker_role: e.g., a manager] shared [his/her] evaluation of the situation with the participant, either by depicting [condition_A_description] or [condition_B_description]. [He/She] emphasized either: (a) [cue_set_A: e.g., positive outcomes, focusing on ideals, potential for advancement, future gains, and opportunities]; or (b) [cue_set_B: e.g., negative outcomes, obligations and duties, potential for stability, future losses, and threats]. The words used in the two videos were selected carefully to create two contrasting videos with similarly structured sentences, and were checked by [N] academic experts. Non-verbal language was kept consistent in both videos (e.g., similar body language, no demonstration of emotions).
**与原骨架差异**: 现有实验变体（Falchetti, Li et al.）均使用纯文本操纵。Ahmadi et al. 引入了两个创新：(1) **视频操纵**——组织情境通过视频中的管理者讲话来操纵（而非vignette文字），增加生态效度（"increase the chance of managers becoming immersed in the context"）；(2) **两阶段数据收集**——T1测量个体差异特质（regulatory focus trait），T2（2周后）进行实验，时间分离减少common method bias和demand characteristics——参与者不太可能将T1的特质量表与T2的操纵联系起来。视频操纵的关键质量控制：(a) 两个视频使用"similarly structured sentences"，(b) 非言语行为（body language, emotions）保持一致，(c) 由学术专家检查措辞。适用于需要操纵组织情境/文化/氛围的实验——视频比文字更能传递"contextual feel"。
**诚实边界**: 视频操纵引入了额外的混淆可能性——speaker的外表、声音、语调可能在条件间产生非预期的差异。必须在Methods中报告控制措施（如"non-verbal language was kept consistent"）并在操纵检验中验证条件间仅在目标构念上有差异。

### 变体 4: M3 双极量表选择辩护 — continuous vs orthogonal 测量论证 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ahmadi, Khanagha, Berchicci & Jansen 2017 (Journal of Management Studies)
**原始句锚点**: While there is no universal argument about whether exploration and exploitation should be considered continuous vs orthogonal variables, we theorize and operationalize them as continuous variables, given our context.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> While there is no universal argument about whether [construct_A] and [construct_B] should be considered continuous vs orthogonal variables, we theorize and operationalize them as [choice: continuous / orthogonal] variables, given our context. Following prior research, we use a [scale_type: bipolar / separate] scale to better [rationale: e.g., resemble the trade-off nature of the exploration and exploitation decisions made by managers at the individual level for a single domain] (see [citation_1]; [citation_2]). To do this, we adapted the original measure for [construct] ([citation]) to match the specific decision-making context that [participants] encountered in this experiment (see [Appendix]). For example, the items included: '[high_pole_item]' versus '[low_pole_item]'. The reliability score is at [value].
**与原骨架差异**: 标准量表选择段落仅报告"we measured X using Y's scale (α = .XX)"。Ahmadi et al. 增加了**量表类型的理论辩护**：(1) 承认学术争议——"there is no universal argument about whether X and Y should be considered continuous vs orthogonal"；(2) 明确选择continuous/bipolar的理由——"to better resemble the trade-off nature... at the individual level for a single domain"；(3) 引文支持（Emmert & Barker 1989, Gupta et al. 2006）；(4) 上下文适配声明——"adapted... to match the specific decision-making context"。适用于任何存在"连续vs正交"争议的构念测量（exploration/exploitation, promotion/prevention, learning/performance goal orientation等）。关键策略：不是回避争议而是正面承认并给出理由。
**诚实边界**: bipolar scale 假设构念是零和的（more exploration = less exploitation），这在个体-单领域层面可能成立但在组织-多领域层面不成立。必须明确声明bipolar假设的理论适用范围（"at the individual level for a single domain"），否则审稿人会质疑"为什么不使用separate scales"。

### 变体 5: M1 专业样本同质性辩护 — 单组织内同质管理者群体 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ahmadi, Khanagha, Berchicci & Jansen 2017 (Journal of Management Studies)
**原始句锚点**: Second, we identified a homogenous population of managers within a single organization who had the same level of decision-making authority and similar relevant experience to ensure the quality of the experimental design (Webster and Sell 2014).
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M1
**骨架**:
> This setting is appropriate for the purpose of our study for a number of reasons. First, [industry_feature_1: e.g., although the telecommunications industry is at the forefront of innovation activities, it is also populated by large and old incumbents that need to be innovative as well as efficient]. This makes [theoretical_tension: e.g., trade-offs between exploration and exploitation] particularly significant for [participant_type] working in this industry. Second, we identified a homogenous population of [participant_type] within a single organization who had the same level of [decision_making_authority / relevant_attribute] and similar [relevant_experience] to ensure the quality of the experimental design ([citation]). Third, we did not involve participants who were solely responsible for [biased_role: e.g., growth and innovation], and might therefore be biased by their roles and the context of their work. Instead, we invited [participant_type] who were responsible both for [duty_A: e.g., dealing with short-term product demand... and efficiency] and for [duty_B: e.g., long-term product advancement strategies and growth]. As [influential_role_description: e.g., influential middle managers], they were an ideal subject group to be used for studying [theoretical_phenomenon].
<!-- wb:ahmadi_2017_jms:legacy_实验_5 -->
**与原骨架差异**: 现有实验corpus中的样本辩护主要针对"为什么用学生/MTurk/Prolific"。Ahmadi et al. 展示了**专业决策者样本的辩护策略**——特别是从单一大型组织（110,000+ employees, 180+ countries）中招募同质管理者群体：(1) 行业特征匹配理论张力——"industry at forefront of innovation but populated by incumbents → exploration/exploitation trade-offs particularly significant"；(2) 组织内同质性——"same level of decision-making authority and similar relevant experience" → 减少个体差异噪音，增强实验设计的内部效度；(3) 角色平衡——排除纯创新/纯效率角色，选择同时负责两者的中间管理者 → 避免角色偏见。适用于任何使用专业/管理者样本的实验——特别是当样本来自单一组织时，需要解释"为什么不使用更异质的样本"。
**诚实边界**: 单组织样本的优势（同质性→内部效度）同时也是其劣势（同质性→外部效度受限）。必须在Methods或Discussion中承认"findings may be specific to this organizational context"并建议跨组织复制。
### 变体 6: M5/M8 测量过程+操纵过程双设计 + rival accounts battery (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ilicic & Brennan 2026 (Journal of Marketing)
**原始句锚点**: This study focuses on gambling and employs a measurement of process design to systematically compare sense of agency and perceived product danger to a range of alternative constructs linked to political ideology and addictive products.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-22
**槽位**: M5/M8
**骨架**:
> [Study N] examines the psychological process underlying [effect]. This study employs a measurement of process design to systematically compare [proposed_mediator] to a range of alternative constructs linked to [IV] and [DV]. Participants reported on [proposed_mediator_1] (alpha = [value]) and [proposed_mediator_2] (alpha = [value]), alongside a battery of alternative constructs: [rival_1] (alpha = [value]), [rival_2] (alpha = [value]), ... [rival_N]. The order of presentation of mediators and their items was randomized across participants. [Attention/data-quality controls, e.g., two attention-check questions placed after four and nine construct measures].
>
> To test whether the association between [IV] and [DV] was consistent with serial mediation through [M1] and [M2], we employed a bootstrapping procedure ([PROCESS macro]; n = [N_bootstraps], Model 6). The 95% bootstrapped CI of the indirect effect did not include zero. As a competing-order sensitivity check, we repeated the analysis after reversing the two mediators ([Fairchild and McDaniel 2017]); the CI included zero, providing no evidence for that alternative sequence. This comparison is consistent with, but does not establish, the proposed temporal ordering. Auxiliary analyses exploring alternative mediators (i.e., [rival_1], ..., [rival_N]) are reported in [Web Appendix]; none reached significance.
>
> [Complementary study] adopts a moderation of process design ([Spencer, Zanna, and Fong 2005]), manipulating [proposed_mediator] ([absent] vs. [present]). [Proposed_mediator] interacts with [IV] to shape [DV], providing convergent causal evidence for the mechanism.
**与原骨架差异**: 本骨架填补 corpus 的消费者行为实验机制证明传统空白。management/strategy 实验变体（1-5）以"操纵->行为"为主，无 (a) measurement of process（一次性测量 proposed mediator + 全部 plausible rivals 逐一排除）+ (b) moderation of process（操纵 proposed mediator 建立因果）的 Spencer, Zanna & Fong (2005) 双过程收敛设计。两个关键手法：(1) **rival accounts battery**——一次研究内同时测量 9+ 个竞争构念并逐一作为替代中介排除，高密度抗辩性写作；(2) **随机化中介呈现顺序**（"The order of presentation of mediators and their items was randomized"）预判 common method bias / order effect 质疑。区别于 `references/slot-R8.md` 的"测 1-2 个 alternative mediator"结果报告——本变体是 methods 侧的设计传统 + 大规模排除。
**诚实边界**: measurement of process 只能提供与某一排序一致的相关证据，不能排除反向因果；反转中介后“不显著”也不等于两个间接效应彼此显著不同。若结果变量先于中介测量，尤其不能把 PROCESS 路径写成时间过程。应以预注册的时间分离、随机操纵或 moderation-of-process 设计补强机制识别；两类证据互补而非替代。rival accounts battery 的规模（9+）会增加疲劳和共同方法负担，需随机化呈现顺序并报告数据质量控制。

### 变体 7：被试自选对象的品牌定制情境材料——自涉入个性化 + 条件匹配 DV 措辞 + debrief 防迁移收尾

[功能标签]: M2 程序/材料 — scenario 实验中让被试先选择自己真实使用的对象（品牌/产品/服务），随后全部材料围绕该对象逐人定制

[骨架]: "First, participants selected their current [product] brand so we could tailor all subsequent questions to their [object]. Second, participants rated [moderator_construct], which served as the moderator. We used a measure from [citation], which consists of [k] [scale_point]-point scales and captures [facet_1] ('[item_1]') and [facet_2] ('[item_2]') ([AVE = value], α = [value]). ... Third, participants read [the stimulus] of a [product] [firm_type] which we tailored to their [object] to increase involvement. ... We manipulated [factor] ([level_A] vs. [level_B]) randomly between subjects by telling participants in the [level_A] ([level_B]) condition that [condition_content], see [appendix]. Fourth, we assessed the dependent variable ('[DV_wording_A]' ([level_A]), '[DV_wording_B]' ([level_B])), which served as a proxy for [behavioral_outcome]. ... Last, participants indicated [covariates] and provided demographics. We thanked and debriefed participants to prevent carry-over effects to actual [downstream_perceptions]."

[结构要点]: 四个手法——(a) 被试自选真实对象（"selected their current [product] brand"）使后续全部材料自涉（self-referencing）；(b) 定制理由显式入文（"tailored to their [object] to increase involvement"），把个性化与卷入度直接挂钩；(c) DV 问句措辞按条件一一对应（括号内并列两条件的确切问句），避免 DV 与操纵措辞错位；(d) 收尾 debrief 句（"to prevent carry-over effects to actual [downstream_perceptions]"）把实验与被试的真实下游态度显式隔离

[原文锚点]: "First, participants selected their current laptop brand so we could tailor all subsequent questions to their brand. ... participants read the recall of a laptop manufacturer which we tailored to their brand to increase involvement."

[可迁移性]: 高 — 一切"对象自选 + 情境定制"的消费者/员工实验直接可用

[范式排他性]: 中 — 需要被试对刺激对象有真实既有关系（自选式个性化）；虚构情境实验退回变体 1

[设计变体]: 无真实使用关系的对象改为随机指派虚构品牌（纯文本 vignette 路径）；调节者被测量而非操纵时，把调节者量表放在操纵之前并重报信度（AVE/α）

[区别于变体 1（单实验标准段落）与变体 3（视频操纵）]: 变体 1 的刺激是统一 vignette、变体 3 更换操纵媒介（视频）；本变体的新维度是刺激内容随被试自选对象逐人定制，且 DV 问句与条件一一对应、debrief 句收尾防迁移

### 变体 8：操纵检验失败样本的包容政策句——报错误数 + 全样本保留 + 排除稳健性一句并列

[功能标签]: M8 操纵检验 — 操纵检验存在错误作答时的样本处理政策与透明披露

[骨架]: "[Step k], we asked them '[manipulation_check_question]', which served as the manipulation check. [N_incorrect] respondents answered the question incorrectly. To maximize sample size, we included those participants in all our analyses, but our pattern of results does not change when excluding them."

[结构要点]: 三步一句链——(a) 报告操纵检验错误作答的确切人数；(b) 处理政策显式命名并给理由（"To maximize sample size"）；(c) 同句给出排除版稳健性结论（"our pattern of results does not change when excluding them"），把审稿人"为何不剔除"的质疑预判在 Methods 内

[原文锚点]: "Sixty-seven respondents answered the question incorrectly. To maximize sample size, we included those participants in all our analyses, but our pattern of results does not change when excluding them."

[可迁移性]: 高 — 一切带操纵检验的实验通用（attention check / catch question 场景同构）

[范式排他性]: 高 — 只在报告操纵检验的实验设计中需要

[设计变体]: 错误率过高时政策对调——剔除为主分析、保留为稳健性；attention check 失败按惯例直接剔除并报 N（不适用本模板）；两个研究同构时第二研究只报差异项（错误人数不同）

[区别于变体 2（开放式 RA 评分操纵检验）]: 变体 2 更换操纵检验形式（RA 评分替代 self-report 以规避 demand characteristics）；本变体不动检验形式，处理的是"检验未通过的那部分样本"的披露与保留政策
