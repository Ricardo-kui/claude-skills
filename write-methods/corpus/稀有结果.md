## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `稀有结果` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

- **核心问题**: 低事件率 → 条件/单元 FE 因缺乏 within-unit DV 变异而大量丢弃「从未发生事件」的单元
- **主估计器选择**: 时间虚拟变量 + 单元聚类稳健 SE 的 Logit/Probit（保样本）优于强制 FE
- **识别补强**: 若其他 DV 使用 IV/2SLS，稀有二元主分析须诚实降级因果语言，并在稳健性中报告 IV-Probit / bivariate Probit
- **跨论文复现率**: 1/1（Zorn et al. 2017；EMERGING）

**来源论文**: Zorn, Shropshire, Martin, Combs & Ketchen (2017, SMJ)
**原始句锚点**: "Given the low base rate occurrence of financial restatement (i.e., many firms never restate), fixed effects models drop a significant number of observations due to lack of variance in the dependent variable. Thus, in modeling the likelihood of financial misconduct, we control for firm effects by clustered standard errors (Long & Freese, 2014)."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇；设计类型首次填充）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-05
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> To model [rare binary DV], which is a low base-rate event (many [units] never experience [the event]), we use [logistic / probit] regression with [time] dummies and robust standard errors clustered by [unit] ([citation]). Fixed-effects models would drop a significant number of observations due to lack of variance in the dependent variable within [units] that never [experience the event]. We therefore control for [unit]-level dependence through clustered standard errors rather than conditional fixed effects ([citation]). [Optional identification bridge:] Because an exact [logistic] analogue of 2SLS is unavailable, we confirm these results using an instrumental-variable [bivariate probit / IV-probit] model in robustness checks.

**与原骨架差异**: `非线性模型` 变体 7 处理的是条件 FE 负二项下「全零面板」审计但仍试图保留 FE；本变体明确选择**放弃 FE** 以保全从未发生事件的单元，并用聚类 SE 吸收单元相关。诚实边界：不得把聚类 Logit 表述为与同文 2SLS+FE 同等的因果识别；须报告事件发生率；IV-Probit 若写入 Methods 预告则必须在 Results 出现。

**适用**: 财务重述、欺诈、重大违规、破产、极端事故等低发生率二元面板结果，且理论同时检验连续结果（需 FE/IV）时。

**跨 skill 对齐**: 与 `IV-2SLS.md` 变体 13 配套；OR 翻译见 `../write-results/corpus/Logit-Probit-Ordered-Probit.md` 变体 2。

**槽位**: M2
**骨架**:
> To test [hypotheses], we constructed a panel spanning [start] through [end] by integrating data from multiple sources. We began by identifying all [units] operating in [regulatory domain that generates the outcome]. This yielded a comprehensive sample of [units] exposed to [outcome] risk, irrespective of whether [the event] occurred during the study period. **来源论文**: Lun, Zurbruegg, Mount & Cheong 2026 (*Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice*)
**原始句锚点**: "Thus, 97.5% of our observations fall within the 0 to 1 recall range, indicating most variation occurs at the extensive margin—whether a recall occurs—rather than the intensive margin."
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-08-13
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> [Events] are rare events. Of the [N] [unit-period] observations, [p0]% experienced no [event], [p1]% experienced one, and [p2]% experienced multiple [events]. Thus, [p_zero_or_one]% of observations fall within the 0 to 1 range, indicating most variation occurs at the extensive margin—whether [the event] occurs—rather than the intensive margin—how many occur conditional on one taking place. Accordingly, our primary analysis uses [panel conditional logit], consistent with prior studies ([citations]). We code [DV] as a binary indicator equal to [1] if the [unit] experienced at least one [event] in a given [period], and [0] otherwise. This specification aligns with our theoretical focus on whether [IV] is associated with the likelihood of [observable failure].
**与原骨架差异**: 变体 1 用低基线率论证放弃 FE；本变体用 margin 分解把二元编码绑在 estimand。
**诚实边界**: 骨架用 associated with，不用 increases；若理论关注次数/严重度，不可套用此塌缩。

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **industry+year FE 误称为 two-way FE 且不辩护放弃 unit FE（M7）** | 把 industry+year FE 的条件 logit 称作 two-way fixed effects，且不辩护为何不用 unit FE | FE 维度与方程一致；不用 unit FE 须写 collinearity/样本保全（Hoffmann 2024 变体 5 已给出正确辩护）；观测性设计不用 TWFE 暗示准实验。Lun et al. 2026 ETP 警示案例 |

## 诚实边界

- 稀有二元下放弃 firm FE 需要 collinearity/样本保全论证；误标 TWFE 会把观测性条件 logit 写成准实验强度。
- 不按 DV 抽样是强制槽位：样本框须含从未发生事件的暴露单元。
