# 自激点过程 — 自激点过程（Hawkes 自激励点过程）设计族：在事件流中检验跨单位自激励/事件聚类，强度方程=背景率项+激励项，EM 迭代估计并在收敛时输出潜事件角色分类（leading/following）供下游事件研究消费。适用于事件时间戳数据且理论断言事件跨单位非独立的设计。

## 功能描述
自激点过程（Hawkes 自激励点过程）设计族：在事件流中检验跨单位自激励/事件聚类，强度方程=背景率项+激励项，EM 迭代估计并在收敛时输出潜事件角色分类（leading/following）供下游事件研究消费。适用于事件时间戳数据且理论断言事件跨单位非独立的设计。


## 适用场景
- 事件时间戳数据，理论断言事件跨单位非独立（自激励/聚集），需估计激励强度与衰减
- 需要模型收敛输出的潜事件角色分类（leading/following）作为下游检验（如事件研究）的输入
- 事件计数/聚类检验面对共享上游输入（共同供应商/共同平台），需排除伪聚类

## 句法模板
### 变体 1: M7 模型选择三理由链——基准排除 + 收敛副产物 + 下游假设输入（mukherjee2022 型）

[功能标签]: 为非常规估计器建立选择正当性：先排除默认基准模型（现象按定义违反其独立性假设），再主张模型收敛副产物的独特价值，最后把潜变量输出接到下游假设检验——使估计器选择成为论证链而非工具声明

[骨架]: "We selected the [self-exciting point process model] to examine support for [event clustering] for three reasons. First, [the phenomenon], by definition, contradicts an assumption of [benchmark model] that events are random and independent across time ([methods citation]). Using a [benchmark model] to model [event occurrences] would be suboptimal if [an event by one unit excites events by other units]. Recent research has demonstrated the benefit of using [self-exciting point process models], as opposed to [benchmark models], to test for self-excitation of events in data structured similarly to ours ([citation]). Second, we leverage the [model] because of a valuable output derived from the model at convergence. [The model] uses a [two-step EM algorithm] for parameter estimation and convergence. ... In our model, the [leading and following event designations] are latent. However, this [designation] assigned at model convergence allows us to comprehensively and transparently describe the prevalence of [the phenomenon]. Third, the [leading and following event designations] are necessary inputs to our [downstream event study models] used to test Hypotheses [2a-2c]. Without a means to reliably categorize [events] as [leading or following] in [a cluster], we would not be able to examine how [the outcome] reacts differently to [events] based on their position within [a cluster]."

[原始句锚点]: "We selected the Hawkes process model to examine support for clustering for three reasons. First, recall clustering, by definition, contradicts an assumption of Poisson process models that events are random and independent across time (Wooldridge 2010)."

[可迁移性]: 中 — 三理由链（基准排除/副产物价值/下游接口）适用于任何"潜变量承载下游分析"的非常规估计器；常规估计器不需要第三步
[范式排他性]: 高 — 只在估计器产出潜分类/潜状态且该输出被后续检验消费的设计中完整出现
[设计变体]: 潜类别模型（latent class 喂调节检验）、文本机器学习分类（潜类别喂后续回归）可复用第二、三步；第一步的基准排除对象随设计替换（Poisson→线性模型/事件历史）
[与已有语料差异]: 非线性模型.md 变体1/2 的模型选择骨架止步于"DV 性质→估计器"的 because 链；本骨架增加"收敛副产物 + 下游假设接口"两步，覆盖潜变量设计特有的选择论证

### 变体 2: M7 强度方程逐项叙事——背景项/激励项对举 + 参数显著性→假设映射（mukherjee2022 型）

[功能标签]: 让非线性强度方程可读：先按"两项对举"拆解方程（背景项=不受既往事件影响、激励项=本设计的关注项），逐参数给实质解释，再把参数符号与显著性直接映射为假设裁决语言

[骨架]: "In Equation ([N]), the [event rate] consists of two terms. The first term represents the [background rate] for [unit] on [day] and is not influenced by prior [events]. We allow this background rate to vary depending on the covariates described in [Section X]. The second term represents the excitation of the [rate] due to prior [events]. This term differentiates a [self-exciting model] from a [benchmark model] and is used to examine evidence of self-excitation, or [focal phenomenon]. Parameter [theta] captures the excitation of [the rate] because of [a prior event by another unit]. If the excitation parameter [theta] is positive and statistically significant, then the [rate] is statistically higher than the [background rate], indicating support for [the phenomenon] and Hypothesis [1]. An additional feature of the [model] is the excitation decay. ... If this parameter is positive and significant, we can conclude that the excitation of future [events] decays with the time since the [leading event]."

[原始句锚点]: "This term differentiates a Hawkes process model from a Poisson process model and is used to examine evidence of self-excitation, or recall clustering."

[可迁移性]: 高 — 任何"基准项 + 关注项"叠加式非线性方程（强度、hazard、选择方程）都能套用"两项对举 + 逐参数叙事 + 显著性→假设"
[范式排他性]: 中 — 方程叙事通用；"激励衰减参数"叙事为时序依赖设计专属
[设计变体]: 生存模型把第二项换成风险集项；空间计量换成溢出项；叙事结构不变——方程项功能对举 + 参数级假设映射

### 变体 3: M7 迭代估计算法实现段——两步循环 + 收敛判据 + 重抽样标准误 + 附录指针（mukherjee2022 型）

[功能标签]: 把迭代估计器写成可审计的实现段：步名与步功能（E 步做什么分类、M 步算什么目标函数）、循环与收敛判据（目标函数停止改进）、稳健推断手段（重抽样标准误）、正文与附录的分工（机制留附录）

[骨架]: "The [model] uses a [two-step EM] algorithm that is executed iteratively until the model converges ([citation]). In the first step, called the [expectation (E)] step, [events] are classified as [leading, following, or neither] based upon [the time elapsed since the most recent event]. ... In the second step, called the [maximization (M)] step, the [log-likelihood] value is computed using the [classification] from the previous step. ... The two steps are executed iteratively until convergence, when the [log-likelihood] stops improving. We use [bootstrapping] to calculate robust standard errors ([citation]) ... Please refer to the online appendix for a detailed description of the [algorithm] and the [bootstrapping] method."

[原始句锚点]: "The two steps are executed iteratively until convergence, when the log-likelihood stops improving. We use bootstrapping to calculate robust standard errors (Piatti and Trojani 2020) in the estimation of the maximum likelihood in the second step of the EM algorithm."

[可迁移性]: 高 — 任何迭代估计器（EM/Baum-Welch/迭代极值）实现段同构；四件套=步骤命名+收敛判据+推断手段+附录分工
[范式排他性]: 中 — 迭代算法专属；闭式解估计器不需要此段
[设计变体]: MCMC 版替换为 burn-in 与收敛诊断；网格搜索版替换为参数网格与选择准则；步骤命名惯例（expectation/maximization）保留原样以便审稿人对照文献

### 变体 4: M2 共因伪聚类排除漏斗——共享上游输入的事件剔除（mukherjee2022 型）

[功能标签]: 在事件流设计中剔除"共享上游原因"制造的事件，防止把共同冲击误读为跨单位激励；排除规则以 because 从句给出威胁机制 + 第三方数据源 + 剔除数量，构成可审计漏斗步骤

[骨架]: "We collected data for all [events] recorded by [agency] spanning [N] years ([period]). [Event] data include [firm, product, date, size, component, and initiator fields]. [Event] data were combined with [archival firm databases] for firm-level controls. Using the [campaign identifiers] assigned to each [event], we identified [N0] [events] for all [population definition]. Because [clusters] may be artificially created by [units] using the same [shared input] that has a [quality problem], we used [defect and noncompliance reports] to determine whether an [event] was due to [problems at the shared input] ([citation]) and removed [n] [such events]. Our final sample consists of [N] [events] ([n1] [type 1] and [n2] [type 2]) across the [k] [units]."

[原始句锚点]: "Because clusters may be artificially created by firms using the same supplier that has a quality problem, we used defect and noncompliance reports provided by the NHTSA to determine whether a recall was due to quality problems at the supplier (Shah et al. 2017) and removed 675 supplier-related recalls."

[可迁移性]: 高 — 任何"事件计数/聚类检验 + 共享供给方"场景（共享供应商、共同审计师、同一平台）可直接复用
[范式排他性]: 中 — 排除动作通用；排除理由锚定"伪聚类"威胁，与自激励设计共轭
[设计变体]: 与 giannetti2022 的档案事件去重规则（同单位+同根因+同时间戳计一次）互补——去重防重复计数，本骨架防共同原因伪聚类；两者可串联成漏斗
[诚实边界]: 排除依赖第三方报告对根因的分类质量；未验证报告分类误差时不得声称排除完备

### 变体 5: M6 背景率协变量饱和——目的声明 + 非线性中心化 + FE 参照系声明（mukherjee2022 型）

[功能标签]: 在自激励设计中论证"背景率已被协变量充分吸收"：开头声明观测单位与背景率定位，逐协变量给 because（既往研究用于预测事件计数/时点），非线性项给中心化构造顺序（中心化→平方→取对数，避免共线），时间/单位 FE 给参照类别声明——使残差聚类可归因于激励项而非遗漏的背景异质性

[骨架]: "Accordingly, the unit of observation in our analysis is [days], equating to [N] [days] across [period]. We include several covariates that past research has used to predict [event count or timing]. [Ln_X]: [X] represents [definition]. ... we include the natural log of [X] to control for [mechanism], which may be associated with [events] ([citation]). [Ln_X squared]: The inclusion of the square term, combined with [X], is intended to control for potential nonlinear effects of [X] on [events]. ... To avoid excessive collinearity, we centered the [nontransformed] measure, then squared it, and then took the natural log. [Time dummies]: To control for time fixed effects, we include [period] dummies. ... Hence, we incorporate [N-1] [period] fixed effects, with the first [period] serving as the reference ([citation]). [Unit]: Because [units] experience different rates of [events], we include [unit] fixed effects for [k-1] of the [k] [units] and use [unit j] as the reference category ([citation])."

[原始句锚点]: "Because firms experience different rates of recalls, we include firm fixed effects for five of the six firms and use Chrysler as the reference category (Haunschild and Rhee 2004)."

[可迁移性]: 高 — 逐协变量 because + 参照系声明是通用控制变量写法；"饱和背景率"的定位声明使本段与强度模型绑定
[范式排他性]: 中 — 控制变量叙事通用；开头"单位=天，共 N 天"与背景率定位为强度模型专属
[设计变体]: 生存设计把"背景率"换成"基线风险"；计数面板换成"条件均值"；中心化三步构造与参照系声明照搬

**适用**:
- 事件时间戳数据，理论断言事件跨单位非独立（自激励/聚集），需估计激励强度与衰减
- 需要模型收敛输出的潜事件角色分类（leading/following）作为下游检验（如事件研究）的输入
- 事件计数/聚类检验面对共享上游输入（共同供应商/共同平台），需排除伪聚类
**禁忌**:
- 事件相互独立、无时序依赖的计数结果——用非线性模型.md 计数模型族
- 持续时间/风险集/复发事件问题——用事件历史+事件研究.md
- 同伴效应需要工具变量识别因果溢出——本族只做自激励存在性的描述性检验与下游衔接
