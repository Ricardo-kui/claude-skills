## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `两阶段模型` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

- **核心方法**: Control Function Approach / Two-Stage Least Squares (2SLS) / Heckman Selection
- **目的**: 处理内生变量(endogenous regressor)导致的估计偏误
- **第一阶段**: 预测内生变量（通常用OLS/probit/生存分析）
- **第二阶段**: 用第一阶段残差作为控制变量，得到无偏估计
- **外生识别变量**: 必须满足排他性约束(exclusion restriction)——影响第一阶段DV但不影响第二阶段DV
- **适用场景**: 策略性行为、选择性偏差、 simultaneity 等内生性问题

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: Control Function Approach 引入
**来源论文**: Eilert2017 JM
**原始句锚点**: "We account for this endogeneity using the control function approach (Petrin and Train 2010). The logic of this approach is that if there are unobservables that drive the time-to-recall decision, including a control variable in the structural regression model (second stage) reduces the correlation between the endogenous regressor and the error term."
**验证状态**: 通过 (1/3 但高价值)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-19
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> A potential econometric concern for assessing [outcome] is that [endogenous_variable] is endogenous. That is, [actors] are likely to be aware of [consequences] and would therefore (rationally) make decisions about [decision] on the basis of [considerations], some of which may be unobserved. Accordingly, the model needs to account for the endogeneity of [variable] to yield unbiased coefficients. We account for this endogeneity using the control function approach ([citation]) because this approach is better suited for addressing endogeneity for a non-continuous [independent/choice] variable ([citation]). The logic of this approach is that if there are unobservables that drive [variable], including a control variable in the structural regression model (second stage) reduces the correlation between the endogenous regressor and the error term.
>
> The model estimation proceeds in two stages. In the first stage, we model [DV_1_process] using [estimator_1]. In the second stage, we test [DV_2_process]. By including [control_variable] from the first stage, [estimator_2] can be used to generate unbiased coefficients.
**与原骨架差异**: 这是两阶段模型（Control Function / 2SLS）的标准引入结构。关键要素：(1) 内生性威胁的理论论证（为什么DV是策略性的）；(2) Control Function的逻辑解释（残差消除相关性）；(3) 两阶段流程说明。**Pupovac et al. (2026) 升级**：增加了"CF 更适合处理非连续内生变量"的方法选择辩护，为二元/离散选择型内生变量提供了明确的方法论依据。与IV-2SLS的区别：Control Function直接在第二阶段加入残差，而2SLS用预测值替代内生变量。

### 变体 2: 外生识别变量六步论证链
**来源论文**: Eilert2017 JM
**原始句锚点**: "The exogeneity of past publicity stems from the efficient market hypothesis, which argues that the effects of past recalls that were publicized should already be incorporated in the stock prices. Thus, past publicity is not "news" per se and should not elicit any reaction from stock markets."
**验证状态**: 通过 (1/3 但高价值)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-19
**槽位**: M6 (特殊控制变量)
**骨架**:
> [Variable] is the exogenous variable that identifies the system. From a theoretical perspective, [variable] is exogenous because although it is likely to influence [DV_1], it should not be related to [DV_2] in the second stage. The exogeneity of [variable] stems from [theoretical_framework], which argues that [mechanism]. Thus, [variable] is not "news" per se and should not elicit any reaction from [outcome]. To understand the relevance of [variable] for [DV_1], we estimated an alternative specification that excludes [variable] from the model. The [fit_statistic] for this alternative model is [value], which is [comparison] the model that includes [variable] ([value]). Thus, [variable] improves the model fit and does a good job of predicting [DV_1].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是**两阶段模型/IV研究中最精彩的骨架之一**，完整的六步论证链：(1) 声明识别变量；(2) 理论论证外生性（为什么影响DV1但不影响DV2）；(3) 理论框架支撑；(4) 排除规格检验（从模型中排除该变量）；(5) 模型拟合比较（BIC/AIC/F统计量）；(6) 结论（improves model fit + predicts DV1 well）。该骨架可迁移至任何需要论证工具变量/识别变量外生性的研究（IV、2SLS、Control Function、Heckman）。

### 变体 3: M7/M8 Heckman 选择模型 + 同行 Prevalence 排他性限制 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Chung, Low & Rust (2022, JAMS)
**原始句锚点**: "This suggests that a selection bias may arise in our analysis if we do not account for the non-random selection of firms with and without CMOs. Therefore, we use a Heckman selection model to address the selection bias when we test our hypotheses (Nath & Mahajan, 2011)."
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M7/M8
**骨架**:
> Not all firms have a [role]. In our sample before requiring non-missing values for all the key variables, about [percentage]% of the [unit-years] have a [role], similar to the statistic reported by [citation]. In un-tabulated results, we find that firms with [role] differ from firms without [role] in terms of [characteristic 1], [characteristic 2], and [characteristic 3]. This suggests that a selection bias may arise if we do not account for the non-random selection of firms with and without [role]. Therefore, we use a Heckman selection model to address the selection bias when we test our hypotheses ([citation]). As an exclusion restriction in the Heckman selection model, we follow [citation] and use the proportion of peer firms that have a [role] in their [organizational unit]. We obtain the primary and secondary [industry segments] in which each focal firm operates from [data source]. We focus on industries defined at the [two-digit SIC / NAICS] level. We then find the peer firms that operate in each of the industry segments of the focal firm. The exclusion restriction, [Peer Prevalence], is defined as the proportion of peer firms (excluding the focal firm) in each industry segment with a [role]. In cases where the firm operates in multiple segments, we calculate a weighted average prevalence across segments by using the number of peer firms in each industry as weights.
**与原骨架差异**: Heckman 选择模型中同行 prevalence 作为排他性限制的完整段落。关键要素：(1) 选择偏差诊断（有/无 [role] 的公司在可观测特征上是否不同）；(2) 同行 prevalence 的定义和跨 segments 加权；(3) 模仿/制度同形机制作为排除限制的理论基础。与 `micro-templates/heckman-peer-prevalence-exclusion.md` 配套使用。
**诚实边界**: 必须论证 peer prevalence 影响 [role] 存在但不直接影响 [DV]；多 segments 加权的目的是扩大同伴池、减少直接竞争导致的间接渠道；第二阶段必须报告 inverse Mills ratio / rho。
**跨 skill 对齐**: `../write-results/econometric-models/OLS-FE.md` 变体24（R2 Heckman 两阶段表格导航 — 第一阶段 Table 3 → 第二阶段 Columns 1-4）。

### 变体 4: M8 控制函数法识别变量：基于有效市场假说的排除限制
**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (POM)
**原始句锚点**: "We reason that our excluded variable is likely relevant—that is, it is associated with the potentially endogenous variable of disclosure decision. Further, we reason that our instrument meets the exclusion restriction—that is, it is uncorrelated with unobserved determinants of the shareholders' reactions."
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇高价值，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> We reason that our excluded variable is likely relevant—that is, it is associated with [endogenous_variable]. [Theory of mechanism]: [actors] lack information about [unit]. [External factor] attenuates this information asymmetry (e.g., [citation_1]; [citation_2]). Therefore, all else equal, a [unit] covered by [external factor] has less incentive to reduce [actors]' information asymmetry by [action]. Conversely, a [unit] that lacks [external factor] has more reason to [action]. Therefore, we expect [excluded_variable] to be [negatively/positively] associated with [endogenous_variable].
>
> Further, we reason that our instrument meets the exclusion restriction—that is, it is uncorrelated with unobserved determinants of [DV]. We reason so because [excluded_variable] occurred before [event]. Thus, [theory] suggests that [excluded_variable] is known to [actors] and factored into [outcome] before [event].
**与原骨架差异**: 与 Eilert2017 变体2 的"外生识别变量六步论证链"互补：本变体专门处理**事件发生前已公开的信息作为CF识别变量**的排除限制。核心逻辑：(1) 相关性通过信息不对称理论建立；(2) 排除限制通过有效市场假说/信息已提前纳入价格建立。这是事件研究+CF组合设计中的关键说服单元。
**诚实边界**: "信息已纳入价格"是强假设；若市场对 [external factor] 的反应在 [event] 前并不充分，或 [external factor] 与 [event] 相关（如媒体同时报道召回与供应商），则排除限制受威胁。需在M8报告替代事件日期的稳健性。

### 变体 5: M8 Heckman选择模型：总部所在地作为排除限制
**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (POM)
**原始句锚点**: "The first-stage regression requires an excluded variable that affects the supplier's decision variable but not the outcome variable, shareholders' reactions. Our exclusion variable equals 1 if the supplier is headquartered in the United States or Canada, and 0 otherwise."
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇高价值，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> The first-stage regression requires an excluded variable that affects [selection_decision] but not [outcome]. Our exclusion variable equals 1 if [unit] is headquartered in [region], and 0 otherwise. Prior research has reasoned that [region]'s [market/institutional feature] is more [developed/mature] than those of other countries. Therefore, [actors] scrutinize [units] headquartered in [region] less than they scrutinize [units] headquartered elsewhere ([citation_1]; [citation_2]). By extension, [units] headquartered outside [region] have a greater incentive to [disclose/action], limiting [actors]' perceived information disadvantage and promoting market transparency ([citation_3]; [citation_4]). Therefore, [excluded_variable] should be associated with [selection_decision], thereby meeting the relevance criterion.
>
> Further, [excluded_variable] should not be associated with the error term of [outcome] model for two reasons. First, [decision_to_change_variable] is a resource-demanding process. Thus, managers cannot easily make such a decision and implement it. Second, [variable] should not be of primary concern for [actors] during [event]. Thus, on average, [event] should not cause [actors] to assess [unit]'s future performance based on [variable]. We present results with and without Heckman's correction.
**与原骨架差异**: 与 Chung 变体3 的"同行 prevalence 排除限制"形成对照：本变体提供**制度/地理位置型排除限制**的完整论证。关键结构：(1) 区域制度成熟度→披露激励（相关性）；(2) 变量难以改变 + 非事件关注焦点（排除限制）。适用于Heckman选择模型中无法找到同行prevalence时的替代方案。
**诚实边界**: 总部所在地可能与未观测的公司质量、国际化程度或投资者基础相关，从而间接影响 [outcome]。必须控制这些混淆因素，并在稳健性中排除总部高度集中在某区域的样本。

### 变体 6: M7/M8 多内生性威胁—修正方法配对账本

**来源论文**: Moon, Tuli & Mukherjee (2023, *Journal of Marketing*)
**原始句锚点**: "Therefore, we use the method of two-stage residual inclusion, a special case of the control function method for binary regressors, to address the potential endogeneity of disclosure of advertising spending (e.g., Terza, Basu, and Rathouz 2008). Similarly, we estimate a probit model to generate the inverse Mills ratio to account for the potential selection bias due to Kantar Media's coverage of firms (Frennea, Han, and Mittal 2019)."
**验证状态**: 单篇高价值 reference-level 变体，待跨论文验证
**写入日期**: 2026-08-03
**槽位**: M7/M8
**骨架**:
> Our system contains [N] distinct identification threats. First, [binary strategic choice] may be endogenous because [managerial foresight/self-selection]; we use [two-stage residual inclusion/control function for a binary regressor]. Second, [continuous covariate] may be strategically chosen; we include its [control-function correction]. Third, [mediator] may share omitted causes with [outcome]; we add a separate [mediator control-function term]. Fourth, [data-provider/sample coverage] may induce selection; we estimate a selection equation and include the [inverse Mills ratio]. Each correction term enters only the structural equation exposed to its corresponding threat. We report the instrument or exclusion source, first-stage diagnostics, and coefficient for every correction term.

**与原骨架差异**: 现有变体多处理单一 control function 或单一 Heckman selection。本变体先建立**威胁账本**，再把 binary choice、continuous regressor、endogenous mediator 与 coverage selection 分别映射到 2SRI、control function、额外 mediator correction 与 IMR，避免多个修正项在长公式中失去解释。

**诚实边界**: Correction terms 齐全不等于识别有效；每个修正项必须有独立且可辩护的识别来源。不得用同一个缺乏排除限制的 peer mean 同时“解决”所有内生变量而不讨论共同冲击。若某一 correction term 不显著，仍需解释保留或删除它的规则。

**适用**: 同一系统含战略二元选择、连续投入、中介变量和非随机数据覆盖的观察性面板研究。

### 变体 7: M8 结构需求多内生变量账本 — 价格 BLP-IV + 广告截距/斜率 CF + 跨品类媒体 IV (1篇高价值)

**来源论文**: Liu & Shankar 2015 (Management Science)
**原始句锚点**: "To account for the endogeneity of price, we follow BLP (1995) and use the classic instrumental variable estimation techniques developed for the random coefficient model. Following BLP (1995), Sudhir (2001), and Balachander et al. (2009), we use functions of own and competitors' product characteristics as instruments."

**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇；扩展变体6 至 BLP/结构需求场景）

**槽位**: M8

**骨架**:
> Price and advertising are endogenous because firms adjust [price/ad spending] in anticipation of demand shocks unobserved to the researcher (e.g., [facelift/new design/private information about advertising effectiveness]). We address price endogeneity following [BLP 1995; Sudhir 2001]: instruments are functions of own and competitors' [product characteristics] computed within two similarity subsets defined by [country of origin × segment] and [design class × segment], taking within-firm and across-firm sums of characteristics such as [MPG, horsepower/weight]. For advertising, we use the control function approach ([Petrin & Train 2010; Luan & Sudhir 2010]) for both intercept and slope endogeneity: instruments include (1) [media-specific unit costs] in adjacent categories that are common across brands but vary over time, and (2) the same characteristic-based instruments used for price. Media coverage of [events] may be endogenous because newsworthy events attract attention correlated with consumer response; we correct it with a control function using instruments based on [parent-brand media coverage of recent recalls in other product categories], plausibly correlated with sedan coverage but with limited focal-category demand impact after [~13 months]. Event-level controls [severity, prior quality] absorb residual event-specific correlation between media and demand.

**与原骨架差异**: Moon 变体6 为通用威胁账本；本变体绑定 **BLP 特征 IV 双相似性子集构造** + **广告双端 CF（截距+斜率）** + **跨品类滞后召回媒体 IV**，并显式要求 event-level severity/quality 作为 media 识别的补充控制。结构需求/IO Marketing 论文可整段迁移。

**诚实边界**: 跨品类媒体 IV 在 parent-brand 层面，无法排除同学科 unobserved event factors；characteristic IV 假设短期不可改产品特征；广告成本 proxy 来自 adjacent categories 需论证可传递性。

### 变体 8: M8 内生性"balancing act"诊断先行叙事 — RIR 数量化 → 治愈第二 → naïve/cured 配对（2026-08-12）

**来源论文**: Ridge, Hill, Ingram, Kolomeitsev & Worrell 2024 (*Academy of Management Journal*)
**原始句锚点**: "Addressing endogeneity "involves a balancing act" (Semadeni et al., 2014: 1070) for two related reasons. First, the effect of endogeneity can be inflationary or suppressive... Second, tools to "cure" any bias can introduce inflationary or suppressive biases themselves."（英文原句——源论文为英文，非中文锚点）
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇；`section_variant`）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-12
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> A concern in testing how [actor characteristics] like [construct] affect [firm-level outcomes] is endogeneity, especially from [nonrandom mechanism], which may mean [actor characteristics] are not randomly distributed across [units]. Addressing endogeneity "involves a balancing act" ([citation]) for two related reasons. First, the effect of endogeneity can be inflationary or suppressive, meaning the coefficients are just as likely to be biased upward as downward, but there is no method to predict the direction. Second, tools to "cure" any bias can introduce inflationary or suppressive biases themselves, and thus may be ill-advised if the concern is not a strong threat. We therefore follow best practices to diagnose potential endogeneity and assess robustness across analyses. Specifically, we first use the robustness of inference to replacement (RIR). RIR results suggest that to alter our inferences for [DV1], [N1] observations would have to be replaced with observations for which there is an effect of zero, and for [DV2], [N2] observations would have to be replaced. Collectively, the large number of observations that must be replaced by zero to alter inferences suggests that our results are likely broadly robust to concerns of endogeneity. Next, though RIR suggests endogeneity is unlikely to bias our estimates, we follow [citations] to conduct robustness tests by pairing so-called "naive" analyses that do not use tools to "cure" endogeneity with analyses that do attempt such a cure.
