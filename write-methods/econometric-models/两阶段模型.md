# 两阶段模型 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 填空段落骨架 → `两阶段模型`。

## 设计特征摘要

- **核心方法**: Control Function Approach / Two-Stage Least Squares (2SLS) / Heckman Selection
- **目的**: 处理内生变量(endogenous regressor)导致的估计偏误
- **第一阶段**: 预测内生变量（通常用OLS/probit/生存分析）
- **第二阶段**: 用第一阶段残差作为控制变量，得到无偏估计
- **外生识别变量**: 必须满足排他性约束(exclusion restriction)——影响第一阶段DV但不影响第二阶段DV
- **适用场景**: 策略性行为、选择性偏差、 simultaneity 等内生性问题

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: Control Function Approach 引入
**来源论文**: Eilert2017 JM
**验证状态**: 通过 (1/3 但高价值)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-19
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> A potential econometric concern for assessing [outcome] is that [endogenous_variable] is endogenous. That is, [actors] are likely to be aware of [consequences] and would therefore (rationally) make decisions about [decision] on the basis of [considerations], some of which may be unobserved. Accordingly, the model needs to account for the endogeneity of [variable] to yield unbiased coefficients. We account for this endogeneity using the control function approach ([citation]). The logic of this approach is that if there are unobservables that drive [variable], including a control variable in the structural regression model (second stage) reduces the correlation between the endogenous regressor and the error term.
>
> The model estimation proceeds in two stages. In the first stage, we model [DV_1_process] using [estimator_1]. In the second stage, we test [DV_2_process]. By including [control_variable] from the first stage, [estimator_2] can be used to generate unbiased coefficients.
**与原骨架差异**: 这是两阶段模型（Control Function / 2SLS）的标准引入结构。关键要素：(1) 内生性威胁的理论论证（为什么DV是策略性的）；(2) Control Function的逻辑解释（残差消除相关性）；(3) 两阶段流程说明。与IV-2SLS的区别：Control Function直接在第二阶段加入残差，而2SLS用预测值替代内生变量。

### 变体 2: 外生识别变量六步论证链
**来源论文**: Eilert2017 JM
**验证状态**: 通过 (1/3 但高价值)
**写入日期**: 2026-05-19
**槽位**: M6 (特殊控制变量)
**骨架**:
> [Variable] is the exogenous variable that identifies the system. From a theoretical perspective, [variable] is exogenous because although it is likely to influence [DV_1], it should not be related to [DV_2] in the second stage. The exogeneity of [variable] stems from [theoretical_framework], which argues that [mechanism]. Thus, [variable] is not "news" per se and should not elicit any reaction from [outcome]. To understand the relevance of [variable] for [DV_1], we estimated an alternative specification that excludes [variable] from the model. The [fit_statistic] for this alternative model is [value], which is [comparison] the model that includes [variable] ([value]). Thus, [variable] improves the model fit and does a good job of predicting [DV_1].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是**两阶段模型/IV研究中最精彩的骨架之一**，完整的六步论证链：(1) 声明识别变量；(2) 理论论证外生性（为什么影响DV1但不影响DV2）；(3) 理论框架支撑；(4) 排除规格检验（从模型中排除该变量）；(5) 模型拟合比较（BIC/AIC/F统计量）；(6) 结论（improves model fit + predicts DV1 well）。该骨架可迁移至任何需要论证工具变量/识别变量外生性的研究（IV、2SLS、Control Function、Heckman）。

### 变体 3: M7/M8 Heckman 选择模型 + 同行 Prevalence 排他性限制 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Chung, Low & Rust (2022, JAMS)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M7/M8
**骨架**:
> Not all firms have a [role]. In our sample before requiring non-missing values for all the key variables, about [percentage]% of the [unit-years] have a [role], similar to the statistic reported by [citation]. In un-tabulated results, we find that firms with [role] differ from firms without [role] in terms of [characteristic 1], [characteristic 2], and [characteristic 3]. This suggests that a selection bias may arise if we do not account for the non-random selection of firms with and without [role]. Therefore, we use a Heckman selection model to address the selection bias when we test our hypotheses ([citation]). As an exclusion restriction in the Heckman selection model, we follow [citation] and use the proportion of peer firms that have a [role] in their [organizational unit]. We obtain the primary and secondary [industry segments] in which each focal firm operates from [data source]. We focus on industries defined at the [two-digit SIC / NAICS] level. We then find the peer firms that operate in each of the industry segments of the focal firm. The exclusion restriction, [Peer Prevalence], is defined as the proportion of peer firms (excluding the focal firm) in each industry segment with a [role]. In cases where the firm operates in multiple segments, we calculate a weighted average prevalence across segments by using the number of peer firms in each industry as weights.
**与原骨架差异**: Heckman 选择模型中同行 prevalence 作为排他性限制的完整段落。关键要素：(1) 选择偏差诊断（有/无 [role] 的公司在可观测特征上是否不同）；(2) 同行 prevalence 的定义和跨 segments 加权；(3) 模仿/制度同形机制作为排除限制的理论基础。与 `micro-templates/heckman-peer-prevalence-exclusion.md` 配套使用。
**诚实边界**: 必须论证 peer prevalence 影响 [role] 存在但不直接影响 [DV]；多 segments 加权的目的是扩大同伴池、减少直接竞争导致的间接渠道；第二阶段必须报告 inverse Mills ratio / rho。
