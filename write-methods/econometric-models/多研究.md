# 多研究 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `多研究` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

多研究实验设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**研究梯度的可读性**：读者必须一眼看出每个研究回答什么问题、与前一个研究的关系、以及为何不能合并为一个研究。典型结构包括：(1) 项目总览段落；(2) 每个研究的独立 Methods（参与者→材料/程序→操纵→操纵检验→测量）；(3) 跨研究一致性与变异性的明确说明。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: 多研究实验项目总览（研究梯度图）
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M1/M15
**骨架**:
> We conducted [number] experimental studies to examine [core relationship]. Study [1] tests [hypothesis] with [audience 1] using [stimulus type] and measures [DV]. Study [2] replicates the test with [audience 2] to examine [boundary]. Study [3] extends the findings to [new stimulus/DV] and explores [mechanism]. Study [4] confirms the pattern with [audience/stimulus/DV]. Across studies, [manipulation] is held constant while [audience/stimulus/DV] varies, allowing us to assess the robustness and boundary conditions of [theoretical claim].
**与原骨架差异**: 多研究实验必须在 Methods 开头给出**研究梯度图**，不是简单罗列"Study 1... Study 2..."，而要说明每个研究的 unique contribution 和变异维度。关键：明确什么保持不变（manipulation）、什么变化（audience/stimulus/DV）、以及变化的目的。

### 变体 2: 操纵检验 Pilot Study 段落（嵌入主 Methods）
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M4/M8
**骨架**:
> To verify that our manipulation of [IV] produced the intended difference in [perceived construct], we conducted a pilot study with [N] participants recruited from [source]. Participants were randomly assigned to [condition A] or [condition B] and asked to rate the [stimulus] on [measure]. We excluded participants who [attention-check rule]. A one-way ANOVA confirmed that participants in [condition A] perceived the [stimulus] as significantly more [construct] (M = [value], SD = [value]) than those in [condition B] (M = [value], SD = [value]), F([df1], [df2]) = [value], p [relation] [threshold]. We therefore concluded that the manipulation was effective and used it in the main studies.
**与原骨架差异**: 操纵检验可以作为独立 pilot study 嵌入 Methods，而不是放在附录。本骨架强制包含：(1) pilot 目的；(2) 样本与排除规则；(3) 测量；(4) ANOVA 结果（M/SD/F/p）；(5) 结论。关键：让读者相信主实验的 IV 确实操作化了 intended construal。

### 变体 3: M2 混合方法多研究设计的情境+数据源衔接 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Bamberger, Homburg & Wielgos 2021 (Journal of Marketing)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M2
**骨架**:
> We conducted [N] complementary studies using different data sources and methods. Study 1 uses [data_type_A] from [N_A] [units/respondents] in [setting_A] to test [Hypotheses_set_A]. [Key design feature: e.g., key informant survey with B2B firms]. Study 2 extends these findings by using [data_type_B] — a [N_B]-[unit] panel of [industry_B] [units] over [period] — to replicate Study 1's core relationships and extend the analysis to [new DV/scope]. This multi-study design allows us to [triangulate / test robustness / extend generalizability] while leveraging the complementary strengths of [method_A: internal/measurement precision] and [method_B: external validity/longitudinal scope].

### 变体 4: M9 三研究递进设计论证 — 内部效度→概念复制→生态效度 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M9
**骨架**:
> We conducted three complementary studies to examine [research question]. Specifically, we conducted [design_type_1] (Study 1) with [sample_description]. [Design_type_1_advantages: e.g., Experiments are quite common in... research because... Random assignment equates the different experimental conditions...]. In these studies, we manipulated [IV] and [moderator]. The measured dependent variables were [DV_list]. Study 2 was similar to Study 1, except that we operationalized [key_variable] differently ([replication_type: conceptual replication]). In Study 3, we used [design_type_3] to directly test [theoretical_relationship], demonstrating the [validity_type: ecological validity] of our model at the [level: market level].
**与原骨架差异**: 本骨架与变体1（Falchetti 四研究梯度图）和变体3（Bamberger 混合方法衔接）互补——Li et al. 提供了**内部效度→概念复制→生态效度**的三研究递进叙事：(1) 实验1 建立内部效度（随机分配排除混淆）；(2) 实验2 概念复制——"same design except operationalization"（将字面死亡词替换为伪死亡词），测试跨操作化稳健性；(3) 现场面板研究3 建立生态效度——将实验发现的机制推广到真实市场反应。关键策略：每个研究的设计选择都有显式的方法论理由（"experiments avoid endogeneity" / "conceptual replication" / "ecological validity at the market level"），而非仅罗列三个研究。名称标签（internal validity / conceptual replication / ecological validity）让审稿人一眼看懂研究梯度的逻辑。
**诚实边界**: 研究梯度的命名标签（如"conceptual replication"、"ecological validity"）必须与设计实质匹配——若 Study 2 仅改变样本而非操作化，应称为"direct replication"而非"conceptual replication"。
### 变体 5: M9 Empirical Plan 因果阶段化预告段 — foundation->effect->process->intervention (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ilicic & Brennan 2026 (Journal of Marketing)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-22
**槽位**: M9
**骨架**:
> We report on [N] studies that utilize multiple methodologies, [data_type_examples]. To illustrate our argument that [core relationship], we first conducted [N_pilot] pilot studies analyzing [real-world data source] in terms of [phenomenon signals]; the results support our assertion, with [aggregate unit] reporting higher [DV signal] ([appendix location]). Building on this foundation, Study [1] tests [main effect] using [data/setting]. Study [2] extends generalizability by [new data/domain], demonstrating that the effect [does not apply to non-target category]. Study [3] manipulates [IV] and examines its causal impact through [incentive-compatible design]. Study [4] tests the psychological process and explores alternative explanations using both measurement of process and moderation of process designs. Having established the effect and its underlying process, we next investigated whether [targeted intervention] could attenuate [the effect]. To provide preliminary evidence, we first conducted a pilot study examining [real-world intervention/campaign] ([appendix location]). Building on this, Study [5] tests the moderating role of [moderator], while Supplementary Studies extend the analysis to [other threat/product types].
**与原骨架差异**: 本骨架与变体4（Li 2026 internal->replication->ecological validity **方法论效度阶梯**）正交——本变体是**理论因果阶段阶梯**：用因果递进短语（"Building on this foundation" / "Having established the effect and its underlying process, we next investigated whether..."）把每个研究锚定到理论论证链条的因果环节（建立现象->主效应->因果->机制->干预），而非方法论效度环节。两个 corpus 缺失的导航工具：(1) **foundation pilot**（现象建立型 pilot）——用 archival/field 真实数据建立现象存在性，区别于变体2 的操纵检验 pilot（验证 IV 工作）；(2) **"Having established... we next investigated whether..." 因果阶段转折句**——让审稿人一眼看到研究在论证链上的位置。适用于 consumer psychology / 干预导向的多研究论文（5+ 研究）；management/strategy 单一主假设论文较少使用。
**诚实边界**: 因果阶段标签必须与设计实质匹配——若 Study 3 仅改变样本而非操纵 IV，不能称"establishes causal impact"。foundation pilot 的现象证据（如州级相关）只能支持"现象值得研究"，不能替代实验因果。

### 变体 6: M2/M9 同一 IV 跨决策阶段管道 — 各阶段不同分析单元 + 条件性样本递减 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Kim & Lee 2026 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-22
**槽位**: M2/M9
**骨架**:
> [Framework citation] characterizes [phenomenon] in [N] sequential stages: [stage_1], [stage_2], [stage_3]. Our empirical approach uses distinct units of analysis across stages. For the [stage_1] stage, our sample consists of all possible [decider-option combinations], including both actual [choices] and counterfactual pairs in which a [decider] did not [choose]. For the [stage_2] stage, our sample consists of the [decider-option pairs] defined by all observed [offers]. In some analyses, we take a within-[decider] approach limited to the subsample of [deciders] who received multiple [options], thus allowing us to directly observe how the same [decider] chooses between different [options] ([within-decider design citation]). For the [stage_3] stage, our sample is at the [employee/survivor] level. Because later stages are observed only for [deciders] who advanced through earlier stages, sample size conditionally narrows across the pipeline ([stage_1 N] -> [stage_2 N] -> [stage_3 N]).
**与原骨架差异**: 区别于变体1-5（每研究独立样本的 cross-study 梯度/效度阶梯），本变体是**同一决策者池跨序贯决策阶段的管道**：(a) 不是独立研究而是同一数据的多阶段切片；(b) 每阶段不同分析单元（pairs/offers/employees）；(c) 样本因后期阶段条件性递减（post-treatment attrition）。适用于 strategic human capital/招聘/客户漏斗/组织社会化等"同一 IV 跨决策阶段"设计（Schneider ASA、funnel 模型）。
**诚实边界**: 必须声明 post-treatment 样本递减限制跨阶段比较的内部效度——不同阶段估计量来自非随机子样本。within-decider FE 比较仅适用于有多选项的子样本，drop singletons 后外部效度更窄。配套 write-results 见 OLS-FE.md 变体27（多阶段管道衰减 profile）。

### 变体 7: M9 同一理论模型跨 facet-DV 双研究复制（商业价值型 vs 根本性创新型）(2026-07-30)
**来源论文**: Zhou, Gao & Zhao (2017, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇，待第二篇交叉验证）
**槽位**: M9
**骨架**:
> "In Study [1], we measured [outcome] as [DV_1 — e.g., new product output ratio], which captures [one facet, e.g., the commercial value of product innovation]. To examine the impact of [IV] on [a different facet, e.g., more fundamental and revolutionary innovation], we conducted Study [2] using [DV_2 — e.g., patent count] as the [outcome] indicator to analyze data on [a different sample, e.g., publicly listed firms]. We retrieved archival data of [sample 2] from [database A] and [database B] ([years]). We restricted our samples to [scope] and merged them with [supplementary data—e.g., patent information from SIPO/USPTO]. After deleting firms with incomplete information, we obtained [a balanced panel of N firms / M firm-year observations]. We employed [estimator_2, e.g., Poisson] [to deal with the count nature of DV_2] ([citations])."
**与原骨架差异**: 区别变体 1–6（cross-study 独立样本梯度 / 效度阶梯 / 决策阶段管道）——本变体是**同一理论模型跨两个捕捉不同 facet 的 DV** 的复制：Study 1 用 [商业价值型 DV]（new product ratio，Tobit），Study 2 用 [根本性创新型 DV]（patent，Poisson）。核心逻辑：一个现象有多个 facet（commercial vs fundamental innovation），单一 DV 只捕捉一面；跨 facet 复制既验证稳健性（核心假设在两个 facet 都成立）又扩展构念边界（揭示 facet-specific 差异，如某资源假设只在商业 facet 成立）。两个 study 常配不同样本（非上市 vs 上市）与不同估计器（由 DV 性质决定：corner-solution 用 Tobit、count 用 Poisson）。
**适用**: 构念多维、单一 DV 不能完整捕捉的研究——innovation（product vs patent）、performance（accounting vs market）、commitment（attitudinal vs behavioral）、quality（perceived vs objective）。
**禁忌**: 两 DV 必须真正捕捉**不同 facet**（不是同一 facet 的替代测量，否则只是稳健性而非复制）；跨 study 的估计器差异须由 DV 性质（corner/count/continuous）而非数据可得性驱动；核心假设在两 study 的收敛/发散须在 Results 给出理论解释（见 write-results 多研究 变体6）。
