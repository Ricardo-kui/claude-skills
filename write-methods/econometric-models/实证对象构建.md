# 实证对象构建 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `实证对象构建` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

实证对象构建指核心分析变量无法直接从数据库字段读取，需研究者从原始痕迹（文本概率向量、行为日志、排名数据）**理论化地构建**出分析对象——典型如"成功参照组""相似度/重叠度""距离"。这类 DV 的 Methods 叙事核心是**每一步构建选择的 because 链**：阈值为何取此值、参照组如何定义、相似度用何种度量、为何这种构建对应理论构念。Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (OS) 的 peripheral function overlap（DV2）是黄金范文：1/N 阈值理论化 + grossing-rank 倒数代理月度收入 + 75th percentile 定义成功 + 平均 pairwise Jaccard 相似度 + intersection/union 集合隐喻。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M2 构建步骤可审计、M3 阈值与参照组 because、M7 度量公式化。常见薄弱点：阈值任意化（无理论支撑）、参照组定义含糊、相似度度量无公式。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M3 相似度/重叠度 DV 构建（阈值理论化 + 参照组操作化 + 度量公式化）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> Our [second] dependent variable is [DV_name], which captures the extent to which a [unit] [differentiates from / imitates] existing [successful units] sharing the same [core construct]. [Peripheral constructs] are defined as [meaningful noncore constructs] in a [unit]. By design of the [model], all [constructs] have a [positive weight], but not every [noncore construct] is equally important. As [weights add up to one], and there are [K] [constructs] in total, we consider [weights more than 1/K] as meaningful and count a particular [construct] as [peripheral] when its weight is [less than the core construct weight and more than 1/K].
>
> To identify [successful units], we utilize the [performance metric] in the [category] to measure performance. In the [platform], data on [revenues] are not publicly available, but [platform operator] discloses [ranking lists] by [metric_1] and by [metric_2], within and across [categories]. [Units] that rank higher on the lists offer clear evidence of [performance superiority] ([citation]). Following prior research, we calculate the [reciprocal of the daily rank] in the [grossing list] for each [unit] as a proxy for [daily revenue] and calculate the [monthly revenue proxy] by aggregating these [reciprocals] over a particular [month] ([citation]). We define [successful units] as those above the [75th percentile] in the [monthly revenue proxy] within the [core construct], in the [time period] [N months] prior to the [focal unit entry].
>
> [DV_name] is calculated as the [average pairwise Jaccard similarity] between the [focal unit] and all [successful units]. For both the [focal unit] and a [successful unit], we find the [sets of peripheral constructs] they incorporate in support of the [core construct]. [Jaccard similarity] is calculated by the [intersection] of the two [sets] divided by the [union] of the two [sets] (J(A,B) = (A∩B)/(A∪B)). The [intersection] reflects the [overlap] between the [units], and the [union] captures the [total space] incorporated by both [units].
**与原骨架差异**: 实证对象构建 DV 的三层 because 链，每一层都有理论支撑：(1) **阈值理论化**——peripheral 定义为 weight ∈ (1/K, core_weight)，用 1/K（均匀分布期望）作 cutoff，非任意值；(2) **参照组操作化**——成功 = grossing rank 倒数 → 月度聚合 → 75th percentile，把不可得的 revenue 用排名代理并明确公式；(3) **相似度公式化**——Jaccard + intersection/union 集合隐喻，让读者直观理解"重叠=交集，总空间=并集"。关键：**阈值、参照组、度量三者都必须有 because，且相似度必须给公式 + 集合解释**。可迁移到任何"模仿/差异化/重叠度" DV；度量可替换为 cosine / Euclidean / Jensen-Shannon，参照组可替换为 top-10 / 存活超 N 年。

### 变体 2: M2 监管产品竞争组构建 — 从审批路径到药理机制分类 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M2
**骨架**:
> To measure [product competition], we began by selecting all [regulator]-approved [products] from [directory]. We specifically identified [products] approved under [regulatory clauses] with the aid of [reference source]. [Clause A] governs [product type A], while [clause B] governs [product type B]. Under these pathways, the timeline for most new [product] launches is reduced to approximately [time]. We then collected information on each [product]'s [attributes] from [N] specialized databases: [source 1], [source 2], [source 3], and [source 4] ([citation]). A researcher in [discipline] who was not part of our team used this information to help classify all listed [products] into competing groups. For example, [example A] and [example B] are considered direct competitors in [market], since they use the same [mechanism]. We then linked the competing [products] to their producers and market introduction dates with the aid of [source].
**与原骨架差异**: 复杂实证对象构建的典范——从监管数据库出发，通过审批路径缩小范围，再用药理机制定义"竞争组"。关键要素：(1) 监管路径的法律依据；(2) 多专业数据库交叉验证；(3) 外部专家独立分类（避免研究者偏差）；(4) 具体例子说明竞争组定义。行业特殊性较强，但作为该设计类型的首个示例具有高价值。
**诚实边界**: 监管路径和药理机制分类可能随时间和监管实践变化，需报告分类的时间窗口和一致性检验；外部专家分类需说明编码者间一致性。

### 变体 3: M4 连续相似度指数构建 — 计数比率公式 + 平滑常数 + 归一化 + 数值示例三连 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Du & Tsolmon 2024 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-25
**槽位**: M4（自变量操作化，当自变量是构建的相似度指数时）
**骨架**:
> We measure [similarity construct] at the [unit] level and construct a continuous variable for each [pair] of the degree to which the [unit A] and [unit B] have similar [attribute]. We measure the degree of [attribute] as [countable operationalization] ([citation]). These [units] represent [theoretical meaning], and therefore can be compared across [units]. We calculate the [similarity index] using the formula: 1 − |[A_count] − [B_count]| / ([A_count] + [B_count]), where we scale the counts by adding [small constant] to each to incorporate cases of zero counts while preserving the scale. The normalized index ranges from zero to one, where zero indicates completely dissimilar [attribute], and one indicates identical [attribute]. [Numerical example 1: identical → index = 1.0]. [Numerical example 2: moderate → index = 0.6]. [Numerical example 3: extreme → index → 0]. The sample mean of the index is [value]. We also construct a binary measure based on [median/median-split criterion] as an alternative measure in robustness checks.
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体1（Jaccard 离散集合相似度）不同构——本变体是**连续计数比率型**相似度，适用于"两主体在某个可计数属性上的接近程度"（如结构单元数、规模、资源量）。核心要素：(1) 底层 countable 操作化 + because（"represent the lowest level of profit-center responsibility, and therefore can be compared across firms"）；(2) 1−|A−B|/(A+B) 公式（Jaccard 之外的比率替代）；(3) 平滑常数处理零值（类比 ln 加小常数）；(4) 0-1 归一化语义明确；(5) **三个数值示例**（identical=1.0 / moderate=0.6 / extreme→0）让读者直观校准；(6) 报告样本均值；(7) 附 binary 替代版本作稳健性。可迁移到任何"两主体连续接近度"构念（地理距离倒数、规模相似度、资源相似度、经验相似度）。
**诚实边界**: 平滑常数选择需论证或做敏感性检验（本文未做——见反模式）；公式对称性（|A−B| 不区分 A vs B 方向）需确认与理论一致（若理论上 A 对 B 与 B 对 A 作用不对称，此公式不适用）；countable 操作化的跨单元可比性必须论证。
