## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `实证对象构建` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

实证对象构建指核心分析变量无法直接从数据库字段读取，需研究者从原始痕迹（文本概率向量、行为日志、排名数据）**理论化地构建**出分析对象——典型如"成功参照组""相似度/重叠度""距离"。这类 DV 的 Methods 叙事核心是**每一步构建选择的 because 链**：阈值为何取此值、参照组如何定义、相似度用何种度量、为何这种构建对应理论构念。Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (OS) 的 peripheral function overlap（DV2）是黄金范文：1/N 阈值理论化 + grossing-rank 倒数代理月度收入 + 75th percentile 定义成功 + 平均 pairwise Jaccard 相似度 + intersection/union 集合隐喻。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M2 构建步骤可审计、M3 阈值与参照组 because、M7 度量公式化。常见薄弱点：阈值任意化（无理论支撑）、参照组定义含糊、相似度度量无公式。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M3 相似度/重叠度 DV 构建（阈值理论化 + 参照组操作化 + 度量公式化）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**原始句锚点**: "Following prior research, we calculate the reciprocal of the daily rank in the photo video grossing list for each app as a proxy for daily revenue and calculate the monthly revenue proxy by aggregating these reciprocals over a particular month (Kapoor and Agarwal 2017). We define successful apps as those above the 75th percentile in the monthly revenue proxy within the core function, in the time period three months prior to the focal app entry."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> Our [second] dependent variable is [DV_name], which captures the extent to which a [unit] [differentiates from / imitates] existing [successful units] sharing the same [core construct]. [Peripheral constructs] are defined as [meaningful noncore constructs] in a [unit]. By design of the [model], all [constructs] have a [positive weight], but not every [noncore construct] is equally important. As [weights add up to one], and there are [K] [constructs] in total, we consider [weights more than 1/K] as meaningful and count a particular [construct] as [peripheral] when its weight is [less than the core construct weight and more than 1/K].
>
> To identify [successful units], we utilize the [performance metric] in the [category] to measure performance. In the [platform], data on [revenues] are not publicly available, but [platform operator] discloses [ranking lists] by [metric_1] and by [metric_2], within and across [categories]. [Units] that rank higher on the lists offer clear evidence of [performance superiority] ([citation]). Following prior research, we calculate the [reciprocal of the daily rank] in the [grossing list] for each [unit] as a proxy for [daily revenue] and calculate the [monthly revenue proxy] by aggregating these [reciprocals] over a particular [month] ([citation]). We define [successful units] as those above the [75th percentile] in the [monthly revenue proxy] within the [core construct], in the [time period] [N months] prior to the [focal unit entry].
>
> [DV_name] is calculated as the [average pairwise Jaccard similarity] between the [focal unit] and all [successful units]. For both the [focal unit] and a [successful unit], we find the [sets of peripheral constructs] they incorporate in support of the [core construct]. [Jaccard similarity] is calculated by the [intersection] of the two [sets] divided by the [union] of the two [sets] (J(A,B) = (A∩B)/(A∪B)). The [intersection] reflects the [overlap] between the [units], and the [union] captures the [total space] incorporated by both [units].
**与原骨架差异**: 实证对象构建 DV 的三层 because 链，每一层都有理论支撑：(1) **阈值理论化**——peripheral 定义为 weight ∈ (1/K, core_weight)，用 1/K（均匀分布期望）作 cutoff，非任意值；(2) **参照组操作化**——成功 = grossing rank 倒数 → 月度聚合 → 75th percentile，把不可得的 revenue 用排名代理并明确公式；(3) **相似度公式化**——Jaccard + intersection/union 集合隐喻，让读者直观理解"重叠=交集，总空间=并集"。关键：**阈值、参照组、度量三者都必须有 because，且相似度必须给公式 + 集合解释**。可迁移到任何"模仿/差异化/重叠度" DV；度量可替换为 cosine / Euclidean / Jensen-Shannon，参照组可替换为 top-10 / 存活超 N 年。

### 变体 2: M2 监管产品竞争组构建 — 从审批路径到药理机制分类 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: "We specifically identified drugs approved under clauses 505(b)(2) and 505(j) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act) with the aid of the FDA's Approved Drug Products with Therapeutic Equivalence Evaluations (the "Orange Book"). Clause 505(j) governs generic drug applications, while clause 505(b)(2) governs the modifications of previously approved brand-name drugs."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M2
**骨架**:
> To measure [product competition], we began by selecting all [regulator]-approved [products] from [directory]. We specifically identified [products] approved under [regulatory clauses] with the aid of [reference source]. [Clause A] governs [product type A], while [clause B] governs [product type B]. Under these pathways, the timeline for most new [product] launches is reduced to approximately [time]. We then collected information on each [product]'s [attributes] from [N] specialized databases: [source 1], [source 2], [source 3], and [source 4] ([citation]). A researcher in [discipline] who was not part of our team used this information to help classify all listed [products] into competing groups. For example, [example A] and [example B] are considered direct competitors in [market], since they use the same [mechanism]. We then linked the competing [products] to their producers and market introduction dates with the aid of [source].
**与原骨架差异**: 复杂实证对象构建的典范——从监管数据库出发，通过审批路径缩小范围，再用药理机制定义"竞争组"。关键要素：(1) 监管路径的法律依据；(2) 多专业数据库交叉验证；(3) 外部专家独立分类（避免研究者偏差）；(4) 具体例子说明竞争组定义。行业特殊性较强，但作为该设计类型的首个示例具有高价值。
**诚实边界**: 监管路径和药理机制分类可能随时间和监管实践变化，需报告分类的时间窗口和一致性检验；外部专家分类需说明编码者间一致性。

### 变体 3: M4 连续相似度指数构建 — 计数比率公式 + 平滑常数 + 归一化 + 数值示例三连 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Du & Tsolmon 2024 (Organization Science)
**原始句锚点**: "We scale the base counts by adding 0.1 to each count to incorporate cases of zero base unit counts (e.g., a standalone firm with no subsidiaries) while preserving the scale of the base unit counts. To illustrate, when the acquirer and target have the same number of units (e.g., both with five base units), the similarity index equals one: 1 − |5 − 5|/(5 + 5) = 1.0."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-25
**槽位**: M4（自变量操作化，当自变量是构建的相似度指数时）
**骨架**:
> We measure [similarity construct] at the [unit] level and construct a continuous variable for each [pair] of the degree to which the [unit A] and [unit B] have similar [attribute]. We measure the degree of [attribute] as [countable operationalization] ([citation]). These [units] represent [theoretical meaning], and therefore can be compared across [units]. We calculate the [similarity index] using the formula: 1 − |[A_count] − [B_count]| / ([A_count] + [B_count]), where we scale the counts by adding [small constant] to each to incorporate cases of zero counts while preserving the scale. The normalized index ranges from zero to one, where zero indicates completely dissimilar [attribute], and one indicates identical [attribute]. [Numerical example 1: identical → index = 1.0]. [Numerical example 2: moderate → index = 0.6]. [Numerical example 3: extreme → index → 0]. The sample mean of the index is [value]. We also construct a binary measure based on [median/median-split criterion] as an alternative measure in robustness checks.
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体1（Jaccard 离散集合相似度）不同构——本变体是**连续计数比率型**相似度，适用于"两主体在某个可计数属性上的接近程度"（如结构单元数、规模、资源量）。核心要素：(1) 底层 countable 操作化 + because（"represent the lowest level of profit-center responsibility, and therefore can be compared across firms"）；(2) 1−|A−B|/(A+B) 公式（Jaccard 之外的比率替代）；(3) 平滑常数处理零值（类比 ln 加小常数）；(4) 0-1 归一化语义明确；(5) **三个数值示例**（identical=1.0 / moderate=0.6 / extreme→0）让读者直观校准；(6) 报告样本均值；(7) 附 binary 替代版本作稳健性。可迁移到任何"两主体连续接近度"构念（地理距离倒数、规模相似度、资源相似度、经验相似度）。
**诚实边界**: 平滑常数选择需论证或做敏感性检验（本文未做——见反模式）；公式对称性（|A−B| 不区分 A vs B 方向）需确认与理论一致（若理论上 A 对 B 与 B 对 A 作用不对称，此公式不适用）；countable 操作化的跨单元可比性必须论证。

### 变体 4: M3 multi-item formative objective index + cross-year rescaling — 无形构念的客观指标测量 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pollock, Lee, Jin & Lashley (2015, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: "It is an objective as opposed to perceptual measure of reputation in that it is composed of objective measures expected to influence stakeholders' perceptions. Objective reputation measures are often employed when perceptual measures are unavailable, and they can be constructed retrospectively for use in longitudinal analyses."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-30
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> "Our [construct] measure is a modified version of the [prior index name] index ([citation]). The [index] is a multi-item, time-varying index that reflects [actor]'s ability to [theoretical dimension 1] and [theoretical dimension 2], the [N] performance dimensions important to [stakeholders] ([citation]). It is an **objective as opposed to perceptual** measure of [construct] in that it is composed of objective measures expected to influence stakeholders' perceptions. Objective [construct] measures are often employed when perceptual measures are unavailable, and they can be constructed retrospectively for use in longitudinal analyses.
>
> Our index included [N] formative indicators of [actor] [construct]: (1) [indicator 1], (2) [indicator 2], (3) [indicator 3], (4) [indicator 4], and (5) [indicator 5]. [The prior index] also included [variable], which we excluded from our index because we used it as a [moderator] in our theory and analyses. [These indicators] were also standardized and aggregated using a [N]-year moving average.
>
> As in prior work, we needed to make the values comparable across time. [Market dynamics] can make cross-year comparisons difficult; for example, what was considered a large [indicator value] was very different in [year 1] and [year 2]. Thus we rescaled the index so that within-year differences are maintained while cross-year variance in values due to [market conditions] is eliminated, and we converted our measure to a [100]-point scale that is comparable across years. As the standardized index can take on negative values, for each year we added a constant equal to .01 plus the positive value of the lowest score that year to all scores (making all values positive) and divided each score by the largest value that year. We then multiplied the resulting value by 100. This rescaling allows for cross-year comparisons while maintaining the relative ratings among [actors] within each year. Like [other construct], this final measure was standardized so that the relative effects of [the two constructs] could be compared."
**与原骨架差异**: 无形构念（reputation / legitimacy / capability）的客观多项**形成性**指数构建法，区别于感知测量。四个关键手法：(1) **形成性（formative）而非反映性**——指标"形成"构念而非"反映"，故指标选择由理论维度驱动（本文 5 指标反映 2 个绩效维度），并显式声明"objective as opposed to perceptual"；(2) **排除理论需另用的变量**——排除 age 因 age 作调节变量，体现测量服务于理论；(3) **跨年可比性 rescaling**——保持年内相对排序、消除年际市场条件方差、转 100 分制（先加常数使为正 → 除以年内最大值 → ×100），解决"1991 vs 1999 的'大额资本'含义不同"问题；(4) **标准化以跨构念比较效应大小**（为同时方程系数比较铺路）。可迁移到任何需纵向可比的客观无形资产指数。
**诚实边界**: 客观指数非感知直接测量，需论证 face validity 或与感知测量相关性（pollock2015 引 Hallen & Pahnke 2015 感知测量佐证）；多项指标可能与 size 相关，需讨论 size 的判别效度（pollock2015 引 Lee et al. 2011 的稳健性）；5 年移动平均与 .01 平滑常数的选择需论证或做敏感性检验。

### 变体 5: M3 label-ambiguity 构念从共属重叠构建 — fuzziness + leniency (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pontikes (2012, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: "Building on this, I used the opposite of contrast, fuzziness, as one measure of label ambiguity. When fuzziness is high, it means a large number of affiliated organizations also identify with at least one other label."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-30
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> "To test [hypotheses about construct X—e.g., label ambiguity], I needed to create measures of [X]. I drew on research showing that [when members of a label primarily identify elsewhere, the focal label is less likely to develop a shared social meaning] ([citations]). Previous research built on the concept of a label's [contrast]—how clearly a label is defined relative to others ([citation]). I used the opposite of contrast, **[fuzziness]**, as one measure of [X]: [fuzz = 1 − contrast]; when [fuzziness] is high, [many affiliated actors also identify with at least one other label].
>
> I also created an alternate measure, **[leniency]**, which captures [the extent to which a label constrains affiliated actors]. [A label can be fuzzy if all its members identify with the SAME alternate label—high overlap but still constraining]. [Leniency] builds on [fuzziness] but also takes into account whether a label overlaps with [many DIFFERENT labels]: [leniency = fuzz × ln(NO_distinct_other_labels)].
>
> I conceived of labels as **fuzzy sets**, so that an actor can be partially associated with a label. I calculated an actor's **grade of membership** in a label as [the number of times it mentioned the label / total mentions of any label] in [year], taking values between 0 and 1. [Contrast = fuzzy density / support]; [fuzz = 1 − contrast]. To measure an actor i's labels' [fuzziness/leniency], I computed a weighted average: [Σ over labels μ_i(l) × fuzz_l], weighting each label's [fuzziness] by the actor's grade of membership in it."
**与原骨架差异**: 从**共属模式（co-affiliation overlap）**构建"标签/类别模糊度"构念的两步测量：(1) **fuzziness = 1 − contrast**——标签成员中有多少也认同其他标签（边界是否清晰）；(2) **leniency = fuzziness × ln(不同其他标签数)**——区分"成员都重叠到**同一个**标签"（高重叠但仍 constraining）vs"重叠到**很多不同**标签"（不 constraining）。两个关键技术：(a) **fuzzy-set grade of membership**——actor 对标签是"部分归属"（按提及比例 μ∈[0,1]）而非二分归属，符合认知心理学分类方式（Rosch 1978）；(b) **加权聚合到 actor 层**——actor 的模糊度 = 其所属各标签模糊度按 membership grade 加权平均。区别变体 1（Jaccard 相似度）/ 变体 3（计数比率相似度）/ 变体 4（多项形成性指数）：本变体的构念是**标签本身的属性**（label-level），从成员的**共属网络结构**推导，而非从 actor 自身属性聚合。可迁移到任何"从共属/共现网络结构测量类别、标签、群体的属性"的研究（category typicality、niche overlap、boundary strength、genre fuzziness）。
**诚实边界**: fuzziness 与 leniency 通常高度相关（pontikes2012 consumer analysis r=.90、VC analysis r=.94）——须**分别入模型**并报告两者结果一致（互为稳健性）；grade-of-membership 的连续归属假设须论证（并报告二分归属的稳健性）；须**显式区分 label-level 模糊**（fuzziness/leniency）**与 organization-level 泛化**（number of labels）——pontikes2015 声明两者正交，且组织可"专精于模糊标签"或"泛化于清晰标签"；数据源（press releases 作 self-claimed identity 记录）须论证能捕获非正式分类（无 master list 的领域）。
