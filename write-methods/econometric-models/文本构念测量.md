# 文本构念测量 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `文本构念测量` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

文本构念测量指用文本挖掘（典型为 LDA topic modeling、word embeddings）从非结构化文本痕迹（产品描述、专利、新闻、10-K、招聘贴）中**构建潜在构念**，再把每个单元在该构念空间上的概率/权重向量转化为可分析的 DV/IV。本设计的 Methods 叙事核心是**效度论证链**：为何选该文本方法、为何选 K 个主题/维度、概率向量如何映射为分析变量、与替代操作化是否一致。Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (OS) 是该设计类型的黄金范文：LDA → 18 functions → 概率向量 → focus on core (DV1) / peripheral overlap (DV2) / dissatisfaction (IV1) / heterogeneity (IV2)，全程配 coherence + interpretability 双标准与 Herfindahl(0.955)/Blau(0.99) 交叉验证。

强制槽位（Phase 1.5）：M2 样本漏斗（含语料构建与语言筛选）、M3 构念效度链（统计标准+理论标准+交叉验证）、M4 测量选择 because。常见薄弱点：M8 identification（文本构念仍为观测数据，需讨论测量误差与内生性）、M7 未报诊断（VIF 等）。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M2 文本构念样本漏斗（语料构建 → 分析样本 三段式）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M2
**骨架**:
> To identify [construct], we restricted the sample to [initial population] with [language/filtering criteria], ensuring comparability. Our final sample includes [N_corpus] [units] launched between [year_start] and [year_end], whose [text traces] we used to build the corpus for textual analysis and to identify [latent structure]. For analysis, we tracked [target events] from [date_start] to [date_end] as [feedback/contextual data] became systematically available since [date]. The procedure yielded [N_mid] total [units] launched by [N_actors], of which [N_final_actors] initially launched [N_final_units] upon entering the market.
**与原骨架差异**: 文本构念设计的样本漏斗需区分**语料构建样本**（全历史，用于训练 LDA/embedding）与**分析样本**（有反馈/上下文数据的子集）。关键：语言筛选前置（机器语言识别）+ 时间分层（语料用长窗口、分析用反馈可得窗口）+ 单位明确（units vs actors）。原文 12,459（语料）→ 6,864（有反馈）→ 4,957 by 3,780 developers（主分析）。

### 变体 2: M3 LDA 参数选择效度论证链（双重标准）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> One of the most important researcher-imposed parameters in the [LDA topic] model is the number of [topics/dimensions]. We selected the number of [topics] to ensure that the [topics] are [clear], [well bounded], and [interpretable] ([citation]). Therefore, we first selected [topics] based on [local optimal coherence scores] (high coherence suggests clear and well-bounded topics) and then based on the [interpretability of the constructs] to the [intended audience], resulting in [K] [constructs]. Using [top keywords] that characterize each [topic], we gave [labels] to each of the [constructs] identified and present them in [Table/Figure] with [top keywords] characterizing them.
**与原骨架差异**: 任何文本构念测量都必须回答"为何选 K 个主题"——本骨架给出**双重效度标准**：统计标准（coherence score）+ 理论标准（interpretability to everyday user）。关键策略：(1) 显式承认"researcher-imposed parameters"并透明化；(2) 统计与理论标准并列（非仅靠其一）；(3) 用 top keywords + 标签表让读者可审计。这是 LDA 论文 M3 的黄金效度论证链。

### 变体 3: M3 文本构念 DV 操作化（概率向量 → 分析变量 → 交叉验证）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> The model assigned each [unit] a [probability vector] of [K] dimensions, and the weights in these [K] dimensions add up to one. This [probability vector] defines the [unit]'s position in the [multidimensional construct space]. The [highest-weight] topic represented its [core construct], defining its main [demand/competitive environment].
>
> Our [first] dependent variable is [DV_name], which captures the extent to which a [unit] focuses on its [core construct] relative to [peripheral constructs]. We calculated the measure by the [highest weight] in the [probability vector] ([citation]). Because [weights add up to one], the more weight allocated to the [core construct], the less is allocated to [peripheral constructs]. This measure is highly correlated ([0.9X]) with alternative operationalizations, such as the [Herfindahl index / concentration measure].
**与原骨架差异**: 从文本概率向量到 DV 的完整操作化三层：(1) 利用数学约束（weights add up to one → 核心与外周零和）；(2) DV = 最高权重（或阈值化权重）；(3) 与替代操作化（Herfindahl/concentration）交叉验证（高相关 → 构念效度）。关键：**效度交叉验证是文本构念 DV 的必要步骤**——不可只报一种操作化。

### 变体 4: M4 测量选择 because 链（多选一操作化的辩护）
**来源论文**: Zhao/Ding/Gaba 2023 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-06-17
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> We calculated [IV_name] using an [entropy measure]. First, we [pooled all individual ratings] of existing [units] and calculated the [proportion of observations] at [category_1, category_2, ..., category_K] (*P_i*); second, we used these [K] proportions to calculate the [entropy measure] *E* at the [aggregate level], defined as [E = Σ P_i · ln(1/P_i)]. A high value of [IV_name] indicates [theoretical meaning].
>
> The correlation between our [entropy measure] and the [alternative index, e.g., Blau index = 1 − Σ P_i²] is very high ([0.9X]), but we use the [entropy measure] because it has better features in [aggregation and decomposition] ([citation]).
**与原骨架差异**: 任何在多个等价操作化中"多选一"的测量决策（entropy vs Blau、Jaccard vs cosine、Herfindahl vs concentration），都应回答"为何选 A 而非 B"。本骨架的黄金范式：(1) 给出所选指标的完整公式 + 理论含义；(2) 报告与替代指标的相关（高相关 → 量效度）；(3) 给出选择的 because（如 entropy 的 aggregation/decomposition 性质）。关键：**because 必须是统计性质（aggregation / decomposition / additivity），不能只是"习惯"**。

### 变体 5: M3 复合文本指标构建（多子维度 → 分别测量 → 平均合成）
**来源论文**: Cutolo & Ferriani 2024 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> [Construct] is a multidimensional property that reflects [theoretical facet 1], [theoretical facet 2], and [theoretical facet 3]. We capture each facet with a distinct textual indicator. [Facet 1] is measured by [tool/dictionary] as [score description]. [Facet 2] is measured by [tool/dictionary] as [score description]. [Facet 3] is measured by [tool/dictionary] as [score description]. We then compute [construct] as the [mean/sum/weighted average] of the three component scores. Higher values indicate [interpretation]. In Additional Analyses we also examine each component separately to assess whether the effect is driven by a particular facet.
**与原骨架差异**: 当理论构念本身是多维的（如 cohesion = lexical + causal + semantic），文本测量也应分层：(1) 每个子维度对应一个理论facet；(2) 每个facet用最适合的工具测量（可能跨 LIWC/TAACO/LSA）；(3) 用平均/加权合成复合指标；(4) 预告将做component disaggregation。关键：**不能把所有文本信号塞进一个黑箱字典**——要让审稿人看到构念-测量映射。

### 变体 6: M3 类别相对文本常规性操作化（LDA topic → category-average regression slope）
**来源论文**: Cutolo & Ferriani 2024 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M3/M4
**骨架**:
> We conceptualize [construct] as the extent to which a [unit]'s [text] emphasizes topics that are also central in other [units]' [texts] within the same [category]. We first estimate an LDA topic model on the full corpus and identify [K] topics. For each [category], we compute the average weight of each topic across all other [units] in that [category]. We then regress each [unit]'s topic distribution on the corresponding [category]-average topic distribution. The slope of this regression serves as our measure of [construct]: a slope greater than [1/threshold] indicates that the [unit] overemphasizes conventional topics, while a slope below [1/threshold] indicates underemphasis.
**与原骨架差异**: "常规性/典型性"在平台/市场场景中必须是**相对概念**（相对同类别其他卖家）。本骨架给出从 LDA topic weights 到 category-relative 分数的完整步骤：(1) 全语料训练 LDA；(2) 计算每个 category 的 topic 平均分布；(3) 个体分布对 category 平均分布回归；(4) slope 作为常规性指标。关键：此方法把"与谁比较"（category peers）和操作化规则都透明化。

**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M10
**骨架**:
> To ensure that our automated measure captures the intended construct, we manually examined a random sample of [N] [texts]. Two coders independently rated each [text] on [dimension(s)] using a [scale]. Inter-rater reliability was [reliability statistic]. The automated scores correlated [correlation] with the manual ratings, suggesting that the measure adequately reflects [construct]. We also inspected cases at the extremes of the distribution to confirm that high [construct] texts indeed [description] and low [construct] texts [description].
**与原骨架差异**: 任何文本自动化测量都需要**人工校验环**来闭合效度链。本骨架给出最小可行版本：(1) 随机样本；(2) 独立编码 + 信度；(3) 自动分与人工评分相关；(4) 极端案例质性检查。关键：人工验证不必大规模，但必须在 Methods 中明确报告，否则审稿人会质疑 dictionary/tool 是否真的捕捉了理论构念。

### 变体 8: M3 访谈言语→LIWC 双成分净得分 + face validity 引语锚定 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Mannor, Wowak, Bartkus & Gomez-Mejia 2016 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> We measured [perceptual construct] using a psycholinguistic approach ([citation]). We first recorded and professionally transcribed [speech] from [actor] about each [decision/event]. Importantly, our focus on the [decision] level of analysis required that we capture [construct] at the time of each [focal unit]; we thus treated each discussion of a [decision] as a discrete body of text. Once we deleted all passages not spoken by the [actor], we used [text analysis tool] and its established dictionaries to analyze [actor]'s word usage during the entire portion of the [interview/meeting] pertaining to the focal [decision].
>
> [State A] occurs when [theoretical definition]. We captured this by assessing each [actor]'s use of [word category A] and [word category B]. The combinations of words in these dictionaries had an average coefficient alpha of [value] ([citation]), and our final [dictionary] included [N] words (such as [examples]). This component was calculated as the relative percentage of words spoken by the [actor] contained in the [dictionary A].
>
> [State B], on the other hand, occurs when [theoretical definition]. We thus measured this by assessing the [actor]'s use of [word category C] and [word category D], which comprised an aggregate dictionary of [N] words (such as [examples]) whose average coefficient alpha was [value].
>
> Next, we standardized the [component A] and [component B] scores. We then used these standardized scores to create a net [construct] score, calculated as [component A − component B]. This yielded a single parsimonious indicator of the degree to which the [actor]'s speech was more indicative of [state A] (positive scores) or [state B] (negative scores). [Note: real-world states are not mutually exclusive; the net combination determines the predominant orientation.] Results were also robust to using separate [component A] and [component B] measures.
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体5（Cutolo, 复合文本指标）的关键区别——(1) Mannor 使用的是**双极构念的净得分**（gain − loss = net context），而非多维平均；(2) 明确的双成分结构：State A 词典 + State B 词典 → 分别标准化 → 作差 → 净得分；(3) 包含 face validity 引语锚定（在独立 corpus 文件中有完整模板）；(4) 结尾的稳健性预告（"also robust to using separate measures"）一石二鸟。适用于任何可概念化为对立两端的感知构念（gain/loss, approach/avoidance, promotion/prevention）。

### 变体 9: M3 Face Validity 引语锚定 — 访谈摘录+百分位评分 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Mannor, Wowak, Bartkus & Gomez-Mejia 2016 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> A few examples from our [interviews/transcripts] help illustrate how [construct] manifested in [actors]'s speech. In describing the decision to [decision A], the [actor] of a [company type] said: "[Quote illustrating state A]." While this is only one quote from a larger body of speech, it suggests an emphasis on [state A]. Our measurement technique scored this decision in the [Nth percentile] of our sample for [construct]. As another example, the [actor] of a [company type] described the decision to [decision B] by saying: "[Quote illustrating state B]." As before, this is only one excerpt from [duration] of speech describing the decision, but it emphasizes that [state B was paramount]. According to our measurement technique, this decision was scored in the [Nth percentile] of all [construct] scores in our sample.
**与原骨架差异**: 文本构念测量最常见的质疑是"字典法真的捕捉了构念吗？" Face validity 引语锚定通过两个具体例子的对比（高值→引语→百分位 vs 低值→引语→百分位）将抽象的数字分数拉回到可感知的现实。关键：(1) 选择两个极端案例；(2) 每个引语后接"this suggests..."解释；(3) 再回到量化分数——闭环。适用于所有自动化文本测量。

### 变体 10: M4 LIWC 默认字典效度链 — 效度引用+补充实验验证+区段聚焦理由 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> Following prior research (e.g., [citation_1]; [citation_2]), we derived a score for [construct] through textual analysis. Specifically, we assessed [construct] during [communication_setting] using the LIWC [version] default dictionary for [dictionary_name] ([citation]). This measure has been empirically tested for construct validity, internal reliability ([citation]), and discriminant validity ([citation]). Here, we provide an example of [speaker]'s language use based on a [transcript_type] to illustrate the [construct]. A quote from [speaker_name], CEO of [firm], stated: "[example_quote]." This statement illustrates [construct] through words such as "[example_word_1]" and "[example_word_2]." We conducted [validation_study_type] that provided additional validity evidence supporting the [construct] measure ([appendix_location]). LIWC calculated each word category as the count of associated keywords divided by the total number of words in [text_unit]. We focused on the [specific_section] of the [communication_setting] to better capture [speaker]'s natural communication with [audience] (e.g., [citation]).
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体7（随机样本人工校验）和变体8/9（LIWC双成分+Mannor风格）的关键区别——本骨架提供的是**LIWC默认字典的完整效度论证链**，而非自定义字典或人工校验：(1) 引用字典开发者的效度证据——"empirically tested for construct validity, internal reliability, and discriminant validity"（Pennebaker et al. 2007, Schultheiss 2013）；(2) **定性例句展示**——从实际 transcripts 中摘取引语，标注触发关键词，将抽象分数锚定到具体语言；(3) **补充实验验证**——在 appendix 中进行独立实验验证该字典在目标语境中捕捉了目标构念；(4) **区段聚焦理由**——解释为何仅使用 Q&A section（非 presentation script），因为 Q&A 更能反映 CEO 的自然沟通。四个要素构成闭环效度论证，远超"we used LIWC [year] dictionary"的单句声明。
**诚实边界**: LIWC 默认字典的效度引用不能替代领域内构念效度验证。若构念在特定语境（如 Q&A 区段、特定行业术语）中使用，需提供补充效度证据（如本骨架的 supplementary experimental study 或变体7的人工校验）。词典法的字面含义可能与语境含义存在偏差——报告至少一个定性例句可让审稿人自行判断 face validity。

### 变体 11: M4 手工内容分析编码规则 + 边界案例 + 焦点 actor 视角 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Cui, Yang & Vertinsky (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> [Predictor] is calculated as [ratio definition] over a [N]-year moving window ([citation_1]; [citation_2]). We used a [N]-year window because [database] seldom report [termination dates] of [alliances], and the life span of an [alliance] is normally no more than [N] years ([citation]). We constructed this variable using a content analysis of [text source] to identify [coding objective] for each [alliance] in our sample. Specifically, [category A] with the objective of [objective A] were coded as [code A], while [category B] such as [examples], which mainly [objective B], were classified as [code B] ([citation_3]; [citation_4]; [citation_5]). Since [actors] may have different intentions within an [alliance], we undertook this coding from the [focal actor]'s perspective ([citation]). It is important to note that not all [category A] are [code A]; within our data set, if [boundary condition], that [category A] was coded as [code B]. The value of this variable ranges from [min] to [max].
**与原骨架差异**: 当前 corpus 变体 1–10 以 LDA/LIWC/TAACO 自动化方法为主，缺少"手工内容分析 + 边界案例 + 焦点 actor 视角"的变体。关键要素：(1) 编码规则明确（探索 vs 利用型 alliance）；(2) 边界案例透明化（"not all R&D collaborations are exploration oriented"）；(3) 焦点 actor 视角（从 focal firm 而非 dyad 双方编码）；(4) 变量取值范围。适用于 alliance/contract/战略文本的手工分类。
**诚实边界**: 手工内容分析必须报告编码者数量、培训过程、编码者间一致性（inter-coder reliability）和分歧解决程序。仅报告编码规则不足以支撑构念效度。

### 变体 12: M4 手工二元文本编码 + 多源聚合 + embedded/peripheral 构念边界 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Kim & Lee 2026 (Strategic Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-22
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> Our focal predictor is a binary indicator for [construct], operationalized through manual coding of text descriptions from [N] sources: ([source_1], typically from [location_1]); ([source_2], from [location_2]); and ([source_3], from [location_3]). Combining these texts created an aggregate description (averaging [N] words) including descriptions by the [actor] itself and by third parties. Our coding was based on a definition of [construct] as [theoretical definition — e.g., an identity claim that the actor's core activities improve X]. We therefore coded [construct] as a binary variable indicating whether a [unit]'s description explicitly mentioned [domain-specific criteria]. Critically, [peripheral activities — e.g., volunteering / philanthropy / CSR programs outside core activities] were insufficient for coding [construct] = 1. This approach distinguishes our measure from studies focused on narrower [peripheral] activities, and identifies [units] for whom [construct] is a [core identity / embedded] attribute rather than a [peripheral / symbolic] attribute. Both authors first pilot coded [N_pilot] randomly selected [units] to develop a reliable shared understanding. Following discussion, we independently coded the full sample. Inter-rater agreement was [%]% and Cohen's kappa exceeded [threshold] (kappa = [value]). Differences were resolved by discussing the texts until consensus.

## 诚实边界（全局）

- LIWC 默认字典效度引用不能替代自定义字典的领域内效度验证——若使用自定义字典，必须报告词典构建过程、信度、和至少一项人工校验。
- Q&A 区段 vs Presentation Script 的选择需要理论理由（如"CEO's natural communication"或"less scripted responses"），不能仅说"following prior research"。
- 若文本构念作为交互项的一个成分，需讨论测量误差在交互项中的传播——LIWC 的分类误差在交互项中可能放大或衰减。
- **手工内容分析未报告编码者间一致性**是常见反模式：relative exploration 等依赖 deal summary 手工编码的变量，未报告 inter-coder agreement 会削弱构念效度。
