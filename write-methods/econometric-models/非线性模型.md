# 非线性模型 — Methods 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-methods/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-M*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `非线性模型` 专用变体）。

## 设计特征摘要

非线性模型（Logit/Probit/ordered/negative binomial/Tobit/survival）的 Methods 叙事核心是**解释为何线性模型不适用**以及**结果将如何解释**。必须明确：(1) DV 的分布特征（有界、计数、删失、顺序、事件）；(2) 所选模型的分布假设；(3) 系数将翻译为概率/边际效应/预期计数/IRRs/风险比/时间变化，而非直接当作线性变化。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: M7 计数模型选择（负二项回归 + 过度分散诊断）
**来源论文**: Cutolo & Ferriani 2024 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 通过（单篇入库，待第二篇交叉验证）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> The dependent variable, [DV name], is a non-negative count of [events/units]. A standard Poisson model is inappropriate because the data exhibit overdispersion: the conditional variance exceeds the conditional mean. We therefore estimate [negative binomial] regression, which relaxes the equidispersion assumption by including a dispersion parameter. Standard errors are [robust/clustered] to account for [violation]. A [likelihood-ratio/Lagrange multiplier] test rejects the Poisson specification in favor of the [negative binomial] model ([statistic] = [value], p [relation] [threshold]). In interpreting the results, we translate coefficients into [incidence rate ratios / expected counts / marginal effects] rather than interpreting them as linear changes.
**与原骨架差异**: 计数模型 Methods 段落必须完成三件事：(1) 说明 DV 是 count；(2) 给出过度分散证据（LR/LM 检验或均值-方差比较）；(3) 预告解释尺度。很多论文只说"用 negative binomial"而不解释为什么拒绝 Poisson——本骨架强制完成这个 because 链。

### 变体 2: 负二项回归选择 — 针对理论竞争焦点 DV 的模型论证 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Haunschild, Polidoro & Chandler 2015 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> Given that our dependent variable, [DV], is a count of [events], we estimated [negative binomial] models, which are appropriate when the dependent variable is a count. We also estimated zero-inflated negative binomial models (ZINB), but likelihood-ratio tests indicated that zero-inflated models did not significantly improve fit over the standard negative binomial (p > 0.10). Because [setting rationale for overdispersion: e.g., firm heterogeneity in event propensity], negative binomial regression is preferred over Poisson regression, which assumes equidispersion. In interpreting the results, we note that the regression coefficients represent the log of the expected count change for a one-unit increase in the predictor. For substantive interpretation, we transform coefficients using e^β − 1 to express effects as percentage changes in the rate of [DV].
**与原骨架差异**: 与变体1（Cutolo, 过度分散诊断驱动）的互补——Haunschild 的三层论证：(1) DV 是 count → 负二项；(2) ZINB 拒绝（LR test p > 0.10）→ 标准负二项；(3) 预告解释尺度（e^β − 1 百分比变化）。增加了 ZINB 排除步骤——如果理论预测存在"从不发生"的零膨胀子群体，此步骤必要；否则可省略。

### 变体 3: 竞争焦点 DV 互控 — 排除伪 trade-off (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Haunschild, Polidoro & Chandler 2015 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M6
**骨架**:
> Our theory suggests that firms face a trade-off between [Focus_A] and [Focus_B]. In models estimating [DV_A], we controlled for [DV_B]; conversely, in models estimating [DV_B], we controlled for [DV_A]. This cross-control ensures that the effect of [IV] on [DV_A] is not confounded by [DV_B], and vice versa. To ensure that findings were not driven by collinearity involving the respective variables, we also ran models dropping the alternative focus from the equation. Both approaches showed fully robust results, thus increasing confidence that the findings reflect a genuine reallocation of [resources/attention] between [Focus_A] and [Focus_B].
**与原骨架差异**: 适用于理论预测两个 DV 之间存在竞争性 trade-off 的研究。关键：(1) 双向互控（A 控制 B，B 控制 A）；(2) 必须报告无互控模型的稳健性以防多重共线性质疑（两种 DV 通常在 baseline 上相关）。

### 变体 4: 替代测量构造效度三角 — 双层测量正当性 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Haunschild, Polidoro & Chandler 2015 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M3
**骨架**:
> To measure a [unit]'s [construct] in a given [period], we used [primary_measure] because [theoretical justification — what dimension of the construct this captures]. Importantly, this variable captures [unit]'s [generalized/focused] concern with [construct_domain], as opposed to [narrower_response]. As an alternative measure, we also used [alternative_measure]. [Alternative justification — what different theoretical dimension this captures and why it differs from primary]. Because [alternative measure] relies on a different [data source / institutional process] than [primary measure], finding consistent results across the two measures increases confidence that our findings reflect [construct] rather than measurement artifact.
**与原骨架差异**: 与简单的一变量一段落的区别——(1) 主测量与替代测量各锚定不同理论维度（而非简单的同一构念的两种测量）；(2) "Importantly, this variable captures... generalized concern, as opposed to focused response" 的措辞区分了广义 vs 特定响应；(3) 替代测量的理论理由独立于主测量（如 clinical trials 捕捉资源配置，scientific articles 捕捉信息披露意愿）。

### 变体 5: M7 RE 面板 Logit + odds-ratio 报告惯例声明 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pfarrer, Pollock & Rindova 2010 (Academy of Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> [Table X] presents descriptive statistics and a correlation matrix for the variables used in testing our hypotheses. The means and standard deviations reflect values for raw rather than transformed measures. All variance inflation factors were below [threshold], with an average of [value]. Thus, multicollinearity is not a concern. We estimated [random-effects logistic regression] because [between-firm focus / binary outcome justification]. We report odds ratios to allow easier interpretation of the magnitude of effects. An odds ratio greater than one indicates the likelihood that an event will occur increases with a one-unit increase in the independent variable; an odds ratio less than one indicates the likelihood decreases.
**与原骨架差异**: Pfarrer 的 M7 结构是**四合一密集段落**：描述统计 + 诊断检验（VIF）+ 估计器声明 + odds-ratio 惯例说明。这种"高密度 M7"在 AMJ/ASQ 中常见（篇幅限制使 Methods 和 Results 中 R1 的描述统计需整合到同一段落）。

### 变体 6: M4 理想型二分化 + 复合媒体构念测量 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pfarrer, Pollock & Rindova 2010 (Academy of Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M4
**骨架**:
> Because one of the goals of our study was to compare the effects of [Construct A] and [Construct B] under different conditions, we followed prior research suggesting that the construction of "ideal types" based on actors' possessing the highest levels of relevant characteristics was both theoretically and empirically useful for representing the distinctiveness of individual constructs. We thus identified [Group A] as those that [criterion A] and [Group B] as those that [criterion B]. This approach is also consistent with [theory], in that the value of these assets is related to their level of accumulation. Finally, research suggests that discrimination is meaningful at the tops and bottoms of rankings, but not in the middle ranges—continuous rankings may tend to create artificial differences where no actual differences exist, thereby introducing additional error.
>
> Since [construct] is defined as a combination of [component 1: visibility/prominence] and [component 2: affect/valence], we used both components in creating our measure. [Component 1 operationalization and source description]. To capture overall [component 2], we used the [coefficient name, e.g., Janis-Fadner coefficient] which has been used in past research to assess [phenomenon]: [formula].
**与原骨架差异**: 双重论证：先论证为什么将连续构念切分为理想型（理论效度 + 测量精度），再论证复合媒体构念的双成分如何操作化。适用于 reputation/celebrity/status 等难以从档案数据直接获得的"受众感知型"构念。

### 变体 7: M7 条件固定效应负二项 + 全零面板审计 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Desai 2011 (Academy of Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> The dependent variable is a count of [events] and takes non-negative integer values. Because the data exhibit overdispersion (the conditional variance exceeds the conditional mean), Poisson regression is inappropriate. We therefore estimated [conditional fixed-effects negative binomial] models. Conditional FE negative binomial controls for all time-invariant [unit]-level heterogeneity by conditioning on each [unit]'s total count of [events] over the observation period. We verified that no [unit] had zero [events] across the full panel (which would cause these observations to drop from conditional FE estimation); [N] [units] with zero [events] were identified and are included only in robustness checks using [alternative estimator]. Standard errors are clustered by [cluster_level] to account for within-[cluster] correlation.
**与原骨架差异**: Desai 的条件 FE 负二项段落增加了**全零面板审计**——一个经常被忽略的步骤：条件 FE 负二项会丢弃所有时期 DV=0 的面板。透明报告这些被丢弃的观测数量及替代处理方式（如随机效应或 pooled negative binomial 稳健性）。适用于任何条件 FE 计数模型。

### 变体 8: M8 稳健性三角组织 — 测量替代→机制行为→结果效度三管齐下 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Desai 2011 (Academy of Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M8
**骨架**:
> We assessed the robustness of our findings through a triangulation approach organized around three dimensions. First, to address [measurement_concern], we replaced [primary measure] with [alternative measures: e.g., alternative operationalizations of the key construct]. Second, to probe the [behavioral/mechanism] underlying our results, we examined [mechanism_behavior: e.g., whether the effect manifests in firm actions vs. mere disclosure]. Third, to validate [outcome_validity], we tested whether [alternative DV] produces consistent results. Across all three dimensions, the results were substantively consistent.
**与原骨架差异**: 稳健性三角不是按方法罗列，而是将稳健性检验组织为三个理论维度：(1) 测量替代——构念的不同操作化是否一致；(2) 机制行为——不同的行为表现是否遵循同一模式；(3) 结果效度——替代因变量是否重复主结果。每个维度回答一个特定的 validity threat。适用于稳健性检验多于 5 个、需要赋予理论叙事结构的场景。

### 变体 9: M7 Heckman 两阶段 + 政策工具作为 selection instrument (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Malik, Wang, Martin & Gomez-Mejia 2025 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: M7
**骨架**:
> Since not all [units] in our sample [experienced the event] in a given [period], we used Heckman selection models to control for selection bias by estimating a [unit]'s likelihood of [experiencing the event] using a selection instrument, which should be related to selection (i.e., [event] likelihood) but not outcomes (i.e., [DV]; [citation]). We used [policy/regulation name] as our selection instrument. [Policy], enacted in [date], aimed to [policy goal]. [Policy mechanism: e.g., expanded the regulator's authority, leading to more events due to improved surveillance]. However, [policy] focused solely on [specific domain] and did not specify [DV-relevant details: e.g., whether events must occur on specific days or be communicated in specific ways]. Therefore, it did not directly affect [units]' [DV]. We used a binary variable to create this selection instrument, assigning it a value of 1 if a [unit-period] observation was subject to this policy change (i.e., [year] and after).
**与原骨架差异**: 与 Pfarrer 的 Heckman-as-robustness（R7）不同——Malik 将 Heckman 作为**主识别策略**（M7），且 selection instrument 的论证有三层：(1) 政策如何增加事件概率（relevance）；(2) 政策为何不影响 DV（exclusion）；(3) 第一阶段表格确认 relevance（b=0.288, p<0.05）。适用于只有部分样本经历 treatment 的选择性样本研究。
