## 证据节奏摘要

三向交互 Results 的叙事核心是**逐层条件化**：不满足于报告三向交互项系数——必须分解为"在哪个调节水平上两向交互显著"、"在哪个两向条件下简单斜率差异显著"、"模式是否对称（仅一侧边界条件成立vs双侧都成立）"。典型节奏：三向系数→条件两向交互（高/低Z水平分别报告）→简单斜率差异t-test→分面图（高/低Z分面）。

### 变体 1: R4 三向交互条件分解 — 两向交互条件化 + 简单斜率差异 t-test (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ahmadi, Khanagha, Berchicci & Jansen 2017 (Journal of Management Studies)
**原始句锚点**: The result confirmed that the two-way interaction is indeed significant (B = 0.54, p < 0.05) under the high complexity condition, but non-significant (B=−0.11, p > 0.05) under the low complexity condition. Moreover, we tested the difference between simple slopes.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R4
**骨架**:
> [Model N] shows the results of the three-way interaction between [moderator_1], [moderator_2], and [IV]. The coefficient is statistically significant (B = [value], SE = [value], p < [threshold]), which is consistent with hypothesis [H_3way]. Further, we tested the conditional effect of two-way interactions at the two values of [moderator_2]. The result confirmed that the two-way interaction is indeed significant (B = [value], p < [threshold]) under the high [moderator_2] condition, but non-significant (B = [value], p > [threshold]) under the low [moderator_2] condition. Moreover, we tested the difference between simple slopes. The difference is significant (t = [value], p < [threshold]) between the slope of the [condition_A]-high [moderator_2] condition and the slope of the [condition_B]-high [moderator_2] condition. However, a similar test on the difference between the slope of the [condition_A]-low [moderator_2] condition and the slope of the [condition_B]-low [moderator_2] condition proved to be non-significant (t = [value], p > [threshold]).
>
> For ease of interpretation, we have created two Figures ([Figure_N]a, [Figure_N]b). Figure [N]a shows the interaction between [IV] and [moderator_1] on [DV] in conditions of low [moderator_2], while Figure [N]b illustrates the same interaction in conditions of high [moderator_2]. Figure [N]b shows the impact of [condition_A vs condition_B] on the relationship between [IV] and [DV] in high [moderator_2] conditions. It suggests that [substantive_interpretation: e.g., promotion cues in the organizational context can boost the positive effect of promotion focus trait on the inclination for exploratory activities when managers need to deal with a high degree of complexity]. However, in situations of far less [moderator_2], this kind of mechanism does not play a significant role.
**与原骨架差异**: 标准两向交互报告仅需交互系数+简单斜率+一张图。三向交互升级为**四层分解**：(1) 三向交互项系数——判断整体三向是否成立；(2) 条件两向交互——在moderator_2的每个水平（高/低）上重新估计两向交互，回答"两向交互在哪个Z水平上成立"；(3) 简单斜率差异t-test——在关键条件（如高Z水平）上比较不同条件间的简单斜率，回答"简单斜率差异只在特定Z水平上显著"；(4) 分面图——不是一张三向图，而是两张两向图并排（低Z vs 高Z），让读者直观比较不同Z水平上的交互模式变化。关键策略：Ahmadi et al. 的不对称发现（H3a promotion系统三向显著，H3b prevention系统三向不显著）通过此四层分解得到精确展示——"only under high complexity does promotion fit matter; prevention fit is not moderated by complexity"。
**诚实边界**: 三向交互的条件分解涉及多重比较——若在多个Z水平上测试两向交互，应在Methods中预设关键切割点（如±1SD或理论阈值）而非事后搜索。分面图中的简单斜率应标注置信区间或标准误。若三向交互仅在一侧（如仅高Z水平）显著，应讨论"为什么低Z水平不成立"——可能是理论边界也可能是统计功效不足。

### 变体 2: R4 连续调节变量三向交互 — 边际效应表（Table 5 Panels B/C 风格）(1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Chung, Low & Rust 2022 (Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science)
**原始句锚点**: The coefficient on the triple interaction term among CEO confidence, CMO confidence, and CMO power is negative and significant, indicating that the attenuating effects of CMO confidence are made more negative as CMO power increases.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-08
**槽位**: R4
**骨架**:
> Model [N] of Table [X] shows the full specification including the triple interaction among [IV], [moderator_1], and [moderator_2]. The coefficient on the triple interaction term is [positive / negative] and significant (β = [value], p < [threshold]), consistent with Hypothesis [H_3way]. The marginal effects are reported in Panel [B/C] of Table [Y]. They show the coefficient on [IV] (δ1) at [one standard deviation below / above] the mean of [moderator_1] and [moderator_2]. As [moderator_1] increases, the [positive / negative] effect of [IV] on [DV] becomes [stronger / weaker]; this [amplification / attenuation] is [greater / smaller] when [moderator_2] is higher. Thus, the triple interaction supports the prediction that [theoretical interpretation of conditional boundary].
**与原骨架差异**: 变体 1（Ahmadi et al.）适用于 **操纵型/类别型** 调节变量（trait × manipulated context × complexity），强调条件两向交互的显著性差异和简单斜率 t-test。本变体适用于 **连续型** 调节变量（如 CMO power、board independence），核心工具是 **边际效应表**：在两个连续调节变量各自的 ±1 SD 处报告 IV 的系数，并比较对角线差异（如高 moderator_1 × 高 moderator_2 vs 低 moderator_1 × 低 moderator_2）。关键：用"δ1 at ±1 SD" 的表格语言替代分面图，使读者能直接读取具体数值。
**诚实边界**: 连续调节变量的 ±1 SD 切割必须基于实际数据分布；若某调节变量偏态严重，±1 SD 可能超出观测值范围，此时应改用理论阈值或实际分位数（如 P25/P75）。必须报告每个单元格的观测数量或至少说明 N 足够稳定。

### 变体 3: R4 三向交互"线收敛"图解——调节变量作为差距消除器（gap closer）(1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Zhou, Gao & Zhao (2017, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: It shows that the effect of R&D intensity is stronger for non-SOEs than SOEs at lower levels of industrial competition, but the two lines converge at higher levels of industrial competition.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-30
**槽位**: R4
**骨架**:
> "We further plotted the moderating effects. We displayed the effect of [IV] on [DV] at different levels of [moderator_1] for [group A] and [group B] in Figure [XA]. It shows that the effect of [IV] is [stronger] for [group A] than [group B] at [lower] levels of [moderator_1], but **the two lines converge** at [higher] levels of [moderator_1]. Figure [XB] exhibits the effect of [IV] on [DV] for [group A] and [group B] depending on [grouping condition—e.g., established firms vs. start-ups]. Consistent with our prediction, the marginal impact of [IV] is [stronger] for [group A] when they are [established], whereas [IV] contributes more to [DV] for [group B—e.g., state start-ups]."
**与原骨架差异**: 区别变体 1（条件分解 + 简单斜率 t-test）、变体 2（连续调节边际效应表 ±1SD）——本变体的核心是**三向交互的图形解读聚焦"线收敛"（lines converge）**：两条线（group A vs group B）在某调节变量低水平下分离、高水平下**收敛**（差距闭合）。"converge" 是关键叙事信号——它表示 [moderator] 消除了两组的差距（如高竞争消除 SOE 与非 SOE 的效率差距、state start-up 的 R&D 产出效率向非 SOE 靠拢）。配套图：一张画 "[IV] 效应 × [moderator] 水平"的**双线图**（group A vs group B），强调**线收敛**而非交叉或平行。这把三向交互从"谁更强"升级为"什么条件下差距消失"——对" remedy / leveler"类理论（竞争是 SOE 低效的解药）尤其有力。
**适用**: 三向交互中 [moderator] 被理论化为"差距消除器 / leveler"的研究——竞争消除所有制效率差距、监管消除大小企业差距、市场化消除制度优势、培训消除禀赋差距。
**禁忌**: "converge" 必须由图形与系数共同支撑（不可只靠视觉）——须确认高 [moderator] 下两组简单斜率差异不再显著；若线是"交叉"（crossing，一组由弱变强）而非"收敛"（差距缩小但顺序不变），应用不同措辞；须报告图形所基于的模型（哪个 Model/Figure）。

### 变体 4: R4 中和阈值随连续 Z 下降，强制报告不推翻的一侧 (2026-08-13)
**来源论文**: Lun, Zurbruegg, Mount & Cheong 2026 (Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice)
**原始句锚点**: "This suggests that in early-stage contexts, COO power substantially weakens—but does not overturn—the positive EO-recall relationship."
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-08-13
**槽位**: R4
**骨架**:
> In H[N], we argued that the weakening influence of [W] on the positive [IV]–[DV] relationship would be contingent on [Z]. To test this, we included the lower-order interactions and a three-way interaction in Model [M]. In support of H[N], we find a significant [negative] three-way interaction term (β = [value], p < [threshold]). Figure [F2] plots the average marginal effect of [IV] across levels of [W], distinguishing [low Z] versus [high Z]. For [low Z] (solid line), [IV] exhibits a strong positive association when [W] is low; this marginal effect declines as [W] increases, eventually becoming non-significant. This suggests that in [low-Z] contexts, [W] substantially weakens—but does not overturn—the positive relationship. By contrast, for [high Z] (dashed line), [W] attenuates the effect more quickly: the association becomes non-significant at a [W] threshold of roughly [t_highZ], and at above-mean [W] becomes negative and statistically significant. Figure [F3] plots the [W] threshold required to neutralize [IV]'s effect across [Z]. The downward-sloping curve shows that as [Z] [matures/increases], the [W] required to offset the risk declines substantially ([t_lowZ] vs [t_highZ]).
**与原骨架差异**: 变体1 条件两向+t-test；变体2 ±1SD 表；变体3 线收敛。本变体是中和阈值随连续 Z 下降，并强制 “weakens but does not overturn”。
**诚实边界**: 三向支持 ≠ 两侧都能推翻；必须报告 weakens-but-does-not-overturn 的一侧。本文未报告正式简单斜率差异检验。

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **三向交互的黑箱报告** | 仅报告三向系数而不分解为条件两向交互 | 必须展示"在哪个Z水平上X×W交互显著"——否则审稿人无法判断三向交互的实质模式 |
| **三向交互图的过量信息** | 在一张图中画6条线（2×3条件组合） | 分面为2张两向交互图（按Z水平分面）或3张两向交互图（按W水平分面） |
| **三向显著却只报能推翻的一侧** | 只写晚期/高 Z 下反转，不报早期 weaken-but-does-not-overturn | 两侧都写；不推翻的一侧用 substantially weakens—but does not overturn |

## 诚实边界

- 三向交互的最低报告要求：(1) 三向项系数B/SE/p；(2) 条件两向交互（至少两个Z水平上的X×W交互）；(3) 至少一个简单斜率差异t-test确认"简单斜率差异只在特定条件下显著"；(4) 分面图（推荐）：按Z水平分面的两向交互图。
- 不对称发现（如H3a显著但H3b不显著）必须诚实报告两侧——不能仅报告显著侧而忽略不显著侧。
