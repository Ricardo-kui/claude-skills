## 证据节奏摘要

事件研究法 Results 的叙事核心是**窗口敏感性与多指标收敛**：不同于标准回归的"一个系数一个假设"，事件研究通常涉及多窗口（2-day, 3-day, 5-day）和多DV（CAR, ATV, sentiment），需要管理"预设窗不显著→替代窗显著"和"一个DV不显著→另一个DV显著"的复杂性。诚实报告+探索标签+三DV互补一致性是核心说服策略。

### 变体 1: R6 非显著主窗 → 替代窗探索 — 诚实+建设性处理 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**原始句锚点**: Table 6 shows that there was no significant interaction effect of CEO achievement expression and mortality salience on the two-day CAR (Model 1: B = 6.54, SE = 8.07, p = .418). We further analyzed different windows of CAR, and the results indicated a strong interaction effect on the five-day CAR following the event (online Appendix 6).
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R6
**骨架**:
> [Table N] shows that there was no significant interaction effect of [IV] and [moderator] on the [primary_window] [DV] (Model [N]: B = [value], SE = [value], p = [value]). We further analyzed different windows of [DV], and the results indicated a strong interaction effect on the [alternative_window] [DV] following the event ([appendix_location]).
**与原骨架差异**: 事件研究法中预设窗不显著但更宽窗（或不同窗）显著的情况很常见——市场反应可能因信息扩散延迟、流动性约束、或噪音交易者而滞后。Li et al. 的处理策略：(1) 诚实报告预设窗的 null result——不跳过、不淡化；(2) 立即过渡到 "We further analyzed different windows..."——暗示可能有延迟反应；(3) 将替代窗的结果放在 appendix 而非正文——既保持了透明性，又避免了"cherry-picking window"的批评。关键："further analyzed" 的措辞暗示探索性而非确认性——如果该替代窗是事前预设的，应使用 "we also examined a [N]-day window to capture delayed market reactions"。
**诚实边界**: 若预设窗（如 2-day）是唯一事前规定的窗，则替代窗（如 5-day）的显著结果应明确标记为 "exploratory" 或 "in supplementary analyses"——不能将其作为主证据重新解释。多窗分析最安全的做法是在 Methods 中预设一个窗范围（如 "we examine windows ranging from 2 to 7 days"），然后在 Results 中报告全范围。

**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (Production and Operations Management)
**原始句锚点**: The average value of suppliers' cumulative abnormal stock returns to a manufacturer-customer's recall is 0.40% on the day of the announcement (t = −3.181, p < .01). The insight is that the supplier's shareholders react punitively to a manufacturer-customer's recall.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: R2
**骨架**:
> Table [x] reports the event-study estimates of [actor] reactions to [event]. The average cumulative abnormal return in the [window] surrounding the event is [negative/positive] and statistically significant ([value], p < [threshold]), indicating that [event] is associated with a [percentage] change in [actor] value. Table [y] reports cross-sectional [OLS/FE] regressions in which [CAR_window] is regressed on [focal_predictor], [control_variables], and [fixed_effects]. Column I presents estimates that treat [endogenous_predictor] as exogenous; Columns II and III present the two-stage [control_function / Heckman] estimates, with Column II reporting the first-stage [logit/probit] and Column III reporting the second-stage coefficient.
**与原骨架差异**: 现有 R2 变体（OLS-FE 变体16/19/24）针对纯截面或面板回归的层次表导航。本骨架是**事件研究两阶段设计的专用导航**：先用一句话报告无模型证据（平均 CAR）建立基线市场反应，再过渡到解释变量如何调节该反应的截面回归表。关键：将"事件研究 → 截面回归"的因果链条在 R2 就明确，让读者理解为什么 DV 是 CAR 但估计器是 OLS/FE。

### 变体 10：分组 CAR 表格导航→假设裁决四拍（westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management 型）

**模板**:
> "The results in column [1] indicate significantly positive [excess returns] for all [adopting units] across all [N] time intervals, providing consistent evidence for [a favorable market reaction] to [the event], but the results reported in columns [2] and [3] of table [x] support H[x], which predicted that [units] would experience [a positive reaction] to [the event], whether or not [the implementation condition]. [Excess returns] are significantly positive for [group A] and for [group B] across all [N] intervals examined."

**来源**: westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management (ASQ), Results §Table 2 P1

**原文锚定**:
> "Excess returns are significantly positive for substantive adopters and for symbolic adopters across all four intervals examined."

**关键特征**:
- 列导航即裁决：column 1（全体）先给基线图景，columns 2-3（分组）才是假设检验对象——"but the results in columns two and three support H1" 用一个转折把描述性全体结果与假设裁决分开，全表四窗×七组的证据一段收拢
- 假设复述自带理论尾从句（"whether or not the plans were implemented"）——H1 的 decoupling 核心在裁决句中原文回响，读者无需翻回理论节
- 支持判断句不带系数不带 p 值，以"significantly positive across all four intervals"的全窗一致性作判断依据——分组 CAR 范式下显著性由表内星号承载，正文只负责节奏

**适用**: 以分组均值 CAR（而非回归系数）为主检验的事件研究；假设按"全体基线→关键分组对比"两步裁决的表格结构

**禁忌**: 全体基线结果与分组裁决必须分工明确（column 1 是铺垫不是主检验），不得把 column 1 的显著性冒充分组假设的证据；"whether or not"类理论尾从句必须与理论节假设表述逐字一致

**验证状态**: VERIFIED — expert_audit_override (user 2026-08-28: 单源足矣; paper_count=1)

### 变体 6: R3 — 分组 AR 多检验 + 组间对比四拍（model-free 主检验）(1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Chen, Ganesan & Liu 2009 (Journal of Marketing)
**source**: chenganesanliu2009
**skeleton_id**: `r3_eventstudy_subgroup_ar_multitest_contrast`
**原始句锚点**: Therefore, significantly negative abnormal stock return is associated with proactive recalls but not with passive recalls. ... Thus, our hypothesis is supported, suggesting that the stock market interprets a proactive product-recall strategy as a signal of severe financial losses.
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（单篇 section_variant）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-12
**槽位**: R3
**骨架**:
> Table [X], Panel A, shows that the average abnormal return for [strategy_A] is [signed_pct], which is consistently significant in the common t-test, the t-Patell test, and the t-BMP test. In contrast, none of the three tests are significant for [strategy_B]. We further compare the abnormal returns by conducting a two-sample t-test and a nonparametric Wilcoxon rank-sum test. As Table [X], Panel B, shows, [strategy_A] have significantly more negative abnormal returns than [strategy_B]. The difference is [signed_pct] (p < [threshold]). Overall, these results provide consistent evidence that [strategy_A] are associated with significantly more negative abnormal returns than [strategy_B]. On average, the abnormal return for [strategy_A] is approximately [N] percentage points lower than that for [strategy_B] (p < [threshold]). Thus, Hypothesis [H] is supported, suggesting that the stock market interprets [strategy_A] as a signal of [severe_loss_construct].
**与原骨架差异**: 区别于变体 3（CAR 为 DV 的**截面回归**四拍 + 基线锚定）——本变体是**回归前**的无模型分组 AR 主检验：组内多检验统计（t / Patell / BMP）→ 对照策略三检验全 null 正面报告 → 组间 t + Wilcoxon → 百分点差幅度翻译 → 支持判断 + 市场信号解读。主假设在截面回归之前即裁决；百分点幅度嵌入 R3，无需独立 R5。
**诚实边界**: 分组 AR 的显著性支持市场反应差异，不等于截面因果；策略效应仍须截面/选择修正确认。因果措辞优先 `associated with`。不得只报显著组 AR、跳过对照策略的 null AR。

**适用**: 事件研究以策略/组别对比为 H1；主证据是 model-free 分组 AR/CAR 而非回归系数。

### 变体 7: R7 — Heckman null-λ → 原截面策略估计无偏确认 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Chen, Ganesan & Liu 2009 (Journal of Marketing)
**source**: chenganesanliu2009
**skeleton_id**: `r7_eventstudy_heckman_null_lambda_unbiased_confirmation`
**原始句锚点**: As Model 2 in Table 5 shows, this is indeed the case. The coefficients of the self-selection bias parameters βλ1 and βλ0 are not significantly different from zero for the proactive subsample and the passive subsample.
**验证状态**: VERIFIED（单篇 section_variant）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-12
**槽位**: R7
**骨架**:
> If firm characteristics are unobservable to the researcher but affect both market outcomes and firm strategy, self-selection bias may arise in estimating the impact of [strategy] on [AR/CAR] ([citations]). We carry out the Heckman two-step estimation to show that the cross-sectional analysis is not subject to self-selection bias. The first stage is a probit on [strategy_choice]. For identification, we exclude [exclusion_vars] from the second-stage OLS because there is no a priori reason to believe that they significantly influence [AR/CAR], which is also confirmed by [prior_table]. As [Table], Model [N] shows, the coefficients of the self-selection parameters λ are not significantly different from zero for the [strategy_A] subsample and the [strategy_B] subsample. Therefore, the cross-sectional model yields an unbiased estimate of the effect of [strategy] (i.e., [strategy_A] remain associated with more negative [AR/CAR]). Together with the significance of [strategy] in [Eq.], the Heckman results further demonstrate that [strategy] are key influencers of abnormal stock returns, regardless of firm and product characteristics.
**与原骨架差异**: 区别于变体 5（多检验紧凑枚举 + consistent 收尾）——本变体是**单一 self-selection threat** 的完整 Heckman 叙事：威胁定位 → 两步估计 → 显式 exclusion restriction（a priori + 前表确证）→ 双 subsample null λ →「原截面策略估计无偏」收束。不是再报一遍主系数。

**适用**: 事件研究 → 截面策略选择；审稿人可能质疑策略内生/self-selection。

### 变体 9：检验统计量选择的三重背书（模拟证据 + 惯例先例）（westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management 型）

**模板**:
> "To assess the significance of [excess returns] for each group, we used the following test statistic suggested by [author] ([year]): [formula], where [A_t] is the average cumulated [return measure] over the relevant observation period, and [S(A_t)] is the time-series standard deviation of [excess returns] over a [N]-day estimation period. When [assumptions hold], this statistic has a [distribution] under the null hypothesis. Moreover, simulation studies suggest that this test statistic is well-specified for samples of [N] or more and for observation periods of various lengths ([citations]). This estimation approach is used in most of the prior event studies of [the event] and has become a standard event methodology in the [field] literature ([citations])."

**来源**: westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management (ASQ), Analysis §Analyzing Stock Market Reactions

**原文锚定**:
> "Moreover, simulation studies suggest that this test statistic is well-specified for samples of 50 or more and for observation periods of various lengths (Dodd and Warner, 1983; Brown and Warner, 1985). This estimation approach is used in most of the prior event studies of LTIP adoption..."

**关键特征**:
- 统计量选择不靠引用一句了事，而是三重背书链：原始出处（Brown and Warner 的统计量本身）→ 模拟证据限定适用域（样本量 ≥N、窗长各异时 well-specified）→ 惯例先例（本领域多数同类研究采用）——把"你为什么用这个检验"一次封死

**适用**: 事件研究中分组均值 AR/CAR 显著性检验（t 检验类）的方法选择声明；任何需要为非回归检验统计量辩护的补充分析段

### 变体 11：调节效应的分组对比 t 检验裁决（westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management 型）

**模板**:
> "[H#] predicted that when [the event] is accompanied by [the moderator], it would engender a more favorable [response], irrespective of [the implementation condition]. Results on [the outcome] for cases of [the event] with and without [the moderator] provide strong and consistent evidence for H#: [events] providing [the moderator] elicit more positive [responses] than [events] without [the moderator], and this holds true for both [category A] and [category B]. T-tests indicated highly significant differences between [category A] with [the moderator] and [category A] without [the moderator] for each of the [N] [windows]; values ranged from [t1] to [t2]."

**来源**: westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management (ASQ), Results §Table 2 P2

**原文锚定**:
> "T-tests indicated highly significant differences between symbolic adoption with an agency explanation and symbolic adoption without an agency explanation for each of the four time windows; values ranged from 8.69 to 11.98."

**关键特征**:
- 调节效应不用乘积项而用组间对比检验裁决：先方向陈述（"elicits more positive... holds true for both"），再正式检验（"T-tests indicated highly significant differences"）——方向→检验→区间报告值（t 从 8.69 到 11.98）三拍，把"差异是否统计显著"从暗示升级为证据
- "for each of the four time windows" 的逐窗复现声明 + 区间式报告（values ranged from）——多窗结果不逐窗罗列数值而用区间压缩，节奏不因窗口数膨胀
- "strong and consistent evidence for H2" 的强度评级前置——先给证据强度定性，再给具体检验，读者带着结论读细节

**适用**: 二分调节×二分处理的分组均值对比设计（四格拆分的 Results 侧兑现）；事件研究中调节效应需正式组间检验的场景

### 变体 12：交互显著后的主效应保护段（westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management 型）

**模板**:
> "The fact that [events] without [the moderator] show weaker positive [responses] should not be interpreted as suggesting that [the moderator] is more important than [the event] itself. [The event], whether [form A or form B], is a prerequisite to providing [the moderator], since [the moderator] points specifically to [the event] in claiming that [the theoretical claim]. Also, as [methodologist] ([year]: [pages]) noted in their discussion of main effects and interaction effects, knowing that a positive relationship between [X] and [Y] (i.e., [the predictor] and [the outcome]) is greater in the presence of [W] (i.e., [the moderator]) does not 'diminish the utility of the information' that there is an average main effect. The results show that [the event] leads to [the response] (H[x]) and that the presence of [the moderator] is a powerful moderator of that positive relationship."

**来源**: westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management (ASQ), Results §Table 2 P2（段内转折）

**原文锚定**:
> "The fact that LTIP adoptions without an agency explanation show weaker positive excess returns should not be interpreted as suggesting that verbal explanations are more important than LTIP adoption."

**关键特征**:
- 反直觉误读预辩：先预料读者会把"无调节时反应更弱"误读为"调节比事件本身更重要"，用 "should not be interpreted as" 一句拦截——负向比较结果的双重解释在段内就地裁决
- 结构性不可分论证（prerequisite logic）：调节变量在逻辑上依赖主效应存在（"no explanation without an adoption"），主效应与交互不是竞争关系而是层级关系——比单纯引用统计惯例更硬的保护
- 引方法论权威（Jaccard et al. 对主效应/交互效应的讨论）给"交互显著不贬损主效应用途"背书，再以 "H1 + powerful moderator" 的双判决句收束——主效应与调节同段各得其所，不违反"交互后主效应不可独立解释"反模式（这里主效应由 prerequisite 逻辑与 H1 裁决句承接，非再解读系数）

**适用**: 调节变量与主效应在逻辑上绑定（框架依附于事件、披露依附于行为）的交互设计；审稿人可能用交互显著性贬损主效应贡献的场景

**禁忌**: 保护段只在主效应构念上先于调节变量存在（定义性前提）时合法——纯统计意义的 main effect 不得借本模板复活；双判决句必须与表格证据对应，不得为保护主效应虚报其显著性

### 变体 13：假设范围外模式的"最强检验"框架（westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management 型）

**模板**:
> "Although our hypotheses were limited to [the focal condition], this pattern of results also applies to [the broader condition], which is also as we would expect. Our prediction is not that [the focal condition] will have a larger impact on [the outcome] than [the broader condition] but, rather, that they too can have a significant effect on [the outcome]. While the results show that [the moderator] also accentuates the market's response to [the broader condition], the significant hypothesized effect of [the moderator] for [the hardest case]—[adopted but not implemented]—provides a particularly strong test of the consequences of [the focal claim]."

**来源**: westphal_zajac_1998_symbolic_management (ASQ), Results §Table 2 P3（假设范围外延伸段）

**原文锚定**:
> "the significant hypothesized effect of agency explanations for decoupled LTIPs—adopted but not implemented—provides a particularly strong test of the consequences of symbolic management."

**关键特征**:
- "particularly strong test" 修辞：把假设未覆盖却在最难情形（形式采纳、无实施）中成立的结果，从"越界发现"重构为"焦点主张的最强检验"——范围外证据转化为可信性增益而非叙事漏洞
- 预测限定句先立边界（"Our prediction is not that symbolic actions will have a larger impact... but, rather, that they too can have a significant effect"）——防止读者把"实质与符号效应相当"误读为假设失败
- 让步结构（"While the results show that... also accentuates..."）先承认范围外模式同样出现，再把焦点拉回最难情形——承认与强调两拍俱全，不回避也不越界声称

**适用**: 核心构念在假设范围外的对照组中也显著、且该对照组恰是主张的最难情形的设计（decoupling/wide-brush symbolic claims）；假设边界易被误读为预测失败的场景

**禁忌**: "particularly strong test" 只能用于最难情形证据确凿且方向与焦点主张一致时；范围外结果若与理论预期冲突，必须如实报告为边界发现而非包装为强检验

> 源自 Zajac & Westphal (2004, ASR)——把高潮答案放进图形而非表格：年度异常收益时间序列图先声夺人，让"市场反应从显著为负翻转为显著为正"一图看尽，再用期初 vs 期末的 t 统计量差异检验把"翻转不是噪音"钉死，最后用交叉引用把"符号反转与未实施证据同步增长"的悖论舞台搭给 Discussion。

**句式**:

```
Hypothesis [N] is tested in an event study analysis of [the focal events]. The
results are displayed in Figure [X]. We assess the significance of [excess

[X], [market reactions] were significantly positive in the [later period],
consistent with results from the prior event study literature. However, the
results also clearly show that [the response] became more positive over time
and were significantly negative in the [early years of the time period]. These
[negative responses] are significantly different from the [positive responses]
in the later period, consistent with Hypothesis [N] (the difference in
[test statistics] between the beginning and the end of the study period is
significant at the [p <= .01] level). This [shift] in [the response] from
[negative] to [positive] occurred even as [the countervailing evidence] was
growing over the same period, as shown in Figure [Y].
```

**关键特征**:
- 图形先导（figure-first）：段首即 "The results are displayed in Figure [X]"，主检验答案的载体是图不是表——时序符号反转靠图自明，四拍中的"方向"拍由图承担
- "However, the results also clearly show" 拐点句：先用一句惯例一致性（"consistent with results from the prior event study literature"）垫平预期，再转出反常识发现——高潮感来自转折而非悬念
- 跨期对照替代系数解读：全文不报任何回归系数，用 "significantly negative in the early years ... significantly different from ... in the later period (difference in t-statistics ... p <= .01)" 的期初 vs 期末差异检验完成显著性拍
- 悖论交叉引用收尾：末句把图 [X] 的符号反转与图 [Y] 的反向证据（未实施率上升）用 "even as" 并置，制造同步悖论张力，把解释权交给 Discussion——Results 高潮与 Discussion 钩子在此缝合

> "However, the results also clearly show that market reactions became more positive over time and were significantly negative in the early years of the time period. **诚实边界**: 图形先导不豁免数字责任——检验统计量、显著性水平、差异检验必须在正文或图注中可核查；"consistent with the prior literature" 垫句只适用于与先例同向的那部分结果，翻转部分不得借先例背书。

### 变体 15：竞争假设配对裁决 + "In effect" 实质收束（Rival-Pair Null Adjudication）（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: r6_rival_hypothesis_null_adjudication_punchline
build_type: R6 竞争对手 null 裁决型
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
confidence: medium（单源首次入库）
status: VERIFIED
verification_basis: "expert_audit_override (user 2026-09-06: Pollock/Westphal/Gulati 系单源即 VERIFIED)"
-->

> 源自 Zajac & Westphal (2004, ASR)——理论节配对推出的竞争假设（H[N] vs H[N]a）在 Results 侧以"支持—反证"连珠裁决：支持句报完方向与跨窗一致性后，紧跟 "Conversely, Hypothesis [N], the [rival-label] hypothesis, ..., is not supported"，把对手的 null 当作本方证据的另一半，再用 "In effect" 一句把裁决升维成超越两个假设的实质结论。

**句式**:

```
The results in Table [Z] consistently support Hypothesis [N]a: [the predictor]
is [positively] associated with [the outcome], for all [three] event periods.
Conversely, Hypothesis [N], the [rival-label] hypothesis, which argued that
[rival mechanism], is not supported. Note that [the co-predictor] is also
associated with [more positive outcome]. In effect, [the predictor family]
predicts [the outcome] regardless of whether [the rival-relevant condition
holds].

The results in Table [Z] also show support for Hypothesis [M]a. In particular,
after controlling for [context controls], [the predictor] is [positively]
associated with [the outcome]. This result, which holds for all [three] event
periods, is consistent with our contention that [theoretical contention].
Accordingly, [the rival-label] hypothesis (Hypothesis [M]) is not supported.
```

**关键特征**:
- 支持与反证连珠：竞争对的裁决不分居两段而是同段连发——"consistently support Hypothesis [N]a ... Conversely, Hypothesis [N] ... is not supported"，null 不是歉意附注而是裁决的另一半
- 竞争对手点名制：null 句内嵌 rival 的理论标签与机制复述（"the market-learning hypothesis, which argued that ..."），读者不翻回理论节也知道被否决的是什么
- "In effect" 实质收束：裁决后用一句升维总结，把结果从两个假设的胜负提升为对整个预测元家族的实质判断（"predicts ... regardless of whether ..."），null 的建设性含义被显式兑现
- 跨窗一致性拍："for all three event periods / holds for all three event periods" 用多窗复现压缩报告，替代逐窗系数罗列

**适用**: 理论节以竞争假设对推演、Results 以同一回归表同时裁决双方的设计；null 方是理论对手而非自家假设失败的场景

**禁忌**: 竞争假设的 null 只能裁决理论胜负，不得改写为"证明了对手机制不存在"；"In effect" 收束句的概括范围不得超出已检验的预测元家族；rival 标签须与理论节假设表述逐字对应

**原文锚点**:
> "Conversely, Hypothesis 2, the market-learning hypothesis, which argued that markets would begin to discount repurchase announcements given accumulating evidence of nonimplementation, is not supported. ... In effect, the number of previously adopted plans predicts market reactions regardless of whether those plans were implemented."

### 变体 16：对手最佳反驳封堵段（"It might be suggested that..." Falsification Coda）（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

<!--
pattern_id: r8_rival_mechanism_falsification_battery
build_type: R8 竞争解释证伪封堵型
source_papers: ["zajac_westphal_2004_asr"]
confidence: medium（单源首次入库）
status: VERIFIED
verification_basis: "expert_audit_override (user 2026-09-06: Pollock/Westphal/Gulati 系单源即 VERIFIED)"
-->

> 源自 Zajac & Westphal (2004, ASR)——Results 收尾用一段"替怀疑者把话说完"的证伪封堵：以 "It might be suggested that ..." 亮出对手最后的退路（效率解释及其时变版本），再用一整套以 null 为载荷的补充分析（主效应 null、与时间交互 null、滞后结构稳健）逐一堵死，以 "Thus ... not an artifact of" 判决句回到主结论。这是稳健性从"防御"升级为"主动证伪"的终段形态。

**句式**:

```
It might be suggested that [the headline finding] obtains despite [the
countervailing evidence] because (1) [alternative explanation A], or (2)
[alternative explanation B]. To examine these possibilities, we conducted
supplementary analyses of [the rival mechanism]. We used [data] for a random
sample of [N] [units] from [frame] from [period]. We estimated [two measures
of the rival outcome] using [panel estimator], but results were robust to the
[alternative estimator]. As shown in Table [W] of the Appendix, [the focal
intervention] does not have a significant effect on either measure of [the
rival outcome]. The interaction of [intervention] and [time] is also not
significant: [the rival effect] does not increase or decrease over time. This
result was robust to the specification of [alternative lag structures]. Thus,
our findings regarding [the headline outcome] are not an artifact of [the
rival mechanism] or of the change in such [benefits] over time.
```

**关键特征**:
- 对手代言开场："It might be suggested that ..." 用怀疑者的口吻替他们陈述最好的反驳——反驳不是稻草人而是对手真正的最后退路，编号 (1)/(2) 列全其两个版本
- null 即载荷：封堵弹药不是显著的新效应而是三个独立 null（水平效应 null、时间交互 null、滞后结构稳健），"does not have a significant effect" 正面陈述而非回避
- 每条退路对应一记封堵：(1) 效率水平 → 主效应 null；(2) 效率随时间被学习 → 交互 null；滞后质疑 → 4-5 年滞后复现——fork 与封堵一一对应
- "not an artifact of" 判决句收束：null 证据被翻译回主结论的语言，明确说主发现不是 [rival 机制] 的假象——检验性 null 升级为对 headline 的保护

**适用**: 理论节存在被否决的竞争解释、且该解释隐含可检验的衍生预测（对手机制应产生某效应但没有）的设计；作为 Results 终段把稳健性升级为主动证伪

**禁忌**: 衍生预测必须真可能在对手理论下成立（非稻草人），并如实报告检验规格与样本；null 只封堵已陈述的解释版本，不得宣布"竞争理论被推翻"；补充分析样本与主样本不同（如 random subsample）须显式说明

**原文锚点**:
> "It might be suggested that stock market reactions to repurchase plans become more positive over time despite a lower likelihood of implementation because (1) the efficiency benefits from plans that were implemented increased over time ... Thus, our findings regarding stock market reactions to repurchase plans are not an artifact of the efficiency benefits of implementing the plans or of the change in such benefits over time."

### 变体 17：前置 Analysis 段设计参数双重正当化（Pre-Results Design-Parameter Defense）（zajac_westphal_2004 型）

> 源自 Zajac & Westphal (2004, ASR)——在 Results 之前设独立 Analysis 段，把估计器选择、序列相关修正与样本窗设计一次辩护到位；其中设计参数（实施窗口）的辩护用"经济逻辑 + 经验证据"双重正当化：先说经济上没有延迟理由、受众也不会预期延迟，再补一句量化经验事实（一年窗与三年窗的未实施率差异 <10%）。

**句式**:

```
[Prior literature] often [uses subgroup analyses]. Several authors have
advocated [the regression approach] instead, in order to control for possible
third variables ([citation]). Given that [the data structure], [threat] could
occur ([citation]). We use [primary correction] to correct for [the threat].
In separate analyses we use [alternative correction], and the results are very
similar ([citation]). ... To ensure that our results are not sensitive to this
research design, we conducted two sets of additional analyses [with
alternative parameter choices], and the results were consistent with those we
report below. A relatively short [window] seems justified, given that [units]
typically have no obvious [economic] reason for [the delayed behavior], and
[the relevant audience] would not expect such a delay. And from an empirical
standpoint, [units] that [comply] typically do so shortly after [adoption]
([quantified empirical detail]).
```

**关键特征**:
- 前置辩护位（pre-Results Analysis）：估计器与样本辩护不在稳健性尾段补救，而是在 Results 之前一次交代——读者带着"设计已被封死"的预设进入高潮，高潮段因此零杂音
- 双重正当化（economic + empirical）：设计参数先用经济逻辑辩护（"no obvious economic reason for delaying ... [the audience] would not expect such a delay"），再补经验证据（"increases by less than 10 percent when [the parameter] is measured over a [shorter] window"）——参数选择从作者偏好升格为逻辑必然
- 修正方法配对报告：主修正（[Cochrane-Orcutt]）+ 备选修正（[Prais-Winsten]）+ "the results are very similar"——每个威胁给主备两套处理而非单选
- 替代参数预算式声明：替代参数选择（[1 年窗]、[6 个月窗]）先于正文结果声明 "consistent with those we report below"，把潜在批评在高潮前消化

**适用**: 事件研究/回归设计中有研究者自定参数（实施窗口、事件窗、滞后结构）且该参数可能被质疑 cherry-picking 的场景；与主检验分离的前置 Analysis/Estimation 段写作

**禁忌**: 经验佐证必须来自真实计算（如窗宽敏感性重跑），不得以直觉数字代替；前置辩护不豁免 Results 内的稳健性段——样本选择威胁（如 Heckman）仍需单独处理；"results are very similar" 须与备注/附录中的实际结果一致

**原文锚点**:
> "A relatively short implementation window seems justified, given that firms typically have no obvious economic reason for delaying implementation, and investors would not expect such a delay. And from an empirical standpoint, firms that implement their plans typically do so shortly after adoption (e.g., the portion of plans that are not implemented increases by less than 10 percent when implementation is measured over a one-year window)."

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **窗选择性报告** | 仅报告显著的窗，不报告不显著的预设窗 | 预设窗无论是否显著都必须报告——替代窗的探索可在预设窗 null 后提及但必须在 appendix 中透明展示全范围 |
| **三DV结果选择性叙事** | CAR 不显著时转而强调 ATV/sentiment 的显著结果 | DV 角色应在 Methods 中预先明确（primary vs secondary），若 CAR 为 primary 但不显著，ATV/sentiment 的显著不能替代核心假设检验 |
| **事件窗数量膨胀** | 检验 10+ 个窗但未做多重比较校正 | 应在 appendix 中报告所有检验窗并讨论多重比较问题 |
| **只报显著组 AR、跳过对照 null** | 仅强调显著策略组的负 AR，不报告对照策略三检验全不显著 | 对照策略的 null AR 是对比证据，必须正面报告（变体 6） |

## 诚实边界

- 事件窗的选择必须在 Methods 中有理论基础（如 "2-day window is standard because..."），不能在 Results 中事后选择最优窗。
- 若多DV（CAR, ATV, sentiment）中仅部分显著——必须在 Discussion 中解释 DV 间的差异原因（如 "CAR captures price reaction which may be attenuated by noise traders; ATV captures overall market attention which is sensitive to even subtle cues"），而非忽略不显著者。
- ATV 和 sentiment 作为补充 DV 时，应定位为 "converging evidence" 而非 "alternative primary test"。
- **分组 AR ≠ 截面因果**：分组 AR 显著性支持市场反应差异，不等于策略的截面因果效应；仍须截面/选择修正确认。（变体 6）
- **Heckman null λ**：降低 self-selection 关切，不证明无内生性；因果语言保持 `associated with`。（变体 7）
