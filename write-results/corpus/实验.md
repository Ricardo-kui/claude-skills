### 变体 1: Experimental Main-Effect Cadence (ANOVA 五拍)
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: "We first checked for the presence of outliers for our dependent variable, idea attractiveness, and identified one outlier based on the Z-scores threshold of 2.5 SD (Meyvis & van Osselaer, 2017; van Selst & Jolicoeur, 1994). We removed this outlier from subsequent analyses. A one-way ANOVA showed that construal framing affected idea attractiveness (F [1,114] = 5.82, p = .017, η² = .049): novices evaluated the idea as more attractive in response to the abstract (M = 9.73, SD = 2.48) than the concrete (M = 8.56, SD = 2.71) framing. In support of H1, the results offer initial evidence that abstract framing construals increase the attractiveness of novel ideas for novices."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: R3
**骨架**:
> Hypothesis [x] predicted that [condition A] would elicit higher [DV] than [condition B] among [audience]. We first checked for outliers using [rule] and [removed/retained] [number]. A one-way ANOVA revealed a significant effect of [IV] on [DV], F([df1], [df2]) = [value], p [relation] [threshold], η² = [value]. Participants in [condition A] reported higher [DV] (M = [value], SD = [value], 95% CI [[lower], [upper]]) than those in [condition B] (M = [value], SD = [value], 95% CI [[lower], [upper]]). These results support Hypothesis [x].
**与原骨架差异**: 实验 ANOVA 段落必须包含五拍：(1) 异常值检查；(2) F/p/η²；(3) 条件均值/SD/CI；(4) 图/表引用；(5) 假设支持。η² 是实验论文常缺失但审稿人要求的效应量指标。关键：不要只报 F 和 p。

### 变体 2: Mediation Analysis Cadence (Hayes PROCESS)
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: "To examine the overall mediation model, we applied Hayes's (2013) PROCESS procedure (model 4) that uses bootstrapping to test the indirect effects. Mediation occurs when the 95% confidence interval of the indirect effects does not include zero. We conducted the mediation analysis by using 5,000 bootstrap iterations. While we found evidence for the indirect effect of construal framing on investment propensity through usefulness (b = 0.32, SE = 0.16, 95% CI [0.08; 0.74]), we found no evidence for the indirect effect of construal framing on investment propensity through both positive and negative affect (respectively, b = −0.02, SE = 0.08, 95% CI [−0.27; 0.08]; b = 0.03, SE = 0.07, 95% CI [−0.05; 0.27])."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: R8/R11
**骨架**:
> To examine whether [mediator] explains the effect of [IV] on [DV], we first tested whether [IV] influenced [mediator]. A one-way ANOVA showed that [condition A] led to [higher/lower] [mediator] (M = [value], SD = [value]) than [condition B] (M = [value], SD = [value]), F([df1], [df2]) = [value], p [relation] [threshold]. We also tested [alternative mediator 1] and [alternative mediator 2] and found no significant effect of [IV] (respectively, [F/p pattern]). We then estimated the indirect effect of [IV] on [DV] through [mediator] using Hayes's PROCESS model [4] with [number] bootstrap iterations. The indirect effect was b = [value], SE = [value], 95% CI [[lower], [upper]]. Because the confidence interval [does/does not] include zero, [mediator] [significantly/ does not] mediate the relationship. These findings suggest that [mechanism interpretation].
**与原骨架差异**: 实验中介分析段落必须完成四步：(1) mediator 是否受 IV 影响；(2) 替代机制是否被排除；(3) PROCESS 间接效应（b/SE/CI）；(4) CI 是否包含零的结论。关键：不要只报 total effect 或 Sobel test；Hayes PROCESS + bootstrap CI 是标准。

### 变体 3: R3 被调节的序列中介五拍报告 — PROCESS first-stage moderation (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**原始句锚点**: Supporting Hypothesis 1, the interactive effect of CEO achievement expression and mortality salience on agentic leader stereotypes was positive (B = .28, SE = .12, p = .024; see Figure 2). Simple slope analysis indicated that the effect of CEO achievement expression on agentic leader stereotypes was positive when mortality salience was higher (B = .248, SE = .088, p = .005), but nonsignificant when mortality salience was lower (B = −.034, SE = .088, p = .696).
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R3
**骨架**:
> [拍1-交互提醒]: Hypothesis 1 predicted that [moderator] moderates the positive relationship between [IV] and [mediator_1], such that the relationship becomes more positive when [moderator] is higher.
>
> [拍2-交互检验]: Supporting Hypothesis 1, the interactive effect of [IV] and [moderator] on [mediator_1] was [positive/negative] (B = [value], SE = [value], p = [value]; see Figure [N]).
>
> [拍3-简单斜率双侧]: Simple slope analysis indicated that the effect of [IV] on [mediator_1] was [positive/negative] when [moderator] was higher (B = [value], SE = [value], p = [value]), but nonsignificant when [moderator] was lower (B = [value], SE = [value], p = [value]).
>
> [拍4-M→Y序列路径]: Consistent with Hypothesis 2, [mediator_1] was positively related to [mediator_2] (B = [value], SE = [value], p < [threshold]). Also, in line with Hypothesis 3, [mediator_2] was positively related to [DV] (B = [value], SE = [value], p < [threshold]).
>
> [拍5-被调节的间接效应+CI]: Finally, in line with Hypothesis 4, we found a moderated mediation effect: The positive effect of [IV] on [DV] through [mediator_1] and [mediator_2] sequentially was stronger when [moderator] was higher (indirect effect = [value], SE = [value], 95% CI [[lower], [upper]]) versus lower (indirect effect = [value], SE = [value], 95% CI [[lower], [upper]]). The index of moderated mediation was significant (index = [value], SE = [value], 95% CI [[lower], [upper]]).
**与原骨架差异**: 标准四拍（方向→显著性→幅度→支持）在被调节的序列中介中被升级为**五拍**：(1) 交互提醒——重复理论预测的调节方向；(2) 交互检验——B/SE/p + 图引用；(3) 简单斜率双侧——**同时报告高/低调节水平**的 B/SE/p，非显著侧也诚实报告；(4) M→Y 序列路径——两条中介路径分别报告，标注 H2 和 H3 对应；(5) 被调节的间接效应+CI——条件间接效应（高 vs 低调节水平）分别给出点估计、SE、95% CI，最后以 index of moderated mediation 闭环。关键差异：被调节的中介必须报告 (a) index of moderated mediation（整体检验），(b) 条件间接效应（高/低调节水平分别的间接效应），两者缺一不可。仅报 index 审稿人无法判断中介在哪个调节水平上显著——这正是 Hayes (2018) 强调的"significant index does not tell you where mediation is significant."
**诚实边界**: 被调节的中介最低要求：(1) 交互检验的 B/SE/p；(2) 双侧简单斜率（含 p 值——非显著侧也必须报告）；(3) M→Y 每条路径的 B/SE/p；(4) index of moderated mediation + 95% CI；(5) 条件间接效应（高/低调节水平分别的 estimate + SE + CI）。缺少 (4) 或 (5) 均不足以支持被调节的中介结论。另外：若 total effect 和 direct effect 未被报告，应在脚注中说明原因。

### 变体 4: R4 三条件干预的层级拆解 — omnibus → 跨组 → 组内 → 稳定组（1篇高价值）

**来源论文**: Ilicic & Brennan 2026 (Journal of Marketing), Study 5
**原始句锚点**: As predicted, a significant interaction between political ideology and threat appeal was found (F(2, 433) = 11.89, p < .001, η² = .052). Post hoc comparisons revealed that conservatives (compared with liberals) reported a more favorable attitude toward smoking/vaping when the threat appeal was absent (F(1, 137) = 30.11, p < .001, η² = .180) and when a nonpersonally directed threat appeal was present (F(1, 142) = 17.46, p < .001, η² = .109).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-03
**槽位**: R3/R4

**骨架**:
> We first tested the [group × three-level intervention] interaction using [ANOVA/regression]. The omnibus interaction was [statistic and p]. We then decomposed it in a prespecified order. First, [Group A] differed from [Group B] under [baseline] and [generic intervention], but not under [high-relevance intervention]. Second, within [Group A], [generic intervention] changed [DV] relative to baseline, and [high-relevance intervention] produced an additional change relative to [generic intervention]. Third, within [Group B], the corresponding contrasts were not distinguishable from zero. This pattern supports the bounded claim that the intervention narrows the baseline group difference primarily by changing [Group A], with the strongest attenuation under [high-relevance form].

**报告顺序**:
1. 先报 omnibus interaction，证明三条件下组间模式并非同质。
2. 再报每个条件内的 Group A vs Group B，对应“差异是否被消除”。
3. 再报目标组内 baseline vs generic、generic vs intensified 的预设 contrasts，对应“干预是否逐级增强”。
4. 最后报告非目标组的相同 contrasts，建立稳定性而非把 null 隐藏。
5. 配图只用于解释模式，不能替代直接的 contrast 检验。

**诚实边界**:
- “某条件显著、另一条件不显著”不等于两条件效应显著不同；必须报告 interaction 或直接 contrast。
- 三水平处理不是两个独立 moderator，也不自动构成三向交互。
- 若多重 contrasts 未预注册，应标记探索性并说明 multiplicity 处理。
- 目标组显著、稳定组不显著只能支持有限的异质性叙事；若要声称稳定组“无效应”，需等效性检验或有意义的最小效应界限。

### 变体 5: R8 反转中介顺序作为竞争排序敏感性检查（1篇高价值）

**来源论文**: Ilicic & Brennan 2026 (Journal of Marketing), Study 4
**原始句锚点**: To verify the causal ordering of variables, we conducted a second serial mediation analysis reversing the order of the mediators (Fairchild and McDaniel 2017), testing whether political ideology was associated with perceived product danger (M1), which in turn was associated with sense of agency (M2), and subsequently with gambling severity. The results showed that the 95% bootstrapped CI for the indirect effect of political ideology on gambling severity included zero (effect = −.01, SE = .001, 95% CI = [−.01,.01]), indicating no significant serial mediation effect.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-03
**槽位**: R8

**骨架**:
> The proposed serial indirect effect through [M1] and [M2] was [estimate, SE, bootstrap CI]. We next estimated the competing sequence [X → M2 → M1 → Y]. Its bootstrap interval [included/excluded] zero, providing [no/some] evidence for that alternative sequence. The pattern is more consistent with the proposed ordering when considered alongside the theoretical argument and any temporal or experimental evidence; the reverse-order comparison alone does not establish causal order.

**与原骨架差异**: 该变体保留 reverse-order analysis 的诊断价值，同时撤除“反向不显著即可排除替代因果排序”的过度主张。它要求把三个命题分开：主序列是否有间接证据、竞争序列是否有证据、两者是否真的彼此不同。

**诚实边界**: 横截面 measurement-of-process 无法凭 PROCESS 路径建立时间优先性；显著与不显著的差异不等于显著差异；若 DV 在中介之前测量，更不得将路径顺序叙述为发生顺序。详见 `SEM-moderated-mediation.md` 的 competing-order sensitivity 条目。

### 变体 6：R3 并行多中介 PROCESS model 7 判决报告（raithel2024 型）

> 论证角色：Evidence（多候选中介在同一 PROCESS run 中并行进入，使焦点中介的判决以竞争对手在场为条件；index of moderated mediation 逐中介交付，条件间接效应按调节变量低/中/高三档枚举）

**模板**:
> "We employed the SPSS bootstrapping macro developed by Hayes ([year], model [7]) with [N] bootstrap samples and entered all [K] potential mediators as parallel mediators. This allowed us to test (1) if our two focal mediators [mediator_A] and [mediator_B] mediate the effect of [IV] on [outcome] and (2) if [moderator] moderates these two mediators. Results revealed that [mediator_B] (β = [value], SE = [value]; 95% CI: [lower], [upper]) mediated the impact of [IV] on [outcome], supporting H[a], whereas [mediator_A] (β = [value], SE = [value]; 95% CI: [lower], [upper]) did not, failing to support H[b]. The interactions of [IV] and [moderator] were significant for our two focal mediators ([mediator_A]: β = [value], p = [value]; [mediator_B]: β = [value], p = [value]). Most importantly, the index of moderated mediation was only significant for [mediator_A] (95% CI: [lower], [upper]) and [mediator_B] (95% CI: [lower], [upper]), indicating that the effect of [IV] on [outcome] is not only mediated by [mediator_A] and [mediator_B] but also moderated by [moderator], supporting H[c] and H[d]. Specifically, for [mediator], the indirect effects for [low-moderator units] (β = [value], SE = [value]; 95% CI: [lower], [upper]) and [medium-moderator units] (β = [value], SE = [value]; 95% CI: [lower], [upper]) were not significant, whereas the indirect effect for [high-moderator units] (β = [value], SE = [value]; 95% CI: [lower], [upper]) was significant. Finally, the direct effect of [IV] became non-significant when the mediators and the moderator were added (β = [value], SE = [value], p = [value])."

**节奏标记**: [方法设定+并行中介双目标] → [逐中介 a-path 判决对偶（supported/failed 同构句式）] → [双中介交互系数并报] → [逐中介 index + low/medium/high 条件间接效应枚举] → [直接效应塌缩收口]

**来源**: raithel_2024_product_recall_effectiveness_and_consumers_part (JAMS), Study 2/3 Results Moderated mediation 段

**原文锚定**:

> "Most importantly, the index of moderated mediation was only significant for perceived benefits (95% CI: 0.011, 0.330) and self-efficacy (95% CI: 0.032, 0.266), indicating that the effect of remedy on participation likelihood is not only mediated by perceived benefits and self-efficacy but also moderated by reputation, supporting H7a and H7b."

**关键特征**:

- 并行中介竞争设计：[K] 个候选中介进入同一模型，"mediated / did not" 判决隐含"在其余中介在场条件下"——机制判别力强于把替代中介逐个用单独检验排除（对照变体 2）

- 判决对偶句式："X mediated ..., supporting H[a], whereas Y ... did not, failing to support H[b]"——支持与失败共用同构句式，双假设一拍并决，不另起炉灶

- "Most importantly" 把 index of moderated mediation 定位为该段最高证据层级；"only significant for" 的 only 在五候选中介中完成判别——其余候选中介不另占版面即被淘汰

- 条件间接效应按 low/medium/high 三档枚举：medium 桥档显式展示显著性从无到有的切换位置，比高/低两档更能定位机制边界

- 直接效应塌缩句（"became non-significant when the mediators and the moderator were added"）作为全中介证据收口；跨研究重复同一 run 结构时，两 study 判决互换（S2 self-efficacy 传导 / S3 benefits 传导）本身即机制边界证据，但须显式讨论而非留白

**适用**: 实验或多观测设计中有多个理论化候选机制、需判别哪些中介真正传导效应的 first-stage moderated mediation（PROCESS model 7 并行多中介）；多研究论文用同构 run 跨 study 重复检验时尤佳。

**禁忌**: 条件间接效应的 CI 与叙述判词必须一致——原文两处反例（Study 2 与 Study 3 的 benefits 高声誉档 95% CI 含零却写 "was significant"）为反模式，采用时修正；置信水平不得随结果切换（原文 Study 3 self-efficacy index 静默改用 90% CI 判显著——反模式，须全文统一并显式声明）；index 显著而 a-path 中介不成立的组合不得合并宣称中介成立；序列中介用变体 3、第二阶段调节 index 用 SEM-moderated-mediation 变体 11，与本变体（并行 + first-stage）互斥。

### 变体 7：R3 实验主效应 marginal/null 校准判决（raithel2024 型）

> 论证角色：Evidence（主效应未达常规显著或仅边际显著时的诚实判决拍——方向声称与差异声称分离，判决词按证据强度降档，null 与既有证据链对齐）

**模板**:
> "[IV label]: [Condition A] directionally achieved higher [outcome] (M_[A] = [value], SD = [value] vs. M_[B] = [value], SD = [value]; F([df1], [df2]) = [value], p = [value], d = [value]), providing marginal support for H[x]. / In line with the [prior evidence source] (Study [N]), H[y] is not supported."

**节奏标记**: [条件均值对比 + F/p/d 效应量] → [marginal support 校准判决 或 not supported 判决] → [跨证据源 null 对齐（可选）]

**来源**: raithel_2024_product_recall_effectiveness_and_consumers_part (JAMS), Study 2/3 Results 主效应段

**原文锚定**:

> "Recalls with high incident likelihood directionally achieved higher recall effectiveness (M_high=5.05, SD=1.84 vs. M_low=4.74, SD=1.88; F(1,369)=2.97, p=0.101, d=0.17). In line with the secondary data (Study 1), H2 is not supported."

**关键特征**:

- "directionally achieved higher" 把方向主张与差异主张分离——不显著时仍可报告方向，但不声称条件间存在差异

- p 落在 0.05–0.10 区间时判决词降档为 "marginal support for H[x]"（原文 p=0.078），并在段尾 Overall 支持清单中保留 marginal 档与 supported 分层（"marginal support for H1" 与 "support for H5b" 并列不混计）

- 报告 Cohen's d 而非只报 F/p——边际证据的实质解读由效应量支撑（d=0.17/0.18 小效应与 marginal 判决自洽）

- "In line with the secondary data (Study 1)" 把本实验的 null 锚定到既有证据链：null 不是孤立失败，而是与先前证据一致的模式——跨证据对齐使不支持判决更可信，也为"哪个研究该为 null 负责"提供定位

**适用**: 多研究论文后续实验中复制主效应检验、结果为 null 或边际显著时的判决段；与变体 1（显著主效应 ANOVA 五拍）构成同一文件的主效应判决对。

**禁忌**: marginal support 只可用于 p 明确报告且落在预声明区间的项，段尾支持清单必须保留 marginal 与 supported 分层，不得把边际项计入显著支持（对照 ap_did_marginal_p_as_full_support 同族反模式）；跨研究对齐句要求两项证据同构（同构念、同方向），不得把异质 null 包装为"一致"；显著主效应用变体 1 完整五拍（含 η² 与异常值检查），本变体只接管 marginal/null 情形。
