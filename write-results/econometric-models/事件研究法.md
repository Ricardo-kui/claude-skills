# 事件研究法 — Results 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-results/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-R*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `事件研究法` 专用变体）。

## 证据节奏摘要

事件研究法 Results 的叙事核心是**窗口敏感性与多指标收敛**：不同于标准回归的"一个系数一个假设"，事件研究通常涉及多窗口（2-day, 3-day, 5-day）和多DV（CAR, ATV, sentiment），需要管理"预设窗不显著→替代窗显著"和"一个DV不显著→另一个DV显著"的复杂性。诚实报告+探索标签+三DV互补一致性是核心说服策略。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: R6 非显著主窗 → 替代窗探索 — 诚实+建设性处理 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Li, Chiu, Kong, Cropanzano & Ho 2026 (Journal of Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R6
**骨架**:
> [Table N] shows that there was no significant interaction effect of [IV] and [moderator] on the [primary_window] [DV] (Model [N]: B = [value], SE = [value], p = [value]). We further analyzed different windows of [DV], and the results indicated a strong interaction effect on the [alternative_window] [DV] following the event ([appendix_location]).
**与原骨架差异**: 事件研究法中预设窗不显著但更宽窗（或不同窗）显著的情况很常见——市场反应可能因信息扩散延迟、流动性约束、或噪音交易者而滞后。Li et al. 的处理策略：(1) 诚实报告预设窗的 null result——不跳过、不淡化；(2) 立即过渡到 "We further analyzed different windows..."——暗示可能有延迟反应；(3) 将替代窗的结果放在 appendix 而非正文——既保持了透明性，又避免了"cherry-picking window"的批评。关键："further analyzed" 的措辞暗示探索性而非确认性——如果该替代窗是事前预设的，应使用 "we also examined a [N]-day window to capture delayed market reactions"。
**诚实边界**: 若预设窗（如 2-day）是唯一事前规定的窗，则替代窗（如 5-day）的显著结果应明确标记为 "exploratory" 或 "in supplementary analyses"——不能将其作为主证据重新解释。多窗分析最安全的做法是在 Methods 中预设一个窗范围（如 "we examine windows ranging from 2 to 7 days"），然后在 Results 中报告全范围。

### 变体 2: R2 — 事件研究 CAR + 截面回归双表导航 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (Production and Operations Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: R2
**骨架**:
> Table [x] reports the event-study estimates of [actor] reactions to [event]. The average cumulative abnormal return in the [window] surrounding the event is [negative/positive] and statistically significant ([value], p < [threshold]), indicating that [event] is associated with a [percentage] change in [actor] value. Table [y] reports cross-sectional [OLS/FE] regressions in which [CAR_window] is regressed on [focal_predictor], [control_variables], and [fixed_effects]. Column I presents estimates that treat [endogenous_predictor] as exogenous; Columns II and III present the two-stage [control_function / Heckman] estimates, with Column II reporting the first-stage [logit/probit] and Column III reporting the second-stage coefficient.
**与原骨架差异**: 现有 R2 变体（OLS-FE 变体16/19/24）针对纯截面或面板回归的层次表导航。本骨架是**事件研究两阶段设计的专用导航**：先用一句话报告无模型证据（平均 CAR）建立基线市场反应，再过渡到解释变量如何调节该反应的截面回归表。关键：将"事件研究 → 截面回归"的因果链条在 R2 就明确，让读者理解为什么 DV 是 CAR 但估计器是 OLS/FE。
**诚实边界**: 平均 CAR 的"显著性"只能支持市场反应存在，不能用于假设检验；假设检验必须依赖截面回归中的解释变量系数。

### 变体 3: R3 — CAR 为 DV 的截面回归四拍 + 基线锚定 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (Production and Operations Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: R3
**骨架**:
> Hypothesis [x] predicted that [predictor] would [attenuate/aggravate] [actor] reactions to [event]. Model [y] of Table [z] shows that the coefficient for [predictor] is [positive/negative] and statistically significant (β = [value], p < [threshold]). In substantive terms, [predictor] is associated with a [N] percentage-point difference in [CAR_window] relative to the baseline [percentage] average reaction reported earlier. Thus, Hypothesis [x] is supported.
**与原骨架差异**: 现有 slot-R3 的 "GLM / 事件研究 CAR 专用" 变体已提供四拍，但缺少**基线锚定**——本论文在 R3 中回溯引用 Model-free results 的平均 CAR，将调节变量效应解释为"相对于平均市场反应的幅度变化"。这使经济显著性自然嵌入 R3，无需独立 R5 段落。适用于任何以 CAR 为 DV 的截面回归。
**诚实边界**: "relative to the baseline average reaction" 要求该基线必须在 Results 前文已报告（通常是 R1/R2 的 model-free evidence），不能首次在 R3 出现。

### 变体 4: R6 — 非显著控制变量 + 意外符号反转的理论解释 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (Production and Operations Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: R6
**骨架**:
> Additionally, although [predictor] exhibits the expected [negative/positive] coefficient, the association does not reach statistical significance (β = [value], p > .1), a finding consistent with prior research ([citation]). [In contrast / Separately], [second_predictor] is [negative/positive] and statistically significant (β = [value], p < [threshold]), which is contrary to our expectation that [theoretical_prediction]. We turned to [theory_A] and [literature_B] to make sense of this seemingly counterintuitive sign. [Theory_A] suggests that [mechanism], increasing uncertainty and punitive reactions. [Literature_B] has revealed that [contextual_pattern], reinforcing [mechanism].
**与原骨架差异**: 现有 R6 "方向相反诚实解释变体" 要求结果方向与假设相反且有理论解释。本论文扩展为**同一上下文下的双重处理**：(1) 对非显著控制变量诚实报告并引用前人研究佐证其常见不显著；(2) 对意外符号反转变量，从两个互补理论视角（如信号理论 + 行业文献）给出 post-hoc 解释。关键：用 "We turned to ... to make sense of" 明确理论调用是事后解释，不假装为事前假设。
**诚实边界**: 事后解释必须在 Discussion 中再次标记为 post-hoc；不能将探索性理论解释升级为正式假设。

### 变体 5: R7 — 事件研究法紧凑稳健性包 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Pupovac, Astvansh, Carrillat & Legoux 2026 (Production and Operations Management)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-21
**槽位**: R7
**骨架**:
> We undertake [N] robustness tests on the [full sample / subsample]. ([1]) We [treat_year_as_continuous / include_additional_covariates] because [reason, e.g., multicollinearity with year FE]. ([2]) We measure abnormal return using the [alternative_model, e.g., Fama-French] instead of the market model. ([3]) We [Winsorize / truncate] the outcome variable at the [percentile] to limit the influence of extreme values. ([4]) We exclude [clustered events / specific subsample] within the [window]. ([5]) We restrict the sample to [subsample, e.g., suppliers in a specific region]. The sign, magnitude, and significance levels of the estimated coefficients are consistent with those reported in [Tables X and Y]. [Appendix_table] presents the estimates.
**与原骨架差异**: 现有 R7 变体 2（叙事型逐威胁）和变体 15（五威胁标签化）适用于一般 OLS/FE。本骨架是**事件研究法 Results 的紧凑稳健性枚举**：将事件研究特有的威胁（异常收益模型选择、事件窗聚类、极端 CAR 值）与一般截面回归威胁（样本限制、协变量设定）打包。关键：用 "The sign, magnitude, and significance levels ... are consistent" 一句收尾，避免对每个检验重复叙述。
**诚实边界**: 若某稳健性检验改变了关键系数的显著性，不能用此统一收尾句掩盖；必须单独讨论。

## 反模式

| 反模式 | 表现 | 应做 |
|--------|------|------|
| **窗选择性报告** | 仅报告显著的窗，不报告不显著的预设窗 | 预设窗无论是否显著都必须报告——替代窗的探索可在预设窗 null 后提及但必须在 appendix 中透明展示全范围 |
| **三DV结果选择性叙事** | CAR 不显著时转而强调 ATV/sentiment 的显著结果 | DV 角色应在 Methods 中预先明确（primary vs secondary），若 CAR 为 primary 但不显著，ATV/sentiment 的显著不能替代核心假设检验 |
| **事件窗数量膨胀** | 检验 10+ 个窗但未做多重比较校正 | 应在 appendix 中报告所有检验窗并讨论多重比较问题 |

## 诚实边界

- 事件窗的选择必须在 Methods 中有理论基础（如 "2-day window is standard because..."），不能在 Results 中事后选择最优窗。
- 若多DV（CAR, ATV, sentiment）中仅部分显著——必须在 Discussion 中解释 DV 间的差异原因（如 "CAR captures price reaction which may be attenuated by noise traders; ATV captures overall market attention which is sensitive to even subtle cues"），而非忽略不显著者。
- ATV 和 sentiment 作为补充 DV 时，应定位为 "converging evidence" 而非 "alternative primary test"。
