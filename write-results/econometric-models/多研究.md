## 证据节奏摘要

多研究 Results 的叙事核心是**累积收敛**：每个研究独立报告后，必须有一个 synthesis 段落告诉读者“尽管有这些差异，模式一致”。节奏为：Study 1 主效应 → Study 2 复制/边界 → Study 3 扩展/机制 → Study 4 确认 → 跨研究综合。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: Cross-Study Synthesis（跨研究综合）
**来源论文**: Falchetti, Cattani & Ferriani 2022 (Strategic Management Journal)
**原始句锚点**: "Overall, Study 3 and Study 4 further confirm that the construal framing effect on novel idea evaluation is the opposite for novices and experts. Although these two experiments used different ideas, different modes of presentation, and different dependent variables, they replicated the results of Study 1 and Study 2. In sum, our four experimental studies offer supporting evidence that an abstract framing increases idea appreciation by novices, while a concrete framing increases idea appreciation by experts."
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇入库）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: R18/R19
**骨架**:
> Across [number] studies, the findings converge on a consistent pattern: [summary of supported hypotheses]. This convergence is notable because the studies varied [dimensions: ideas, presentation modes, dependent variables, audiences]. Study [1] showed [result with audience A and DV1]. Study [2] replicated the effect with [audience B and DV1]. Study [3] extended it to [DV2] and provided evidence for [mechanism]. Study [4] confirmed the pattern with [audience C and DV3]. Together, these results suggest that [theoretical claim] is robust across [boundary conditions], although [remaining limitation/boundary] warrants future research.
**与原骨架差异**: 多研究论文不能只是罗列结果后直接进入 Discussion。本骨架强制完成三步：(1) 陈述收敛模式；(2) 明确列出跨研究变异维度（idea/stimulus/DV/audience）；(3) 逐个简要总结每个研究的贡献；(4) 给出有边界的理论结论。关键：让读者相信多个小研究加起来比单个大研究更有说服力。

### 变体 4: R6/R9 跨研究差异嵌入 Results 讨论 — 差异承认+理论解释+替代解释 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ahmadi, Khanagha, Berchicci & Jansen 2017 (Journal of Management Studies)
**原始句锚点**: One difference between the two studies, however, was that only Study 1 showed a significant positive relationship between promotion focus and exploration. In Study 2, while the same coefficient is still positive, it is not statistically significant.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R6/R9
**骨架**:
> Therefore, the results of Study 2 are generally consistent with those of Study 1: [convergent_findings_summary]. One difference between the two studies, however, was that only Study 1 showed [finding_A], while in Study 2, [finding_A_replication_status: e.g., the same coefficient is still positive, it is not statistically significant]. This means that we cannot fully reject the null hypothesis for [hypothesis_label]. We believe this discrepancy arises from [theoretical_explanation: e.g., the difference in the work experience of the participants in the two experiments]. This is consistent with [citation], who also suggest that [parallel_finding]. In fact, [elaboration_of_explanation: e.g., the relationship in H1 is measured based on a work-related regulatory focus scale designed for individuals with work experience... the professionals, who had an average of ten years work experience, may evaluate their own persistent regulatory focus in a working context differently to students]. When we compare the two studies, we also observe that [additional_unexpected_difference: e.g., the regulatory focus of the organizational context has a significant effect on the exploration orientation of the students but not on that of the professionals], although we did not hypothesize an effect of this kind. This observation is interesting, as it might reside again in [same/different explanation]. An alternative explanation could be that [alternative_account: e.g., the company in Study 1 might have specific regulatory focus characteristics, and if so, the participants in Study 1 might have been affected by that].
**与原骨架差异**: 现有变体1-3均聚焦"多研究结果一致"的积极叙事。Ahmadi et al. 提供了更诚实和更有理论深度的模式——**在Results中直接讨论研究间差异**而非推迟到Discussion：(1) 先总结一致性（"results are generally consistent"），建立整体可信度；(2) 明确指出差异——"only Study 1 showed significant X... in Study 2, same coefficient positive but ns"；(3) 给出理论解释——"discrepancy arises from the difference in work experience"；(4) 引用平行文献支持该解释（Wang & Wong 2012）；(5) 详细推演因果链——"professionals had 10 years work experience → evaluate regulatory focus differently from students"；(6) 报告额外非预期差异——"context has effect on students but not professionals... we did not hypothesize this"；(7) 提供替代解释——"company in Study 1 might have specific characteristics"。关键策略：(a) 差异不是弱点而是理论洞察的来源——"work experience matters for how regulatory focus scales function"；(b) 诚实但不破坏核心结论——"generally consistent"先行；(c) 非预期发现被标注为"interesting observation"而非post hoc假设。适用于任何多研究设计中发现部分结果不一致的情况——特别是专业样本vs学生样本的场景。
**诚实边界**: 此模式仅适用于"整体一致+局部差异"的情况——若核心假设在两个研究中方向相反或都消失，不能使用此模式。差异解释必须引用平行文献（"this is consistent with X who also found..."），不能仅凭推理。替代解释必须被承认（"an alternative explanation could be..."）但不需要完全消解——可留给Discussion或future research。
### 变体 5: R9 逐研究 Discussion 接力立项 — 限制->下一研究补救 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Ilicic & Brennan 2026 (Journal of Marketing)
**原始句锚点**: A limitation of Study 1 is the lack of a nonaddictive product control, making it unclear whether conservatives’ favorable responses are specific to addictive products or reflect a general evaluative tendency. We address this question in Study 2.
**验证状态**: EMERGING
**写入日期**: 2026-07-22
**槽位**: R9
**骨架**:
> [本研究的核心贡献一句话]. [承认本研究设计的具体限制：缺少 X 对照 / 不能 disentangle Y / 仅 correlational / 仅 self-report]. [Study N+1 / Supplementary Study N+1] addresses this [question / limitation] by [新研究的设计特征：新增对照组 / 操纵而非测量 / 用真实行为 DV / 采用 moderation-of-process 而非 measurement-of-process]. [可选：补一句下一研究的预期或初步证据].
>
> 范例（Study 1->2）："A limitation of Study [1] is the lack of a [control category], making it unclear whether [focal finding] is specific to [target] or reflects a [general tendency]. We address this question in Study [2]."
**与原骨架差异**: 现有变体1-4分别聚焦跨研究综合（变体1）、研究开场复制声明（变体2）、三研究递进结果（变体3）、跨研究差异讨论（变体4）。本变体捕获**每个 Study Discussion 末段的固定三拍**：贡献->具体限制->下一研究如何补救（"We address this question in Study 2" / "Supplementary Study 2 complements" / "To test generalizability... Supplementary Study 4 adopts a moderation of process design"）。这是 consumer psychology / JCP / JM 多研究论文的标志性接力节奏——把多研究读成自我修正、自我升级的论证链，而非孤立研究的堆叠。区别于变体2（Study 开场"we replicate then extend"）——本变体是 Study **收尾**（"this study is limited by X, next study fixes X"）。
**诚实边界**: 接力立项的"限制"必须是真实的设计局限（如缺对照组、不能 disentangle、仅相关），不能是伪装成谦逊的吹嘘（"a limitation is that our sample was too large"）。承诺的"下一研究补救"必须真正在下一研究兑现，否则审稿人会追溯追责。

### 变体 6: R9 同一模型跨 facet-DV 双研究：核心收敛 + 边缘发散 + 发散由样本/情境差异解释 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Zhou, Gao & Zhao (2017, Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: Overall, the findings of Study 2 with the number of patents as the innovation output are largely consistent with the results of Study 1, which uses new product output ratio as the innovation measure. When SOEs are listed publicly, they rely primarily on the market to obtain capital resources, making state ownership less important for resource allocation.
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-30
**槽位**: R9
**骨架**:
> "The findings of Study [2] with [DV_2 — e.g., patent count] as the [outcome] measure are largely consistent with the results of Study [1], which uses [DV_1 — e.g., new product output ratio] as the [outcome] measure. [Core hypothesis H_x — e.g., H1b/H1c/H3]: [convergent—supported in both studies]. [However, hypothesis H_y — e.g., H1a/H2 — diverges]: in contrast with [Study 1's finding / our prediction], [the coefficient is not significant in Study 2]. [Theoretical explanation of the divergence, anchored in a sample/context difference]: this is consistent with our reasoning that [mechanism]—[e.g., when [firms] are [publicly listed], they [rely primarily on the market to obtain capital resources], making [state ownership] less important for [resource allocation]]. Thus, [the divergence itself becomes a finding that identifies a study-level boundary condition]."
**与原骨架差异**: 区别变体 4（跨研究差异嵌入 Results 讨论——ahmadi 差异承认+理论解释+替代解释）：本变体是**同一理论模型跨 facet-DV 双研究**（见 write-methods 多研究 变体7）的"**核心收敛 + 边缘发散 + 发散由样本/情境差异解释**"三段叙事。核心假设（H1b 效率、H1c 倒 U、H3 竞争）在两 study 收敛（稳健性），而**资源分配假设（H1a/H2）在 Study 2（上市企业）发散**——发散本身被解释为一个 study-level boundary condition（上市企业靠市场融资 → state ownership 的资源分配作用失效）。这把"跨 study 不一致"从弱点升级为"识别情境边界"的发现。关键：发散的解释锚定到两 study 的**样本/情境差异**（上市 vs 非上市），而非数据噪声。
**适用**: 同一模型跨样本/DV 复制且部分假设条件性成立的研究；两 study 样本在理论上相关的维度（上市状态、行业、国别）上不同。
**禁忌**: 发散不可默认为"Study 2 样本小/数据差"——必须给理论解释；收敛的假设与发散的假设须区分清楚（哪些是稳健的核心、哪些是条件性的）；不可只报收敛、隐瞒发散。

### 变体 7: R3/R9 方向冲突效应的时间视野裁决（2026-08-02）
**来源论文**: Bamberger, Homburg & Wielgos (2021, *Journal of Marketing*)
**原始句锚点**: These opposing indirect and direct effects reflect an inconsistent mediation (Aguinis, Edwards, and Bradley 2017), suggesting that the firm performance benefits from wage inequality are mitigated by the simultaneously harmed customer satisfaction. … Second, as hypothesized in H4b, the positive direct effect of wage inequality on firm profitability vanishes in the long run (β = .05, p > .10).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源）
**槽位**: R3/R6/R9
**骨架**:
> Study [1] separates the association of [IV] with [performance] into a [positive/negative] direct component and an opposing indirect component through [proximal stakeholder outcome]. The aggregated indirect association is [direction and significance, with bootstrap CI], while the direct association is [direction and significance]. Because these components point in opposite directions, we report the total association separately rather than inferring it from either component alone. Study [2] then evaluates whether this decomposition changes with the outcome horizon. At the [short] horizon, [direct component status] and [indirect component status]. At the [long] horizon, the direct component [persists / weakens / is not distinguishable from zero], whereas the indirect component [persists / accumulates]. Thus, the cross-study evidence supports [bounded headline: a transient direct benefit alongside a more durable stakeholder-mediated cost], while leaving [unresolved causal or null-effect question] open.

**报告顺序**:
1. 逐条报告 specific indirect effects，包括不显著路径，不跳过失败假设。
2. 报 aggregated indirect effect 及 bootstrap CI。
3. 报 direct effect；若方向相反，明确命名 inconsistent mediation / suppression（仅在统计条件满足时）。
4. 单独报 total effect，避免用显著 direct effect 代替净结论。
5. Study 2 按同一顺序比较短期与长期窗口，最后才给跨研究收束。

**与既有变体的差异**: `SEM-moderated-mediation.md` 已收录“不一致中介→抑制变量”的单研究报告。本变体不重复该统计模板，而是增加**跨研究、跨时间窗口的裁决结构**：第二项研究不是再次证明有中介，而是检验相反路径中哪一条更持久。

**诚实边界**:
- 长期 direct coefficient 不显著只能写“we do not detect a persistent direct association”，不能写“the direct effect is zero”；若理论假设要求无效应，需等效性检验或有意义的最小效应界限。
- FE/SEM 结果用 association / predicts / is consistent with；不得把滞后或多来源数据写成 causal proof。
- 若 total effect 边际显著、跨规格不稳定或 CI 跨零，必须标记为 mixed / imprecise，不得用显著 direct effect 覆盖。
- 经济/实质幅度应在 R3/R5 报告；若原文只在 Discussion 量化，蒸馏时将其记录为可改进点，不把缺失本身固化为模板。

### 变体 8: R3/R9 按理论命题建立跨研究证据账本（2026-08-02）
**来源论文**: Reinwald, Kanitz, Bamberger, Backmann & Hoegl (2026, *Organization Science*)
**原始句锚点**: We did not find significant interaction effects between political dissimilarity and the election event on lie-sum (coefficient = 0.36, p = 0.11) and forward-sum (coefficient = −0.14, p = 0.37). Consistent with this hypothesis, we observed a significant interaction between political dissimilarity and the election term for both perspective-taking (coefficient = −0.31, p < 0.001) and empathic concern (coefficient = −0.23, p = 0.007).
**验证状态**: EMERGING（单篇来源；仅作 `section_variant`）
**槽位**: R3/R6/R9
**骨架**:
> Across the studies, evidence should be summarized by theoretical claim rather than by counting “successful studies.” The direct [difference × event] effect on [behavioral outcome] is [supported in Study 1 / not distinguishable from zero in Studies 2–3]. In contrast, the event-contingent effect on [proximal mediator] is [replicated across Studies 2–3], and the conditional indirect effect on [outcomes] is present after the event but not before it. Postevent trend tests show [no detected attenuation] within the observed windows, while end-of-window contrasts remain [status]. Exploratory subgroup differences are reported separately and do not alter the status of the preregistered claims.

**最小证据账本**:

| 理论命题 | Study 1 | Study 2 | Study 3 | 可允许的收束 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| direct `X × event → Y` | 支持 | 不支持/混合 | 不支持 | 直接行为效应不一致 |
| `X × event → M` | 未测 | 支持 | 两个机制维度均支持 | 近端机制复制 |
| conditional indirect effect | 未测 | 事件后支持、事件前不支持 | 事件后支持、事件前不支持 | 条件间接效应一致 |
| postevent duration | 短窗未见衰减 | 未检验 | 三周内未见衰减且末端差异仍在 | 观察窗内未检测到衰减 |
| partisan asymmetry | 不支持 | 不支持 | 仅 empathic concern 出现 | 探索性、维度特定 |

**报告规则**:
1. 把 direct、mediator、indirect、duration、subgroup 分开判定；不得用中介证据替直接效应“翻案”。
2. 每个不支持结果保留方向、区间或 p 值，不用“largely supportive”遮蔽。
3. 条件间接效应可在 direct/total effect 不显著时成立，但结论限于机制一致性，不等于处理对行为的直接作用复制。
4. 不显著 posttrend 写作 “we did not detect attenuation over [window]”；如要证明近似为零，需等效性检验。

**与变体 1/4 的差异**: 变体 1 强调跨研究收敛，变体 4 解释局部差异；本变体先拆分理论命题，再允许某些命题收敛、另一些不收敛。它尤其适合 moderated mediation 在多研究中比 direct interaction 更稳定的证据结构。
