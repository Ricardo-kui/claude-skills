## 主骨架

目前 `write-results/SKILL.md` 中暂无专门的定性 Findings 主骨架。本文件作为首批 EMERGING 变体，待第二篇**纯定性过程研究**论文交叉验证后，建议提升为独立主骨架。

## 设计特征摘要

定性过程研究的 Findings（通常不称 Results）叙事核心是**阶段性过程模型与关系空间的对照呈现**：读者需要看到一个清晰的 process model，每个阶段由什么事件触发，集体在前台做什么、个体在后台做什么、哪些矛盾在侧台被部分公开。典型结构包括：(1) 过程模型总览；(2) 逐阶段展开（前台行动→后台生存→侧台协商）；(3) 按受众的有限成功评估。

## 累积变体

### 变体 1: 过程模型总览段落
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: A key finding of our study is that the stigma reduction process was a contentious, multiphased effort involving front-stage collective actions that convey the industry’s moral agenda and practices, backstage organizational coping activities by medical cannabis entrepreneurs and dispensary owners that conflicted with the message presented on the front stage, and collective side-stage negotiations. Figure 2 illustrates that stigma reduction unfolded through three partially overlapping phases: initiating a moral agenda, moral prototyping, and morality infusion.
**验证状态**: 单来源（Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 无对应定性 Findings）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: F1
**骨架**:
> Our findings suggest that [process] unfolds through [number] partially overlapping phases: [phase names]. We use [analytical lens] to understand how [collective actors] and [individual actors] pursue [competing goals] across different relational spaces. [Figure/Table x] summarizes our data structure and the resulting process model.
**与原骨架差异**: 定量 Results 通常以假设检验为骨架，而定性 Findings 必须以过程模型为骨架。关键：一开始就亮出阶段名称和分析透镜（如 front-stage / backstage / side-stage），让读者知道后续章节的组织逻辑。

### 变体 2: 前台—后台对照段落
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: Overall, to distance medical cannabis from stigmatizing labels and stereotypes, on the front stage the collective positioned the medical cannabis category as the antithesis of the black market category and as the solution to the “black market problem.” … Thus entrepreneurs and dispensary owners engaged in backstage activities that departed from the moral prototype that was being constructed on the front stage but allowed them to fortify their resource position, presumably until crucial resources were more widely available.
**验证状态**: 单来源（Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 无对应定性 Findings）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: F3/F4
**骨架**:
> On the front stage, the collective [action] to signal [value/identity]. For example, [actor type] [practice], which projected [intended meaning]. While the collective publicly [front-stage action], individual organizations often [backstage action] to access [resource]. This created a tension between [collective goal] and [organizational survival need]. Actors reconciled this tension by [justification], treating backstage actions as necessary until [condition changes].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是定性过程研究的核心修辞装置——同一段落内并置公开话语与私下行为，凸显理论与经验的张力。关键：不要分别写“前台一节、后台一节”，而是要在同一阶段内对照呈现，才能显示过程的张力。

### 变体 3: 侧台协商段落
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: Situated between the front-stage collective actions and the backstage organizational actions were side-stage debates that resulted from goal incongruence and disagreements over how to normalize the industry. These activities were side-stage because while the messiness of the disagreements was not in full public view, the public could see evidence that they were occurring.
**验证状态**: 单来源（Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 无对应定性 Findings）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: F5
**骨架**:
> These debates were side-stage because [why partially visible]. Public confrontations over [issue] revealed [fissures among actors], but they also allowed the collective to clarify [norm/expectation]. Although messy, these negotiations were part of establishing [shared prototype/agenda].
**与原骨架差异**: 侧台不是“噪音”，而是规范澄清的场所。关键：说明为何这些冲突是部分可见的、它们暴露了哪些内部分裂、以及它们如何反而帮助集体确立共享原型。

### 变体 4: 受众区分的有限成功评估
**来源论文**: Lashley & Pollock 2020 (Administrative Science Quarterly)
**原始句锚点**: This means that destigmatization can also be achieved with some audiences and not others. This industry is not yet fully destigmatized, so in assessing the amount of stigma reduction in our context, we argue that the relevant audiences for the medical cannabis industry are state and federal governments; the general public, including the media; and the medical community.
**验证状态**: 单来源（Park 2013 / Keeves 2017 无对应定性 Findings）
**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: F6
**骨架**:
> We assessed progress with [audience A], [audience B], and [audience C]. With [audience A], we see evidence of reduced [stigma/evaluation] in [indicator]. With [audience B], [indicator] suggests [partial/limited change]. [Audience C] remains [unchanged/resistant]. Thus [outcome] is audience-specific and incomplete, bounded by [remaining structural/legal/cultural conditions].
**与原骨架差异**: 定性研究通常无法给出“成功/失败”的二元结论。关键：按受众分别评估进展，明确哪些变化已发生、哪些仍未发生，避免过度宣称“去 stigma 已经完成”。

### 变体 5: Data Narrative + Theory Narrative 双轨交织段落 (Pollock Ch09 / Bansal & Corley 2012)
**来源论文**: Bansal & Corley (2012, AMJ FTE) 框架 + Pollock (2025) Ch09 + Elsbach & Kramer (2003, AMJ) 实例
**验证状态**: 框架级（Pollock Ch09 + Bansal & Corley FTE 双源确立；实例锚定 Elsbach & Kramer 2003）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-01
**槽位**: F2–F5（贯穿 Findings 主体，而非单一段落）
**骨架**:
> [Data narrative 句]: In the field, [actor type] described how [specific practice/event], noting "[power quote illustrating the experience]" ([data source]). [Theory narrative 句]: This pattern reveals [theoretical insight], which extends [existing theory] by showing [what was previously unseen about the mechanism]. [Data narrative 回扣]: We observed this dynamic repeatedly—for instance, when [second instance from different data source]—suggesting it is not idiosyncratic to [first instance's context].
>
> *The data are needed to give the theory context, and the theory is needed to give the data meaning. Qualitative articles use current theory as the backdrop for interpreting the data, the data to provide the context and describe the phenomenon in-depth, and the emergent theory to expose the phenomenon in a new light.* — Bansal & Corley (2012: 511)
**与原骨架差异**: 变体 1-4 是**单轨过程模型叙事**（前台/后台/侧台的关系空间对照）。本变体补 Pollock Ch09 明确的**双轨交织**——data narrative（田野发生了什么：具体行为、引语、事件）与 theory narrative（什么理论洞见浮现：机制、概念延伸）在**段落内**交替推进，而非把数据堆完后另起一段讲理论，也非机械列 themes。Elsbach & Kramer (2003) 的范例：他们把理论织入发现叙事（如指出 catchers 对创造力的假设与实际增强创造产出的研究相矛盾），打破逐个 prototype 列举的 "listy-ness"，增强 motion and pacing。**关键纪律**：data narrative 给 theory 以 context，theory narrative 给 data 以 meaning——两者缺一，或割裂成两段，都会让 Findings 失去说服力。

### 变体 6: Power Quotes vs Proof Quotes 引语选择框架 (Pratt 2008 / Rockmann & Vough 2024)
**来源论文**: Pratt (2008) + Rockmann & Vough (2024) + Pollock (2025) Ch09
**验证状态**: 框架级（Pratt 2008 + Rockmann & Vough 2024 双源）
**写入日期**: 2026-08-01
**槽位**: 跨 F1–F6（引语选择是 Findings 全程的微观决策，非单一段落）
**骨架/框架**:
定性 Findings 必须用引语"展示"数据（show the data），因为定性没有统计检验可信度，全靠数据本身的展示 + 聚合 + 分析过程的透明度。引语按两维度分类，承担不同段落角色：

| 引语类型 | Comprehensiveness（代表性） | Vividness（生动性） | 段落角色 |
|---------|---------------------------|---------------------|---------|
| **Power quote** | 可高可低 | **高** | 放正文，制造 human face + 情感冲击；生动、强证据力；让读者"看见"现象 |
| **Proof quote** | **高** | 可高可低 | 放正文或表格，证明 theme 确实存在于数据中；代表性强，覆盖多源 |
| **低-低引语** | 低 | 低 | 通常删除或仅放附录 |

**关键纪律**（Pollock Ch09 明确）：
1. **No recycling**——正文引语 ≠ 表格引语。若正文和表格用同一条引语，读者会认为你 cherry-picked 唯一支持故事的引语。正文用 power quote（高 vividness），表格用不同的 proof quote（高 comprehensiveness，展示 theme 的代表性）。
2. **多引语支撑同一 theme**——在正文和表格中都提供多条引语说明同一 theme 确实存在，而非一条孤证。
3. **数据表格非标准化**——定性数据表不像定量结果表有标准格式，需自行决定如何最好地呈现数据（按 theme 聚合、按阶段排列、按 actor 分组等）。
4. **图灵活**——可用 figure 在 Findings 开头（总结后详述）或结尾（收束故事），形式不限于 boxes-and-arrows。

**Pratt (2008) 原始区分**：power quotes = vivid + evidentiary basis 强；proof quotes = 证明性。Rockmann & Vough (2024) 细化为 comprehensiveness × vividness 两维度，指出不同组合的引语可承担不同角色并以不同方式组合。

**与原骨架差异**: 变体 1-4 的骨架含 `[actor type] [practice]` 类占位但未区分引语类型。本框架补 Pollock Ch09 明确但之前缺失的引语**选择与放置纪律**——这是定性 Findings 区别于定量 Results 的核心微观工艺（定量用系数，定性用引语，引语的选择直接决定可信度）。



定性过程研究的 Findings 必须回应 Methods 中的以下承诺：
1. **阶段承诺**: Methods 中划分了哪些阶段，Findings 必须逐一展开。
2. **数据来源承诺**: Findings 中的证据应覆盖 Methods 中提到的所有数据源（访谈、观察、档案）。
3. **分析透镜承诺**: 如果 Methods 声明使用 front-stage / backstage / side-stage 透镜，Findings 每个阶段都应明确标记行动发生的空间。
4. **可信性承诺**: Findings 的 claim strength 不应超出 Methods 中建立的可信性机制所支持的范围。
