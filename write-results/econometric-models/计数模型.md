# 计数模型 — Results 骨架

## 主骨架

参见 `write-results/SKILL.md` → 槽位骨架加载 → 本类型适用的 `references/slot-R*.md`（各 slot 文件内含 `计数模型` 专用变体）。

## 证据节奏摘要

- **Negative binomial IV FE**: 系数→方向解释→经济显著性 (1 SD → N-unit change in count)
- **与 OLS 的关键区别**: 不报告 R²，报告 Wald χ²；不报告 beta 的经济显著性，报告 predicted change in count
- **经济显著性**: predicted count change per SD (而非 elasticity 或 IRR)

### 变体 4: Count-Model Moderation Translation — 交互项的预测计数解释

**写入日期**: 2026-07-06
**槽位**: R4/R5
**骨架**:
> [Model] adds the interaction between [baseline predictor] and [moderator]. The interaction is [direction] and statistically significant (β = [value]; p < [threshold]), while the main effect of [moderator] is [status]. Because the interaction term is in log-count units, we translate it into predicted [outcome] differences. At the minimum observed value of [moderator], [high baseline group] is predicted to [experience] approximately [N] fewer [outcomes] than [low baseline group]. At the maximum observed value of [moderator], this gap narrows to [N] fewer [outcomes], representing a [percent] reduction in the penalty. [When reversed:] At the maximum of [moderator], the penalty is overturned and [high baseline group] is predicted to [experience] [N] more [outcomes] than [low baseline group].
**与原骨架差异**: 计数模型中，交互项系数不能直接解释为 count difference。本骨架强制使用预测值（predicted counts）在 moderator 的 min/max 处做对比，并计算 penalty reduction percentage。关键：必须说明"log-count units"到 predicted counts 的翻译。

### 变体 7: 计数模型主效应四拍 + e^β−1 百分比解释 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Haunschild, Polidoro & Chandler 2015 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R3
**骨架**:
> Hypothesis [N] predicts that [IV] has a [positive/negative] impact on [DV]. Consistent with the prediction, the coefficient on [IV] has a significantly [positive/negative] effect on [DV] (Model [Y]: β = [value], p < [threshold]). Likewise, the coefficient on [IV] has a significantly [positive/negative] effect on [alternative_DV] (Model [Z]: β = [value], p < [threshold]). These estimates show that [event] has a significant impact on a firm's subsequent rate of [DV]. These rates are [N]% and [M]% greater in the [period] following [event] (the impact implied by a negative binomial regression coefficient β is e^β − 1), controlling for [key_controls]. Thus, Hypothesis [N] is supported.
**与原骨架差异**: 关键节奏：(1) 假设提醒→方向+显著性（含两个DV平行报告）；(2) "These estimates show that..." 将系数翻译为实质语言；(3) e^β − 1 = N% 百分比翻译嵌入同一句；(4) 支持判断。与 OLS R3 的区别：报告的是"预测计数"而非"线性变化"，百分比翻译必须出现。

### 变体 8: 非线性模型中的无显式交互项调节效应 — 图形+子样本边际效应检验 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Haunschild, Polidoro & Chandler 2015 (Organization Science)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R4
**骨架**:
> Hypothesis [N] predicts that the [positive/negative] impact of [IV] on [DV] [diminishes/intensifies] over [moderator]. The traditional approach to testing such a contingency effect through interaction terms is inappropriate in the context of our analysis because we adopt a [nonlinear specification] and because the incidence of [treatment] may vary systematically across different levels of [moderator] ([citations]). As such, we tested this hypothesis through graphical analysis and a formal econometric test of differences in marginal effects of [IV] across subsamples containing different levels of [moderator]. As Figures [a] and [b] show, the slope of the fitted line is [less/more] [steep] for [high_group] than for [low_group], indicating that the marginal impact of [IV] on [DV] [diminishes/intensifies] as [moderator] [increases/decreases]. Comparing differences in the marginal effect across subsamples produced results consistent with this pattern (t = [value], p < [threshold]). These results strongly support Hypothesis [N].
**与原骨架差异**: 这是非线性模型（负二项/Poisson/Logit）中检验调节效应的**替代方案**——当传统交互项在非线性模型中解释困难或假设不满足时（如处理组/控制组在调节变量各水平的分布不均），使用图形+子样本边际效应差异 t 检验。关键论证步骤：(1) 先说为什么交互项不适用；(2) 再说替代方法是什么；(3) 报告图形模式；(4) 报告正式统计检验。诚实边界：必须引用 Hoetker (2007) 或类似文献来正当化为何不使用交互项。

### 变体 10: 负主效应+正交互 — 条件反转汇报 (1篇高价值)
**来源论文**: Desai 2011 (Academy of Management Journal)
**验证状态**: 待第二篇交叉验证
**写入日期**: 2026-07-07
**槽位**: R4
**骨架**:
> The main effect of [predictor] is consistently significant and [negative/positive]. As [predictor] increases, [units] are generally [less/more] likely to [outcome]. However, the interaction between [predictor] and [moderator] is consistently [positive/negative] and significant, suggesting that [moderator] moderates the direct effect of [predictor]. When [high-moderator condition holds], [units] become [more/less] likely to [outcome] following increases in [predictor]. This finding supports Hypothesis [N].
**与原骨架差异**: 当主效应方向与交互方向相反时，本骨架防止读者将主效应误解为"X always hurts Y"。关键：先报告一致的主效应（"consistently negative"），再用"However"转向交互的反向调节——"when M is high, X instead increases Y"。必须引用理论支持这种条件反转，不可事后编造。
