### R9. Results 证据收束

**通用填空段落**：

```text
Taken together, the results indicate that [headline answer supported by the reported evidence]. The supplemental analyses [strengthen / qualify / fail to resolve] concerns about [specific alternative explanation or validity threat]. The evidence leaves [remaining unresolved question] open.
```

**多研究专用**： 🔬 EXPERIMENTAL（1-2 篇范文）⚠️ 保守替代：通用 R9 段落
```text
Across Studies [x–y], the evidence converges on [theoretical pattern] while progressively addressing [validity concerns]. Taken together, these results provide [support/partial support] for [core claim].
```

**多维稳健性 bundle 收束变体**（li_venkataraman_2026_pom 型）： 🔬 EXPERIMENTAL（1 篇范文）— 当 §Robustness 按 threat 分节（替代解释 / 分析层次 / 替代测量 / 内生性）后需要一句总收束
```text
Taken together, the analyses above demonstrate the robustness of the results across [alternative explanation A], [level-of-analysis sensitivity B], [alternative measures and estimators C], and [endogeneity approaches D]. Collectively, these tests indicate that our conclusions are not driven by [dominant threat 1], [dominant threat 2], or [dominant threat 3].
```

**关键特征**:
- **枚举四类 threat 而非表格清单**：读者得到"我们系统回应了什么"，不是"我们又跑了 12 个模型"
- **放在 Robustness 末段**：作为 falling action，不替代 R3 主效应叙事
- **与 R6 posthoc 分工**：R6 解释 null/contrary；R9 bundle 收束稳健性

**适用**: 大型 robustness 节（≥3 类 threat）；supply chain / panel 计数模型；POM/JOM

**禁忌**: 若某类 threat 未实际检验，不可空喊；不要在此段引入新理论

Do not use R9 to generate theoretical implications, practical implications, limitations, or future-research prose.
