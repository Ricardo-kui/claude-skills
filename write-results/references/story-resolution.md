## Status Rules

- `supported`: the declared evidence answers the storyline in the predicted direction and survives the relevant credibility checks.
- `mixed`: models, measures, samples, conditions, or sub-studies disagree.
- `unsupported`: the predicted answer is not supported or points in the opposite direction.
- `unresolved`: the available evidence cannot adjudicate the promise.

## Narrative Placement

- R1/R2 orient the reader and should be compressed when they delay the answer.
- R3 is the empirical climax: state the headline answer with direction, uncertainty, magnitude, and support judgment.
- R4–R6 qualify conditional, mediated, null, or surprising patterns.
- R7/R8 are falling action: organize them around threats and show whether the climax holds, narrows, or unravels.
- R9 is optional evidence closure, not a Discussion template.

Never convert mixed evidence into a clean contribution claim. Never infer a magnitude from statistical significance alone.
