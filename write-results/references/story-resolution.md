## Status Rules

- `baseline verdict` evaluates the designated hypothesis test only.
- `stable`: the material credibility checks preserve direction, inference, and a comparable magnitude.
- `qualified`: the baseline answer remains but has a named sample, measure, period, specification, or identification boundary.
- `mixed`: models, measures, samples, conditions, or sub-studies disagree in ways that cannot be reduced to one boundary.
- `unresolved`: the available supplementary evidence cannot adjudicate stability.

## Narrative Placement

- R1/R2 orient the reader and should be compressed when they delay the answer.
- R3 is the empirical climax: state the headline answer with direction, uncertainty, magnitude, and support judgment.
- Report R3 in hypothesis-number order unless the user or active Theory explicitly defines another order. A storyline's theoretical importance affects emphasis, not automatic placement.
- R4–R6 qualify conditional, mediated, null, or surprising patterns.
- R7/R8 are falling action: organize them around threats and show whether the climax holds, narrows, or unravels.
- R9 is optional evidence closure, not a Discussion template.

Never convert mixed evidence into a clean contribution claim. Never infer a magnitude from statistical significance alone.
