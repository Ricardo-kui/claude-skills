# Blind Evaluation Rubric

Evaluate paired outputs without seeing whether they used Legacy or Indexed retrieval.

Critical failure: fabricated source claim; construct drift; prediction unsupported by
the why-chain; level/time/estimand mismatch; forced mediator/moderator/numbered
hypothesis; unsupported causal language; or paper-specific reference copied as prose.

Score each dimension from 1 to 5:

1. mechanism completeness;
2. premise and warrant soundness;
3. construct discrimination;
4. boundary, level, and time precision;
5. prediction testability;
6. Introduction–Theory contract alignment;
7. adaptability rather than templating;
8. epistemic honesty.

Record a pairwise choice: Indexed win, Legacy win, or tie. Do not infer the
condition from length, terminology, or formatting.
