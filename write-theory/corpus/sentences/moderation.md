# 调节机制修改句语料库

## 缓冲型 (Buffering)

```
"The [positive/negative] effect of [X] on [Y] is weakened when [Z] is [high/present] 
because [Z] reduces the [cost/risk/uncertainty/constraint] that underlies the 
[X]→[Y] mechanism. Specifically, when [Z is high], [how the baseline mechanism 
is dampened], thereby [consequence for X→Y relationship]."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — 缓冲型调节

---

## 增强型 (Enhancing)

```
"The [positive/negative] effect of [X] on [Y] is strengthened when [Z] is [high/present] 
because [Z] amplifies the [baseline mechanism] by [amplification logic]. When 
[Z is high], [how the baseline mechanism is reinforced], thereby [consequence]."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — 增强型调节

---

## 拮抗型 (Antagonistic)

```
"Although [X] and [Z] each [positively/negatively] affect [Y] through their 
respective mechanisms, their interaction produces an opposing effect because 
[specific logic for why the combination backfires/overrides]. At high levels 
of [Z], the [mechanism of Z] counteracts the [mechanism of X], such that 
[net effect reversal or attenuation]."
```

---

## 排除反向交互

```
"We theorize [Z] as moderating the [X]→[Y] relationship rather than [X] 
moderating the [Z]→[Y] relationship because [theoretical reason: 
e.g., Z is temporally/causally prior / Z is an exogenous contextual condition / 
X moderating Z→Y lacks a coherent theoretical mechanism / Z operates at 
a different level of analysis]."
```

---

## 调节直接效应区分

```
"Z [strengthens/weakens] the [X]→[Y] relationship because [Z] changes the 
[baseline mechanism] in the following way: [specific modification logic]."

"When Z is [high/present], [how mechanism changes]; when Z is [low/absent], 
[how baseline mechanism operates without modification]."

"The moderating effect of Z operates through [mechanism channel], which is 
distinct from Z's direct effect on Y through [separate channel]."
```

---

## 分组调节对称对比

```
"For [W=A], [mechanism A]. [Evidence A]. In contrast, for [W=B], [mechanism B]. 
[Evidence B]. Given these differences, we expect:"
```

**语料锚定**：
- Darby 2024 (MSOM) — manufacturing vs design defects
- Darby 2025 (JSCM) — severity 分组

---

## 极端案例对比（分类调节器）

**模板**：
```
"High-[category] [events] involve [extreme consequence]. As an example, consider [Case A]. [Details]. In contrast, low-[category] [events] are less of a threat because [limited consequence]. As an example, consider [Case B]. [Details]."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Darby 2023 (MSOM) — high-severity vs low-severity recalls with specific case illustrations

---

## 跨层调节前置声明

```
"The focal unit of analysis is [Level-1 unit, e.g., the firm-year]. 
[Level-1 units] are nested within [Level-2 units, e.g., industries], 
which are in turn nested within [Level-3 units, e.g., countries]. 
This nesting structure means that [Level-1 observations] within the same 
[Level-2 unit] share common characteristics and are not independent. 

We theorize that [Level-2/3 variable Z] moderates the [Level-1 X → Level-1 Y] 
relationship because [cross-level mechanism: e.g., Z creates institutional 
conditions that alter the costs/benefits of X's effect on Y]. 

We also account for the direct effect of [Z] on [Y] through [separate mechanism], 
distinguishing this cross-level direct effect from the cross-level interaction effect."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Andersson, Cuervo-Cazurra, & Nielsen (2014) JIBS Editorial

## Rhetorical Question 调节开场（Eilert 2017 型）

**适用**：假设树型或调节效应型论文中，用问句开启 moderator 小节

**模板**：
```
How does the relationship between [IV] and [DV] vary as a function of [moderator]? 
[Moderator] is [definition]. [Theoretical justification for moderator importance]. 
There are [N] reasons why [moderator] should moderate the relationship between [IV] and [DV].

[Reason 1 — 动机/能力路径]: [Mechanism logic]. [Citation]. Consequently, [prediction].

[Reason 2 — 能力/动机路径]: [Mechanism logic]. [Citation]. Thus, [prediction].

Overall, as [moderator] increases, the [ability/motivation] to [behavior] will [direction]. 
Thus, we expect: H[N]: The higher a [moderator], the [weaker/stronger] the relationship 
between [IV] and [DV].
```

**关键特征**：
- 问句直接承接主效应机制，形成自然过渡
- 定义 moderator 后立即给出其理论重要性
- "There are N reasons" 预告多路径论证（通常 ability + motivation）
- 每个 reason 独立论证，最后 Overall 收敛

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — "How does the relationship between problem severity and time to recall vary as a function of brand reliability?" (H2)
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — "How is the relationship between problem severity and recall timing affected when brand diversification increases?" (H3)

**反模式**：
- 问句后无直接回答，转为泛泛讨论 moderator → 问句沦为修辞装饰
- "There are two reasons" 后只给出一个理由 → 承诺未兑现

## Ability-Motivation 双路径调节论证（Eilert 2017 型）

**适用**：组织决策、企业行为类主题中，解释 moderator 如何通过 ability 和 motivation 两条路径改变主效应

**模板**：
```
[动机路径]: The damage to [asset] from a crisis is a function of [expectation theory]. 
[Stakeholders] are particularly sensitive to [condition] ([citation]). 
[IV condition] already violates those expectations. Consequently, [quick action] would 
be more consistent with expectations. [Reasoning]. Thus, [moderator level] will be 
motivated to [action] ([citations]).

[能力路径]: [Moderator level] is indicative of the firm's ability to [capability]. 
Firms that [moderator characteristic] are likely to do so by building [underlying resource]. 
This expertise could help the firm [specific action]. Thus, [mechanism conclusion].
```

**关键特征**：
- Ability 路径通常涉及知识、资源、专长、流程
- Motivation 路径通常涉及政治成本、声誉保护、问责规避
- 两条路径独立论证，最后 Overall 收敛到同一假设
- 适用于 brand reputation, firm capability, resource slack 等 moderator

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — H2 (brand reliability): motivation via expectation consistency + ability via R&D expertise
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — H3 (brand diversification): ability via component complexity + motivation via spillover fear

**可视化工具**：可用 Table 2 式矩阵总结（Ability / Motivation / Net Effect / Rationale）

**反模式**：
- Ability 和 motivation 路径区分模糊 → 审稿人质疑 "这难道不是同一个机制？"
- 两条路径推导方向矛盾而未解释 net effect → 必须明确总体方向

If [actors] [behavior] in an attempt to [goal], this effect is likely to be stronger when [moderator condition]. This may be because [moderator] have a [characteristic] than [comparison group]. In the context of [theory], a [characteristic] may create a more "[actionable] target."

## Discretion-Based Boundary (Mayo et al. POMS)

If [phenomenon] explains [finding], then [characteristic] inherent in [decision] should influence [outcome]. Relatedly, [events] associated with [high characteristic] may be [outcome 1] because they may be [condition], whereas [events] associated with [low characteristic] may be [outcome 2]. If [phenomenon] explains [finding], it would lend support to our proposed mechanism that [theoretical claim].

## 共享调节器反向调节模式（Same Moderator, Opposite Pattern Across DVs）

**适用**: 同一 moderator 对两个不同 DV 产生相反方向的调节效果——对 DV1 在 condition A 中增强，对 DV2 在 condition B 中增强

**结构要求**: 必须先建立两个 DV 的基线机制（各自有独立假设），再引入调节

**模板**:
```
[Theory framework introduction — acknowledge that the same moderator affects both paths].

[Path 1 moderation]: Our first hypothesis argued that [IV] will [DV1]. We posit that this 
effect will become even more pronounced when [moderator condition A]. For [actors with IV], 
[condition A] may make [relevant cues/threats] particularly salient ([citations]). 
[Condition A] is generally associated with [consequence mapping onto mechanism], but a 
given [actor's] level of [IV] should influence the perceived severity of (and the 
subsequent behavioral response to) [this consequence]. We thus argue that [condition A] 
will amplify, or strengthen, the [direction] relationship between [IV] and [DV1].

The corollary to this argument is that [condition B] will attenuate, or weaken, the 
influence of [IV] on [DV1]. [Reasoning about why DV1's mechanism is less needed in 
condition B].

H[N]: The influence of [IV] on [DV1] will be stronger (more [direction]) in [condition A] 
than in [condition B].

---

[Path 2 moderation]: We similarly anticipate that [moderator] will moderate the influence 
of [IV] on [DV2]. [Background on how the moderator affects DV2 in general]. We argue that 
the effect of [IV] on [DV2] will depend on [moderator]. Our [Nth] hypothesis argued that 
[IV direction] [DV2], and we posit that this effect will become even more pronounced in 
[condition B]. [Reasoning specific to DV2's mechanism — why condition B intensifies it].

An extension of this logic is that [condition A] should somewhat offset the [direction] 
relationship between [IV] and [DV2]. [Reasoning about why condition A exerts opposing 
influence — usually via competing motivational forces].

H[N+1]: The influence of [IV] on [DV2] will be stronger (more [direction]) in 
[condition B] than in [condition A]."
```

**语料锚定**: mannor_wowak_bartkus_gomez-mejia_2016 (SMJ) — H3: loss context amplifies social buffering / H4: gain context amplifies risk aversion

**关键特征**:
- 两个调节假设必须方向相反（否则 moderator 只是重复同一逻辑）
- "amplify/strengthen" 和 "attenuate/weaken" 配对使用
- "corollary to this argument" / "extension of this logic" 作为路径间对称过渡
- 必须先建立主效应（H1 + H2），再引入调节（H3 + H4）
- 每个路径的 reasoning 必须独立且有独特理论依据

**反模式**:
- 两个调节假设方向相同 → moderator 只是重复主效应逻辑，无理论增量
- 调节论证只有一条路径被充分解释，另一条用 "by similar logic" 带过
- 两条路径的机制区分不清晰 → moderator 看似反向实则可被整合为单一机制

## 多调节器同向设计（Multiple Moderators, Same Direction）

**适用**: 所有调节器都增强（或都减弱）主效应——每个 moderator 通过独特机制运作但方向相同

**设计优势**: 同向设计简化理论叙述——读者只需理解 "什么条件下主效应更强/更弱" 而无需跟踪方向反转

**模板**:
```
"We next consider contingencies that affect the relationship between [IV] and [DV]. Our 
goal is to identify the conditions under which [baseline force] is particularly strong, 
thereby [binding/amplifying] [outcome]. In these conditions, we would expect there to be 
minimal, if any, [shift/effect].

Several factors can enhance [baseline force] for [actors]. While numerous mechanisms 
could play a role, we focus on [M1], [M2], and [M3]. These factors are particularly 
valuable to consider because [unifying theoretical rationale for the selection].

---
[Moderator 1] — [dimension: e.g., internal governance]:
[M1 mechanism — why it enhances baseline force]. Consequently, we predict that [entities] 
with greater [M1] will be less sensitive to [treatment]. Even if they perceive [treatment 
effect], under the greater [M1 mechanism], [actors'] attention to [baseline target] will 
be sticky. Thus, higher levels of [M1] will mitigate the [main effect direction].

H2: The [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV] will be weaker for [entities] with higher 
rather than lower levels of [M1].

---
[Moderator 2] — [dimension: e.g., external monitoring]:
Complementing [M1], [M2] are [brief description]. We expect that [entities] with greater 
[M2] will be less sensitive to [treatment] for two reasons. First, [reason 1 — salience/attention 
logic]. Second, [reason 2 — discretion/constraint logic]. Thus, [convergence].

H3: The [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV] will be weaker for [entities] with higher 
rather than lower levels of [M2].

---
[Moderator 3] — [dimension: e.g., structural/institutional]:
Lastly, in addition to [M1] and [M2], we examine [M3]. [Definition and theoretical grounding]. 
We expect [entities] with [stronger M3] will be less responsive to [treatment] for two 
reasons. First, [reason 1]. Second, [reason 2]. As a result, [convergence].

H4: The [direction] effect of [IV] on [DV] will be weaker for [entities] with stronger 
rather than weaker [M3]."
```

**语料锚定**: park_lange_jeon (SMJ) — H2-H4: outside director ownership, dedicated institutional ownership, shareholder governance provisions (全部减弱 H1 主效应)

**关键特征**:
- 所有 moderator 方向相同 — 叙述逻辑简洁
- 每个 moderator 至少 2 个独立理由
- Moderator 按维度组织（如 internal → external → structural），增强论证层次感
- 引入段落提供统一框架（"our goal is to identify conditions under which [baseline force] is particularly strong"）
- "sticky" 作为 attention-based 论证的关键术语

**与 Same Moderator Opposite Pattern 的区分**:
| | 同向多调节器 | 共享调节器反向 |
|---|---|---|
| Moderator 数量 | ≥3 | 1 (作用于 2 个 DV) |
| 调节方向 | 全部相同 | 对 DV1 和 DV2 方向相反 |
| 核心挑战 | 确保每个 moderator 机制独立 | 确保两条 DV 路径的调节论证独立 |

## 不对称调节模式（Asymmetric Moderation: Same Moderator, Opposite Directions on Two Tracks）

**适用**: 双轨并行机制推演型论文 — 同一调节变量削弱 Track A 的效应但增强 Track B 的效应

**模板**:
```
[Track A moderation — weakening]: We have argued that [actors] with [Track A characteristic] tend to employ [behavior]. However, this tendency should diminish under [moderator condition]. [Moderator] is critical in [shaping/monitoring function] ([citations]). It "[quotation about moderator's social function]" ([citation]). By [monitoring mechanism], [moderator] increases the visibility of [actor] actions ([citations]).

[Behavior] are effective when information is [ambiguous/scarce]; however, they risk exposure when audiences gain access to clearer data. [Moderator] amplifies scrutiny from [audience groups], putting [actor]'s [asset at risk] at risk. This external pressure leads [actors] to exercise greater caution ([citations]). Moreover, heightened scrutiny can lead to stricter internal oversight by [governance bodies], further limiting [actor]'s ability to [self-serving behavior].

[Moderator] has increasingly "[stigmatized label]" [actor characteristic] ([citation]), portraying it as a source of "[negative label]" ([citation]). For [actors] with [Track A characteristic], [moderator] increases the risks of using [behavior] to conceal [adverse events]. Under intense scrutiny, concealment can harm [actor]'s [asset] and increase [occupational risks] if exposed, potentially outweighing any gains ([citation]).

In sum, high levels of [moderator] put significant pressure on [actors], making those with [Track A characteristic] less likely to engage in [behavior].

H[N]a: The [positive] relationship between [Track A IV] and [DV] is weaker (less positive) with more [moderator].

---

[Track B moderation — strengthening]: We previously argued that [actors] with [Track B characteristic] are less likely to use [behavior], focusing instead on [alternative positive action]. We now propose that the [negative] relationship between [Track B IV] and [behavior] should be **enhanced** by a high level of [moderator].

Under [moderator condition], [actors] must carefully weigh the long-term implications of their decisions. [Positive action] enables them to portray [entity] as responsible, potentially improving [long-term outcome] and enhancing their [Track B characteristic] ([citation]). [Moderator] reinforces the importance of [positive behavior], as [negative behavior] can damage [actor]'s [asset] and hinder the perception of [positive attribute]. By [positive action], [actors] can strengthen both their own and [entity]'s reputation for sound governance, thereby mitigating the risks associated with [evasive behavior]. Thus, [actor] who is already inclined to avoid [behavior] due to [Track B characteristic] becomes even less likely to employ [behavior] under greater [moderator].

H[N]b: The [negative] relationship between [Track B IV] and [DV] is stronger (more negative) with more [moderator].
```

**语料锚定**:
- malik_wang_martin_gomezmejia2025 (JM) — H3-H4: Negative media coverage weakens the positive effect of current option wealth on IM tactics (Track A), but strengthens the negative effect of prospective option wealth on IM tactics (Track B). Media serves as "society's watchdog" that deters self-interested behavior but reinforces already-responsible inclinations.

**关键特征**:
- Moderator 对两条轨道的作用方向相反 —— weaken Track A, strengthen Track B
- Track A 的 weakening 逻辑: moderator 增加外部监督 → 自利行为风险 > 收益
- Track B 的 strengthening 逻辑: moderator 强化 long-term orientation → 负责任行为获得更多回报
- 使用 "should be enhanced by" 而非 "moderated by" — 方向明确的措辞
- 两个子假设 (a/b) 独立论证, 有各自的理论依据
- Moderator 必须有统一的理论角色（如 "社会控制代理" / "外部监督者"）来统摄两个相反方向

**与共享调节器反向调节的区分**:
| | 不对称调节 (Asymmetric) | 共享调节器反向 (Same Moderator Opposite) |
|---|---|---|
| 适用结构 | 双轨并行 (Track A vs Track B) | 双 DV (DV1 vs DV2) |
| Moderator 方向 | weaken Track A, strengthen Track B | 对 DV1 增强, 对 DV2 减弱 |
| 核心挑战 | 确保 moderator 统一理论角色统摄双方向 | 确保两条 DV 路径的调节论证独立 |
| 典型 moderator | 外部监督者 (media, analysts, regulators) | 情境变量 (loss vs gain context) |

**反模式**:
- Asymmetric moderation 的两个方向没有统一的理论角色 → 看起来像两个独立的调节拼在一起
- Track B 的 strengthening 论证薄弱（"similarly, by similar logic..."）→ 审稿人质疑非对称性是否成立
- "enhanced by" 措辞过于强烈 → 如果实证不支持 strengthening，改为 "further reinforced" 更保守
- Moderator 小节长度极度不均（暗示某些 moderator 是后加）
- 只有一个理由支撑 moderator → 审稿人攻击点
- 引入段落缺乏统一理论框架（"we chose these three moderators..." but WHY these three?）

## 双边论证调节推演（Bilateral Moderation Argument）

**适用**: 调节变量对 IV→DV 关系同时存在增强和削弱两种理论可能性，需要展示 nuanced thinking 而非单向预测

**模板**:
```
[Moderator] increases the complexity of [decision/phenomenon]. On the one hand, [condition that could strengthen the IV effect] — [mechanism logic], so [actors] might [behavior A]. If [investigation/analysis] shows [mitigating condition], the [actor] may [extreme outcome]. On the other hand, [condition that could weaken the IV effect] — avoiding [proper action] creates the risk of [negative consequence 1] and [negative consequence 2]; [real-world example with quantified cost]. We aim to examine if and how [moderator] moderates the influence of [IV] on [DV]. Because [actor]'s costs go up substantially as [moderator] increases, the role of [IV] in [DV] decisions should be contingent on [moderator], such that if [actors tend to take action Z when moderator increases], the effects of [IV] on [DV] should [direction of change].

H[N]: The [direction] association between [IV] and [DV] [weakens/strengthens] in magnitude with increased [moderator].
```

**语料锚定**:
- singh_grewal2023 (JMR) — H2: defect severity (deaths) 削弱 lobbying→recalls 的负向关系。On the one hand: 严重缺陷增加财务损失, 企业可能延迟召回以争取调查时间。On the other hand: 避免召回带来品牌损害和诉讼风险 (GM $900M settlement)。最终预测: defect severity 削弱 lobbying 的影响。

**关键特征**:
- **"On the one hand... On the other hand..."**: 承认调节变量可能产生双向影响，但通过权衡论证收敛到单一方向预测
- 双边论证建立理论 nuanced 印象——作者考虑了多种可能性而非简单预测
- 必须明确 "We aim to examine if and how..." → 公开研究目标，然后在理论推导中给出方向
- 双边分析后必须有收敛句: "Because [costs go up substantially as moderator increases], the role of [IV] should [direction]"
- 使用量化真实案例 (如 "$900 million settlement") 为 risk-avoidance 路径提供具体支撑

**反模式**:
- 双边论证后无收敛——"it could go either way" 不是假设
- 双边论证篇幅严重失衡——一边有案例支撑另一边只有断言
- "We aim to examine" 但假设已有明确方向 → 修辞矛盾

## 注意力基础观调节论证（Attention-Based Moderator: Media Coverage）

**适用**: Media coverage / public attention / stakeholder scrutiny 作为调节变量，通过注意力机制改变 IV→DV 关系

**模板**:
```
As an attention measure, we consider [moderator construct], or the amount of [information type] that [events] receive. [Event type] attract varying amounts of [information type], which establishes public awareness of [issue], in line with the key functions of [information source] with respect to [actors] ([citation]). [Author(s)] describe [N] major roles of [information source]: [role 1], [role 2], and [role 3]. Information provision helps reduce information asymmetry between [stakeholder group 1] and [stakeholder group 2] and influences public perceptions, which guide [actor] behaviors. [Author(s)] also assert that such influences on [actor] behaviors are consistent with an attention-based view ([citation]), according to which [actor]'s decisions depend on which issues grab their attention. [Information source] reports thus can influence [actor] legitimacy in the eyes of multiple stakeholders ([citation]).

In line with the attention-based view, [moderator] should be especially influential during [adverse event type], when negative [information source] attention threatens the [actor]'s reputation. [Actors] subject to negative [information source] attention likely act to appease the [information source] and demonstrate how they intend to rectify the problems. Therefore, [moderator] makes [events] more visible ([citation]) and leads to more negative [audience] reactions ([citation]). Similarly, [related literature] predicts that [moderator] determines outcomes in [IV] contexts ([citation]). Accordingly, [moderator] should alter the relationship between [DV] and [IV]. If [moderator] increases, [actor] may be more hesitant to [avoidance action]. Such hesitancy then would diminish the potential effect of [IV].

H[N]: The [direction] association between [IV] and [DV] [weakens] in magnitude as [moderator] increases.
```

**语料锚定**:
- singh_grewal2023 (JMR) — H3: media coverage 削弱 lobbying→recalls 的负向关系。媒体三重角色 (Bednar et al. 2013): 平台、报道、调查。Attention-based view (Ocasio 1997)。信息不对称降低 → 公众关注增加 → 企业更难避免召回 → lobbying 影响减弱。

**关键特征**:
- 引用媒体的制度功能（如 Bednar et al. 2013 的三重角色），而非简单说 "media matters"
- Attention-based view (Ocasio 1997) 作为调节论证的核心理论锚点
- "reduce information asymmetry" → 媒体作为信息中介的机制论证
- "hesitancy then would diminish the potential effect of [IV]" → 简洁收敛
- 可与缺陷严重程度等其他 moderator 形成信息×注意力分类框架

**反模式**:
- 媒体调节论证只有 citation 无机制（"media coverage has been shown to moderate..." but not WHY）
- 与同论文其他 moderator 无概念区分——如果两个 moderator 都通过 "visibility" 运作，需解释为何是两个独立构念
- 注意力基础观被引用但未展开核心 insight（如未解释 "which issues grab their attention" 如何适用于当前 context）

## 竞争基线+调节解决型（Competing Baseline → Moderation Resolution，desai2012 型）

**适用**: 研究问题本身有对立理论预测（如 defense beneficial vs. defense risky），先呈现competing baseline建立审慎印象，再用moderator条件解决对立

**模板**:
```
[Theory] can be adapted to suggest at least two [responses] in this situation. 
First, [actors] may seek to [response A: proactive] by [mechanism A] ([citations]). 
Such efforts could [benefits] ([citations]).

On the other hand, [response A] may be risky following [trigger]. [Theory] recognizes 
that [attempts] may backfire, as [reason for failure] ([citations]). Following [trigger], 
[efforts] may be met with [negative reaction]. [Actors] may benefit by [response B: passive] 
([citations]). This avoidance could be combined with other steps to [additional passive actions] 
([citations]). Collectively, [actors] may [overall tendency toward B] following [trigger], 
given the risks associated with [response A].

The perspectives forwarded above suggest contrasting [responses]. [Response A] may help 
[benefit], but [response A] may instead backfire, [cost] ([citations]). Therefore, it is 
important to determine the conditions under which [actors] either engage in—or avoid—[behavior].
```

**关键特征**:
- **先建competition再解决**: 不在理论开头就给出答案，而是先呈现两种对立的可能性
- **"On the other hand"** → 显式辩证标记
- **"Therefore, it is important to determine the conditions under which..."** → 从对立到调解的自然过渡
- 每个对立面独立有完整的理论支撑（不只是一句带过）
- 适合没有明确主效应假设的研究——所有假设都是关于 boundary conditions

**语料锚定**: desai2012 (AMJ) — "Defensive Efforts following Disruptions" 整节

## Extension Logic 型调节推导（Firm-level→Field-level Extension，desai2012 型）

**适用**: 从已知领域（如firm-level）向新领域（如field-level）扩展理论逻辑，用extension作为moderator选择的理论依据

**模板**:
```
Although little work in [target domain] addresses [behavior], research on [analogous domain] 
provides some insight into this process ([citations]). According to this literature, [actors] 
are more likely to engage in [analogous behavior] when [condition 1] and when [condition 2] 
([citation]). Extending this process to [target domain] suggests two broad considerations. 
First, [consideration 1 derived from condition 1]. [Elaboration with examples]. Second, 
[consideration 2 derived from condition 2]. [Elaboration with decision-making theory support] 
([citation]).

A broad variety of factors affect [outcome domain]. Of these factors, prior research points 
most directly to [factor 1] and [factor 2], given their central influence on both [mechanism A] 
and [mechanism B] following [trigger] ([citations]).
```

**关键特征**:
- **"Although little work in [target]... research on [analogous] provides insight"** → 不声称完全空白，承认analogous领域已有基础
- **"Extending this process to [target domain] suggests..."** → 扩展逻辑是推导的核心方法论
- 引用decision-making理论（如Gavetti & Levinthal 2000）为extension提供合法性
- **"A broad variety of factors affect... Of these, prior research points most directly to..."** → 透明选择——承认有多种因素，但聚焦最关键的两个
- 可选附："其他因素也可能影响但我没有考虑"的透明段落

**语料锚定**: desai2012 (AMJ) — "Determining the Use of Defensive Efforts" 节 + Oliver (1991) extension

**反模式**:
- Analogous domain与被扩展的domain差异过大（extension的逻辑断裂）
- "Extending this logic" 但未解释扩展为何成立（缺乏过渡理论）
- 忽略了analogous domain与新domain之间的关键差异（extension失效）
