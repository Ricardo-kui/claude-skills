# 调节机制修改句语料库

## 缓冲型 (Buffering)

```
"The [positive/negative] effect of [X] on [Y] is weakened when [Z] is [high/present] 
because [Z] reduces the [cost/risk/uncertainty/constraint] that underlies the 
[X]→[Y] mechanism. Specifically, when [Z is high], [how the baseline mechanism 
is dampened], thereby [consequence for X→Y relationship]."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — 缓冲型调节

---

## 增强型 (Enhancing)

```
"The [positive/negative] effect of [X] on [Y] is strengthened when [Z] is [high/present] 
because [Z] amplifies the [baseline mechanism] by [amplification logic]. When 
[Z is high], [how the baseline mechanism is reinforced], thereby [consequence]."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — 增强型调节

---

## 拮抗型 (Antagonistic)

```
"Although [X] and [Z] each [positively/negatively] affect [Y] through their 
respective mechanisms, their interaction produces an opposing effect because 
[specific logic for why the combination backfires/overrides]. At high levels 
of [Z], the [mechanism of Z] counteracts the [mechanism of X], such that 
[net effect reversal or attenuation]."
```

---

## 排除反向交互

```
"We theorize [Z] as moderating the [X]→[Y] relationship rather than [X] 
moderating the [Z]→[Y] relationship because [theoretical reason: 
e.g., Z is temporally/causally prior / Z is an exogenous contextual condition / 
X moderating Z→Y lacks a coherent theoretical mechanism / Z operates at 
a different level of analysis]."
```

---

## 调节直接效应区分

```
"Z [strengthens/weakens] the [X]→[Y] relationship because [Z] changes the 
[baseline mechanism] in the following way: [specific modification logic]."

"When Z is [high/present], [how mechanism changes]; when Z is [low/absent], 
[how baseline mechanism operates without modification]."

"The moderating effect of Z operates through [mechanism channel], which is 
distinct from Z's direct effect on Y through [separate channel]."
```

---

## 分组调节对称对比

```
"For [W=A], [mechanism A]. [Evidence A]. In contrast, for [W=B], [mechanism B]. 
[Evidence B]. Given these differences, we expect:"
```

**语料锚定**：
- Darby 2024 (MSOM) — manufacturing vs design defects
- Darby 2025 (JSCM) — severity 分组

## 调节变量的理论嵌入策略——将 Moderator 映射到核心理论的次级假设

**适用**: 引入边界条件时，避免 moderator 成为外部补丁（"随便加了一个调节变量"），而是从核心理论内部自然生长。

**模板**:
> "The [core theory] consists of two related parts: the [hypothesis_1_name] considers that [core mechanism description] ([citations]), and the [hypothesis_2_name] considers that the [core mechanism] effects accumulate and compound over time with the support of [moderator theoretical resource] ([citations]). [Moderator construct] reflects the availability of [theoretical resource] ([citation]). Thus, [moderator] should [strengthen/weaken] the [X]→[Y] relationship because [specific mechanism linking moderator to core theory's secondary hypothesis]."

**语料锚定**:
- wang2024 (SMJ) — broaden-and-build theory 的 broaden hypothesis + build hypothesis。social capital → build hypothesis 的 social resources，使调节假设从理论内部自然生长。

**关键特征**:
- **不单独介绍 moderator**: moderator 不是新理论，而是核心理论已有次级假设的具体化
- **"consists of two related parts"**: 将核心理论拆分为两个子假设， moderator 映射到第二个子假设
- **理论一致性**: social capital 作为 "social resources" 是 build hypothesis 的内在要素，而非外部变量
- **避免 "we also examine the moderating role of..."**: 这种措辞暗示 moderator 是事后添加的

**与标准调节嵌入的区分**:
| | 理论内部嵌入型 (wang2024) | 标准调节型 |
|---|---|---|
| 引入方式 | 从核心理论子假设自然生长 | "We also examine the moderating role of Z" |
| 理论基础 | moderator 是理论的内在要素 | moderator 是外部边界条件 |
| 审稿人感知 | 理论设计 elegant | 可能质疑 "为什么选这个 moderator？" |
| 适用场景 | 核心理论有明确的次级假设可映射 | 理论无明确子假设，需引入外部情境变量 |

**反模式**:
- moderator 介绍段落与核心理论段落之间无逻辑连接 → 审稿人质疑 "为什么突然跳到 moderator？"
- 用 "In addition, we examine..." 引入 moderator → 暗示是事后添加
- moderator 的理论映射过于牵强 → 如将 "board independence" 硬塞进 "social resources"

## 跨层调节前置声明

```
"The focal unit of analysis is [Level-1 unit, e.g., the firm-year]. 
[Level-1 units] are nested within [Level-2 units, e.g., industries], 
which are in turn nested within [Level-3 units, e.g., countries]. 
This nesting structure means that [Level-1 observations] within the same 
[Level-2 unit] share common characteristics and are not independent. 

We theorize that [Level-2/3 variable Z] moderates the [Level-1 X → Level-1 Y] 
relationship because [cross-level mechanism: e.g., Z creates institutional 
conditions that alter the costs/benefits of X's effect on Y]. 

We also account for the direct effect of [Z] on [Y] through [separate mechanism], 
distinguishing this cross-level direct effect from the cross-level interaction effect."
```

**语料锚定**：
- Andersson, Cuervo-Cazurra, & Nielsen (2014) JIBS Editorial

## 统一框架型调节变量选择（hoffmann2024 型）

**功能**: 当研究有多个调节变量时，用一个 organizing framework/taxonomy 系统化地为调节变量的选择提供理论理由，而非逐个引入。

**核心逻辑**: `[Secondary organizing framework] distinguishes [N] types of [constraint/mechanism] — [type 1: internal/intrinsic] and [type 2: external/extrinsic]. Each maps to a distinct moderator with a distinct theoretical mechanism.`

**句法模板**:
```
The [secondary] literature distinguishes two main mechanisms to constrain 
[self-interest-seeking behavior] — [type 1: internal/intrinsic, defined as X] 
and [type 2: external/extrinsic, defined as Y] ([citation]). 

This literature considers [type 1] as a means for [alignment logic] that operates 
by [mechanism: e.g., making agents internalize norms and values such that they 
intrinsically want to do the right thing]. [Type 2], in contrast, works by 
[monitoring/enforcement logic], which [mechanism: e.g., imposes external 
accountability such that agents are forced to do the right thing]. 

A key distinction between these two solutions to [core agency problem] is thus 
that the former relies on [agents] intrinsically *wanting* to 
[behave appropriately], while the latter is based on extrinsically *forcing* 
them to do so by means of [formal accountability mechanism].

Next, we discuss our expectations on how [type 1], in terms of 
[specific moderator 1], and [type 2], in terms of [specific moderator 2], 
will shift the main effect of [treatment] on [outcome].
```

**关键特征**:
- **统一框架优先**: 先引入分类系统，再逐个讨论调节变量——让读者理解"为什么是这两个调节变量，而不是一个或三个"
- **intrinsic vs extrinsic 区分**: 两个调节变量分别归属不同约束类型，创造概念对称性
- **"wanting to" vs "being forced to"**: 用对立动词区分两个调节机制的本质差异，增强理论优雅性
- **理论分工明确**: 主理论解释"为什么 X 影响 Y"，次要框架解释"X 的影响在什么条件下变化"
- **从框架到操作的逐级映射**: 框架层（intrinsic/extrinsic constraint）→ 构念层（corporate culture / institutional monitoring）→ 操作层（具体测量）

**与标准调节嵌入的区分**:
| | 统一框架型 (hoffmann2024) | 理论内部嵌入型 (wang2024) | 标准调节型 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 调节变量来源 | 外部 organizing framework | 核心理论子假设自然生长 | 逐个引入 |
| 选择 justification | 框架提供系统性理由 | 理论内部逻辑 | "也可能重要" |
| 变量间关系 | 对称配对（intrinsic vs extrinsic） | 平行并列（broaden + build） | 各自独立 |
| 审稿人感知 | 理论设计 elegant + 系统 | 理论一致性高 | 可能质疑 ad-hoc |

**适用**: Incompleteness × Boundary 组合。当主效应的理论无法自然推导出边界条件（或推导出的不够系统），且有两个或以上调节变量需要一个统一分类框架时。

**语料锚定**: hoffmann_cheong_phan_zurbruegg2024 (JM) — business ethics taxonomy (Husted 2007) → corporate culture (intrinsic) + institutional monitoring (extrinsic)

**禁忌**:
- 次要框架必须是一个真实的理论/文献分类系统（不能是作者自创的便捷分类）
- 两个调节变量必须分属不同类型——如果两个都是 intrinsic 约束，框架分类失效
- 不能在引入框架后只讨论一个调节变量——框架承诺了两个类型，必须兑现
- "A key distinction... is thus that the former relies on... while the latter is based on..." 型收束句必须出现
