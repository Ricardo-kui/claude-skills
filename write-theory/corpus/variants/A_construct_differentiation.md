# 变体 A：构念辨析型

> **适用**: 区分两个易混淆构念的理论差异
> **范文**: Pollock 2015 (ASQ), Han 2024 (AMP), Pontikes 2012 (ASQ)
> **最佳期刊**: ASQ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | AMJ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | SMJ ⭐⭐⭐⭐

---

## 段落功能地图

| 段落 | 功能 | 推荐词数 | 必须度 |
|------|------|----------|--------|
| P1 | 构念 A 界定（定义 + 核心要素 + scope conditions） | 80-150 | ✅ |
| P2 | 构念 B 界定（定义 + 核心要素 + scope conditions） | 80-150 | ✅ |
| P3 | 表面相似性说明（为什么这两个构念容易被混淆） | 60-100 | ✅ |
| P4-P7 | 系统差异辨析（First... Second... Third... Fourth...） | 各 60-100 | ✅ |
| P8 | 构念辨析合成（Taken together, this research establishes that A and B are distinct along dimensions X/Y/Z...）——非文献综述段，而是差异化成果的合成 | 50-80 | ✅ |
| P9-P11 | 基于差异的理论机制推演 | 各 70-120 | ✅ |
| P12-P14 | 假设陈述 | 各 30-60 | ✅ |
| P15 | [不推荐] 独立 Closure 段落——管理学标准是最后一个假设后即进入 METHODS | — | ✗ 非标准 |

> **注意**: 管理学顶刊不要求独立的 Closure 段落。最后假设的自然收敛即为 Theory 的终点。

## 关键句式模板

**构念界定**：参见 `corpus/sentences/construct_definition.md` 变体 A/B/C

**表面相似 → 实质差异转折**：
```
"Although these definitions are conceptually similar — both involve [shared feature] — 
they differ in [N] fundamental ways."
```

**差异辨析段落**（每个差异一段）：
```
"First, [Construct A] is derived from [theoretical origin A], whereas [Construct B] 
emerges from [theoretical origin B]. This difference in lineage means that [construct A] 
emphasizes [aspect], while [construct B] prioritizes [different aspect]."
```

**具象化差异**（用例子）：
```
"[Author]'s example vividly illustrated the differences between [A] and [B]. Although 
[A characteristic holds], [B characteristic differs]. Further, though [another 
similarity], [counterpoint showing difference]."
```

**收束**：
```
"Taken together, this research establishes clear theoretical differences between 
[Construct A] and [Construct B]. By disentangling these constructs, we can develop 
more precise predictions about their distinct effects on [outcome]."
```

## 假设陈述格式

| 类型 | 模板 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 差异主效应 | "[Construct A] will have a [stronger/weaker] [positive/negative] effect on [DV] than [Construct B]." | "Reputation will have a stronger positive effect on status than celebrity." |
| 条件效应（基于辨析） | "When [condition derived from differentiation], [Construct A]'s effect on [DV] will be [enhanced/diminished]." | "When firms are young, reputation will have a greater effect on status." |

- [ ] 每个差异（First/Second/Third/Fourth）是否都有文献和例子支撑？
- [ ] 是否避免了"一个构念是另一个的子集"的隐含假设？
- [ ] 差异是否直接导致了不同的理论预测（而非纯粹的语义区分）？
- [ ] 辨析是否对应了可检验的假设差异？

## DV 选择论证型子变体（habel2016 型）

> **适用**: 将构念辨析作为手段（论证 DV 选择合理性），而非将区分本身作为贡献
> **范文**: Habel et al. 2016 (JM), T1 段落
> **与标准构念辨析型的区别**: 标准型区分两个 IV 构念并推导不同效应；本变体区分多个候选 DV 并论证为何选择 focal DV

### 段落功能地图

| 段落 | 功能 | 推荐词数 | 必须度 |
|------|------|----------|--------|
| P1 | Focal construct 定义 + 理论家族 | 80-120 | ✅ |
| P2 | 分类模型定位：用信息处理/因果链模型将 focal construct 定位于具体阶段 | 120-180 | ✅ |
| P3 | 核心区分：focal vs 最相似替代构念 | 80-120 | ✅ |
| P4 | 三理由论证：theoretical fit / mechanism alignment / causal positioning | 150-250 | ✅ |
| P5 | Robustness 预告：将在替代 DV 上验证 | 40-60 | ✅ |

### 关键句式模板

**分类模型定位**:
```
"In this subsection, to substantiate its eligibility and focal role in our model, we 
distinguish [focal construct] from related constructs in [domain]. To this end, we rely 
on the [classification model] firmly established by [research stream] ([citations]). 
According to this model, [actors] process [stimuli] in [N] steps: [step enumeration]."
```

**核心区分**:
```
"Importantly, [focal construct] likewise belongs to the [specific step] because it 
comprises [key feature]. Although both [focal construct] and [related construct] 
represent [shared category], the key difference pertains to [differentiating dimension]."
```

**三理由论证**:
```
"We selected [focal construct] as the key [DV/IV] for [N] reasons: First, [theoretical 
fit — both constructs share theoretical roots]. Second, [mechanism alignment — focal 
construct captures the benefit-cost balance that drives the phenomenon]. Third, [causal 
positioning — focal construct is an antecedent of, not a substitute for, alternative 
outcomes]."
```

**Robustness 预告**:
```
"Importantly, although we focus on [focal construct] in our conceptual model, we 
validate our results for [alternative construct 1], [alternative construct 2], and 
[alternative construct 3] to lend additional support to our conceptualization."
```

### 语料锚定
- habel2016 (JM), T1 — Price fairness vs price-value ratio vs WTP vs sacrifice：用 price information processing model (4-step) 定位 price fairness 在 price evaluation 阶段；三理由论证（social exchange theory fit / distributive justice mechanism alignment / causal positioning as predictor of WTP）

### 关键特征
- **分类模型定位** — 不是纯直觉区分，而是用公认的学术框架（信息处理模型、因果链模型）为构念定位
- **三理由必须独立** — 不能是同一理由的不同表述（"理论契合"≠"机制匹配"≠"因果定位"）
- **Robustness 预告** — 末尾声明将在替代构念上验证，增强方法论可信度
- **区分是手段不是目的** — 构念辨析服务于"为什么选这个 DV"的论证，而非作为独立贡献

### 反模式
- 三理由概念重叠（"A和B都根植于社会交换理论" 和 "A和B都反映equity" 是同一理由）
- 无分类模型直接直觉区分 → 缺乏学术框架支撑
- 忘记 robustness 预告 → 审稿人会问"为什么不检验替代构念？"
- 辨析篇幅过长（超过 Theory 总篇幅的 30%）→ 压缩为核心论证，详情移至 Appendix
