## E2. 跨层调节 (Cross-Level Moderation) — 9 步协议 + T6

**前置声明模板**：
```
"The focal unit of analysis is [Level-1 unit, e.g., the firm-year]. 
[Level-1 units] are nested within [Level-2 units, e.g., industries], 
which are in turn nested within [Level-3 units, e.g., countries]. 
This nesting structure means that [Level-1 observations] within the same 
[Level-2 unit] share common characteristics and are not independent. 

We theorize that [Level-2/3 variable Z] moderates the [Level-1 X → Level-1 Y] 
relationship because [cross-level mechanism: e.g., Z creates institutional 
conditions that alter the costs/benefits of X's effect on Y]. 

We also account for the direct effect of [Z] on [Y] through [separate mechanism], 
distinguishing this cross-level direct effect from the cross-level interaction effect."
```

**段落功能地图**：

| 步骤 | 段落功能 | 推荐词数 | 必须度 |
|------|---------|----------|--------|
| Step 1 | 焦点分析单元声明 + Y 在哪个层级 | 40-60 | ✅ |
| Step 2 | 理论嵌套结构描述 | 50-80 | ✅ |
| Step 3 | 各层级的理论来源 | 60-100 | ✅ |
| Step 4 | Level 1 X→Y 直接效应 + H1 | 70-120 | ✅ |
| Step 5 | 高层/低层 Moderator 选择理由 | 60-100 | ✅ |
| Step 6 | Cross-level 直接效应（如适用） | 50-80 | ⚠️ |
| Step 7 | Cross-level 交互机制推演 | 70-120 | ✅ |
| Step 8 | 排除反向交互（嵌套逻辑） | 40-60 | ✅ |
| Step 9 | 跨层调节假设 | 30-60 | ✅ |
| Step 10 | T6 Closure：框架锁定 + 假设逻辑显性化 | 80-120 | **准强制** |

**跨层假设模板**：
```
"H[N]. The relationship between [Level-1 X] and [Level-1 Y] varies with 
[Level-2 Z] such that the [positive/negative] effect of [X] on [Y] is 
[stronger/weaker] for [Level-1 units] nested in [Level-2 units] with 
[higher/lower] levels of [Z]."
```

## 延迟理论透镜嵌入 (Delayed Theory Lens Embedding)

在部分论文中，理论透镜（如前景理论）是在边界条件推导的**中途**引入的，而非前置的 T2 理论透镜段落。这种模式适用于理论本身轻量且仅服务于特定边界解释的场景。

**语料锚定**：
- Darby 2023 (MSOM) — prospect theory introduced midway through severity boundary derivation

## Rhetorical Question 调节开场模式（Eilert 2017 型）

**适用**：假设树型或调节效应型论文中，用修辞问句开启 moderator 论证段落

**模板**：
```
How does the relationship between [IV] and [DV] vary as a function of [moderator]? 
[Moderator] is [definition]. [Theoretical justification]. There are [N] reasons why 
[moderator] should moderate this relationship.

[Reason 1]: [Mechanism logic] ([citation]). Consequently, [prediction].

[Reason 2]: [Mechanism logic] ([citation]). Thus, [prediction].

Overall, as [moderator] increases, [mechanism effects]. Thus, we expect: 
H[N]: The higher a [moderator], the [weaker/stronger] the relationship between [IV] and [DV].
```

**关键特征**：
- 问句直接承接主效应机制，形成自然过渡
- 定义 moderator 后立即给出其理论重要性
- "There are N reasons" 预告多路径论证
- 每个 reason 独立论证，最后 Overall 收敛

**语料锚定**：
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — H2 opening: "How does the relationship between problem severity and time to recall vary as a function of brand reliability?"
- Eilert 2017 (JM) — H3 opening: "How is the relationship between problem severity and recall timing affected when brand diversification increases?"

**与标准 Step 3 的区别**：
- 标准 Step 3（Moderator Z 的理论选择理由）是声明式："We theorize that Z moderates..."
- Rhetorical Question 模式是探索式："How does... vary as a function of...?"
- 前者更 formal，后者更具 reader engagement，适用于 JM 等允许叙事风格的期刊

**反模式**：
- 问句后无直接回答，转为泛泛讨论 moderator → 问句沦为修辞装饰
- "There are two reasons" 后只给出一个理由 → 承诺未兑现

## E3. 三向交互 (Three-Way Interaction) — 扩展协议

**适用**: 两个调节变量分层作用于同一基线关系，形成条件化的条件

**前置逻辑模板**:
```
"Given that [IV1] may not be sufficient to protect all [actors], particularly [subgroup], 
from [DV], we now investigate if there are ways in which [behavior] can be [modified] 
that do not violate [boundary condition]. Prior theorizing has assumed that [construct] 
is a unitary construct, such that [attribute 1] and [attribute 2] are both [characteristic]. 
However, because [construct] has the potential to [positive consequence], we argue that 
[construct dimension] can be [alternative characteristic] in nature if it [condition]. 
As a result, not all [construct] may be interpreted as violating [boundary condition]."
```

**语料锚定**: kundro_rothbard (AMJ) — "Given that structural power may not be sufficient to protect all moral objectors, particularly women, from retaliation, we now investigate if there are ways in which moral objections can be raised that do not violate gender expectations."

**段落功能地图**:

| 步骤 | 段落功能 | 推荐词数 | 必须度 |
|------|---------|----------|--------|
| Step 1 | 第一层调节回顾 + 问题升级 | 40-60 | ✅ |
| Step 2 | 第二层调节器（W2）的理论选择理由 | 60-100 | ✅ |
| Step 3 | 解构"单一构念"假设，提出变异形式 | 50-80 | ✅ |
| Step 4 | W2 如何改变第一层交互的机制推演 | 70-120 | ✅ |
| Step 5 | 三向交互假设陈述 | 30-60 | ✅ |

**三向交互假设模板**:
```
"H[N]. [IV], [moderator 1], and [moderator 2] interact, such that when [behavior] has a 
[condition A], the relationship between [IV] and [DV] is [direction] for [group 1] compared 
to [group 2]. However, when [behavior] has a [condition B], [DV] is [outcome] and consistent 
across [groups]."
```

**语料锚定**: kundro_rothbard (AMJ) — H3: "Structural power, gender, and moral objection framing interact, such that when a moral objection has a standard frame, the relationship between structural power and retaliation is more negative for men compared to women. However, when a moral objection has an organizational frame, retaliation is mitigated and consistent across gender and structural power levels."

**关键规则**:
- 第二层调节器的引入必须回答"Given that 第一层调节不足..."的问题
- 必须先解构现有文献对核心构念的"单一性"假设
- 三向交互的 mechanism 必须明确说明 W2 如何改变 W1 的调节方向/强度
- 不能只说"三者交互"，必须说明"在 W2=A 时 W1 的调节存在，在 W2=B 时 W1 的调节消失/反转"

**反模式**:
- W2 的引入缺乏理论依据，只是"为了复杂性而加变量" → 审稿人质疑 empirical convenience
- 未先建立第一层交互（X×W1→Y）就引入第二层 → 读者无法理解 W2 的增量贡献
- 三向交互假设只写"三者交互"，未说明条件化模式（W2=A 时 vs W2=B 时）→ 假设无法检验
- 解构"单一构念"时，新提出的维度与原有维度区分不清 → 审稿人质疑"这难道不是同一个东西？"
- W2 改变 W1 的机制逻辑跳跃：只说"W2 改变了 W1"，未解释**为什么**和**如何**改变

## E4. 有中介的调节 (Moderated Mediation) — 机制链升级

**适用**: 调节变量改变的是中介路径（X→M→Y）而非直接路径，且需要形式化 moderated mediation 假设

**机制定位声明**:
```
"We further argue that since violating [role expectation] leads to perceptions of [mediator], 
observers will perceive [group A] (relative to [group B]) as being lower in [mediator] 
([citations]), even when they occupy [condition]."

"In sum, we expect that [group A with condition] will face [DV outcome]. We argue that this 
is because [group A], relative to [group B], are viewed as [mediator level] as they [change condition]."
```

**语料锚定**: kundro_rothbard (AMJ) — "In sum, we expect that higher-powered men will face lower levels of retaliation as they increase in power compared to women. We argue that this is because higher-power men, relative to women, are viewed as higher in self-control as they increase in power."

**有中介的调节假设模板**:
```
"H[N]. When a [behavior] has a [frame A], the [positive/negative] indirect effect of [IV] 
on [DV], through [mediator], is [stronger/weaker] when [moderator condition A] versus 
[moderator condition B]. When a [behavior] has a [frame B], there is not any difference 
in the indirect effects of [IV] on [DV], through [mediator], for [condition A] or [condition B]."
```

**语料锚定**: kundro_rothbard (AMJ) — H4

**关键规则**:
- 必须先建立 X→M→Y 的基础中介链（通常在 H1/H2 中）
- 调节假设必须明确指定调节发生在中介路径的哪个环节（X→M? M→Y? both?）
- 有中介的调节假设通常与三向交互假设配对出现
- 必须报告 moderated mediation index (IMM) 或 conditional indirect effects

**反模式**:
- 未建立基础中介链（X→M→Y）就直接提出 moderated mediation → 审稿人质疑"M 的存在依据是什么？"
- 只说"调节发生在中介路径"，但未指明是 X→M 还是 M→Y → 机制描述模糊
- 假设形式化错误：使用主效应假设形式（X→Y）而非 indirect effect 形式 → 检验方法不匹配
- 有中介的调节与三向交互未配对出现 → 理论结构不完整（仅报告 moderated mediation 而无三向交互解释）
- 忽略 IMM 或 conditional indirect effects 的报告要求 → 实证部分被审稿人要求补做

## E5. 多层调节器分类结构（Multi-Level Moderator Classification）

**适用**: 多个调节变量需要按分析层次（环境/组织/个体）或理论维度分类，以展示系统性边界条件框架

**"Shift Parameters" 引入模板**:
```
"Given the benefits and detriments of [IV], it is essential to identify shift parameters 
that alter their comparative costs and effectiveness. The [theoretical lens] suggests that 
the impacts of [IV] are contingent upon [level 1] and [level 2] factors associated with 
[resource/theme] ([citations]). At the [level 1], [resource/theme] is determined by [determinant 1] 
and [determinant 2]. On the [dimension 1], [explanation]. On the [dimension 2], [explanation]. 
Therefore, we focus on the contingent roles of [moderator 1], an indicator of [dimension 1], 
and [moderator 2], an indicator of [dimension 2].

At the [level 2], firms differ widely in [resource endowment] ([citations]). Firms with 
[resource advantage] have less need to rely on [IV] for [resource acquisition] ([citations]). 
Firms can access resources from [source 1] and [source 2]. [Source 1 details]. [Source 2 details]. 
Accordingly, we assess the contingent roles of [moderator 3] and [moderator 4], as these two 
factors respectively capture a firm's access to resources from [source 1] and [source 2]."
```

**语料锚定**: shen_zhou_wang_zhang (JOM) — Introduction P3 + Theory 2.2

**段落功能地图**:

| 步骤 | 段落功能 | 推荐词数 | 必须度 |
|------|---------|----------|--------|
| Step 1 | "Shift parameters" 框架声明 + 理论依据 | 50-80 | ✅ |
| Step 2 | 第一层（环境层）调节器引入 + 维度解释 | 60-100 | ✅ |
| Step 3 | 第二层（组织层）调节器引入 + 资源来源解释 | 60-100 | ✅ |
| Step 4 | 概念模型图预告 | 20-40 | ⚠️ |

**关键特征**:
- "shift parameters" 作为调节变量的理论包装术语（源自政治嵌入视角/制度理论）
- 明确分层：环境层（environment level）vs 组织层（firm level）
- 每个层次内的调节器有统一的理论维度（如环境层：资源供给 vs 资源需求；组织层：内部资源 vs 外部资源）
- 调节器的引入与资源依赖逻辑紧密相连

**假设推导的层级一致性规则**:
- 环境层调节器（如 factor market development, industrial competition）的推导应基于**市场制度逻辑**
- 组织层调节器（如 foreign shareholding, customer concentration）的推导应基于**企业内部资源禀赋逻辑**
- 同一层次内的调节器不应相互矛盾（如两个环境层调节器不应一个增强、一个削弱，除非有明确的理论解释）

**反模式**:
- 调节器分层缺乏理论依据（如"我们选择这四个变量因为文献常用"）
- 同一层次内调节器数量过多（>3个）导致层次功能模糊
- 环境层和组织层调节器的机制推导使用同一逻辑（未体现层次差异）

## QC 检查点（E1, E2, E3, E4, E5 共用）

- [ ] X→Y baseline mechanism 是否在调节假设前明确写出？
- [ ] Moderator 的选择是理论驱动还是 empirical convenience？
- [ ] Z→Y 的 direct effect 机制是否与 moderation 机制明确区分？
- [ ] 交互模式（enhancing/buffering/antagonistic/existence/competing）是否明确命名或可推断？
- [ ] 假设语言是否与实证检验匹配（differential prediction vs differential validity）？
- [ ] 是否排除了反向交互（时序/层级/理论方向）？
- [ ] 对于跨层模型：unit of analysis, nesting, 和 level-specific theory 是否在假设前声明？
- [ ] **E3 新增**: 三向交互中，W2 是否明确改变 W1 的调节方向/强度（而非泛泛的"三者交互"）？
- [ ] **E4 新增**: 有中介的调节中，调节是否明确发生在中介路径的具体环节（X→M vs M→Y）？
- [ ] T6 Closure 是否存在？**强制！** 调节效应型 4+ 假设后若无 Closure，理论碎片化——审稿人常见攻击点。实证证据: singh_grewal2023 (JMR) — 4 假设后无 Closure; desai2012 (AMJ) — 4 调节假设后直接进入 Methods，无整合框架收束。
